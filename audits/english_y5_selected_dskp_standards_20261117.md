# English SK Year 5 2027 teacher-choice standards

The selections below apply to the CikguLaju activities used for seven Language Awareness lessons and two classroom projects. The official [Year 5 SK SOW](https://www.teacherfiera.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Primary-Year-5-SoW-SK.pdf) leaves the standards for these lessons to teacher selection. Codes are from the [Year 5 SK DSKP, printed pp. 22, 26, 31 and 35](https://www.teacherfiera.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/DSKP-ENGLISH-YEAR-5-SK-30-12-2020-Latest.pdf). The content standard is the first two numbers of each learning standard.
