# RPT DLP Year 6 SK 2027

- Mathematics has 46 numbered lessons, eight review sessions and 96 teaching hours. The 38 Learning Standards follow the English edition of the DSKP KSSR Revised 2017 published in January 2022. Textbook references use printed pages in the DBP 2021 edition, ISBN 978-983-49-3328-9.
- Science has 52 numbered lessons, nine review sessions and 64 teaching hours. The 96 Learning Standards include the 12 Scientific Skills standards. Textbook references use printed pages in the DBP 2021 edition, ISBN 978-983-49-3322-7.
- Both plans follow the [KPM Academic Calendar 2027](https://www.moe.gov.my/index.php/surat-siaran-kpm-bil1-tahun-2027-kalendar-akademik) for 42 teaching windows for groups A and B. No lessons are allocated during the two full school closure windows. The Deepavali week has 90 Mathematics minutes and 60 Science minutes. Schools place these minutes around their own timetable and state closures.
- Source comparison used the [English DSKP from SK Penampang](https://sites.google.com/moe-dl.edu.my/kurikulumskpenampang/home/dskp), the [Mathematics textbook](https://anyflip.com/qgfbo/cjnf/basic), the [Science textbook](https://anyflip.com/ejbrb/ncye/basic), and RPT 2026 references for [Mathematics](https://www.dminspired.com/kids-school/) and [Science](https://www.scribd.com/document/1058554647/Rpt-Sc-Dlp-Year-6-Sk-2026-by-Rozayus-Academy-Kump-a-Kump-b). The Mathematics BT086001 and Science BT076001 codes follow the [SPBT ordering list](https://www.scribd.com/document/851746648/Borang-Pra-Pesanan-Buku-Teks-SPBT-Tahun-2025-Kegunaan-2026).
- Verified all Learning Standard codes, ordered lesson numbers, 42 date pairs, planned teaching minutes, and seven A4 landscape pages in each PDF rendered from the matching Word file. The PBD tasks are CikguLaju planning suggestions.
