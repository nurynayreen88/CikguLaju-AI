DAILY LESSON PLAN

English · Year 3

My Daily Routine

Kelas / Class
Tarikh / Date
Masa / Time
Bil. Murid / Pupils
Tema / UnitTheme: World of Self, Family and Friends · Lesson: Daily Activities
Standard KandunganUnderstand and communicate simple information about daily activities.
Standard PembelajaranAsk and answer simple questions about familiar routines using appropriate vocabulary.
Objektif PembelajaranBy the end of the lesson, pupils can describe three daily activities in simple, meaningful sentences.
Kriteria KejayaanPupils name common routines, arrange events in order, and communicate a short sequence to a partner.
Pengetahuan Sedia AdaPupils can name familiar times of day and activities at home.
Bahan Bantu MengajarStudent Book, Activity Book, picture cards and routine-sequencing strips.
EMK / NilaiPersonal hygiene, responsibility and respect for others.
PAK21 / KBATPair work, guided speaking and collaborative sequencing.
PentaksiranTeacher observation, oral responses and individual sentence completion.
Set InduksiThe teacher displays three routine pictures. Pupils identify the actions and say when each activity usually happens.
Aktiviti UtamaBuild key vocabularyThe teacher models words such as wake up, have breakfast and go to school. Pupils match pictures to the expressions and practise speaking in complete sentences.Speak and sequenceWorking in pairs, pupils arrange routine cards in a logical order. Each pupil describes the sequence while a partner listens and asks one simple question.Independent practice and assessmentPupils complete a short Activity Book task or workbook exercise on daily routines. The teacher checks vocabulary, sequence and sentence clarity.
PenutupPupils share one daily habit they wish to practise consistently. The teacher recaps useful expressions and checks individual responses.
Pemulihan / PengayaanSupport: Use picture prompts and sentence frames. Extension: Pupils compare two routines using additional time expressions.
RefleksiRecord the number of pupils who met the objective and the language support required for the next lesson.

Representing Fractions

Kelas / Class
Tarikh / Date
Masa / Time
Bil. Murid / Pupils
Tema / UnitUnit: Fractions
Standard KandunganRecognise and use fractions in familiar contexts.
Standard PembelajaranRepresent parts of a whole and explain the numerator and denominator.
Objektif PembelajaranBy the end of the lesson, pupils can represent and label at least four fractions correctly.
Kriteria KejayaanPupils divide shapes into equal parts, shade the appropriate number and explain each fraction.
Pengetahuan Sedia AdaPupils have worked with equal parts of a shape.
Bahan Bantu MengajarFraction strips, coloured paper and task cards.
EMK / NilaiAccuracy, collaboration and reasoning.
PAK21 / KBATHands-on learning, pair discussion and problem solving.
PentaksiranFraction diagrams, oral explanation and short individual exercise.
Set InduksiThe teacher displays a paper strip divided into equal parts and asks pupils to identify the whole and the shaded sections.
Aktiviti UtamaModel a fractionThe teacher demonstrates equal partitions and labels the numerator and denominator. Pupils create similar fraction representations using paper strips.Represent and explainIn pairs, pupils shade different fractions and explain their diagrams to partners. The teacher checks that all parts are of equal size.Independent assessmentPupils draw and label four fractions. The teacher checks each representation and identifies misconceptions for follow-up.
PenutupPupils explain the difference between the numerator and denominator. The teacher summarises the importance of equal parts.
Pemulihan / PengayaanSupport: Provide pre-divided templates. Extension: Pupils compare two fraction models.
RefleksiRecord attainment and pupils needing more support in representing equal parts.
