RPH Contoh — Bahasa Inggeris Tahun 3

Topic: Greetings & Introductions
Learning objective: Pupils can introduce themselves using simple sentences.
Materials: Name cards and picture prompts.

Set induction (5 min): Teacher greets pupils and models “Hello, my name is…”; pupils respond.

Main activities (20 min): 1. Teacher demonstrates introductions with name cards. 2. Pupils practise in pairs and swap partners. 3. Pupils share their partner’s name.

Assessment (5 min): Teacher checks each pupil’s ability to introduce themselves clearly.

Closure (5 min): Pupils recap two useful expressions.

Contoh ilustrasi sahaja. Belum dipadankan dengan DSKP, SOW, Student Book atau Workbook yang disahkan.
