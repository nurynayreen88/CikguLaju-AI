# PPKI English Year 2 — RPT 2027 planning draft

- Join the 57-row release index and dated proposal by PdP. Both preserve the released source order.
- Proposed 60 minutes per PdP (57 hours), not a mandatory standalone English allocation. The Asas 3M DSKP describes combined teaching time; reconcile the three components against current applicable circular and school timetable before final approval.
- Date windows, not fixed class dates. A: Sunday–Thursday; B: Monday–Friday. Both start 4 January 2027. End of proposed content 4/5 November 2027.
- Excludes full windows around Chinese New Year, March break, midyear assessment/holidays and August break. Other public/festival and state-specific holidays inside windows must be checked and actual sessions shifted to available days. Do not treat the entire window as school days.
