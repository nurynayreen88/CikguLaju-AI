# Science (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 1 Scientific Skills

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-01 · Observe with the senses

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.1
**References** · Textbook pp. 2–3 · Activity Book pp. 1–2
**Learning objective** · Record three observations and name the sense used for each.
**Materials** · Fabric swatches, closed shaker and a three-row recording sheet.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Show two cloths that look alike. Ask how pupils could tell them apart. | Compare what sight can tell them before touching the cloths. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Model one property-and-sense pair. Let pairs compare the cloths and listen to a closed shaker. | Describe a texture, compare sounds and use sight to record a visible property. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Give each pupil a different swatch and shake a sealed container. Check three property-and-sense pairs. | Record three observations and name the sense used for each. |
| Closure · 3 min | Ask pupils which sense helped with texture. | Name the sense and give a checkable example. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-02 · Classify living things

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.2
**References** · Textbook pp. 4–5 · Activity Book pp. 3
**Learning objective** · Sort six pictures by one visible feature and state the sorting rule.
**Materials** · Six animal cards per pupil and two sorting mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Display a hen and a cat. Ask which visible body covering differs. | Point out feathers and fur in the pictures. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Model two groups for one feature. Give pairs six cards to sort, then ask them to test another visible feature. | Sort the cards, check a partner's placements and explain why the groups change with the rule. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Check all six placements in an independent sort using clear bird and mammal pictures. | Create two groups for one visible feature and state the rule. |
| Closure · 3 min | Place a duck on the fur mat. | Move it and point to the evidence. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-03 · Measure and record length

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.3
**References** · Textbook pp. 6–7 · Activity Book pp. 4–5
**Learning objective** · Select a ruler, measure two objects from the zero mark and record each reading in centimetres.
**Materials** · 30 cm rulers, whole-centimetre strips and a recording grid.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Place two strips with staggered ends. Ask how to compare them fairly. | Line up the ends and suggest a ruler. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Model starting at zero and looking down at the far end. Let partners alternate measuring and checking. | Measure practice strips and record each length in cm. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Give each pupil an 8 cm and a 13 cm strip. Check both readings and ruler placement. | Measure both strips independently and write each reading with cm. |
| Closure · 3 min | Show a strip starting at the 1 cm mark. | Show how to reposition it before reading. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-04 · Make an inference

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.4
**References** · Textbook pp. 8–10 · Activity Book pp. 6–7
**Learning objective** · State one observation and use it to support one plausible explanation.
**Materials** · Photos of two similar plants, evidence cards and a two-column sheet.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Display a wilted plant and ask pupils what they can see. | Give visible details without proposing a cause yet. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Reveal photos of moist and dry soil. Model how a detail can support a possible reason. Ask groups to propose two explanations and a way to check one. | Separate observations from possible causes. Compare which explanation has better evidence and describe an extra check. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Show a new picture of a wet bag beneath a bottle with a loose cap. Check that each explanation draws on a visible clue. | Write one observation and one plausible inference linked by because. |
| Closure · 5 min | Read a claim with no clue attached. | Supply evidence that would support the claim or suggest what to check. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-05 · Predict from evidence

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.5
**References** · Textbook pp. 11–12 · Activity Book pp. 8
**Learning objective** · Make a prediction and cite a pattern or an earlier observation to support it.
**Materials** · Pattern cards and a record of three puddle outlines at equal time intervals.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Show an unfinished counter pattern. | Say what comes next and point to the repeated part. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Show three outlines of a shrinking puddle. Mark their order and ask pairs to predict the fourth outline. | Draw the next outline and tell a partner which earlier outline supports it. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Give a fresh sequence of damp marks photographed in order. Check the prediction and the evidence cited. | Sketch the next likely mark and write one supporting observation. |
| Closure · 3 min | Reveal the final photo and invite a comparison. | Say how the new photo supports or changes the prediction. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-06 · Communicate findings

**Standards** · CS 1.1 · LS 1.1.6
**References** · Textbook pp. 13–14 · Activity Book pp. 9
**Learning objective** · Present three observations in a labelled table or drawing and explain one finding.
**Materials** · Hand lenses, leaf specimens, pencils and blank grids.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show three loose notes about leaves. Ask what would help a reader see which note belongs to which leaf. | Suggest identifiers or headings. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model one row with a specimen label and a property. Give groups three leaves and invite them to choose a table or labelled drawings. | Observe all three leaves, agree how to organise the details and explain a comparison to a partner. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give each pupil three new leaves labelled A–C. Check three accurate observations, labels and one finding. | Record one visible property per leaf and explain one finding from the record. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show one row without a label. | Add a useful label and explain why it helps. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-07 · Use apparatus and handle specimens

**Standards** · CS 1.2 · LS 1.2.1, 1.2.2
**References** · Textbook pp. 15–17 · Activity Book pp. 10–11
**Learning objective** · Demonstrate safe use of two tools and transfer a specimen without damaging it.
**Materials** · Hand lenses, plastic forceps, shallow trays and fallen leaves.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a curled leaf and a hand lens. Ask how to bring its edge into view without tearing it. | Suggest placing the leaf in a tray and moving the lens. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Demonstrate lifting a leaf by its stem with forceps and adjusting a hand lens. Supervise pair practice in both roles. | Transfer the leaf, examine its edge and switch roles with a partner. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Watch each pupil use both tools and transfer a new fallen leaf intact. Record safe use and handling on a checklist. | Lift and place a leaf with forceps, then use the lens to show one edge detail. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how to handle a delicate specimen next time. | Name one action that keeps it intact. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U01-08 · Sketch, clean and store

**Standards** · CS 1.2 · LS 1.2.3, 1.2.4, 1.2.5
**References** · Textbook pp. 18–20 · Activity Book pp. 12–14
**Learning objective** · Draw and label a specimen and a beaker, then demonstrate how to clean, dry and store the beaker.
**Materials** · Potted seedlings, plastic beakers, water trays, towels, a labelled sample bottle and labelled storage.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two sketches of a seedling and ask which one describes what is visible. | Point to the outline and labels for the leaf and stem. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model a clear sketch and place the used beaker in the cleaning area. Show how to seal and shelve the labelled sample bottle. Guide pairs through sketching, rinsing, drying and returning the apparatus. | Draw a seedling and sketch the beaker beside it. Take turns cleaning and storing the beaker, then return the closed sample bottle to its shelf. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's labelled sketch and observe rinsing, drying and correct storage of the beaker and sealed sample bottle. | Submit labelled sketches and demonstrate storing the clean beaker and the sample bottle. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where a wet beaker belongs before storage. | Point to the drying area and explain the order. |
