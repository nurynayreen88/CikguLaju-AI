# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 4 Money

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-36 · Add two or three amounts

**Standards** · CS 4.1 · LS 4.1.1, 4.1.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 2–3 · Activity Book pp. 77–78
**Learning objective** · Calculate two totals within RM10,000 and show correct ringgit-and-sen alignment.
**Materials** · Play money, price cards and ringgit-and-sen place-value mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Combine two groups of sen coins and ask when to exchange them for a ringgit. | Group coins or cards into 100 sen and show the exchange. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model addition with aligned ringgit and sen. Add two amounts, then three, showing an exchange across the decimal point. | Build and record totals in pairs. Estimate each total first and check that sen appears as two digits. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give RM1,263.75 + RM485.60 and RM2,450.90 + RM385.75 + RM64.35. Check RM1,749.35 and RM2,901.00. | Calculate both totals individually and label one exchange from sen to ringgit. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask how to write a total with zero sen. | Write the amount with .00 and explain its meaning. |

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-38 · Mix addition and subtraction of money

**Learning objective** · Use a running balance to solve a transaction sequence within RM10,000.
**Materials** · A miniature account ledger, transaction cards and place-value mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-39 · Multiply amounts of money

**Standards** · CS 4.4 · LS 4.4.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 9–10 · Activity Book pp. 82–83
**Learning objective** · Calculate totals for repeated costs using a one-digit multiplier and powers of ten within RM10,000.
**Materials** · Order forms, play money and ringgit-and-sen grids.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two identical price tags at RM4.25. | Find the total for the pair and say which operation can represent repeated costs. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model multiplying a price by a one-digit number and use place value to handle 10, 100 and 1,000. Keep the totals within RM10,000. | Complete sample order forms, record total prices and use repeated groups or place value to check. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give RM18.75 × 4, RM12.45 × 10, RM8.35 × 100 and RM4.65 × 1,000. Check RM75.00, RM124.50, RM835.00 and RM4,650.00. | Calculate all four totals and explain one place-value movement. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why the answer to an order of four RM18.75 items is around RM80. | Use rounding to check that the answer is reasonable. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-40 · Divide amounts of money

**Standards** · CS 4.5 · LS 4.5.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 11–13 · Activity Book pp. 84
**Learning objective** · Share totals equally and check the per-person amounts against the original totals.
**Materials** · Group mats, play ringgit and sen, and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show RM9.00 to share equally among three groups. | Give RM3.00 to each group and explain that the shares are equal. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model RM18.75 shared among three pupils. Exchange ringgit for sen when needed and demonstrate exact division by 10, 100 and 1,000. | Record per-person amounts and multiply each share by the number of groups to check. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give RM18.75 ÷ 3, RM126.50 ÷ 10, RM835.00 ÷ 100 and RM4,650.00 ÷ 1,000. Check RM6.25, RM12.65, RM8.35 and RM4.65. | Calculate the shares and use multiplication to verify one result. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what would go wrong if sen were dropped from a share. | Explain that the original total would not be recovered. |

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-42 · Read the value of RM1

**Standards** · CS 4.6 · LS 4.6.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 16 · Activity Book pp. 86
**Learning objective** · Read equivalent values of RM1 from a current rate table supplied by the teacher and record the source date.
**Materials** · Teacher-printed source-dated rate table, currency-name cards and reading slips.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two table headings, units per RM1 and ringgit per unit. Ask which heading answers today's question. | Identify units per RM1 and point to its unit label. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Use a recent dated rate table supplied by the teacher. Guide pupils to find the three currency rows and read the column in the correct direction. Set out the values for RM1 before class if the provider quotes larger units. | Read three equivalent values for RM1, copy the currency units and note the publication date. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask pupils to read three specified rows and verify all entries and the date against the provided table. | Record the three values and state how a different date may produce different values. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why the date should be written beside each record. | Explain that the same currencies can have different values on different days. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U04-43 · Distinguish needs, wants and saving goals

**Standards** · CS 4.7 · LS 4.7.1, 4.7.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 17–19 · Activity Book pp. 87
**Learning objective** · Sort a sample budget and explain why saving or investing can support a future goal.
**Materials** · Household-scenario cards, a simple budget template and savings-goal cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show cards for drinking water and an extra game. Ask how each would fit into a budget. | Explain why one is usually essential and the other depends on the situation. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Use a fictional budget to allocate money to essentials and a future goal. Explain savings as money set aside and an investment as using money to seek future value, which can go up or down. | Sort the expense cards and explain contextual choices. Compare setting aside RM5 each week with the idea of an investment. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give a weekly RM20 example with RM12 for required transport and RM5 set aside. Check the RM3 balance and an explanation of saving toward a future goal. | Label a need and a want, calculate the balance and explain how saving helps meet the stated goal. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether investment value is guaranteed to rise. | Explain that it can rise or fall using the fictional example. |

**Learning objective** · Balance a short set of transactions and check the answer against a budget limit.
**Materials** · A classroom shop ledger, product tags and checking slips.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Give a budget of RM60 and display two possible purchases. | Estimate whether both items fit the budget. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model recording spending in a ledger. Use an estimate before calculating and compare the exact amount with the budget. | Record purchases in order and explain the running balance to a partner. |

| Closure · 5 min | Ask which cost could be reduced to make the total fit. | Propose a reduction of at least RM0.45 and check the resulting total. |
