## DLP-MAT-Y3-U02-11 · Add two whole numbers

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 28–35 · Activity Book pp. 17–21
**Learning objective** · Solve two additions within 10,000 and explain the regrouping in one calculation.
**Materials** · Place-value mats, base-ten blocks and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show a tray of nine tens and ask what happens when four more tens are added. | Exchange ten tens for one hundred and name the remaining tens. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model 2,786 + 1,457 in aligned columns. Mark each exchange and let pairs explain where the extra ten or hundred goes. | Build one column with place-value blocks, then calculate a fresh example and explain one exchange. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 2,786 + 1,457 and 4,068 + 2,795. Check 4,243 and 6,863 and one labelled exchange in the second sum. | Calculate independently, circle an exchange and explain how it was recorded. |
| Closure · 10 min | Show a sum with the carried digit omitted. | Point to the affected column and explain the correction. |
