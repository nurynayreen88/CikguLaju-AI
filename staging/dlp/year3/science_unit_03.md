## DLP-SCI-Y3-U03-10 · Types of teeth and functions

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.1
**References** · Textbook pp. 30–31 · Activity Book pp. 17–19
**Learning objective** · Match incisors, canines and molars with cutting, tearing and grinding.
**Materials** · Large tooth images and three action cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Display a snipped and a crushed piece of modelling clay. | Describe how the two actions changed the clay. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Use tooth pictures to demonstrate cutting, tearing and grinding with clay models. | Match tooth shapes to actions and explain one match to a partner. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Give three unfamiliar tooth images and three function cards. Check all three pairings. | Name each tooth type and match it with its function. |
| Closure · 3 min | Hold up the broad flat tooth. | Name it and give its job. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U03-20 · Explain what happens after digestion

**Standards** · CS 3.3 · LS 3.3.3, 3.3.4
**References** · Textbook pp. 49–54 · Activity Book pp. 35
**Learning objective** · Use arrows in a diagram to distinguish nutrient absorption from the removal of undigested waste.
**Materials** · Large digestive-system diagram, coloured pencils and caption cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two arrows leaving the intestine diagram. Ask what each might represent. | Predict where useful nutrients and waste go. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Use two arrow colours for nutrients entering the blood from the small intestine and for undigested material moving on. Trace waste through the large intestine to the anus. | Make and explain a two-colour diagram with a key. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's arrows for nutrient absorption and waste removal. Ask one pupil at a time to explain the difference. | Draw and label both paths and write what each arrow means. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what happens to food material that the body cannot digest. | Trace the waste route to the exit. |
