# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 5 Time

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-45 · Use an activity schedule

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.1, 5.1.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 26–27 · Activity Book pp. 93
**Learning objective** · Extract three facts from a schedule and record times before, during and after a named activity.
**Materials** · Printed school schedules, clock faces and before/during/after cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a schedule with arrival at 8:10 a.m. and reading at 8:30 a.m. | Identify which activity begins first and read both times aloud. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model reading a row in a schedule and checking morning or afternoon. Use an event before, a time during and an event after the activity. | Mark the three times on a timeline and check that the middle time lies within the activity. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give arrival at 11:40 a.m., rehearsal from 11:50 a.m. to 12:20 p.m. and a quiz at 12:30 p.m. Accept 11:40 a.m., 12:05 p.m. and 12:30 p.m. | Name the activity and record one time before, one during and one after it. Label each time a.m. or p.m. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where 12:15 p.m. belongs on the rehearsal timeline. | Refer to its recorded end time. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-47 · Relate and convert time units

**Standards** · CS 5.2 · LS 5.2.1, 5.2.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 30–32 · Activity Book pp. 95
**Learning objective** · State the calendar relationships and convert examples between hours and minutes and between minutes and seconds.
**Materials** · A calendar, clock faces, conversion cards and short number lines.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 60 marks on a clock face and ask how many minutes make one hour. | Name 60 minutes and point to a full turn. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Use the calendar to relate years to months and weeks to days. Use clocks to relate hours to minutes and minutes to seconds. | Group conversion cards and solve examples using multiplication by 60 or regrouping into groups of 60. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask for months in two years, days in three weeks, minutes in 2 hours 15 minutes and seconds in 3 minutes 20 seconds. Check 24, 21, 135 and 200. | Record the relationship used and write each answer with its unit. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether every calendar month has the same number of days. | Compare two months on the calendar. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-48 · Add and subtract time

**Standards** · CS 5.4, 5.5 · LS 5.4.1, 5.5.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 34–37 · Activity Book pp. 96–99
**Learning objective** · Solve one sum and one difference including compound time units and show any regrouping.
**Materials** · Duration cards, two-column time mats and number lines.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 1 hour 45 minutes and 35 minutes. | Estimate whether their sum reaches two hours. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model the sum by exchanging 60 minutes for one hour. For subtraction, show how to exchange one hour for 60 minutes before removing minutes. | Use time mats for hours with minutes and minutes with seconds. Verify regrouping with the matching unit relationship. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 1 hour 45 minutes + 35 minutes and 3 minutes 10 seconds − 1 minute 35 seconds. Check 2 hours 20 minutes and 1 minute 35 seconds. | Show both calculations and label where the exchange occurs. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what makes an exchange of 60 possible in both examples. | Link the exchanges to 60 minutes per hour and 60 seconds per minute. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-49 · Mix addition and subtraction of time

**Standards** · CS 5.6 · LS 5.6.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 38–39 · Activity Book pp. 100
**Learning objective** · Track two changes on a timeline and give the resulting time in the requested units.
**Materials** · Timeline strips, activity cards and duration tables.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show 2 hours of planned activity with an extra 25 minutes and a cut of 40 minutes. | Predict whether the revised total will be below or above two hours. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Draw two arrows on a timeline to represent the changes. Record the interim amount and regroup hours and minutes where needed. | Mark increases and decreases in order and compare the timeline with a written calculation. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 2 hours 10 minutes + 35 minutes − 50 minutes and 3 minutes − 55 seconds + 20 seconds. Check 1 hour 55 minutes and 2 minutes 25 seconds. | Track both changes on timelines and record the results in the stated compound units. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask how reversing the last arrow checks an answer. | Recover the intermediate duration by using the opposite operation. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-50 · Multiply and divide time

**Standards** · CS 5.7, 5.8 · LS 5.7.1, 5.8.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 40–43 · Activity Book pp. 101–102
**Learning objective** · Calculate repeated durations and equal parts of a duration using a one-digit factor or divisor.
**Materials** · Duration strips, clock faces and group mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show four sessions that each take 25 minutes. | Count repeated intervals and explain why they can be multiplied. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model multiplying 25 minutes by four and converting 100 minutes. Show dividing 2 hours 24 minutes among four groups. Add a short example with minutes and seconds. | Represent repeated and equal intervals and check that the same duration is used per interval. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 25 minutes × 4 and 2 hours 24 minutes ÷ 4. Check 1 hour 40 minutes and 36 minutes. | Show both calculations and multiply the share back to verify the division. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what number is held constant when converting units. | Explain that the total duration stays the same. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U05-51 · Create and solve time problems

**Standards** · CS 5.9 · LS 5.9.1, 5.9.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 44–47 · Activity Book pp. 103–104
**Learning objective** · Write a matching time story and use a schedule or timeline to solve a daily problem.
**Materials** · A school programme schedule, timeline templates and story cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show the expression 35 minutes + 45 minutes. | Propose two activities with those durations. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model turning the expression into a question, then use a school timetable to find how long two activities and a break take. | Write a matching story and use a timeline to justify a partner's answer. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask for a story about 35 + 45 minutes, then give a programme starting at 9:20 a.m. with 35 minutes of reading, a 10-minute break and 25 minutes of drawing. Check 80 minutes and an end time of 10:30 a.m. | Write the story, solve both tasks and check the end time by adding the full 70-minute timetable duration. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask which quantity in the programme is a clock time. | Identify the start or end time and distinguish it from a duration. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-R05 · Review: Time

**Standards** · CS 5.1, 5.9 · Review LS 5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.9.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 48 · Activity Book pp. 105–106
**Learning objective** · Use a timetable to plan a sequence of activities and check the total duration.
**Materials** · Activity cards, a blank timetable and a clock-face model.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a 9:00 a.m. start and three activity cards. | Place the activities in the stated order. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model adding one duration to a starting time. Let pairs draft a timetable and use a running check. | Make a schedule and check that no two consecutive activities overlap. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give a 9:00 a.m. start, 25 minutes of reading, a 10-minute break and 35 minutes of drawing. Check a 10:10 a.m. end time and a 70-minute total including the break. | Record each start time, final time and total duration. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how to verify the end time a second way. | Compare the time difference with the sum of the three durations. |
