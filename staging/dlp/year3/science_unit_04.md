# Science (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 4 Animals

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U04-21 · Group animals by what they eat

**Standards** · CS 4.1 · LS 4.1.1, 4.1.2
**References** · Textbook pp. 60–63 · Activity Book pp. 37–39
**Learning objective** · Classify six examples as herbivores, carnivores or omnivores using food evidence.
**Materials** · Six animal cards showing food examples and three sorting mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a cow eating grass and a kingfisher holding a fish. | Identify the food visible in each image. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model sorting by what the animal normally eats. Introduce herbivore, carnivore and omnivore with plant-only, animal-only and mixed diet examples. | Sort the cards in pairs and cite a food example for each group. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give each pupil six cards with diet clues. Check every classification and one evidence statement. | Classify six animals and write one food clue beside a selected answer. |
| Closure · 5 min | Say that a hen eats both grain and insects. | Name its group and explain the mixed diet. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U04-22 · Infer the group from an animal's diet

**Standards** · CS 4.1 · LS 4.1.3
**References** · Textbook pp. 62–63 · Activity Book pp. 40–41
**Learning objective** · Use a food record to infer the group of an unfamiliar animal and justify the answer.
**Materials** · Short feeding records for familiar and unfamiliar species.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Read that one animal eats insects and fruit. | Infer which diet group fits the record. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model using several records of foods eaten by a species. Discuss cases with too little information. | Compare a mixed diet record and an incomplete one. Explain when the evidence supports an inference. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give each pupil a record of a new animal eating fruit and beetles. Check the group and two clues. | Identify the animal as an omnivore and cite the plant and animal foods. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show a record with one food item only. | Suggest one extra observation that could strengthen an inference. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U04-23 · Compare animal teeth

**Standards** · CS 4.1 · LS 4.1.4
**References** · Textbook pp. 64–66 · Activity Book pp. 42–43
**Learning objective** · Compare two dental features across three animal groups and relate one feature to diet.
**Materials** · Herbivore, carnivore and omnivore skull images and feature grids.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Display two large tooth images and ask what visible features differ. | Compare the shape and surface of each tooth. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Label example cutting, tearing and crushing surfaces. Compare a goat, a cat and a human using the images. | Record two visible features per skull and discuss one connection to diet. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's two-feature comparison across all three groups and one link grounded in the images. | Complete the grid and explain how one pictured feature helps with a food type. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask which pictured surface is useful for grinding plant food. | Point to it and give a reason. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U04-24 · Explain evidence about animal diets

**Standards** · CS 4.1 · LS 4.1.5
**References** · Textbook pp. 67–68 · Activity Book pp. 44
**Learning objective** · Present a classification with one supporting observation and explain an example of changed feeding behaviour.
**Materials** · Feeding observations across seasons and folding poster sheets.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two diet records for the same monkey in different seasons. | Describe a change in the foods recorded. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model a labelled chart with foods grouped by season. Discuss availability as one possible explanation for a change. | Create a chart and rehearse an explanation tied to one observation. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask each pupil to submit a caption naming a group, a diet clue, and a change that can be seen in the records. | Present a chart or diagram and support the classification and change with evidence. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what more would help test an explanation for the change. | Suggest collecting records of which foods were available. |
