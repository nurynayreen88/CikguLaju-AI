# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 6 Length, Mass and Volume of Liquid

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U06-52 · Convert metres and centimetres

**Standards** · CS 6.1 · LS 6.1.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 50–51 · Activity Book pp. 107
**Learning objective** · Convert in both directions between centimetres and metres with centimetres.
**Materials** · Metre tapes, centimetre rulers and conversion cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show one metre alongside a 100 cm tape. | Read both labels and explain how they describe the same length. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model splitting 246 cm into two groups of 100 cm with 46 cm left. Reverse the process for a measurement in metres and centimetres. | Group lengths using tape marks or place-value cards and record both forms. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 307 cm and 4 m 28 cm. Check 3 m 7 cm and 428 cm. | Convert both independently and show the relationship 1 m = 100 cm. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether 3 m 7 cm is 370 cm. | Use the remaining seven centimetres to justify 307 cm. |

**Learning objective** · Combine up to three lengths or remove up to two lengths and record the answer in m and cm.
**Materials** · Ribbon strips, length cards and m/cm grids.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two joined ribbons with 75 cm and 40 cm marked. | Decide whether their combined length exceeds one metre. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model adding three lengths and regrouping every 100 cm. Then show how to exchange one metre when taking away two lengths. | Record the metres and centimetres in separate columns and label each exchange. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 2 m 75 cm + 1 m 48 cm + 3 m 12 cm, and 8 m 20 cm − 2 m 45 cm − 1 m 30 cm. Check 7 m 35 cm and 4 m 45 cm. | Calculate both and show the regrouping in one. |
