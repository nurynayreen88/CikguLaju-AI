# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 3 Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-25 · Recognise proper fractions

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.1
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 82 · Activity Book pp. 53–54
**Learning objective** · Divide shapes into equal parts and name the fraction represented in four models.
**Materials** · Paper strips of equal length, shape cards and pencils.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two rectangles cut into four parts, one with unequal parts. Ask which can show quarters. | Choose the equally divided rectangle and explain the choice. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Fold a strip into two and then four equal parts. Shade three fourths and link the top number to shaded parts and the bottom number to equal parts in the whole. | Make several equal partitions and label the shaded portions. Explain how to check that parts are equal. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give four equally divided models with 1 of 2, 2 of 3, 3 of 4 and 4 of 5 shaded. Check 1/2, 2/3, 3/4 and 4/5. | Write the four fractions individually and label the numerator and denominator in one. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show an unevenly partitioned strip again. | Explain why counting the uneven pieces alone does not give a fraction of equal parts. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-26 · Find equivalent and simplest fractions

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.2, 3.1.3
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 83–86 · Activity Book pp. 55–56
**Learning objective** · Use fraction strips to find an equivalent fraction and simplify two fractions.
**Materials** · Fraction strips for halves through tenths, squared paper and matching cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Place a half-strip above two fourths. | Compare the shaded lengths and name the amount each shows. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Use equal-length strips to show 1/2 = 2/4. Model simplifying by grouping an equal number of small parts in numerator and denominator. Ask pairs to justify one match. | Line up strips, pair forms for the same length and show that the whole stays the same. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Ask for an equivalent fraction to 2/3 with denominator 6. Give 4/8 and 6/9 to simplify. Check 4/6, 1/2 and 2/3. | Record each result independently and support one answer with a sketch or a grouping step. |
| Closure · 10 min | Show 2/5 and 4/10. | Explain why doubling both counts keeps the same shaded share. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-27 · Identify improper fractions and mixed numbers

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.7
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 87–89 · Activity Book pp. 57–58
**Learning objective** · Identify both forms in two models and explain how each shows more than or equal to one whole.
**Materials** · Paper circles of equal size, fourths and thirds strips and number cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show one full circle and one quarter of another. | Count five quarter-pieces while keeping the same size of whole. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Build 5/4 and name it as one whole and one fourth. Show that 4/4 fills exactly one whole and that 1 1/4 includes an additional quarter. | Build one model in each form and point to the complete whole and leftover equal parts. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give a model of five thirds and one of nine quarters. Check 5/3 = 1 2/3 and 9/4 = 2 1/4. Ask where 4/4 belongs. | Label both models with an improper fraction and a mixed number. In a separate model, show why 4/4 fills exactly one whole. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show 4/5 and 5/5. | State which is a proper fraction and explain which model fills one whole. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-28 · Add proper fractions

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.5
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 90–92 · Activity Book pp. 59–60
**Learning objective** · Use diagrams to add proper fractions with equal and related denominators.
**Materials** · Fraction strips for denominators 2–10, equal-length bars and task cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Place one eighth next to two eighths and ask what is being counted. | Count three pieces of one eighth each. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model 1/2 + 1/8 by expressing one half as four eighths. Use matching strips to practise 2 with 4, 6, 8, 10, 3 with 6, 9, 5 with 10 and 4 with 8. | Match equal lengths, rename fractions in the same unit and check each sum remains below one. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 1/8 + 2/8, 1/2 + 1/6, 1/3 + 2/9 and 1/5 + 3/10. Check 3/8, 2/3, 5/9 and 1/2, accepting equivalent forms. | Draw one equal-denominator and one related-denominator example, then calculate all four sums. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask why the denominator is kept once the parts have the same size. | Explain that the denominator names the size of each part. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-29 · Subtract proper fractions

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.6
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 93–95 · Activity Book pp. 61–62
**Learning objective** · Use equivalent fractions to subtract pairs of proper fractions with related denominators.
**Materials** · Fraction strips for denominators 2–10, equal-length bars and markers.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Shade 5/8 of a bar and take away 2/8. | Identify the 3/8 that remains. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model 3/4 − 2/8 by renaming three fourths as six eighths. Rotate through tasks using equal denominators and each related pair in the standard. | Represent the starting fraction, cover the amount removed and name the remaining fraction. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 5/8 − 2/8, 3/4 − 2/8, 5/6 − 1/3 and 7/10 − 2/5. Check 3/8, 1/2, 1/2 and 3/10, accepting equivalent forms. | Calculate each difference and sketch the equal parts used to find one answer. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask whether changing 3/4 to 6/8 changes the amount available. | Compare equal lengths to justify the answer. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-30 · Connect hundredths and decimals

**Standards** · CS 3.1, 3.2, 3.4 · LS 3.1.4, 3.2.1, 3.2.2, 3.4.1
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 96–98 · Activity Book pp. 63–64
**Learning objective** · Shade a hundred grid and write its value as a fraction and a decimal, then reverse the process.
**Materials** · Hundred-square grids, place-value mats and fraction and decimal cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show one shaded square in a hundred grid. | Name one hundredth and count how many squares make the whole. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model 7/100 as 0.07 and say zero point zero seven. Contrast 0.07 with 0.70 using complete rows and single squares, then reverse from a numeral to a grid. | Say and write decimals in words and numerals. Shade cards that include 0.01, 0.40 and 0.99 and match fractions of hundredths. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Show 34 shaded cells, give the numeral 0.06 and say zero point nine nine. Check 34/100 = 0.34, six shaded cells with 6/100, and 0.99 respectively. | Record the grid in fraction and decimal forms, then shade and name a decimal supplied in numeral or word form. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask which grid shows more, 0.06 or 0.60. | Point to the grid and name the number of shaded cells in each. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-31 · Compare decimals

**Standards** · CS 3.2 · LS 3.2.3
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 99 · Activity Book pp. 65
**Learning objective** · Use a number line or grid to compare pairs of decimals to two places.
**Materials** · Paired hundred grids, decimal cards and a 0–1 number line.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 0.4 and 0.39 and ask which reaches farther along a number line. | Locate or estimate both positions before comparing. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Shade 0.40 and 0.39 on equal grids. Model checking tenths first and then hundredths when needed. Include an equality such as 0.5 = 0.50. | Compare pairs with both tools and use the number of shaded squares to support one answer. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 0.40 and 0.39, 0.07 and 0.70, and 0.5 and 0.50. Check >, < and = respectively. | Record all three symbols and justify one with a grid or number line. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether more decimal digits always mean a greater value. | Use one of the comparisons to explain why place value matters. |

## DLP-MAT-Y3-U03-32 · Add and subtract decimals

**Standards** · CS 3.2 · LS 3.2.4, 3.2.5
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 100–103 · Activity Book pp. 66–67
**Learning objective** · Solve one addition and one subtraction with values up to 0.99 and check place-value alignment.
**Materials** · Tenths and hundredths mats, hundred grids and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show 0.4 and 0.07 on a place-value mat. | Place the 4 and 7 in their correct columns. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model 0.27 + 0.36 by exchanging ten hundredths for one tenth. Then model 0.72 − 0.38 by exchanging one tenth for ten hundredths. | Align decimal points and check a partner's addition or subtraction using the mat before recording the result. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 0.47 + 0.28 and 0.80 − 0.37. Check 0.75 and 0.43 plus correct alignment and one inverse check. | Solve both problems independently and verify one with the opposite operation. |
| Closure · 10 min | Show an answer that aligns 0.4 and 0.07 by their last digits. | Rewrite 0.4 as 0.40 and align tenths and hundredths correctly. |

**Learning objective** · Read and write four percentages using %, then interpret and shade a hundred grid.
**Materials** · Hundred grids, percentage cards and coloured pencils.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a grid with one cell shaded and one fully shaded. | Count the shaded cells and describe the two cases. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model reading and writing 1%, 24%, 70% and 100% and link the percent sign to a share out of one hundred. Practise moving from numeral to grid and back. | Read the cards aloud, record the percent symbol and make matching grids. Describe how many cells remain unshaded. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask each pupil to read and write 1%, 9%, 36% and 100%, shade 36 cells and write the percentage for 72 shaded cells. Check 72%. | Write and say all four percentages, shade the requested grid and identify the percentage represented by the second grid. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what 100% looks like on a grid. | Point to all 100 shaded cells and read the percentage aloud. |

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 48 shaded cells with the label 0.048. | Identify how many hundredths are shaded. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Link 48/100, 0.48 and 48% to the model. Guide pupils to correct a decimal sum using aligned place values. | Write equivalent forms, compare solutions in pairs and explain one correction. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Show 62 shaded cells and the incorrect calculation 0.52 − 0.18 = 0.46. Check 62/100 = 0.62 = 62% and the corrected difference 0.34. | Write all three forms and correct the subtraction independently, showing the place-value exchange. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask which number in 0.34 shows the hundredths. | Name the 4 and explain how it appears on the grid. |
