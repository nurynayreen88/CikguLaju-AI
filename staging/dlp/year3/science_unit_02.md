# Science (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 2 Science Room Rules

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U02-09 · Follow science room rules

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1
**References** · Textbook pp. 24–26 · Activity Book pp. 15–16
**Learning objective** · Demonstrate one rule before, during and after a science room activity and explain one consequence.
**Materials** · Plastic beakers, paper towels and three picture cards for room rules.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Show a crowded doorway and ask how to enter safely. | Demonstrate lining up before entering. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Supervise a short water-transfer activity. Rehearse carrying a beaker carefully, reporting a spill and tidying the station. | Enter in order, handle equipment safely and return it after use. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Use a checklist for each pupil's conduct before, during and after. Ask what could happen if one demonstrated rule is ignored. | Demonstrate one rule at each stage and explain one consequence. |
| Closure · 3 min | Show a picture of a spill beside a walkway. | Give the first appropriate action according to the room rules. |
