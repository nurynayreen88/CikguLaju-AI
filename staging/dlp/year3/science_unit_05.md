# Science (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 5 Plants

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U05-25 · Identify ways plants reproduce

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.1
**References** · Textbook pp. 72–73 · Activity Book pp. 45–46
**Learning objective** · Give one plant example for each of the six reproduction methods and explain one pairing.
**Materials** · Plant photos and method cards for seeds, spores, cuttings, leaves, suckers and underground stems.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show chilli seeds and the underside of a fern frond. | Describe a visible difference. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Demonstrate how textbook examples link one plant to each method. Let pairs inspect labelled specimens or photographs. | Match chilli, fern, hibiscus, Bryophyllum, banana and ginger to the appropriate examples. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's six matches and ask them to explain one match using a visible or given clue. | Match one plant to every method and describe the clue for one pairing. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show a labelled ginger rhizome. | Identify an underground stem as the reproduction method shown. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U05-26 · Explain why reproduction matters

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.2
**References** · Textbook pp. 74–75 · Activity Book pp. 47–48
**Learning objective** · Draw a cause-and-effect diagram linking new plants to food, habitats or the survival of a species.
**Materials** · Plant and animal pictures, food and habitat cards and diagram sheets.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a fruit tree with birds feeding and nesting. | Identify two ways the birds use the tree. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model a chain from seed to new plant to food for an animal. Let pairs make a different chain linking plants to habitats or continued survival. | Create and explain a chain using three labelled boxes and two directional arrows. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's new chain for a new plant, a living thing helped by it and a clear reason. | Draw and explain one complete cause-and-effect chain. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what an empty middle box would leave unexplained. | Name the link between reproduction and the benefit. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U05-27 · Investigate more than one propagation method

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.3
**References** · Textbook pp. 76 · Activity Book pp. 49–50
**Learning objective** · Set up two teacher-approved methods for one suitable plant and start a dated observation log.
**Materials** · Water spinach seeds and cuttings, two pots, labels, soil and a dated log.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a packet of water spinach seeds and a stem cutting. | Suggest how both could be used to grow the same kind of plant. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Demonstrate sowing seeds and setting cuttings in separate labelled pots. Agree on regular light, watering and observation times. | Set up both pots, record the starting date and sketch the starting condition of each. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Inspect each pupil's setup notes for the two methods, accurate labels and one stated common growing condition. | Explain both methods and enter two baseline records in a dated log. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what would count as evidence of new growth. | Name a visible sign to look for and record later. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U05-28 · Report the plant project

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.3, 5.1.4
**References** · Textbook pp. 76–77 · Activity Book pp. 50–52
**Learning objective** · Compare dated records from both propagation methods and support one conclusion using class records or mature samples.
**Materials** · Dated water spinach logs, teacher-grown reserve samples and reporting sheets.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show an early log entry and a later entry for one pot. | Identify evidence of change over time. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Compare dated observations for seeds and cuttings. Provide reserve samples from the same species if class samples have yet to grow. | Use the logs or dated reserve records to make a comparison. Mark which entries came from class or teacher samples. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's written or illustrated report for evidence from both methods and a conclusion supported by the dates. | Report one sign of growth for each method and explain what those records show. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how pupils should report a pot with no visible growth yet. | Record the result as of the observation date. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils met the learning objective. Follow-up: ____________________.
