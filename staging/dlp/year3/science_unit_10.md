# Science (DLP) · Year 3 SK · Unit 10 Machine

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U10-49 · Identify a pulley and its purpose

**Standards** · CS 10.1 · LS 10.1.1
**References** · Textbook pp. 128 · Activity Book pp. 85
**Learning objective** · Label the wheel and rope of a pulley and describe what the device helps people do.
**Materials** · Fixed pulley model, cord, light load and a simple sketch.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 4 min | Show a flag being raised and ask how the rope moves it. | Describe what they see in the flagpole example. |
| Main activity · 13 min | Show the grooved wheel and cord on a small model. Demonstrate raising the load and let pairs sketch the parts. | Point out the wheel and rope and describe the load movement. |
| Practice and assessment · 10 min | Check each pupil's diagram for both labels and one correct use statement. | Label the pulley and describe how it helps lift an object. |
| Closure · 3 min | Show the model unthreaded and ask what is missing. | Identify the rope and the groove it follows. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U10-50 · Explain a fixed pulley

**Standards** · CS 10.1 · LS 10.1.2
**References** · Textbook pp. 129–130 · Activity Book pp. 86
**Learning objective** · Use arrows to show the direction of pull and the direction of load movement on a model.
**Materials** · Stable fixed-pulley stand, cord, light blocks and direction arrows.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Hold up a load and ask which way to pull the free end to raise it. | Predict the direction of the pull. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Secure the model and show that a downward pull raises the load. Have pairs repeat the motion while observing the wheel axle. | Operate the model using a light load, observe that the axle stays in place and place the arrows. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's demonstration or diagram for arrows showing a downward pull and an upward load movement. | Label the fixed position and use arrows to explain the change in direction. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask which part stays attached to the stand. | Point to the fixed axle or support. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U10-51 · Find pulley applications

**Standards** · CS 10.1 · LS 10.1.3
**References** · Textbook pp. 131–132 · Activity Book pp. 87–89
**Learning objective** · Identify three uses of pulleys and explain the benefit in one case.
**Materials** · Photos of a flagpole, well and crane and application cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a flagpole rope and ask where the pulley sits. | Point out the likely position at the top. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Compare the three photos. Ask pairs to trace what the rope moves and identify its benefit in each example. | Match each photo to its task and mark the load path. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check each pupil's three examples and one explanation tied to the motion observed. | Name three applications and explain how the pulley helps in one. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show a lift with a different arrangement. | Identify the object being moved and suggest where the pulley helps. |

## DLP-SCI-Y3-U10-52 · Build and explain a pulley model

**Standards** · CS 10.1 · LS 10.1.4, 10.1.5
**References** · Textbook pp. 133–135 · Activity Book pp. 90–92
**Learning objective** · Raise a small test load with a working model and use a labelled sketch to explain its operation.
**Materials** · Teacher-secured stands, small pulleys, cord, light loads and design sheets.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a small load below a stand. Ask how to raise it using a pulley. | Sketch a starting idea for the rope path. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Check that stands are secured and loads are light. Guide pairs to route the cord through the groove, test the lift and adjust any slipping cord. | Build the model, test it twice and label the wheel, cord, load and direction arrows. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Watch each pair lift the load. Check each pupil's own labelled sketch and explanation of how the pull raises it. | Demonstrate the lift and use a labelled sketch to explain how the model works. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask pupils to name a change that improved their model. | Explain how the change helped the cord or load move smoothly. |
