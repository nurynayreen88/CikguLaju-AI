## DLP-MAT-Y4-L49 · Lesson 49 · Recognise length units and measure in millimetres

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  1-5 Aug · B  2-6 Aug

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.1, 5.1.4

**References** · Textbook pp. 171-173

**Learning objective** · Select a suitable length unit for two contexts and measure three small items with a ruler.

**Materials** · Millimetre rulers, small cards, paper clips and erasers

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a paper clip and a classroom wall. | Choose sensible units for their lengths. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model aligning a small object with zero and reading millimetre divisions from above. | Measure three small items and record each reading and unit. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Check a suitable unit for a pencil and school corridor and observe one measurement from each pupil. | Select centimetres or millimetres for the pencil and metres for the corridor, then record three measured lengths in mm. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why starting at the ruler's edge may give an incorrect reading. | Point to the actual zero mark. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Repeat with a small object starting at a marked non-zero value and calculate the difference.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L50 · Lesson 50 · Relate and convert length units

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  1-5 Aug · B  2-6 Aug

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.2, 5.1.3

**References** · Textbook pp. 174-175

**Learning objective** · Convert between millimetres and centimetres and between metres and kilometres, explaining the conversion factor.

**Materials** · Metric rulers, conversion mat, matching cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show 10 mm on a ruler and ask how many centimetres it covers. | Identify 1 cm. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Use the ruler and a map legend to relate mm to cm and m to km. Model both conversion directions. | Convert mixed and single units and explain when to multiply or divide. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 37 mm and 2,350 m and ask for each in a suitable mixed form. | Record 3 cm 7 mm and 2 km 350 m and name the conversion factors. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask how to turn 3 cm 7 mm back into millimetres. | Multiply the centimetres by ten and add the remaining millimetres. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Check conversions in both directions before combining units.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L51 · Lesson 51 · Estimate a distance in kilometres

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  8-12 Aug · B  9-13 Aug

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.5

**References** · Textbook p. 176

**Learning objective** · Use a known distance to estimate a second distance and justify the scale of the estimate.

**Materials** · Simple neighbourhood map with a measured 1 km reference, route cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show one school-to-park route of 1 km. | Use its length as a reference for nearby routes. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model laying off the reference length along a second route and include an approximate partial length. | Compare two routes and explain why estimates need a scale or known distance. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Provide a route about two and a half times the 1 km reference. | Estimate about 2.5 km and justify by counting reference lengths. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask when straight-line distance may differ from a walking route. | Point to bends and route choices on the map. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Re-estimate a new route using a different known distance.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L52 · Lesson 52 · Add and subtract lengths

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.6, 5.1.7

**References** · Textbook pp. 177-180

**Learning objective** · Add or subtract mixed units and show any regrouping needed.

**Materials** · Marked measuring tape, mixed-length cards, exchange slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show lengths 2 m 75 cm and 1 m 80 cm. | Predict whether the total exceeds 4 m. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model adding centimetres and exchanging 100 cm for 1 m. Model borrowing a metre in a separate subtraction. | Use drawings or conversions to solve two mixed-unit operations. |

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L56 · Lesson 56 · Solve a measurement problem

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  22-26 Aug · B  23-27 Aug

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 5.4 · LS 5.4.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 192-196

**Learning objective** · Choose suitable units, calculate the required measurement and check whether it fits the situation.

**Materials** · Fictional picnic drink cards, capacity bars and check slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show that twenty cups require the same volume each. | Suggest using multiplication and choose millilitres. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Write the total capacity needed, compare the available stock, then calculate a remaining amount. | Draw a labelled bar and verify the answer using a rough estimate. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give 20 cups of 180 mL and 4 L of drink. Ask whether it is enough. | Calculate 3,600 mL needed and 400 mL left, then explain why the result fits the context. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask how using 200 mL per cup helps estimate the answer. | Explain that 20 × 200 mL is exactly 4 L, so 180 mL cups leave some drink. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Recheck the conversion before comparing quantities.
