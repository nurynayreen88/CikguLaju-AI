## DLP-MAT-Y4-L32 · Lesson 32 · Add and subtract amounts of money

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  2-6 May · B  3-7 May

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 3.1 · LS 3.1.1, 3.1.2

**References** · Textbook pp. 105-109

**Learning objective** · Calculate the total and change in RM and sen with the correct alignment.

**Materials** · Sample prices, mock RM/sen notes, receipt slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show RM8.05 and ask what 05 represents. | Identify five sen. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model adding prices and subtracting a total from an amount paid. Align ringgit and sen. | Role-play two purchases and prepare mock receipts with working. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Give prices RM18.75, RM6.80 and RM13.40, paid with RM50. | Find RM38.95 total and RM11.05 change and check the units. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask why change by addition gives another check. | Show that total plus change returns the paid amount. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Practise regrouping one ringgit into 100 sen.

| --- | --- | --- |

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L36 · Lesson 36 · Plan a budget for a savings target

**Standards** · CS 3.3 · LS 3.3.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 118-119

**Learning objective** · Prepare daily, weekly and monthly budgets for a short-term target and explain an adjustment.

**Materials** · Sample weekly allowance, budget tables, saving goal cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a RM24 goal and ask what amount is available to save each day. | Suggest comparing allowance and spending. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model building a daily budget and extending it to a five-day week and a sample month of four weeks. | Plan costs and savings and check that the spending fits the assumed allowance. |

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L38 · Lesson 38 · Compare financial choices

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 3.4 · LS 3.4.1, 3.4.2, 3.4.3

**References** · Textbook pp. 122-123

**Learning objective** · Prioritise a need, compare two sources of information and explain the likely effect of a choice.

**Materials** · Fictional product labels, price list and advertisement cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show a school notebook and decorative stickers with only enough money for one. | Explain which is the immediate need and why. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Compare a dated price list with an advertisement. Discuss availability and which source shows the exact price. | Use both cards to prioritise a purchase and explain an effect on the remaining budget. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Check whether a choice is supported by the actual budget and source details.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-R03 · Review 03 · Money Review

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · Review LS 3.1.1, 3.1.2, 3.4.2

**References** · Textbook p. 134

**Learning objective** · Compare two purchase plans and verify one running balance against a budget.

**Materials** · Two fictional shopping lists, budgeting slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two purchase plans with a common RM30 budget. | Predict which plan leaves more money. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Have pupils work out both totals and verify one remaining balance by addition. | Compare their totals and discuss if each plan stays within the budget. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Use plan A RM18.95 + RM6.40 and plan B RM14.80 + RM12.35. | Find RM25.35 and RM27.15 and check plan A using RM4.65 change. |
