## DLP-MAT-Y4-L20 · Lesson 20 · Convert improper fractions and mixed numbers

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  21-25 Mar · B  22-26 Mar

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 67-70

**Learning objective** · Use a bar model to justify a conversion in both directions.

**Materials** · Fraction bars split into quarters and fifths, answer slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show nine quarters as equal pieces. | Group four pieces into each whole. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model grouping eleven fourths as two wholes and three fourths. Reverse the grouping to convert back. | Build a bar for seven fifths and explain both forms. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Assess conversions of 11/4 and 3 2/5 in opposite directions. | Draw the bars, show 2 3/4 and 17/5 and explain the grouping. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what stays the same in a conversion. | State that the value and piece size remain equal. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Practise grouping equal parts into complete wholes.
