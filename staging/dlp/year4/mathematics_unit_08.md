# Mathematics DLP Year 4 SK 2027

Unit 8 Data Handling

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L67 · Lesson 67 · Construct a pictograph and bar chart

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  10-14 Oct · B  11-15 Oct

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 8.1 · LS 8.1.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 233-235

**Learning objective** · Use the same ungrouped data to draw two displays with a title, key or scale and accurate labels.

**Materials** · Tally cards, graph paper, pictograph key cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show four categories with ungrouped responses. | Suggest tallying each response once. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model a pictograph with one symbol for two votes and a matching bar chart with an even-numbered scale. | Tally the data and construct both displays with titles, labels and a key or scale. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Provide cycling 8, walking 6, bus 4 and car 2. Check all four counts on both displays. | Draw both displays and verify that each represents a total of 20 votes. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask how to show eight votes in the pictograph. | Use four symbols according to the key. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Check for consistent scales and complete labels.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L68 · Lesson 68 · Interpret charts built in class

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  17-21 Oct · B  18-22 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 8.1 · LS 8.1.2

**References** · Textbook pp. 236-238

**Learning objective** · Answer comparison and total questions and use the key or scale to justify each answer.

**Materials** · Class pictograph and bar chart, comparison slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a bar and ask how to read its actual value. | Read the scale rather than counting grid lines without units. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model matching a category across the two displays and calculating a difference and a total. | Answer paired questions and cite the scale or key for one response. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Use the figures 8 cycling, 6 walking, 4 bus and 2 car. Ask for the highest pairwise difference and the total. | Find a difference of 6 between cycling and car, and a total of 20, showing the evidence. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why four symbols may represent eight pupils. | Use the one-symbol-for-two key. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Repeat a reading question using a scale that starts at zero.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L69 · Lesson 69 · Solve a problem with data

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  17-21 Oct · B  18-22 Oct

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 8.2 · LS 8.2.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 239-241

**Learning objective** · Select a chart, extract two values and justify a decision based on the findings.

**Materials** · Two chart cards, fictional club-planning scenario

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show two displays and ask which would support a quick comparison between clubs. | Select a readable chart and explain what it displays. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Model extracting two values and linking their difference to a simple decision. | Compare proposals using the same data and justify a choice in pairs. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Provide the bar-chart counts of 12 for sports, 8 for art and 6 for games. Ask which club gets the largest room if attendance decides. | Choose sports, cite 12 versus 8 and explain that sports has four more votes than the next club. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask whether a graph title gives enough evidence for the choice. | Explain why the decision needs the category values. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Practise supporting a decision with two clearly identified values.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-R08 · Review 08 · Data Handling Review

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  17-21 Oct · B  18-22 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · Review LS 8.1.2, 8.2.1

**References** · Textbook p. 242

**Learning objective** · Compare two displays of the same data and justify a conclusion using the scale or key.

**Materials** · Matching bar chart and pictograph, independent slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two representations of the same survey. | Identify one way to verify that the counts match. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Review reading a key and a numerical scale. Ask pupils to compare one value across displays and draw a conclusion. | Extract matching counts and calculate a comparison independently. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Provide cycling 8, walking 6 and bus 4, with two votes per pictograph symbol. | Explain that cycling exceeds bus by four votes and verify both values in both displays. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how a wrong scale could change the conclusion. | Show how the same bar could be misread if the intervals are ignored. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Revisit a display for which the pupil used an incorrect key or scale.
