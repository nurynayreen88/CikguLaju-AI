Unit 7 Coordinates, Ratio, and Proportion

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L63 · Lesson 63 · Read and plot coordinates

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  26-30 Sep · B  27 Sep-1 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 7.1 · LS 7.1.1, 7.1.2

**References** · Textbook pp. 215-217

**Learning objective** · Label both axes and origin, then plot and read three first-quadrant coordinates in the correct order.

**Materials** · First-quadrant grids, map cards and rulers

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a grid with no labels and ask where its two axes meet. | Point to the origin. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Label the horizontal x-axis and vertical y-axis, then model moving across before up. | Plot coordinates and read them back in x, y order. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Provide (2, 4) and (5, 1) to plot and an existing point at (3, 2) to read. | Label the axes and origin and record all three locations independently. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether (2, 4) and (4, 2) refer to the same point. | Plot both and describe the difference. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Reinforce moving horizontally before moving vertically.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L64 · Lesson 64 · Represent a relationship as a ratio

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  26-30 Sep · B  27 Sep-1 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 7.2 · LS 7.2.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 218-221

**Learning objective** · Represent comparable quantities as ratios and interpret one scale ratio of 1:100 or 1:1,000.

**Materials** · Coloured counters, scale cards, ruler and ratio slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show one red counter and five blue counters. | State the relationship from red to blue. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model writing a ratio of one to several in the named order. Group 2 red and 10 blue counters into equal groups to show 1:5, then model a scale of 1:100. | Represent two comparisons as ratios and interpret a simple plan distance. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask for the ratio of red to blue for two red and ten blue counters and the real distance represented by 3 cm at 1:100. | Write 1:5 with a grouping explanation and find 300 cm or 3 m for the distance. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why a scale ratio needs the same unit on both sides before conversion. | Explain matching centimetres to centimetres. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Practise naming each quantity before writing a ratio.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L65 · Lesson 65 · Solve a proportion using the unitary method

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  3-7 Oct · B  4-8 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · CS 7.3 · LS 7.3.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 222-225

**Learning objective** · Find the value of one unit first and use it to find an unknown quantity.

**Materials** · Unitary-method table and fictional shopping cards

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show that three plain folders cost RM12. | Suggest finding the cost of one item first. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model dividing to get a one-unit cost and then multiplying for the required quantity. | Write a quantity-cost table and label the one-unit row. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Ask what seven folders cost if three cost RM12 at the same rate. | Find RM4 per folder and RM28 for seven, and show both steps. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how to check the answer using the per-folder cost. | Divide RM28 by seven and recover RM4. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Revisit which operation finds one unit and which scales it.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-L66 · Lesson 66 · Solve a coordinate or proportion problem

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  3-7 Oct · B  4-8 Oct

**Duration** · 90 min

**Standards** · CS 7.4 · LS 7.4.1

**References** · Textbook pp. 226-229

**Learning objective** · Use a grid or ratio table to represent a daily situation and check the solution.

**Materials** · Classroom coordinates and fictional recipe ratio table

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 10 min | Show a point (3, 5) and a recipe quantity for four portions. | Identify one representation useful for each situation. |
| Main activity · 45 min | Use a grid for the location and a unitary or ratio table for scaling a recipe. Model checking the order of coordinates and the scale factor. | Solve a short grid task and a short proportional recipe task before checking. |
| Practice and assessment · 25 min | Ask pupils to plot (4, 2) and find the flour for six portions if four use 240 g. | Mark the coordinate correctly and find 360 g, explaining the one-portion amount of 60 g. |
| Closure · 10 min | Ask what quantity stays constant when the recipe is scaled. | Identify flour per portion. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Give a second example with a scale-up and a scale-down check.

## DLP-MAT-Y4-R07 · Review 07 · Coordinates, Ratio, and Proportion Review

**RPT 2027 dates** · A  10-14 Oct · B  11-15 Oct

**Duration** · 60 min

**Standards** · Review LS 7.1.2, 7.3.1

**References** · Textbook p. 231

**Learning objective** · Plot two locations and solve a proportion by finding the value of one unit.

**Materials** · First-quadrant grid, unitary-method slips

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a map coordinate and ask which axis is read first. | Identify x as the first coordinate. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Set two marked locations and one proportion problem to check independently. | Plot the points and use a one-unit row to solve the proportion. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give (1, 3), (4, 2) and three markers costing RM9. Ask for five markers at the same rate. | Plot both points and find RM15 by first finding RM3 for one marker. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether either coordinate can be read in reverse order. | Use the plotted points to show the difference. |

**Reflection** · ___ / ___ pupils met the objective. Give targeted practice on either axis order or finding one unit.
