# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 2 SK · Unit 2 Basic Operations · Subtraction

Three 60-minute RPH for CS 2.2. See the [source mapping](../../../audits/dlp_sk_sources_20261114.md#year-2-unit-2-source-check).

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-06 · Subtract Without Regrouping

**Standards** · CS 2.2 · LS 2.2.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 62–65 · Activity Book pp. 42–43  
**Learning objective** · Find at least three of four differences without regrouping and represent one as backward jumps on a number line.  
**Materials** · Open number lines, base-ten blocks and place-value mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Put down 346 with blocks and remove two ten rods. Ask which place changes. | Name the tens place and say the number left. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 568 − 34 using jumps of 30 and 4, then record the same difference in columns. | Solve pairs of examples with a partner. Match each jump to the place being subtracted. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 684 − 23, 507 − 4, 796 − 352 and 950 − 120. Check the differences and one accurate number line. | Independently find all four differences and draw a labelled number line for one. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where to write the 6 in 769 − 6. | Point to the ones column and give the difference. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found at least three differences and showed the jumps correctly. Revisit alignment with a labelled mat.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-07 · Subtract With Regrouping

**Standards** · CS 2.2 · LS 2.2.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 66–72 · Activity Book pp. 44–48  
**Learning objective** · Find at least three of four differences involving regrouping and explain one exchange across place-value columns.  
**Materials** · Base-ten blocks, place-value mats and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show four tens and two ones. Ask how to remove seven ones. | Exchange one ten for ten ones and show the available ones. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 432 − 157 using two exchanges. For 503 − 76, show how to regroup through the zero tens before writing the column calculation. | Build the starting amount, show each exchange and match it to the written changes. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 642 − 25, 851 − 380, 602 − 147 and 500 − 36. Check three or more correct differences and one accurate explanation. | Independently solve all four and explain one exchange using a drawing or words. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show an exchange where the original column count was left unchanged. Ask for a correction. | Correct the count and explain how the exchange preserves the amount. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils met both checks. Practise regrouping through a zero with pupils who skipped the intermediate column.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-08 · Subtract Successively

**Standards** · CS 2.2 · LS 2.2.2  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 73–75 · Activity Book pp. 49–51  
**Learning objective** · Find at least three of four differences after two successive subtractions, showing both steps for each correct answer.  
**Materials** · Situation cards, open number lines and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Start with 150 tokens on a card. Say that 20 and then 30 are used. Ask for the amount after the first change. | Give 130 and explain which amount should be used for the next step. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 485 − 126 − 94. Write the first difference and use it as the starting value for the second subtraction. | Solve a partner's two-step card and explain which value is the intermediate difference. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 650 − 120 − 55, 724 − 215 − 98, 903 − 175 − 208 and 410 − 109 − 87. Check each intermediate and final difference. | Independently show both steps for all four expressions. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why starting again at the original number gives the wrong final amount. | Point to the intermediate difference and explain the two changes. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils showed both correct steps on at least three items. Provide two labelled spaces for pupils who omitted a step.
