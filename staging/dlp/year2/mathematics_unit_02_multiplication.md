# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 2 SK · Unit 2 Basic Operations · Multiplication

Four 60-minute RPH for CS 2.3. See the [source mapping](../../../audits/dlp_sk_sources_20261114.md#year-2-unit-2-source-check).

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-09 · Build Equal Groups and Arrays

**Standards** · CS 2.3 · LS 2.3.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 81–83 · Activity Book pp. 56–58  
**Learning objective** · Represent at least three of four equal-group situations with matching repeated addition, multiplication and totals.  
**Materials** · Counters, paper plates and square grid cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Put four counters on each of three plates. Ask which number tells how many plates and which tells how many per plate. | Count the plates and describe the equal groups. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model three groups of four as 4 + 4 + 4 and 3 × 4. Rearrange the counters into an array and turn it to compare 3 × 4 and 4 × 3. | Build equal groups, record matching sentences and explain how turning an array keeps the same total. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give cards for 2 groups of 6, 4 groups of 3, 5 groups of 4 and 3 groups of 7. Check the drawing, both sentences and total. | Independently draw and label each group model, then write repeated addition and multiplication for each. |
| Closure · 5 min | Show groups with different sizes and ask what must change before one multiplication fact can represent them. | Make the groups equal and name the two factors. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils matched all representations for at least three cards. Use plates to clarify the roles of group count and group size.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-10 · Build Facts for 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6

**Standards** · CS 2.3 · LS 2.3.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 84–86 · Activity Book pp. 59–63  
**Learning objective** · Find at least five of six multiplication facts from the 2 to 6 times tables, and explain one using a known fact.  
**Materials** · Grid cards, counters and blank fact cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show six pairs of counters. Ask how counting in twos finds the total. | Count in twos and record 6 × 2. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Join two two-column arrays to build a four-column array. Repeat with two three-column arrays for six. Practise facts for five with equal rows. | Build arrays for the 2 to 6 times tables. Use a known product to find a related product in the four or six times table. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 7 × 2, 5 × 3, 6 × 4, 8 × 5, 4 × 6 and 6 × 6. Check five or more products and one valid link between facts. | Independently find each product and show how one known fact helped. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask for a quicker way to find 7 × 6 using 7 × 3. | Double 21 and explain why this gives 42. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found at least five products and explained one strategy. Rebuild the fact family that caused errors.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-11 · Build Facts for 7, 8 and 9

**Standards** · CS 2.3 · LS 2.3.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 87–90 · Activity Book pp. 64–67  
**Learning objective** · Find at least five of six facts from the 7, 8 and 9 times tables, and show one product by splitting an array or using a neighbouring fact.  
**Materials** · Grid paper, counters and highlighters.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 5 × 8 as two 5 × 4 rectangles. Ask why the products add. | Count both rectangles and name the full product. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 6 × 7 as 6 × 5 plus 6 × 2. Show how an extra row of nine turns 4 × 9 into 5 × 9. | Split or extend arrays and explain the link to the fact being solved. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 2 × 7, 5 × 7, 3 × 8, 8 × 8, 6 × 9 and 7 × 9. Check the six products and one model with matching calculations. | Independently calculate each product and annotate one array or neighbouring fact. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what happens to the product when one more group of seven is added. | Say it increases by seven and show the extra row. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found at least five products and justified one. Revisit the group-size increment with pupils who added only one.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-12 · Multiply by 0, 1 and 10

**Standards** · CS 2.3 · LS 2.3.1, 2.3.2  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 91–93 · Activity Book pp. 68–70  
**Learning objective** · Find at least five of six products by 0, 1 or 10, with at least one correct product for each factor, and explain a product by ten.  
**Materials** · Empty cups, counters, ten rods and place-value mats.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show six empty cups and six cups with one counter each. Ask what the two totals are. | Count zero and six, then link each total to its groups. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Contrast six groups of zero, one and ten. Build 6 × 10 with six ten rods and show why the total is 60. | Build and record matching facts for the three cases, then explain how each factor affects the total. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 7 × 0, 0 × 4, 9 × 1, 1 × 3, 4 × 10 and 8 × 10. Check all three cases and one explanation in tens. | Independently solve all six and show one product by ten using rods or a drawing. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why 5 × 10 is 50. | Explain that five groups of ten make five tens. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils met the product and explanation checks. Rebuild the factor cases pupils confused.
