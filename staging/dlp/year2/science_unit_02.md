## DLP-SCI-Y2-U02-01 · Follow Science Room Rules

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1  
**References** · Textbook pp. 18–20 · Activity Book pp. 11–12  
**Learning objective** · Follow the agreed entry, incident-reporting and clean-up routines during a practical activity, and explain how one rule prevents harm.  
**Materials** · Plastic cups, plastic spoons, water, trays, cleaning cloths, incident picture cards and a pupil checklist. Use a picture to simulate a spill or damaged item.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Mark the bag area and show how to keep the walkway clear. Record each pupil's entry action on the checklist. | Leave bags in the agreed area, walk to the assigned station and wait for instructions. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Demonstrate transferring a spoonful of water between cups over a tray. Show an incident card to one group at a time and observe how each pupil reports it. | Take turns moving water over the tray. When shown an incident card, stop, leave the affected item alone and tell the teacher where it is and what happened. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Observe each pupil clean and return one item. Use three checklist columns for entry, reporting and clean-up. Recheck an action after a short prompt where needed. | Clean and return assigned equipment independently. Explain to the teacher or write how a clear walkway or immediate reporting keeps people safe. |
| Closure · 5 min | Review the response to a damaged item and ask which action to take first. | State the first action. Use Activity Book pp. 11–12 for follow-up if available. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils followed all three routines independently and explained one safety reason. Practise the missed routine with pupils who needed a prompt before the next practical activity.
