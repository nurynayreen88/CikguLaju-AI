# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 2 SK · Unit 2 Basic Operations · Addition

These five 60-minute RPH cover CS 2.1. The codes identify staging records. See the [source mapping](../../../audits/dlp_sk_sources_20261114.md#year-2-unit-2-source-check).

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-01 · Add Ones, Tens and Hundreds

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 p. 46 · Activity Book p. 31  
**Learning objective** · Find at least three of four sums by adding ones, tens or hundreds, and label one jump correctly on a number line.  
**Materials** · Open number lines, numeral cards and base-ten blocks.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 231 and offer a unit cube, a ten rod and a hundred flat. Ask how much each would add. | Match each block to a jump of 1, 10 or 100. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model how 231 changes when 5, 40 or 200 is added separately. Show how a long jump can represent several equal small jumps. | Represent each addend with blocks, then draw a jump and write the new total. Swap start cards with a partner. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 304 + 5, 304 + 60, 304 + 200 and 421 + 30. Check the sums and the direction and size of one labelled jump. | Independently find the four totals and draw a labelled jump for one. Use Activity Book p. 31 for further practice if available. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask which digit changes when 40 is added to 212. | Name the tens digit and give 252 as the total. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found three or more totals and labelled one correct jump. Revisit the value of a jump with pupils who moved by the wrong amount.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-02 · Add Two Numbers Without Regrouping

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 47–48 · Activity Book pp. 32–34  
**Learning objective** · Align digits by place value and find at least three of four sums without regrouping.  
**Materials** · Hundreds-tens-ones mats, base-ten blocks, mini-whiteboards and pencils.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Place 54 below 312 with 5 in the hundreds column. Ask pupils to move the digits to their correct places. | Put 5 under the tens and 4 under the ones. Explain why. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 312 + 54 on the mat. Transfer the model to vertical form, then show a second example with a zero in the tens place. | Build and add two numbers on the mat. Record each sum in columns and check the column headings before adding. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 241 + 36, 405 + 73, 133 + 246 and 510 + 208. Check both alignment and the sum for each item. | Independently write and solve all four in columns. Use Activity Book pp. 32–34 for follow-up if available. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where to write the 7 when 7 is added to 640. | Place 7 in the ones column and give the total. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils aligned and solved at least three items correctly. Give pupils who misaligned digits a labelled place-value mat for the next task.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-03 · Add Two Numbers With Regrouping

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 51–55 · Activity Book pp. 35–37  
**Learning objective** · Solve at least three of four additions that require regrouping, and explain one exchange using place-value language.  
**Materials** · Base-ten blocks, a place-value mat and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Put eight and five unit cubes in the ones column. Ask pupils to show the same quantity using fewer blocks. | Exchange ten cubes for one ten rod and identify the three remaining ones. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 148 + 75 with blocks. Record each exchange above the receiving column in vertical form. Then compare with a tens-to-hundreds example. | Build and solve examples with a partner. Point to each exchange on the mat and show where it appears in the written calculation. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 246 + 17, 365 + 72, 287 + 156 and 506 + 194. Check the totals and ask pupils to explain one exchange. | Independently solve all four sums and explain one exchange with blocks, a drawing or a sentence. Use Activity Book pp. 35–37 if available. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask what happens if a ten rod is formed but left in the ones column. | Move the rod to tens and explain why the total would otherwise be wrong. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils solved at least three and explained one exchange. Revisit the specific column where each pupil lost a regrouped unit.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-04 · Add Two Numbers Near 1,000

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.1  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 56–57 · Activity Book p. 38 (section reference printed on p. 56)  
**Learning objective** · Find three exact totals within 1,000, including one equal to 1,000, and identify which of the other two totals is closer to 1,000.  
**Materials** · Place-value mats with a thousands column, base-ten blocks and numeral cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show nine hundred flats and ask what one more flat makes. | Exchange ten hundreds for one thousand and name the result. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model 476 + 524 and show how the exchanges produce one thousand. Offer two sums just below 1,000 and compare the completed totals. | Add the pairs on the mat, record the totals and say which is closer to 1,000. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 420 + 569, 486 + 512 and 735 + 265. Check the three sums and ask which of the two totals below 1,000 is closer to it. | Independently calculate all three sums. Show the last exchange that makes 1,000 and compare the other totals by their distance from 1,000. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where the new 1 belongs when ten hundreds are formed. | Point to thousands and explain why each remaining column shows zero. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found all three totals and identified the nearer of the two totals below 1,000. Practise the hundreds-to-thousands exchange where needed.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U02-05 · Add Three Numbers Within 1,000

**Standards** · CS 2.1 · LS 2.1.2  
**References** · Textbook Part 1 pp. 58–61 · Activity Book pp. 39–41  
**Learning objective** · Solve at least three of four three-addend sums and explain one useful choice of which two numbers to add first.  
**Materials** · Numeral cards, place-value mats and squared paper.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 145, 55 and 230. Ask which pair makes an easy subtotal. | Choose 145 and 55, then explain how a subtotal of 200 helps. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model finding the same total by adding in stages and by aligning all three addends in columns. Include a case where two exchanges occur. | Solve a set of sums with a partner. Compare the two methods and circle a pair that makes a multiple of ten or a hundred. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give 123 + 45 + 211, 186 + 34 + 279, 250 + 175 + 325 and 306 + 284 + 410. Check the totals and one grouping explanation. | Independently solve all four sums. Annotate one calculation to show why a chosen pair is useful. Use Activity Book pp. 39–41 if available. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask whether changing the order changes the total. | Use one of the sums to explain why the total stays the same. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils found at least three totals and explained one grouping. Give a two-step subtotal prompt to pupils who omitted an addend.
