# Mathematics (DLP) · Year 2 SK · Unit 5 Time

Six 60-minute RPH for CS 5.1–5.3. See the [source mapping](../../../audits/dlp_sk_sources_20261114.md#year-2-unit-5-source-check).

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-01 · Recognise Minute Graduations

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 39–41 · Activity Book pp. 115–116
**Learning objective** · Match at least four of five minute-hand positions to elapsed minutes counted from 12, including a quarter hour or half an hour.
**Materials** · Analogue clock with 60 ticks, minute labels in multiples of five, movable minute hands and marked clock faces.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Point to two neighbouring small ticks. Ask how far the minute hand travels in one minute. | Move a model hand one small tick and describe the movement. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Start at 12 and group the ticks in fives. Model one quarter turn to 3 and one half turn to 6. Ask pairs to test other minute-hand positions. | Label the groups of five, move a hand to selected positions and count aloud from 12. Match quarter and half turns to 15 and 30 minutes. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give faces with the minute hand at 2, 3, 6, 8 and 11. Check at least four matches, including either the quarter or half hour. | Independently record 10, 15, 30, 40 and 55 minutes for the respective positions. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how far the minute hand moves in a full turn. | Count the 12 groups of five and state 60 minutes. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils matched at least four positions, including a quarter or half hour. Review any errors by counting the five-minute jumps from 12.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-02 · Read and Show Time on Clocks

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.2
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 42–44 · Activity Book pp. 117–118 (shared section reference)
**Learning objective** · State two shown times and set an analogue model for two given times, with at least three correct responses including one of each type.
**Materials** · Teaching clock with linked hands, pupil clocks, analogue pictures and digital time cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 3:00 on a clock and ask which hand gives hours and which gives minutes. | Point to each hand and describe what it shows. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Advance the clock from 3:00 to 3:25 and then 3:30, drawing attention to the moving hour hand. Give pairs time cards in five-minute steps. | Take turns reading a clock and setting the hands from a card. Check that the hour hand sits between the hour numbers when minutes have passed. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give clock faces showing 2:20 and 8:45. Ask pupils to read them, then set 5:10 and 7:30 on their model clocks. | Independently state both shown times and position both hands for the two given times. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where the hour hand should be at 7:30. | Point between 7 and 8 and explain that half an hour has passed since 7:00. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils gave at least three correct responses including one reading and one clock setting. Practise tracing the hour hand through the hour with pupils who placed it directly on the numeral.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-03 · Convert Time Between Words and Numerals

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.3
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 43–44 · Activity Book pp. 117–118 (shared section reference)
**Learning objective** · Convert at least three of four times between words and numerals, including one conversion in each direction.
**Materials** · Word and numeral cards, an analogue clock model, and individual response slips.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show 6:05 and “five minutes past six”. Ask pupils to point to the digits that represent the minutes. | Identify 05 and explain the leading zero. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model “a quarter past nine” as 9:15 and 1:30 as “one thirty” or “half past one”. Give partners a matching set with whole hours and five-minute steps. | Match each phrase to its numeral form, then use the clock to justify two matches. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give “a quarter past four” and “five minutes past eight” to write in numerals, plus 2:30 and 10:45 to write in words. Accept equivalent clear time phrases. | Independently record 4:15 and 8:05, and write the other two times in words. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how 8:05 differs from 8:50. | Read both and explain the place of the minute digits. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils converted at least three times with one correct response in each direction. Reteach the use of two minute digits where needed.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-04 · Record Times of Daily Activities

**Standards** · CS 5.1 · LS 5.1.4
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 45–46 · Activity Book pp. 119–120
**Learning objective** · Record the correct time in hours and minutes for three activities and list the activities in the order shown.
**Materials** · School timetable example, three illustrated event cards with clocks, and blank time-and-activity tables.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show two activities from a class timetable and ask which starts earlier. | Read the times and identify the earlier activity. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model one timetable row and explain how “in the morning” or “in the afternoon” clarifies a time. Give pairs a shuffled school-event set. | Read each clock, add its activity to a table and sort the rows by time. Compare the record with a partner. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Provide three new cards showing reading at 8:10, recess at 10:20 and art at 11:45 in the morning. Check the times, activity labels and sequence. | Independently write the three time–activity pairs in chronological order. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask where a new 9:05 morning activity would fit. | Place it between reading and recess and explain the time comparison. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils recorded all three times and ordered the activities correctly. Review any transposed hours and minutes using the model clock.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-05 · Relate Days, Hours and Minutes

**Standards** · CS 5.2 · LS 5.2.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 47–48 · Activity Book p. 121
**Learning objective** · State that one hour equals 60 minutes and one day equals 24 hours, and explain one relationship using a clock or timeline.
**Materials** · Linked-hand analogue clock, two-colour 24-hour strip and quarter-, half- and whole-hour cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Ask how long the minute hand takes to return to 12 after it starts at 12 and makes one full turn. | Predict and trace one full turn. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Show a full minute-hand turn and two hour-hand turns from midnight to the next midnight. Mark noon at the meeting point of two 12-hour sections. | Count 12 five-minute groups for one hour, then join two 12-hour sections and say how many hours make a day. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give two incomplete equivalences and ask for a labelled representation of either. Check the numbers, units and model. | Independently complete 1 hour = 60 minutes and 1 day = 24 hours, then show either relationship on a clock or strip. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask how many minutes two half hours make together. | Join the two half-hour cards and state 60 minutes. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils stated both relationships and supported one with a model. Rebuild the 24-hour strip with pupils who counted only one 12-hour turn.

## DLP-MAT-Y2-U05-06 · Solve Everyday Time Problems

**Standards** · CS 5.3 · LS 5.3.1
**References** · Textbook Part 2 pp. 49–51 · Activity Book p. 122
**Learning objective** · Solve two everyday start-and-finish problems using a clock or timeline and label each answer as a time of day.
**Materials** · Analogue clock models, timeline strips and teacher-made activity cards.

| Phase | Teacher | Pupils |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set induction · 5 min | Show a club starting at 9:00 with a 15-minute activity. Ask which number tells the starting time and which tells the elapsed minutes. | Identify the start and the duration in the prompt. |
| Main activity · 30 min | Model a jump from 9:00 to 9:15 on a timeline and a one-hour jump from 2:20 to 3:20. Invite partners to solve a similar card and compare their models. | Label each start, move the model clock or draw jumps, and state the finish as a time of day. |
| Practice and assessment · 20 min | Give a craft session starting at 10:00 in the morning and lasting 35 minutes, and a club starting at 2:25 in the afternoon and lasting one hour. Check both models and answers. | Independently show the elapsed time and record 10:35 in the morning and 3:25 in the afternoon. |
| Closure · 5 min | Ask why 35 minutes is different from 10:35 in the first problem. | Explain that one describes the duration and the other tells when the activity finishes. |

**Reflection** · ___ of ___ pupils solved both problems and labelled the times correctly. Revisit the difference between the start, the jump and the finish using one timeline.
