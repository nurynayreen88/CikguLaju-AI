# English Year 2 — Lesson 082: Get dressed! — Speaking cards (original supplementary resource)

**Use:** Optional teacher-created material for the existing Non-Textbook speaking lesson. Not an official Student Book or Workbook extract.

## Card A — Describe the outfit
A child is wearing **a red shirt**, **blue shorts** and **white shoes**.

## Card B — Describe the outfit
A child is wearing **a yellow dress**, **a green hat** and **black shoes**.

## Partner dialogue
A: What are you wearing?
B: I'm wearing a red shirt and blue shorts.
A: What colour are your shoes?
B: My shoes are white.

## Pupil speaking frame
Hello. My name is ______.
This is our clothing poster.
I can see ______, ______ and ______.
Thank you.

**Scoring guidance:** 0–3 intelligible clothing descriptions, plus 0–1 clear self-introduction. Assess pupils individually, even when they work in pairs.

**Teacher guidance:** Draw or print the two outfit descriptions as picture cards. Give partners different cards. Model the dialogue, then invite pupils to present three items. Accept accurate alternative clothing descriptions.
