# CikguLaju AI — Standalone RPH Policy

## Mandatory rule for every subject
Every year/form, subject, and lesson/PdP must resolve to a **pre-authored, lesson-specific, reviewed RPH record**. Do not generate generic teaching activities or substitute a different lesson when a record is missing.

## Required lesson record
- Stable subject, year/form, lesson/PdP identifier and title
- Verified curriculum mapping (SK/SP, or corresponding standards)
- Lesson-specific learning objectives and measurable success criteria
- Set induction, sequenced teaching and pupil activities, assessment, closure
- Relevant textbook, teacher's book, SOW/RPT and material references with provenance
- Review status, version and evidence; mark unknown fields explicitly rather than inventing them

## Generation contract
1. Look up the exact standalone record by subject, year/form and lesson/PdP.
2. Reject records not approved for public generation; display a clear unavailable/in-review message.
3. Personalise only teacher/class/date/time and other non-curricular metadata.
4. Format the approved lesson content into an A4 one-page RPH without changing its instructional meaning.
5. Never fabricate standards, workbook pages, audio tracks or Teacher's Book steps.
6. Preserve existing databases and routes until a separately tested migration is explicitly approved.

## Migration and QA
- Inventory current lessons and prior curriculum audits before creating any new lesson content.
- Convert audited lesson-specific guidance into complete records; source-derived notes are not automatically approved RPHs.
- Test completeness, lesson identity, source provenance, teacher/pupil instructions, and PDF output.
- Roll out subject-by-subject only after regression tests; keep the legacy system intact until verified.

**Status:** policy and migration specification; not evidence that every lesson has already been converted.
