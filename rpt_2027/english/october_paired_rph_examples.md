# English SK 2027 · Examples of integrated RPH

Each pair uses one 60-minute RPH. The numbers identify the two SOW references. The plan combines selected activities in one sequence with a shared introduction, PBD and closure. Adjust the time spent on individual activities to pupils' needs.

| Year and SOW references | One teaching sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Year 2 · 002 + 003 · Free Time | Introduce day cards. Pupils identify days from SB p.58 and reorder the cards, then use the same cards to match people and activities from SB p.59 and say what they do on different days. Check heard days, activity matches and oral sentences. Recap a day-and-activity sentence. |
| Year 3 · 013 + 014 · Welcome! | Introduce Fay's picture in SB p.11. Pupils read her text, underline details and describe a character. Use the same text as a model for two family sentences. Check the reading evidence and description, then pupils correct their writing after feedback. End by sharing a corrected sentence. |
| Year 4 · 102 + 103 · Helping out | Introduce helping-out vocabulary. Pupils listen to Activity 4 and describe an object or person, then use the Activity 5 dialogue and a guessing exchange to ask whose an object is. Check listening details and the accuracy of descriptions and ownership responses. Recap a possessive form. |
| Year 5 · 101 + 102 · Food and health | Use the SB p.58 café price list as the shared context. Pupils infer menu words and listen for prices, then use those prices to order and pay in a role-play. Check recognised prices and details recalled from a partner's order. Recap a useful polite expression. |
| Year 6 · 077 + 078 · Getting around | Open with PB p.68 ferry-ticket context. Pupils listen for travel details and practise question intonation. Use the same context and a timetable to role-play a ticket exchange with clarification. Check travel details, question intonation and effective clarification. Recap a useful question. |
