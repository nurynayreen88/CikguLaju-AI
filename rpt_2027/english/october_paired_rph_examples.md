# English SK 2027 · Sample RPH for paired Lessons

Each pair occupies one 60-minute slot. Use the first 30 minutes for the first Lesson and the second 30 minutes for the next. Keep both Main and Complementary SK/SP and collect evidence for each half. The original Lesson files under `rph_standalone/english` provide the full activities and differentiation. These samples use selected tasks from those sources.

## Year 2 · Lesson 002 + 003 · Free Time

| Time | SK/SP | Teaching, learning and PBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 min · Lesson 002 | Main 1.2 / 1.2.1. Complementary 2.1 / 2.1.1. | **5 min:** Teacher revises days with flashcards. **15 min:** Pupils listen and point to days in SB p.58 using CD2 Track 18. Teacher replays a short section and pupils sequence the cards. **10 min:** Pupils identify at least five days from spoken cues and answer a favourite-day question. |
| 30–60 min · Lesson 003 | Main 2.1 / 2.1.1. Complementary 1.2 / 1.2.1. | **5 min:** Teacher models an activity and day using the cards from Lesson 002. **10 min:** Pupils match at least three spoken descriptions to pictures or names in SB p.59 Activity 1 (CD2 Track 20). **10 min:** Pupils practise three personal activity-and-day sentences in pairs using the SB p.59 speaking model. **5 min:** Teacher checks spoken sentences and recaps one activity. |

## Year 3 · Lesson 013 + 014 · Welcome!

| Time | SK/SP | Teaching, learning and PBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 min · Lesson 013 | Main 3.2 / 3.2.2. Complementary 2.1 / 2.1.5. | **5 min:** Teacher shows the picture of Fay in SB p.11. **15 min:** Pupils read the text, find two details and underline the evidence. **10 min:** Pupils explain one answer and describe one character aloud. Teacher checks both the text evidence and oral description. |
| 30–60 min · Lesson 014 | Main 4.3 / 4.3.1. Complementary 4.3 / 4.3.3. | **5 min:** Teacher uses Fay's text and TB p.27 to model one sentence with a capital letter and full stop. **15 min:** Pupils draft two sentences about their own family using SB p.11 as a model. **7 min:** Partners check punctuation, then each pupil corrects one sentence. **3 min:** Pupils submit their corrected sentences for PBD. |

## Year 4 · Lesson 102 + 103 · Helping out

| Time | SK/SP | Teaching, learning and PBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 min · Lesson 102 | Main 1.2 / 1.2.2. Complementary 2.1 / 2.1.5. | **5 min:** Teacher previews the listening goal. **15 min:** Pupils listen to SB p.73 Activity 4, CD2 Track 25, and record two details. **10 min:** Pupils check the two details against the recording and describe one person or object from it. |
| 30–60 min · Lesson 103 | Main 2.1 / 2.1.5. Complementary 1.2 / 1.2.5. | **5 min:** Teacher models “Whose …?” and an ownership answer using a real object. **10 min:** Pupils listen to CD2 Track 26 for SB p.73 Activity 5 and respond to supported questions. **10 min:** Pairs guess the owner of a sketched belonging. **5 min:** Teacher checks whether pupils ask and answer about ownership and understand a spoken question. |

## Year 5 · Lesson 101 + 102 · Food and health

| Time | SK/SP | Teaching, learning and PBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 min · Lesson 101 | Main 1.1 / 1.1.1. Complementary 3.2 / 3.2.3. | **5 min:** Teacher introduces a price list from the existing SB p.58 tasks. **15 min:** Pupils use menu or dialogue clues to infer unfamiliar café words and listen to the prices in SB p.58 Activities 1–4. **10 min:** Pupils write three heard prices and say two aloud. Teacher checks price pronunciation and the clues used for the word guesses. |
| 30–60 min · Lesson 102 | Main 2.1 / 2.1.1. Complementary 1.2 / 1.2.5. | **5 min:** Teacher models a polite order using the same price list. **15 min:** Pairs complete the SB p.58 Activities 5–6 café exchange, switching customer and server roles. **10 min:** Pupils report a partner's order and check the total and change. Teacher checks details spoken and understood. |

## Year 6 · Lesson 077 + 078 · Getting around

| Time | SK/SP | Teaching, learning and PBD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 min · Lesson 077 | Main 1.2 / 1.2.5. Complementary 1.1 / 1.1.1. | **5 min:** Teacher previews the ferry-ticket context in PB p.68. **15 min:** Pupils listen to the verified recording or read-aloud from TB p.110 Activities 1–2 and record four travel details. **10 min:** Pupils identify three ticket questions and repeat one with the modelled intonation. |
| 30–60 min · Lesson 078 | Main 2.2 / 2.2.1. Complementary 1.2 / 1.2.5. | **5 min:** Teacher uses a timetable to model one clarification phrase. **15 min:** Pairs listen to the SOW dialogue, then role-play a ticket exchange and check understanding three times. **10 min:** Pupils respond to two spoken questions. Teacher notes whether pupils understood the questions and used clarification effectively. |
