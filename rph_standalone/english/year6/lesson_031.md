# English Year 6 — Lesson 031: Toes (Language Arts 6)

**Year:** 6
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 31, M08 (week indicative only)
**Main CS/LS:** 5.3/5.3.1
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.1
**Language focus:** Simple-present action verbs to describe body parts
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems Years 4–6, Toes by Judith Nicholls p.30; BPK Poetry Guidebook; body-part role cards.
**Learning objective:** By the end of the lesson, in groups, pupils adapt at least four lines of Toes to a chosen body part with suitable verbs and orally explain two actions before reciting their original work.
**PBD evidence:** A four-line original body-part verse, two suitable action-verb details and an individual spoken explanation; peer feedback using Two Stars and a Wish.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Toes poem and demonstrate a selection of its action verbs.
**Pupils:** Recite or mime two verbs from the poem.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign each group a different body part, such as eye, nose or hand; model how to choose action verbs.
**Pupils:** Choose a body part and brainstorm five relevant actions, e.g. eyes blink and hands clap.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to adapt the structure of Toes into a new original poem without copying whole stanzas.
**Pupils:** Compose a four-line creative poem, checking that the verbs fit their chosen body part.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Coach group recitals and appropriate personal descriptions.
**Pupils:** Recite their poem and explain two functions of the chosen body part.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess observable lesson products and individual contributions against the stated measurable learning objective; give one actionable correction.
**Pupils:** Submit evidence and revise one specific error after feedback.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Facilitate SOW Two Stars and a Wish feedback after each group performance.
**Pupils:** Offer two strengths and one specific improvement suggestion.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture cards, word banks, structured roles and extra time. Extension: independently justify an imaginative choice with precise evidence.
