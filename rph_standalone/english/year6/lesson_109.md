# English Year 6 — Lesson 109: Music and Song (Listening 21)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 109, M28 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.3/3.3.1  
**Language focus:** Review of language in Unit 7  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 92-95; Teacher Book pages 149-150; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Understand the principal events and problem-solving ideas in the PB pp.92–95 play, then explain two possible outcomes using heard and read evidence.  
**PBD evidence:** Four correctly sequenced heard events from the play (Listening 1.2.3) and a supported independent reading response (Reading 3.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce words from the play using a short vocabulary review.  
**Pupils:** Predict a character's problem using a picture clue.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.149 pre-reading; use Audio 2.6 only if source identity verified, otherwise deliver the accurate printed play orally.  
**Pupils:** Listen once without the book and note the setting and first event.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Allow pupils to read PB pp.92–95 after the uninterrupted listening and follow the reading guidance.  
**Pupils:** Read the play and order four principal events.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.150 Activities 1, 3 and 4 on memory, problem solving and alternatives.  
**Pupils:** Answer comprehension questions and suggest one plausible alternative resolution.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four heard event positions and a text-supported explanation.  
**Pupils:** Correct one sequence error and give a reason for a proposed solution.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss the value of theatre and music without using an unverified optional video.  
**Pupils:** Name one lesson that can be learned from the characters.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
