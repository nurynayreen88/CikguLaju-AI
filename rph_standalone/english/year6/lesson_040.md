# English Year 6 — Lesson 040: Adventure time (Listening 8)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 40, M10 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.3/1.3.1  
**Language focus:** Present perfect review (e.g. What’s the worst experience you’ve ever had?)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.39; TB pp.68–69; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Understand four important details from a PB p.39 interview, infer two vocabulary items from spoken definitions and compare Mike's work and home life.  
**PBD evidence:** Four interview details and two correctly recognised vocabulary items (Listening 1.2.2/1.3.1), noting support needed for inferred words.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display PB p.39 travel and work pictures; ask what a travelling worker might experience.  
**Pupils:** Describe one picture and predict the kind of interview to be heard.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.68 Activity 1: teach what an interview is and model matching pictures to spoken ideas.  
**Pupils:** Predict one fact about the interviewee's work and prepare to recognise unfamiliar words.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Using verified audio or a precise teacher reading of the PB p.39 interview, guide definition-matching and TB p.68 Activity 2 fact files.  
**Pupils:** Listen for two words matching the definitions and complete four facts about Mike.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.69 Activities 3–4: check from memory before replay and compare Mike's work and home life.  
**Pupils:** Correct four fact-file details, discuss two differences and respond to a Values question on travel.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess two listening vocabulary inferences and four text-supported interview facts.  
**Pupils:** Explain one inferred word and correct one work/home detail after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.69 Cooler: All around the world and the PB p.39 Values box.  
**Pupils:** Share one benefit or challenge of travelling using a fact from the interview.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
