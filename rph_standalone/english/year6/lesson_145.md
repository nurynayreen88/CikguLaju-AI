# English Year 6 — Lesson 145: It’s a mystery! (Reading 28)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 145, M37 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.5  
**Language focus:** Question tags (e.g. It’s hot, isn’t it?)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 122-123; Teacher Book pages 190-191  
**Learning objective:** Read PB pp.122–123 detective mystery for four specific clues and build a mind map of the suspects using the text.  
**PBD evidence:** Four detailed reading answers and one correctly supported suspect mind map (Reading 3.2.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce familiar mysteries and ask pupils to name qualities of a good detective.  
**Pupils:** Name a mystery or fictional detective and one useful trait.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.190 Activity 1; preview the museum mystery and main suspects.  
**Pupils:** Predict one possible event from the illustrations.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.190 Activity 2: skim for the main event, then read closely.  
**Pupils:** Find four source-supported facts from PB pp.122–123.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.190 Activity 3 and TB p.191 Reading.  
**Pupils:** Infer two words from context and map suspects, locations and clues.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check reading facts and connections in the suspect map.  
**Pupils:** Correct a wrong clue and show its location in the text.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.191 Cooler Stickman.  
**Pupils:** Describe one character using an accurate mystery word.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
