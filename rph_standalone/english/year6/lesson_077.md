# English Year 6 — Lesson 077: Getting around (Listening 15)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 77, M20 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Language focus:** Functional language: getting around, e.g. Could I have two return tickets, please?  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.68; TB pp.110; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Listen to PB p.68 travel conversation for four details and distinguish three spoken ticket questions with natural intonation.  
**PBD evidence:** Four correct travel facts and three recognised questions (Listening 1.2.5) plus recognisable question intonation (Listening 1.1.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.110 Board race to introduce travel words.  
**Pupils:** Recall two travel words and state a trip destination.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview PB p.68 photographs and TB p.110 vocabulary.  
**Pupils:** Predict a ferry-ticket conversation and one likely cost detail.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use verified audio or accurate TB p.110 reading for Activities 1–2.  
**Pupils:** Listen for four journey details and identify three questions on the SOW worksheet.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the question intonation and guide pair ticket-dialogue practice.  
**Pupils:** Practise ticket questions and answers, comparing price with local transport.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check question recognition, answers and speech intonation.  
**Pupils:** Correct one listening answer and repeat a question with appropriate stress.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review travel phrases in a short oral check.  
**Pupils:** Ask for a return ticket with a polite, clear question.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
