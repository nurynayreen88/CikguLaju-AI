# English Year 6 — Lesson 081: How is it made? (Reading 16)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 81, M21 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.4  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Language focus:** Present forms: passive (e.g. be made) and active (it takes…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 70-71; Teacher Book pages 114-115  
**Learning objective:** Read PB pp.70–71 about how jeans are made; use a dictionary to check four unfamiliar words and explain the steps of the process.  
**PBD evidence:** Four context-checked vocabulary meanings (Reading 3.2.4) and the main idea of the jeans-making account (Reading 3.2.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a pair of jeans and ask how the material was produced.  
**Pupils:** Suggest two materials or steps involved in making jeans.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.114 Activity 1 and introduce the jeans-making reading in PB pp.70–71.  
**Pupils:** Discuss the introductory questions and predict one production step.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.114 Activity 2; pupils read for gist, then use a dictionary or PB p.139 word list.  
**Pupils:** Read the process and locate four boxed words, checking each against the text.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.115 Activity 4 and a definition game.  
**Pupils:** Describe the sequence in which jeans are made and match words to definitions.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four dictionary meanings and two supported production facts.  
**Pupils:** Show a text clue, verify one word and correct a mistaken step.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss recycling clothes as an environmentally responsible choice.  
**Pupils:** Name one way to reuse jeans and explain why it helps.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
