# English Year 6 — Lesson 037: Akbar's Dream (Language Arts 7)

**Year:** 6
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 37, M10 (week indicative only)
**Main CS/LS:** 5.3/5.3.1
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1
**Language focus:** Past simple and oral story sequencing
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Akbar's Dream by Jane Langford, cover only; Akbar's Dream Teacher's Guidebook pp.35–36; authorised picture-sequence cards. Do not invent story events as confirmed plot.
**Learning objective:** By the end of the lesson, in groups, pupils use the guidebook picture sequence to imagine at least three connected story events and narrate a coherent predicted story orally.
**PBD evidence:** Original predicted story using at least three picture-linked events and clear oral sequencing; individual contribution and peer feedback.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the cover of Akbar's Dream and elicit its title, author, publisher and blurb; do not reveal the real plot.
**Pupils:** Identify two cover features and predict a general story idea.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask groups to brainstorm possibilities using only the cover and guidebook pictures.
**Pupils:** Suggest story characters, setting and events without treating guesses as real plot.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute the SOW picture set from the Teacher's Guidebook pp.35–36 and guide groups to order events.
**Pupils:** Build an original possible storyline from the pictures and practise three connected past-tense events.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Manage group narrations and peer feedback without correcting the invented plot against an unread original.
**Pupils:** Narrate the prediction, listen to peers and ask one relevant question.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess observable lesson products and individual contributions against the stated measurable learning objective; give one actionable correction.
**Pupils:** Submit evidence and revise one specific error after feedback.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Conduct the SOW class vote for the most likely prediction and record it for later comparison with the actual text.
**Pupils:** Choose one story and explain a picture clue supporting the prediction.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture cards, word banks, structured roles and extra time. Extension: independently justify an imaginative choice with precise evidence.
