# English Year 6 — Lesson 012: It’s an emergency! (Writing 3)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 12, M03 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.3  
**Language focus:** Past simple & continuous for interrupted actions with when/while; Direct speech  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.15; TB pp.32–33; WB pp.14–15  
**Learning objective:** Plan a short newspaper report with a headline, introduction, body and conclusion; draft a coherent report using past tenses and direct speech, then revise after feedback.  
**PBD evidence:** Headline and correctly sequenced introduction/body/conclusion, a story draft with direct speech and appropriate past tenses, and one improved revision (Writing 4.2.3 / Writing 4.3.3).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use mime to review -ed/-ing adjectives from PB p.15 Working with words.  
**Pupils:** Choose an appropriate adjective for a character's reaction and explain it.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.32 Working with words; model highlighting a headline and underlining direct speech in the SOW's jumbled newspaper-article worksheet.  
**Pupils:** Identify the article's headline, one example of direct speech and the intended introduction/body/conclusion sections.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use WB p.14 Prepare to Write Activities 1–3 (TB p.33): order the story pictures and plan the report's main sections.  
**Pupils:** Sequence the pictures, reorganise the article's jumbled paragraphs and draft an introduction, body and conclusion plan.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow WB p.15 Activity 4 and TB p.33 to guide a newspaper report using adjectives, interrupted actions and punctuated direct speech.  
**Pupils:** Draft a short newspaper article with a headline, logically ordered events and one accurately punctuated quotation; revise against a checklist.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess narrative order, clarity, past-tense choices and direct-speech punctuation; collect the draft and one response to feedback.  
**Pupils:** Show the plan and article, explain one writing choice and correct a sentence after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a pupil to use an -ed/-ing adjective to describe a moment from the report.  
**Pupils:** Say one sentence about the report with a suitable adjective and identify one improvement.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
