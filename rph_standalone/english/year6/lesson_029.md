# English Year 6 — Lesson 029: Life in the past (Listening 6)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 29, M08 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** Future forms review, e.g. I think people will travel by plane.  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.30; TB pp.51, 54; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify the main idea of two longer simple spoken texts or extracts about life then and now with little support.  
**PBD evidence:** Two accurate gist statements with an appropriate supporting clue.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show then-and-now photographs and ask: “How was everyday life different in the past?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach transport, homes, past routines; display then-and-now photographs and ask pupils to predict what they may hear. Emphasise the language focus: Future forms review, e.g. I think people will travel by plane..  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read or play a verified passage twice and contrast its main message with distractor ideas.  
**Pupils:** Identify the main idea for each of two listening extracts and give one supporting detail.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about life then and now, and replay/read relevant parts where needed. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases. Give a short oral explanation to a partner and respond to one question.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “How was everyday life different in the past?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
