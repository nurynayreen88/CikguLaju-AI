# English Year 6 — Lesson 029: Life in the past (Listening 6)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 29, M08 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** Future forms review, e.g. I think people will travel by plane.  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.30; TB pp.51, 54; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify the order and main idea of three spoken predictions about future technology, the environment and family life; express one personal plan orally.  
**PBD evidence:** Correct order and gist of three teacher-presented scenarios (Listening 1.2.1), plus a relevant personal plan using future forms (Speaking 2.1.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB p.51 Cooler question to discuss one change pupils expect in the next 50 years.  
**Pupils:** Make one clear prediction about life in the future.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.54 Vocabulary and PB p.30 Activity 1; gather future ideas under environment, technology and everyday family life.  
**Pupils:** Sort ideas into three future themes and predict one likely development.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give the SOW teacher-prepared three-topic presentation, orally and in a different order to the displayed themes.  
**Pupils:** Listen for the order of technology, environment and family life and write the main idea of each section.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Repeat the same brief oral presentation to check gist; then model the pair question 'What are you going to do in the future?'  
**Pupils:** Compare the three topic summaries, decide whether each prediction is positive/negative and tell a partner one future plan.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess the three-topic order, gist statements and clarity of one spoken future plan.  
**Pupils:** Record the three main ideas and correct one prediction or future-form expression.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit one prediction that would improve the environment and one personal plan.  
**Pupils:** State a future change and a clear response using will or going to.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
