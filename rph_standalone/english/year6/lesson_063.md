# English Year 6 — Lesson 063: My Sister’s Eating Porridge (Language Arts 12)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 63, M16 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 5.2/5.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2  
**Language focus:** Adjectives  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems Years 4-6: My Sister's Eating Porridge page 31; original pupil response card  
**Learning objective:** Describe 2 actions or feelings of a character in “My Sister’s Eating Porridge” and give a text-based reason for each.  
**PBD evidence:** Two character action/feeling statements, each supported by a reason from the text.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the text title and invite a prediction.  
**Pupils:** Predict a story/poem idea from the title.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the title “My Sister’s Eating Porridge” and ask for predictions using the supplied poem/story text.  
**Pupils:** Observe illustrations or title clues and predict one idea or emotion.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the authorised literary text, focusing on a character’s actions, feelings and supporting textual details.  
**Pupils:** Note two character action/feeling examples and explain each with evidence from the text.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a short original creative response: freeze-frame, dialogue, four-line poem or illustrated explanation consistent with the focus. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Prepare and share a brief creative response; explain how it connects to one part of the text. Write 2 connected sentences reflecting the lesson language.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask for one connection between the literary text and the creative response.  
**Pupils:** Share one line, image or idea and say why it matters.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
