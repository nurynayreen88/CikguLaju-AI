# English Year 6 — Lesson 003: Welcome (Listening 1)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 3, M01 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Language focus:** Present simple for defining vocabulary (e.g. This is a person who…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.6–7; TB p.20; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify at least 5 specific facts from a spoken passage about meeting new classmates with little or no support.  
**PBD evidence:** Five correct details from the oral text; note independent/repeated support.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show profile and personality cards and ask: “How would you introduce yourself to a new classmate?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach personal details, personality, goals; display profile and personality cards and ask pupils to predict what they may hear. Emphasise the language focus: Present simple for defining vocabulary (e.g. This is a person who…).  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the cited listening task with a confirmed audio track or an approved teacher read-aloud; give pupils a five-item grid.  
**Pupils:** Listen for details and complete five information questions before paired comparison.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about meeting new classmates, and replay/read relevant parts where needed. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases. Listen to a partner or a short approved utterance and identify one meaningful detail.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “How would you introduce yourself to a new classmate?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
