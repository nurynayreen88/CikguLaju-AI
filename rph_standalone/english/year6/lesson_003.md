# English Year 6 — Lesson 003: Welcome (Listening 1)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 3, M01 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Language focus:** Present simple for defining vocabulary (e.g. This is a person who…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.6–7; TB p.20; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify at least four jobs or places from spoken definitions and pronounce three long job names with recognisable syllable stress.  
**PBD evidence:** Four spoken definition-to-word answers (Listening 1.2.2) and three modelled multi-syllable job words with correct stressed syllable (Listening 1.1.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the jobs-and-places topic with a quick word categorisation game.  
**Pupils:** Classify at least two familiar words as a job or a place.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow the TB p.20 Warm-up and PB pp.6–7 Activity 1: model listening, pointing to a picture and saying the word with its syllable stress.  
**Pupils:** Find the pictured jobs and places; listen, point and repeat photographer, businesswoman and police officer while marking stress.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read aloud four prepared descriptions such as 'a person who rides waves on a board'; pause to allow pupils to identify and write each answer.  
**Pupils:** Listen to four oral definitions, locate the matching pictured word and write four answers independently.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pairs to invent one accurate short job or place definition each and exchange the clues orally.  
**Pupils:** Describe a pictured job or place for a partner to identify; listen to the partner's definition and answer.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four spoken-word matches and listen to pronunciation/stress of three longer job names; give corrective modelling.  
**Pupils:** Show the four written answers and repeat the three multisyllabic names with the stressed part clear.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask which jobs or places pupils encounter in their daily lives.  
**Pupils:** Name a useful job or place and give one correct definition.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
