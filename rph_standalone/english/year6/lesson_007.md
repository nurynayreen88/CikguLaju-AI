# English Year 6 — Lesson 007: It’s an emergency! (Writing 2)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 7, M02 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Language focus:** Past simple & continuous for interrupted actions with when  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.10–11; TB pp.26–27  
**Learning objective:** Write at least four grammatical sentences combining past simple and past continuous with when, then orally recount the sequence to a partner.  
**PBD evidence:** Four paired-card 'was/were ...-ing when ...' sentences using relevant past forms (Writing 4.2.3), plus a short oral recount (Speaking 2.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one true/false question about the previous electricity reading and recall an interrupted action.  
**Pupils:** Give one example of something they were doing when another action happened.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB p.10 Activity 3 and TB p.26 Activity 1/Personalisation: contrast past simple and past continuous in interrupted events.  
**Pupils:** Identify the continuous action and interruption in an example, then relate one sentence to their own experience.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide PB p.11 Activity 2 and TB p.27 Activity 3. Model cutting paper into sentence cards with past-simple actions.  
**Pupils:** Choose correct verb forms, write four short past-event cards and check the spelling with a partner.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate the SOW sentence-card game: pairs combine cards using when to describe imagined events, then share a funny sentence.  
**Pupils:** Combine cards to write at least four 'was/were ...-ing when ...' sentences and orally recount two connected events.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check accuracy of past continuous, interrupted past simple and when in four sentences; listen to a short pair recount.  
**Pupils:** Read two sentences aloud, correct one verb form and show four written combinations.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to share the most interesting sentence-card combination.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate interrupted-event sentence and identify its two actions.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
