# English Year 6 — Lesson 017: Life in the past (Reading 4)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 17, M05 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Language focus:** Used to / didn’t use to  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.22–23; TB pp.42–43  
**Learning objective:** Read the PB pp.22–23 account about Samuel Pepys and the Great Fire of London; explain the main idea and infer four words using evidence from their context.  
**PBD evidence:** Main idea and two reading details from PB pp.22–23 (Reading 3.2.1), plus four plausible word meanings supported by contextual clues (Reading 3.2.3).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show an illustration of seventeenth-century London and ask who Samuel Pepys was.  
**Pupils:** Identify London in the past and predict an event from the picture.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB pp.42–43 Activities 1–2: introduce Samuel Pepys, predict the scenes and discuss visible clues.  
**Pupils:** Identify Samuel Pepys and describe two details visible in the historical pictures.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.43 Activities 3–4; guide the first reading for meaning, then model inferring unfamiliar words from context.  
**Pupils:** Read about the Great Fire of London, explain the main idea and infer four new words using nearby text clues.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pairs to record useful words with short English meanings; discuss one positive and negative consequence of the Great Fire.  
**Pupils:** Record four context-based meanings, compare evidence with a partner and express one supported historical consequence.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the text's main idea, two supported details and four word-context pairs; correct one unsupported inference.  
**Pupils:** Locate the evidence for two answers and revise one vocabulary definition after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the TB Games Bank to review words from the historical account.  
**Pupils:** Explain one new word in English and recall one accurate detail about Samuel Pepys.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
