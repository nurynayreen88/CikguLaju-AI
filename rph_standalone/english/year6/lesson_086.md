# English Year 6 — Lesson 086: How is it made? (Reading 17)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 86, M22 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2  
**Language focus:** Passive voice: statements  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 77; Teacher Book pages 124  
**Learning objective:** Read two competing versions of a recipe, identify which is credible using text evidence, and locate four accurately spelled passive verbs.  
**PBD evidence:** Two evidence-backed choices about a recipe (Reading 3.2.2) and four accurately recorded passive words (Writing 4.3.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce unfamiliar food words through a short spelling activity.  
**Pupils:** Spell two ingredients or kitchen verbs correctly.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute the SOW definitions sheet and model how to identify a recipe.  
**Pupils:** Match food definitions and predict which recipe version is reliable.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide two recipe versions and ask for individual reading before discussion.  
**Pupils:** Choose the credible recipe and underline two clues that support the decision.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to find passive forms and mime the correct recipe sequence.  
**Pupils:** Underline four passive phrases and rehearse the cooking steps with a partner.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess text evidence and common-word spellings in the selected recipe.  
**Pupils:** Explain why one instruction is incorrect and correct a passive form.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.124 Learning to Learn to discuss fake online instructions.  
**Pupils:** State one check that helps judge internet information safely.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
