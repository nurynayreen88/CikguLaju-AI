# English Year 6 — Lesson 103: Music and Song (Writing 20)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 103, M26 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Language focus:** Phonology: rhythm and rhyme  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 88-89; Teacher Book pages 142  
**Learning objective:** Spell at least 8 of 10 relevant high-frequency words correctly while writing about a school music performance.  
**PBD evidence:** Spelling check: 8 of 10 target words correct in independent sentences.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show instrument and performance pictures and ask: “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a short response linked to “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?”. Highlight useful language: Phonology: rhythm and rhyme.  
**Pupils:** Identify the model’s key ideas and rehearse three expressions related to a school music performance.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Select ten high-frequency words from the actual lesson material; model checking spelling within sentences.  
**Pupils:** Write original sentences with the 10 selected words; self-check against the source before finalising.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on content, grammar and punctuation using a concise checklist. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Re-read, revise two points, then share the final writing with a partner. Listen to a partner or a short approved utterance and identify one meaningful detail.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
