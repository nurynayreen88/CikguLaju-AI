# English Year 6 — Lesson 103: Music and Song (Writing 20)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 103, M26 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Language focus:** Phonology: rhythm and rhyme  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 88-89; Teacher Book pages 142  
**Learning objective:** Recognise and pronounce rhyming words from PB pp.88–89, spell four target words accurately and write a new short verse with a consistent rhyme pattern.  
**PBD evidence:** Four correctly spelled common words in an original verse (Writing 4.3.2) and two recognisably rhyming oral pairs (Listening 1.1.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the SOW rhyming-word game with teacher-chosen words.  
**Pupils:** Say a new word that rhymes with a given word.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use PB p.88 examples of matching and differing rhyme spellings.  
**Pupils:** Compare same/game and first/worst and identify their shared sounds.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Reread the authorised song text and follow TB p.143 Activity 4.  
**Pupils:** Locate two rhyming pairs and complete the missing words from the printed word box.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.143 Activity 5; pupils create a short additional verse rather than copying the original.  
**Pupils:** Draft an original four-line verse, mark its rhymes and check spelling with a partner.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four spelled words and clear repetition of two rhyme pairs.  
**Pupils:** Revise one line where the rhyme or spelling is inaccurate.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to perform only their original verses and discuss rhythm.  
**Pupils:** Read their own verse with appropriate stress and rhythm.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
