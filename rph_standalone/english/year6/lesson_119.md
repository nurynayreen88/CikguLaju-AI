# English Year 6 — Lesson 119: Tell me a story (Writing 23)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 119, M30 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Language focus:** Narrative forms (e.g. past simple: He discovered a special goose)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 102-103; Teacher Book pages 162  
**Learning objective:** Revisit the first-draft email from Lesson 114 and write a clearer pair summary of PB pp.96–97 using TB p.162's events plan and feedback.  
**PBD evidence:** A more coherent revised moral-story summary (Writing 4.2.3) and a text-supported main idea (Reading 3.2.1), showing two improvements since Lesson 114.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review an important event in the PB pp.96–97 story.  
**Pupils:** Recall the story's moral and one turning point.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.162 Activities 1–2 to reread and make notes on the main events.  
**Pupils:** Build a sequence plan with the setting, main problem and resolution.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the TB p.162 Activity 3 model, then return teacher-marked Lesson 114 drafts.  
**Pupils:** Compare the model with original email comments and identify two revision priorities.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.162 Activity 4; pairs rewrite their story summary from events notes and draft.  
**Pupils:** Write a clearer story summary, retaining accurate events and improving two weak sentences.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare the old email and new summary; keep examples of recurring errors.  
**Pupils:** Show evidence of two meaningful edits and locate one supporting text clue.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask groups to correct anonymised common errors from the drafts.  
**Pupils:** Explain one change that improves clarity.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
