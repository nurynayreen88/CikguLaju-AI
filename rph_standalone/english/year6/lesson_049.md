# English Year 6 — Lesson 049: Cool jobs (Reading 10)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 49, M13 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.4  
**Language focus:** Present perfect with for (e.g. I’ve been a pilot for 12 years) and since (e.g. … since 2001)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.48–49; TB pp.80–81  
**Learning objective:** Read the four Unusual Jobs texts in PB pp.48–49, infer four vocabulary meanings from context and check them using a dictionary.  
**PBD evidence:** Four supported vocabulary inferences and one accurate matching answer from the unusual-jobs texts (Reading 3.2.3/3.2.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Run the SOW dictionary race using words from the Unusual Jobs topic.  
**Pupils:** Find one job-related word, then predict a less familiar occupation.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.80 Warm-up; assign each pair one of four PB pp.48–49 Unusual Jobs texts.  
**Pupils:** Predict the job from pictures and read the assigned text for its main topic.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pairs to locate PB p.48 boxed words, infer their meaning from context and compare with PB p.137 or a dictionary.  
**Pupils:** Record four contextual word meanings and the supporting phrases from their assigned text.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Re-form mixed-text groups, require pupils to peer-teach their unusual jobs and use TB p.80 Activity 3.  
**Pupils:** Explain a job and the source of one word inference, then read and match across all four short texts.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four word meanings against context and one all-text reading match; correct false dictionary interpretations.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate how the printed text supports one meaning and revise one inaccurate match.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the unusual job vocabulary through a quick class game.  
**Pupils:** Give one new word and its correctly contextualised meaning.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
