# English Year 6 — Lesson 049: Cool jobs (Reading 10)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 49, M13 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.4  
**Language focus:** Present perfect with for (e.g. I’ve been a pilot for 12 years) and since (e.g. … since 2001)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.48–49; TB pp.80–81  
**Learning objective:** Infer the meaning of at least 4 unfamiliar words from known-word clues in the reading text about different occupations.  
**PBD evidence:** 4 meaning-and-clue pairs supported by the printed text; review 1 inference aloud.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show occupation picture cards and ask: “What does a person in this job do?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce jobs, places of work, skills and teach how to scan the stated Pupil’s Book/reading passage for clues. Show the language pattern: Present perfect with for (e.g. I’ve been a pilot for 12 years) and since (e.g. … since 2001).  
**Pupils:** Predict the text from occupation picture cards; highlight two useful clues or words.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the RPT-cited reading material. Model one contextual inference and ask pupils to infer 4 words from surrounding known words; connect clues to Present perfect with for (e.g. I’ve been a pilot for 12 years) and since (e.g. … since 2001).  
**Pupils:** Read the passage and record 4 unfamiliar words, a likely meaning and the exact nearby clue for each.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead a paired evidence-check; invite pupils to justify their answers before checking as a class. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Compare responses with a partner, correct unsupported answers and write one sentence related to different occupations. Locate one additional supporting clue in the cited reading material.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What does a person in this job do?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
