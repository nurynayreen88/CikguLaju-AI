# English Year 6 — Lesson 034: Adventure time (Writing 7)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 34, M09 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Present perfect for experience + never (e.g. I’ve never seen a lion in the wild.)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.36–37; TB pp.61, 63–64  
**Learning objective:** Write two true and two false present-perfect sentences about Emma's adventures, using has/has never correctly, and revise the false sentences after reading.  
**PBD evidence:** Four meaningful, readable sentences about Emma (Writing 4.2.1), with correct present-perfect forms and reading-supported true/false corrections (Reading 3.2.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB p.61 Physical spelling game to review adventure words.  
**Pupils:** Spell two adventure words and recall one activity Emma has done.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Emma's PB pp.34–35 blog and introduce TB p.64 Activity 1, explaining present perfect with has and has never.  
**Pupils:** Identify two true details about Emma and form one positive and one negative present-perfect sentence.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB pp.36–37 Activity 2 and TB p.63 Working with Words: practise irregular participles and evaluate a prepared true/false worksheet.  
**Pupils:** Fill the present-perfect gaps, identify an irregular participle and decide whether three statements about Emma are true or false.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Have pupils write two true and two false statements of their own and exchange them for reading-based verification.  
**Pupils:** Write four clear sentences using has/has never, swap them with a partner and correct the false statements.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check sentence clarity, the present-perfect form, correct participles and whether the blog evidence matches the chosen truth values.  
**Pupils:** Show four statements with supporting clues; revise one false or grammatically inaccurate sentence.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the TB p.61 Slap verb-card review with verbs from Emma's adventures.  
**Pupils:** Choose the correct verb card and say one accurate experience sentence.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
