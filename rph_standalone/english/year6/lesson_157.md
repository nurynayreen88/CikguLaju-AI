# English Year 6 — Lesson 157: Teacher to complete: ____________ (Listening 30)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 157, M40 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Listening 30: teacher to select listening materials based on pupils' needs; unused Pupil Book or Workbook permitted; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify the main idea and at least 5 details from a 60–90-second teacher-selected spoken passage.  
**PBD evidence:** One gist statement and 5 accurate details from a teacher-supplied spoken passage.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Say a brief original two-sentence message about a familiar situation without displaying a transcript; ask for its likely topic.  
**Pupils:** Listen once, state a possible gist and explain one spoken clue they heard.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Prepare a 60–90-second original spoken passage on a familiar topic; introduce key vocabulary.  
**Pupils:** Predict topic from three picture prompts.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read/record the passage twice without adding unverified textbook audio references.  
**Pupils:** Listen for gist first, then complete a 5-item information grid.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read difficult excerpts once more and model checking against what was heard. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers and correct at least one response. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Repeat one short sentence from the approved classroom read-aloud and ask for a specific detail, without displaying the written text.  
**Pupils:** Give one detail heard and identify whether listening for gist or details needs further practice.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
