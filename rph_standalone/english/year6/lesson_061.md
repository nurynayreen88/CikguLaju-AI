# English Year 6 — Lesson 061: Cool jobs (Listening 12)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 61, M16 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.3/1.3.1  
**Language focus:** Functional language (polite language, e.g. I’m afraid I…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.56; TB pp.92; WB pp.46; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Listen to Robert the robot's PB p.56 dialogue, recognise three polite questions or responses, and practise suitable replies.  
**PBD evidence:** Three correctly understood spoken questions (Listening 1.2.5) and one inferred vocabulary meaning (Listening 1.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review past participles using a word game.  
**Pupils:** Say two past participles in simple sentences.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce Robert through PB p.56 Activity 1/TB p.92 and preview vocabulary.  
**Pupils:** Predict one helpful action Robert might carry out.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the printed dialogue accurately or use confirmed audio for TB p.92 Activities 2–3.  
**Pupils:** Prepare questions, listen for three polite replies and practise the conversation.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the Robert role-play and WB p.46 Activity 1.  
**Pupils:** Take turns as Robert and fill polite-language gaps.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check understanding of three spoken questions and two polite expressions.  
**Pupils:** Correct one question and repeat one polite answer.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review a helpful phrase from Robert's conversation.  
**Pupils:** Use one suitable apology or request.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
