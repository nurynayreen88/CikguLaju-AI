# English Year 6 — Lesson 061: Cool jobs (Listening 12)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 61, M16 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.3/1.3.1  
**Language focus:** Functional language (polite language, e.g. I’m afraid I…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.56; TB pp.92; WB pp.46; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Respond correctly to 4 longer supported listening questions related to different occupations.  
**PBD evidence:** Four question-response pairs showing understanding of complex supported questions.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show occupation picture cards and ask: “What does a person in this job do?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach jobs, places of work, skills; display occupation picture cards and ask pupils to predict what they may hear. Emphasise the language focus: Functional language (polite language, e.g. I’m afraid I…).  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the approved listening passage and ask four multi-part but age-appropriate oral questions.  
**Pupils:** Listen, select the meaning of each question and provide four appropriate responses.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about different occupations, and replay/read relevant parts where needed. Check the complementary skill using the short task. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases. Listen to a partner or a short approved utterance and identify one meaningful detail. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What does a person in this job do?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
