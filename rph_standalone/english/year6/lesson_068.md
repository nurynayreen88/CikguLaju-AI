# English Year 6 — Lesson 068: Getting around (Speaking 13)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 68, M17 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.3  
**Language focus:** Modifying adjectives with (not/just) as…as; too… ; not … enough  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.63–64; TB pp.103–105  
**Learning objective:** Compare transport choices with four modified adjectives, describe a real journey to school and justify a greener option.  
**PBD evidence:** Four clear transport comparisons (Speaking 2.1.1) and one justified advice statement (Speaking 2.1.3).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review modes of transport with TB p.103 Getting around.  
**Pupils:** Name two methods of transport and a quality of each.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB pp.63–64 Activities 1–2/TB p.104.  
**Pupils:** Model and practise too, as...as and not...enough.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.105 Activity 3 for school-journey conversations.  
**Pupils:** Compare transport by time, cost and comfort using four target forms.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.104 Shall I take the bus? to discuss responsible travel.  
**Pupils:** Recommend a mode and explain environmental or health benefits.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check modified adjectives and relevant reasons.  
**Pupils:** Correct one comparative and justify one choice.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.105 Cooler Team sentences.  
**Pupils:** Give one accurate travel comparison.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
