# English Year 6 — Lesson 124: Tell me a story (Writing 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 124, M31 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Language focus:** Language to talk about stories (e.g. It’s about… / It takes place in… / The moral of the story is…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book page 104; Teacher Book pages 164-165  
**Learning objective:** Plan and draft an individual book review using the PB p.104 model, identifying the story, its moral and personal recommendation for revision in Lesson 126.  
**PBD evidence:** An independently planned first-draft review (Writing 4.3.3) with a coherent short narrative summary (Writing 4.2.3), saved for Lesson 126.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review book genres using TB p.164 True or false?  
**Pupils:** Recall one event or character from the unit story.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.164 Activity 1: listen to a model review using verified audio or accurate read-aloud.  
**Pupils:** Identify the book title and one opinion in the model.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB p.104 Activity 2; build notes for an introduction, brief plot, moral and recommendation.  
**Pupils:** Plan a review of the pupil's selected book, using the prepared outline.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share a teacher-written model review and have pupils write their own independently.  
**Pupils:** Draft a personal book review with a clear recommendation; keep it for Lesson 126.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check narrative clarity, organisation and two language points without replacing the original draft.  
**Pupils:** Record specific corrections to apply in the later editing lesson.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Remind pupils to bring the chosen book and original review back in Lesson 126.  
**Pupils:** State one improvement they hope to make to their review.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
