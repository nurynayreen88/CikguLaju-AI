# English Year 6 — Lesson 153: It’s a mystery! (Speaking 29)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 153, M39 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Language focus:** Narrative forms (e.g. past simple: someone took his bag, past continuous: he was carrying a bag)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book page 130; Teacher Book pages 202-203  
**Learning objective:** Invent and tell a mystery from PB p.130, invite two clarifying questions and resolve the mystery using a coherent oral narrative.  
**PBD evidence:** A clear event sequence with a supported resolution (Speaking 2.3.1) and understandable clarification responses.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.202 Warm-up Guess what the numbers refer to.  
**Pupils:** Infer one fact from a number clue.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.202 Vocabulary and Activity 1; model a very short mystery.  
**Pupils:** Listen to the puzzle and ask two questions to test a solution.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.202 Activity 2: pairs use a printed model to draft a mystery.  
**Pupils:** Plan a setting, three clues and a plausible resolution.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.202 Activity 3 and its Communicating box on p.203.  
**Pupils:** Tell an original mystery and answer classmates' questions without revealing the solution too early.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess logical narration, interactive questions and a coherent resolution.  
**Pupils:** Clarify one detail and give the correct solution.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.203 Cooler Team spelling.  
**Pupils:** Spell one mystery word and explain why a clue matters.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
