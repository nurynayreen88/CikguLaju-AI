# English Year 6 — Lesson 107: Music and Song (Reading 21)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 107, M27 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Language focus:** Emotions (e.g. frightened, happy)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 91; Teacher Book pages 146-147  
**Learning objective:** Read PB p.91 film scripts for meaning, match four lines to movie genres and narrate one short emotional scene with expressive speech.  
**PBD evidence:** An understood script/genre match and one relevant main idea (Reading 3.2.1) plus a short intelligible emotional scene (Speaking 2.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce film genres with a short guessing game.  
**Pupils:** Name a recent film genre without giving copyrighted dialogue.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.146 Tell me about it and PB p.91 Activity 1.  
**Pupils:** Read short printed scripts and identify the corresponding film type.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use an approved non-copyright-restricted sample or teacher vocal demonstration for TB p.146 Activities 2–3.  
**Pupils:** Discuss how music changes mood and match emotions with film genres.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide PB p.91 Activity 4: read the provided scripts with character voice, mime and gesture.  
**Pupils:** Perform a short script in pairs, showing a change of emotion.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check comprehension of printed dialogue and clear narration or emotional delivery.  
**Pupils:** Explain one script clue and improve the voice in one line.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB p.147 Cooler: Which instruments? to close.  
**Pupils:** Name an instrument that suits a film mood and give a reason.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
