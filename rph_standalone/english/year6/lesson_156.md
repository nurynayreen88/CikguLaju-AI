# English Year 6 — Lesson 156: Teacher to complete: ____________ (Writing 30)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 156, M39 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Writing 30: teacher to select writing materials based on pupils' needs; unused Pupil Book or Workbook permitted  
**Learning objective:** Produce a 2-paragraph personal recount with clear sequence, appropriate tenses and a self-corrected draft.  
**PBD evidence:** Two coherent paragraphs, chronological language and at least two self-corrections.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show three mixed-up captions about a familiar school event and model one past-tense sequencing expression.  
**Pupils:** Put the events in order and name one sequence connector they can use in a recount.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a school-event recount (when, where, who, what happened, reflection).  
**Pupils:** Identify the chronological structure and past-tense verbs.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give a simple 2-paragraph planning frame and checklist; conference with pupils as they draft.  
**Pupils:** Write 2 connected paragraphs about a memorable school event and add sequence connectors.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide peer-response on clarity, tense consistency and punctuation. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Edit at least two aspects of the draft and submit the revised version. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to read a revised sentence from their recount and explain one change in tense, sequence or punctuation.  
**Pupils:** Share one improved sentence and name the editing check they will use next time.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
