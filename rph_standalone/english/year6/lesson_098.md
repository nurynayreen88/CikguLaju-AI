# English Year 6 — Lesson 098: Music and Song (Writing 19)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 98, M25 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.5  
**Language focus:** Adjectives to describe personality; prefixes: im- / un- / dis-  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 82-84; Teacher Book pages 132, 134  
**Learning objective:** Write four accurately spelled sentences describing Robin Hood's personality and discuss two reasons for the choices of adjectives.  
**PBD evidence:** Four coherent personality sentences (Writing 4.2.1) and two justified personal opinions about the character (Speaking 2.1.5).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.132 One-question interview to review music interests.  
**Pupils:** Answer one simple interview question.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit PB p.84/TB p.134 Activity 1, then introduce the legend of Robin Hood.  
**Pupils:** Read and recall two details describing the hero.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.134 Activity 2 and Working with Words to compare text features and negative prefixes.  
**Pupils:** Form two adjectives with un-, im- or dis- and discuss how they change meaning.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs identify traits of Robin Hood, plan examples and draft four sentences.  
**Pupils:** Write four personality descriptions using accurate adjectives, then compare with another pair.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check adjective meaning, spelling and evidence given for two traits.  
**Pupils:** Correct one spelling and explain why an adjective fits Robin Hood.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a peer group to say whether they agree with one personality description.  
**Pupils:** Give a respectful opinion with a specific reason.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
