# English Year 6 — Lesson 070: Getting around (Reading 14)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 70, M18 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Language focus:** Narrative tenses review, e.g. past simple, past continuous  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.60–61, 66; TB pp.108  
**Learning objective:** Choose a text-supported summary of the PB pp.60–61 story and orally narrate a picture-based time-travel scene with beginning, middle and end.  
**PBD evidence:** Accurate reading gist with evidence (Reading 3.2.1), plus a connected three-part oral story (Speaking 2.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall the earlier story's title and characters.  
**Pupils:** Name one remembered event.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Offer SOW alternative summaries and refer to TB p.108 Activity 1.  
**Pupils:** Choose the accurate summary and check using PB pp.60–61.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce time travel and distribute the separate picture sequence.  
**Pupils:** Order pictures imaginatively and plan beginning, middle and end.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs share their invented stories in groups; use TB p.108 Activity 2.  
**Pupils:** Tell a three-stage scene and predict the next event in picture five.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess reading evidence and fluency across three events.  
**Pupils:** Support a choice with a text detail and improve one transition.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss the alternative story version.  
**Pupils:** Say which story they prefer and why.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
