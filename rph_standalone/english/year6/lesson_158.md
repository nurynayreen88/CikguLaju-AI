# English Year 6 — Lesson 158: Teacher to complete: ____________ (Speaking 30)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 158, M40 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Speaking 30: teacher to select speaking materials based on pupils' needs; unused Pupil Book or Workbook permitted  
**Learning objective:** Narrate a personal experience for 60–90 seconds and respond to 2 audience questions.  
**PBD evidence:** Time/structure checklist, complete narration and 2 follow-up responses.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show teacher-selected topic visuals and ask: “What can you say about this topic?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model an appropriate experience story with opening, sequence and ending.  
**Pupils:** Prepare a 4-point speaking note rather than a full script.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a pair rehearsal with prompts on who/where/when/what happened.  
**Pupils:** Narrate the experience for 60–90 seconds, using picture prompts if needed.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two follow-up questions per speaker and give focused feedback. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Answer two partner questions and improve one part of the account. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What can you say about this topic?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
