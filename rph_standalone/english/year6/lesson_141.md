# English Year 6 — Lesson 141: What’s your opinion? (Listening 27)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 141, M36 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.2/2.2.2  
**Language focus:** Narrative forms (review)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Teacher-selected short stories; picture and story matching worksheet; poster materials; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Listen to short stories from different cultures, recognise three plot details and plan an illustrated poster or acted scene for Lesson 142.  
**PBD evidence:** Three understood story facts (Listening 1.2.3), an accurate overall message (Listening 1.2.1), and a documented project plan.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to recall a story from another culture.  
**Pupils:** Describe one character or moral in a known story.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide title/picture worksheets for approved short spoken stories.  
**Pupils:** Predict which pictures match the story titles.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read or play each approved story, stopping for supported answer checks.  
**Pupils:** Listen and match sentences to the correct story, noting three events.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide groups to select Task A illustrated poster or Task B a short acted scene.  
**Pupils:** Plan project steps and write a first version of their poster text or dialogue for Lesson 142.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check comprehension of the three stories and the usefulness of the plan.  
**Pupils:** Explain a moral and revise an unclear project step.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the SOW mime guessing game.  
**Pupils:** Act one scene and guess another group's story.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
