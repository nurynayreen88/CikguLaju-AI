# English Year 6 — Lesson 025: Life in the past (Speaking 5)

**Year:** 6
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 25, M07 (week indicative only)
**Main CS/LS:** 2.2/2.2.2
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4
**Language focus:** Future forms: will/won't for predictions; going to and present continuous for plans
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Teacher-selected futuristic-classroom pictures; poster paper, pencils, pens, dictionaries; optional supervised online research. Non-textbook SOW activity, not Poetry Anthology.
**Learning objective:** In groups, pupils agree at least four clear steps and group roles to design a classroom-of-the-future poster, then explain two future plans or predictions to the class.
**PBD evidence:** Four agreed group steps and assigned roles; clearly labelled future-classroom poster; each pupil contributes one intelligible future-plan sentence and answers or asks a question.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review will and won't using the SOW Stand up/Sit down prediction game about future schools; contrast a prediction with a plan.
**Pupils:** Stand or sit to show agreement; supply one sentence with will or going to.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show futuristic classroom pictures and invite ideas; assign groups of four and model negotiating responsibilities.
**Pupils:** Discuss the images and agree four project steps and roles: designer, writer, ideas researcher and presenter.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide poster paper, explain how to draft and check labels; monitor cooperative English conversation and safe research.
**Pupils:** Draft and make a labelled poster showing a classroom of the future; use a dictionary and practise two future-plan statements.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Arrange group presentations and require at least one audience question or comment per group; assess every pupil's contribution.
**Pupils:** Present the labelled poster, answer one question and listen respectfully to other presentations.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess observable lesson products and individual contributions against the stated measurable learning objective; give one actionable correction.
**Pupils:** Submit evidence and revise one specific error after feedback.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide feedback on content, language and teamwork, inviting one specific peer suggestion.
**Pupils:** State one useful improvement and one future classroom prediction.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture cards, word banks, structured roles and extra time. Extension: independently justify an imaginative choice with precise evidence.
