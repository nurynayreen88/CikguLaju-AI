# English Year 6 — Lesson 023: Life in the past (Writing 5)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 23, M06 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.1  
**Language focus:** Informal written language (e.g. Yuck!; Ha! Ha! Ha!)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.28–29; TB p.52  
**Learning objective:** Plan and write an understandable informal diary entry about a school or family trip, using a sequence of events, at least two informal expressions and accurate punctuation.  
**PBD evidence:** A sequenced diary entry on a trip (Writing 4.2.3), with correctly used exclamation marks/brackets or other punctuation (Writing 4.3.1).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall Gemma's diary from PB p.28 and ask whether its voice is formal or informal.  
**Pupils:** Name one informal expression and one punctuation mark seen in the diary.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.52 Activity 4: analyse PB p.28 informal writing, exclamation marks and explanatory brackets.  
**Pupils:** Find two informal expressions and explain how one punctuation mark changes the reader's impression.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.52 Activity 5; pairs plan a real or imagined school trip, using notes about sequence and reactions.  
**Pupils:** Make a three-part diary plan with a setting, an event and a reaction; discuss suitable informal words.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB p.29 Activity 6/TB p.52 to draft the trip diary and peer-check readability, events and punctuation.  
**Pupils:** Write a diary entry with ordered events, an informal reaction and appropriately placed exclamation marks; revise one error.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess story order, clarity and two correctly punctuated informal expressions; provide brief individual writing feedback.  
**Pupils:** Submit the diary and show one corrected punctuation or grammar choice.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.52 Learning to Learn; ask pupils which strategy helped them write.  
**Pupils:** Explain one useful planning or revising strategy for their next diary.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
