# English Year 6 — Lesson 021: The Sandwich (Language Arts 4)

**Year:** 6
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 21, M06 (week indicative only)
**Main CS/LS:** 5.3/5.3.1
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.1
**Language focus:** First, Next, After that, Finally; capital letters, full stops and commas in lists
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** *The Sandwich* by Tony Bradman, Anthology of Poems Years 4–6 pp.28–29; official SOW Appendix 1 Flow Map; sandwich-step cards; optional school-approved demonstration materials
**Learning objective:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can place at least five steps for making a sandwich in a logical flow map and write the sequence with suitable capitals, full stops and commas in a list.
**PBD evidence:** Five-step flow map, individual sequence sentences with capitals/full stops and correctly punctuated comma lists; teacher checklist records imaginative response and punctuation accuracy.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Conduct the official SOW Memory Chain pre-lesson task with sandwich ingredients.
**Pupils:** Repeat and extend the ingredient sequence while listening carefully.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute the official Flow Map and demonstrate sandwich-making steps, with an approved video or picture cards.
**Pupils:** Predict and sequence steps using the given flow-map template.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model how to complete five steps and use sequencing adverbs with correct capitals, commas and full stops.
**Pupils:** Complete a group Flow Map, then independently write at least five step sentences using punctuation accurately.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide safe real or simulated sandwich preparation; avoid food handling when school rules or allergies make it unsuitable.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the steps and peer-review their sequence and punctuation.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check order of five steps and independent punctuation, including one comma list; provide one correction.
**Pupils:** Revise one punctuation error and identify one imaginative detail.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Conduct the official SOW gallery walk of Flow Maps, asking groups to explain their order.
**Pupils:** Present their Flow Map with First, Next, After that and Finally.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: pictures, numbered step cards and sentence frames. Extension: write a short clear recipe with varied conjunctions.
