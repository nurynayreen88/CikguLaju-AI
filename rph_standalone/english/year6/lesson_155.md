# English Year 6 — Lesson 155: Teacher to complete: ____________ (Reading 30)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 155, M39 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Reading 30: teacher to select reading materials based on pupils' needs; unused Pupil Book or Workbook permitted  
**Learning objective:** Read a teacher-selected 180–250-word text; state the main idea and answer 5 detail questions with evidence.  
**PBD evidence:** One main idea and 5 detail answers supported by evidence; no fixed official LS selected.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show teacher-selected topic visuals and ask: “What can you say about this topic?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Choose a level-appropriate 180–250-word text and pre-teach five words without giving answers.  
**Pupils:** Predict the text’s topic and use headings to activate relevant knowledge.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a think-aloud for identifying gist, then set five text-dependent detail questions.  
**Pupils:** Read independently; write a one-sentence main idea and locate details to answer five questions.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use peer-check followed by evidence checking; differentiate based on observed reading needs. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Underline supporting lines, revise responses and explain one answer. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What can you say about this topic?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
