# English Year 6 — Lesson 121: Tell me a story (Speaking 23)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 121, M31 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.2  
**Language focus:** 1st conditional if clauses (If + present, will / might / may / could + vb: e.g. If I go to Japan, I might visit…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Future prediction picture cards; teacher-prepared materials  
**Learning objective:** Give a 45–60 second spoken response on a short story with at least 3 accurate details and respond to one follow-up question.  
**PBD evidence:** Oral checklist: intelligibility, 3 relevant details, two exchanges and one follow-up response.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story sequence panels and ask: “What makes this story interesting?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model question–answer exchanges on “What makes this story interesting?”, drawing on 1st conditional if clauses (If + present, will / might / may / could + vb: e.g. If I go to Japan,….  
**Pupils:** Listen, rehearse 2 complete sentence frames and practise pronunciation with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Present a role-play or information-gap task using story sequence panels; circulate and prompt elaboration.  
**Pupils:** Interview a partner or role-play the situation; ask at least 2 questions and give 3 relevant details.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise 45–60 second pair-sharing and note clarity, vocabulary and response to questions.  
**Pupils:** Present one short response, listen to a peer, and answer one question.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What makes this story interesting?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
