# English Year 6 — Lesson 118: Tell me a story (Reading 23)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 118, M30 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Reading assessment prepared by teacher  
**Learning objective:** Complete an individual SOW reading-assessment practice task, justify two answers from the passage, and identify one reading strategy for improvement.  
**PBD evidence:** Evidence-based answers and recorded reading-comprehension strengths or needs (Reading 3.2.2/3.2.1), used formatively, not as an invented official UASA score.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review reading-task types and explain the classroom's practice purpose.  
**Pupils:** Name one useful strategy for a difficult reading question.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the supplied reading assessment and simple success criteria.  
**Pupils:** Read instructions and preview the text and questions silently.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give adequate individual time to read and complete the provided task without prompting answers.  
**Pupils:** Answer the practice reading assessment independently.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Where time permits, guide reflective checking of two answers and text evidence.  
**Pupils:** Find two supporting clues and revisit one unsupported selection.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record formative results and common reading difficulties; avoid fictional grades.  
**Pupils:** Identify one successful reading strategy and one skill to improve.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** End with an active low-stakes word or gist game.  
**Pupils:** Share one reading approach they will apply next time.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
