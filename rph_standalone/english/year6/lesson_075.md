# English Year 6 — Lesson 075: Getting around (Reading 15)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 75, M19 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.5  
**Language focus:** Cohesive devices; Modal verbs for rules & obligation: have to and need to  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.69; TB pp.112  
**Learning objective:** Read the PB p.69 game puzzle and write four sequential instructions using suitable linkers and have to/need to.  
**PBD evidence:** Four supported puzzle steps (Reading 3.2.2) and four readable linked instructions (Writing 4.2.5).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a game-themed warm-up from the SOW.  
**Pupils:** Name a familiar video game and one instruction.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.112 Activities 1–2 to introduce the PB p.69 puzzle.  
**Pupils:** Read the description and identify the problem.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activity 3 and model ordering the puzzle solution.  
**Pupils:** Explain four steps with first, next, then and finally.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to describe a game they both know.  
**Pupils:** Write and peer-check four instructions using need to/have to.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess correct order and clear required actions.  
**Pupils:** Correct one step using reading evidence.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to recommend their game.  
**Pupils:** Share one step and justify its order.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
