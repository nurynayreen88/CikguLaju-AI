# English Year 6 — Lesson 129: What’s your opinion? (Reading 25)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 129, M33 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.3  
**Language focus:** Language for agreeing, disagreeing and giving opinion (e.g. That’s true! / You’re so right!)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 108-109; Teacher Book pages 170-171  
**Learning objective:** Read PB pp.108–109 Sugar, Sugar for its main message, infer four unfamiliar words and evaluate one fact about sugary foods using text evidence.  
**PBD evidence:** An accurate gist statement (Reading 3.2.1) with four source-backed word inferences and a relevant health-related response.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.170 Food review and ask where hidden sugar might be found.  
**Pupils:** Name two foods with high sugar content and predict their effect.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview Sugar, Sugar and the TB p.170 pre-reading questions.  
**Pupils:** Predict whether the reading encourages or warns against added sugar.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.170 Activity 2; provide time to read for overall meaning.  
**Pupils:** Explain the main idea and locate one surprising fact.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.171 Activity 3 and Memorising; guide context clues before dictionary checks.  
**Pupils:** Infer four words and make two short sentences using new vocabulary.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check whether reading claims are supported; avoid adding unsourced health statistics.  
**Pupils:** Show the text sentence supporting one answer and correct a weak inference.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a short healthy-food discussion to reflect on choices.  
**Pupils:** Name one change that could reduce excess sugar.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
