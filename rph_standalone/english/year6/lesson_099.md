# English Year 6 — Lesson 099: Music and Song (Listening 19)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 99, M25 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** Present simple for future meaning  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 84, 86; Teacher Book pages 138-139; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Listen to a PB p.86 concert-travel dialogue, extract four schedule details and explain one planned future activity using present simple for timetables.  
**PBD evidence:** Four accurately heard schedule facts (Listening 1.2.2) and an understandable future plan (Speaking 2.1.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit PB p.84 questions and ask whether pupils have been to a concert.  
**Pupils:** Name one type of live music event.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce TB p.138 vocabulary and show the concert-trip picture in PB p.86.  
**Pupils:** Predict one time or transport detail from the image.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use verified Audio 2.2 or accurate teacher delivery of the printed script with the SOW MCQ worksheet.  
**Pupils:** Listen with books closed, select answers and note four timetable details.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.138 Activities 2–3; contrast 'The bus leaves at 6.30' with present-time facts.  
**Pupils:** Practise a dialogue using present simple for fixed future schedules and present one personal plan.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the heard details and whether the time refers to a scheduled future event.  
**Pupils:** Correct one incorrect time and rephrase one future-plan statement.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.139 Cooler Itinerary.  
**Pupils:** Describe one departure and arrival fact from an itinerary.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
