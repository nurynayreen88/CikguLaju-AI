# English Year 6 — Lesson 001: Welcome (Reading 1)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 1, M01 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.4  
**Language focus:** Present simple to describe personality (e.g. I’m kind Sara.) and expectations (e.g. This year I want to…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.4–5; TB p.18  
**Learning objective:** Read the four self-introductions in PB pp.4–5, explain which child they identify with using a text clue, and write at least two accurate sentences introducing themselves.  
**PBD evidence:** Identify one child's main idea with a relevant detail from the text/audio script; write two understandable self-introduction sentences (Reading 3.2.1 / Writing 4.2.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review personality, family and hobby words with name cards, then model one personality adjective.  
**Pupils:** Say a name and adjective and recall one word about family, school or free time.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow the PB p.4 big-picture warm-up; ask where the children are and have pupils read the four short texts before deciding which child they agree with most.  
**Pupils:** Read the four introductions, locate an idea they agree with and explain their choice to a partner.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB p.5 Activity 1 and TB p.18: use a verified recording, or teacher read-aloud with the supplied script, while pupils read the four children's words; ask for each child's key idea.  
**Pupils:** Read and listen to the four self-introductions, match each child to one key idea and justify one answer from the script.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model how to use a personality adjective plus two personal notes to introduce oneself, then organise introductions in new groups.  
**Pupils:** Write two self-introduction sentences about personality or hopes for this year and read them to new group members.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check one independently selected child's main idea and two self-written descriptive sentences, noting reading evidence separately from writing evidence.  
**Pupils:** Show the text clue for one child's idea and revise one personal sentence after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a volunteer to introduce themselves and say which child's view they share.  
**Pupils:** Introduce themselves with two complete sentences and name one idea from the PB reading.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
