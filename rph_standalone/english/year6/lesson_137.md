# English Year 6 — Lesson 137: What’s your opinion? (Speaking 26)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 137, M35 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.2/2.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Language focus:** Functional language: giving opinion (e.g. I don’t believe that…), checking understanding, e.g. using reported speech (e.g. You said ….., didn’t you?)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Teacher-prepared debate notes worksheet and speaking contribution cards  
**Learning objective:** Take part in a three-stage group debate with clear For/Against arguments, notes about opposing views and two clarification questions.  
**PBD evidence:** Three purposeful communication checks in a debate (Speaking 2.2.1) and evidence of respectful collaboration.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review multi-tasking, uniforms, advertising and sugar as debate topics.  
**Pupils:** Say one opinion on a familiar issue.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the For/Against worksheet and let groups choose a specific motion.  
**Pupils:** Record at least two supporting and two opposing arguments.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign positions in groups of four; rehearse using notes rather than memorising a script.  
**Pupils:** Prepare an argument even when it differs from personal preference.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Run the SOW three-stage debate: For speaks, Against speaks, then open discussion.  
**Pupils:** Present arguments, listen and ask two clarification or reported-speech questions.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check that each group records counterarguments and responds respectfully.  
**Pupils:** Improve one unclear argument and correct a reported opinion.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask groups what changed after hearing the other side.  
**Pupils:** Explain one idea learned from opposing arguments.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
