# English Year 6 — Lesson 126: Tell me a story (Speaking 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 126, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Language focus:** Language to talk about stories (e.g. It’s about… / It takes place in… / The moral of the story is…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book page 104; Teacher Book page 165  
**Learning objective:** Narrate a short event about a short story with a clear sequence of 3 events and an appropriate ending.  
**PBD evidence:** Three ordered events and a clear concluding sentence in an intelligible narrative.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story sequence panels and ask: “What makes this story interesting?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model question–answer exchanges on “What makes this story interesting?”, drawing on Language to talk about stories (e.g. It’s about… / It takes place in… / The moral of the story is…).  
**Pupils:** Listen, rehearse 2 complete sentence frames and practise pronunciation with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide three sequential picture prompts and model time connectives from the lesson.  
**Pupils:** Narrate three linked events in order; add one descriptive detail and an ending.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise 45–60 second pair-sharing and note clarity, vocabulary and response to questions. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Present one short response, listen to a peer, and answer one question. Listen to a partner or a short approved utterance and identify one meaningful detail.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What makes this story interesting?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
