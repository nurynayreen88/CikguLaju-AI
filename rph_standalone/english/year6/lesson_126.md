# English Year 6 — Lesson 126: Tell me a story (Speaking 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 126, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.3/2.3.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.5  
**Language focus:** Language to talk about stories (e.g. It’s about… / It takes place in… / The moral of the story is…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book page 104; Teacher Book page 165  
**Learning objective:** Revise the original Lesson 124 book review and present the book in a short unscripted group talk with two follow-up questions.  
**PBD evidence:** A clear three-event oral story presentation (Speaking 2.3.1), with purposeful follow-up questions from peers.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review book genres and show the original reviews from Lesson 124.  
**Pupils:** Recall a character or turning point in their chosen book.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Return the first-draft review and model one correction; follow TB p.165 Activity 3.  
**Pupils:** Identify an unclear section and improve the review before presenting.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give time to plan a book talk using notes rather than a memorised script.  
**Pupils:** Prepare a brief setting, three-event plot and an opinion.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.165 Activity 4: group presentations with the physical book when available.  
**Pupils:** Present a short story summary and ask two questions about other pupils' books.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess a coherent narrative, two understood questions and the draft's meaningful corrections.  
**Pupils:** Answer a follow-up question and improve one spoken explanation.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each group which book they would most like to read and why.  
**Pupils:** Recommend a peer's book with a text-supported reason.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
