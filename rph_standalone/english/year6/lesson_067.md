# English Year 6 — Lesson 067: Getting around (Listening 13)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 67, M17 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.1/1.1.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Comparative forms: (just/not) as … as; weak sound (schwa) /ə/  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.62–63; TB pp.100, 102; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Recognise and produce four weak /ə/ sounds in as...as and related comparison phrases, then complete PB p.63 sentences.  
**PBD evidence:** Four identified weak syllables and intelligible examples (Listening 1.1.1), with two reading-based comparison answers (Reading 3.2.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.102 Secret words for transport vocabulary.  
**Pupils:** Name transport words and one useful adjective.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review -ful/-less and comparatives in TB p.100.  
**Pupils:** Form two adjectives and one as...as comparison.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.102 Activity 1 with approved audio or accurate read-aloud.  
**Pupils:** Identify the reduced /ə/ sound in four worksheet phrases.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.102 word-card game and Activity 2.  
**Pupils:** Complete comparison gaps and listen for weak sounds in partner reading.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess four marked syllables and clear natural pronunciation.  
**Pupils:** Repeat two phrases and correct one over-stressed word.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review a weak-sound phrase using a comparison.  
**Pupils:** Say one fluent as...as sentence.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
