# English Year 6 — Lesson 128: Tell me a story (Language Awareness 7)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 128, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 106-107; Teacher Book pages 168-169; unit review sheet and self-assessment checklist  
**Learning objective:** Read a teacher-selected short story, identify its main idea and four details, then write at least two connected sentences for an alternative ending.
**PBD evidence:** A main-idea statement, four answers with text support and a two-sentence alternative ending logically linked to the original events.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display three shuffled story-sequence pictures and ask pupils to predict which scene might be first.
**Pupils:** Order the pictures and justify one prediction using a visual clue.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use PB pp.106–107 and TB pp.168–169 as reference pages; guide a teacher-selected story extract to establish character, setting and problem without assuming a particular printed task.
**Pupils:** Find the main characters and setting and predict a possible problem.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the selected story; model locating the main idea, then set four specific questions answerable from the text.
**Pupils:** Write a one-sentence main idea and answer four detail questions with evidence.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to propose a plausible different event or ending; model keeping characters and earlier facts consistent.
**Pupils:** Write at least two connected ending sentences and explain the link to an earlier story event.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the main idea, four details and story-consistent ending; give feedback on one unsupported detail.
**Pupils:** Show a supporting line and revise one sentence for clarity or coherence.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite one alternate ending and ask how it relates to the story's original problem.
**Pupils:** Read one improved ending sentence and name the clue that informed it.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
