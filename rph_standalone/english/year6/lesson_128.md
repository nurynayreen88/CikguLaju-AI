# English Year 6 — Lesson 128: Tell me a story (Language Awareness 7)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 128, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 106-107; Teacher Book pages 168-169; unit review sheet and self-assessment checklist  
**Learning objective:** Consolidate a short story through a unit review and demonstrate understanding in at least 4 accurate responses.  
**PBD evidence:** Read a short age-appropriate text on 'Tell me a story'; identify one main idea and answer 4 detail questions.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story sequence panels and ask: “What makes this story interesting?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit common errors in the unit; introduce the review materials specified in the RPT where available.  
**Pupils:** Recall two familiar expressions and correct one example collaboratively.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the proposed unit revision task: Pupils read a story and identify the main idea, then write an alternative event or ending and share it.  
**Pupils:** Complete the review task and discuss or compare answers using unit vocabulary.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a short self-assessment checklist; reteach one difficult item and guide correction.  
**Pupils:** Correct two errors, show completed review evidence and state one next learning goal.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What makes this story interesting?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
