# English Year 6 — Lesson 057: Cool jobs (Speaking 11)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 57, M15 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2  
**Language focus:** Jobs (e.g. lawyer), and related vocabulary (e.g. help; work)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB pp.53; TB pp.89; WB pp.43  
**Learning objective:** Explain three future job preferences with reasons and suitable personality qualities, then write two accurately spelled sentences about a chosen job.  
**PBD evidence:** Three oral job choices with supporting reasons (Speaking 2.1.1) and two accurately spelled written job statements (Writing 4.3.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review jobs with the previous lesson's spelling game.  
**Pupils:** Write two correctly spelled job names.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.89 Activity 5; invite pairs to discuss Jean's work and their top job preferences.  
**Pupils:** Rank four jobs in a notebook and explain the first choice.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask for three preferences, each with a reason and a relevant job personality adjective.  
**Pupils:** Tell a partner three jobs they might like and explain why the qualities fit.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Form groups of pupils sharing a preferred job; guide two- or three-sentence writing about it.  
**Pupils:** Find another pupil with a shared job preference and write two accurate sentences.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check intelligible reasons, job-personality vocabulary and common-word spellings.  
**Pupils:** Read one sentence and correct a spelling or reason after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Sort WB p.43 Activity 1 word cards into make and do expressions.  
**Pupils:** Say one correct make collocation and one do collocation.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
