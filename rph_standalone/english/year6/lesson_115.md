# English Year 6 — Lesson 115: Tell me a story (Listening 22)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 115, M29 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** 1st conditional if clauses (If + present, will + verb: If you rest, you’ll feel better)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 99; Teacher Book pages 156-157; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify at least 5 specific facts from a spoken passage about a short story with little or no support.  
**PBD evidence:** Five correct details from the oral text; note independent/repeated support.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story sequence panels and ask: “What makes this story interesting?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach character, setting, problem, ending; display story sequence panels and ask pupils to predict what they may hear. Emphasise the language focus: 1st conditional if clauses (If + present, will + verb: If you rest, you’ll feel better).  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the cited listening task with a confirmed audio track or an approved teacher read-aloud; give pupils a five-item grid.  
**Pupils:** Listen for details and complete five information questions before paired comparison.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about a short story, and replay/read relevant parts where needed. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases. Give a short oral explanation to a partner and respond to one question.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What makes this story interesting?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
