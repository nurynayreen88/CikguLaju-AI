# English Year 6 — Lesson 115: Tell me a story (Listening 22)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 115, M29 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** 1st conditional if clauses (If + present, will + verb: If you rest, you’ll feel better)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 99; Teacher Book pages 156-157; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify six correct and incorrect first-conditional verbs from a teacher-read PB p.99 text, then give two personal future predictions.  
**PBD evidence:** Six accurately corrected sentences heard from the story-like passage (Listening 1.2.2) and two relevant future plans using if + present, will (Speaking 2.1.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce if + present clauses with a familiar real-life example.  
**Pupils:** Make one simple future prediction with will.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.156 Warm-up Future predictions and PB p.99 Activity 1.  
**Pupils:** Predict two plausible outcomes and spot the if-clause form.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read aloud the six PB p.99 Activity 2 sentences with intentionally correct/incorrect verbs while books are closed.  
**Pupils:** Listen, decide which verb forms sound incorrect and write a corrected paragraph.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB p.157 Activity 3 and let pupils check against the printed PB p.99 text.  
**Pupils:** Circle the correct forms, then compare two future predictions with a partner.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check six verb choices and the meaning of two conditional predictions.  
**Pupils:** Correct one if + present, will combination independently.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.157 Cooler Complete the sentences.  
**Pupils:** Finish one meaningful first-conditional sentence.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
