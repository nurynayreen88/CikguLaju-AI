# English Year 6 — Lesson 151: It’s a mystery! (Writing 29)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 151, M38 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.1  
**Language focus:** Punctuation (direct speech/script: e.g. ‘I’m hungry’, she said / I’m hungry)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 128-129; Teacher Book pages 200, 203; Workbook page 110  
**Learning objective:** Write a coherent a short mystery explanation using at least 5 connected sentences and appropriate language for a classroom mystery.  
**PBD evidence:** Collect the written product; check relevance, 5 connected sentences, target language and revision.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show clue cards and picture sequence and ask: “What clues explain the mystery?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a short response linked to “What clues explain the mystery?”. Highlight useful language: Punctuation (direct speech/script: e.g. ‘I’m hungry’, she said / I’m hungry).  
**Pupils:** Identify the model’s key ideas and rehearse three expressions related to a classroom mystery.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the RPT-referenced Pupil’s Book task as the source or model; demonstrate a 3-point planning frame.  
**Pupils:** Plan a sequence of ideas with topic vocabulary and draft 5–7 sentences for a short mystery explanation.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on content, grammar and punctuation using a concise checklist. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Re-read, revise two points, then share the final writing with a partner. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What clues explain the mystery?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
