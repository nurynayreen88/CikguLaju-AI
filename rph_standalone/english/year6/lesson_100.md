# English Year 6 — Lesson 100: Music and Song (Speaking 19)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 100, M25 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.2  
**Language focus:** Future forms: will and going to  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 85; Teacher Book pages 136-137; Workbook pages 74  
**Learning objective:** Give a 45–60 second spoken response on a school music performance with at least 3 accurate details and respond to one follow-up question.  
**PBD evidence:** Oral checklist: intelligibility, 3 relevant details, two exchanges and one follow-up response.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show instrument and performance pictures and ask: “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model question–answer exchanges on “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?”, drawing on Future forms: will and going to.  
**Pupils:** Listen, rehearse 2 complete sentence frames and practise pronunciation with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Present a role-play or information-gap task using instrument and performance pictures; circulate and prompt elaboration.  
**Pupils:** Interview a partner or role-play the situation; ask at least 2 questions and give 3 relevant details.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise 45–60 second pair-sharing and note clarity, vocabulary and response to questions. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Present one short response, listen to a peer, and answer one question. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
