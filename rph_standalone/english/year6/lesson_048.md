# English Year 6 — Lesson 048: Adventure time (Language Awareness 3)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 48, M12 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Complementary CS/LS:** PILIHAN GURU (SOW)  
**Language focus:** Unit review: vocabulary choices, present perfect experiences and interview questions  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Workbook pp.36–37, Check-up Challenge; Teacher's Book printed p.75. No unverified audio recording is prescribed in this proposed review.  
**Learning objective:** Complete the Workbook pp.36–37 check-up challenge, write two accurate present-perfect experience sentences and record a partner interview.  
**PBD evidence:** Three correct source vocabulary choices, two accurate experience sentences and one written partner question/answer.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit familiar Unit 3 experience words and model one have you ever question as preparation for Workbook review.  
**Pupils:** Recall two words and respond to the model question with a complete answer.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Workbook p.36 Check-up Challenge 'read and circle the correct word', as shown in TB p.75; check one item aloud.  
**Pupils:** Read the source sentences, circle suitable words and discuss one choice.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow the Workbook p.37 task about things pupils have done; model one present-perfect sentence and monitor tense use.  
**Pupils:** Complete the printed exercise and write two additional accurate sentences about experiences.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the Workbook p.37 partner-interview task; elicit a question frame and require written answers, not an invented recording.  
**Pupils:** Interview a partner using have you ever questions and record at least one question-and-answer pair.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check vocabulary choices, two experience sentences and one interview answer against the teacher-selected criteria.  
**Pupils:** Correct one form and explain a chosen answer using the Workbook task.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask for one accurate sentence about a completed experience and the review area pupils need next.  
**Pupils:** Share one sentence and identify an individual practice need.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
