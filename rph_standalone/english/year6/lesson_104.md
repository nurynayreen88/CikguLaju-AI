# English Year 6 — Lesson 104: Music and Song (Listening 20)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 104, M26 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.3/1.3.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Describing music (e.g. catchy)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 87; Teacher Book pages 140; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Infer the meanings of 3 unfamiliar spoken words about a school music performance from known words or sound clues.  
**PBD evidence:** Three inferred word meanings, each with a relevant contextual clue.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show instrument and performance pictures and ask: “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach music genres, instruments, performance; display instrument and performance pictures and ask pupils to predict what they may hear. Emphasise the language focus: Describing music (e.g. catchy).  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Present three unfamiliar words in an approved spoken context and model inference from surrounding familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Infer meanings from listening clues; note each guess and the clue that prompted it.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about a school music performance, and replay/read relevant parts where needed. Check the complementary skill using the short task.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases. Locate one additional supporting clue in the cited reading material.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “Which song or instrument would you choose and why?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
