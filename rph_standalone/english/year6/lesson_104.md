# English Year 6 — Lesson 104: Music and Song (Listening 20)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 104, M26 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.3/1.3.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Describing music (e.g. catchy)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book pages 87; Teacher Book pages 140; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Infer three unfamiliar spoken music words from PB p.87 listening and choose two accurate contextual reading responses from a dialogue worksheet.  
**PBD evidence:** Three plausible word-in-context inferences (Listening 1.3.1), plus two supported reading choices in the dialogue task (Reading 3.2.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review school concert and song vocabulary without claiming an unverified recording.  
**Pupils:** Predict one kind of music discussed in a radio programme.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.140 Activities 1–2; display the PB p.87 vocabulary box.  
**Pupils:** Predict listening content and identify clues about concert choices.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a verified source or accurate teacher reading; focus on the spoken words and Activity 3 gapped items.  
**Pupils:** Infer three meanings from surrounding known words, then compare with the PB p.140 dictionary.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute the SOW dialogue-reading worksheet and model how options fit the context before and after a gap.  
**Pupils:** Complete two reading choices independently and check both sides of each dialogue line.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess three audio-context inferences and two supported dialogue answers.  
**Pupils:** Explain one clue and revise an incorrect choice.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask which of the described songs sounds most appealing and why.  
**Pupils:** Express one preference grounded in the listening content.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
