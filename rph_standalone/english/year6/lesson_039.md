# English Year 6 — Lesson 039: Adventure time (Writing 8)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 39, M10 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.2/4.2.3  
**Language focus:** Past simple (e.g. I went skiing) & present perfect (e.g. the best I’ve ever done)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.41; TB p.70  
**Learning objective:** Write a coherent a short adventure account using at least 5 connected sentences and appropriate language for an outdoor adventure.  
**PBD evidence:** Collect the written product; check relevance, 5 connected sentences, target language and revision.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show adventure scene cards and ask: “Which adventure would you try and why?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a short response linked to “Which adventure would you try and why?”. Highlight useful language: Past simple (e.g. I went skiing) & present perfect (e.g. the best I’ve ever done).  
**Pupils:** Identify the model’s key ideas and rehearse three expressions related to an outdoor adventure.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the RPT-referenced Pupil’s Book task as the source or model; demonstrate a 3-point planning frame.  
**Pupils:** Plan a sequence of ideas with topic vocabulary and draft 5–7 sentences for a short adventure account.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on content, grammar and punctuation using a concise checklist.  
**Pupils:** Re-read, revise two points, then share the final writing with a partner.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “Which adventure would you try and why?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
