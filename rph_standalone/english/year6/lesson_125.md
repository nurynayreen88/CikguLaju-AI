# English Year 6 — Lesson 125: Tell me a story (Listening 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 125, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.1  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Listening assessment prepared by teacher; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Complete a formative listening-assessment practice task independently, check two responses against the recording or teacher script, and identify one strategy to improve.  
**PBD evidence:** Source-grounded comprehension responses for Listening 1.2.2, recorded formatively without invented exam grades.  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the format of the listening practice and review useful strategies.  
**Pupils:** Predict which details are likely to appear in an assessment-style task.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute the approved listening worksheet and explain the simple classroom criteria.  
**Pupils:** Read the questions silently and identify key information to listen for.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a verified assessment recording or approved text read aloud; do not claim unverified track identity.  
**Pupils:** Listen individually and record answers to the provided questions.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** If time allows, provide a supported replay and guide evidence-based corrections.  
**Pupils:** Check two answers and correct one detail after hearing it again.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record formative listening strengths and areas to reteach without generating official scores.  
**Pupils:** Name one listening strategy and a personal improvement goal.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use a short active vocabulary game and remind pupils to bring the Lesson 124 book reviews.  
**Pupils:** Recall one helpful listening tip and prepare their review for Lesson 126.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
