# English Year 6 — Lesson 125: Tell me a story (Listening 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 125, M32 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.2  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 1.2/1.2.1  
**Language focus:** relevant vocabulary and language from this unit  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Listening assessment prepared by teacher; visuals / approved read-aloud script (audio track only if verified)  
**Learning objective:** Identify the main idea and at least 4 specific details from spoken language about a short story.  
**PBD evidence:** Listening grid with one gist item plus 4 detail items; record independent / repeated support / reteach.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story sequence panels and ask: “What makes this story interesting?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pre-teach character, setting, problem, ending; display story sequence panels and ask pupils to predict what they may hear.  
**Pupils:** Predict two likely words and say why they match the context.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the lesson audio only if its track is verified; otherwise read aloud the approved passage from the cited material twice. First listen for gist, second for details.  
**Pupils:** Listen without writing first, then note the main idea and four specific facts on a listening grid.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the grid with prompts about a short story, and replay/read relevant parts where needed.  
**Pupils:** Compare answers with a partner, identify one correction and repeat useful key phrases.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What makes this story interesting?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
