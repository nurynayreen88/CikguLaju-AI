# English Year 6 — Lesson 092: How is it made? (Writing 18)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 92, M23 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Language and vocabulary review  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Workbook page 70 activity 1; invention worksheet  
**Learning objective:** Use the WB p.70 ketchup-pen model to plan and draft an invention description; keep the first draft for feedback and improvement in Lesson 94.  
**PBD evidence:** An intelligible invention first draft with a supporting illustration (Writing 4.3.3) and a correct choice of missing words in the model text (Reading 3.2.2).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review an invention that makes a familiar task easier.  
**Pupils:** Name an invention and the job it performs.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the WB p.70 Activity 1 ketchup pen and distribute the SOW cloze worksheet.  
**Pupils:** Describe the object and choose the best words to complete the model.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display other inventions and elicit descriptive words, materials and uses.  
**Pupils:** Select or invent a product, make brief notes and draft a paragraph.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to draw their invention, then collect the original first draft for correction coding.  
**Pupils:** Add a labelled picture and submit the writing without prematurely rewriting it.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review errors using the correction code, keeping notes for Lesson 94.  
**Pupils:** Identify one weakness to address when the draft is returned.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite an oral description of one invention's benefit.  
**Pupils:** Explain what the invention does and who might use it.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
