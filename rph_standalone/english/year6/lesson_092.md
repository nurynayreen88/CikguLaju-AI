# English Year 6 — Lesson 092: How is it made? (Writing 18)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 92, M23 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.3  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.2  
**Language focus:** Language and vocabulary review  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Workbook page 70 activity 1; invention worksheet  
**Learning objective:** Produce, check and improve a draft of two paragraphs on making a familiar product, making at least two meaningful revisions.  
**PBD evidence:** Plan, two-paragraph draft and two identifiable improvements.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four process-stage pictures and ask: “How is this useful item made?”  
**Pupils:** Offer one prediction or personal example using relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a short response linked to “How is this useful item made?”. Highlight useful language: Language and vocabulary review.  
**Pupils:** Identify the model’s key ideas and rehearse three expressions related to making a familiar product.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate how to plan and draft two related paragraphs and revise ideas for clarity.  
**Pupils:** Create a two-paragraph first draft, then highlight two revisions addressing clarity or organisation.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on content, grammar and punctuation using a concise checklist. Check the complementary skill using the short task. Incorporate only the Workbook page actually cited in the RPT, after checking printed instructions.  
**Pupils:** Re-read, revise two points, then share the final writing with a partner. Locate one additional supporting clue in the cited reading material. Complete the relevant Workbook task from the cited page, if assigned.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the lesson evidence against the target and give one actionable correction.  
**Pupils:** Show the evidence, correct a response and complete an exit check.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “How is this useful item made?” and collect an exit response.  
**Pupils:** Share one accurate new sentence or fact; identify one skill to practise next.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
