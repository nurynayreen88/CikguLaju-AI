# English Year 6 — Lesson 015: The Sandwich by Tony Bradman (Language Arts 3)

**Year:** 6
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 15, M04 (week indicative only)
**Main CS/LS:** 5.3/5.3.1
**Complementary CS/LS:** 4.3/4.3.2
**Language focus:** Ingredient nouns; spelling of high-frequency words
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems Years 4–6, *The Sandwich* by Tony Bradman pp.28–29; official SOW Appendix 1 *The Silly Sandwich* template; teacher-approved song or original chant; paper and crayons
**Learning objective:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can create an imaginative Silly Sandwich by listing 10 ingredients, spell at least 8 familiar ingredient words correctly and explain two imaginative choices.
**PBD evidence:** SOW Appendix 1 Silly Sandwich list with 10 ingredients; teacher ticks 8/10 correctly spelled familiar words, illustration, imaginative response and two verbal reasons.

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce food vocabulary and a short original chant or approved recording, then display the poem title.
**Pupils:** Predict ingredients from the title and share one example.

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to recite the poem from the Anthology and locate ingredients named in the literary text.
**Pupils:** Recite with expression and identify words used for ingredients.

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the official SOW Silly Sandwich template and model the spelling and meaning of a familiar ingredient.
**Pupils:** List 10 imaginative ingredients of their choice; check the spelling of at least eight familiar words.

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide drawing materials and guide pupils to illustrate their imagined sandwich with meaningful labels.
**Pupils:** Draw and label their Silly Sandwich, share two creative choices with a partner, and revise spelling.

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the 10-ingredient list, spellings and creative response against the stated criteria.
**Pupils:** Point to two text or ingredient links and correct one misspelling.

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite short presentations and summarise strategies for checking spelling.
**Pupils:** Explain two ingredient choices and state one spelling improvement.

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: ingredient pictures, spelling bank and additional time. Extension: write two sentences giving reasons for unusual choices.
