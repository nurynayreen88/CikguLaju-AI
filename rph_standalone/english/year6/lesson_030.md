# English Year 6 — Lesson 030: Life in the past (Speaking 6)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 30, M08 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 2.2/2.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.4  
**Language focus:** Review of past and future forms; Functional language – checking understanding (e.g. Can I check something, please?)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Y6 PB p.30  
**Learning objective:** In a museum-guide role-play, use at least three checking-understanding phrases to compare past, present and future, then explain a future perspective to peers.  
**PBD evidence:** Three purposeful confirmation/clarification questions in a sustained conversation (Speaking 2.2.1), plus one relevant explanation of future plans or predictions (Speaking 2.1.4).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the previous lesson's three future themes and one past-life difference.  
**Pupils:** Recall one change from past to present and one possible future development.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow PB p.30 functional-language box: copy and rehearse phrases such as 'Can I check something, please?' and 'Do you mean ...?'  
**Pupils:** Practise three clarification phrases and identify when each helps a listener.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the SOW past–present–future worksheet and TB p.54 Activity 2; assign half the class as present-day museum guides and half as future museum guides.  
**Pupils:** Fill the three-column time table and role-play a museum visit, using each checking phrase at least once.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB p.55 Activity 3: introduce presentism and ask what future visitors might find strange about life today.  
**Pupils:** Exchange one idea about today's life seen from the future, ask a clarifying question and justify the answer.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each role-play for three appropriate communication checks and meaningful responses; correct one unclear exchange.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate three clarification attempts and improve one answer after feedback.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask one museum guide to give a memorable comparison drawn from the discussion.  
**Pupils:** Explain one difference between past, present and future using a checking phrase.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
