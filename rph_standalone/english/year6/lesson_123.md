# English Year 6 — Lesson 123: Tell me a story (Reading 24)

**Year:** 6  
**RPT:** 2027, Lesson 123, M31 (week indicative only)  
**Main CS/LS:** 3.2/3.2.1  
**Complementary CS/LS:** 2.1/2.1.3  
**Language focus:** Genres of literature (e.g. science fiction, adventure); Functional language: Giving advice (I think you should…)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Academy Stars Year 6 Pupil Book page 105; Teacher Book page 166  
**Learning objective:** Read PB p.105 book genres, compare favourite book types, and recommend a personally chosen book with two source-supported reasons.  
**PBD evidence:** One main idea from the book descriptions (Reading 3.2.1) and two relevant spoken recommendations (Speaking 2.1.3).  

## Set induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to show a book they brought or name a favourite title.  
**Pupils:** Describe one book genre and a character or setting.  

## Activity 1 — Prepare (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.166 Activities 1–2: match book covers to genres and read descriptions.  
**Pupils:** Identify the genres and justify one choice from a printed clue.  

## Activity 2 — Guided core task (18 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB p.166 Activity 3; listen to model book preferences using approved audio or accurate teacher reading.  
**Pupils:** Select a book they might like and notice useful recommendation language.  

## Activity 3 — Apply and share (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to plan notes about their own book, moral, genre and plot without scripting whole sentences.  
**Pupils:** Tell a partner the book's story, suggest whether they should read it and give two reasons.  

## Reinforcement / PBD (8 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess gist comprehension and appropriate personal book advice.  
**Pupils:** Correct one unclear recommendation and explain a text feature.  

## Closure (4 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a short, voluntary peer book recommendation.  
**Pupils:** Offer to share a book responsibly and explain why it is worth reading.  

## Differentiation and follow-up
Support: picture/word bank, guided modelling and extra processing time. Extension: justify an answer independently or add precise supporting detail.
