# English Year 3 — Lesson 98: Speaking

**Unit:** Out and About
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.65; Teacher's Book pp.102–103; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays Simon Says with directions and 'please'.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils listen to the directions song and mime movements.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher guides the direction team game. Pupils give and follow simple spoken directions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks clarity of directions and listening comprehension.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils give two directions.
