# English Year 3 — Lesson 116: Writing

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM SOW Lesson 116; SB pp.76–77.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews adjectives from Lesson 115.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Groups receive an adjective card and draw or write associations with that word.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils read short examples, then write their own sentences using adjectives and check spelling.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks spelling and reading details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils read one adjective sentence.
