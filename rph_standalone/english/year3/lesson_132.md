# English Year 3 — Lesson 132: Writing

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart Plus 3 Student's Book p.87; Teacher's Book pp.132–133; Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher revisits the forest-trip story. Pupils recall the sequence of events.

## Activity 1 — Past tense (15 minutes)
Teacher introduces past verb forms from the Grammar Box. Pupils identify examples about the weekend.

## Activity 2 — Writing practice (25 minutes)
Teacher guides the writing activity on page 87. Pupils write sentences about past holiday activities and check their work with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks spelling and understanding of the reading prompts.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one sentence using a past-tense verb.
