# English Year 3 — Lesson 159: Language Arts

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** Language Arts 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book pp.102–103; Teacher's Book pp.154–155; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask about familiar fairy stories and show a story picture.
**Pupils:** Name a character and predict what might happen.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the princess and model adjectives for describing characters.
**Pupils:** Suggest adjectives and describe a character.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide picture predictions, reading on Student's Book pp.102–103 and story listening; prompt discussion of values.
**Pupils:** Predict the strongest, funniest or fastest characters, read to check, listen to the story and share responses.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each pupil to identify a favourite character or event, give one story-based reason and read one short section independently.
**Pupils:** Explain a favourite character or event with one supporting reason.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a short reflection.
**Pupils:** Share a favourite event and one lesson learned.
