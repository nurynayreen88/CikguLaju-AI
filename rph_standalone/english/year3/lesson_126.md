# English Year 3 — Lesson 126: Writing

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.81; TB pp.124–125; diary.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews spellings of past-time vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher shows a diary and introduces Kelly's Saturday entry; pupils read the example and the Writing Tip.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils draft their own short diary entries about a day, then review spelling and punctuation.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks diary drafting and spelling.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one diary sentence.
