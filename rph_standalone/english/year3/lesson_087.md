# English Year 3 — Lesson 87: Speaking

**Unit:** Food, Please!
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.57; Teacher's Book p.91; notebooks and colouring pencils.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews food vocabulary and question forms. Pupils name omelette ingredients.

## Activity 1 — Speaking model (15 minutes)
Teacher guides Activity 4 from Teacher's Book p.91. Pupils ask and answer about ingredients.

## Activity 2 — Find the same omelette (25 minutes)
Pupils draw their own omelette and mingle, asking 'Are there any tomatoes in your omelette?' and similar questions to find classmates with matching ingredients.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (CikguLaju material):** Distribute the original role cards in `materials/lesson_087_speaking_omelette_information_gap.md`; observe pupils asking ingredient questions, answering partners and identifying the hidden omelette card.
**Pupils (CikguLaju material):** Ask at least three ingredient questions, answer their partner and describe the partner's omelette using the new role cards.
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil asking one ingredient question, answering a partner and describing an ingredient.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer about their omelette ingredients.

**Workbook practice:** Pupils complete Workbook p.45, Activity 2, by answering questions about the ingredients in Anna's and Greg's omelettes. Teacher checks answers and provides guidance.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share a question they used to find a partner.
