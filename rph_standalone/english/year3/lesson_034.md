# English Year 3 — Lesson 34: Speaking

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.1 / 1.1.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.25; Teacher's Book pp.46–47; action pictures.
**Learning objective:** Describe a person in an action picture and reproduce the ending sound in two modelled action words.
**Success criteria:** One intelligible picture description and two recognisable -ing endings.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show an action picture with part of it covered.
**Pupils:** Guess the action and name the clue they can see.

## Activity 1 — Pronunciation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Grammar Box on p.25 to model short forms and the final sound in -ing.
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat and practise two action phrases with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Picture descriptions (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Run Group Pantomime (Teacher’s Book p.47), then set a short drawing task for Student’s Book p.25 Activity 2.
**Pupils:** Describe the mime, then show a drawing and tell a partner what the person is doing.
**Workbook:** Follow the lines and write sentences about the sports in Workbook p.18, Activity 2.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen during paired picture descriptions and prompt two modelled action words.
**Pupils:** Give one intelligible description and say the two action words with recognisable -ing endings.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the unit song and cue pupils to show their drawings.
**Pupils:** Hold up the matching drawing as they hear its action.
