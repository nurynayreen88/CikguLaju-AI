# English Year 3 — Lesson 37: Language Arts

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Language Arts 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 37; Student's Book p.26; paper circles, pencils and paper clips.
**Learning objective:** Make a circular book with four household actions and describe two of its pictures orally.
**Success criteria:** Four recognisable action drawings and two intelligible present continuous descriptions.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to recall what Anna and Greg do in the story on Student’s Book p.26.
**Pupils:** Name a story action and suggest another household chore.

## Activity 1 — Draw the actions (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model dividing a paper circle into four and draw one example.
**Pupils:** Draw a different household action in each of the four sections.

## Activity 2 — Assemble and share (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show how to remove a quarter from the top circle and help pupils fasten it over the first circle.
**Pupils:** Rotate the window and take turns showing and describing their pictures in small groups.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check that four actions are recognisable and listen as each pupil shows two pictures.
**Pupils:** Reveal two sections and give an intelligible present continuous description for each.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a few pupils to share a chore they saw in a classmate’s book.
**Pupils:** Name the chore and say who is doing it.
