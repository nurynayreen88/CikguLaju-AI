**Skill:** Reading | **Unit:** Year In, Year Out | **Not an official Workbook/SOW worksheet**

Name: __________________ Class: __________ Date: __________

### Text A — Australia
In Australia, December is in summer. Many families go to the beach when it is hot. Some people swim and play games outside.

### Text B — Canada
In Canada, December is in winter. It can be very cold and snowy. Some children wear coats and gloves when they play outside.

### A. Find the information
Write **Australia** or **Canada**.
1. December is in summer. __________________
2. Children may wear gloves in December. __________________
3. Families may go to the beach in hot weather. __________________
4. December can be snowy. __________________

### B. Find evidence
Underline **one sentence** in each text that helped you answer.

### C. Ask for help
Ask your partner: “Can you help me find the sentence about ______, please?”
