## Describe and Draw a Farm
**Skill:** Speaking | **Unit:** Out and About | **Original supplementary assessment, not official Workbook content**

Name: __________ Partner: __________ Date: __________

### Card A — Describe without showing
“My farm has two cows next to the tree. There is one duck in the pond. There are three chickens near the gate.”

### Card B — Describe without showing
“My farm has one horse near the barn. There are two sheep next to the fence. There is one duck in the pond.”

### Pupil task
Work in pairs. Each pupil takes one card and **keeps it hidden**. Take turns:
1. Describe your farm using full sentences.
2. Listen to your partner and draw the animals in the correct places.
3. Ask one question, e.g. “Where is the duck?”
4. Compare drawings and tell your partner one difference.

### Teacher PBD
Check each pupil for **two clear animal descriptions**, **two correct details drawn from listening**, **one relevant question**, and **one spoken comparison**. Model the task using a different example. No Workbook p.53 Activity 2 is assigned.
