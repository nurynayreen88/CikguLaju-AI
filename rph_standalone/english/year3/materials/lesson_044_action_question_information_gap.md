# CikguLaju Original Material — English Year 3 Lesson 44
## Ask and Guess the Action
**Skill:** Speaking | **Unit:** Right Now | Original CikguLaju task

Name: __________ Partner: __________ Date: __________

### Secret action cards (cut apart)
Card A: A child is swimming.
Card B: Two children are playing football.
Card C: A teacher is writing.
Card D: A child is reading.

### Pair game
Choose one card without showing it. Your partner asks **three** questions such as:
- “Is the person swimming?”
- “Are they playing football?”
- “What is the person doing?”

Answer with **Yes, he/she is**, **No, he/she isn't**, or a full action sentence. Swap roles.

### Teacher PBD
Observe each pupil asking **three understandable questions**, giving **three appropriate responses**, and making **one full present-continuous description**. Model one exchange before the game. This does not reuse Workbook p.21 Activity 2 from Lesson 39.
