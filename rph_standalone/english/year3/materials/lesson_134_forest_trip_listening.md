## Forest Trip: Listen for Facts
**Listening — On Holiday**

Name: ______ Class: ______

Listen to the teacher twice. Circle **True / False**.

1. Bob and Lucy went to a forest. True / False
2. They saw three birds. True / False
3. They had a picnic by a river. True / False
4. They went home in the morning. True / False

Correct one false sentence: ______________________________

**Teacher script (original, not a transcript of Student's Book audio):** “On Saturday, Bob and Lucy visited a forest. They saw two birds in a tree. They had a picnic near a river. They went home in the afternoon.” Read twice.

**Key/PBD:** 1 True; 2 False (two birds); 3 True; 4 False (afternoon). Assess four details and one correction. This is an independent listening assessment, not Workbook p68 Activity 3 from Lesson 130.
