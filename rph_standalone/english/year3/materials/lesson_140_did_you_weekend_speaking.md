# CikguLaju Original Material — English Year 3 Lesson 140
## Did You? Weekend Question Cards

**Unit:** On Holiday | **Main skill:** Speaking | **Original supplementary material, not an official Workbook exercise**

Name: __________ Partner: __________ Date: __________

### A. Cut-out question prompts
- visit a park
- play football
- watch a film
- help at home
- go swimming
- read a book

### B. Ask your partner
Choose **three** different prompts and ask questions in the past tense.

1. Did you __________________________? Answer: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
2. Did you __________________________? Answer: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
3. Did you __________________________? Answer: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

### C. Report
Tell the teacher one answer: “My partner __________.”

### Teacher guidance and PBD
Model **Did you play football?** and the two short answers. Pupils may give imaginary answers if they prefer not to share personal activities. Record each pupil's **three understandable Did you questions**, **three appropriate short answers**, and **one oral report**. Accept simple reports such as “My partner played football” or “My partner didn't play football.” Do not assign Workbook p.71 Activity 1 again; it was used in Lesson 135.
