**Skill:** Speaking | **Unit:** My New House | **Original CikguLaju supplementary material**

Name: __________ Partner: __________ Date: __________

### Card A — Keep hidden
Living room: sofa, lamp. Bedroom: bed, wardrobe. Kitchen: table.

Living room: sofa, table. Bedroom: bed, lamp. Kitchen: cupboard.

### Partner task
Do not show your card. Ask at least **three** questions:
- “Where is the lamp?”
- “Is there a table in the kitchen?”
- “Can you help me find the wardrobe, please?”

Listen and write **two differences** between the rooms:
1. __________________________________________________
2. __________________________________________________

Say one sentence: “In my partner's house, the ______ is in the ______.”

### Teacher guide / PBD
Pair A with B. Model one exchange and assess each pupil for **3 questions**, **2 relevant answers**, **1 polite help request**, and **1 accurate descriptive sentence**. Expected differences include lamp location (A living room, B bedroom) and table location (A kitchen, B living room). Do not reuse Workbook p.37 Activity 2.
