## Listen and Identify Farm Animals
**Skill:** Listening | **Unit:** Out and About | **Original supplementary assessment, not official Workbook content**

Name: __________ Class: __________ Date: __________

### Pupil task
Listen to each clue twice. Circle the animal and write **one** word.
1. cow / sheep / chicken: __________
2. duck / horse / cow: __________
3. sheep / chicken / horse: __________
4. cow / duck / sheep: __________

Listen again and complete two sentences:
5. They have got __________. (wings / horns)
6. They can __________. (swim / fly)

### Teacher script — keep hidden from pupils
1. “It is a big farm animal. It gives us milk. It says moo.”
2. “It has got feathers. It swims in the pond. It says quack.”
3. “It has got wool. It says baa.”
4. “It is big. People can ride it. It says neigh.”
5. “Listen to the chickens. They have got wings.”
6. “Look at the ducks. They can swim.”
Read each clue twice without naming the answer.

### Key / PBD
1 cow; 2 duck; 3 sheep; 4 horse; 5 wings; 6 swim. Record **six correct listening responses** and support pupils with picture cards where appropriate. This does not repeat Workbook p.53 Activity 2.
