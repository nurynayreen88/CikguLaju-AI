# CikguLaju Original Material — English Year 3 Lesson 87
## Speaking: Find my omelette partner
**Unit:** Food, Please! | **Skill:** Speaking | **Status:** Supplementary material, not an official Workbook/SOW exercise.

### Teacher preparation
Print and cut the four role cards below. Give each pupil one card, keeping the ingredient list private. Pair pupils with **different** cards. Pupils must ask questions; they may not show cards.

### Role cards
**Card A — Sunny Omelette:** eggs ✓, cheese ✓, tomatoes ✓, mushrooms ✗, onions ✗.

**Card B — Garden Omelette:** eggs ✓, cheese ✗, tomatoes ✓, mushrooms ✓, onions ✗.

**Card C — Cheesy Omelette:** eggs ✓, cheese ✓, tomatoes ✗, mushrooms ✗, onions ✓.

**Card D — Mushroom Omelette:** eggs ✓, cheese ✗, tomatoes ✗, mushrooms ✓, onions ✓.

### Pupil task
Name: __________________  Partner: __________________

Ask your partner at least **three different questions**. Listen and complete the notes without seeing the partner's card.

Useful questions:
- “Is there any cheese in your omelette?”
- “Are there any tomatoes in your omelette?”
- “Are there any mushrooms in your omelette?”

Useful answers:
- “Yes, there is.” / “No, there isn't.”
- “Yes, there are.” / “No, there aren't.”

My partner's omelette:
- Cheese: Yes / No
- Tomatoes: Yes / No
- Mushrooms: Yes / No
- Onions: Yes / No

I think my partner has Card: **A / B / C / D**.

Say one complete sentence: “There is some ______ in my partner's omelette.” or “There are some ______ in my partner's omelette.”

### PBD (individual, 10 minutes)
Teacher listens to each pupil ask **three intelligible ingredient questions**, respond to **two partner questions**, and say **one accurate descriptive sentence**. Record a simple checklist (3 questions / 2 answers / 1 description). Use the role-card key to verify the partner's identified card. Pupils do not complete or reuse Workbook p.45 Activity 2.

### Teacher note
This original information-gap task supports the RPH's existing ‘Find the same omelette’ speaking activity. If time is limited, use it for the 10-minute PBD section only.
