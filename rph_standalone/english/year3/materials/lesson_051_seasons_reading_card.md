### Reading text — The School Calendar
Aina looks at the school calendar with her brother. “It is June,” she says. “Our school holiday is coming soon.” Her brother points to a picture of a tree with yellow leaves. “Some countries have autumn in September,” he says. “The leaves can change colour and fall.” Aina smiles. “In Malaysia, we do not have four seasons like that. We have hot and rainy days.”

### A. Choose the best title
☐ My Favourite Sport
☐ Months, Weather and Seasons
☐ Food for Lunch

### B. Find the details
1. Which month is it in the story? __________________
2. What can happen to leaves in autumn? __________________
3. Does Aina say Malaysia has four seasons like some other countries? **Yes / No**

### C. Tell your partner
Say **one difference** between weather in Malaysia and autumn in some other countries.

### Teacher key and PBD
A: Months, Weather and Seasons. B: 1 June; 2 They change colour and/or fall; 3 No. Assess **main idea (1)**, **details (3)**, **oral comparison (1)**.
