# CikguLaju Original Material — English Year 3 Lesson 25
## Jobs: Write Your Own Clues
**Unit:** Every Day | **Main skill:** Writing | **Type:** Original supplementary worksheet (not an official Workbook exercise).

Name: __________________ Class: __________ Date: __________

### Word bank
teacher · dentist · firefighter · lifeguard

### A. Match and write
Write the correct job for each description.
1. This person helps people at a swimming pool. __________________
2. This person helps put out fires. __________________
3. This person looks after our teeth. __________________
4. This person helps children learn at school. __________________

### B. Create your own clues
Choose **two different jobs** from the word bank. Write a complete clue for each without using the job name.
1. This person ______________________________________________.
2. This person ______________________________________________.

### C. Ask for help
Read one clue to your partner. If you need help, say: **“Can you help me, please?”**
Partner's guess: __________________

### D. Check your writing
☐ I started my sentences with capital letters.
☐ I used full stops.
☐ My clues describe the jobs correctly.

### Teacher key and PBD
A: 1 lifeguard; 2 firefighter; 3 dentist; 4 teacher.
For B, accept meaningful descriptions that identify each job. Assess **4 correct job words**, **2 understandable original clues**, and **one polite request for help** observed during partner work. Check spelling and punctuation; give one improvement target. Unlike Workbook p.12 Activity 1, pupils write **new clues**, not the same crossword again.
