## My Town: Then and Now
**Writing — Where Were You Yesterday?**

Name: ______ Class: ______

Write two things that were different in your town in the past. Use **There was / There were / There wasn't / There weren't**.

1. In the past, ____________________________________.
2. In the past, ____________________________________.
3. Now, __________________________________________.

Circle the correct form: **There wasn't / There weren't** any shopping centres here long ago.

Check: ☐ capital letters ☐ apostrophes ☐ full stops ☐ past-time meaning.

**Teacher PBD:** Check two plausible past-time sentences, one now-sentence and correct apostrophe/verb agreement. Accept imagined local history; do not treat invented claims as facts. Ask pupil to improve one sentence. **Answer:** There weren't any shopping centres. This replaces the repeated Workbook p62 Activity 2, already assigned in Lesson 120.
