## My Daily Routine Interview
**Skill:** Speaking | **Unit:** Every Day | **Type:** Original supplementary interview card, not official Workbook content.

Name: __________________ Partner: __________________ Date: __________

### A. Ask your partner
Ask all three questions. Listen and write **short notes**, not full copied phrases.
1. What time do you get up? Answer: __________
2. Do you have breakfast every day? Answer: __________
3. What do you do after school? Answer: __________

### B. Tell the teacher
Say **two complete sentences** about your partner's routine.
1. My partner ___________________________________________.
2. My partner ___________________________________________.

### C. Your turn
Answer your partner's three questions without reading from your notes.

### Teacher instructions / PBD
Model a brief interview with one pupil, then have pairs exchange roles. Assess **three understandable questions**, **three relevant oral answers**, and **two sentences reporting partner's routine**. Notes support the speaking task; do not assign Workbook p.10 Activity 1 again. Record each learner's oral performance with a 3/3/2 checklist.
