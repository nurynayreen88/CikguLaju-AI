# CikguLaju Original Material — English Year 3 Lesson 86
## Listening: What's in the lunchbox?
**Unit:** Food, Please! | **Skill:** Listening | **Status:** Supplementary material, not an official Workbook/SOW exercise.

### Pupil listening sheet
Name: __________________  Class: __________  Date: __________

Listen to the teacher **twice**. Tick (✓) the ingredients you hear for each lunchbox.

| Food | Lunchbox A | Lunchbox B |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Eggs | ☐ | ☐ |
| Cheese | ☐ | ☐ |
| Tomatoes | ☐ | ☐ |
| Mushrooms | ☐ | ☐ |
| Onions | ☐ | ☐ |

Circle the correct answer:
1. Which lunchbox has cheese? **A / B / Both**
2. Which lunchbox has tomatoes? **A / B / Both**
3. Which lunchbox has mushrooms? **A / B / Both**

### Teacher script — read aloud; do not display during listening
**First reading:** “Lunchbox A has an omelette with eggs, cheese and tomatoes. There aren't any mushrooms or onions in it. Lunchbox B has an omelette with eggs, mushrooms and onions. There isn't any cheese or tomato in Lunchbox B.”

**Second reading:** Repeat at a natural pace. Pause after each lunchbox description. Do not point to the correct answers.

### Answer key
A: eggs ✓, cheese ✓, tomatoes ✓; mushrooms ✗, onions ✗.
B: eggs ✓, mushrooms ✓, onions ✓; cheese ✗, tomatoes ✗.
Questions: 1 A; 2 A; 3 B.

### PBD (individual, 10 minutes)
Teacher reads the script twice, checks each pupil's ticked table and three answers, then records **ingredients identified correctly (out of 10)** and **detail questions correct (out of 3)**. Allow pointing to picture cards for pupils who need support. This is a fresh listening task, not Workbook p.45 Activity 2.
