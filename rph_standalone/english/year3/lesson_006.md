# English Year 3 — Lesson 6: Listening

**Unit:** Welcome!  
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials / references:** SB p.7 Activity 3; Workbook p.3 Activity 2; TB p.21; CD 1 Track 7; word and punctuation cards.
**Source:** KPM Primary Year 3 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 6. Lesson outline adapted for classroom use.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review have got questions and vocabulary with flashcards. **Pupils:** Respond and recall relevant language.

## Activity 1 — Guided preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display character flashcards, ask 'What has he/she got?', and model a sentence using word and punctuation cards, including capitals, full stops and apostrophes. **Pupils:** Listen for character details, arrange a model sentence and explain its punctuation.

## Activity 2 — Core lesson task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play CD 1 Track 7 for SB p.7 Activity 3 and check the numbers pupils hear. Model one contraction in Workbook p.3 Activity 2. **Pupils:** Record the numbers from the audio, complete one Workbook sentence and write a character sentence with capitals, full stops and any needed apostrophe.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check two details from listening and a correctly punctuated sentence. **Pupils:** Identify two spoken character details and write one sentence with a capital letter and full stop.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Unjumble a mixed-up sentence and punctuation as a class. **Pupils:** Summarise one thing learned.
