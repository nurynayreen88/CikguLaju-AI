# English Year 3 — Lesson 44: Speaking

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.29, Activity 4 and p.30, Activity 1. Teacher's Book pp.51–53. Picture cards from Student's Book p.125.
**Learning objective:** Describe a pictured person’s actions and respond accurately to supported action questions.
**Success criteria:** Give one intelligible description and answer at least two of three spoken questions in a guessing game.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a pupil to mime a sport and model a yes-or-no question about the action.
**Pupils:** Listen, answer and take turns miming an action.

## Activity 1 — Spot the change (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Activity 4 on p.29 to model comparing its picture with the one in Activity 3.
**Pupils:** Take turns describing one action that changes between the pictures.

## Activity 2 — Guess the hidden card (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the p.30 game with one hidden card. Keep question starters visible.
**Pupils:** Take turns choosing a card, asking questions and answering until their partner identifies it.
**Workbook:** Match the four action questions to the short answers in Workbook p.21, Activity 2.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen for one clear description and check each pupil’s responses to three supported questions.
**Pupils:** Describe one pictured action and answer three questions from a partner.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead one round of the mime game on Teacher's Book p.53.
**Pupils:** Ask a question to guess the action and answer in role.
