# English Year 3 — Lesson 122: Language Arts

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM SOW Lesson 122; Damia supermarket story worksheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces Damia and asks pupils to predict five things she bought.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils read Damia's supermarket story and check predictions; teacher explains unfamiliar past verbs using context.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils solve the shopping puzzle in Damia's story and explain the answer. They create an Alvin shopping puzzle, exchange it with classmates, and solve their partners' puzzles.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks each pupil's shopping-puzzle answer against the story and asks them to explain one clue. Pupils share their own puzzle and solve a partner's.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one item from their story.
