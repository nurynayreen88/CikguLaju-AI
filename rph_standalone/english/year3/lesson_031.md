# English Year 3 — Lesson 31: Language Arts

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Stories
**Main SK/SP:** Language Arts 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book pp.22–23; Teacher's Book pp.42–43; paper and colour pencils.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask what pupils might see at a school career day. **Pupils:** Name two occupations and share a reason people might be interested in them.

## Activity 1 — Story preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the word career, show the story pictures from Student's Book pp.22–23 and model a prediction based on a character's action. **Pupils:** Predict one event, name a character they notice and explain a picture clue to a partner.

## Activity 2 — Story and response (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the career-day story using Student's Book pp.22–23 and Teacher's Book pp.42–43; invite pupils to express a response to a character and draw a chosen occupation. **Pupils:** Follow the story, discuss one character's job or action, draw an occupation and describe the drawing in a short group exchange.
**Workbook:** Pupils complete Workbook p.15, Activity 1, by matching story characters to their jobs and actions.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe whether pupils respond to one story event and describe a chosen occupation in an understandable sentence; check that each response matches the story. **Pupils:** Name one character and the related job or action, then explain their drawing with one clear spoken sentence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils which job interested them and invite a brief reason. **Pupils:** Name a job they would like to try and give one reason.
