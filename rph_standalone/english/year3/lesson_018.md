# English Year 3 — Lesson 18: Speaking

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book p.15 Activities 1–2; Teacher's Book pp.32–33.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the familiar daily-routine song and pause at a known action. **Pupils:** Sing or mime the action, then name the matching daily-routine phrase.

## Activity 1 — Vocabulary preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Match routine flashcards to word cards, then model a question and answer using always, sometimes or never. **Pupils:** Match the pictures and phrases, rehearse a complete frequency answer and take turns asking a partner one routine question.

## Activity 2 — Speaking task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the routine table and speaking activity in Student's Book p.15 Activities 1–2. Model recording a classmate's answer with a short written note before the pair interview. **Pupils:** Record their own routines in the table, ask two classmates about their routines, note the answers and share one finding in a complete spoken sentence.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen to individual question-and-answer exchanges and check whether each pupil asks one understandable routine question, answers with a suitable frequency word and records one classmate's response. **Pupils:** Interview a partner, answer one follow-up question and show the written record of one routine.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two volunteers to report a classmate's routine and correct one common question form. **Pupils:** Share one accurate routine statement and repeat the corrected question form.
