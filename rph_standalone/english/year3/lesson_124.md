# English Year 3 — Lesson 124: Speaking

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM SOW Lesson 124; interview worksheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews places, activities and adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher models 'Where were you yesterday?', 'When were you there?' and 'How was it?'.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils interview classmates about yesterday or last weekend and report short answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks exchange of personal information and understanding of questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one interview finding.
