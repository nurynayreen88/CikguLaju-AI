# English Year 3 — Lesson 80: Project-Based Learning

**Unit:** My New House
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 80; house question cards, paper and colouring materials.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher asks pupils to imagine their homes and respond to questions about rooms and colours.

## Activity 1 — Group planning (15 minutes)
Teacher places house question cards in groups of four. Pupils take turns choosing a card, asking and answering about rooms, furniture and home features.

## Activity 2 — House project (25 minutes)
Groups draw a house plan and label rooms and objects. Teacher encourages pupils to use the questions to describe their design and check spelling and punctuation.

## PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher observes clear spoken descriptions and accurate written labels and sentences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Groups present their house plans and identify one improvement.
