# English Year 3 — Lesson 80: Project-Based Learning

**Unit:** My New House
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 80; house question cards, paper and colouring materials.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to imagine their homes and prompt questions about rooms and colours.
**Pupils:** Name rooms and answer orally.

## Activity 1 — Group planning (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give each group house question cards and model asking and answering.
**Pupils:** Take turns asking and answering about rooms, furniture and home features.

## Activity 2 — House project (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide house-plan drawing, spoken descriptions and checks of spelling and punctuation.
**Pupils:** Draw a house plan, label rooms and objects, and describe their design.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil's spoken description and check written labels and sentences against both SPs.
**Pupils:** Describe one room and write at least two labelled details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite short presentations and feedback.
**Pupils:** Present their house plans and name one improvement.
