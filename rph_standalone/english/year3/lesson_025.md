# English Year 3 — Lesson 25: Writing

**Unit:** Every day
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; confirm against SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; confirm against SOW):** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Duration (draft):** 60 minutes
**Verified reference mapping from previous audit:** Get Smart Plus 3 Student's Book p.19 Activity 3; Teacher's Book p.37; Class CD1 Track 28.
**Objective (draft):** Pupils complete the textbook writing task in Activity 3 and produce a legible response using routine vocabulary.
**Materials:** Student's Book p.19, Teacher's Book p.37, Class CD1 Track 28, exercise books.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the daily-routine pictures from Student's Book p.19 and elicit familiar expressions.
**Pupils:** Name activities and predict the lesson focus.

## Activity 1 — Textbook task preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Student's Book p.19 Activity 3. Use Teacher's Book p.37 as the source for instructions and Class CD1 Track 28 where indicated by the verified mapping.
**Pupils:** Observe the examples and respond to guided questions before writing.

## Activity 2 — Writing practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Activity 3 and provide support with spelling, spacing and sentence structure. Do not replace the textbook task with generic copying.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 3, compare responses with a partner and revise their own work.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Inspect each pupil's completed textbook response for meaning and legibility. Record whether the pupil needed assistance.
**Pupils:** Submit an individual response and make one correction if required.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two routine expressions and invite a pupil to explain one correction.
**Pupils:** Share a useful expression from the task.
