# English Year 3 — Lesson 86: Listening

**Unit:** Food, Please!
**Main SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.57; Teacher's Book p.91; food flashcards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review omelette ingredients with food cards.
**Pupils:** Name familiar foods.

## Activity 1 — Missing food game (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Run Kim's game using 'There is some...' and 'There aren't any...'.
**Pupils:** Listen, identify missing cards and respond orally.

## Activity 2 — Listening (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the Student's Book p.57 listening task and replay key parts as needed.
**Pupils:** Listen for ingredients and answer detail questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess listening by asking pupils to identify ingredients mentioned in the Student's Book audio and answer oral detail questions. Use Workbook p.45, Activity 2 (Look and write. Answer the questions) separately to reinforce 'Is there any...?' and 'Are there any...?' with Anna's and Greg's omelette pictures; this printed task does not itself test listening.
**Pupils:** Listen and answer questions about ingredients heard, then write answers to the printed omelette questions in Workbook p.45, Activity 2.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap the listening clues and sentence patterns.
**Pupils:** Describe one present and one absent ingredient.
