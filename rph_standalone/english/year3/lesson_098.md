# English Year 3 — Lesson 98: Speaking

**Unit:** Out and About
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.65; Teacher's Book pp.102–103.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews directions using Simon Says.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils listen to a directions song and mime the movements.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils give and follow directions in a team game.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks speaking and listening skills.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils give two simple directions.
