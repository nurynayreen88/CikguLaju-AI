# English Year 3 — Lesson 151: Speaking

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.97; Teacher's Book p.147; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews comparative adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher models simple comparisons using willing volunteers and gives spoken instructions for pupils to follow. Teacher emphasises respectful language.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils listen to five True/False comparative sentences, record answers and explain corrections aloud. They complete Student's Book p.97 Activity 4 and practise a respectful quiz in groups.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Workbook p.79, Activity 2; explain the printed task, check individual answers and record achievement against the lesson objectives.
**Pupils:** Pupils complete Workbook p.79, Activity 2, by reading the questions and answering about themselves using superlative adjectives.
Teacher reads two comparative statements. Pupils mark true or false, correct one statement and give a respectful spoken comparison.

**Workbook practice:** Pupils answer the questions aloud, then complete Workbook p.79, Activity 2 with written answers about themselves. Teacher checks answers and provides guidance.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one comparative sentence and explain why differences should be respected.
