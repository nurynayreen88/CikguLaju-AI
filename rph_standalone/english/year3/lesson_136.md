# English Year 3 — Lesson 136: Reading

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB pp.88–89; TB pp.133–134.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher asks pupils about keeping a diary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils predict vocabulary from Katie's holiday diary.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils listen while reading Katie's diary, infer key vocabulary, check meanings in a dictionary, and complete TB p.134 Activity 2.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks reading details and dictionary use.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one diary detail.
