# English Year 3 — Lesson 7: Speaking

**Unit:** Welcome!  
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials / references:** SB p.7 Activity 4; Workbook p.3 Activity 3; TB p.21; CD 1 Track 8; paper strips.
**Source:** KPM Primary Year 3 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 7; classroom adaptation of its learning outline.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review have got vocabulary with a short game. **Pupils:** Respond and activate prior knowledge.

## Activity 1 — Modelling and preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display character flashcards and elicit 'He/She has got ...' before contrasting 'I have got ...'. Demonstrate writing one possession on a paper strip and asking 'Have you got ...?' **Pupils:** Transform third-person sentences into first-person sentences and rehearse questions in pairs.

## Activity 2 — Main learning task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play CD 1 Track 8 and model the question and short answer from SB p.7 Activity 4. Use Workbook p.3 Activity 3 as a question-and-answer prompt. **Pupils:** Match two questions to their short answers in the Workbook. Ask classmates about possessions written on paper strips and listen to identify the owners.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe two descriptions and supported listening exchanges. **Pupils:** Describe two possessions and answer a partner's 'Have you got ...?' question.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share an interesting fact about a friend. **Pupils:** State one thing learned.
