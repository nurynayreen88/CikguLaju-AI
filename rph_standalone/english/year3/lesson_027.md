# English Year 3 — Lesson 27: Listening

**Unit:** Every day
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils can identify four details in a short spoken daily-routine description and sequence three activities.
**Materials:** Daily-routine picture cards, sequencing strips, teacher-prepared listening script, exercise books.
**Source status:** Get Smart Plus 3 / Teacher's Book pages and audio tracks not verified. The listening script is teacher-created, not attributed to the textbook.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display pictures of waking up, eating breakfast, going to school and doing homework. Elicit familiar routine expressions.
**Pupils:** Name activities and predict their usual order.

## Activity 1 — Guided listening (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read a short teacher-prepared daily-routine description at a natural pace. Read it again, modelling how to listen for action words and time expressions. Check two examples.
**Pupils:** Listen first for overall meaning, then point to pictures when mentioned and identify two details.

## Activity 2 — Independent listening and sequencing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read a different short daily-routine description twice. Provide a four-item picture checklist and three sequencing strips. Pause after the second reading for responses and pair comparison.
**Pupils:** Tick four details heard, order three activity strips and compare answers with a partner before correction.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect each pupil's four listening responses and three-step sequence. Record the number of correct details and whether prompts were needed.
**Pupils:** Submit an individual listening checklist and sequencing task.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit two time or routine expressions and ask pupils to explain the order of one activity.
**Pupils:** Say one routine expression and identify what comes next.
