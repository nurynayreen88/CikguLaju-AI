# English Year 3 — Lesson 158: Writing

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Teacher's Book p.153; poster paper; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays Missing Planets.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Groups imagine a new Solar System and plan its planets.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils write descriptions of their imaginary planets and create an illustrated poster.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks spelling and descriptions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Groups present one invented planet.
