# English Year 3 — Lesson 147: Reading

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book pp.96–97; Teacher's Book pp.146–147; KPM SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher displays sea animal flashcards. Pupils name familiar animals.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher shows shark and other sea animal flashcards, elicits adjectives including scary, and asks pupils to predict answers to the SOW sea-animal comparison worksheet.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils listen to the accompanying audio while reading Student's Book pp.96–97 silently, then check their worksheet predictions. They circle science and project, discuss meanings using picture clues, and complete the Grammar Box and Activity 2 with teacher guidance.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Workbook p.78, Activity 1; explain the printed task, check individual answers and record achievement against the lesson objectives.
**Pupils:** Pupils complete Workbook p.78, Activity 1, by looking at the pictures and completing the sentences using 'close', 'far', 'around' and 'planet'.
Teacher checks two sea-animal facts located in the text and asks pupils to explain one unfamiliar word using a picture or sentence clue.

**Workbook practice:** Pupils complete Workbook p.78, Activity 1, by writing the words close, far, around and planet under the pictures. Teacher checks answers and provides guidance.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one sea animal fact.
