# English Year 3 — Lesson 130: Speaking

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.85; Teacher's Book pp.130–131; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews holiday pictures and words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pairs match sentence strips about holiday activities.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher guides Activity 2. Pupils tell partners what they did on holiday and listen to short recounts.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks recounts and reading details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one holiday activity.
