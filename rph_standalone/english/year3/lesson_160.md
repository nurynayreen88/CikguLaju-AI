# English Year 3 — Lesson 160: Writing / Project-Based Learning

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.119; Teacher's Book p.182; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces camel and reviews Malaysian animals.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils compare two types of camel and revisit endangered animals from Lesson 148.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Groups research from provided reading materials and create a short illustrated information project about an animal.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks written facts and reading details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Groups present their animal project.
