# English Year 3 — Lesson 160: Writing / Project-Based Learning

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.119; Teacher's Book p.182; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show camel pictures and prompt a review of Malaysian animals.
**Pupils:** Name animals and share one fact.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide a comparison of two types of camel and recall Lesson 148 descriptions.
**Pupils:** Compare features and retrieve their earlier animal descriptions.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Support sentence revision using facts and comparisons; organise a matching display.
**Pupils:** Improve their Lesson 148 endangered-animal descriptions, draw animals separately, and match pictures to texts using string.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check each revised animal description for two relevant facts, a clear comparison and accurate spelling; assess the picture-to-text match.
**Pupils:** Submit a revised description and match a text to its correct picture.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite brief project presentations and feedback.
**Pupils:** Present their animal project and share one improvement.
