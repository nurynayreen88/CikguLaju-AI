# English Year 3 — Lesson 104: Reading

**Unit:** Out and About
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.68; Teacher's Book p.106; enlarged texts and place pictures; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays Stop! with action words. Pupils freeze when instructed.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils brainstorm town places and discuss rules for each place.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher displays four texts around the classroom. Pupils read and match them to place pictures, then identify key meanings.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher asks each pupil to match one text to a place, state its main idea and explain one word from context. Pupils point to supporting text.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share a rule for one place.
