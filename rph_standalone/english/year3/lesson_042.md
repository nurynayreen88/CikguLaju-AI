# English Year 3 — Lesson 42: Language Arts

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Language Arts 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work pp.87–88, One Finger, One Thumb, Keep Moving. Sports picture cards.
**Learning objective:** Create and perform new verses for a sports action song and describe a classmate’s movement.
**Success criteria:** Contribute one original sports line or movement and describe one classmate’s action intelligibly.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show sports cards and model naming and miming one sport.
**Pupils:** Take turns naming a sport and miming it for a partner.

## Activity 1 — Sing and mime (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the first two verses from the SOW with simple, safe actions.
**Pupils:** Join in, mime the sports and tell a partner what one classmate is doing.

## Activity 2 — Create two verses (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give groups of four or five the SOW prompts for verses 3 and 4. Offer action cards to groups that need ideas.
**Pupils:** Choose two sports and matching movements, write two new lines and rehearse both verses.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check each pupil’s contribution and listen for one clear description of a classmate’s movement.
**Pupils:** Show their own line or movement and describe what a classmate is doing.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite groups to perform and ask the audience to identify the sports.
**Pupils:** Perform the verses with actions and name a sport from another group’s song.
