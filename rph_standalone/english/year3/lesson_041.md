# English Year 3 — Lesson 41: Writing

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Writing 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.29, Activity 3 and Grammar Box. Cut-up sentences and writing slips.
**Learning objective:** Read clues to identify pictured children and write simple negative sentences describing their actions.
**Success criteria:** Identify the correct child or pair from two written clues and write two accurate negative action sentences.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime a sport and make one true and one false statement about the mime.
**Pupils:** Identify the false statement and say what the teacher is doing.

## Activity 1 — Read and match (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pairs mixed-up words from a negative action sentence and model how to check its meaning against a picture.
**Pupils:** Order the words, read the sentence and point to the matching child or pair on p.29.

## Activity 2 — Write a guessing slip (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two clues about one child using the Grammar Box. Provide an action word bank and a sentence starter as needed.
**Pupils:** Write two negative clues about one pictured child or pair. Exchange slips, read a classmate’s clues and identify the match.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check two picture matches and two written sentences per pupil. Mark any errors in the slips for Lesson 43.
**Pupils:** Show the two matches and hand in their own clues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read one slip aloud and collect it with the others for Lesson 43.
**Pupils:** Guess the match and name one word that helped them decide.
