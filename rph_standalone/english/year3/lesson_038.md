# English Year 3 — Lesson 38: Listening

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.27; Teacher's Book p.49; action flashcards.
**Learning objective:** Identify two actions from short dialogues and infer one spoken phrase from a visual clue.
**Success criteria:** Two correct dialogue-picture matches and one plausible guess linked to a visual clue.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Draw a person doing an action one part at a time.
**Pupils:** Guess the action as more of the drawing is revealed.

## Activity 1 — Prepare to listen (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the picture pairs in Student’s Book p.27 to review actions and character names.
**Pupils:** Describe how the two pictures in a pair differ and predict words they might hear.

## Activity 2 — Listen and tick (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the short dialogues for p.27 Activity 3. Pause after each and replay to check.
**Pupils:** Tick the picture matching each dialogue, then practise action phrases through paired mime.
**Workbook:** Use the pictures to answer items 4 and 5 in Workbook p.19, Activity 2.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay two dialogue cues. Say one new action phrase while showing a matching mime or picture.
**Pupils:** Select two correct pictures and explain one visual clue that helped them guess the new phrase.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to recall one new action phrase.
**Pupils:** Mime the phrase for a partner to identify.
