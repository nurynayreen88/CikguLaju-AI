# English Year 3 — Lesson 82: Speaking

**Unit:** Food, Please!
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.55; Teacher's Book pp.88–89; food picture cards and food items.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead a food-themed TPR game with short instructions.
**Pupils:** Listen and perform the actions.

## Activity 1 — Song and grammar (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the food song and model the difference between 'There's' and 'There are'.
**Pupils:** Listen, underline the phrases and identify food examples.

## Activity 2 — Speaking practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display food items and model 'There is some...' and 'There are some...'.
**Pupils:** Describe food quantities in pairs and ask follow-up questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the fridge picture in Workbook p.44, Activity 2 (Look and write) as a speaking prompt first. Ask pupils to describe pictured foods using 'There's some...' and 'There are some...' and assess their spoken sentences; then guide the printed writing task.
**Pupils:** Describe the food in the fridge aloud in pairs, then write the sentences in Workbook p.44, Activity 2.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap the sentence patterns and correct a common error.
**Pupils:** Share one accurate food sentence.
