# English Year 3 — Lesson 82: Speaking

**Unit:** Food, Please!
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.55; Teacher's Book pp.88–89; food picture cards and food items.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher leads the food TPR game. Pupils follow spoken actions.

## Activity 1 — Song and grammar (15 minutes)
Teacher plays the food song. Pupils underline 'There's' and 'There are', then identify examples with food words.

## Activity 2 — Speaking practice (25 minutes)
Teacher displays food items and models 'There is some...' and 'There are some...'. Pupils describe quantities and ask questions using food vocabulary.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks clear descriptions and understanding of food quantities.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one sentence with 'There is' and one with 'There are'.
