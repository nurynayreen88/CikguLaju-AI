# English Year 3 — Lesson 121: Writing

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB pp.78–79; TB pp.120–121.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays True/False about the New York text; pupils respond with gestures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher reviews key places and time expressions from the reading.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher reviews There weren't any and the Grammar Box, then guides Student's Book p.79 Activities 3 and 4. Pairs complete the comparison table and write at least two sentences about their hometown in the past, checking apostrophes, spelling and punctuation.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Workbook p.62, Activity 2; explain the printed task, check individual answers and record achievement against the lesson objectives.
**Pupils:** Pupils complete Workbook p.62, Activity 2, by writing sentences about a town in 1900 using 'was', 'wasn't', 'were' and 'weren't'.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils read one sentence about a place.
