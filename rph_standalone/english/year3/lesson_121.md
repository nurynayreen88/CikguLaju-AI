# English Year 3 — Lesson 121: Writing

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB pp.78–79; TB pp.120–121.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays True/False about the New York text; pupils respond with gestures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher reviews key places and time expressions from the reading.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils plan and write short sentences about a place they visited, then check spelling and punctuation.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks clear descriptions and writing conventions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils read one sentence about a place.
