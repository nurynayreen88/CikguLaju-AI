# English Year 3 — Lesson 131: Reading

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.86; Teacher's Book p.132; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher shows a forest picture and asks pupils what they might do there.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils predict a forest-trip story using the illustrations.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils read the story, infer word meaning from context and use a dictionary when needed.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks contextual vocabulary and dictionary use.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one new forest word.
