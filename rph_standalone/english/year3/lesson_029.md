# English Year 3 — Lesson 29: Reading

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book p.115 Activities 1–2; Teacher's Book p.178; charity vocabulary flashcards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces familiar Malaysian charity organisations and what they do. Pupils suggest ways to help.

## Activity 1 — Preparation (15 minutes)
Teacher previews vocabulary and pictures on Student's Book p.115. Pupils predict the meaning of unfamiliar words from visuals.

## Activity 2 — Reading (25 minutes)
Teacher guides Student's Book p.115 Activities 1–2 using Teacher's Book p.178. Pupils read the texts, match ideas to pictures and identify details; then suggest 'We can ...' sentences about helping others.

## PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks reading details and meaning inferred from pictures. Pupils justify answers using textual or visual clues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one practical way to help a charity.
