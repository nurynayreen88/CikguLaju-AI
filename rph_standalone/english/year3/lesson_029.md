# English Year 3 — Lesson 29: Reading

**Unit:** Every day
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Student's Book reference (previous audit):** Get Smart Plus 3 p.115
**Teacher's Book reference (previous audit):** p.178
**Source status:** VERIFIED_SOW reference mapping; the earlier Year 3 audit marked audio as PENDING, so no track is assigned.
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils locate at least three details from the reading passage and justify answers by pointing to words in the text.
**Materials:** Get Smart Plus 3 p.115, reading response sheet, exercise books.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit familiar daily-routine expressions and ask pupils to predict the reading topic.
**Pupils:** Share two familiar expressions.

## Activity 1 — Guided reading (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Student's Book p.115 and introduce the reading task with reference to Teacher's Book p.178. Model finding one answer by locating supporting text.
**Pupils:** Read the passage and underline a phrase supporting the model answer.

## Activity 2 — Reading for details (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to reread the text and identify three further details; encourage pupils to cite a word or sentence for each response.
**Pupils:** Read individually, answer three comprehension prompts and compare textual evidence with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check three individual answers and their supporting evidence; record accuracy and whether prompting was needed.
**Pupils:** Submit their individual response sheet.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share one answer with the sentence that supports it.
**Pupils:** Read aloud or point to one supporting phrase.
