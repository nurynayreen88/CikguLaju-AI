# English Year 3 — Lesson 29: Reading

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book p.115 Activities 1–2; Teacher's Book p.178; charity vocabulary flashcards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a picture of people helping others and ask what help they might need. **Pupils:** Describe one visible act of help and suggest one way they could contribute.

## Activity 1 — Preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview pictures and key charity words from Student's Book p.115; model using a picture clue to predict the meaning of one unfamiliar word. **Pupils:** Match words with pictures, share one prediction and explain which detail in the picture supports it.

## Activity 2 — Reading (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Student's Book p.115 Activities 1–2, using Teacher's Book p.178 for the existing task sequence. Ask pupils to match ideas to pictures and find evidence for two details before discussing 'We can ...' sentences. **Pupils:** Read the texts, locate two relevant details, infer the meaning of an unfamiliar word using context or visuals and propose one helpful action.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check whether each pupil identifies two accurate reading details and explains one inferred word meaning using a clue from the text or picture. **Pupils:** State two details from the reading, point to their supporting clues and explain one unfamiliar word.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a practical suggestion connected to the text and correct one misunderstood word. **Pupils:** Complete one 'We can ...' sentence about helping others and say what the action means.
