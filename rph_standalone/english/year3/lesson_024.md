# English Year 3 — Lesson 24: Reading

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book p.18; Teacher's Book p.36; dictionary, occupation picture and word cards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show an occupation picture and ask what the person does. **Pupils:** Name the occupation if familiar and use one picture clue to describe the job.

## Activity 1 — Dictionary modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review alphabetical order and demonstrate looking up cat in a dictionary, then show how to find a new occupation-related word. **Pupils:** Practise locating one word alphabetically and explain how they found its entry.

## Activity 2 — Occupations reading (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display firefighter, lifeguard and dentist pictures with fire, save, fix and teeth word cards. Guide reading of the Student's Book p.18 texts and ask pupils to connect each occupation to evidence in the text. **Pupils:** Look up an unfamiliar word, read the short texts, match jobs to descriptions and locate two relevant facts.
**Workbook:** Pupils complete Workbook p.12, Activity 1, using occupation clues to fill in the crossword.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check individual dictionary use and ask for two details from the reading; note pupils who can find a word and locate evidence without prompting. **Pupils:** Find one occupation-related word in the dictionary and state two accurate facts from the text.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to explain one new occupation word and correct its meaning where needed. **Pupils:** Say the new word, give its meaning and identify a text or picture clue that helped.
