# English Year 3 — Lesson 24: Reading

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book p.18; Teacher's Book p.36; dictionary, occupation picture and word cards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces the word teacher and shows a dictionary. Pupils describe what a teacher does.

## Activity 1 — Dictionary modelling (15 minutes)
Teacher reviews alphabetical order and models finding cat in a dictionary. Pupils practise finding simple words.

## Activity 2 — Occupations reading (25 minutes)
Teacher displays pictures and word cards for firefighter, lifeguard and dentist, including fire, save, fix and teeth. Pupils use a dictionary to investigate unfamiliar words, then read the Student's Book p.18 texts and answer detail questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Workbook p.12, Activity 1; explain the printed task, check individual responses and record achievement against the lesson objectives.
**Pupils:** Pupils complete Workbook p.12, Activity 1, by using occupation clues to fill in the crossword.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils explain one new occupation word.
