# English Year 3 — Lesson 139: Listening

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.89; pupils' writing from Lesson 137.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher returns writing feedback from Lesson 137.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pairs edit and prepare final drafts of their holiday accounts.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pairs read their stories to another pair. Listeners identify details and ask follow-up questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks listening for detail and spelling corrections.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one detail heard.
