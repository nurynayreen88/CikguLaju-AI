# English Year 3 — Lesson 30: Writing

**Unit:** Every day
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 4.1 / 4.1.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Verified reference mapping (prior Year 3 audit):** Get Smart Plus 3 Student's Book p.21 Activity 1; Teacher's Book p.40; Class CD1 Track 33.
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils complete the writing task in Student's Book p.21 Activity 1 and produce a legible, meaningful response.
**Materials:** Student's Book p.21, Teacher's Book p.40, CD1 Track 33, exercise books.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall two Every day expressions and direct pupils to the illustrations on p.21.
**Pupils:** Name familiar actions and predict the writing focus.

## Activity 1 — Textbook task introduction (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce Student's Book p.21 Activity 1 using Teacher's Book p.40. Play CD1 Track 33 as indicated in the audited reference mapping; demonstrate how to respond to the printed task.
**Pupils:** Follow the printed prompts and complete one guided example.

## Activity 2 — Individual writing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Have pupils complete Activity 1, checking spelling, spacing and meaning. Provide prompts for pupils needing support and encourage independent checking.
**Pupils:** Complete the textbook task, reread their writing and make corrections.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check each pupil's completed Activity 1 response for relevance, legibility and accuracy; record whether assistance was required.
**Pupils:** Submit their own written work.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful expressions and demonstrate one common correction.
**Pupils:** Share a corrected example.
