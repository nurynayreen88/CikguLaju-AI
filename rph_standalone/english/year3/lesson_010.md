# English Year 3 — Lesson 10: Language Arts

**Unit:** Welcome!  
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Action cards, song/video and teacher-prepared song-line strips per SOW  
**Source:** KPM Primary Year 3 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 10.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime can and can't actions. **Pupils:** Respond and recall prior learning.

## Activity 1 — Guided preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model target vocabulary, language and task steps. **Pupils:** Practise with visual and oral scaffolding.

## Activity 2 — Main task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen to the SOW action song; mime, sequence prepared lines and compose a short original action-song verse in groups. **Pupils:** Complete the referenced tasks, practising both standards.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe imaginative participation and response to the song. **Pupils:** Show independent learning evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Perform original group action verses. **Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
