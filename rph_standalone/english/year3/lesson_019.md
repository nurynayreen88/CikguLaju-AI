# English Year 3 — Lesson 19: Reading

**Unit:** Every Day
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Get Smart plus 3 Student's Book pp.16–17; Teacher's Book p.34; routine flashcards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show routine flashcards and ask pupils to connect one action to a possible story event. **Pupils:** Name the routines and suggest what the characters may do.

## Activity 1 — Preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the story pictures from Student's Book p.16 and model a prediction using one visual clue. **Pupils:** Predict where the characters are going and explain their prediction to a partner using the pictures.

## Activity 2 — Reading (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide independent reading of the story on Student's Book pp.16–17. Ask where the children are going and why Anna travels with Greg's dad; show how to locate a supporting sentence instead of supplying the answer. **Pupils:** Read for the main event, find the relevant story lines, answer both questions and compare text evidence with a partner.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each pupil to state the main idea and identify two details supported by the text; note who can point to a matching line without prompting. **Pupils:** Say the story's main idea, identify two accurate details and point to one line supporting an answer.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Return to the pupils' predictions and clarify any mistaken detail. **Pupils:** State one event they confirmed from the story and explain whether their prediction was correct.
