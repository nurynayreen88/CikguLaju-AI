# English Year 3 — Lesson 112: Writing

**Unit:** Out and About
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SOW Lesson 112; familiar-area map and directions worksheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews directions and places.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Groups read written directions and trace routes from one point to another on a simple map.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils identify correct and incorrect directions on a worksheet, then write short corrected instructions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks accurate written directions and reading details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one correct route instruction.
