# English Year 3 — Lesson 36: Writing

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book pp.26–27; Teacher's Book p.49; action-word crossword.
**Learning objective:** Spell three familiar words in guided writing and locate two specific details in the picture story.
**Success criteria:** Three correctly spelt words and two answers supported by the story.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a household action without naming it.
**Pupils:** Name the action and suggest its first letter.

## Activity 1 — Spelling and crossword (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model how to unscramble one action word, then hand out the SOW crossword.
**Pupils:** Solve the picture clues in pairs and use Student’s Book p.26 to check uncertain spellings.

## Activity 2 — Read for details (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the story on p.26 and guide Activity 2 on p.27. Ask for the story panel that supports each answer.
**Pupils:** Reread the relevant panels, compare answers and write the key words.
**Workbook:** Answer items 2 and 3 in Workbook p.19, Activity 2, and check spellings against the word box.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give three picture cues for guided spelling and ask two questions about the story.
**Pupils:** Write three correctly spelt words and point to the panels supporting two answers.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit one spelling the class found difficult.
**Pupils:** Correct it, then name one way they help at home.
