# English Year 3 — Lesson 33: Listening

**Unit:** Right Now
**Main SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.25; Teacher's Book p.46; action flashcards.
**Learning objective:** Identify two details from the action song and infer one spoken action from a visual clue.
**Success criteria:** Two correct action-picture matches and one plausible guess linked to a visual clue.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime three familiar actions.
**Pupils:** Name each action and copy the mime.

## Activity 1 — Action flashcards (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give spoken action cues, then point pupils to the flashcard stations.
**Pupils:** Follow the cues, choose a card and say what they are doing.

## Activity 2 — Listen and number (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Student’s Book p.25 Activity 1. Pause for picture numbering, then replay the song.
**Pupils:** Number the pictures, check with a partner, then sing and mime the actions.
**Workbook:** Choose am, are or is for the pictured actions in Workbook p.18, Activity 1.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay two short song cues. Say one less familiar action and show a matching mime.
**Pupils:** Select two matching pictures and explain which visual clue helped them guess the action.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead the TPR game in Teacher’s Book p.46.
**Pupils:** Follow the spoken commands and repeat one action word.
