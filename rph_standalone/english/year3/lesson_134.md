# English Year 3 — Lesson 134: Listening

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.87; TB p.133.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews action verb flashcards.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pupils mime weekend activities and identify past-tense verbs.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher guides the listening activity. Pupils identify past activities and respond to questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks listening details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils name one past activity.
