# English Year 3 — Lesson 125: Reading

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.118; TB p.181; transport worksheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces familiar transport words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher follows the warm-up and vocabulary guidance. Pairs match car and bicycle pictures to the correct worksheet squares.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils read short transport texts and identify specific information and contextual vocabulary.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks reading details and word meaning.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share a transport fact.
