# English Year 3 — Lesson 113: Listening

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.75; TB p.116.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces yesterday with questions about the previous day.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher uses place and time flashcards, modelling 'I was at...' and reviewing locations.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher plays the song. Pupils put place flashcards in morning, afternoon and evening order, then do the TPR listening activity from TB p.116.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks specific details about yesterday.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils say where they were yesterday.
