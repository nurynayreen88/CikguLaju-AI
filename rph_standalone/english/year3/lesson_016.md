# English Year 3 — Lesson 16: Language Awareness

**Unit:** Welcome!  
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Unit 1 SOW worksheet; character and action flashcards  
**Source:** KPM Primary Year 3 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 16.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Unit 1 vocabulary with pictures. **Pupils:** Respond and recall prior learning.

## Activity 1 — Guided preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show character flashcards and elicit 'He/She has got ...'; contrast with 'I have got ...' using pupils' own examples on the board. **Pupils:** Say and write one first-person and one third-person sentence before completing the worksheet.

## Activity 2 — Main task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review has got/have got with character flashcards, guide SOW worksheet Sections A and B, then model can/can't and Can you? exchanges before guiding worksheet Section C and punctuation review. **Pupils:** Compare I have got with he/she has got, complete SOW worksheet Sections A–C, ask and answer 'Can you...?' and check punctuation.
**Workbook:** Pupils use Workbook p.9, Activity 5, to say sentences with 'has got' and 'can' from the pictures.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check reading answers and guided sentence punctuation. **Pupils:** Answer two reading questions and write one correctly punctuated 'has got' or 'can' sentence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Correct common errors and complete unit self-assessment. **Pupils:** Summarise their learning.
