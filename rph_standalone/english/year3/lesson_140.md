# English Year 3 — Lesson 140: Speaking

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.90; TB pp.136–137.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher plays a revision game using past verbs.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher models 'Did you...?' and 'Yes, I did / No, I didn't'.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils practise Activity 1 and a Snap game, asking questions about past activities.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the original speaking cards `materials/lesson_140_did_you_weekend_speaking.md`; observe three past-tense questions, appropriate short answers and one oral report.
**Pupils:** Ask a partner three different 'Did you...?' questions and respond using 'Yes, I did' or 'No, I didn't', without repeating Workbook p.71, Activity 1.
Pupils ask two questions beginning 'Did you' and respond using 'Yes, I did' or 'No, I didn't'. Teacher records responses.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one past-tense answer.
