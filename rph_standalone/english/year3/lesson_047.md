# English Year 3 — Lesson 47: Language Arts

**Unit:** Right Now
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Language Arts 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 47; sports jigsaw template and scissors.
**Learning objective:** Create a sports jigsaw with intelligible pictures and accurately spelt labels.
**Success criteria:** Draw three recognisable sports, spell at least two labels correctly and match two pieces in a peer's jigsaw.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime a familiar sport and invite pupils to add their own examples.
**Pupils:** Name the sport and demonstrate another one.

## Activity 1 — Spelling relay (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set up four teams and check the words written on the board.
**Pupils:** Take turns writing sports names and help teammates check the spelling.

## Activity 2 — Make a sports jigsaw (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a two-row layout with a picture above each label. Show how to cut the pieces.
**Pupils:** Draw three sports in the top row, label them in the second row and cut out the pieces.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check three pictures and their labels. Observe two independent matches in the exchanged puzzle.
**Pupils:** Exchange puzzles in groups of three or four and read each label before matching it.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each group to share one label that helped them solve a puzzle.
**Pupils:** Show the matched pieces and spell the label aloud.
