# English Year 3 — Lesson 155: Listening

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.99; Teacher's Book p.149; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews adjectives and planets.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher revisits Activity 3 and pupils' worksheets from Lesson 153.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils ask planet questions in groups, record guesses, and check answers using worksheets from Lesson 153.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to Workbook p.80, Activity 2; explain the printed task, check individual answers and record achievement against the lesson objectives.
**Pupils:** Pupils complete Workbook p.80, Activity 2, by completing the Solar System crossword using the numbered clues.
Teacher asks two spoken planet questions. Pupils answer and locate one supporting fact on the worksheet.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one planet answer.
