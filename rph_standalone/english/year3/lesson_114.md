# English Year 3 — Lesson 114: Speaking

**Unit:** Where Were You Yesterday?
**Main SK/SP:** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.75; TB pp.116–117.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Pupils revisit the previous song and perform TPR actions.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher points to place cards and models 'I was at the shopping centre' and 'She was at...'.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils ask classmates where they were yesterday and report partners' answers using was and were.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher observes short recounts and requests for clarification.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one partner's answer.
