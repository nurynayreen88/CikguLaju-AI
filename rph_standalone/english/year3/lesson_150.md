# English Year 3 — Lesson 150: Listening

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.97; Teacher's Book p.147; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews animal comparisons.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pairs prepare plural animal word cards.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils listen to comparative clues and choose matching animal cards.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks listening details and descriptions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils give one comparison.
