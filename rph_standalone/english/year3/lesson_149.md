# English Year 3 — Lesson 149: Language Arts

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.97; Teacher's Book p.147; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews animals and adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher reads fact and comparison clues. Groups identify possible animals.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pairs create animal fact and comparison cards and present them creatively.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks creative language and descriptions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one comparison.
