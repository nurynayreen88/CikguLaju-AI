# English Year 3 — Lesson 149: Language Arts

**Unit:** The World Around Us
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.97; Teacher's Book p.147; KPM Year 3 SOW.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews animal vocabulary and comparative adjectives through a quick guessing game.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher reads five pairs of animal-clue sentences. Groups guess each animal, then identify how the second sentence compares it with another animal.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Teacher models a fact-plus-comparison pair of sentences. Pairs select two animals, write two clue sentences for each and practise singing or miming their clues.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Pairs read their clues to groups of six or eight. Classmates guess the animals; teacher assesses imaginative responses and accurate comparative descriptions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one comparison.
