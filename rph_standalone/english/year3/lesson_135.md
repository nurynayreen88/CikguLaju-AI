# English Year 3 — Lesson 135: Speaking

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB p.87; TB p.133.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews weekend verbs.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Teacher models 'What did you do at the weekend?' and a suitable answer.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pupils practise questions and answers, request clarification when needed and write short notes.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks interaction and spelling.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share a partner's answer.
