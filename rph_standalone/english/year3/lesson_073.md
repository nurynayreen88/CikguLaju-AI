# English Year 3 — Lesson 73: Writing

**Unit:** My New House
**Main SK/SP:** Writing 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** Writing 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 73; pictures of Japanese and traditional Malay houses, writing worksheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews wood, water and light. Pupils name familiar building materials.

## Activity 1 — Compare houses (15 minutes)
Teacher displays pictures of Japanese and traditional Malay houses. Pupils discuss how design responds to the local environment.

## Activity 2 — Guided writing (25 minutes)
Teacher models a short description of a traditional Malay house. Pupils plan and write short sentences about its features, then revise spelling and punctuation with teacher guidance.

## PBD (10 minutes)
Pupils submit two descriptive sentences, show a short plan and correct one sentence after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one feature of a traditional Malay house.
