# English Year 3 — Lesson 12: Speaking

**Unit:** Welcome!  
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Materials:** Student's Book p.10 Activity 1; Teacher's Book pp.24–25; toy clock  
**Source:** KPM Primary Year 3 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 12.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review numbers one to twelve. **Pupils:** Respond and recall prior learning.

## Activity 1 — Guided preparation (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Move the hands on a toy clock to show o'clock and half past, then model one question and answer. **Pupils:** Read three model clock faces and rehearse 'What time is it?' in pairs.

## Activity 2 — Main task (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Student's Book p.10 Activity 1, rotate pair roles, and check how pupils read the hour and minute hands. **Pupils:** Ask and answer about the pictured clock times using 'o'clock' and 'half past', then complete SB p.10 Activity 1 with a partner.
**Workbook:** Pupils complete Workbook p.9, Activity 4, by reading the pictured clock times.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check telling-the-time exchanges and listening. **Pupils:** Ask and answer two clock-time questions using 'o'clock' and 'half past'.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play What's the time, Mr Wolf. **Pupils:** Share one learning point.
