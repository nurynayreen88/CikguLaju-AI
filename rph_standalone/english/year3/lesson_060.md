# English Year 3 — Lesson 60: Speaking

**Unit:** Year In, Year Out
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.39; Teacher's Book p.65; sports and holiday activity flashcards.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher introduces the word holiday and asks when school holidays occur. Pupils share ideas.

## Activity 1 — Sports vocabulary (15 minutes)
Teacher reviews sports and activities from the unit, adding familiar holiday activities. Pupils identify and mime actions.

## Activity 2 — Speaking (25 minutes)
Teacher guides Student's Book p.39 using Teacher's Book p.65. Pupils ask and answer about activities they do during school holidays and describe sports they would like to try.

## PBD (10 minutes)
Pupils ask and answer one activity question and describe two activities using suitable phrases.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share a holiday activity and one reason for choosing it.
