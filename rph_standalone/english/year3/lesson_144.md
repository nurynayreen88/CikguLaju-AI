# English Year 3 — Lesson 144: Language Awareness

**Unit:** On Holiday
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** SB pp.85,87,91,94; TB pp.142–143.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews present-tense verbs using cards.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
Pairs write verbs on blank cards and match them to past forms.

## Activity 2 (25 minutes)
Pairs write past verbs on the backs of their cards, make past-tense sentences and turn the cards over to check their answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks written descriptions and question comprehension.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils give one present/past verb pair.
