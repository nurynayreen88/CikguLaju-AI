# English Year 3 — Lesson 48: Language Awareness

**Unit:** Right Now
**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends
**Main SK/SP:** Writing 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.1 / 1.1.1
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** KPM Year 3 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 48; Student's Book p.34; Teacher's Book pp.58–59; present continuous worksheet.
**Learning objective:** Describe pictured actions in writing and reproduce contractions heard in an action chant.
**Success criteria:** Write two accurate action sentences and say two contractions intelligibly after listening.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime familiar actions and review the verbs with the class.
**Pupils:** Name each action and make a present continuous sentence.

## Activity 1 — Hear and say contractions (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play or lead an action chant. Repeat short lines with I'm, he's or they're.
**Pupils:** Mime on the first listening, then join in and repeat the contractions.

## Activity 2 — Check and rewrite (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Part A of the SOW worksheet for Yes/No checks. Model correcting one false statement against the picture.
**Pupils:** Mark the statements, then rewrite two false descriptions to match the actions shown.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect two sentences for new actions and listen as pupils repeat two contractions.
**Pupils:** Write a sentence for each of two new actions and say two contractions from the chant.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share one anonymised correction and ask pupils to check their own work.
**Pupils:** Read one accurate sentence aloud and underline the verb form.
