# English Year 3 — Lesson 91: Listening

**Unit:** Food, Please!
**Main SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.59; Teacher's Book p.93.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews food vocabulary. Pupils identify foods in Student's Book p.59.

## Activity 1 — Questions (15 minutes)
Teacher asks 'Are there any...?' questions about pictured ingredients. Pupils listen and respond.

## Activity 2 — Listening (25 minutes)
Teacher guides Activity 3 from Teacher's Book p.93. Pupils listen for specific details and respond in full sentences. They then answer short questions about a crepe and lunch choices.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
Teacher checks listening details and understanding of supported questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils share one ingredient they would put in a crepe.
