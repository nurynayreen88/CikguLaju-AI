# English Year 3 — Lesson 70: Listening

**Unit:** My New House
**Main SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**References:** Student's Book p.47; Teacher's Book p.77; pupils' classroom belongings.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
Teacher reviews clothes vocabulary with picture prompts. Pupils name items.

## Activity 1 — Whose is it? (15 minutes)
Teacher collects a few classroom items and models 'Whose pencil is this?' and 'Whose pencils are these?' Pupils listen and identify owners.

## Activity 2 — Listening (25 minutes)
Teacher revisits the Grammar Box on Student's Book p.47 and guides Activity 3 from Teacher's Book p.77. Pupils listen for ownership information and respond to questions.

## PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the original listening sheet and teacher script in `materials/lesson_070_whose_is_it_listening.md`; read the ownership statements twice and assess six listening details.
**Pupils:** Listen, identify who owns each object and answer two detail questions; do not repeat Workbook p.37, Activity 2.
Pupils identify two listening details and answer one question about the recording.

## Closure (5 minutes)
Pupils ask and answer one ownership question.
