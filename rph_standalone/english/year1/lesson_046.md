# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 46 — Listening (10)  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2  
**Learning objective:** Develop listening and acting through sequenced classroom instructions.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 20, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 20  
**Audio guidance:** SB p20 uses CD1 Track 27.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Review familiar imperatives by having pupils take turns giving simple instructions.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p20 listen and mime a sequence, listen again to number pictures, read and order story instructions, then practise giving/following instructions in pairs.
**Pupils:** Follow the textbook task, listen/read and respond as directed.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p20: follow the illustrated instructions to make a pencil holder.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs compare sequencing answers before class checking; monitor whether instructions are followed in order.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Pairs write four instructions, exchange them and act out the partner's sequence.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
