# English Year 1 — Lesson 53: Reading 11

**Unit:** Let’s Play!
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 3.1 / 3.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 3.1 / 3.1.4
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can recognise two familiar play words in print.
**Materials:** Play-action pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a play-action picture and elicit one familiar action word.
**Pupils:** Point to the picture and name or demonstrate the action.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Display two play word cards with matching action pictures and model one match.
**Pupils:** Read or recognise two words and match them to pictures.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the action, word or partner and monitor pupils' responses.
**Pupils:** Repeat the task using a new example with reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two independent word-picture matches.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final action, word or response without a full model.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target response from the lesson.
