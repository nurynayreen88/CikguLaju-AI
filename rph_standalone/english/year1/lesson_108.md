# English Year 1 — Lesson 108: Reading 22

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.3
**Learning aim:** Develop reading using the Unit 3 textbook material.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify the main idea and locate at least one supporting detail from the reading task on SB p43.
**Student's Book:** 43
**Audio:** No verified track recorded

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Show the title or pictures on SB p43 and ask pupils to predict a familiar word.
**Pupils:** Look at the page and predict one word likely to appear in the reading.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to read SB p43 first for gist, then reread for the required details; facilitate partner comparison.
**Pupils:** Read SB p43 for overall meaning, reread to locate the required details and compare answers with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to show the word, phrase or picture clue for one answer and check whether it supports the response.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal, pending review)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify one detail and point to its supporting text or picture.
**Pupils:** Identify one detail from the textbook page and show the supporting clue.
