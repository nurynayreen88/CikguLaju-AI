# English Year 1 — Lesson 13: Reading 3

**Unit:** Friends
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 3.1 / 3.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.1 / 4.1.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can recognise two familiar printed Friends words.
**Materials:** Picture/word cards, board and exercise books as appropriate.
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK, confirmed in the prior Year 1 SOW lesson index. No Student's Book page or audio track assigned; exact SOW procedure remains to be checked.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a familiar Friends picture and elicit a known word or greeting.
**Pupils:** Identify one familiar word or greeting.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show two familiar word cards and matching pictures; model one match, then change the card order.
**Pupils:** Read or recognise two words and match each to the correct picture.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the example or partner and invite individual responses; support pupils who need another model.
**Pupils:** Complete another example independently or with a new partner.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two independent word-picture matches.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate one final response and recap the target language.
**Pupils:** Show one successful response from the lesson.
