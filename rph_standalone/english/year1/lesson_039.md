# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 39 — Reading 8  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Learning objective:** Extend Art through English and complete a colour project.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 19, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 19  
**Audio guidance:** No exact audio track is introduced on this Teacher's Book page.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Recall previous colour vocabulary and distinguish the primary colours.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p19 read and answer about colours, predict how white changes colours, then make a picture using primary/secondary colours and colour mixing.
**Pupils:** Read and answer questions about colours on SB p19; predict how white changes a colour and create a picture by mixing primary and secondary colours.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p19: complete the colour-code picture.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs discuss predictions, then compare results after mixing/colouring; review project language orally.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Complete a Today I've... reflection focused on colour learning and the finished picture.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
