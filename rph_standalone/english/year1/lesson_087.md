# English Year 1 — Standalone RPH: Lesson 87

**Unit:** Pet Show  
**Skill:** Speaking 18  
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.1 / 1.1.1  
**Objective:** Develop speaking using Unit 3 pet language.  
**Student's Book:** 36

## Activity 1 — Textbook sequence
**Teacher:** Model the pet-language exchange using SB p36, invite choral practice and organise paired turns.
**Pupils:** Look at SB p36, listen to the modelled pet-language exchange, rehearse the response as a class and take turns speaking with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Monitor whether pupils use the target form and pet word accurately.
**Pupils:** Give responses and explain or correct them during checking.

## Closure — Teacher-developed proposal (not verified Teacher's Book)
**Teacher:** Invite each pair to perform one final exchange and listen for accurate pet vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Exchange one final pet-language question and answer without teacher modelling; both partners take a speaking turn.
