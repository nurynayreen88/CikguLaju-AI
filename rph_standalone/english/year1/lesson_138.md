# English Year 1 — Lesson 138: Speaking 28

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Learning aim:** Develop speaking from the Unit 4 textbook stimulus.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can take at least two speaking turns using the SB p54 model.
**Student's Book:** 54
**Audio:** No audio required in the current verified mapping.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the SB p54 speaking stimulus and model one short response.
**Pupils:** Name one item in the stimulus and repeat a modelled response.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Use the SB p54 model to practise the target food-language exchange, taking turns in pairs or groups.
Use SB p54 to establish the speaking context, model the target language and run structured pair/group exchanges.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Observe two speaking turns and record whether the pupil uses the modelled language.
Monitor accurate food vocabulary and the target speaking pattern.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Invite each pair to perform one final SB p54 food-language exchange.
**Pupils:** Take turns asking and answering one food-language exchange.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
