# English Year 1 — Lesson 112: Speaking 23

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can take two turns in a simple pet-related exchange.
**Materials:** Pet pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a pet picture and ask pupils to name one animal.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one familiar pet.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Model a short pet question and answer and organise paired practice.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer about a pet picture and switch roles.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or partner and invite another response.
**Pupils:** Complete a second example with less prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Observe two intelligible speaking turns.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate one final target response.
**Pupils:** Show one word, gesture, spoken or written response.
