# English Year 1 — Lesson 136: Listening 28

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning aim:** Develop listening and writing support through Unit 4 food language.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify the required information from the SB p52 listening task and give one supported response.
**Student's Book:** 52
**Audio:** Use verified CD2 Tracks 15 and 16.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the SB p52 pictures and ask pupils to recall two food words needed for listening.
**Pupils:** Point to or name two pictured food items before the listening task.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Preview the SB p52 listening task and required response; present the mapped audio, replay for checking and prompt pupils to support one answer.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Study SB p52, listen for the information required, replay to confirm and give a short supported response using the results.


## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Check one listening answer and ask for the clue supporting it.
Pairs compare listening evidence before class confirmation.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to select one completed word or sentence from SB p52 and read it aloud.
**Pupils:** Read one completed food-related word or sentence aloud.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
