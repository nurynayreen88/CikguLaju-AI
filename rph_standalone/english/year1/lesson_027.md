# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 27 — Writing 6  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4  
**Learning objective:** Present and practise positive classroom imperatives and following instructions.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 13, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 13  
**Audio guidance:** SB p13 uses CD1 Tracks 19 and 20.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Show classroom objects one at a time; pupils write the words, compare spelling in pairs, then check as a class.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p13 pupils listen and choose the correct stickers, write the object words, mime the instructions, repeat the imperative forms, then play a chain game in small groups where each pupil follows and gives a new instruction.
**Pupils:** Complete the Student's Book practice and exchange answers with a partner before class feedback.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p13: read and write classroom-object words, then read and match instructions/items by number.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs compare sticker/listening choices, then check with the class; monitor the chain game for correct imperative use.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Play a 'Please' response game: pupils act only when the instruction includes please.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
