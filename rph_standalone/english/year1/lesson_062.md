# English Year 1 — Lesson 62: Speaking 13

**Unit:** Let’s Play!
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can ask and answer a simple play-related question twice.
**Materials:** Play-action picture cards, word cards, short teacher-made text or exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a play-action picture and ask pupils to recall one familiar word.
**Pupils:** Name or demonstrate one familiar action.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Model a short question and answer about a play action; arrange paired turns.
**Pupils:** Ask, answer and swap roles with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Offer a second action, text example or speaking partner and observe responses.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Observe two complete speaking turns per pupil.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate one final target response.
**Pupils:** Show one word, action or written example from the lesson.
