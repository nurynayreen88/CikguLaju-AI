# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 47 — Writing 10  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4  
**Learning objective:** Review unit language, consolidate classroom objects/colours and reflect on learning.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 21, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 21  
**Audio guidance:** No exact audio track is introduced on this Teacher's Book page.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Unscramble colour words in pairs.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p21 pairs make a poster, then use classmates' posters for a memory task about school objects and colours; review classroom-object vocabulary through the picture dictionary.
**Pupils:** Follow the textbook task, listen/read and respond as directed.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p21: draw and write examples of what pupils know from the unit.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs/groups compare posters and memory statements; review written/drawn learning examples.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Ask pupils for their favourite unit song, chant or game and repeat one with the class.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
