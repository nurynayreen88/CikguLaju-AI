# English Year 1 — Lesson 10: Language Arts 2

**Unit:** Friends
**Main SK/SP (verified existing SOW mapping):** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP (verified existing SOW mapping):** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK SOW task (verified Year 1 mapping). No Student's Book page or audio track should be invented.
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils participate in a simple rhythmic chant and recognise or say two familiar Friends expressions.
**Materials:** Friends picture cards, board, teacher-prepared chant cue.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of two friends meeting and elicit familiar greetings.
**Pupils:** Say one greeting and point to the matching picture.

## Activity 1 — Chant and actions (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce a short, teacher-created greeting chant with two repeated expressions and actions; model each line and invite echo responses.
**Pupils:** Echo each line, match gestures to the words and join the chant as a class.

## Activity 2 — Group performance (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide pupils into small groups. Give each group one picture cue, invite them to perform the chant and replace a greeting expression with the appropriate familiar alternative.
**Pupils:** Perform with actions, take turns saying the target expression and listen to other groups.

## Assessment / PBD (3 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe whether each pupil joins the chant and says two familiar expressions; record who needs prompting.
**Pupils:** Give two brief oral responses.

## Closure (2 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a final class performance and recap two expressions.
**Pupils:** Repeat the chant ending with a greeting.
