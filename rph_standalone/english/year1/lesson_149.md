# English Year 1 — Lesson 149: Listening 30

**Unit:** Lunchtime review
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify two food items from short spoken clues.
**Materials:** Food picture and word cards, board and exercise books.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a familiar food picture and elicit one known word.
**Pupils:** Recall and say a familiar word.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show three food pictures and give two short oral clues twice.
**Pupils:** Listen and select the matching food pictures.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the example or partner, then observe without full modelling.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with less support.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Record two correct independent selections.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final independent response and recap progress.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target word, spoken response or written example.
