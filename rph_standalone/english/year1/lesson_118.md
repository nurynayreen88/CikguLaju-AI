# English Year 1 — Lesson 118: Reading 24

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning aim:** Develop reading and speaking around Unit 4 food language.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can complete the SB p47 reading task and identify one supporting word or picture clue.
**Student's Book:** 47
**Audio:** SB p47 uses verified CD2 Track 04; WB uses verified CD2 Track 05.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show SB p47 and ask pupils to predict one food word they might read.
**Pupils:** Look at the page and predict one word from its pictures or heading.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Display SB p47; model how to locate a supporting word or picture clue, allow individual responses and lead partner checking before class feedback.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Read the text or prompts on SB p47, complete the textbook response, compare answers in pairs and practise the food language orally.


## Activity 2
Use the verified WB activity linked to this page.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Check one reading response and ask pupils to identify the supporting word or picture.
Pupils identify the textual/picture clue supporting each answer.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Ask each pair to choose one food word from SB p47 and use it in a short response.
**Pupils:** Locate one food word in the text and use it in a short spoken response.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
