# English Year 1 — Lesson 11: Listening 3

**Unit:** Friends
**Main SK/SP (verified SOW mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP (verified SOW mapping):** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK SOW task, according to the previously audited Year 1 lesson index. Do not invent textbook pages or audio tracks.
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils respond correctly to at least three of four spoken greetings or simple classroom cues.
**Materials:** Four Friends-themed picture cards, board, teacher-prepared oral cue list, individual response sheet.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display pictures of children greeting or meeting friends. Elicit a familiar greeting.
**Pupils:** Point to the appropriate picture and say the greeting.

## Activity 1 — Listen and match (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate one spoken cue and matching picture. Give four short oral cues, pausing for pupils to select a picture.
**Pupils:** Listen without reading, then hold up or point to the matching picture for each cue.

## Activity 2 — Individual response (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Repeat the cues in a new order. Give pupils a four-box response sheet and observe independently completed answers.
**Pupils:** Mark a picture for each spoken cue, then compare after all four have been heard.

## Assessment / PBD (3 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's number of correct matches out of four and identify who requires repetition.
**Pupils:** Submit the individual response sheet.

## Closure (2 minutes)
**Teacher:** Repeat one cue and elicit the corresponding expression.
**Pupils:** Respond orally or with a gesture.
