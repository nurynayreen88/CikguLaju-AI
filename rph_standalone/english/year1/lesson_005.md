## Set Induction — proposed
**Teacher:** Show a familiar Friends picture and elicit one relevant word.
**Pupils:** Point to the picture and recall a word.

## Activity 1 — proposed
**Teacher:** Lead a short Friends-themed rhythm chant with gestures and pause for pupils to repeat target words.
**Pupils:** Join the chant, follow the gestures and repeat two familiar words.

## Assessment / PBD — proposed
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil's participation and recognition of two words during a final repetition.

## Closure — proposed
**Teacher:** Invite one final response without full modelling.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target response.
