# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 38 — Writing 8  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Learning objective:** Integrate Art through English and classify primary/secondary colours.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 18, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 18  
**Audio guidance:** No exact audio track is introduced on this Teacher's Book page.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Create a colour mind map and elicit classroom objects associated with each colour.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p18 identify primary colours, discuss objects with those colours, then read about and predict how secondary colours are made before mixing/confirming.
**Pupils:** Name primary colours and matching objects; predict secondary colours, mix the colours and check the prediction against the result.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p18: complete the primary/secondary colour chart, then read, write and colour using pupils' knowledge.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Groups share colour examples and discuss colour-mixing answers before class confirmation.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Pupils complete the reflection prompt Today I've learnt about... in their notebooks.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
