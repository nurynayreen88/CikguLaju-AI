# English Year 1 — Lesson 34: Writing 7

**Unit:** At School
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.2 / 4.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can copy two classroom words with legible letter formation.
**Materials:** Classroom picture cards, word cards, board and exercise books as appropriate.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no invented page or audio reference.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a classroom object or picture and ask pupils to recall its name.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one familiar classroom object.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate the letter strokes of two familiar words and guide tracing and copying.
**Pupils:** Trace then copy the two words and check spacing against the model.

## Activity 2 — Independent or paired practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or speaking partner and invite another attempt.
**Pupils:** Complete a second example with less modelling.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two copied words for recognisable letters and spacing.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask for one final target response and recap the language.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one word, response or written example.
