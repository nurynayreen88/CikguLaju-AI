# English Year 1 — Lesson 25: Language Arts 5

**Unit:** At School
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can join in a short classroom-themed chant and say two familiar words.
**Materials:** Classroom object pictures/word cards, board, exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified. No page or audio track claimed.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show two familiar classroom objects or pictures and elicit their names.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one familiar classroom object.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Lead a rhythmic chant about classroom objects with gestures and pauses.
**Pupils:** Join the chant and say two familiar words at the correct moments.

## Activity 2 — Independent or paired practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or partner; provide one further practice turn and monitor pupils who need support.
**Pupils:** Complete a second example or repeat the exchange with a new partner.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil saying two target words.
**Evidence:** Pupil's independent oral, visual or written response.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask for one final classroom word or response without a full model.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful response from the lesson.
