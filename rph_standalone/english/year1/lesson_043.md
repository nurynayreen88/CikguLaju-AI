# English Year 1 — Lesson 43: Reading 9

**Unit:** At School
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 3.1 / 3.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can recognise two classroom words in print.
**Materials:** Classroom pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as relevant.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a classroom picture and elicit one familiar word.
**Pupils:** Name or point to the object.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Display two classroom word cards, demonstrate one match and shuffle the cards.
**Pupils:** Read or recognise two words and match to pictures.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or partner and monitor individual responses.
**Pupils:** Repeat the task with a new example and reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two individual word-picture matches.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final independent response and recap the lesson language.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one word, action or response.
