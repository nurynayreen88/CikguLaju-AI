# English Year 1 — Lesson 154: Writing 31

**Unit:** Year 1 Revision
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write a short familiar-topic sentence using a model.
**Materials:** Picture and word cards from Friends, At School, Let's Play!, Pet Show and Lunchtime, board and exercise books.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures from previously learned units and elicit one known word.
**Pupils:** Recall and say a familiar word.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Model a simple sentence and show a small word bank.
**Pupils:** Copy the sentence and change one word appropriately.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the example or partner, then observe without full modelling.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with less support.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check one legible and relevant sentence.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final independent response and recap progress.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target word, spoken response or written example.
