# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 28 — Listening 6  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Learning objective:** Present the unit picture story and review classroom objects and imperatives.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 14, 15, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 14  
**Audio guidance:** SB story uses CD1 Track 21; WB p14 uses CD1 Track 22.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Recall the Super Friends, their powers and the cat before revisiting the story context.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Use SB pp14-15: identify characters and classroom objects, play the story, pause at key frames for comprehension, discuss helping each other, then replay for listen-and-repeat and role-play.
**Pupils:** Follow the textbook task, listen/read and respond as directed.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p14: listen and tick the matching story pictures, find Flash in the story, and match key phrases to numbers.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs compare before whole-class checking; pause at selected story frames to check comprehension.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Groups of seven take character roles, practise the story and perform it.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
