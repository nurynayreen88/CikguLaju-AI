# English Year 1 — Lesson 117: Speaking 24

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Learning aim:** Introduce and practise food vocabulary in the Unit 4 opening context.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify and name at least three food items on SB p46.
**Student's Book:** 46
**Audio:** Use verified SB p46 audio CD2 Tracks 02 and 03.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the food pictures on SB p46 and ask pupils to identify familiar items.
**Pupils:** Point to and name two familiar food items before the textbook task.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Look at the lunchtime scene on SB p46, point to food items named in the recording, repeat the food words and identify items orally.
Use SB p46 to establish the lunchtime scene, identify target food, listen/point/repeat and recycle the vocabulary through short oral identification.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Point to three food pictures in mixed order and record whether each pupil names them correctly.
Point to food items in random order and elicit names; use pair checking before class feedback.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Point to three food pictures from SB p46 in mixed order and ask for names without a model.
**Pupils:** Name three food items independently.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
