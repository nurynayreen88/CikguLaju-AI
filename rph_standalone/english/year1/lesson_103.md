# English Year 1 — Lesson 103: Reading 21

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify a pet and one supporting clue from a short text.
**Materials:** Pet pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a pet picture and elicit one familiar animal word.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one animal.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show a teacher-made two-sentence pet description, model locating a pet word and a clue.
**Pupils:** Read the short description and point to the pet and one relevant clue.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, clue or partner and observe another attempt.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with less support.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check one correct pet identification and supporting clue.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask for one final pet word, action or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful target response.
