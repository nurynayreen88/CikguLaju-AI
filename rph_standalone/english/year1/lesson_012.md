# English Year 1 — Lesson 12: Speaking 3

**Unit:** Friends
**Main SK/SP (verified SOW mapping):** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP (verified SOW mapping):** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK SOW lesson, confirmed in the previous Year 1 lesson index; no textbook page or audio track assigned.
**Objective (teacher-developed):** Pupils participate in a short exchange with a partner, initiating a greeting and giving an appropriate reply.
**Materials:** Two greeting picture cards per pair, board, observation checklist.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures of friends meeting and ask pupils what they might say.
**Pupils:** Suggest a greeting and response.

## Activity 1 — Model the exchange (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate a two-turn conversation with a volunteer. Repeat using a second greeting and emphasise listening before responding.
**Pupils:** Echo both turns, then practise with a partner using the first picture.

## Activity 2 — Change partners and roles (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give each pair a different picture prompt and ask pupils to swap who starts the conversation. Listen to each pupil's contribution.
**Pupils:** Initiate one exchange, respond in another and practise with a new partner.

## Assessment / PBD (3 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record whether each pupil initiates and responds intelligibly with or without prompts.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one short exchange.

## Closure (2 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a pair to demonstrate and review polite turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Share one greeting they can use with a friend.
