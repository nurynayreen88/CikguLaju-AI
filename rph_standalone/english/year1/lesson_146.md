# English Year 1 — Lesson 146: Speaking 30

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Learning aim:** Consolidate Unit 4 language through the textbook speaking task.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can perform one complete food-language speaking exchange using the SB p55 stimulus.
**Student's Book:** 55
**Audio:** No audio required in the current verified mapping.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Revisit the SB p55 speaking stimulus and ask pupils to recall a familiar exchange.
**Pupils:** Recall one food-language question or answer and rehearse it with a partner.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Demonstrate the SB p55 exchange, organise repeated partner turns and invite selected pairs to share a complete dialogue.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Look at SB p55, follow one model speaking exchange and take repeated turns using the target language with a partner.


## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Observe one complete paired exchange and check that each pupil contributes a turn.
Invite pairs to demonstrate and correct only language that affects the target objective.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Invite two pairs to demonstrate an SB p55 exchange and ask listeners to identify one food word.
**Pupils:** Perform the dialogue or listen and identify one target food word.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
