# English Year 1 — Lesson 116: Writing 24

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Learning aim:** Consolidate Unit 3 through the final textbook-linked writing task.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can complete the SB p45 writing task and produce one short response following the page model.
**Student's Book:** 45

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Show a familiar Pet Show picture and ask pupils to recall one word needed for the writing task.
**Pupils:** Say one relevant pet word and locate it in the SB p45 model where available.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Show the SB p45 model, guide the final Unit 3 task and support pupils in producing individual written responses.
**Pupils:** Use the SB p45 model to complete the textbook task and write a short individual response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Check each pupil's written response for task completion and one accurate use of modelled unit language.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to recall one pet-related word, phrase or sentence from the lesson.
**Pupils:** Share one word, phrase or sentence they can now say, read or write about pets.
