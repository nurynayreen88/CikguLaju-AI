**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Learning aim:** Revisit SB p42 as a reading-focused SOW lesson without changing its verified page mapping.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can recognise and read at least two familiar words or phrases on SB p42 and complete the related reading task.
**Student's Book:** 42

## Set Induction
Recall the earlier speaking work from the same page.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Revisit the written language on SB p42, model recognition of familiar words and guide the reading-focused task.
**Pupils:** Read the written language on SB p42, identify familiar words and complete the reading task using the page cues.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Ask each pupil to point to or read two familiar words or phrases and check their textbook-task response.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal, unverified)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to share one response and locate the supporting textbook clue.
**Pupils:** Share one answer with a partner and point to the supporting clue.
