# English Year 1 — Lesson 139: Writing 28

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write one short food-related sentence from a model.
**Materials:** Food picture cards, word bank, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a food picture and ask pupils to recall one food word.
**Pupils:** Name or point to one familiar food.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Display a short food sentence, model copying and provide a food word bank.
**Pupils:** Copy the sentence and replace one food word appropriately.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the food picture, text clue or partner and monitor responses.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with less prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check one legible, relevant written sentence.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final food word or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful target response.
