# English Year 1 — Lesson 71: Listening 15

**Unit:** Let’s Play!
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify two play actions from spoken instructions.
**Materials:** Play-action picture cards, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a familiar play-action picture and elicit a word or gesture.
**Pupils:** Name or demonstrate one play action.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show three play-action cards, give two spoken cues and repeat once in a different order.
**Pupils:** Listen and point to or demonstrate each matching action.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the action, word or partner and monitor responses.
**Pupils:** Repeat the task with a new example and reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two correct independent responses.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask for one final response and recap the target language.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one word, action or written response.
