# English Year 1 — Lesson 147: Writing 30

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Learning aim:** Develop guided writing using the Unit 4 textbook model.
**Student's Book:** 56
**Audio:** Use verified SB CD2 Track 17.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
Teacher shows pictures of familiar food items and asks pupils to name or point to them before the textbook task.

## Activity 1
Study the SB p56 model, identify useful words/structure, complete the guided stage and then produce a short individual response.

## Assessment
Pairs check vocabulary and structure against the page model before final class feedback.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
Pupils read one sentence from their guided writing and check it with a partner.
