# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 48 — Speaking 10  
**Unit:** Let's Play!  
**Main SK / SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Learning objective (source guidance):** Present toy vocabulary and practise listening, pointing and naming.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 22

### Set Induction — PROPOSED / NOT TEACHER'S BOOK
**Teacher:** Teacher shows three toy pictures from the unit scene. Pupils point to a toy they recognise and name it.
**Pupils:** Respond to the opening prompts, name or demonstrate the target language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Use the opening unit scene to identify toys, play the vocabulary recording for pupils to point and repeat, then recycle the words through short pair questions.
**Pupils:** Follow the source activity, listen/read, point, repeat or respond as specified.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Check recognition by pointing to different toys in random order and eliciting names.
**Pupils:** Give answers, compare with peers and correct errors after feedback.

### Closure — PROPOSED / NOT TEACHER'S BOOK
**Teacher:** Teacher points to the same toy pictures in a different order. Pupils name each toy without the recording.
**Pupils:** Complete the consolidation task and respond independently.
