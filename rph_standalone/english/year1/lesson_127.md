# English Year 1 — Lesson 127: Listening 26

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Learning aim:** Follow and understand the Unit 4 picture story.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify two events in sequence and one character action in the SB pp50–51 story.
**Student's Book:** 50, 51
**Audio:** SB story uses verified CD2 Track 11; WB uses verified CD2 Track 12.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
Teacher shows pictures of familiar food items and asks pupils to name or point to them before the textbook task.

## Activity 1
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Look at the pictures on SB pp50–51 and predict events; listen or read, identify the story order and practise lines in assigned roles.
Preview SB pp50-51, predict from pictures, listen/read the story, pause for comprehension and sequence, then replay/reread for role practice.

## Activity 2
Use the verified story-linked Workbook task.

## Assessment
Ask pupils to order key events and identify speakers/actions using the story frames.

## Closure
Groups rehearse and perform a shortened story retelling.
