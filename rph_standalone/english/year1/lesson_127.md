# English Year 1 — Lesson 127: Listening 26

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Learning aim:** Follow and understand the Unit 4 picture story.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify two events in sequence and one character action in the SB pp50–51 story.
**Student's Book:** 50, 51
**Audio:** SB story uses verified CD2 Track 11; WB uses verified CD2 Track 12.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the first SB pp50–51 story frame and ask pupils to predict what happens next.
**Pupils:** Identify a character or food item and make one simple prediction.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Show SB pp50–51 story frames; invite predictions, play or read the mapped story, pause to check event order and support role rehearsal.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Look at the pictures on SB pp50–51 and predict events; listen or read, identify the story order and practise lines in assigned roles.


## Activity 2
Use the verified story-linked Workbook task.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Ask pupils to sequence two story frames and identify one character action.
Ask pupils to order key events and identify speakers/actions using the story frames.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Assign short roles and prompt groups to retell two events from SB pp50–51.
**Pupils:** Take roles and retell two story events in sequence.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
