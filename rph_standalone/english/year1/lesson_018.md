# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 18 — Listening 4  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Learning objective:** Present and practise classroom objects and perform the unit chant.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 10, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 10  
**Audio guidance:** SB p10 uses CD1 Tracks 12 and 13.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Run a true/false colour review using objects around the classroom.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Present classroom-object flashcards, play the SB p10 recording for pupils to point and repeat, then use the chant first as a class and then in groups while pupils hold up relevant objects.
**Pupils:** Point to and name classroom objects; repeat after the recording and perform the chant holding up matching objects.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p10: match classroom-object words to pictures, then colour the objects as instructed.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Use pair pointing/naming practice, then check workbook matching and colours together.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Display flashcards around the room; call objects for pupils to point to, then reverse the task by pointing for pupils to name them.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
