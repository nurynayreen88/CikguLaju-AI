# English Year 1 — Lesson 135: Language Arts 27

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.4
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can join a short food rhyme and say two familiar food words.
**Materials:** Food pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a familiar food picture and elicit one food word.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one food item.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Lead a two-line food rhyme using picture cards, gestures and pauses for missing words.
**Pupils:** Join in, point to the pictures and supply two food words.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or partner and monitor another attempt.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Observe two correctly produced food words.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final food word, action or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful target response.
