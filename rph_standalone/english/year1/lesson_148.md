# English Year 1 — Lesson 148: Reading 30

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning aim:** Develop reading and consolidate the final textbook-linked Unit 4 content.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can complete the SB p57 reading task and locate one detail supporting an answer.
**Student's Book:** 57
**Audio:** No audio required in the current verified mapping.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Display the SB p57 reading page and ask pupils to predict one word from its visual cues.
**Pupils:** Point to a clue and predict one food-related word before reading.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Guide pupils to read SB p57 for overall meaning, reread for a requested detail and justify one answer with page evidence.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Read SB p57 for overall meaning, reread to find the requested information and complete the textbook activity before pair checking.


## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Check one reading response and ask pupils to show the supporting word or picture.
Ask pupils to support responses using evidence from the page.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to find one food word and one useful language pattern on SB p57.
**Pupils:** Locate one food word and read or repeat one useful pattern from the page.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
