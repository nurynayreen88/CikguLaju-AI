# English Year 1 — Lesson 137: Reading 28

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning aim:** Develop reading comprehension using the Unit 4 textbook page.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify the main idea and at least one supporting detail from SB p53.
**Student's Book:** 53
**Audio:** No audio required in the current verified mapping.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the SB p53 reading page and ask pupils to predict one detail from the pictures.
**Pupils:** Look at the page and predict one food word or detail they expect to read.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Read SB p53 for the main idea, reread for specific information, complete the page task and compare answers with a partner.
Read SB p53 first for gist and then for the specific information required by the textbook task; compare answers with a partner.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Check one reading response and ask pupils to point to a supporting clue.
Pupils point to the word, phrase or picture evidence supporting each answer.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to locate one reading detail on SB p53 and show its evidence.
**Pupils:** Point to one detail and explain which word or picture supports it.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
