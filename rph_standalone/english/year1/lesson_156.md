# English Year 1 — Lesson 156: Listening 32

**Unit:** Year 1 Revision
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify two familiar words from short spoken cues.
**Materials:** Picture and word cards from Friends, At School, Let's Play!, Pet Show and Lunchtime; board and exercise books.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures from earlier units and ask pupils to recall the words.
**Pupils:** Name one familiar word or action.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show pictures from two previously learned units and give two spoken clues.
**Pupils:** Listen and select the matching pictures.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Switch the unit or picture, monitor pupils and give support only when necessary.
**Pupils:** Complete another example independently or with a new partner.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Record two independent correct choices.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final response and recap two useful words.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target word, response or written example.
