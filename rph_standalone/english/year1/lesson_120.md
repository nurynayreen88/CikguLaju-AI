# English Year 1 — Lesson 120: Language Arts 24

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can join a short food-themed chant and say two familiar food words.
**Materials:** Food pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a food picture and elicit one familiar food word.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one food item.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Lead a short food chant with picture cards and pause for two target words.
**Pupils:** Join the chant, point to the food and supply two words.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the picture, word or partner and observe a second response.
**Pupils:** Complete another example with reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Observe two correct independent food words.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final food word, action or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target response from the lesson.
