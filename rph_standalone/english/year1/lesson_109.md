# English Year 1 — Lesson 109: Listening 22

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning aim:** Develop listening using the Unit 3 textbook task.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify the overall answer and at least one requested detail from the SB p44 listening task.
**Student's Book:** 44
**Audio:** No verified track recorded

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Point to the SB p44 pictures and review two familiar pet words before listening.
**Pupils:** Name or point to two familiar pets in the listening-task pictures.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Preview the SB p44 listening task, present the relevant listening material for overall meaning and specific details, then check responses.
**Pupils:** Study the SB p44 listening task, listen for the main idea and again for specific details, then compare responses.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Check the responses after listening and ask pupils to identify the heard clue for one answer; replay only if source audio is available.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal, pending review)
**Teacher:** Give one short listening cue and ask pupils to select the matching picture.
**Pupils:** Listen to the cue and point to the matching picture independently.
