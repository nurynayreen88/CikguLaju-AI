# English Year 1 — Lesson 84: Writing 17

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.2 / 4.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write two familiar pet words from a model.
**Materials:** Pet picture cards, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show two pet pictures and ask pupils to name a familiar animal.
**Pupils:** Point to and name one pet.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show two familiar pet words, demonstrate letter formation and guide copying.
**Pupils:** Trace then copy two pet words legibly.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the animal picture, word or partner and invite another response.
**Pupils:** Complete a second example with reduced prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check two readable written words.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final pet word, action or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful target response.
