# English Year 1 — Lesson 126: Speaking 26

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning aim:** Develop speaking using the Unit 4 textbook stimulus.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can take two turns in a modelled food-language exchange using SB p49.
**Student's Book:** 49
**Audio:** Use verified CD2 Tracks 09 and 10.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
Teacher shows pictures of familiar food items and asks pupils to name or point to them before the textbook task.

## Activity 1
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Listen to the teacher's model on SB p49, practise the food-language exchange as a class and take turns with different food items in pairs.
Use SB p49 to model the target exchange, practise chorally, then move to repeated pair speaking with different food items.

## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Observe two paired speaking turns for each pupil and note intelligible food vocabulary.
Monitor target language and intelligible pronunciation; invite selected pairs to demonstrate.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
Pairs exchange one food-related question and answer using the practised model.
