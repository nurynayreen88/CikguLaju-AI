# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 37 — Speaking 8  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5  
**Learning objective:** Practise integrated reading, speaking, writing and listening with classroom objects and imperatives.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 17, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 17  
**Audio guidance:** WB p17 uses CD1 Track 26.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Replay the Please imperative game and vary the classroom objects/instructions.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p17 read and choose the correct pictures, ask/answer in pairs, then draw a school bag and write a short modelled description.
**Pupils:** Follow the textbook task, listen/read and respond as directed.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p17: listen and number classroom objects according to the instructions.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Check picture choices as a class; monitor pair questions and review written bag descriptions before final copying.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Volunteers show their bag pictures and read descriptions; classmates listen for how many items are mentioned.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
