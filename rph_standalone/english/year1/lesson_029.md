# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 29 — Reading 6  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 1.1 / 1.1.1  
**Learning objective:** Discuss story meaning and practise the short vowel sound a.  
**Measurable objective (editorial, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can recognise and read at least two words containing the target short a sound on SB p15.
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 15, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 15  
**Audio guidance:** SB p15 uses CD1 Track 23; WB p15 uses CD1 Track 24.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Recycle polite/helping phrases from the story through a quick pass-the-object routine.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** On SB p15 replay and reread the story, find matching pictures and identify who says a target phrase; isolate the /a/ sound and practise the sound sentence at different speeds and volumes.
**Pupils:** Reread the story, match the pictures and practise the short /a/ sound sentence.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p15: identify the helping-each-other value, then write/match words containing the short a sound and listen/repeat.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Observable PBD evidence (editorial):** Teacher will ask pupils to identify two target words and check their sound-letter recognition.
**Teacher:** Check matching answers as a class and compare sound/spelling responses with partners.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Dictate the sound sentence, compare versions in pairs, then run a spelling check with familiar short-a words.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
