# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 26 — Speaking 6  
**Unit:** At School  
**Main SK / SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Learning objective:** Sing the classroom-object song and practise It's my / It isn't my language.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 12, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 12  
**Audio guidance:** SB p12 uses CD1 Track 17.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Flash classroom-object cards quickly and ask pupils to identify each item.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Use SB p12 song: identify desk objects, pre-teach 'in a mess', listen, repeat verse by verse and sing; then personalise the song with pupils' own classroom objects and complete the desk-matching task.
**Pupils:** Follow the textbook task, listen/read and respond as directed.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** WB p12: follow the lines and build sentences using It's my / It isn't my.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pairs compare desk-matching answers before class checking; elicit truthful It's my / It isn't my responses during the song game.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Sing the song again while substituting different classroom objects.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
