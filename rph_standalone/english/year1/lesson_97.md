# English Year 1 — Lesson 97: Listening 20

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP:** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Learning aim:** Follow and understand the Unit 3 picture story.
**Student's Book:** 38, 39
**Audio:** No verified track recorded

## Set Induction
Use the first story frame to predict characters, pets and what may happen.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
Work through the SB pp38-39 story: listen/read, pause at key frames for comprehension, establish event order and replay/reread for repetition and role practice.

## Assessment / PBD
Ask pupils to sequence key events and identify speakers/actions from the frames.

## Closure
Assign story roles in small groups and perform a shortened retelling.
