# English Year 1 — Lesson 94: Writing 19

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write a short pet word or phrase from a model.
**Materials:** Pet pictures, word cards, board and exercise books as needed.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified; no page or audio invented.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show two pet pictures and ask pupils to name one animal.
**Pupils:** Point to and name a pet.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Show a pet word or short phrase, demonstrate letter formation and provide a word bank.
**Pupils:** Copy the model and change one word using the word bank.

## Activity 2 — Practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the pet picture, word or partner and monitor responses.
**Pupils:** Repeat the task with a different example and less prompting.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check one legible and relevant written response.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Invite one final pet word or response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one target response.
