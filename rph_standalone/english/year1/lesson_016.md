# ENGLISH YEAR 1 — STANDALONE RPH

**Lesson:** 16 — Reading 4  
**Unit:** Friends  
**Main SK / SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK / SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Learning objective:** Present the picture story and recycle language from Friends.  
**Teaching materials:** Student's Book pp. 8, 9, Workbook activity; Teacher's Book pp. 8  
**Audio guidance:** Use the verified story recording for SB pp8–9; WB p8 uses CD1 Track 11.

### Set Induction
**Teacher:** Review pupils' names and ages through short teacher-pupil exchanges.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and demonstrate recalled language.

### Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Review the four Super Friends, play the story, pause to identify characters and the cat, then replay frame by frame for pupils to listen and repeat.
**Pupils:** Identify the four Super Friends and the cat; listen to each story frame, point to the speaker and repeat the dialogue.

### Activity 2 — Workbook
**Teacher:** Use WB p8 to review story phrases and match each Super Friend to the correct power.
**Pupils:** Complete the specified Workbook task and compare answers.

### Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Pause at key frames and ask pupils to identify who is speaking; check WB responses as a class.
**Pupils:** Provide responses and explain or correct answers during checking.

### Closure
**Teacher:** Put pupils in groups of five to rehearse and perform the story roles.
**Pupils:** Participate in the specified consolidation task.
