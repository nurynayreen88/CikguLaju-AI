# English Year 1 — Lesson 144: Writing 29

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write one legible food sentence using a model.
**Materials:** Food picture cards, word cards, board and exercise books.
**Textbook / Teacher's Book page:** Not verified.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a food picture and ask pupils to name it.
**Pupils:** Recall one food word.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Write a simple food sentence, model letter formation and offer a word bank.
**Pupils:** Copy the sentence and replace one food word appropriately.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the example and monitor pupils working individually or in pairs.
**Pupils:** Repeat the task with less support.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check one legible sentence with an appropriate word.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask for one final independent response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one successful target response.
