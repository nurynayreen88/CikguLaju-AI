# English Year 1 — Lesson 119: Listening 24

**Unit:** Lunchtime
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning aim:** Develop listening and practise food language from the textbook.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify the overall response and at least one requested detail from the SB p48 listening task.
**Student's Book:** 48
**Audio:** Use verified CD2 Tracks 06, 07 and 08.

## Set Induction (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Show the SB p48 listening pictures and review the food words needed for the task.
**Pupils:** Identify two pictured foods before listening.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed induction; pending Teacher's Book review.

## Activity 1
**Teacher (editorial, source-aligned):** Preview the SB p48 task; play the mapped listening material for gist, replay for one requested detail and check answers against the pictures.
**Pupils (editorial, source-aligned):** Look at the food pictures on SB p48, listen for the overall meaning, listen again for specific information and complete the page task.


## Assessment
**Observable PBD evidence (teacher-developed, pending review):** Check the overall listening response and one specific detail from the task.
Pairs compare before replay and whole-class checking.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal; pending review)
**Teacher:** Point to two SB p48 food pictures and ask which words were heard in the task.
**Pupils:** Identify two food words heard and point to the matching pictures.
**Provenance:** Teacher-developed closure; pending Teacher's Book review.
