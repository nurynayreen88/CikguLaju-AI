# English Year 1 — Lesson 106: Writing 22

**Unit:** Pet Show
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Learning aim:** Develop writing and language consolidation using the Unit 3 textbook page.
**Measurable objective (teacher-developed, pending review):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can complete the guided SB p41 writing task and produce one short response using a word or sentence frame from the model.
**Student's Book:** 41
**Audio:** No verified track recorded

## Set Induction
Run a short oral retrieval of pet vocabulary.

## Activity 1 — Student's Book
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to examine the SB p41 model, highlight useful words and sentence frames, demonstrate the guided task and support individual writing.
**Pupils:** Read the SB p41 model, identify useful words and sentence frames, complete the guided task and write a short independent response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Teacher checks each pupil's written response against the SB p41 model for a relevant word or sentence frame and gives one correction where needed.

## Closure (teacher-developed proposal, pending review)
**Teacher:** Invite partners to read their completed writing and check one target pet word.
**Pupils:** Read the written response aloud to a partner and check one target word against the model.
