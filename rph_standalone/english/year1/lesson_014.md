# English Year 1 — Lesson 14: Writing 3

**Unit:** Friends
**Main SK/SP (SOW):** 4.1 / 4.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW):** 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Objective (teacher-developed):** By the end of the lesson, pupils can copy two familiar words legibly.
**Materials:** Picture/word cards, board and exercise books as appropriate.
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 1 SOW lesson index; no textbook page or audio assigned. Exact SOW activity procedure still requires verification.

## Set Induction (5 min)
**Teacher:** Show a familiar Friends picture and elicit a known word or greeting.
**Pupils:** Identify one familiar word or greeting.

## Activity 1 — Guided practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate the starting point and formation of letters in two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Trace then copy two words in an exercise book.

## Activity 2 — Independent practice (10 min)
**Teacher:** Change the example or partner and invite individual responses; support pupils who need another model.
**Pupils:** Complete another example independently or with a new partner.

## Assessment / PBD (3 min)
**Teacher:** Check letter formation and readability of two copied words.

## Closure (2 min)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate one final response and recap the target language.
**Pupils:** Show one successful response from the lesson.
