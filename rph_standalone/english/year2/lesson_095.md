# English Year 2 — Lesson 95: Language Arts 19

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a four-line robot-themed dialogue and perform three lines with clear expression.
**Success criteria:** four drafted lines and three intelligible spoken lines.
**Teaching materials:** Teacher-created robot cards, board and individual record sheet.

**Original four-line dialogue:** “A: Hello, robot! What's your name? / B: My name is Zippy. / A: What can you do? / B: I can sing and dance!” **Creative substitutions:** Zippy→Blinky; sing→jump; dance→wave.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and invite a story idea.
**Pupils:** Suggest a character or action.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Perform the original four-line dialogue with a pupil and model substituting one action.
**Pupils:** Echo lines with expression.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pairs adapt the original dialogue to make four lines and rehearse expressive speaking.
**Pupils:** Draft four lines and perform in pairs.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check four drafted lines and at least three clear spoken lines per pupil.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate individual participation.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Invite a short final performance.
**Pupils:** Share a favourite line.
