# English Year 2 — Lesson 40: Language Arts 8

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils create and share two short lines about a house and recognise two spoken words.
**Success criteria (draft):** two meaningful creative lines and two accurate listening choices.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the prior Year 2 audit. Detailed SOW activity sequence remains unverified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Display house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a short teacher-created verse about a house.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide pupils to compose two lines and respond to two oral word cues.
**Pupils:** Share two lines and identify two spoken words.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
