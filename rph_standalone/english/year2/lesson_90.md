# English Year 2 — Lesson 90: Language Arts 18

**Unit:** The robot  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 5.3 / 5.3.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK. All scripts and tasks below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Objective:** Pupils perform three dialogue lines and add one creative robot action.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the stated numerical targets independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original four-line robot dialogue:** “A: Hello, little robot! / B: Beep! I can dance. / A: Can you jump too? / B: Yes! Watch me jump!” **Variation frame:** “I can ____.” Suggestions: sing, spin, clap.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a relevant picture and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name visible items and predict the topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Act out the original dialogue with robot gestures and demonstrate replacing *dance* with *sing*.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs rehearse the four lines, select a new action and perform to another pair.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen for three clear spoken lines and one suitable new action. Note independent accuracy and prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
