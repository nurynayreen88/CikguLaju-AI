# English Year 2 — Lesson 2: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Source:** Year 2 SOW mapping: Student's Book p.58. Super Minds Teacher's Book **printed p.T58** (PDF page 128), verified visually. SB p.58; Workbook p.58. Audio: CD2 Track 18 (Listen and look), CD2 Track 19 (Listen and chant).  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) identify and sequence at least five days of the week from spoken input and (2) give one intelligible spoken answer about a favourite day.  
**Success criteria:** At least five of seven days correctly identified in listening; one understandable oral response, e.g. “My favourite day is Saturday.”  
**Materials:** SB p.58, WB p.58, audio CD2 Tracks 18–19 or teacher-read script, seven day flashcards, board and pencils.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write today's day and date on the board. Ask “What day is it today?” and “What is your favourite day?” Elicit known day names without requiring all seven yet.  
**Pupils:** Answer orally and listen to classmates.

## Activity 1 — Listen, look and sequence (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display seven day flashcards in order. Say Monday–Sunday clearly; pupils repeat. Shuffle the cards. Direct pupils to SB p.58 and play CD2 Track 18. If audio is unavailable, read: “School on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Football match on Saturday! Hurray! Trip to the lake on Sunday! Hurray!” Play/read again, pausing after day names.  
**Pupils:** Point to the corresponding days in the book, repeat and help reorder the cards Monday–Sunday. Teacher checks listening comprehension by saying days in random order.

## Activity 2 — Chant and speaking practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play CD2 Track 19 and guide pupils to follow the chant in SB p.58; repeat each verse, then perform in groups. If the recording is unavailable, use a teacher-led rhythmic sequence of the seven day names (not represented as the original recording). Give each group a day flashcard. Ask “What's your favourite day?” and model “My favourite day is Sunday.”  
**Pupils:** Chant in groups, raise the matching day card when heard, then ask and answer the favourite-day question in pairs. Complete WB p.58 Activity 1 (write the days of the week) and Activity 2 (draw a favourite day and write its name), with support where needed.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Say seven day names in a mixed order and observe each pupil pointing to the correct flashcard or printed word. Individually ask pupils “What's your favourite day?” Record listening accuracy and oral intelligibility separately; offer scaffolding to pupils who need it.  
**Pupils:** Identify spoken day names and give their own short oral answer.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Hide one day flashcard and ask pupils to name it. Recap Monday–Sunday and invite two pupils to share their favourite day.  
**Pupils:** Name the missing day and respond.
