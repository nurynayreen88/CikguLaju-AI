# English Year 2 — Lesson 2: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify three spoken free-time words and answer one oral question.
**Success criteria (draft):** Three correct word-picture matches and one relevant oral response.
**Materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.58 (SOW mapping), pictures and word cards. Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures connected to Free Time and elicit two words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer a question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read a short teacher-created free-time description twice and model identifying one word.
**Pupils:** Follow the demonstration and respond once.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give three listening cues with picture cards and ask one oral question.
**Pupils:** Match three spoken words and answer one question.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Give one independent response.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful expressions.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one learned response.
