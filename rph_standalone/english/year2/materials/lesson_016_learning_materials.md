# Year 2 English — Lesson 016 | Learning Materials | Listening
**Teacher-created.** Aligned to the existing standalone RPH; official SOW verification pending.

## Original listening script — TEACHER ONLY
Read twice slowly; do not show pupils the script during listening.

“Hello! I'm Aina. On Monday, I read a book. On Tuesday, I draw a cat. On Wednesday, I ride my bike. On Friday, I play football with my friends. On Saturday, I swim with my sister. On Sunday, I rest at home.”

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Printable activity cards
| read a book | draw a cat | ride a bike | play football | swim |

## Pupil listening practice sheet
**Name:** __________ **Class:** __________  
Listen to Aina. Circle the activity you hear.

1. Monday: **read / swim**
2. Tuesday: **draw / football**
3. Wednesday: **bike / book**
4. Friday: **football / draw**
5. Saturday: **swim / rest**

## Letter naming practice
Name the letters in **read**, **draw**, **bike** and **swim**, then match each word to its activity card.

## Teacher key
1 read; 2 draw; 3 bike; 4 football; 5 swim.

## Teacher activity timing
Set induction 5 min (mime); Activity 1 15 min (read twice, pupils circle); Activity 2 25 min (third read, check and name letters); Assessment 10 min (separate independent PBD sheet); Closure 5 min (ask about Saturday).
