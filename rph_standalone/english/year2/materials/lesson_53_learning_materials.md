# English Year 2 Lesson 53 — Learning Materials

Unit: The old house. Speaking 2.2.2 and Listening 1.2.5.

Make two picture plans. Plan A: kitchen with a table; bedroom with one bed; living room with a sofa. Plan B: kitchen with two chairs; bedroom with two beds; living room with two sofas.

Teach the SOW expressions: "I know the answer!" "Can I try, please?" "Can I guess, please?"

Ask three short questions: "Where is the sofa?" "How many beds are there?" "Is there a table in the kitchen?"

Pupils practise requesting a turn before responding. Swap plans and repeat.
