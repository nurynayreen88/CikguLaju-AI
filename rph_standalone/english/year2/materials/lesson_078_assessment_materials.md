# English Year 2 Lesson 78 — assessment material

Unit: Get dressed! Source: visually verified Super Minds 1 Student's Book and Teacher's Book, with Year 2 SOW. 

After SB p.90 CD3 Track 15, ask pupils to match three material sources (cotton plant, cow, sheep) and name the material for three clothing items. Record correct source matching /3 and spoken material descriptions /3 separately. Key: cotton plant to cotton, cow to leather, sheep to wool. Accept woollen as adjective where suitable. Use SB pictures as visual clues.
