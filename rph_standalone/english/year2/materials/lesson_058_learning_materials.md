# Year 2 English Lesson 058 — Learning resource

Unit: Get dressed!
Focus: Reading — blend and segment phonemes.

Teacher-made practice, based on the Year 2 Scheme of Work. Use cards showing cap, sock, stop, and clap. The teacher models the sounds separately and pupils practise blending the sounds to say each word, then segmenting each word back into sounds. Keep letter names separate from phoneme sounds.

The Scheme of Work recommends selecting vocabulary from Student's Book page 82. These practice words have not yet been checked against that page. Do not describe them as copied from the book.
