# Year 2 English — Lesson 40 | Learning Materials | Language Arts

Original teacher-developed resource aligned to the existing standalone RPH. Official SOW verification and printable output QA pending.

Original model verse: In my bedroom, I can sleep. In the kitchen, I can eat. In the living room, I can play. In my house, I like to stay. Print cards bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, living room, sleep, eat, wash, play. Pupil frames: In the ____, I can ____. In the ____, I can ____. Pupils compose and rehearse two meaningful lines. For listening pictures draw contrasting pairs: ordinary bedroom vs moon-shaped bedroom; ordinary kitchen vs kitchen on roof.

## Teacher administration
Use within the existing 60-minute RPH structure: set induction 5 minutes; Activity 1 15 minutes; Activity 2 25 minutes; independent assessment 10 minutes; closure 5 minutes. Do not show answer keys during pupil assessment. Record individual evidence separately from supported practice.
