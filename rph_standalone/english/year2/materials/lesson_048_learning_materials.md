# English Year 2 Lesson 48: Learning Materials

**Skill:** Writing. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Print models: My bedroom has a bed and a desk. My house is small but comfortable. Model message: Hi Amir, My bedroom has a bed and a desk. From Ali. Pupil chooses two rooms and writes two short messages to classmates, checking greeting, room/object, capital and full stop, closing. Each message must contain a sentence using and or but meaningfully.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
