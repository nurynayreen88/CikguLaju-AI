# Year 2 English Lesson 90 — learning

Language Arts robot chant. Based on official Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Teacher-created robot chant: Robot, robot, turn around. Robot, robot, touch the ground. Robot, robot, clap your hands. Robot, robot, now you stand. Pupils mime, then change two actions and create their own chant.
