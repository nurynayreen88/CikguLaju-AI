# Lesson 067 — Assessment Materials

Show the Student's Book p.85 picture and conduct a pair guessing game. Each pupil asks two third-person questions about what a person is wearing and answers two with Yes, he/she is or No, he/she isn't. Record meaningful questions 0–2 and answers 0–2. For the complementary writing standard, pupils write two guided sentences with initial capitals and full stops. Record punctuation 0–2. Verify picture and audio are accessible in bot. NOT READY.
