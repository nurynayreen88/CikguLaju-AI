# Year 2 English Lesson 91 — learning

Topic: The robot. Non-textbook extension grounded in Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Listen to five teacher-created robot descriptions supported by pictures, then pupils identify body-part and action details. Use SB pp.92–95 vocabulary, but label all spoken scripts teacher-created. Finish by describing two pictured robot parts.
