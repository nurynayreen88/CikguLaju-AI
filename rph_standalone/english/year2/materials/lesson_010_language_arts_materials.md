# Year 2 English — Lesson 010 | Language Arts | Free Time
**Teacher-developed supplementary materials.** Uses the **original chant supplied in the existing RPH**; official SOW verification remains pending.

## Original class chant: “My Happy Day”
I read my book, one, two, three!  
I draw a picture, look at me!  
I ride my bike, round and round!  
I kick a ball across the ground!

## Four action cards
1. **READ** — mime opening and reading a book.
2. **DRAW** — mime drawing a picture.
3. **RIDE** — mime holding bicycle handlebars.
4. **KICK** — mime kicking a ball.

## Group chant strips (cut along the lines)
**Group A:** I read my book, one, two, three!  
**Group B:** I draw a picture, look at me!  
**Group C:** I ride my bike, round and round!  
**Group D:** I kick a ball across the ground!

## Pair performance card
**Partner A:** Say the first two lines with actions.  
**Partner B:** Say the last two lines with actions.  
**Together:** Perform all four lines. Swap roles.

## Oral response cards
**Question 1:** Which activity do you like?  
**Answer:** I like __________.  
**Question 2:** What do you do after school?  
**Answer:** I __________ after school.

## Activity timing (matches existing RPH)
- 5 min: select a favourite activity card.
- 15 min: teacher models the original chant and gestures; groups practise.
- 25 min: pairs rehearse and perform, then answer two questions.
- 10 min: assess four lines, two matching gestures and two oral answers.
- 5 min: final group performance.
