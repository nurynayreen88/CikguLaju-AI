# Year 2 English — Lesson 027 | Learning Materials | Speaking
Teacher-created; official SOW verification pending.

## Picture and action cards
| READING | SWIMMING | FOOTBALL | CYCLING |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read a book | Swim | Play football | Ride a bicycle |

## Model pair dialogue
A: Do you like swimming?  
B: Yes, I do.  
A: What do you do on Saturday?  
B: I play football.  
A: Do you like reading?  
B: No, I don't.

## Three-step instruction strip
**First,** eat fruit.  
**Next,** play outside.  
**Finally,** drink water.

Pupils may choose other suitable healthy actions while keeping the three-step sequence.

## Three teacher question cards
1. Do you play outside?
2. Do you eat fruit?
3. What do you do on Saturday?

## Classroom sequence (60 minutes)
5 min: mime four activities.  
15 min: model the dialogue and `First / Next / Finally`.  
25 min: interview a partner with three questions, swap roles and give a three-step healthy-living sequence.  
10 min: use separate PBD sheet.  
5 min: state a favourite activity and one instruction.

**Note:** The sequence is a spoken healthy-habit instruction task, not a medical recommendation.
