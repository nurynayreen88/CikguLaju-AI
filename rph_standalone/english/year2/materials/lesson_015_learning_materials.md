# Year 2 English — Lesson 015 | Learning Materials | Language Arts 3
**Teacher-created.** Aligned to the existing standalone RPH; official SOW verification pending.

## Pupil rhyme sheet: My Free Time
I read a book on Monday.  
I draw a star on Tuesday.  
I play a game on Wednesday.  
I swim and smile on Saturday.

## Four action cards (cut out)
| READ | DRAW | PLAY | SWIM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open a book | Draw a star in the air | Mime a ball game | Move arms as if swimming |

## Phonics cut-out cards
| sun | run | clap | stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| s - u - n | r - u - n | c - l - a - p | s - t - o - p |

## Guided pupil tasks
1. Listen as the teacher reads the rhyme; point to each line.
2. Echo all four lines and do the matching actions.
3. In groups of four, practise one line each, then recite all four lines together.
4. In pairs, perform all four lines with at least two gestures.
5. Say the sounds on each phonics card, then blend them into a word.

## Teacher activity timing
Set induction 5 min (mime activities); Activity 1 15 min (read, echo and model blending); Activity 2 25 min (group and pair performance, phonics); Assessment 10 min (use separate assessment sheet); Closure 5 min (recall one line and favourite activity).

**Important:** The phonics cards are an extension from the existing RPH, not a quotation from the official SOW.
