# Year 2 English — Lesson 007 | Speaking | Free Time
**Teacher-developed supplementary materials.** Aligned to the existing standalone RPH; official SOW verification remains pending.

## Materials to print
Cut out four picture cards. Each card should display its label and an illustration added by the teacher: **reading a book**, **cycling**, **swimming**, **playing football**. These text labels also work as a low-ink alternative.

## Card prompts
1. READING A BOOK — A child reads a book.
2. CYCLING — A child rides a bicycle.
3. SWIMMING — A child swims in a pool.
4. PLAYING FOOTBALL — A child kicks a football.

## Pupil interview strip (one per pair)
A: Do you like ______?  
B: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.  
A: What do you do after school?  
B: I ______ after school.  

**Task:** Take turns. Ask and answer **three** questions using different cards. Change roles.

## Listening choice sheet
**Name:** __________ **Class:** ______  
Teacher reads the cues in mixed order. Pupils circle one answer for each.
1. football / reading / swimming / cycling
2. football / reading / swimming / cycling
3. football / reading / swimming / cycling
4. football / reading / swimming / cycling

**Teacher script (read in this order):**
1. “I kick a ball.” → football
2. “I turn the pages of a book.” → reading
3. “I move in the water.” → swimming
4. “I ride on two wheels.” → cycling

## Activity timing (matches existing RPH)
- Set induction 5 min: mime two activities.
- Activity 1 15 min: model dialogue and two cues.
- Activity 2 25 min: pair interviews and all four listening cues.
- Assessment 10 min: independently check **three** oral exchanges and **three** listening responses per pupil.
- Closure 5 min: “Which activity do you like best?”
