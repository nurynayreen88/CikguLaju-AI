# English Year 2 Lesson 46: Learning Materials

**Skill:** Listening. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Teacher-only script: This house has three rooms. In the kitchen, there is a round table. In the bedroom, there are two beds. In the living room, there is a green sofa. Pupil worksheet: draw three boxes labelled kitchen, bedroom, living room. Listen twice and draw a round table, two beds, and a green sofa in their correct boxes. Practise describing one room with In the ____, there is/are ____.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
