# Year 2 English — Lesson 37 | Assessment / PBD Materials | Speaking

Original teacher-developed resource aligned to the existing standalone RPH. Official SOW verification and printable output QA pending.

Teacher checks each pupil independently asks three intelligible information questions and describes two objects accurately using their own plan. Plan A key: table/kitchen, bed/bedroom, sofa/living room. Plan B key: two chairs/kitchen, two beds/bedroom, two sofas/living room. Record questions /3, descriptions /2, singular/plural accuracy and prompts. Accept accurate questions and descriptions based on assigned plan.

## Teacher administration
Use within the existing 60-minute RPH structure: set induction 5 minutes; Activity 1 15 minutes; Activity 2 25 minutes; independent assessment 10 minutes; closure 5 minutes. Do not show answer keys during pupil assessment. Record individual evidence separately from supported practice.
