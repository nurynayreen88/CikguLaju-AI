# English Year 2 Lesson 47: Learning Materials

**Skill:** Reading. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Original pupil text: Nina lives in a little house. There is a blue sofa in the living room. There are two chairs in the kitchen. Nina reads a book in her bedroom. Her cat sleeps near the sofa. Questions: What colour is the sofa? How many chairs are in the kitchen? Where does Nina read? Where does the cat sleep? Writing frame: In Nina's ____, there is/are ____. Model The sofa is blue.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
