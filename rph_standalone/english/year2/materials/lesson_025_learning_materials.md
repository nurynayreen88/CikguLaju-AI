## Pupil rhyme — Free-Time Days
Monday, Monday, read with me.  
Tuesday, Tuesday, draw a tree.  
Saturday, Saturday, ride my bike.  
Sunday, Sunday, swim — what fun I like!

## Four cut-out activity cards
| READ A BOOK | DRAW A TREE | RIDE A BIKE | SWIM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mime opening a book | Trace a tree in the air | Hold imaginary handlebars | Mime swimming |

## Teacher-guided learning
5 min: show four cards, mime each action.  
15 min: read the rhyme aloud with gestures, pupils echo optionally.  
25 min: four groups practise one line each; reread the rhyme with picture responses, then practise selecting the main idea of two spoken sentences.  
10 min: use the independent assessment sheet.  
5 min: pupils choose a favourite activity.

**Important:** Main skill assesses non-verbal response and enjoyment, not memorised recitation.
