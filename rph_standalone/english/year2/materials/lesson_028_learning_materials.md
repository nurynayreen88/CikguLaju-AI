## Pupil passage — Our Saturday
On Saturday, Adam plays football in the park with his brother. His sister Sara reads a book under a tree. After lunch, Adam rides his bike. Sara draws a picture of a cat. In the evening, they watch TV with their parents.

## Reading questions
1. Where does Adam play football?
2. Who plays football with Adam?
3. What does Sara do under a tree?
4. What does Adam do after lunch?
5. Who watches TV in the evening?

## Writing instruction models
“Read a book.”  
“Draw a cat.”

Pupils write **two new or adapted meaningful instructions** using action verbs from the passage.

## Teacher key
1 in the park; 2 his brother; 3 reads a book; 4 rides his bike; 5 Adam, Sara and their parents.

## Timing
5 min picture prompts; 15 min model locating `park`; 25 min read, answer and write instructions; 10 min independent assessment; 5 min discuss a textual clue.
