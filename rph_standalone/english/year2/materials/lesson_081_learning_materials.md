# Year 2 English Lesson 81 — learning

Listening and speaking. Based on official Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Use a miming extension from SB p.89 Activity 4. Teacher points to or mimes a cap, shoes and shirt while giving two spoken clothing messages. Pupils identify the indicated object and describe its colour using the available cards. This is a teacher-created extension, not SB text.
