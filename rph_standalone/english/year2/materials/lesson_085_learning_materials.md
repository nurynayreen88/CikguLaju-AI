# Year 2 English Lesson 85 — learning

Language Arts song. Based on official Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Teacher-created action song about familiar clothing: Hat on my head, shoes on my feet. Shirt on my body, ready for the street. Use gestures and clothing cards. Pupils listen, sing and respond by pointing to the named item. SOW calls for song response; teacher-created song is not official audio.
