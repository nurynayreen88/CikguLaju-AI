# English Year 2 Lesson 61 — Learning Materials

Unit: Get dressed! Focus: Listening. Original teacher-developed resource based on Primary Year 2 SOW. NOT READY: exact Student's Book vocabulary/character and print QA pending.

Clothing cards: one red T-shirt, one blue T-shirt, one green T-shirt and one yellow cap. Teacher asks five short questions: Which T-shirt is red? Which T-shirt is blue? Is this cap yellow? Which T-shirt do you like? Is this T-shirt green? Teacher points to each item while speaking; pupils use the visual clues. SOW suggests comparing styles and designs of clothes; cards are original.
