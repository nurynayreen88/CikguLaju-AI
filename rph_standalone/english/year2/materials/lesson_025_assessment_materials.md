## Task A: Non-verbal rhyme responses
Teacher reads three lines separately, without displaying the matching cards:
1. “Monday, Monday, read with me.”
2. “Saturday, Saturday, ride my bike.”
3. “Sunday, Sunday, swim — what fun I like!”

Pupils point to the correct activity card or mime the action.

## Task B: Listening main idea
Teacher reads **without displaying the text**:
1. “On Tuesday, I draw a tree.”
2. “On Sunday, I go swimming.”

Pupil sheet: Circle the matching activity.
1. **draw a tree / ride a bike**
2. **read a book / go swimming**

**Answer key:** A: read, ride a bike, swim. B: draw a tree, go swimming.
