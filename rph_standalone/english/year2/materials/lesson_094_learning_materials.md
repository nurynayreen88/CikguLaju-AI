# Year 2 English Lesson 94 — learning

Topic: The robot. Non-textbook extension grounded in Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Use teacher-created robot instruction cards: Touch your head. Raise your arms. Turn around. Clap your hands. Pupils write three short instructions, then swap cards and play a sentence-level reading game.
