# Year 2 English — Lesson 019 | Learning Materials | Writing
**Teacher-created; official SOW QA pending.** Unit: Free Time.

## Pupil planning and drafting sheet
**Name:** ______ **Class:** ______

**Word bank:** read a book · ride my bike · play football · draw pictures · go swimming · watch TV · play computer games

**Day cards:** Monday | Wednesday | Saturday | Sunday

### Model paragraph
On Monday, I read a book. On Wednesday, I ride my bike and draw pictures. On Saturday, I play football. On Sunday, I draw pictures.

### Editing example
`on saturday i play football` → `On Saturday, I play football.`

### Draft your own four sentences
1. On Monday, I ____________________________________.
2. On Wednesday, I ______________ and ______________.
3. On Saturday, I __________________________________.
4. On Sunday, I ____________________________________.

### Self-check
☐ Capitals at sentence beginnings  
☐ Capital letters in day names  
☐ Spaces between words  
☐ Full stops  
☐ `and` joins two activity phrases  
☐ Sentences make sense

**Revision:** Correct two genuine errors, or, if already accurate, explain two checks you made. Preserve the draft for comparison.

## Teacher sequence (60 minutes)
5 min mime and day cards; 15 min analyse model and correction; 25 min plan, draft and revise; 10 min independent assessment using separate sheet; 5 min share a proofreading strategy.
