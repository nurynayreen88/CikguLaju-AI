# Year 2 English Lesson 84 — learning

Spelling and reading game. Based on official Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Pupils create two sentence-clue cards using familiar clothing words and exchange them in pairs. Teacher models: You wear this on your head. Answer: hat. Pupils spell hat, shoes, shirt and socks in their answers.
