# English Year 2 Lesson 134 — learning

Unit: At the beach. Source: Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Use the SOW beach-trip planning idea. Pairs plan together using present-time suggestions such as On Monday, let's build a sandcastle. Good idea. Pupils then write the plan in a diary using words or short notes, not future-tense sentences.
