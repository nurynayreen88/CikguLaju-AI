# Year 2 English Lesson 93 — learning

Topic: The robot. Non-textbook extension grounded in Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Use robot-unit phonics cards for hat, hand, leg and step. Model blending and segmenting phonemes in each. Pupils sound out and blend, then segment independently. Explicitly distinguish letters from phonemes.
