Teacher-created; official SOW verification pending.

## Original example verse — After School
I read my book when school is done.  
I play with friends and have some fun.

## Cut-out word cards

|---|---|---|---|---|
| book | ball | bike | picture | friends |

## Pupil verse worksheet
**Name:** ______ **Class:** ______

**Frame A**  
I __________ when school is done.  
I __________ and have some fun.

**Alternative Frame B**  
I like to __________ today.  
I __________ and laugh and play.

**Example substitution:**  
I draw a picture when school is done.  
I ride my bike and have some fun.

**My two original lines:**
1. ____________________________________
2. ____________________________________

Rehearse your two lines aloud with a partner. Use one suitable gesture per line.

## Teacher sequence
5 min read model; 15 min notice activity words and `done/fun` endings; 25 min create, peer review and rehearse; 10 min individual PBD; 5 min volunteer performances.

**Mark meaning and participation, not perfect rhyme.** The RPH permits the frame without rhyme for pupils who need support.
