## Pupil reading passage: Hana's Busy Week
Hana likes her free time. On Monday, she reads a storybook with her sister. On Wednesday, she rides her bicycle in the park. On Friday, she plays football with her friends. On Saturday, she helps her mother in the garden. On Sunday, she draws pictures at home. Hana likes Sunday because she loves drawing.

## Guided questions
1. What does Hana do on Monday?
2. Where does she ride her bicycle?
3. Who does she play football with?
4. What does she do on Saturday?
5. Why does she like Sunday?

**Teacher answer key:** 1 reads a storybook with her sister; 2 in the park; 3 her friends; 4 helps her mother in the garden; 5 because she loves drawing.

5 min vocabulary gestures; 15 min model scanning for Monday; 25 min pairs locate details, then independently answer questions; 10 min use separate assessment sheet; 5 min explain one textual clue.
