# English Year 2 Lesson 63 — Assessment Materials

Unit: Get dressed! Focus: Reading. Original teacher-developed resource based on Primary Year 2 SOW. NOT READY: exact Student's Book vocabulary/character and print QA pending.

Key: this for one; these for several. Score correct matches /4 and independent rereading /2. No invented characters.
