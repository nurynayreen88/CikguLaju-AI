## Pupil phonics check (six items)

Listen as your teacher reads each word. Tick one column.

**Key:** Long-a, Other, Long-a, Other, Long-a, Long-a.  
**Letter-naming check:** Ask pupils to name each letter in **play**, **day** and **game**.

## Activity timing (matches existing RPH)
- 5 min: find the letter **a** on the cards.
- 15 min: teacher models contrasting vowel sounds.
- 25 min: pupils sort all eight cards and name letters.
- 10 min: independent six-item check and letter naming.
- 5 min: pupil gives one long-a example.
