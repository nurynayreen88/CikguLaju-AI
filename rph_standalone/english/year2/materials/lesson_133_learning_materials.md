# English Year 2 Lesson 133 — learning

Unit: At the beach. Source: Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Use SOW picture-card matching: prepare pictures of objects in different places, such as a fish in the sea. Pupils read questions such as Where's the fish? and match them to answers such as It's in the sea. Include five picture-question-answer sets using beach vocabulary.
