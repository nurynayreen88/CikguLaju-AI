# English Year 2 Lesson 72 — learning

Unit: Get dressed! Skill: Speaking. Original teacher-developed material based on Year 2 SOW.

Prepare clothing cards and model a three-step instruction sequence: Pick up the shirt. Put on the shirt. Show me your shoes. Pupils give the steps in order while partners respond by nodding and pointing. Swap roles.
