# English Year 2 Lesson 48: Assessment Materials

**Skill:** Writing. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Collect two independent revised messages. For each, assess meaningful conjunction use, capitals, full stop and clear room/object description. Accept and to join objects or but to contrast descriptions. Record conjunction sentences /2, punctuation /2 and message components /2, noting prompts. Do not mark as correct a conjunction that does not connect meaningful phrases.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
