### 5. Read aloud
Read one of your sentences to the teacher.

## Teacher reference
**Editing key:** “On Saturday, I swim.”  
**Possible model answers:** “I like to read a book.” “On Saturday, I ride my bike.” “After school, I draw a picture.” Other meaningful grammatical answers are acceptable.

## Activity timing (matches existing RPH)
- 5 min: discuss free-time picture prompts.
- 15 min: model three sentences and edit the example.
- 25 min: write, peer-check and revise.
- 10 min: inspect three sentences and hear one independently read aloud.
- 5 min: recall one punctuation rule.
