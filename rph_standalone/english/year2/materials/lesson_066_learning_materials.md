# Lesson 066 — Learning Materials

Use Student's Book p.84: Cool Cats song, CD3 track 06. Prepare clothes cards for shoes, jackets, socks, shorts, T-shirts, sweaters, skirts and hats. After listening, pupils identify clothing words and point to matching pictures. Model colour descriptions using this and these, as in Student's Book Activity 2. Recycle cool and super.

Teacher notes: use the book's original audio and picture. Do not substitute the old raincoat story for the page activity. SOW: Listening 1.3.1 and Speaking 2.1.5. NOT READY until delivery QA.
