# English Year 2 Lesson 47: Assessment Materials

**Skill:** Reading. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Use a clean copy of Nina's House. Pupils answer all four questions independently and write one original descriptive sentence. Key: blue; two; in her bedroom; near the sofa. Score reading /4 and sentence separately for meaning /1, relevant room vocabulary /1 and readability /1. Success at least 3/4 reading and one accurate written description.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
