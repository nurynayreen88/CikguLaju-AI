# English Year 2 Lesson 45: Assessment Materials

**Skill:** Language Arts. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Individually name two details from the mini-text, then describe two objects: Aina's small blue boat and Ben's warm red coat. Teacher key accepts Aina, Ben, sea, mountains, boat, coat as correctly identified details; descriptions should mention appropriate properties. Record details /2, accurate descriptions /2, support.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
