# Year 2 English — Lesson 017 | Learning Materials | Speaking
**Teacher-created; official SOW QA pending.** Unit: Free Time. Main skill: speaking; complementary: writing.

## Five printable survey prompt cards
| watch TV | ride a bike | play football | read a book | go swimming |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Dialogue practice
A: Do you ride a bike at the weekend?  
B: Yes, I do. I ride my bike on Saturday.  
A: Do you watch TV at the weekend?  
B: No, I don't. I read a book.

## Pupil partner-survey sheet
**Name:** ______ **Partner:** ______
Ask **three different** questions, then swap roles.

| Activity asked about | Partner's answer (Yes / No) | Partner's extra detail (if given) |
|---|---|---|
| | | |
| | | |
| | | |

**Write two sentences from confirmed survey answers.**
1. My partner ____________________________________.
2. My partner ____________________________________.

**Important:** If a partner answers only Yes/No, ask a follow-up before writing a specific day or activity. Do not invent partner details.

## Teacher sequence (60 minutes)
5 min mime; 15 min model and rehearse; 25 min three-question surveys, swap roles, write two sentences; 10 min independent assessment using separate sheet; 5 min report one partner activity.
