# Year 2 English — Lesson 029 | Learning Materials | Writing
Teacher-created; SOW verification pending.

## Pupil word bank
read a book · play football · ride my bike · draw pictures · go swimming

## Model
After school, I read a book.  
On Saturday, I play football.  
On Sunday, I draw pictures.

## Editing example
`on sunday i ride my bike`  
Correct form: `On Sunday, I ride my bike.`

## Self-edit checklist
☐ Capital at the start  
☐ Capital for Saturday and Sunday  
☐ Spaces between words

**Revision:** Rewrite any sentence needing improvement, keeping the first draft for comparison.

## Timing
5 min mime; 15 min model and edit; 25 min draft, partner check and revise; 10 min assessment sheet; 5 min explain one correction.
