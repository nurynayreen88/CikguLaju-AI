## Independent reading assessment
Use a **clean copy** of the full `Hana's Busy Week` passage from the Learning Materials. Work without partner assistance.

**Task A:** Underline the four day–activity details for **Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday**.

**Task B:** Answer the following from the printed text:
1. Where does Hana ride her bicycle? ____________________
2. Who does Hana play football with? ____________________
3. Why does Hana like Sunday? ____________________

## Teacher key
A: Wednesday—rides bicycle in the park; Friday—plays football with friends; Saturday—helps mother in the garden; Sunday—draws pictures at home.  
B: 1 In the park; 2 Her friends; 3 Because she loves drawing.
