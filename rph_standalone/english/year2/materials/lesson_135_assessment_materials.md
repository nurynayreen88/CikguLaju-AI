# English Year 2 Lesson 135 — assessment

Unit: At the beach. Source: Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

After listening, pupils respond to three broad supported prompts: name one thing they remember, show a favourite part and put two story pictures in order. Record engagement and supported narrative response /3; do not assess exact recall of every word.
