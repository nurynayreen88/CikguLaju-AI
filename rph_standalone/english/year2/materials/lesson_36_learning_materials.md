# English Year 2 — Lesson 36: Learning Materials

## Teacher-read narrative
Mia enters an old house. First, she visits the kitchen and sees two chairs. Next, she goes upstairs to the bedroom and finds a small bed. Finally, she goes downstairs to the living room and sees two sofas.

## Four picture-card descriptions for preparation
A. Kitchen with a table and two chairs.
B. Bedroom with a small bed.
C. Living room with two sofas.
D. Bathroom with a bath.

## Pupil practice
Listen to the narrative twice. Arrange the event cards in story order. Then listen and point to the correct card for these clues:

1. Two chairs are here.
2. There is a small bed here.
3. There are two sofas here.
4. You can see a bath here.

Teacher key: event order A, B, C; clue cards A, B, C, D.

## Classroom sequence
Set induction 5 minutes; modelling 15 minutes; guided practice 25 minutes; independent PBD 10 minutes; closure 5 minutes.
