# English Year 2 Lesson 73 — learning

Unit: Get dressed! Skill: Reading phonics. Original teacher-developed material based on Year 2 SOW.

Use hat, red and stop phoneme cards. Teacher models initial, medial and final sounds, then blending. Pupils identify sounds and blend the words aloud. Picture matching is optional and does not replace phonics.
