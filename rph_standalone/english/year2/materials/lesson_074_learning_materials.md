# English Year 2 Lesson 74 — learning

Unit: Get dressed! Skill: Writing. Original teacher-developed material based on Year 2 SOW.

Give outfit pictures with a red hat, blue shirt and black shoes. Pupils plan two ideas, list four clothing words, draft two sentences, check spelling and revise. Models: I have a red hat. My shoes are black.
