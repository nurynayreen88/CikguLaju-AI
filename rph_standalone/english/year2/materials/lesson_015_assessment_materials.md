# Year 2 English — Lesson 015 | Assessment / PBD
**Name:** __________ **Class:** __________ **Date:** __________

## Task A — Read and perform
Perform all four lines of **My Free Time** (from the Learning Materials). Show at least two matching gestures. Teacher records individual evidence; paired performance is allowed but each pupil must be assessed.

## Task B — Blend and read
Read these four cards in mixed order: **sun**, **run**, **clap**, **stop**. Teacher may prompt individual sounds, but record whether the response was independent.

## Teacher scoring rubric
| Criterion | Full evidence | Partial evidence | Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhyme | 4 intelligible lines | 1–3 intelligible lines | No intelligible line |
| Gestures | ≥2 appropriate gestures | 1 appropriate gesture | No matching gesture |
| Phonics | 4 accurately blended words | 1–3 accurately blended words | No accurate word |

**Answer key:** sun = /s/ /ʌ/ /n/; run = /r/ /ʌ/ /n/; clap = /k/ /l/ /æ/ /p/; stop = /s/ /t/ /ɒ/ /p/ (pronunciation varies by accent). Accept correct word blending in the teacher's modelled English accent.  
**Success criteria:** 4 intelligible rhyme lines, at least 2 gestures, 4 correctly blended words.
