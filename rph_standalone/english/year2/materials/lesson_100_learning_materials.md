# Year 2 English Lesson 100 — learning

Topic: The robot. Non-textbook extension grounded in Year 2 Scheme of Work.

Create a short robot action rhyme with wave, clap, turn and stop. Pupils respond non-verbally to the rhyme, then follow four teacher-given classroom instructions. Use familiar unit language and gestures.
