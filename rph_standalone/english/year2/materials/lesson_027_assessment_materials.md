## Task A: Speaking
Give **three basic healthy-living instructions in sequence** using `First`, `Next`, and `Finally`. Example: “First, eat fruit. Next, play outside. Finally, drink water.” Teacher should allow meaningful original alternatives.

## Task B: Supported listening questions
Teacher asks without displaying written text:
1. Do you play outside?
2. Do you eat fruit?
3. What do you do on Saturday?

Pupil answers appropriately. For yes/no questions, accept either truthful response. For Saturday, accept a relevant personal activity.
