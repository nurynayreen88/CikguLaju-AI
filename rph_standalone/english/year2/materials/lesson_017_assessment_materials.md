# Year 2 English — Lesson 017 | Assessment / PBD | Speaking and Writing
**Name:** ______ **Class:** ______

## Individual assessment instructions
1. Ask your partner **two different** `Do you ... at the weekend?` questions using different prompt cards.
2. Answer **one** partner question appropriately.
3. Write **two understandable sentences** reporting only information actually obtained from your partner.

**Prompt cards:** watch TV; ride a bike; play football; read a book; go swimming.

## Teacher checklist
| Pupil | Q1 formed and intelligible | Q2 formed and intelligible | One relevant answer | Written sentence 1 meaningful | Written sentence 2 meaningful | Independent / prompted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | |

**Success criteria for full lesson:** Three intelligible exchanges during the survey and two understandable written sentences. **Assessment sampling:** the existing RPH specifies observing two questions plus one answer in the 10-minute PBD phase; check the third exchange from the survey record.

**Answer guidance:** Accept `Yes, I do` / `No, I don't` with relevant follow-up and accurate sentences; do not use a fixed key for genuine partner answers. Mark speaking and writing separately.
