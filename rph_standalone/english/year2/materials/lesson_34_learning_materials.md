# Year 2 English — Lesson 34 | Learning Materials | Writing

Original teacher-developed resource aligned to the existing standalone RPH. Official SOW verification and printable output QA pending.

Four-room plan: bedroom—bed; kitchen—table; living room—sofa; bathroom—bath. Print seven word cards: bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, bed, table, sofa, hall. Model: There is a bed in the bedroom. There is a table in the kitchen. There is a sofa in the living room. Pupil draws four rooms, writes three sentences, and asks partner Where is the bed? and What is in the living room? Teacher models capital letters and full stops.

## Teacher administration
Use within the existing 60-minute RPH structure: set induction 5 minutes; Activity 1 15 minutes; Activity 2 25 minutes; independent assessment 10 minutes; closure 5 minutes. Do not show answer keys during pupil assessment. Record individual evidence separately from supported practice.
