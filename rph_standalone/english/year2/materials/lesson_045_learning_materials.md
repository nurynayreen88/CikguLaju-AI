# English Year 2 Lesson 45: Learning Materials

**Skill:** Language Arts. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Read aloud: Aina lives near the sea. Her friend Ben lives near snowy mountains. Aina has a small blue boat. Ben has a warm red coat. They both enjoy playing with friends. Pupil sheet: Circle two people (Aina / Ben / Mia); two places (sea / snowy mountains / school); describe the boat and coat using colour and size. Optional house rhyme from original RPH may be used for enjoyment but is not assessed.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
