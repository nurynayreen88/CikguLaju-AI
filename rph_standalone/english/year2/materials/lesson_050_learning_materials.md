# English Year 2 Lesson 50: Learning Materials

**Skill:** Language Arts. Original teacher-developed content based on the current standalone RPH; SOW and PDF QA pending.

Original habitat rhyme: The penguin walks on snowy ground. The parrot flies and looks around. The camel crosses desert sand. The shark swims in the ocean grand. Prepare gesture cards penguin walks, parrot flies, camel crosses sand, shark swims. Teacher narrative: First, a penguin walks on the snow. Next, it sees a seal near the water. Finally, it swims home. Pupil sequences three event cards.

**Timing:** Set induction 5 min, model 15 min, guided activity 25 min, individual assessment 10 min, closure 5 min. Keep assessment evidence separate from guided practice.
