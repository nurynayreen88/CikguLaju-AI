# English Year 2 Lesson 135 — learning

Unit: At the beach. Source: Primary Year 2 Scheme of Work.

SOW suggests pupils listen to and enjoy a beach story without trying to understand every word. Use a suitable teacher-read short beach narrative or an authorised copy of Kipper the Dog: The Seaside. Focus on enjoyment, sequence and response rather than detailed vocabulary testing. Do not reproduce copyrighted story text.
