# Lesson 068 — Assessment Materials

With Student's Book pp.86–87 visible, ask pupils to select the main idea: A misunderstanding about a cap; a meal; a rainy journey. Key: misunderstanding about a cap. Ask pupils to locate four details from the actual comic: missing item, person identified with the cap, what happens to the cap, and the value of the story. Teacher checks responses against comic panels and records main idea 0–1 and supporting details 0–4. Do not score invented story characters. NOT READY until QA.
