# English Year 2 — lesson 40 assessment materials.md

Original teaching resource aligned with the existing standalone lesson. Official SOW and printable QA pending.

Each pupil creates and performs two meaningful house-themed lines using the lesson word bank. Read two listening prompts without displaying text: The unusual house has a bedroom shaped like a moon. The kitchen is on the roof of the house. Pupils choose pictures showing a moon-shaped bedroom and a rooftop kitchen. Record two meaningful lines, intelligible presentation and two correct listening choices.

Use the existing lesson timing and record independent PBD evidence. Do not show teacher keys before assessment.
