# English Year 2 — Lesson 9: Writing

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in existing Year 2 mapping; examples and tasks are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three sentences about free-time activities using correct capital letters, spacing and full stops and read one sentence aloud.  
**Success criteria:** Three meaningful sentences, at least two with correct punctuation, and one intelligible oral reading.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books and word bank below.

**Word bank:** read a book, draw a picture, ride my bike, play football, swim.  
**Writing frames:** “I like to ____.” “On Saturday, I ____.” “After school, I ____.”  
**Model:** “I like to read a book. On Saturday, I ride my bike. After school, I draw a picture.”  
**Editing example:** “on saturday i swim” → “On Saturday, I swim.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of reading and cycling. Ask “What do you like to do?”  
**Pupils:** Name one preferred activity.

## Activity 1 — Model and edit (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the three model sentences, underline activity phrases and demonstrate correcting the editing example. Emphasise capitals at sentence beginnings and for day names.  
**Pupils:** Identify errors, say the corrected sentence and practise one frame orally.

## Activity 2 — Plan, write and revise (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to select three different activities from the word bank and complete the three writing frames. Pair pupils to check capital letters, spaces and full stops. Invite pupils to revise their own sentences after partner feedback. Offer a two-sentence scaffold to pupils requiring support and an optional fourth sentence for extension.  
**Pupils:** Write three sentences, check punctuation with a partner and revise their writing.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check each pupil's three sentences for meaning and punctuation. Ask each pupil to read one sentence independently; note whether writing was copied, supported or independently produced.  
**Pupils:** Submit their revised sentences and read one aloud.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to state one punctuation rule they used today.  
**Pupils:** Share one rule and a corrected example.
