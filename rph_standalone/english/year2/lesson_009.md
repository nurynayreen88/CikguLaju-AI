# English Year 2 — Lesson 9: Writing

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils write two punctuated free-time sentences and share one sentence orally.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two understandable sentences and one intelligible oral response.
**Materials:** Pictures, word cards and teacher-developed task materials. SOW classification: non-textbook; no Student's Book page assigned.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures connected to Free Time and elicit two words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer a question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a punctuated sentence about a free-time activity and read it aloud.
**Pupils:** Follow the demonstration and respond once.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide two independent sentences and one oral reading.
**Pupils:** Write two sentences and read one aloud.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Give one independent response.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful expressions.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one learned response.
