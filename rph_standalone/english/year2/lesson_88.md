# English Year 2 — Lesson 88: Speaking 18

**Unit:** The robot  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** SB p.94; TB unverified. All scripts and tasks below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Objective:** Pupils describe three robot features and answer two prompts.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the stated numerical targets independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original robot card A:** square head, two blue eyes, long arms, short legs. **Card B:** round head, one green eye, short arms, long legs. **Model:** “My robot has a square head. It has two blue eyes. Its arms are long.” **Oral prompts:** “How many eyes has robot B got?” — one. “Are robot A's legs long?” — no.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a relevant picture and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name visible items and predict the topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Describe robot A using the model, pointing to each feature and modelling *has*.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs describe one robot card without showing it, guess A/B, swap roles and answer two prompts.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess three intelligible features and two accurate oral responses. Note independent accuracy and prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
