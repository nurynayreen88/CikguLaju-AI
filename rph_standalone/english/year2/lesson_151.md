# English Year 2 — Lesson 151: Reading 31

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four details on printed page 117 and answer two short questions.
**Success criteria:** four accurate details and two supported answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.117, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.117 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model finding evidence on the printed page.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide four page-grounded prompts and two questions.
**Pupils:** Locate details and write two answers.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
