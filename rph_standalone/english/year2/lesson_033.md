# English Year 2 — Lesson 33: Reading 7

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify two details in a short illustrated house text.
**Success criteria (draft):** two text-supported answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book pp.74–75, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book pp.74–75 according to the prior Year 2 SOW mapping audit. Exact Teacher's Book steps remain unverified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce The old house with a picture and two questions.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher:** Model the reading 7 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Organise pair or individual practice and provide feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe one individual response and record feedback.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the lesson and invite two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
