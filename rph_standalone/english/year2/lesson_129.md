# English Year 2 — Lesson 129: Reading 26

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five facts on Student's Book p.109 and give two oral answers.
**Success criteria:** 4/5 correct facts and two relevant oral answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.109, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.109 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model finding a fact on the actual printed page 109.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Use five page-grounded fact prompts and two oral questions.
**Pupils:** Locate printed facts and give two oral answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual performance against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach word.
**Pupils:** Share one word or phrase.
