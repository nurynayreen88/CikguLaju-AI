# English Year 2 — Lesson 30: Language Arts 6

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils create and share a short two-line free-time verse with a partner.
**Success criteria (draft):** two meaningful lines and one intelligible spoken sharing.
**Teaching materials:** pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (classified in prior Year 2 SOW audit). Detailed SOW task steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two free time pictures and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a short teacher-created free-time verse and expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide pupils to compose two lines with word cards and share in pairs.
**Pupils:** Create two lines and share them orally.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two target words and one useful example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
