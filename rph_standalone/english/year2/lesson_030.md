# English Year 2 — Lesson 30: Language Arts 6

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 5.3 / 5.3.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.2 / 2.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 2 audit. The verse and writing prompts below are original teacher-developed materials.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a two-line free-time verse using familiar activity words and present it intelligibly to a partner.  
**Success criteria:** Two meaningful lines using at least two activity words and one clear spoken presentation.  
**Materials:** Board, word cards for read, draw, swim, play, ride, book, ball, bike, picture; verse frame below.

## Original example verse — “After School”
“I read my book when school is done.  
I play with friends and have some fun.”

**Verse frames:** “I ____ when school is done.” “I ____ and have some fun.”  
**Alternative frame:** “I like to ____ today.” “I ____ and laugh and play.”  
**Example substitution:** “I draw a picture when school is done. I ride my bike and have some fun.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original example with a steady rhythm and mime reading and playing.  
**Pupils:** Name the two activities and repeat the lines.

## Activity 1 — Notice and model (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the example, underline the activity words and draw attention to the ending sounds *done/fun*. Demonstrate choosing two new activities and replacing the words while preserving two meaningful lines.  
**Pupils:** Identify the action words, rehearse the verse and suggest a replacement.

## Activity 2 — Compose and rehearse (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pairs the word bank and verse frames. Ask pupils to create two lines about their own free-time activities, checking that the meaning is clear. Guide pupils to practise their verse with rhythm and one gesture per line. Encourage confident pupils to add a rhyming word; allow emerging writers to use the frames without rhyme.  
**Pupils:** Draft two lines, practise in pairs and give one helpful suggestion to a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect each pupil's two-line verse and listen to an independent spoken performance. Record use of activity vocabulary, meaning and intelligibility; note when pupils needed frames or prompts.  
**Pupils:** Submit two lines and perform them aloud.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite a few volunteers to perform and ask classmates to identify an activity word.  
**Pupils:** Listen and share one activity they heard.
