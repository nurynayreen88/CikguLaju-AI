# English Year 2 — Lesson 15: Language Arts 3

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the existing Year 2 mapping. The poem, tasks and assessment below are **teacher-created**, not attributed to the Teacher's Book or official SOW.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) perform four short lines of a free-time rhyme with at least two appropriate gestures and (2) recognise and read four printed activity words from the rhyme.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible rhyme lines, two matching gestures, and four correctly identified printed activity words.  
**Materials:** Printed rhyme below (one copy per pupil or displayed on the board), word cards **read, draw, play, swim**, optional picture cards, pencils.

## Original classroom rhyme — “My Free Time”
> I read a book on Monday.  
> I draw a star on Tuesday.  
> I play a game on Wednesday.  
> I swim and smile on Saturday.

**Gesture guide:** open palms like a book (*read*); trace a star in the air (*draw*); mime rolling a ball (*play*); move arms in a swimming motion (*swim*).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the four word/picture cards, mime each activity and ask “What am I doing?”  
**Pupils:** Guess and say **read, draw, play, swim**.

## Activity 1 — Read and echo the rhyme (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the full original rhyme. Read each line slowly while pointing to the words. Demonstrate the gesture for each activity. Highlight **read, draw, play, swim** and model how to find each word in the printed lines.  
**Pupils:** Track the text, echo each line, underline the four activity words and copy the matching gestures.

## Activity 2 — Rehearse and perform (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide the class into four groups, assigning one line per group. Conduct a call-and-response reading, then let groups combine all four lines. Ask pairs to practise the complete rhyme, with gestures on at least two lines. Offer word cards as prompts, then gradually remove them. For extension, pupils may substitute one familiar activity word while preserving the rhythm; clearly mark substitutions as pupil-created.  
**Pupils:** Read their line, perform in groups, rehearse in pairs and practise the full four-line rhyme. Faster pupils invent one additional line.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show each pupil the four printed activity words in mixed order and ask them to read or identify each. Observe an individual or paired four-line performance, recording (a) intelligible lines, (b) at least two matching gestures and (c) word recognition. Distinguish prompted performance from independent evidence.  
**Pupils:** Identify the four words and perform the rhyme with minimal support.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Point to one activity card and ask pupils to say the matching rhyme line. Ask which activity they enjoy most.  
**Pupils:** Recite a line and name a favourite activity.
