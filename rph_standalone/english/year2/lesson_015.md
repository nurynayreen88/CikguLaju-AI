# English Year 2 — Lesson 15: Language Arts 3

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the existing Year 2 mapping. The poem, tasks and assessment below are **teacher-created**, not attributed to the Teacher's Book or official SOW.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) perform four short lines of a free-time rhyme with at least two appropriate gestures and (2) blend and read four short words using phonemes with teacher support.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible rhyme lines, two matching gestures, and four correctly blended and read short words.  
**Materials:** Printed rhyme below (one copy per pupil or displayed on the board), word cards **sun, run, clap, stop**, sound cards, optional picture cards, pencils.

## Original classroom rhyme — “My Free Time”
> I read a book on Monday.  
> I draw a star on Tuesday.  
> I play a game on Wednesday.  
> I swim and smile on Saturday.

**Phonics bank:** sun = s-u-n (CVC); run = r-u-n (CVC); clap = c-l-a-p (CCVC); stop = s-t-o-p (CCVC). Model individual phonemes before blending. These are teacher-created extension cards, not quoted SOW text.

**Gesture guide:** open palms like a book (*read*); trace a star in the air (*draw*); mime rolling a ball (*play*); move arms in a swimming motion (*swim*).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the four word/picture cards, mime each activity and ask “What am I doing?”  
**Pupils:** Guess and say **read, draw, play, swim**.

## Activity 1 — Read and echo the rhyme (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the full original rhyme. Read each line slowly while pointing to the words. Demonstrate the gesture for each activity. Model blending **sun, run, clap, stop** from sound cards, then have pupils echo the rhyme with intelligible rhythm and intonation.  
**Pupils:** Track the text, echo each line, blend the four phonics-bank words with support and copy the matching gestures.

## Activity 2 — Rehearse and perform (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide the class into four groups, assigning one line per group. Conduct a call-and-response reading, then let groups combine all four lines. Ask pairs to practise the complete rhyme, with gestures on at least two lines. Pause for pairs to segment and blend sun, run, clap and stop, then continue the rhyme. Offer sound cards as prompts, then gradually remove them. For extension, pupils may substitute one familiar activity word while preserving the rhythm; clearly mark substitutions as pupil-created.  
**Pupils:** Read their line, perform in groups, rehearse in pairs and practise the full four-line rhyme. Faster pupils invent one additional line.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show each pupil the four phonics-bank words in mixed order and ask them to blend the sounds into words. Observe an individual or paired four-line performance, recording (a) intelligible lines, (b) at least two matching gestures and (c) phoneme blending. Distinguish prompted performance from independent evidence.  
**Pupils:** Blend the four words and perform the rhyme with minimal support.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Point to one activity card and ask pupils to say the matching rhyme line. Ask which activity they enjoy most.  
**Pupils:** Recite a line and name a favourite activity.
