# English Year 2 — Lesson 11: Speaking

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils ask and answer two simple free-time questions in paired exchanges.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two intelligible questions and two relevant answers.
**Materials:** Topic pictures, word cards and exercise books. Textbook page unverified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures related to Free Time and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer one question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two simple questions about free-time activities and corresponding answers using picture cues.
**Pupils:** Observe and give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide two partner exchanges, changing roles and giving corrective feedback.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer two questions with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two key words and invite a final example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned response.
