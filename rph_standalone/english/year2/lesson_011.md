# English Year 2 — Lesson 11: Speaking

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.2  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping; *Super Minds 1* Student's Book p.61, Workbook p.61, Teacher's Book T61 (PDF page 133), visually checked. CD2 Tracks 25–26.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can ask two intelligible “Do you ... at the weekend?” questions and give two appropriate “Yes, I do / No, I don't” answers about free-time activities.  
**Success criteria:** Two correctly formed questions and two appropriate short answers in an exchange.  
**Materials:** SB p.61, WB p.61, CD2 Tracks 25–26 or teacher-read dialogue, activity flashcards, board.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to recall one activity from the “Busy week” song. Mime watching TV and playing computer games; elicit the names.  
**Pupils:** Name the activities and respond orally.

## Activity 1 — Present and model questions (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write “Do you watch TV at the weekend?” and model “Yes, I do.” / “No, I don't.” Show SB p.61 Activity 1; play CD2 Track 25 twice, or read the two short exchanges: “Do you watch TV at the weekend?”—“No, I don't.”; “Do you play computer games at the weekend?”—“Yes, I do.” Direct pupils to circle the corresponding answer in each picture. Play CD2 Track 26 or model the grammar focus for choral repetition.  
**Pupils:** Listen, circle the answers, compare in pairs and repeat the question and short answers.

## Activity 2 — Question game (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate SB p.61 Activity 3 in groups of four to six: one pupil asks the pupil on the left a “Do you ...?” question; “Yes, I do” continues in the same direction, while “No, I don't” reverses the direction. Give prompts: watch TV, play football, play computer games, ride a bike, go swimming. Rotate roles. Direct pupils to WB p.61 Activity 1 (write words and tick) and Activity 2 (follow lines and find answers) for short independent practice.  
**Pupils:** Ask and answer questions in the group game, listen for partners' answers and complete the selected Workbook items.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil asking two different “Do you ...?” questions and answering two partner questions without reading a complete script. Record question form, comprehensibility and relevance of short answers.  
**Pupils:** Perform a two-question exchange with a partner.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write “weekend / football / you / Do / play / at / the / ?” in scrambled order; ask pupils to reorder it into a question and supply both possible short answers.  
**Pupils:** Reconstruct the question and answer orally.
