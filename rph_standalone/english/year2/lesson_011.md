# English Year 2 — Lesson 11: Speaking 3

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (SOW mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Learning objective (draft):** Pupils demonstrate a speaking response using vocabulary from Free Time.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom materials; textbook pages not yet verified.
**Source status:** SOW_MAPPED_ONLY — Teacher's Book steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce Free Time using a familiar picture and two short questions.
**Pupils:** Recall one word or expression connected to the unit.

## Activity 1 — Guided pair speaking
**Teacher:** Model a short exchange using unit vocabulary, then organise pair practice with role changes.
**Pupils:** Take turns asking and answering with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Repeat the target language with additional modelling for pupils who need support; invite confident pupils to extend their answers.
**Pupils:** Practise, correct their responses and share one example.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil's response to the target speaking task and provide immediate feedback.
**Evidence:** Record one observable response against the lesson objective.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the key language and ask two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share or demonstrate one thing learned.
