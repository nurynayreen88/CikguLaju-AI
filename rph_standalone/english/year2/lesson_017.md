# English Year 2 — Lesson 17: Speaking

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 4.2 / 4.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to prior SOW mapping. The dialogue, prompt cards and activities below are teacher-created.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can ask and answer at least three questions about weekend activities and write two sentences reporting a partner's activities.  
**Success criteria:** Three intelligible question–answer exchanges and two understandable written sentences.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books and the five prompt cards below.  
**Prompt cards:** watch TV; ride a bike; play football; read a book; go swimming.  
**Language support:** “Do you watch TV at the weekend?” “Yes, I do.” / “No, I don't.” “I ride my bike on Saturday.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime cycling and reading, and invite pupils to name the activities. Ask “What do you do at the weekend?”  
**Pupils:** Name the activities and share one short response.

## Activity 1 — Model and practise (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write a model dialogue on the board: “Do you ride a bike at the weekend?” “Yes, I do. I ride my bike on Saturday.” Model a second exchange ending “No, I don't. I read a book.” Underline the question pattern and demonstrate rising intonation.  
**Pupils:** Echo the questions and responses, then practise both roles with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Weekend partner survey (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give each pair the five prompt cards. Ask Pupil A to choose three cards and question Pupil B; then reverse roles. Model a simple record table with headings *Activity / Partner's answer*. Guide pupils to use their notes to write two sentences, such as “My partner reads a book on Saturday.” Provide a word bank for pupils who need support.  
**Pupils:** Conduct at least three exchanges each, record short answers, change roles and write two sentences from the survey.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen to each pupil ask two different questions and give a relevant answer to one question. Collect two independently written sentences. Record oral intelligibility and written meaning separately; note when a pupil reads directly from the model.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the exchange and submit the two sentences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to report one surprising or shared weekend activity. Review the difference between “Do you ...?” and “I ...”.  
**Pupils:** Report one partner activity and repeat one correct question.
