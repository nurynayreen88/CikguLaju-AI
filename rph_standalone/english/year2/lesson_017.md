# English Year 2 — Lesson 17: Speaking 4

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils ask and answer two free-time questions and write one simple sentence about an activity.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two intelligible exchanges and one understandable written sentence.
**Materials:** Free-time activity picture cards, board, exercise books.
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (prior Year 2 audit). Detailed SOW steps still require verification.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of reading, cycling and drawing; elicit familiar activity words.
**Pupils:** Name activities and respond to one simple question.

## Activity 1 — Model the exchange (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two free-time question-and-answer exchanges and one written sentence using a picture.
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat the exchange and identify the activity.

## Activity 2 — Paired speaking (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide partner exchanges with role changes, then support one independent written sentence.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer two questions and write one sentence.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record individual evidence against both success criteria and give targeted feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete a two-turn exchange independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite one pair to demonstrate and review two activity words.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the exchange and share a word learned.
