# English Year 2 — Lesson 110: Language Arts 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils respond to a short rhyme using three actions and give two simple personal statements.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate non-verbal responses and two clear personal statements.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Action cues:** tap feet, spin around, wave hello. **Personal statements:** 'My name is __.' 'I can __.' **Original four-line verse:** My robot taps its silver feet / It spins around to a happy beat / It waves hello and starts to play / I wish my robot could stay all day! **Reaction frame:** I like the robot because ____.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name a robot feature.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Perform a teacher-created four-line robot verse and model a reaction.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Perform the verse and invite three actions showing appreciation; pupils then give two personal statements.
**Pupils:** Perform three lines and share one reaction.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three non-verbal appreciation responses (SP 5.1.1) and two personal statements (SP 2.1.1). Note individual support required.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
