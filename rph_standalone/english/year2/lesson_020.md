# English Year 2 — Lesson 20: Language Arts 4

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils perform a short teacher-created chant using two free-time activity words and two gestures.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two intelligible activity words and two matching gestures.
**Materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.65, free-time picture cards, word cards and board.
**Textbook mapping:** Student's Book p.65 in the prior Year 2 SOW audit. Exact Teacher's Book steps and activity alignment remain unverified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of reading, cycling and drawing. Ask pupils to name the activities.
**Pupils:** Name familiar activities and show a matching gesture.

## Activity 1 — Model a chant (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a teacher-created chant with two activity words and two gestures; use page 65 only as a mapped resource.
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat each line and imitate the beat and gestures.

## Activity 2 — Guided performance (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide word substitutions, paired rehearsal and an individual performance.
**Pupils:** Perform a short chant with two words and gestures.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Perform a short part of the chant with minimal prompting.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two activity words and invite one final performance.
**Pupils:** Share one new expression and demonstrate its gesture.
