# English Year 2 — Lesson 20: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Textbook mapping:** Student's Book p.65 according to the existing Year 2 SOW audit. **Teacher's Book p.T65 activity sequence has not been verified for this draft.** The performance text and activities below are original teacher-developed supplements, not a claim about the textbook's contents.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can perform four short lines of an original free-time chant with appropriate expression and gestures, and write two simple sentences about their own free-time activities.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible lines, at least two matching gestures and two understandable written sentences.  
**Materials:** SB p.65 for teacher reference, the original chant below, four activity cards, board, exercise books.

## Original chant — “Free Time Fun”
“Read a book, turn a page!  
Ride a bike, off we go!  
Kick a ball, run and play!  
Draw a picture, show a friend!”

**Gestures:** Turn an imaginary page; hold bicycle handlebars; mime kicking a ball; draw a picture in the air.  
**Writing frames:** “I like to ____.” “On Saturday, I ____.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the activity cards for reading, cycling, football and drawing. Mime each activity and ask pupils to name it.  
**Pupils:** Identify the four activities and repeat the action words.

## Activity 1 — Listen, echo and perform (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the complete original chant. Read each line with rhythm and demonstrate its gesture. Point out action words and model how the gesture communicates meaning. Invite choral repetition, then divide the class into four groups, each responsible for one line.  
**Pupils:** Echo each line, perform the matching gestures and identify action words.

## Activity 2 — Group rehearsal and creative response (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Have groups practise the whole chant, first with displayed text and then with word cards as reduced prompts. Ask pairs to perform to one another and give feedback on clear words and appropriate gestures. Model the two writing frames; pupils select two activities and write their own sentences. Encourage confident pupils to change one chant action and invent a matching gesture, clearly identifying this as their own addition.  
**Pupils:** Rehearse and perform the four lines, give partner feedback and write two personal free-time sentences.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil or pair performing four lines, noting intelligibility, rhythm and at least two appropriate gestures. Collect two independent sentences and check that each communicates a free-time activity. Record performance and writing evidence separately, including any prompts.  
**Pupils:** Perform and submit their two sentences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite one final group performance. Ask pupils which action they liked and why.  
**Pupils:** Perform together and name a favourite action.
