# English Year 2 — Lesson 120: Language Arts 24

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils express two reactions to a short robot verse and perform three lines.
**Success criteria:** two relevant reactions and three clear lines.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Perform a teacher-created four-line robot verse and model a reaction.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide expressive rehearsal and two personal responses.
**Pupils:** Perform three lines and share two reactions.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or phrase.
