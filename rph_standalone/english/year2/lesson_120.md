# English Year 2 — Lesson 120: Language Arts 24

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils demonstrate appreciation with three actions and recite three lines using rhythm and intonation.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate actions and three intelligibly recited lines.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards, individual PBD record and original resources below.

**Actions:** spin, wave and clap as the relevant words are heard. **Original verse:** “My robot spins beneath the sun, / It waves hello to everyone. / It claps its hands and sings with me, / A happy friend for all to see!” **Reaction frames:** “I like ____ because ____.” “My favourite line is ____.”
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Perform a teacher-created four-line robot verse and model a reaction.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Rehearse the verse using three meaningful gestures, then recite three lines with rhythm and intonation.
**Pupils:** Perform three lines and share two reactions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Observe three non-verbal responses (SP 5.1.1) and three clearly recited lines (SP 5.1.2). Note independent performance and support needed.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or phrase.
