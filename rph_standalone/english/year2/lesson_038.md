# English Year 2 — Lesson 38: Reading 8

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.1 / 1.1.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils recognise four printed room or object words and say them clearly.
**Success criteria (draft):** four correctly recognised words and four intelligible spoken attempts.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the prior Year 2 audit. Detailed SOW activity sequence remains unverified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Display house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model recognising and reading four printed house words.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide word-card recognition and spoken practice with corrective feedback.
**Pupils:** Read four printed words aloud.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
