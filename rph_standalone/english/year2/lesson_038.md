# English Year 2 — Lesson 38: Reading 8

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.4  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final SOW verification pending):** 1.1 / 1.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the existing audit. The exact SOW task sequence is unverified; the materials below are original teacher-developed resources, not textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils segment the sounds in four CVC or CCVC words and reproduce four spoken target sounds.  
**Success criteria:** four correct phoneme segmentations and four accurate repetitions of target sounds.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, teacher-created picture/word cards and the printable text below.

**Printable word cards:** kitchen | bedroom | bathroom | living room | stairs | cellar | table | sofa.
**Picture key:** A—kitchen (cooker); B—bedroom (bed); C—bathroom (bath); D—living room (sofa); E—stairs (steps); F—cellar (below ground); G—table; H—sofa.
**Sound-segmentation cards:** bed (b-e-d), cat (c-a-t), stop (s-t-o-p), clap (c-l-a-p). Teacher models individual sounds, not letter names.  
**Reading prompts:** “Find *bedroom*.” “Point to *stairs*.” “Read *bathroom*.” “Match *living room* to its picture.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two house pictures and elicit familiar rooms or objects.  
**Pupils:** Name the rooms and point to the relevant objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display eight printed cards, model separating the sounds in bed and clap, then say each target sound for pupils to hear and repeat.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and demonstrate one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs segment the four sound cards, tapping once for each sound, then recognise and reproduce sounds said by the teacher. Matching room cards is optional extension. Remove pictures for a second round; provide letter-pattern cues as needed.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task, compare evidence with a partner and improve their responses.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each pupil to segment the four target words and reproduce four sounds heard from the teacher. Record segmentation and sound recognition separately.  
**Pupils:** Segment four words and repeat four target sounds independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful words and invite one reflection on the task.  
**Pupils:** Share a word or a sentence from the lesson.
