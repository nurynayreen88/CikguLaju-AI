# English Year 2 — Lesson 149: Speaking 30

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils share three beach preferences and respond to two spoken questions.
**Success criteria:** three intelligible preferences and two relevant responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model expressing a beach preference and answering a question.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide paired exchanges with three preference prompts and two questions.
**Pupils:** Share three preferences and answer two questions.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
