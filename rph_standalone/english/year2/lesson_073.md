# English Year 2 — Lesson 73: Reading 15

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read six familiar clothing words aloud and match four of five printed words to pictures.
**Success criteria:** Six intelligible words and 4/5 accurate matches.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display clothing cards and invite predictions.
**Pupils:** Name pictured items.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model reading six printed clothing words with letter-sound support.
**Pupils:** Read and point to printed words.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Run paired word-card reading and a five-item independent matching task.
**Pupils:** Read six words and complete five matches.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record six oral readings and five independent matches for each pupil.
**Pupils:** Submit matching responses and read aloud.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one challenging word.
**Pupils:** Read one chosen word.
