# English Year 2 — Lesson 73: Reading 15

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK. The scripts, passages and examples below are original teacher-created resources, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify beginning, medial and final sounds in three short words and blend sounds to read three words.  
**Success criteria:** Achieve the measurable targets in the objective.  
**Materials:** Board, clothing picture/word cards, exercise books and the printable resources below.

**Phonics examples:** hat = h-a-t; red = r-e-d; stop = s-t-o-p. Model first, middle and final sounds, then blend the sounds into whole words.
**Printable word cards:** shirt, skirt, shoes, socks, hat, dress, jacket, trousers. **Picture labels:** A shirt, B skirt, C shoes, D socks, E hat, F dress, G jacket, H trousers. **Matching set/key:** skirt→B; shoes→C; hat→E; jacket→G; trousers→H.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display two clothing pictures, elicit known words and ask a prediction.  
**Pupils:** Name items and colours.

## Activity 1 — Teacher modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model reading *shirt*, *skirt* and *shoes* using first-letter and word-shape clues.  
**Pupils:** Follow and practise one example with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs identify the sounds in hat, red and stop, then blend the sounds aloud. Picture matching is optional reinforcement.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity, discuss answers and correct errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check three sound-analysis responses (SP 3.1.2) and three independently blended words (SP 3.1.3). Record independent accuracy and assistance required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skills individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two target words and ask for a relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
