# English Year 2 — Lesson 109: Reading 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five explicit details on Student's Book p.102 and write two factual answers.
**Success criteria:** 4/5 accurate details and two supported written answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.102, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Original alternative passage (not SB p.102 text):** Bolt is a friendly robot in the garden. It has a blue body, two long arms and four small wheels. Bolt waters the flowers every morning. **Five details/key:** name Bolt, place garden, body blue, arms two, wheels four. **Written questions/key:** What does Bolt water? The flowers. When? Every morning.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.102 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name a robot feature.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Display the original alternative passage about Bolt provided above, and model finding evidence by underlining *blue body*. Student's Book p.102 is a mapping reference only until its text and Teacher's Book sequence are verified.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give each pupil the complete original Bolt passage. Ask pupils to identify the five stated details (name, place, body colour, number of arms, number of wheels), underline supporting words and answer both written questions. Use the supplied key; do not claim this is the Student's Book passage.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and write two answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score five individual detail responses (0–5) and two written answers (0–2) against the provided key; note whether each answer is supported by the original passage and record any prompts separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
