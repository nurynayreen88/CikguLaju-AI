# English Year 2 — Lesson 109: Reading 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning objective:** Pupils practise reading 22 using language from The robot.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.102, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.102 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce The robot with a picture and two questions.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher:** Model the reading 22 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Organise pair or individual practice and provide feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe one individual response and record feedback.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the lesson and invite two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
