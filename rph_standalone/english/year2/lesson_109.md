# English Year 2 — Lesson 109: Reading 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils reread three short sentences to clarify meaning and spell four familiar words in guided writing.
**Success criteria:** three meaning explanations after rereading and four accurately spelled familiar words.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.102, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Rereading prompts:** 'It has four wheels.' 'It waters the flowers.' 'It has two long arms.' Pupils reread each sentence and explain its meaning using a picture. **Guided spelling:** arms, legs, head, eyes. **Original alternative passage (not SB p.102 text):** Bolt is a friendly robot in the garden. It has a blue body, two long arms and four small wheels. Bolt waters the flowers every morning. **Five details/key:** name Bolt, place garden, body blue, arms two, wheels four. **Written questions/key:** What does Bolt water? The flowers. When? Every morning.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.102 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name a robot feature.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Display the original alternative passage about Bolt provided above, and model finding evidence by underlining *blue body*. Student's Book p.102 is a mapping reference only until its text and Teacher's Book sequence are verified.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give pupils the original passage, ask them to reread three short sentences when unsure, and explain each meaning using a picture. Then guide spelling of four familiar words. Use the supplied key; do not claim this is the Student's Book passage.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and write two answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score three demonstrated rereading strategies (SP 3.2.3) and four guided spellings (SP 4.3.2); note whether each answer is supported by the original passage and record any prompts separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
