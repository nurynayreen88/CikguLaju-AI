# English Year 2 — Lesson 35: Language Arts 7

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils perform two lines of a teacher-created house rhyme with gestures.
**Success criteria (draft):** two intelligible lines and two appropriate gestures.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.72, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.72 according to the prior Year 2 SOW mapping audit. Exact Teacher's Book steps remain unverified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created two-line house rhyme and matching actions; page 72 is a mapped resource only.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide partner rehearsal and a brief individual performance.
**Pupils:** Perform two lines with two gestures.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
