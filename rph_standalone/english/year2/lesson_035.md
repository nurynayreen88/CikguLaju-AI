# English Year 2 — Lesson 35: Language Arts 7

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate language arts 7 skills using The old house vocabulary.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Show a topic picture and ask two questions.
**Pupils:** Name familiar words.

## Activity 1
**Teacher:** Model the target language arts 7 task using unit vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Listen, respond and practise the model.

## Activity 2
**Teacher:** Organise guided pair or individual practice; provide corrective feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one answer.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe and record one independent pupil response.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Review key language and invite pupil examples.
**Pupils:** Share what they learned.
