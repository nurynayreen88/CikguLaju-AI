# English Year 2 — Lesson 18: Reading 4

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; requires checking):** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; requires checking):** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify two details in a short illustrated free-time text and answer two related questions.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two correctly located details and two text-supported answers.
**Materials:** Relevant topic pictures, word cards, board and exercise books.
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the prior Year 2 audit. These activities are teacher-developed; detailed SOW steps remain unverified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display two pictures linked to Free Time and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and give one oral response.

## Activity 1 — Demonstration (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model reading a short teacher-created illustrated text and finding one supporting clue.
**Pupils:** Observe and complete one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided and independent practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide two detail-finding prompts and two comprehension questions with evidence.
**Pupils:** Locate two details and answer two questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Show their own completed work.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit two key words and summarise the skill.
**Pupils:** Share one correct example.
