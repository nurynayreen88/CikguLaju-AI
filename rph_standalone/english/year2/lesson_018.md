# English Year 2 — Lesson 18: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK under the existing Year 2 mapping. The passage and tasks below are original teacher-developed material; not Teacher's Book content.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils locate at least four explicit day–activity details in a short text and answer three comprehension questions using printed evidence.  
**Success criteria:** Four correctly located details and three text-supported answers.  
**Materials:** Printed passage and questions below, day cards, pencils, board.

## Original reading passage — “Hana's Busy Week”
“Hana likes her free time. On Monday, she reads a storybook with her sister. On Wednesday, she rides her bicycle in the park. On Friday, she plays football with her friends. On Saturday, she helps her mother in the garden. On Sunday, she draws pictures at home. Hana likes Sunday because she loves drawing.”

**Questions:** (1) What does Hana do on Monday? (2) Where does she ride her bicycle? (3) Who does she play football with? (4) What does she do on Saturday? (5) Why does she like Sunday?  
**Answer key:** (1) Reads a storybook with her sister. (2) In the park. (3) Her friends. (4) Helps her mother in the garden. (5) Because she loves drawing.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the words *storybook, bicycle, football, garden, pictures* and ask pupils to match them with quick sketches or gestures.  
**Pupils:** Read familiar words and guess their meanings.

## Activity 1 — Model finding evidence (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the passage aloud once, then model scanning for “Monday” and underlining the words that answer Question 1. Explain that answers must come from the printed text, not guesses.  
**Pupils:** Follow the reading, highlight day names and underline the Monday activity.

## Activity 2 — Guided then independent reading (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to locate the Wednesday, Friday, Saturday and Sunday details. Guide pupils through Question 2, then ask them to answer Questions 3–5 independently. Require pupils to point to the supporting sentence. Provide a day–activity matching grid as support and an extension question: “Which activity would you choose and why?”  
**Pupils:** Read the passage, identify four details and write or say answers to three questions with evidence.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pupils a clean copy of the passage. Ask them to underline four day–activity details and answer Questions 2, 3 and 5 independently. Mark against the answer key and record whether answers are text-supported.  
**Pupils:** Complete the individual reading task without partner assistance.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to name one clue that helped them find an answer. Revisit the distinction between an answer from the text and a personal opinion.  
**Pupils:** Share one day–activity fact from the passage.
