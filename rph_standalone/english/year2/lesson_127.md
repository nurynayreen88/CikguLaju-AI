# English Year 2 — Lesson 127: Speaking 26

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask three basic questions to find out personal information and interpret two messages using visual clues.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate personal-information questions and two understood visual-clue messages.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.107, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Personal-information survey:** 'What's your name?' 'What do you like doing at the beach?' 'Who do you go with?' **Visual clues:** point to self for name and mime swimming for a preferred activity. **Original supplementary scene:** a red bucket, a small shell and a big beach ball. **Model:** The bucket is red. The shell is small. The ball is big. **Questions/key:** What colour is the bucket? Red. Which object is small? The shell.

**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.107 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar objects.
**Pupils:** Name two beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model short descriptions of beach objects and one question.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one description.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide pairs to ask three personal-information questions, record answers and interpret two supported visual clues.
**Pupils:** Describe three beach objects and answer two questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three personal-information questions (SP 2.1.2) and two visual-clue interpretations (SP 1.3.1), separately from oral answers per pupil.
**Pupils:** Provide individual oral evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful descriptive phrase.
**Pupils:** Share one descriptive phrase.
