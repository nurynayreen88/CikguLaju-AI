# English Year 2 — Lesson 51: Listening 11

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) identify at least 4 of 5 familiar house-related words or details after listening twice; (2) respond orally to at least 2 simple prompts using a word or short phrase.  
**Success criteria:** 4/5 correct listening responses; 2 appropriate oral responses.  
**Materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.80, house picture/word cards, teacher-prepared five-item response sheet.  
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.80 (Year 2 SOW audit). The activity below is a teacher-designed adaptation, NOT a verified Teacher's Book sequence.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a picture of a house. Ask “What can you see?” and “Where is it?”; introduce the lesson goal.  
**Pupils:** Name or point to familiar objects and answer with words or short phrases.

## Activity 1 — Listening preparation (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Select five visible words or details from Student's Book p.80; show corresponding picture cards, say each item clearly and model one example response.  
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat the five items and match cards to pictures.

## Activity 2 — Listen and identify (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the listening input associated with p.80 where available; otherwise read aloud a short, clearly labelled teacher-created description consistent with the page. Play/read twice, pausing only after the first complete hearing. Give five picture-choice or tick-box questions.  
**Pupils:** Listen first for gist, then identify five items individually; compare with a partner and correct answers after feedback.

## Activity 3 — Oral response (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two short question-and-answer exchanges about the house picture; provide sentence starters and invite pairs to take turns.  
**Pupils:** Ask and answer in pairs, then each pupil gives two individual responses.

## Assessment / PBD (7 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's listening score (0–5) and oral responses (0–2) using a checklist; reteach items missed by pupils scoring below 4/5.  
**Pupils:** Submit individual response sheets and answer two oral prompts.

## Closure (3 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit the five target items and elicit one exit response per pupil.  
**Pupils:** Name one item learned.

**Differentiation:** Support—picture choices and sentence frames; extension—give a complete sentence describing the house.
