# English Year 2 — Lesson 138: Writing 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three legible beach sentences and correct two spacing errors.
**Success criteria:** three legible sentences and two appropriate corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.112, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original scene:** a big yellow sun, blue sea and two shells. **Models:** The sun is yellow. The sea is blue. There are two shells. **Spacing corrections/key:** Thesun is yellow. -> The sun is yellow.; Thereare two shells. -> There are two shells.

**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.112 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model sentence spacing and neat letter formation.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide three picture-based sentences and two individual corrections.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and revise spacing.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
