# English Year 2 — Lesson 26: Listening 6

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify three spoken details about rooms or objects and copy two relevant words.
**Success criteria (draft):** three accurate listening choices and two legible copied words.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.70, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.70 (page mapped in prior Year 2 SOW audit). Exact Teacher's Book steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two the old house pictures and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model listening for a room or object word in a teacher-created description; use mapped page 70 only as a resource.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Read three short clues and guide pupils to select pictures, then copy two words.
**Pupils:** Identify three details and copy two words.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two target words and one useful example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
