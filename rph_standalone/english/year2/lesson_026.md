# English Year 2 — Lesson 26: Listening 6

**Unit:** The old house — home vocabulary  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Verified textbook references:** *Super Minds 1* Student's Book p.70, Teacher's Book T70 (scanned PDF page index 151), Workbook p.70. SB Activity 1 uses CD2 Track 35 for eight home words; Activity 2 uses CD2 Track 36 for the chant.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify at least six of eight home words when heard and copy four room words accurately.  
**Success criteria:** Six correct listening selections out of eight and four legible correctly spelled room words.  
**Materials:** SB p.70 house picture, home flashcards or labels, board and exercise books. Use the original CD tracks when available; the teacher-created fallback below is not a transcript.

**Eight textbook labels:** 1 bathroom; 2 bedroom; 3 living room; 4 hall; 5 dining room; 6 kitchen; 7 stairs; 8 cellar.  
**Teacher-created fallback listening prompts:** “Where do you sleep? Point to the bedroom.” “Where do you cook? Point to the kitchen.” “Where do you have a bath? Point to the bathroom.” “Where do you eat? Point to the dining room.” “Point to the living room.” “Point to the hall.” “Point to the stairs.” “Point to the cellar.”  
**Answer key for picture labels:** bathroom 1; bedroom 2; living room 3; hall 4; dining room 5; kitchen 6; stairs 7; cellar 8.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Draw a simple house outline and ask pupils to name rooms they know. Introduce *old house* and show the SB p.70 picture.  
**Pupils:** Name familiar rooms and observe the house picture.

## Activity 1 — Listen, point and say (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Present the eight home words with flashcards. Play CD2 Track 35, pausing for pupils to point to the correct numbered place, or read the clearly labelled fallback prompts while pupils point. Model pronunciation and repeat difficult words.  
**Pupils:** Listen, point and repeat each home word.

## Activity 2 — Chant and write (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the SB p.70 chant with CD2 Track 36 if available; otherwise lead pupils in rhythmic oral practice of the room words without presenting a substitute as the textbook chant. Ask pupils to copy four selected room words and draw a simple house with labels. Use WB p.70 for optional word-writing reinforcement.  
**Pupils:** Join oral practice, point to rooms and copy four room labels into their books.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Say eight home words in mixed order and check independent pointing or number choices against the picture key. Check four copied room words for legibility and spelling, noting any prompts.  
**Pupils:** Identify the rooms and submit their written labels.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Hide one flashcard and ask pupils to name the missing room; recap two difficult words.  
**Pupils:** Name the missing room and say one new word.
