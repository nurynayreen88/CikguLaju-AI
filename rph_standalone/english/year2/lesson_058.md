# English Year 2 — Lesson 58: Reading 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior mapping. Materials below are original teacher-developed resources; detailed SOW activity verification pending.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read six clothing words and match five printed words to pictures.  
**Success criteria:** Meet each component of the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, picture/word cards and the text below.

**Word cards:** shirt, skirt, trousers, shoes, socks, hat, dress, jacket.
**Picture key:** A—shirt; B—skirt; C—trousers; D—shoes; E—socks; F—hat; G—dress; H—jacket.
**Five matching prompts/key:** shirt—A, trousers—C, shoes—D, hat—F, jacket—H.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display relevant picture cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display eight word cards, model reading *shirt*, *shoes* and *jacket*, and match each to a picture.  
**Pupils:** Follow the example and practise with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs practise reading six selected words, match the five printed prompts to pictures and swap roles.  
**Pupils:** Complete the tasks and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask each pupil to read six words and independently match five printed words; record accuracy. Note independent responses and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the success criteria individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and invite a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and a short sentence.
