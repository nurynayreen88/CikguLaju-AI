# English Year 2 — Lesson 105: Language Arts 21

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a four-line robot chant and ask and answer two questions about robot abilities.
**Success criteria:** one original four-line chant and two ability question-and-answer exchanges.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Original robot chant:** 'Robot, robot, jump up high! / Robot, robot, touch the sky! / Robot, robot, turn around! / Robot, robot, touch the ground!' **Ability exchange:** 'Can your robot jump?' 'Yes, it can.'
**Original four-line dialogue:** A: Hello, Robo! Can you help me? B: Yes, I can! A: Please carry this book. B: Here you are! **Variation choices:** book→bag or box; Robo→Zippy.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created four-line robot dialogue.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Model the chant, then guide pairs to create four lines by changing robot actions; pupils perform and ask and answer two ability questions.
**Pupils:** Perform three lines and offer a variation.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check a four-line creative chant (SP 5.3.1) and two ability exchanges (SP 2.1.4). Note individual support required.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one robot word or phrase.
