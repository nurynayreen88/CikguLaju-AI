# English Year 2 — Lesson 143: Reading 29

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read four short beach sentences, identify three stated details and reread a phrase when meaning is unclear.
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible sentences, three correct detail responses and one demonstrated rereading strategy.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original four-sentence reading:** Mia is at the beach. She has a blue bucket. She sees two shells. Her brother has a red ball. **Three questions and answers:** Where is Mia? At the beach. How many shells? Two. Who has the red ball? Her brother.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model reading a teacher-created beach sentence, rereading the phrase 'blue bucket' to confirm its meaning and locating the detail.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Provide four short sentences and three detail questions. Ask pupils to reread a relevant phrase when unsure; encourage them to use known words to infer the sentence meaning.
**Pupils:** Read four sentences and locate three details.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three detail answers and ask each pupil to reread one relevant phrase to clarify meaning; record individual reading-strategy evidence.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
