# English Year 2 — Lesson 123: Reading 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify four of five details in simple printed sentences and spell four familiar words accurately.
**Success criteria:** four correct reading details and four accurately spelled familiar words.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original teacher-created supplementary resources (not verified textbook extracts):** **Guided spelling list:** robot, head, arms, wheels. **Original paragraph:** “Dot is a small robot in the kitchen. It has a yellow body, two long arms and three wheels. Dot carries plates to the table.” **Five details/key:** Dot; kitchen; yellow; two arms; three wheels. **Written questions/key:** Where is Dot? In the kitchen. What does it carry? Plates.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit two predictions.
**Pupils:** Name known robot words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model finding evidence in a short teacher-created robot paragraph.
**Pupils:** Locate one printed fact.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils underline five details in the original paragraph, then spell four familiar words in guided writing.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and write two short answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check five printed details (SP 3.2.2) and four guided spellings (SP 4.3.2). Record support required for individual PBD.
**Pupils:** Submit individual reading evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review finding evidence in print.
**Pupils:** Share one fact.
