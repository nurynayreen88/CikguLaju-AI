# English Year 2 — Lesson 94: Writing 19

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three meaningful robot descriptions and check the spelling of two words using a word bank.
**Success criteria:** three understandable sentences and two correct spelling checks.
**Teaching materials:** Teacher-created robot cards, board and individual record sheet.

**Original robot picture:** square head, two eyes, four arms, red body. **Word bank:** square, head, eyes, arms, red. **Three model sentences:** “My robot has a square head. It has four arms. Its body is red.” **Spelling check:** Compare *square* and *arms* with the word bank, letter by letter.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and build a word bank.
**Pupils:** Suggest useful words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model the first sentence with capitals, spaces and a full stop, then check *square* and *arms* in the bank.
**Pupils:** Rehearse a sentence and locate a spelling model.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils draw a robot, write three sentences describing it, and check two spellings with the word bank.
**Pupils:** Write and revise two words after checking.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Collect three individual sentences and two visible spelling checks.
**Pupils:** Submit individual written work.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful spelling strategy.
**Pupils:** Read one sentence.
