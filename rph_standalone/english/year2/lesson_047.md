# English Year 2 — Lesson 47: Reading 10

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils find two details in a short illustrated house text and write one relevant sentence.
**Success criteria (draft):** two text-supported answers and one meaningful sentence.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 mapping audit); detailed SOW task sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model finding a clue in a teacher-created four-sentence house text.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide two reading questions and one related written response.
**Pupils:** Find two clues and write one sentence.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
