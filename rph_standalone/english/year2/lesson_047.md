# English Year 2 — Lesson 47: Reading 10

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK; original teacher-created passage, not a textbook extract.
**Objectives:** Pupils locate three details in a short text and write one meaningful sentence.
**Success criteria:** Three correct answers supported by the passage and one relevant sentence.
**Materials:** Printed passage, question cards, exercise books.

## Original reading text — Nina's House
Nina lives in a little house. There is a blue sofa in the living room. There are two chairs in the kitchen. Nina reads a book in her bedroom. Her cat sleeps near the sofa.

**Questions:** 1. What colour is the sofa? 2. How many chairs are in the kitchen? 3. Where does Nina read? 4. Where does the cat sleep?
**Answer key:** 1. Blue. 2. Two. 3. In her bedroom. 4. Near the sofa.
**Writing frame:** “In Nina's ____, there is/are ____.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show living room and bedroom pictures; elicit room words.
**Pupils:** Name rooms and objects.

## Activity 1 — Read for evidence (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original passage aloud, model finding *blue sofa* and underlining the answer evidence.
**Pupils:** Follow the text and identify one detail.

## Activity 2 — Guided comprehension and writing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide paired reading of all four questions, require pupils to underline evidence, then model the writing frame. Support emerging readers with highlighted room words.
**Pupils:** Answer four questions, underline supporting words and independently write one house sentence.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check three independently answered questions and one original sentence, noting support required.
**Pupils:** Submit answers and writing.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to read one fact about Nina's house.
**Pupils:** Share a text-supported fact.
