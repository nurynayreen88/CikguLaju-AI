# English Year 2 — Lesson 132: Speaking 27

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils share three beach preferences and take four turns in a partner exchange.
**Success criteria:** three clear preferences and four relevant turns.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original preference cards:** swimming, building sandcastles, collecting shells. **Four-turn dialogue (one round):** A: What do you like at the beach? B: I like building sandcastles. A: Do you like collecting shells? B: Yes, I do. **Round 2 (swap roles):** A: What do you like at the beach? B: I like swimming. A: Do you like building sandcastles? B: Yes, I do. Each pupil speaks four turns in total across the two rounds. **Three sentence frames:** I like swimming. I like building sandcastles. I like collecting shells.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model expressing a beach preference and asking a partner.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pairs perform the two complete rounds above, swapping A/B roles so each pupil contributes four turns. Then each pupil independently says all three preference sentences using the three supplied frames; allow truthful substitutions if a pupil dislikes an activity.
**Pupils:** Share three preferences and take four turns.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record four relevant turns for each pupil across both rounds (0–4) and three independently expressed preferences (0–3). Do not count the partner's turns as the pupil's evidence; note prompts separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
