# English Year 2 — Lesson 68: Reading 14

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** Student's Book pp.86–87 (mapping only; TB unverified). All passages, scripts, questions and models below are original teacher-created resources, not textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of a short familiar text (main 3.2.1) and find at least four of five supporting written details (complementary 3.2.2).  
**Success criteria:** Individually select the correct main idea from three choices, identify one supporting clue, and locate at least four of five written details.  
**Materials:** Board, clothing picture cards, exercise books and printable resources below.

**Original fallback passage — “A Rainy Morning”:** “It is raining. Adam wears a blue raincoat and black boots. His sister, Mia, has a yellow umbrella. They walk to school together. At school, they put their wet coats on hooks.”
**Main idea (choose one):** A. Getting ready for school on a rainy day; B. Cooking at home; C. Playing football. **Answer:** A. **Supporting clue:** It is raining; Adam wears a raincoat and boots; they walk to school. **Five details/key:** weather—raining; Adam's raincoat—blue; boots—black; Mia's umbrella—yellow; destination—school.
**Questions/key:** “Who has a yellow umbrella?” — Mia. “Where do they put their coats?” — on hooks.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display two clothing pictures and ask pupils to name items and colours.  
**Pupils:** Identify two familiar clothing words.

## Activity 1 — Teacher modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original passage and model the difference between the main idea (getting ready for school in the rain) and a supporting detail (*blue raincoat*). Explain why answer A covers the whole passage.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided and paired practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils first choose the main idea (A/B/C) and underline a supporting clue. Then they locate five details and answer two comprehension questions. If using the Student's Book, the teacher must separately verify its actual text before assigning page-specific questions.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity, explain their choices and revise errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check each pupil's independent main-idea choice (0–1), supporting clue (0–1) and five details (0–5). Use the two comprehension questions for additional feedback. Record individual accuracy and assistance needed; do not substitute detail scores for the main-idea criterion.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful clothing words and invite a relevant response.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and one sentence.
