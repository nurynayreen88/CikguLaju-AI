# English Year 2 — Lesson 27: Speaking 6

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior Year 2 audit; dialogue and instructions below are original teacher-developed materials.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils give a sequence of three basic healthy-living instructions and understand three supported questions about free-time habits.  
**Success criteria:** One intelligible three-step instruction sequence and three appropriate answers to supported questions.  
**Materials:** Picture cards for reading, swimming, football and cycling; board; prompt cards below.

**Dialogue model:** A: “Do you like swimming?” B: “Yes, I do.” A: “What do you do on Saturday?” B: “I play football.” A: “Do you like reading?” B: “No, I don't.”  
**Pupil instruction model:** “First, eat fruit. Next, play outside. Finally, drink water.” Pupils adapt this three-step sequence with familiar healthy habits.  
**Question prompts (teacher reads):** “Do you play outside?” “Do you eat fruit?” “What do you do on Saturday?”  
**Optional action cards:** “Point to the swimming picture.” “Show me the football card.” “Stand up if you like reading.” “Put the cycling card on your desk.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four activity pictures and ask pupils to name each action.  
**Pupils:** Name and mime the activities.

## Activity 1 — Model questions and instructions (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate the full dialogue with a pupil, showing how to use *Do you like ...?* and *What do you do ...?* Model how to give three sequenced instructions using first, next and finally.  
**Pupils:** Echo the questions, practise response frames and practise giving three short instructions in order.

## Activity 2 — Interview and listen (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise pairs to exchange three questions, then reverse roles. Ask each pupil to give a three-step sequence of healthy-living instructions. Ask the three supported questions orally, without displaying the wording. Give pupils time to act independently before partner feedback. Offer written question frames for pupils needing support and an optional follow-up “Why?” for confident speakers.  
**Pupils:** Complete three paired exchanges, swap roles and give a three-step instruction sequence and answer supported questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually assess a three-step sequence of basic instructions, then ask three supported questions. Record spoken sequencing and listening comprehension separately, noting prompts.  
**Pupils:** Give three instructions in sequence and answer three spoken questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to state a favourite activity and demonstrate one instruction.  
**Pupils:** Give one preference and respond to a final instruction.
