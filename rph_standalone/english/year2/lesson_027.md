# English Year 2 — Lesson 27: Speaking 6

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior Year 2 audit; dialogue and instructions below are original teacher-developed materials.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask and answer three questions about free-time preferences and respond correctly to three spoken instructions.  
**Success criteria:** Three intelligible question–answer exchanges and three appropriate physical responses.  
**Materials:** Picture cards for reading, swimming, football and cycling; board; prompt cards below.

**Dialogue model:** A: “Do you like swimming?” B: “Yes, I do.” A: “What do you do on Saturday?” B: “I play football.” A: “Do you like reading?” B: “No, I don't.”  
**Instruction cards (teacher reads, pupils must not see text):** “Point to the swimming picture.” “Show me the football card.” “Stand up if you like reading.” “Put the cycling card on your desk.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four activity pictures and ask pupils to name each action.  
**Pupils:** Name and mime the activities.

## Activity 1 — Model questions and instructions (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate the full dialogue with a pupil, showing how to use *Do you like ...?* and *What do you do ...?* Say two instructions while modelling the expected response.  
**Pupils:** Echo the questions, practise response frames and follow two spoken instructions.

## Activity 2 — Interview and listen (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise pairs to exchange three questions, then reverse roles. Mix the four picture cards and read the four instruction cards in a new order without showing the words. Give pupils time to act independently before partner feedback. Offer written question frames for pupils needing support and an optional follow-up “Why?” for confident speakers.  
**Pupils:** Complete three paired exchanges, swap roles and respond physically to spoken instructions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually observe three oral exchanges and deliver three instructions in random order. Record question intelligibility, answer relevance and listening responses separately, noting prompts.  
**Pupils:** Ask and answer three questions and follow three instructions without partner cues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to state a favourite activity and demonstrate one instruction.  
**Pupils:** Give one preference and respond to a final instruction.
