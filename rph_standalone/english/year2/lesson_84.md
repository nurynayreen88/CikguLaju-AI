# English Year 2 — Lesson 84: Writing 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 3.3 / 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior mapping; original teacher-created resources below.  
**Objective:** Pupils write four legible clothing words and two sentences, checking one spelling.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the numerical targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Word bank:** jacket, trousers, shoes, shirt, skirt, socks. **Picture prompt:** a boy wearing a green jacket and black trousers.
**Model:** “He is wearing a green jacket. His trousers are black.”
**Spelling check:** Locate *trousers* in the picture dictionary and compare letter by letter.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display clothing cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two sentences and show how to verify the spelling of *trousers* with a reference.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils label four items in an outfit picture, write two sentences and check one spelling independently.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve responses with feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess four legible words, two meaningful sentences and one demonstrated spelling check. Note whether prompting was required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one target word and ask for a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
