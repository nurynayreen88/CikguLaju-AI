# English Year 2 — Lesson 153: Listening 31

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils understand and answer at least three of four spoken Unit 9 review questions during a group quiz.
**Success criteria:** At least three relevant written answers to four spoken questions.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Standalone teacher-created backup quiz (when Lesson 152 questions are unavailable):** 1. What colour is the sea? (Blue.) 2. What can you find on the sand? (Shells.) 3. What do you use to build a sandcastle? (Sand.) 4. What can you take to the beach? (A bucket.) **Materials:** groups' final quiz sheets from Lesson 152, paper for written answers, beach picture cards. Questions and keys are original supplementary examples, not textbook extracts.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model listening to a short spoken Unit 9 review question and writing a brief relevant answer.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Organise groups to ask their Lesson 152 quiz questions orally while other groups listen and write answers; use the four supplied backup questions when needed.
**Pupils:** Take turns asking questions orally and write answers to four questions asked by another group.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Collect each pupil's four written responses to spoken questions and record whether at least three are relevant.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
