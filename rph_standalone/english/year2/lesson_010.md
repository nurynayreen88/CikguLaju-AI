# English Year 2 — Lesson 10: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 5.2 / 5.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to existing Year 2 mapping. The chant, movements and oral prompts are teacher-created.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform four short chant lines with two suitable gestures and give two relevant oral responses about free-time activities.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible lines, two gestures and two relevant answers.  
**Materials:** Board, original chant and activity cards for reading, drawing, cycling and football.

## Original chant — “My Happy Day”
“I read my book, one, two, three!  
I draw a picture, look at me!  
I ride my bike, round and round!  
I kick a ball across the ground!”

**Gesture guide:** Mime opening a book; drawing; turning bicycle handlebars; kicking a ball.  
**Oral questions:** “Which activity do you like?” “What do you do after school?”  
**Response frames:** “I like ____.” “I ____ after school.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display four activity cards and invite pupils to choose a favourite.  
**Pupils:** Name an activity and demonstrate its gesture.

## Activity 1 — Echo and perform (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the full original chant with a steady rhythm, demonstrating each gesture. Repeat line by line, highlighting the action words. Divide the class into four groups to rehearse one line each.  
**Pupils:** Echo the lines, use gestures and join the class performance.

## Activity 2 — Rehearsal and speaking (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to rehearse the complete chant and give one another feedback on clear words and appropriate actions. Remove the written prompts for a second performance. Ask the two oral questions and model responses. Invite confident pupils to substitute an activity word and invent a matching gesture.  
**Pupils:** Perform in pairs, answer two oral questions and optionally create a new action.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil's performance and record intelligibility, two matching gestures and two relevant oral responses separately, noting the degree of prompting.  
**Pupils:** Perform the chant and answer two questions independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead one final group performance and ask pupils to identify a favourite line.  
**Pupils:** Recite and share a favourite activity.
