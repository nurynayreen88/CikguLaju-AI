# English Year 2 — Lesson 59: Writing 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior mapping. Materials below are original teacher-developed resources; detailed SOW activity verification pending.  
**Objective:** Pupils write four clothing words legibly and two meaningful sentences.  
**Success criteria:** Meet each component of the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, picture/word cards and the text below.

**Word bank:** shirt, skirt, trousers, shoes, socks, hat, dress, jacket.
**Model:** “I have a blue shirt. I wear black shoes.”
**Sentence frames:** “I have a ____ ____.” “I wear ____ ____.”
**Editing example:** “i have a red hat” → “I have a red hat.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display relevant picture cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model spelling and letter formation for four clothing words and demonstrate the editing example.  
**Pupils:** Follow the example and practise with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils label four clothing pictures, then use colour words and sentence frames to write two sentences; partners check spaces and capitals.  
**Pupils:** Complete the tasks and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check four legible words and two independent sentences with correct word spacing. Note independent responses and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the success criteria individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and invite a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and a short sentence.
