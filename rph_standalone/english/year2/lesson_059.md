# English Year 2 — Lesson 59: Writing 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write at least four clothing words legibly and two understandable sentences from picture prompts.
**Success criteria:** Four legible words and two meaningful sentences with word spacing.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 mapping audit); detailed SOW task sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Display clothing pictures and build a short word bank.
**Pupils:** Name familiar clothing and read the word bank.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Demonstrate forming four target words and writing a simple sentence from a picture cue; highlight capitals and spacing.
**Pupils:** Copy target words and rehearse one model sentence orally.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Give a new clothing picture and word bank; guide independent writing of four words and two short sentences, then facilitate peer checking.
**Pupils:** Write four words and two sentences, check spelling and spacing, and revise one sentence.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Assess each pupil's four words for legibility and two sentences for meaning and spacing; record results and feedback.
**Pupils:** Submit individual writing and make one correction.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one sentence model and ask for a final example.
**Pupils:** Read one written sentence aloud.

**Differentiation:** Support—word bank and sentence frames; extension—write a third descriptive sentence.
