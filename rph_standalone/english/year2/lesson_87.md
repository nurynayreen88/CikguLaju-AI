# English Year 2 — Lesson 87: Reading 18

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.4  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** SB p.93; TB unverified. All scripts and tasks below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Objective:** Pupils locate four of five details and give one oral explanation.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the stated numerical targets independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original alternative reading:** “Nora is going to school. She wears a white shirt, blue skirt and black shoes. It is cloudy, so she puts a yellow raincoat in her bag. Her friend has a red hat.” **Five details/key:** destination school; shirt white; skirt blue; shoes black; raincoat yellow. **Oral response frame:** “Nora takes a raincoat because ____.” — it is cloudy.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a relevant picture and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name visible items and predict the topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original alternative text and underline *blue skirt* as evidence.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils answer five questions from the alternative passage and give one explanation; SB p.93 remains a resource mapping, not a claim about its text.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check five individual details and one evidence-based oral explanation. Note independent accuracy and prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
