# English Year 2 — Lesson 125: Language Arts 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils name three interesting story details and answer three supported questions about a short narrative.
**Success criteria:** three relevant story details and three accurate narrative responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original teacher-created supplementary resource:** **Original story:** Robo visits a farm. It sees a cow and a duck. Robo helps carry water. At sunset, Robo waves goodbye. **Questions/key:** Who visits? Robo. Which animals? A cow and a duck. What does Robo carry? Water. **Art brief:** Draw a robot with a square head, two eyes, long arms and four wheels; label head, eyes, arms, wheels. **Three spoken cues:** Point to the head; show the wheels; touch the arms.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and elicit two ideas.
**Pupils:** Suggest robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created robot artwork with simple word labels.
**Pupils:** Suggest labels for the model.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Read the original short story twice; pupils name three interesting things or actions and answer three story questions.
**Pupils:** Create artwork and respond to three spoken descriptions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three named story details (SP 5.2.1) and three narrative comprehension responses (SP 1.2.3). Record individual support needed.
**Pupils:** Present individual creative and listening evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Invite pupils to share their artwork.
**Pupils:** Name one creative choice.
