# English Year 2 — Lesson 56: Listening 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.3 / 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book SB p.82. Exact Teacher's Book activity sequence not verified. The script and tasks below are teacher-created alternatives, not textbook transcriptions.  
**Objective:** Pupils identify four of five spoken clothing cues and demonstrate one picture-dictionary lookup.  
**Success criteria:** Achieve the measurable targets stated in the objective.  
**Materials:** Student's Book, board, picture/word cards and resources below.

**Original listening cues:** “Put on your shirt.” “Find the blue shoes.” “Show me the red hat.” “Point to the yellow dress.” “Find the green socks.”
**Five picture keys:** shirt, blue shoes, red hat, yellow dress, green socks. **Reference task:** Use a classroom picture dictionary or labelled word wall to check *dress* and point to its illustration.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display relevant picture cards, elicit familiar words and ask one prediction.  
**Pupils:** Name two words and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model matching the spoken *shirt* cue to a picture and checking *dress* in the picture dictionary.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and practise one example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read five original cues twice in mixed order. Pupils select picture cards and check one unfamiliar word in the class reference resource.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task, discuss answers and correct errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check five individual listening choices and observe one independent reference lookup. Note prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Respond individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two useful words and one lesson skill.  
**Pupils:** Share one correct example.
