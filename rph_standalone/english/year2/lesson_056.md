# English Year 2 — Lesson 56: Listening 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.3 / 3.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify at least 4 of 5 clothing-related words or details after listening twice and use one age-appropriate reference resource to confirm an unfamiliar word.
**Success criteria:** 4/5 individual listening responses and one demonstrated reference lookup.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.82, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.82 (Year 2 SOW mapping audit); exact Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show clothing picture cards, elicit familiar words and ask pupils to predict the listening topic.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar items and predict one word they might hear.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Preview Student's Book p.82, demonstrate one listening response and show how to check one unfamiliar word using a picture dictionary.
**Pupils:** Match clothing pictures to words and practise one supported dictionary lookup.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Use the p.82 listening input where available; otherwise read a clearly identified teacher-created clothing description twice. Give five picture-selection prompts and review answers.
**Pupils:** Listen twice, complete five individual selections, compare answers and explain one choice.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record each pupil's score out of five and whether they can locate a word using a picture dictionary; reteach items missed.
**Pupils:** Submit individual answers and demonstrate one lookup.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review key clothing words and invite an exit response.
**Pupils:** Name one clothing item.

**Differentiation:** Support—picture choices and repeated listening; extension—describe one item in a complete sentence.
