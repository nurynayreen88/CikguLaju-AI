# English Year 2 — Lesson 118: Speaking 24

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils use three suitable non-verbal responses during poster teamwork and make two polite requests for help.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate nods, gestures or eye-contact responses and two suitable help requests.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.105 (unverified mapping), two original robot cards and the complete teacher-created exchange below.
**Poster task:** Groups create an annotated robot poster. **Help phrases:** 'Excuse me, can you help me find a picture?' and 'Please help me with this label.' **Non-verbal cues:** nod, eye contact and pointing. **Original cards:** Robot A has a round head, two eyes and long arms. Robot B has a square head, one eye and short arms.
**Round 1 dialogue:** A: “What is your robot like?” B: “It has a round head.” A: “How many eyes has it got?” B: “It has two eyes.”
**Round 2 dialogue (swap roles):** A: “What is your robot like?” B: “It has a square head.” A: “How many eyes has it got?” B: “It has one eye.”
**Individual evidence:** Each pupil asks two questions and speaks four turns across two rounds (two turns as A, two as B). The dialogue is teacher-created, not copied from Student's Book p.105.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.105 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit two features.
**Pupils:** Name familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a short robot question-and-answer exchange.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one question.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide groups to create an annotated poster while practising nodding, eye contact and pointing, and asking for help politely in English.
**Pupils:** Take four turns and ask two questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three appropriate non-verbal responses (SP 2.2.1) and two suitable requests for help (SP 2.2.2), separately from two independently asked questions (0–2) across both rounds; do not count a partner's turns. Note prompts and support.
**Pupils:** Submit individual speaking evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Share one useful question.
