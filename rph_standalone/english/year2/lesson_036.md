# English Year 2 — Lesson 36: Listening 8

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the existing audit. The exact SOW task sequence is unverified; the materials below are original teacher-developed resources, not textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils understand a short spoken house narrative by ordering three events and identify four specific spoken room details.  
**Success criteria:** three correctly ordered story events and four correct room-detail picture choices.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, teacher-created picture/word cards and the printable text below.

**Original teacher-read script:** “In the kitchen, there is a table and two chairs. In the bedroom, there is a small bed. In the living room, there are two sofas. In the bathroom, there is a bath.”
**Teacher-created narrative:** “Mia enters the old house. First, she visits the kitchen and sees two chairs. Next, she goes upstairs to a bedroom and finds a small bed. Finally, she goes downstairs to the living room and sees two sofas.” **Event order key:** kitchen → bedroom → living room.  
**Picture-card key:** A = kitchen/table/two chairs; B = bedroom/bed; C = living room/two sofas; D = bathroom/bath.
**Four listening prompts:** (1) “Two chairs are here.” (A); (2) “There is a small bed here.” (B); (3) “There are two sofas here.” (C); (4) “You can see a bath here.” (D).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two house pictures and elicit familiar rooms or objects.  
**Pupils:** Name the rooms and point to the relevant objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the short narrative twice, model the first event and show how *two chairs* identifies the kitchen.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and demonstrate one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to order the three narrative events after listening, then mix picture cards A–D and read four prompts in random order. Pupils independently choose cards, then compare and explain which words helped. Repeat once for self-correction.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task, compare evidence with a partner and improve their responses.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the narrative without displaying it and assess the three-event sequence. Then read four prompts in a different order and record each pupil's detail responses.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the stated success criteria without partner help.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful words and invite one reflection on the task.  
**Pupils:** Share a word or a sentence from the lesson.
