# English Year 2 — Lesson 104: Writing 21

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three robot-themed sentences legibly and copy four printed robot words accurately.
**Success criteria:** three meaningful sentences and four accurately copied words.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model word formation, spacing and a simple robot sentence.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Provide robot picture prompts for writing and copying.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and copy four words.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check the stated success criteria and record individual PBD evidence.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one robot word or phrase.
