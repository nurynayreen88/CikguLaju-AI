# English Year 2 — Lesson 34: Writing 7

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the existing Year 2 mapping. All examples and activities below are teacher-developed, not attributed to the Teacher's Book.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils spell at least five familiar house words accurately in guided writing and ask two basic questions about a partner's house.  
**Success criteria:** Five of seven familiar house words spelled accurately and two intelligible questions asked to a partner.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, word cards (*bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, living room, bed, table, sofa*) and the simple house plan below.

**Teacher-created house plan:** Bedroom — one bed; kitchen — one table; living room — one sofa; bathroom — one bath. Draw four labelled boxes on the board.  
**Spelling check:** Teacher dictates bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, bed, table, sofa, hall without displaying the word cards.  
**Writing model:** “There is a bed in the bedroom. There is a table in the kitchen. There is a sofa in the living room.”  
**Writing frames:** “There is a ____ in the ____.” “My ____ has a ____.”  
**Question prompts:** “Where is the bed?” “What is in the living room?”  
**Answer key:** “In the bedroom.” “A sofa.”  
**Editing sample:** “there is a bed in the bedroom” → “There is a bed in the bedroom.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Draw the four-room house plan and ask pupils to identify two rooms and objects.  
**Pupils:** Name rooms and point to objects.

## Activity 1 — Model sentences (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the three model sentences and point to the corresponding boxes in the house plan. Model the frame “There is a ... in the ...”. Demonstrate capital letters, spaces and full stops using the editing sample.  
**Pupils:** Repeat the sentence frame and correct the model punctuation.

## Activity 2 — Write and interview (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to draw their own four-room plan or use the board plan, then write three sentences describing it. Pair pupils to check spelling and punctuation and ask two questions about their partner's house, then reverse roles. Support emerging writers with word cards; invite confident pupils to use *There are two ...* where accurate.  
**Pupils:** Write three sentences, revise them and ask/answer two questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Dictate seven familiar house words without displaying word cards and assess spelling accuracy. Observe each pupil asking two basic questions about a partner's house; record spelling and speaking evidence separately.  
**Pupils:** Write seven dictated words and ask two questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share one sentence and state one punctuation rule.  
**Pupils:** Read a sentence and explain their check.
