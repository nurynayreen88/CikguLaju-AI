# English Year 2 — Lesson 78: Listening 16

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 1.3 / 1.3.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** SB p.90; TB steps not verified. All example scripts and passages below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils interpret two spoken messages using visual clues and describe two clothing materials using suitable words.  
**Success criteria:** Achieve the measurable targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Materials vocabulary:** cotton, wool, woollen, leather. **Visual listening cues:** Show a cotton T-shirt and woollen jumper picture while saying 'This shirt is cotton' and 'This jumper is woollen'. Pupils select the matching picture and describe it: 'It is a cotton shirt.' 'It is a woollen jumper.'
**Original spoken message:** “The school concert starts soon. Amir wears a white shirt and black trousers. His sister wears a blue dress. They are ready to sing on stage.” **Main idea options:** A Getting dressed for a school concert; B Cooking at home; C Playing football. **Key:** A. **Oral questions/key:** What does Amir wear?—white shirt and black trousers. What does his sister wear?—blue dress.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two outfit pictures and elicit familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and colours.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original message twice and show how *concert* and *stage* suggest the main idea.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and practise one example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils listen to two material descriptions with visual clues, select matching clothing pictures and describe two objects aloud. The concert message is optional review only.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity and improve responses after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess two interpretations using visual clues (SP 1.3.1) and two spoken object descriptions (SP 2.1.5). Record independent performance and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two words and ask for a relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share a word and sentence.
