# English Year 2 — Lesson 150: Language Arts 30

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform a four-line beach rhyme and create two original descriptive lines.
**Success criteria:** four expressive lines and two meaningful original lines.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created four-line beach rhyme.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide expressive rehearsal and writing two original descriptive lines.
**Pupils:** Perform four lines and write two new lines.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
