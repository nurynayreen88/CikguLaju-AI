# English Year 2 — Lesson 159: Writing 32

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three legible robot sentences and correct two capital-letter and full-stop errors.
**Success criteria:** three understandable sentences and two accurate punctuation corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original robot scene:** square head, two green eyes and four wheels. **Three models:** It has a square head. Its eyes are green. It has four wheels. **Punctuation examples for teacher modelling:** it has four wheels -> It has four wheels.; its eyes are green -> Its eyes are green. **Unseen individual PBD items:** (1) the robot has a square head -> The robot has a square head. (2) it has two green eyes -> It has two green eyes. Keep the PBD answers hidden until pupils submit their work.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a picture related to The robot and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a robot sentence with a capital letter, clear word spacing and a full stop.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide three independent robot descriptions using the scene, then give the two unseen PBD correction items separately from the displayed models.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct capital letters and full stops.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score three independently written robot sentences (0–3) and two unseen capital-letter/full-stop corrections (0–2); record prompting separately and do not count copying the model as independent writing.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
