# English Year 2 — Lesson 115: Language Arts 23

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Each pupil contributes to a four-line robot dialogue and performs at least three spoken lines with clear expression across two rounds with swapped roles.
**Success criteria:** four dialogue lines and three intelligible spoken lines.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards, individual PBD record and original resources below.

**Original four-line dialogue:** A: “Hello, Robot! Can you help me?” B: “Yes! What do you need?” A: “Please carry my bag.” B: “Here you are!” **Variations:** bag→book; Robot→Zippy. **Round 2:** Repeat the adapted dialogue after partners swap A/B roles. Each pupil therefore speaks four lines in total (two in each round); assess the best three intelligible lines individually.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created short robot dialogue with expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Practise one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pairs draft a four-line dialogue, adapt one word, rehearse round 1 and then swap roles for round 2. Give each pupil an opportunity to deliver at least three lines across both rounds.
**Pupils:** Create four lines and perform three.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check the pair's four-line draft separately from each pupil's spoken evidence. Score at least three intelligible lines per pupil (0–3) across the two role-swapped rounds; note individual support and expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot expression.
**Pupils:** Share one new word or phrase.
