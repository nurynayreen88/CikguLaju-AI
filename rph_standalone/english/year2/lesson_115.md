# English Year 2 — Lesson 115: Language Arts 23

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Each pupil helps create a four-line robot chant and uses two suitable questions or statements to request attention or help.
**Success criteria:** one four-line imaginative chant and two appropriate requests for attention or help.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards, individual PBD record and original resources below.

**Original chant:** 'Robot, robot, roll away! / Help the animals every day! / Robot, robot, clap and sing! / See the joy that robots bring!' **Help phrases:** 'Excuse me, can you help me?' 'Please look at our poster.' **Original four-line dialogue:** A: “Hello, Robot! Can you help me?” B: “Yes! What do you need?” A: “Please carry my bag.” B: “Here you are!” **Variations:** bag→book; Robot→Zippy. **Round 2:** Repeat the adapted dialogue after partners swap A/B roles. Each pupil therefore speaks four lines in total (two in each round); assess the best three intelligible lines individually.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created short robot dialogue with expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Practise one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Groups create a four-line robot or animal chant and practise requesting help or attention using two suitable expressions. Give each pupil an opportunity to deliver at least three lines across both rounds.
**Pupils:** Create four lines and perform three.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check each pupil's contribution to a four-line chant (SP 5.3.1) and two requests for help or attention (SP 2.2.2), separately from each pupil's spoken evidence. Score at least three intelligible lines per pupil (0–3) across the two role-swapped rounds; note individual support and expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot expression.
**Pupils:** Share one new word or phrase.
