# English Year 2 — Lesson 79: Writing 16

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP (SOW check pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW check pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.91; exact Teacher's Book sequence unverified. Tasks below are original teacher-created alternatives.
**Objective:** Pupils write four legible clothing words and two meaningful outfit sentences (main 4.3.2), then give one intelligible oral description (complementary 2.1.5).
**Success criteria:** Independently produce four readable clothing words, two accurate outfit sentences and one relevant spoken description; record scores separately.
**Materials:** Outfit picture cards, word bank and exercise books.

**Word bank:** shirt, skirt, shoes, socks, jacket, trousers.
**Picture prompt:** A girl in a red skirt, white shirt and black shoes. **Independent assessment prompt:** A boy wearing a green shirt, blue trousers, brown shoes and a yellow hat; teacher draws or describes this second picture. **Word key:** shirt, trousers, shoes, hat. **Possible sentence answers:** He is wearing a green shirt. His trousers are blue. Other accurate sentences are accepted.
**Original model:** She is wearing a red skirt. Her shoes are black.
**Writing frames:** She is wearing a ____ ____. / Her ____ are ____.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the picture prompt and elicit two clothing words.
**Pupils:** Name clothing items.

## Activity 1 — Model writing (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate spelling four clothing words, spacing and full stops; construct the two model sentences.
**Pupils:** Copy the model and identify sentence boundaries.

## Activity 2 — Independent writing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide the independent assessment outfit picture specified above, guide pupils to label four items, write two sentences, check punctuation and share one sentence with a partner. Give word-bank prompts only where needed and record that support.
**Pupils:** Complete four labels, two sentences and one oral response.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Using the second picture and answer examples, score four readable labels (0–4), two meaningful sentences (0–2), and one relevant oral description (0–1). Record whether capitals, word spaces and full stops are correct, and any teacher prompts. Assess each pupil separately.
**Pupils:** Submit writing and describe their picture.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one clothing word and one punctuation rule.
**Pupils:** Read one corrected sentence.
