# English Year 2 — Lesson 79: Writing 16

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP (SOW check pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP (SOW check pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.91; exact Teacher's Book sequence unverified. Tasks below are original teacher-created alternatives.
**Objective:** Write four clothing words and two meaningful outfit sentences.
**Success criteria:** Four legible words, two sentences and one relevant oral description.
**Materials:** Outfit picture cards, word bank and exercise books.

**Word bank:** shirt, skirt, shoes, socks, jacket, trousers.
**Picture prompt:** A girl in a red skirt, white shirt and black shoes.
**Original model:** She is wearing a red skirt. Her shoes are black.
**Writing frames:** She is wearing a ____ ____. / Her ____ are ____.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the picture prompt and elicit two clothing words.
**Pupils:** Name clothing items.

## Activity 1 — Model writing (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate spelling four clothing words, spacing and full stops; construct the two model sentences.
**Pupils:** Copy the model and identify sentence boundaries.

## Activity 2 — Independent writing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide another outfit picture, guide pupils to label four items, write two sentences, check punctuation and share one sentence with a partner.
**Pupils:** Complete four labels, two sentences and one oral response.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess four written words, two meaningful sentences and one spoken description independently.
**Pupils:** Submit writing and describe their picture.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one clothing word and one punctuation rule.
**Pupils:** Read one corrected sentence.
