# English Year 2 — Lesson 79: Writing 16

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write four clothing-related words legibly and produce two meaningful sentences about an outfit picture.
**Success criteria:** four legible words and two understandable sentences.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.91, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.91 (Year 2 audit); exact Teacher's Book steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a clothing picture and collect known words.
**Pupils:** Suggest two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Preview Student's Book p.91 and model writing target words and a short sentence with correct spacing.
**Pupils:** Copy words and rehearse one sentence.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Provide a picture and word bank; guide independent writing of four words and two sentences.
**Pupils:** Write, check spacing and revise one response.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record four word forms and two sentences for each pupil.
**Pupils:** Submit individual written evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one spelling or spacing correction.
**Pupils:** Read one sentence.
