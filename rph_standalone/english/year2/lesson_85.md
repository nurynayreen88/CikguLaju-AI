# English Year 2 — Lesson 85: Language Arts 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior mapping; original teacher-created resources below.  
**Objective:** Pupils perform three of four lines and give two relevant personal responses.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the numerical targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original verse:** “A yellow hat upon my head, / My cosy socks are blue and red. / I zip my coat and tie my shoe, / Then wave and say hello to you!”
**Response frames:** “I like ____ because ____.” “My favourite colour is ____.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display clothing cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original verse expressively, demonstrate gestures and model two personal responses.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Groups rehearse the verse; each pupil performs three lines and shares a favourite phrase and colour with a partner.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve responses with feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe three clear lines and two relevant personal responses. Note whether prompting was required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one target word and ask for a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
