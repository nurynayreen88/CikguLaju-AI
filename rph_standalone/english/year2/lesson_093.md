# English Year 2 — Lesson 93: Reading 19

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read six familiar robot-related words aloud and match four of five printed words to pictures.
**Success criteria:** six intelligible readings and 4/5 correct matches.
**Teaching materials:** Teacher-created robot cards, board and individual record sheet.

**Printable words:** head, eyes, arms, legs, jump, wave. **Picture labels:** A head; B eyes; C arms; D legs; E jumping robot; F waving robot. **Five matches/key:** head–A, eyes–B, legs–D, jump–E, wave–F.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display robot picture cards.
**Pupils:** Name pictured parts or actions.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model sounding out *head*, *legs* and *jump* using word and picture cards.
**Pupils:** Read words aloud with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give pairs all six word cards to read aloud, then five individual matching questions.
**Pupils:** Read six words and complete five matches.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record six oral readings and five independently completed matches.
**Pupils:** Submit individual reading evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a challenging word.
**Pupils:** Read one chosen word.
