# English Year 2 — Lesson 133: Reading 27

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five details in a short beach paragraph and answer two questions.
**Success criteria:** four correct detail responses and two supported answers.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model locating evidence in a teacher-created beach paragraph.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Provide five detail prompts and two comprehension questions.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and write two answers.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
