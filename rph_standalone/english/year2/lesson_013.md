# English Year 2 — Lesson 13: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 3.1 / 3.1.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 1.1 / 1.1.1  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping; *Super Minds 1* Student's Book p.63, Workbook p.63, Teacher's Book T63 (PDF page 137), visually checked. CD2 Tracks 29–30.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) identify and read at least four short printed words containing the short vowel sound **u**, and (2) recognise the target sound when listening to a short model sentence.  
**Success criteria:** Four accurately identified/decoded short-u words and one correct recognition or repetition of the /ʌ/ sound in context.  
**Materials:** SB p.63, WB p.63, word cards **much, fun, mum, mud, duck, jump**, board, CD2 Tracks 29–30 or teacher-read model.  
**Value:** Asking for help when needed.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit the story *We're lost!* by writing “Where's the ___?”, “We're ___.” and “I've got an ___.” Ask pupils to complete the sentences from memory.  
**Pupils:** Recall *lake*, *lost* and *idea* and say who helped the children.

## Activity 1 — Read and locate (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to SB p.63 Activity 2 (*Think!*). Read the four possible sentences aloud once, then ask pupils to read them silently and choose the sentence that best fits the story picture. Check the answer “Come with me.” For Activity 3, write **much** and **fun** on the board, highlight **u** and model the short vowel sound. Ask pupils to find “This isn't much fun” in story frame 3 and identify who says it.  
**Pupils:** Read the options, select the matching sentence, find the printed speech bubble and identify Thunder as the speaker.

## Activity 2 — Phonics reading and listening (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model segmenting and blending **m-u-ch** and **f-u-n**. Use SB p.63 Activity 4 with CD2 Track 29, or read the Teacher's Book sound sentence: “Mum jumps in the mud with the ducks.” Ask pupils to identify the repeated short-u sound. Guide WB p.63 Activity 1 (look and circle the picture about asking for help; correct picture 2). Then use WB Activity 2 with CD2 Track 30 or a teacher-read set of the short-u target words; pupils read the printed words and match them to the correct names/objects.  
**Pupils:** Blend and read the word cards, follow the sentence, repeat the short-u sound and complete the Workbook reading tasks. Partners check the printed answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show **much, fun, mum, mud, duck, jump** in a mixed order. Ask each pupil to identify/read four words independently. Say “Mum jumps in the mud” and ask pupils to identify a word containing the target sound. Record printed-word recognition and listening evidence separately.  
**Pupils:** Read four words and identify a spoken short-u word without copying a partner.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask why the characters in the story needed help and remind pupils to ask for help politely. Dictate one short-u word for pupils to write and check.  
**Pupils:** Give one reason for asking for help and write the dictated word.
