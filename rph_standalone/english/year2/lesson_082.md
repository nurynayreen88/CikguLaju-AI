# English Year 2 — Lesson 82: Speaking 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe three clothing objects and introduce themselves to an audience using a fixed phrase before presenting a poster.
**Success criteria:** three intelligible object descriptions and one clear self-introduction using a fixed phrase.
**Teaching materials:** Two teacher-created outfit picture cards and the dialogue below.

**Picture card A:** a child wearing a red shirt, blue shorts and white shoes. **Picture card B:** a child wearing a yellow dress, green hat and black shoes.
**Original model dialogue:** A: “What are you wearing?” B: “I'm wearing a red shirt and blue shorts.” A: “What colour are your shoes?” B: “My shoes are white.”
**Presentation frame:** 'Hello. My name is ____. This is our clothing poster. This shirt is cotton. These shoes are black. Thank you.'
**Speaking prompts:** “What is the child wearing?” “What colour is the hat?” “What colour are the shoes?” **Possible answers for card B:** “A yellow dress.” “Green.” “Black.” **Turn-taking cues:** ask, listen, respond, ask a follow-up.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show clothing pictures and ask what pupils notice.
**Pupils:** Name familiar items.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Display card A, act out the four-line original dialogue with a pupil, then model two turn-taking cues: listen before responding and ask a follow-up question.
**Pupils:** Practise the model with a partner.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give partners different cards A and B. Each pupil introduces themselves using a fixed phrase, presents a clothing poster and describes three visible items to a small group. Use the three speaking prompts as support.
**Pupils:** Describe three items and contribute two turns.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three accurate individual clothing descriptions (0–3) and one fixed-phrase introduction (0–1) for each pupil. Note clarity, turn-taking and prompts needed; do not assign a shared pair score.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate speaking individually within a pair.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review polite turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Share one useful phrase.
