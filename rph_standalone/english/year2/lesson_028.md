# English Year 2 — Lesson 28: Reading 6

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 2 audit. The passage, questions and answer key below are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate three explicit details in a short text and write two short familiar instructions based on the activities.  
**Success criteria:** Three correct text-supported answers and two meaningful written instructions using action verbs.  
**Materials:** Printed passage below, board, pencils and exercise books.

## Original reading text — “Our Saturday”
“On Saturday, Adam plays football in the park with his brother. His sister Sara reads a book under a tree. After lunch, Adam rides his bike. Sara draws a picture of a cat. In the evening, they watch TV with their parents.”

**Questions:** (1) Where does Adam play football? (2) Who plays football with Adam? (3) What does Sara do under a tree? (4) What does Adam do after lunch? (5) Who watches TV in the evening?  
**Answer key:** (1) In the park. (2) His brother. (3) Reads a book. (4) Rides his bike. (5) Adam, Sara and their parents.  
**Instruction prompts:** Write two short instructions for a partner using actions from the passage. **Model answers:** “Read a book.” “Draw a cat.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of a park and a book; elicit two activity words.  
**Pupils:** Name the activities.

## Activity 1 — Read and locate (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the text aloud, model scanning for *park* and underline the evidence for Question 1. Explain that answers should be supported by printed words.  
**Pupils:** Follow the text, highlight key activity words and locate one detail.

## Activity 2 — Answer and write (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to find answers to Questions 2–5, then guide pupils to write two short instructions using action verbs from the text. Give extra support by highlighting names and time words; challenge confident readers to explain what Sara does after lunch.  
**Pupils:** Find evidence, answer questions and write two short instructions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils independently to answer Questions 1, 3 and 4, pointing to evidence in the text. Collect the two written instructions and record reading and writing performance separately.  
**Pupils:** Answer three questions and submit two written instructions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask which word helped pupils find an answer quickly.  
**Pupils:** Share one useful clue.
