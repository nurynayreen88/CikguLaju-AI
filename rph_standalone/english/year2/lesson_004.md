# English Year 2 — Lesson 4: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils locate two details in a short free-time text and recognise two spoken words.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two text-supported answers and two correct listening choices.
**Materials:** Pictures, word cards, relevant textbook unit (page unverified).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures connected to Free Time and elicit two words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer a question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model locating a printed clue and identifying one spoken word.
**Pupils:** Follow the demonstration and respond once.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide two reading questions and two teacher-created listening cues.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and respond to two spoken cues.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Give one independent response.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful expressions.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate one learned response.
