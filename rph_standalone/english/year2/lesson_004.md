# English Year 2 — Lesson 4: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping; *Super Minds 1* Student's Book p.60, Workbook p.60, Teacher's Book T60 (PDF page 132), visually checked. CD2 Track 23 (song).  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can locate at least four days and corresponding activities in the printed song, and identify at least three activity phrases when heard.  
**Success criteria:** Four accurate day–activity matches with evidence in the printed song; three correct listening responses.  
**Materials:** SB p.60, WB p.60, CD2 Track 23 (optional), board, day cards, pencils.  
**Text focus:** Printed song “Busy week” on SB p.60: Monday—swimming; Tuesday—play ball; Wednesday and Thursday—computer games; Friday—football; Saturday—sing; Sunday—hide-and-seek.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime swimming, football and singing; ask pupils to name each activity. Write the day names on the board.  
**Pupils:** Guess and recall the days of the week.

## Activity 1 — Supported reading of the song (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Direct pupils to SB p.60. Point to the printed lines and model scanning for a day name, then reading the activity that follows. Demonstrate: “On Mondays we go swimming.” Play CD2 Track 23 once, or read the printed text aloud while pupils follow.  
**Pupils:** Track the words, underline the day names and circle the corresponding activity words. Repeat short phrases after the teacher.

## Activity 2 — Find evidence and respond (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask text-dependent questions: “What do they do on Fridays?”, “Which days do they play computer games?”, “What happens on Sundays?”, “What do they do on Saturdays?” Require pupils to point to the printed line before answering. In pairs, guide SB Activity 2: point to each illustration and name its day. Use WB p.60 Activity 1 (read the song and tick the matching picture) and Activity 2 (write and draw a new verse), with the latter as extension for faster pupils.  
**Pupils:** Read and locate evidence; answer “On Fridays they play football”, “On Wednesdays and Thursdays they play computer games”, “On Sundays they play hide-and-seek” and “On Saturdays they sing.” Match pictures and days; complete selected Workbook tasks.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually ask pupils to locate four day–activity pairs in the printed song and respond to three teacher-spoken phrases by pointing to the matching illustration. Record reading and listening evidence separately.  
**Pupils:** Point to printed evidence, read short phrases and identify spoken activities.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What do you do on Sundays?” and model a short personal response. Invite two volunteers to share.  
**Pupils:** Say one personal day–activity sentence.
