# English Year 2 — Lesson 60: Language Arts 12

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 5.2 / 5.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior mapping. Materials below are original teacher-developed resources; detailed SOW activity verification pending.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform three of four chant lines and create one suitable word variation.  
**Success criteria:** Meet each component of the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, picture/word cards and the text below.

**Original chant:** “Put on your shirt, one, two, three. / Pull on your socks, come with me. / Tie your shoes and wear your hat. / Ready to go, just like that!”
**Gestures:** put on shirt, pull socks, tie shoes, put on hat. **Variation frame:** “Put on your ____, one, two, three.” **Writing task:** Copy four clothing words from the chant.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display relevant picture cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recite the original chant with gestures and model changing *shirt* to *jacket* in the variation frame.  
**Pupils:** Follow the example and practise with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs rehearse, create a new line, perform and copy four clothing words legibly.  
**Pupils:** Complete the tasks and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe three intelligible lines, one appropriate variation and four copied clothing words. Note independent responses and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the success criteria individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and invite a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and a short sentence.
