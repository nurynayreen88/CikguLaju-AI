# English Year 2 Lesson 098 — supplementary reading evidence pack

**Status: DRAFT / NOT READY.** This does not replace Lesson 098 RPH or change any source mapping, RPT or DB.

## Printable teacher-created reading strip
“Milo is a small robot in the school library. It has a square head, two orange eyes and four wheels. Milo can carry books. Every morning it helps the librarian.”

## Individual pupil tasks
1. Circle the sentence telling **where** Milo works.
2. Underline the sentence telling **what** Milo does to help.
3. Match three short sentences to their main ideas: “The robot moves on four wheels” → movement; “The robot helps the librarian” → helping; “The robot and its friends carry books together” → teamwork.
4. Listen to the teacher say “The robot has four wheels” and “The robot works in a library.” Answer “How many wheels?” and “Where?” separately.

## PBD checklist
Three correct reading main-idea matches (0–3) and two correct supported listening details (0–2). Keep reading and listening evidence separate per pupil.
