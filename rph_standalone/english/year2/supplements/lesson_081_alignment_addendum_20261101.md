## Confirmed issue
Original Activity 2 teacher instruction makes the main-idea question an optional extension, but pupil instruction says pupils select the main idea. Both cannot be the compulsory task at once.

## Proposed supplementary 25-minute Activity 2
- Teacher: Read two short clothing instructions aloud, each accompanied by a mime or picture cue. Ask pupils to match each spoken message to a clothing card. Invite individual pupils to describe two items orally using a colour-and-item phrase. Keep the sports-day main-idea question as an **optional extension** after the two required message interpretations.
- Pupils: Listen, match two spoken messages to the correct cards, and independently say two clothing descriptions. Pupils who finish early may select the main idea of the sports-day script.

## Proposed individual PBD record
Record per pupil: spoken-message interpretation **0–2** and intelligible clothing descriptions **0–2**. Evidence is individual even when cards are used in pairs. Main-idea extension is not scored as a compulsory criterion.
