## Optional teacher-created extension (only after teacher approval)
Provide a drawing box headed **My robot today** and sentence prompts:
1. I drew a robot with ____.
2. It has ____.
3. I like its ____.
4. I used ____ to make my drawing.
5. Next time, I want to add ____.

Pupils draw their own robot, plan ideas with word cards, draft **four sentences** (a fifth optional), then reread and revise with a partner. Teacher checks sentence-start capitals, spacing and full stops. This is a proposed reflective-writing extension, **not an official SOW transcription**.
