## Confirmed issue
The objectives and individual PBD assess personal ability questions and answers, while the original guided-practice pupil instruction also requires robot-feature descriptions. These are different language tasks and must be separated.

## Proposed supplementary Activity 2 (25 minutes)
- Teacher: Model three questions: “Can you swim?”, “Can you dance?”, “Can you run?” Provide the response frames “Yes, I can” and “No, I can't.” Organise partner interviews and rotate who asks and answers. Observe each pupil individually.
- Pupils: Each pupil asks three personal ability questions and gives three answers about their own abilities. Pupils record simple ticks for interview responses if appropriate. Robot pictures may be used as an introductory visual only; describing robot features is **not** a required outcome for this activity.

## Proposed individual PBD record
Per pupil, record **0–3** intelligible ability questions and **0–3** relevant personal answers. Do not score robot-feature descriptions against these objectives.
