# English Year 2 — Lesson 44: Speaking 9

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils ask and answer two questions about rooms and give one short description.
**Success criteria (draft):** two intelligible exchanges and one relevant description.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.78, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.78 (Year 2 SOW mapping audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce The old house with a picture and two questions.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher:** Model the speaking 9 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Organise pair or individual practice and provide feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe one individual response and record feedback.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the lesson and invite two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
