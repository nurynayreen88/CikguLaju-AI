# English Year 2 — Lesson 5: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 5.2 / 5.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to the prior Year 2 mapping. The chant and teaching steps are original teacher-developed material.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can name at least three foods mentioned in an original chant, state one favourite food and identify the main idea of two short spoken sentences with support.  
**Success criteria:** Three correctly named foods, one personal preference and two correct main-idea choices.  
**Materials:** Original chant below, picture cards for rice, noodles, apples and bananas, seven day cards, and two teacher-read listening sentences.

## Original chant — “Food Days”
“On Monday, apples crunch and shine.  
On Tuesday, noodles taste so fine.  
On Wednesday, rice is on my plate.  
On Thursday, bananas taste so great!”

**Teacher-read listening sentences:** (1) “On Monday, I eat an apple.” (main idea: eating an apple on Monday); (2) “On Wednesday, I eat rice.” (main idea: eating rice on Wednesday). For each, show three picture/day combinations, only one correct. Do not display the written sentences during the listening check.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display four food pictures and day cards. Ask which foods pupils like, modelling “I like apples” and “My favourite is rice.”  
**Pupils:** Name familiar foods and share a preference.

## Activity 1 — Listen and name (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original chant aloud twice. Invite pupils to point to each food picture and name the foods in the chant; model the four day-food links.  
**Pupils:** Identify foods of interest in the text and repeat the corresponding day phrases.

## Activity 2 — Food-and-day circle (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Place food pictures in a circle. Invite pupils to select a favourite and say “I like ___” or “My favourite is ___.” Ask which day the chant mentions each food. Practise two short listening sentences with picture choices before the independent assessment.  
**Pupils:** Name at least three foods, share one personal preference, and match day-food combinations after listening.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually ask pupils to name three foods from the chant and state one favourite. Then read the two unseen teacher-read sentences without showing their text and record each pupil's independent main-idea picture choice. Separate independent responses from prompted responses.  
**Pupils:** Name three foods, state a preference and select the main idea of each spoken sentence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the food words and ask pupils which food they would choose for Friday.  
**Pupils:** Share a food choice using a simple phrase.
