# English Year 2 — Lesson 5: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 5.2 / 5.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to the prior Year 2 mapping. The chant and teaching steps are original teacher-developed material.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can perform four lines of a simple chant with at least two suitable actions and recognise three activity words when spoken.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible chant lines, two matching actions and three correct listening choices.  
**Materials:** Printed chant below, board, activity picture cards for reading, drawing, swimming and football.

## Original chant — “After School”
“Read a book, turn a page.  
Draw a picture, colour the page.  
Swim, swim, splash and play.  
Kick a ball at the end of the day!”

**Gestures:** Mime turning a page; drawing and colouring; swimming; kicking a ball.  
**Listening check prompts:** Say **draw**, **swim**, **kick** in random order; pupils point to the corresponding picture. Do not display the written prompt during listening assessment.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four activity pictures and mime two actions. Ask pupils to guess each action.  
**Pupils:** Name the activities and imitate the actions.

## Activity 1 — Echo and respond (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the full original chant. Read it slowly, one line at a time, with the matching gesture. Repeat with a steady beat and draw attention to the action words.  
**Pupils:** Echo each line, follow the rhythm and demonstrate the gestures.

## Activity 2 — Rehearse and listen (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide pupils into four groups and assign one line to each group. Combine the lines into a whole-class performance, then practise in pairs. Hide the chant and say the listening prompts **draw**, **swim** and **kick** in mixed order, asking pupils to select the correct pictures. For extension, invite pupils to replace one action word and create a matching gesture.  
**Pupils:** Rehearse and perform the chant, match three spoken words to pictures and invent an action if ready.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe pupils performing four lines with two actions, then read three activity words in random order and record each pupil's independent picture choices. Note prompted and unprompted performance separately.  
**Pupils:** Perform and respond to three listening cues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to name their favourite activity from the chant and demonstrate its action.  
**Pupils:** Share one activity and gesture.
