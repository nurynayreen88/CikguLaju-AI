# English Year 2 — Lesson 139: Speaking 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils give three short beach descriptions and respond to two listening prompts.
**Success criteria:** three intelligible descriptions and two relevant responses.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.113, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.113 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model beach descriptions and listening before responding.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide picture-supported speaking and two teacher-created listening prompts.
**Pupils:** Describe three objects and respond to two prompts.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
