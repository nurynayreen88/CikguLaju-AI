**Objective:** With support, pupils recognise and reproduce the /ʃ/ sound in *sh* words (main 1.1.1) and blend four familiar printed words using letter sounds (complementary 3.1.3). This is a teacher-developed phonics alternative pending verification of the exact SB p.75/TB sequence.  
**Success criteria:** Individually produce /ʃ/ correctly in at least 3 of 4 target words and blend at least 3 of 4 printed words, with the level of teacher support recorded.  
**Materials:** Board, four word cards (*ship, shop, fish, dish*), picture prompts drawn by the teacher, exercise books and the teacher-created assessment below.

**Additional teacher-created material (not textbook content):** Word cards: **ship, shop, fish, dish**. Teacher says /ʃ/ clearly and models blending: /ʃ/–/ɪ/–/p/ → *ship*; /ʃ/–/ɒ/–/p/ → *shop*; /f/–/ɪ/–/ʃ/ → *fish*; /d/–/ɪ/–/ʃ/ → *dish*. **Answer key:** ship, shop, fish, dish. Prepare simple matching sketches or allow pupils to point to the printed words; no textbook illustration is reproduced.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Say *ship* and *fish* while pointing to the letters **sh**; ask pupils to listen for the shared sound.  
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat and identify the shared /ʃ/ sound.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show **sh** and model mouth position, then segment and blend *ship* and *shop* aloud. Contrast /ʃ/ with /s/ without requiring independent phonetic transcription.  
**Pupils:** Echo the sound, blend the two words with teacher support and point to the matching cards.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display *ship, shop, fish, dish* in mixed order; pronounce each slowly and ask pupils to identify **sh**, then blend and read the word. Demonstrate *fish* once; run pair practice with one pupil pointing and the other blending, then swap roles. Give additional sound-by-sound prompts to pupils who need them.  
**Pupils:** Identify the digraph, blend all four words, practise in pairs and correct errors after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Call each pupil individually to repeat /ʃ/ in four target words and blend four shuffled cards. Record two scores out of four and whether support was needed; avoid scoring pair responses as individual evidence.  
**Pupils:** Produce the sound and read cards individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two shuffled cards and ask what sound **sh** makes. Recap the correct blending sequence.  
**Pupils:** Say /ʃ/ and read one card aloud.
