# English Year 2 — Lesson 67: Speaking 14

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objective:** Pupils practise speaking 14 using language from Get dressed!.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.85, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.85 (Year 2 audit); exact Teacher's Book steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce Get dressed! with a picture and two questions.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher:** Model the speaking 14 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Organise pair or individual practice and provide feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe one individual response and record feedback.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the lesson and invite two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
