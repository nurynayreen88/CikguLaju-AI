# English Year 2 — Lesson 14: Writing

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils write two free-time sentences with capital letters and full stops and identify two spoken activity words.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two correctly punctuated sentences and two accurate listening responses.
**Materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.64, topic pictures, word cards and exercise books. The page mapping is confirmed in the prior Year 2 SOW audit; the exact Teacher's Book procedure has not been checked.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures related to Free Time and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer one question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use page 64 as the mapped resource and model two punctuated sentences; add two teacher-spoken activity cues.
**Pupils:** Observe and give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide writing two sentences and identifying two spoken activities.
**Pupils:** Write two sentences and identify two spoken activities.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two key words and invite a final example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned response.
