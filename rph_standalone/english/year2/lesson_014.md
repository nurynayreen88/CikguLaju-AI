# English Year 2 — Lesson 14: Writing

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 4.3 / 4.3.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping: SB p.64; *Super Minds 1* Teacher's Book T64 (PDF page 140), Student's Book p.64 and Workbook p.64, visually checked. CD2 Track 31.  
**Important alignment note:** TB T64 identifies SB p.64 as a **listening-for-specific-information** lesson, whereas the existing SOW mapping assigns **Writing** as the main skill. This RPH therefore uses the book's listening stimulus but adds an explicit teacher-designed writing outcome. It is not a verbatim TB lesson.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) write at least three short answers using appropriate capital letters, spaces and full stops and (2) identify at least three names or numbers from the spoken dialogue.  
**Success criteria:** Three readable, appropriately punctuated written answers and three correct listening details.  
**Materials:** SB p.64, WB p.64, CD2 Track 31 or teacher-read dialogue, seven day cards, exercise books, pencils.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the TB day-of-the-week paper-ball game. A pupil says a day, passes the paper ball and the next pupil says the following day.  
**Pupils:** Recall and say days in order.

## Activity 1 — Listen and model written responses (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show SB p.64 playground picture. Read the six questions with pupils and introduce the possessive apostrophe in “Mark's school” and “Mark's brother”. Play CD2 Track 31 twice, pausing between questions, or read the following short adapted facts: Mark goes to Park School; his brother is Ben; his teacher is Mrs Green; he plays football two days a week, swims three days a week and plays computer games one day a week. Model turning “Park” into the written answer “Mark goes to Park School.”  
**Pupils:** Listen, note the six key details and help form one correctly punctuated written sentence.

## Activity 2 — Guided writing (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check SB p.64 answers (1 Park; 2 Ben; 3 Mrs Green; 4 two; 5 three; 6 one). Provide three writing frames: “Mark goes to ___ School.” “His brother's name is ___.” “He goes swimming ___ days a week.” Model capital letters and full stops, then ask pupils to write three sentences independently. Use WB p.64 Activity 1 (draw lines to match free-time activity phrases) and Activity 2 (write the days) as additional word-level writing practice.  
**Pupils:** Complete the listening answers, write three sentences, check punctuation with a partner and complete selected WB tasks.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read three details from the dialogue in random order and ask pupils to record the matching names or numbers. Independently inspect each pupil's three sentences for capitals, spacing and full stops. Record listening and writing evidence separately.  
**Pupils:** Identify three spoken details and submit three independently written sentences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite one pupil to mime a free-time activity; ask another to say a sentence about it. Display one incorrectly punctuated sentence and ask pupils to correct it.  
**Pupils:** Name the activity and correct the written sentence.
