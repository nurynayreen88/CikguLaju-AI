# English Year 2 — Lesson 8: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in existing Year 2 mapping; all printed reading materials are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise six printed activity words and read three short activity phrases aloud.  
**Success criteria:** At least five out of six correct word identifications and three intelligible phrases.  
**Materials:** Board, six word cards and six picture cards.

**Word cards:** read, draw, swim, cycle, play, sing.  
**Phrase cards:** read a book; draw a cat; swim in a pool; cycle in the park; play football; sing a song.  
**Matching key:** read—book; draw—cat; swim—pool; cycle—park; play—football; sing—song.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a book and mime drawing. Ask pupils to name the actions.  
**Pupils:** Say the action words and point to the matching pictures.

## Activity 1 — Decode words and phrases (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the six printed action words. Model reading them aloud and identifying initial letters and word shapes. Combine each action word with its matching phrase card, pointing from left to right.  
**Pupils:** Track the print, echo the six words and read three phrases together.

## Activity 2 — Sort, match and read (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mix the six word cards and ask pairs to match them with pictures. Give pupils the six phrase cards in a different order; ask them to read and match each to the correct action. Provide a reduced three-card set for pupils needing support and ask confident readers to read all six phrases independently.  
**Pupils:** Sort six cards, match the phrases and practise reading aloud to a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually present six shuffled word cards, then three shuffled phrase cards. Record correct word recognition and intelligible phrase reading separately; do not count repetition after a teacher model as independent reading.  
**Pupils:** Read five or more words and three phrases independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to choose one card and make a gesture matching the phrase.  
**Pupils:** Read the phrase and mime it.
