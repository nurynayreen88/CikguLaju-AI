# English Year 2 — Lesson 8: Reading

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; final verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in existing Year 2 mapping; all printed reading materials are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise the letter a, distinguish the long-a sound in familiar words and name letters in three words.  
**Success criteria:** At least four of six correct long-a sound choices and correct letter names in three printed words.  
**Materials:** Board, eight word cards and individual sound-choice response grid.

**Word bank from SOW Lesson 8:** play, watch, day, game, ball, what, hurray, match.  
**Sound key:** Long-a sound in play, day, game, hurray; other vowel sounds in watch, ball, what, match.  
**PBD response grid:** Read play, match, day, watch, game, hurray aloud. Pupils choose long-a or other for each. Key: long-a, other, long-a, other, long-a, long-a.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the eight word cards and ask pupils to identify the shared letter a.  
**Pupils:** Point to the letter and name familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model the sounds (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the long-a sound in play, day, game and hurray. Contrast the sound with watch, ball, what and match. Guide pupils to recognise beginning, middle and ending sounds.  
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat and practise sounding out words with support.

## Activity 2 — Sort and name letters (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to sort eight cards by the vowel sound. Check answers, then ask pupils to name letters in play, day and game. Provide additional oral modelling where necessary.  
**Pupils:** Sort cards, practise reading and name individual letters.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read six check words without showing the key. Record independent sound decisions and letter naming for each pupil. Distinguish prompted from unprompted answers.  
**Pupils:** Complete six sound decisions and name the letters in three words.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revisit the four long-a examples and invite one pupil example.  
**Pupils:** Say a long-a word and identify its letter a.
