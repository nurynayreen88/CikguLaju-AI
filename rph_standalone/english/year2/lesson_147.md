# English Year 2 — Lesson 147: Writing 30

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three legible beach sentences and correct two punctuation errors.
**Success criteria:** three legible sentences and two appropriate corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.115, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original scene:** a small crab, two shells and a blue bucket. **Models:** The crab is small. There are two shells. The bucket is blue. **Punctuation corrections for modelling (teacher-created):** the crab is small -> The crab is small.; the bucket is blue -> The bucket is blue. **Separate independent PBD correction items:** (1) there are two shells -> There are two shells. (2) i see a crab -> I see a crab. Show the answers only after assessment.

**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.115 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model legible beach sentences, including initial capitals, word spacing and final full stops.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide three picture-based sentences and model the two supplied correction examples. Then hide the model corrections and give the two different independent PBD correction items.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three legible picture-based sentences (0–3) and the two unseen independent punctuation corrections (0–2); check capitals and full stops in each correction, and note support separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
