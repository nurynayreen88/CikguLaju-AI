# English Year 2 — Lesson 160: Language Arts 42

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform four lines of a teacher-created beach rhyme with expression.
**Success criteria:** four intelligible expressive lines.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a picture related to At the beach and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model an expressive teacher-created beach rhyme.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide rehearsal and individual rhyme performance.
**Pupils:** Perform four lines with expression.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
