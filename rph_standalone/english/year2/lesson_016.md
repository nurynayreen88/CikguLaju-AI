# English Year 2 — Lesson 16: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 3.1 / 3.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to the prior Year 2 mapping. The script and activities below are original teacher-developed materials, not Teacher's Book instructions.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils identify four spoken day–activity details and name the letters in four familiar printed words.  
**Success criteria:** Four accurate listening matches and four accurate letter-naming responses.  
**Materials:** Board; seven day cards; activity cards for reading, drawing, cycling, football and swimming; pupil answer grid below.

## Original listening text
Read twice at a natural, slow pace without showing the text to pupils:

“Hello! I'm Aina. On Monday, I read a book. On Tuesday, I draw a cat. On Wednesday, I ride my bike. On Friday, I play football with my friends. On Saturday, I swim with my sister. On Sunday, I rest at home.”

**Pupil answer grid:** Match each day with one of the two choices: Monday (read / swim); Tuesday (draw / football); Wednesday (bike / book); Friday (football / draw); Saturday (swim / rest).  
**Answer key:** Monday—read; Tuesday—draw; Wednesday—ride a bike; Friday—play football; Saturday—swim.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime two activities, display the five activity cards and elicit the vocabulary.  
**Pupils:** Guess and say the activity names.

## Activity 1 — Listen for detail (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original text once without pauses. Ask pupils which days they heard. Read it again, pausing between sentences, and model matching Monday to *read*.  
**Pupils:** Listen and independently complete the five-item answer grid.

## Activity 2 — Check and read (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the text a third time for checking. Display printed activity cards; ask pupils to name the letters in read, draw, bike and swim, then match each word to its picture. Ask pairs to say one sentence about Aina's week. Reveal the answer key only after independent attempts.  
**Pupils:** Check their grid, name the letters in four printed words and share one day–activity sentence with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read four selected day–activity sentences in mixed order without showing the script. Ask each pupil to point to the correct day and activity, then read four printed phrases. Record listening and reading evidence separately, including support needed.  
**Pupils:** Identify four spoken details and letters in four printed words independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What does Aina do on Saturday?” and “What do you do on Saturday?”  
**Pupils:** Answer the factual and personal questions.
