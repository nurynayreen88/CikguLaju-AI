# English Year 2 — Lesson 107: Speaking 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe three robot features and write two labels.
**Success criteria:** Three clear descriptions and two legible labels.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.100, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.100 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name robot parts.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Preview Student's Book p.100 and model descriptions and labels.
**Pupils:** Rehearse phrases.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide picture-based descriptions and individual labelling.
**Pupils:** Describe three features and write two labels.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three descriptions and two labels per pupil.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful description.
**Pupils:** Share one phrase.
