# English Year 2 — Lesson 107: Speaking 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask three basic questions about a partner's abilities and write two survey questions with their answers.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate spoken questions and two written question-and-answer pairs.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.100, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Original survey prompts:** 'Can you swim?' 'Can you jump?' 'Can you dance?' **Writing frame:** 'Can you ___?' 'Yes, I can.' / 'No, I cannot.' Pupils add at least one new question of their own. **Original robot card:** one round head, three eyes, two long arms. **Model:** It has a round head. It has three eyes. Its arms are long. **Writing labels:** head, arms.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.100 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name robot parts.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Preview Student's Book p.100 and model descriptions and labels.
**Pupils:** Rehearse phrases.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Model an ability survey; pupils ask three questions, write two question-and-answer pairs and create at least one new survey question.
**Pupils:** Describe three features and write two labels.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three basic spoken personal questions (SP 2.1.2) and two written question-and-answer pairs (SP 4.2.1). Note individual support required.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful description.
**Pupils:** Share one phrase.
