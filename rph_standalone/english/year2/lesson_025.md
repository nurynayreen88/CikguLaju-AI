# English Year 2 — Lesson 25: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 2 SOW audit. The rhyme, actions and listening assessment are original teacher-created materials, not Teacher's Book content.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform a four-line free-time rhyme with at least two meaningful gestures and identify three spoken activity words.  
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible lines, two matching gestures and three correct listening choices.  
**Materials:** Board; printed rhyme below; four cards for reading, cycling, swimming and drawing.

## Original rhyme — “Free-Time Days”
“Monday, Monday, read with me.  
Tuesday, Tuesday, draw a tree.  
Saturday, Saturday, ride my bike.  
Sunday, Sunday, swim — what fun I like!”

**Gestures:** Open an imaginary book, draw a tree in the air, hold bicycle handlebars, mime swimming.  
**Listening prompts:** Teacher says *read*, *ride*, *swim*, *draw* in random order. Pupils point to the correct card. **Answer key:** read—book; ride—bike; swim—swimming; draw—tree/pencil.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four activity cards and ask pupils to mime the activities.  
**Pupils:** Name each activity and copy its gesture.

## Activity 1 — Echo the rhyme (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original four-line rhyme with a steady beat and demonstrate gestures. Point to the day names and action words; invite line-by-line repetition.  
**Pupils:** Echo the lines and practise each action.

## Activity 2 — Rehearse and listen (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide pupils into four groups, each taking one line. Combine the lines for a whole-class performance, then have pairs practise the entire rhyme. Remove printed prompts gradually. Read the four listening prompts in mixed order and ask pupils to select cards. Invite pupils to replace an activity word with another familiar word and invent a gesture as an optional extension.  
**Pupils:** Perform, swap group roles and respond to the spoken words.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe each pupil performing the rhyme and using at least two matching gestures. Say three activity words in random order without showing written prompts; record each pupil's picture selections and any support required.  
**Pupils:** Perform and identify three spoken words independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils which activity they enjoy most and invite a final group recital.  
**Pupils:** Name a favourite activity and recite together.
