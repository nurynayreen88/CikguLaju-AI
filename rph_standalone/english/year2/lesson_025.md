# English Year 2 — Lesson 25: Language Arts

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 2 SOW audit. The rhyme, actions and listening assessment are original teacher-created materials, not Teacher's Book content.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils demonstrate enjoyment and understanding of a four-line rhyme through at least three matching non-verbal responses and identify the main idea of two supported spoken sentences.  
**Success criteria:** Three appropriate gestures or picture choices in response to the rhyme and two correct main-idea choices.  
**Materials:** Board; printed rhyme below; four cards for reading, cycling, swimming and drawing.

## Original rhyme — “Free-Time Days”
“Monday, Monday, read with me.  
Tuesday, Tuesday, draw a tree.  
Saturday, Saturday, ride my bike.  
Sunday, Sunday, swim — what fun I like!”

**Gestures:** Open an imaginary book, draw a tree in the air, hold bicycle handlebars, mime swimming.  
**Listening prompts:** Teacher says “On Tuesday, I draw a tree” and “On Sunday, I go swimming” without displaying text. Pupils choose the main-idea picture for each. **Answer key:** drawing a tree; swimming.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show four activity cards and ask pupils to mime the activities.  
**Pupils:** Name each activity and copy its gesture.

## Activity 1 — Echo the rhyme (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original four-line rhyme with a steady beat and demonstrate gestures. Point to the day names and action words; invite line-by-line repetition.  
**Pupils:** Listen for enjoyment, optionally echo lines, and respond with gestures to each activity.

## Activity 2 — Rehearse and listen (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Divide pupils into four groups, each taking one line. Read the rhyme again while pupils respond through gestures or picture choices; optional oral participation is encouraged but not required for the main assessment. Remove printed prompts gradually. Read the two complete listening sentences and ask pupils to select the matching main-idea pictures. Invite pupils to replace an activity word with another familiar word and invent a gesture as an optional extension.  
**Pupils:** Respond non-verbally to the rhyme and choose pictures for the two spoken sentences.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Observe at least three independent non-verbal responses to the rhyme. Read the two complete listening sentences without showing written prompts and record each pupil's main-idea picture choices and support needed.  
**Pupils:** Show three non-verbal responses and identify two spoken sentence main ideas through pictures.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils which activity they enjoy most and invite a final group recital.  
**Pupils:** Point to a favourite activity and optionally join the group recital.
