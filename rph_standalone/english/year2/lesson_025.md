# English Year 2 — Lesson 25: Language Arts 5

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils perform two lines of an original free-time rhyme and recognise two spoken activity words.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two expressive lines and two correct listening choices.
**Teaching materials:** pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (classified in prior Year 2 SOW audit). Detailed SOW task steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two free-time pictures and elicit two activity words.
**Pupils:** Name two activities.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created free-time rhyme with gestures.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide paired rehearsal and two spoken activity-word cues.
**Pupils:** Perform two lines and identify two words.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit or demonstrate individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful activity words.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
