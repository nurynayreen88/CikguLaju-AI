# English Year 2 — Lesson 12: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping; *Super Minds 1* Student's Book pp.62–63 (story “We're lost!”), Workbook p.62, Teacher's Book T62 (PDF page 136), visually checked. CD2 Track 27 (story) and Track 28 (Workbook listening).  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify three key events or speakers from a spoken picture story and match three short spoken utterances to the correct character or picture.  
**Success criteria:** Three accurate responses based on the story; three accurate speaker/picture matches from spoken clues.  
**Materials:** SB pp.62–63, WB p.62, CD2 Tracks 27–28 (if available), story frame cards, board.  
**Core story language:** “Where's the lake?”, “We're lost”, “I've got an idea”, “Come with me”, “Thank you very much”, “Are you OK?”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the opening story picture on SB p.62. Ask “Where are the children?” and “Are they lost?” Introduce the words *lake* and *lost* using the illustration.  
**Pupils:** Predict what happens and repeat the key words.

## Activity 1 — Listen for the story (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show SB pp.62–63. Play CD2 Track 27 or read a concise, teacher-adapted sequence based on the printed frames: children are lost in the woods; Whisper asks where the lake is; the rabbit says “Come with me”; the children find the lake; Whisper asks “Are you OK, rabbit?” Pause at selected frames to ask “Who is speaking?” and “Who helps the children?”  
**Pupils:** Follow the frames in order, point to the speaker and identify the rabbit as the helper.

## Activity 2 — Guided listening and matching (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the story in short sections, pausing after frames to elicit “Where's the lake?”, “I've got an idea” and “Come with me.” Direct pupils to WB p.62 Activity 1 and play CD2 Track 28, or read the three documented cues: (1) Misty: “Where's the lake?” (2) Whisper: “I've got an idea.” (3) Rabbit: “Come with me.” Guide pupils to tick the corresponding picture, then complete WB p.62 Activity 2 (circle story words) and, if time allows, Activity 3 (write short dialogue sentences).  
**Pupils:** Listen and select pictures independently, compare in pairs, then identify the printed words and write short answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Individually read three short lines in random order: “We're lost”, “Come with me”, “Are you OK, rabbit?” Ask pupils to point to the matching story frame or character. Record three independent responses; do not score copied answers as listening evidence.  
**Pupils:** Listen without looking at a written script and identify the relevant frame or speaker.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask “What should we do when we are lost?” and highlight asking for help as the story value. Invite pupils to say “Can you help me, please?” as an additional teacher-created useful expression.  
**Pupils:** Respond orally and practise asking for help politely.
