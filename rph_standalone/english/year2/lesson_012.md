# English Year 2 — Lesson 12: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify three spoken free-time details from a teacher-read description and select matching pictures.
**Success criteria (draft):** Three accurate picture selections.
**Materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.62, topic pictures, word cards and exercise books.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two pictures related to Free Time and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name pictures and answer one question.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read a short description twice and model how to listen for one key word.
**Pupils:** Observe and give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils match three spoken details to pictures and check in pairs. Provide feedback and support.
**Pupils:** Practise and share answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two key words and invite a final example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned response.
