# English Year 2 — Lesson 140: Language Arts 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 5.3 / 5.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a four-line beach dialogue and identify four details heard in a partner performance.
**Success criteria:** four coherent dialogue lines and four accurate listening responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created short beach dialogue.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide paired dialogue creation and four listening-detail questions.
**Pupils:** Perform four lines and respond to four details.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
