# English Year 2 — Lesson 98: Reading 20

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify three main ideas from simple printed sentences and two details from supported spoken sentences.
**Success criteria:** three correct sentence main ideas and two correct listening-detail answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book pp.98–99, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original sentence cards:** 'The robot and its friends carry books together.' (teamwork); 'The robot moves on four wheels.' (movement); 'The robot helps the librarian.' (helping). **Spoken questions:** 'The robot has four wheels.' (How many? four); 'The robot works in a library.' (Where? library).
**Original alternative passage:** “Milo is a small robot in the school library. It has a square head, two orange eyes and four wheels. Milo can carry books. Every morning it helps the librarian.” **Five details/key:** location—school library; head—square; eyes—two orange; wheels—four; task—carry books. **Questions/key:** “Where is Milo?”—in the school library. “Who does Milo help?”—the librarian.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book pp.98–99 (Year 2 SOW audit); exact Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show the page illustrations and invite predictions.
**Pupils:** Predict the text topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Read the original alternative passage and demonstrate underlining *four wheels* as textual evidence.
**Pupils:** Point to printed evidence.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils review the SB picture story on pp.98–99, identify the main ideas of three original sentence cards, and answer two spoken-detail questions. The alternative passage is supplementary, not a transcription.
**Pupils:** Locate details, underline evidence and answer questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three sentence main ideas (SP 3.2.1) and two supported spoken-detail answers (SP 1.2.2).
**Pupils:** Submit individual reading evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review how to locate evidence in a text.
**Pupils:** Share one fact found.
