# English Year 2 — Lesson 50: Language Arts 10

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 5.1 / 5.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK. All scripts and cards below are teacher-created, not textbook quotations.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils show appreciation of an animal-habitat rhyme through gestures and understand the sequence of a short spoken animal narrative.  
**Success criteria:** Two appropriate non-verbal responses to the rhyme and three correctly sequenced narrative events.  
**Materials:** Board, room picture cards, word cards and resources below.

**Original habitat narrative:** 'First, a penguin walks on the snow. Next, it sees a seal near the water. Finally, it swims home.' **Sequence key:** walks on snow → sees seal → swims home.  
**Original habitat rhyme:** 'The penguin walks on snowy ground. The parrot flies and looks around. The camel crosses desert sand. The shark swims in the ocean grand.'  
**Original rhyme:** “Up the stairs, step by step. / In my room, I take a rest. / Down the stairs, here I go. / In the kitchen, food smells good.”
**Actions:** climb, sleep, descend, sniff. **Listening cues/key:** “Point to the stairs.” — stairs; “Point to the kitchen.” — kitchen.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two room pictures and elicit room and object words.  
**Pupils:** Name rooms and objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the habitat rhyme with non-verbal gestures, then read the short animal narrative and demonstrate its first event.  
**Pupils:** Follow and practise one example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs respond to the habitat rhyme using gestures, then listen to the short animal narrative and arrange three event pictures in order.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task in pairs, then repeat individually.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess four lines, two gestures and two independent listening choices. Record accuracy and any prompting.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite one new word and a useful sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share their learning.

**PBD:** Record two appropriate gestures as evidence for SP 5.1.1 and a three-event listening sequence for SP 1.2.3.
