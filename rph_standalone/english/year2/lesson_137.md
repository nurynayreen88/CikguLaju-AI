# English Year 2 — Lesson 137: Listening 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 1.1 / 1.1.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify four of five spoken beach words and match four printed labels to pictures.
**Success criteria:** four correct listening choices and four accurate label matches.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.111, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original picture cards:** A sun, B sand, C sea, D shell, E bucket. **Teacher-read order/key:** shell-D, sea-C, bucket-E, sun-A, sand-B. **Four label matches:** sun-A, sand-B, shell-D, bucket-E.

**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.111 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model recognising spoken beach words with picture cues.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Use page 111 as visual context for five teacher-created spoken cues and four printed labels.
**Pupils:** Listen, choose pictures and match labels.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
