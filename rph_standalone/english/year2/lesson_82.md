# English Year 2 — Lesson 82: Speaking 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 2.1 / 2.1.5  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 2.3 / 2.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior mapping; original teacher-created resources below.  
**Objective:** Pupils describe three clothing items and contribute two relevant conversation turns.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the numerical targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Outfit card A:** red shirt, blue shorts, white shoes. **Outfit card B:** green dress, yellow hat, black shoes.
**Original dialogue:** “What are you wearing?” “I'm wearing a red shirt and blue shorts.” “What colour are your shoes?” “They are white.”
**Useful turn-taking phrases:** “Your turn.” “Thank you.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display clothing cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Act out the original dialogue using card A, modelling question intonation and turn-taking.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs choose different cards, describe three items, ask and answer two questions, then swap roles.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve responses with feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen for three intelligible descriptions and two meaningful conversational turns. Note whether prompting was required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one target word and ask for a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
