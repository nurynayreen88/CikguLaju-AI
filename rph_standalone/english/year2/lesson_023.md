# English Year 2 — Lesson 23: Listening 5

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils identify three details from a teacher-read free-time description.
**Success criteria (draft):** Three correct picture or word selections.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.68, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.68 (page mapped in prior Year 2 SOW audit). Exact Teacher's Book steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two free-time pictures and elicit two activity words.
**Pupils:** Name two activities.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Read a teacher-created description twice and model listening for one detail; use mapped page 68 only as a resource.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Read three clues and guide individual selections.
**Pupils:** Identify three spoken details.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit or demonstrate individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful activity words.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
