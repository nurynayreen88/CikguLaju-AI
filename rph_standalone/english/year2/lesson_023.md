# English Year 2 — Lesson 23: Listening 5

**Unit:** Free Time — Do that!  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final SOW check pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; final SOW check pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Verified references:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.68 and Teacher's Book T68 (scanned TB PDF page index 147). The page uses CD2 Track 34 for a six-action piano-and-cat sequence, followed by sentence ordering and partner instructions.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow four spoken instructions with actions and sequence six pictured story actions.  
**Success criteria:** Four appropriate physical responses and at least five of six pictures placed in the correct sequence.  
**Materials:** SB p.68, board and piano/cat story pictures on the page. CD2 Track 34 if available. For an audio-free classroom, teacher reads the original teacher-written retelling below; it is **not** the CD transcript.

**Teacher-created fallback instructions:** “Sit at the piano. Start playing. Cover your ears. Open the piano. Look inside. Put the cat on the floor.”  
**Picture sequence key (TB T68):** 3, 5, 6, 4, 1, 2 (for the six displayed pictures in reading order).  
**Sentence ordering key (TB T68, SB Activity 2):** 3, 2, 1, 6, 4, 5 (for the six printed sentences in reading order).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime “open your book”, “close your book” and “sit down” without speaking, then say each instruction and ask pupils to act.  
**Pupils:** Listen and respond with actions.

## Activity 1 — Listen and act (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce *piano, cat, cover your ears* using SB p.68 pictures. Play CD2 Track 34 and act out the six-step story, or read the clearly labelled teacher-created fallback instructions while pointing to the pictures. Repeat with pupils acting.  
**Pupils:** Follow the spoken instructions and act out the sequence.

## Activity 2 — Sequence and direct (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to number the six SB p.68 pictures in story order and compare in pairs. Guide them to order the six printed sentences in Activity 2, then practise giving instructions to a partner in Activity 3. Check with the two verified keys above.  
**Pupils:** Sequence pictures and sentences, then give and follow instructions with a partner.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Say four instructions in a new order and record each pupil's independent physical response. Check the six-picture sequence separately; note whether pupils needed gestures or verbal repetition.  
**Pupils:** Respond to four spoken instructions and submit the picture order.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to demonstrate two instructions and ask why order matters in a story.  
**Pupils:** Give and follow instructions and recall the story sequence.
