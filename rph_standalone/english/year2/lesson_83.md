# English Year 2 — Lesson 83: Reading 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW check pending):** 3.3 / 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior mapping; original teacher-created resources below.  
**Objective:** Pupils locate four of five explicit details and look up one word.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the numerical targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original paragraph:** “Lily is getting ready for a picnic. She wears a pink shirt and blue jeans. Her shoes are white. She carries a yellow raincoat in her bag because the sky is cloudy.”
**Five details/key:** activity—picnic; shirt—pink; jeans—blue; shoes—white; raincoat—yellow. **Reference task:** Find *raincoat* in a picture dictionary or labelled word wall and point to its picture.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display clothing cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the paragraph aloud, underline *pink shirt* and demonstrate finding *raincoat* in a picture dictionary.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils read, underline five details, complete five short responses and check *raincoat* in the reference resource.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve responses with feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check five independent details and observe a successful picture-dictionary lookup. Note whether prompting was required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review one target word and ask for a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
