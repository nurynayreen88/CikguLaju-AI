# English Year 2 — Lesson 74: Writing 15

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK. All example scripts and passages below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Learning objective:** Pupils write four clothing words and two meaningful sentences.  
**Success criteria:** Achieve the measurable targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Word bank:** shirt, skirt, shoes, socks, hat, dress, jacket, trousers. **Picture A:** red hat, blue shirt, black shoes. **Model:** “I have a red hat. My shoes are black.” **Frames:** “I have a ____ ____.” “My ____ are ____.” **Checklist:** legibility, spaces, capitals, full stops.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two outfit pictures and elicit familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name items and colours.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model writing four words legibly and forming two sentences from picture A.  
**Pupils:** Follow the model and practise one example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils label four items in a different outfit and write two sentences; partners use the checklist and pupils revise.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity and improve responses after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect four words and two sentences; check legibility, meaning and punctuation. Record independent performance and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two words and ask for a relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share a word and sentence.
