# English Year 2 — Lesson 71: Listening 15

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 1.1 / 1.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; official SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** SB p.87; TB steps not verified. The scripts, passages and examples below are original teacher-created resources, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise and reproduce initial sounds in five familiar clothing words (main SP 1.1.1) and match four printed words to pictures (complementary SP 3.1.4).  
**Success criteria:** Independently identify and produce initial sounds in at least four of five words and match at least four printed words to pictures.  
**Materials:** Board, clothing picture/word cards, exercise books and the printable resources below.

**Picture/word cards:** shirt, skirt, shoes, socks, hat. **Original listening order:** “socks, shirt, hat, shoes, skirt”. **Key:** socks→D, shirt→A, hat→E, shoes→C, skirt→B (card labels A shirt, B skirt, C shoes, D socks, E hat). **Initial-sound key:** socks /s/, shirt /sh/, hat /h/, shoes /sh/, skirt /s/. The phonics examples are teacher-created and require textbook verification.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display two clothing pictures, elicit known words and ask a prediction.  
**Pupils:** Name items and colours.

## Activity 1 — Teacher modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pronounce *shoes* and *socks* slowly, contrast the initial sounds and demonstrate a card choice.  
**Pupils:** Follow and practise one example with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the five words in mixed order twice. Pupils identify and reproduce each initial sound before choosing pictures and matching four printed word cards. Give targeted modelling for pupils who confuse the initial sounds.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity, discuss answers and correct errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess each pupil's identification and reproduction of initial sounds for five words (score 0–5), separately from four printed-word matches (score 0–4). Record accuracy and assistance required; do not count picture matching alone as phoneme evidence.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate their skills individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two target words and ask for a relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
