# English Year 2 — Lesson 85: Language Arts 17

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform three of four lines of a teacher-created clothing rhyme with intelligible pronunciation, rhythm and intonation, and describe two clothing objects.
**Success criteria:** three intelligible lines with suitable rhythm and intonation, and two clear object descriptions.
**Teaching materials:** Teacher-created four-line verse below, clothing picture cards, board and pupil response slips.

**Original teacher-created verse:**
My red hat sits upon my head.
My blue shoes take me where I tread.
My yellow shirt is bright and new.
I smile and wave hello to you.

**Description frames:** 'The hat is red.' 'The shoes are blue.' **Performance cues:** clap a steady beat, stress colour words, and use rising or falling intonation appropriately.
**Response prompts:** “Which line do you like best?” “Which colour or picture do you like, and why?” **Example answers:** “I like the red hat line.” “I like blue shoes because blue is my favourite colour.” These are original examples, not textbook extracts.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display colourful outfit illustrations and invite a prediction.
**Pupils:** Suggest familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Perform the complete original four-line verse above with gestures (point to hat, shoes, shirt and wave). Model a personal response using the supplied prompts and examples.
**Pupils:** Echo lines and practise expression.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Rehearse the four-line rhyme with clapping and gestures. Each pupil describes two clothing objects using suitable words; preference questions are optional extension.
**Pupils:** Perform and share two preferences.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Listen to at least three intelligible verse lines per pupil (0–3) and record two individual clothing-object descriptions (0–2) separately, with pronunciation, rhythm and intonation noted. Note assistance required; group performance alone is insufficient evidence.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate individual participation.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Invite a short final performance.
**Pupils:** Share one favourite phrase.
