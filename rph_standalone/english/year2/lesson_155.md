# English Year 2 — Lesson 155: Language Arts 31

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 5.1 / 5.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.3 / 2.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils perform four lines of a beach dialogue with clear expression.
**Success criteria:** four intelligible lines with suitable expression.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original four-line dialogue:** A: Look at the blue sea! B: I can see a small boat. A: Shall we build a sandcastle? B: Yes, let's build one! **Performance cues:** pause at punctuation, speak clearly, show excitement on final line.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model the four-line teacher-created dialogue with clear pauses, intonation and expressive delivery.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Assign roles A and B, guide paired rehearsal, then have pupils exchange roles so each pupil speaks all four lines.
**Pupils:** Perform four dialogue lines.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Observe each pupil delivering four intelligible lines with suitable expression and record individual performance evidence.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
