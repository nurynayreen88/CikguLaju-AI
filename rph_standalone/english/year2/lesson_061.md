# English Year 2 — Lesson 61: Listening 13

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.3 / 1.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify at least four of five clothing-related details from a short spoken description and respond to two simple follow-up prompts.
**Success criteria:** 4/5 correct listening choices and two relevant responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two outfit pictures and elicit known clothing words.
**Pupils:** Predict what the speaker may describe.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the listening 13 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Listen to a sample description and select the matching picture.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Read two short teacher-created outfit descriptions twice; present five picture-choice questions and two follow-up prompts.
**Pupils:** Listen independently, choose five answers and respond to two prompts.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Mark five individual listening choices and two responses; record results and reteach misunderstood items.
**Pupils:** Submit answers and give individual responses.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one listening strategy and a familiar clothing word.
**Pupils:** Share one word heard.
