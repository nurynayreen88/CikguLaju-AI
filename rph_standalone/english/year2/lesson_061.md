# English Year 2 — Lesson 61: Listening 13

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.5  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.3 / 1.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in prior mapping; detailed SOW task sequence unverified. The material below is original teacher-created content.  
**Objective:** Pupils identify four of five outfit details and answer two oral questions.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the numerical targets in the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and the resources below.

**Original scripts:** A: “Aina is wearing a red shirt, blue trousers and white shoes. She has a yellow hat.” B: “Ben is wearing a green shirt, black trousers and brown shoes. He has no hat.”
**Five prompts/key:** A's shirt—red; A's trousers—blue; A's shoes—white; B's shirt—green; B's hat—none. **Oral prompts:** “Who wears white shoes?”—Aina; “Who wears black trousers?”—Ben.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display two clothing pictures and elicit familiar item and colour words.  
**Pupils:** Name two items and make a prediction.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read A twice, model listening for *red shirt* and selecting a red shirt card.  
**Pupils:** Follow and practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read both scripts twice; pupils answer five picture/colour choices, then ask the two follow-up questions.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task, compare evidence and improve responses.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read once more and check five independent choices plus two spoken answers. Record whether prompts were needed.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the task individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two clothing words and invite one accurate example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and a short response.
