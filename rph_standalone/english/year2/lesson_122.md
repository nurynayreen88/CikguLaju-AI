# English Year 2 — Lesson 122: Speaking 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe three robot features orally and write two short object descriptions.
**Success criteria:** three clear oral object descriptions and two accurate written descriptions.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original teacher-created supplementary resources (not verified textbook extracts):** **Picture dictation task:** Partner A describes a robot using three phrases; Partner B draws it and writes two descriptive sentences, then partners exchange roles. **Robot card:** square head, three green eyes, two arms. **Three questions/key:** What shape is its head? Square. How many eyes? Three. What colour are its eyes? Green. **Writing models:** “It has a square head. Its eyes are green.”
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a robot-themed oral exchange and one short written description.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pairs conduct picture dictation using three spoken object descriptions, then write two short descriptions and swap roles.
**Pupils:** Answer three questions and write two descriptions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three oral object descriptions (SP 2.1.5) and two written descriptions (SP 4.2.4). Record support required for individual PBD.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful robot phrase.
**Pupils:** Share one phrase.
