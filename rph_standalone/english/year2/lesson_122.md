# English Year 2 — Lesson 122: Speaking 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils respond to three robot questions and write two short descriptions.
**Success criteria:** three relevant oral answers and two understandable descriptions.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original teacher-created supplementary resources (not verified textbook extracts):** **Robot card:** square head, three green eyes, two arms. **Three questions/key:** What shape is its head? Square. How many eyes? Three. What colour are its eyes? Green. **Writing models:** “It has a square head. Its eyes are green.”
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a robot-themed oral exchange and one short written description.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pairs ask and answer all three questions, then independently write two descriptions.
**Pupils:** Answer three questions and write two descriptions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three relevant spoken answers and two meaningful sentences. Record support required for individual PBD.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful robot phrase.
**Pupils:** Share one phrase.
