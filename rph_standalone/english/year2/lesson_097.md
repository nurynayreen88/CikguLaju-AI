# English Year 2 — Lesson 97: Speaking 20

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe three robot features and ask two simple questions about a robot.
**Success criteria:** three clear descriptions and two relevant questions.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.97, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original robot card A:** round head, three eyes, two long arms. **Card B:** square head, one eye, four short arms. **Model:** “My robot has a round head. It has three eyes. Its arms are long.” **Question frames:** “How many eyes has it got?” “Is its head round?”
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.97 (Year 2 SOW audit); exact Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model three feature descriptions and the two question forms using robot A.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one model response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Partners exchange three descriptions, ask both questions and identify A or B.
**Pupils:** Describe three features and ask two questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three descriptions and two relevant questions per pupil.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot-related expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
