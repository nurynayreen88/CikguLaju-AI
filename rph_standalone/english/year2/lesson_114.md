# English Year 2 — Lesson 114: Writing 23

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils plan, draft and write three simple survey-report sentences using capitals and full stops correctly.
**Success criteria:** three planned and drafted sentences with correct sentence capitals and full stops.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards, individual PBD record and original resources below.

**Original mini-survey results:** Aina can swim and jump; Ben can run but cannot swim; Mei can dance. **Report models:** 'Aina can swim.' 'Ben can run.' 'Mei can dance.' **Checklist:** capital letter at start, full stop at end. **Original robot sketch:** round head, two blue eyes, long arms, four wheels. **Copying words:** head, eyes, arms, wheels. **Model sentences:** “It has a round head. Its eyes are blue. It has four wheels.”
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model letter formation, spacing and a short descriptive sentence.
**Pupils:** Practise one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils plan three survey-report ideas, draft the sentences and revise capitals and full stops before final writing.
**Pupils:** Write four words and three sentences.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three planned/drafted/final sentences (SP 4.3.3) and capitals/full stops in each (SP 4.3.1). Note independent performance and support needed.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot expression.
**Pupils:** Share one new word or phrase.
