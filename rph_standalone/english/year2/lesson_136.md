# English Year 2 — Lesson 136: Reading 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five details in the printed pages 110–111 and read two short lines aloud.
**Success criteria:** four accurate detail responses and two intelligible lines.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book pp.110–111, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book pp.110–111 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher:** Introduce At the beach with a picture and two questions.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher:** Model the reading 28 task and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher:** Organise pair or individual practice and provide feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete the task and share one response.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher:** Observe one individual response and record feedback.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure
**Teacher:** Recap the lesson and invite two pupils to demonstrate.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
