# English Year 2 — Lesson 136: Reading 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five details in the printed pages 110–111 and read two short lines aloud.
**Success criteria:** four accurate detail responses and two intelligible lines.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book pp.110–111, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book pp.110–111 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model locating a detail in the actual printed pages 110–111.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide five page-grounded questions and supported oral reading.
**Pupils:** Locate four details and read two lines.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
