# English Year 2 — Lesson 136: Reading 28

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate four of five details in the original supplementary Amir passage and read two short lines aloud.
**Success criteria:** four accurate detail responses and two intelligible lines.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book pp.110–111, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original supplementary passage (not SB pp.110–111):** Amir is on the beach with Mum. He sees a small crab, two shells and a blue boat. He puts the shells in his yellow bucket. **Five facts/key:** Amir; Mum; small crab; blue boat; yellow bucket. **Reading lines:** He sees a small crab. He puts the shells in his yellow bucket.

**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book pp.110–111 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a beach illustration and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Display the complete original Amir passage above and model underlining “small crab” as evidence. Do not imply the passage is printed on Student's Book pp.110–111.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give each pupil the original Amir passage. Ask five questions with the supplied key: Who is at the beach? (Amir); Who is with him? (Mum); What animal does he see? (a small crab); What colour is the boat? (blue); What colour is his bucket? (yellow). Pupils underline evidence, then read both specified lines aloud.
**Pupils:** Locate four details and read two lines.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score five independently located details (0–5) and two intelligible lines read aloud (0–2); record prompting and support separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
