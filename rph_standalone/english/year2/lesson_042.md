# English Year 2 — Lesson 42: Reading 9

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book SB p.76. Exact Teacher's Book procedure remains unverified. The scripts, cards and examples below are original teacher-created alternatives, not claimed to reproduce textbook activities.  
**Objective:** Pupils read five familiar house words and answer three text-supported questions.  
**Success criteria:** Independently complete all stated components.  
**Materials:** Board, room cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original short text:** “My house has a kitchen and a bedroom. There is a table in the kitchen. There is a bed in the bedroom. I read in my bedroom.” **Words:** house, kitchen, bedroom, table, bed. **Questions/key:** (1) Where is the table? — kitchen; (2) What is in the bedroom? — bed; (3) Where does the speaker read? — bedroom.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show room pictures and elicit two familiar house words.  
**Pupils:** Name rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original short text aloud and demonstrate finding *table* and its room.  
**Pupils:** Practise the model with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs underline five target words, answer three questions and point to the exact sentence supporting each answer.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity and explain their answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check individual recognition of five words and three independent text-supported answers. Note accuracy and prompts needed.  
**Pupils:** Respond independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share a sentence.
