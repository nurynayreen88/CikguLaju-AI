# English Year 2 — Lesson 42: Reading 9

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.1 / 3.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book SB p.76. Exact Teacher's Book procedure remains unverified. The scripts, cards and examples below are original teacher-created alternatives, not claimed to reproduce textbook activities.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of a short familiar house text (main 3.2.1) and recognise at least four familiar printed words using letter-sound knowledge (complementary 3.1.2).  
**Success criteria:** Individually choose the correct main-idea statement from three options, explain one supporting clue and recognise at least four of five printed words; record any support given.  
**Materials:** Board, room cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Additional teacher-created text (not a textbook extract):** “My house has a kitchen and a bedroom. There is a table in the kitchen. There is a bed in the bedroom. I read in my bedroom.” **Words:** house, kitchen, bedroom, table, bed. **Main-idea question:** What is the text mainly about? A. Rooms in my house; B. My school; C. My favourite food. **Key:** A. **Supporting-detail questions/key:** (1) Where is the table? — kitchen; (2) What is in the bedroom? — bed; (3) Where does the speaker read? — bedroom.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show room pictures and elicit two familiar house words.  
**Pupils:** Name rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original short text aloud; contrast the overall topic (*rooms in my house*) with a small detail (*table*). Model selecting answer A and sound out *bed* as a familiar word.  
**Pupils:** Practise the model with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to select the main-idea option first and justify it using a clue from the text. Then guide them to underline five target words and answer the three supporting-detail questions. Model blending unfamiliar letters without supplying every answer.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity and explain their answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give each pupil the three main-idea options in a new order and ask for one supporting clue. Check recognition of five printed words individually; record main idea (1/1), clue (1/1), word recognition (0–5) and prompts needed.  
**Pupils:** Respond independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share a sentence.
