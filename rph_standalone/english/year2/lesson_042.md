# English Year 2 — Lesson 42: Reading 9

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.1 / 3.1.2
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils read four familiar house words and identify two details in an illustrated text.
**Success criteria (draft):** four recognised words and two text-supported answers.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.76, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.76 (Year 2 SOW mapping audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model reading four printed house words and finding one clue; page 76 is a mapped resource only.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide word recognition and two reading questions.
**Pupils:** Read four words and answer two text questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
