# English Year 2 — Lesson 3: Speaking

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.1  
**Sources:** Year 2 SOW mapping (SB p.59); *Super Minds 1* Teacher's Book printed p.T59 (PDF page 129), visually checked; Student's Book p.59; Workbook p.59.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can (1) say at least three intelligible first-person sentences about activities on days of the week and (2) understand at least three short spoken descriptions of free-time activities.  
**Success criteria:** Three intelligible “I ... on [day]” sentences; three correct matches of spoken activities to people or pictures.  
**Materials:** SB p.59, WB p.59, Unit 5 stickers (or five labelled picture cards), seven day flashcards, CD2 Tracks 20–22 or teacher-read equivalents, board.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the Teacher's Book clapping game: clap twice, say “Tuesday”; invite pupils to respond with the next day, and continue until Monday.  
**Pupils:** Clap, listen and name days of the week in sequence.

## Activity 1 — Present and listen (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write the days of the week and model an activity under each, e.g. “On Mondays, I go swimming.” Mime each activity. Direct pupils to SB p.59 Activity 1. Explain that they must listen and place the five Unit 5 stickers under Sandra, Pat, Maria, Oliver and Bill. Play CD2 Track 20 twice, or read the teacher script below.  
**Pupils:** Repeat the model sentences; listen and match each person to an activity sticker/card, then compare in pairs.

**Fallback listening script (adapted from the Teacher's Book recording):**
- Sandra: “I ride my bike on Sundays.”
- Pat: “I play the piano on Mondays and Fridays.”
- Maria: “I play football on Thursdays.”
- Oliver: “I ride my pony on Tuesdays and Saturdays.”
- Bill: “I go swimming on Wednesdays and Fridays.”

## Activity 2 — Speak and practise (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use SB p.59 Activity 2, CD2 Track 21: model “I go swimming on Mondays” and “I play football on Saturdays.” Have pupils repeat and note plural day forms. In pairs, guide SB Activity 3: “I play football on Fridays. What about you?” / “I go swimming on Fridays.” Rotate partners. Use WB p.59 Activity 1 with CD2 Track 22 (listen and tick activities) and Activity 2 (reorder words into sentences), as time permits.  
**Pupils:** Say and practise personal sentences about days and activities, listen to classmates and respond, then complete the short Workbook tasks.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Independently prompt pupils to describe three activities on three days. Read three short activity descriptions or play excerpts of CD2 Track 22 and ask pupils to select the correct picture/person. Record speaking and listening evidence separately, with support noted.  
**Pupils:** Give three personal sentences and identify three spoken activity cues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime one activity and ask pupils to guess, then ask a pupil to use it in a sentence with a day of the week.  
**Pupils:** Guess and produce a full sentence.
