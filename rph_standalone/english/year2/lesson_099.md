# English Year 2 — Lesson 99: Writing 20

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three short robot descriptions and correct two spelling or punctuation errors.
**Success criteria:** Three meaningful sentences and two appropriate corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original picture prompt:** a robot with a square head, three blue eyes and two long arms. **Model:** “My robot has a square head. It has three blue eyes. Its arms are long.” **Editing/key:** “my robot has a square head.” → “My robot has a square head.”; “It has thre eyes.” → “It has three eyes.”
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit descriptive words.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model writing the three sentences and correcting the capital-letter and spelling errors.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one sentence and locate an error.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils draw a robot, write three sentences and make two visible edits using a checklist.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check three sentences and two independently corrected errors.
**Pupils:** Submit individual writing evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review punctuation and spacing.
**Pupils:** Read one corrected sentence.
