# English Year 2 — Lesson 124: Writing 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three legible robot sentences and correct two spacing or letter-formation errors.
**Success criteria:** Three legible sentences and two appropriate corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit descriptive words.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model sentence formation, letter shapes and spacing.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one short sentence.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide independent writing and use an individual editing checklist.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three sentences and two corrections per pupil.
**Pupils:** Submit individual writing evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review clear spacing and letter formation.
**Pupils:** Read one sentence.
