# English Year 2 — Lesson 124: Writing 25

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils plan, draft and write three simple robot sentences with correct capitals and full stops.
**Success criteria:** three planned and revised sentences with accurate sentence capitals and full stops.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Original teacher-created supplementary resource:** **Writing plan:** 1 head shape; 2 eye colour; 3 arm length. **Revision checklist:** capital letter at the beginning and full stop at the end of every sentence. **Original picture:** round head, one blue eye, two short arms. **Three model sentences:** It has a round head. It has one blue eye. Its arms are short. **Editing key:** It hasa round head. -> It has a round head.; It has oneblue eye. -> It has one blue eye.
**Independent editing items (different from the model examples):** 1. “Myrobot has short arms.” 2. “It has ablue eye.” **Independent key:** 1. “My robot has short arms.” 2. “It has a blue eye.” Pupils insert missing spaces and mark each correction. These two items are reserved for individual PBD; the two model examples above are guided practice only.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and elicit descriptive words.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model planning three ideas, drafting sentences and checking capitals and full stops.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one short sentence.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils plan, draft, revise and write three sentences about the original robot picture. Model the two guided spacing examples, then give the two different independent editing items without displaying the key. Pupils mark the inserted spaces.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score three legible, understandable sentences (0–3) and the two independent spacing corrections (0–2) against the supplied key. Do not count corrections copied from the modelled examples; record individual support separately.
**Pupils:** Submit individual writing evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review clear spacing and letter formation.
**Pupils:** Read one sentence.
