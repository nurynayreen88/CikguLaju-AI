# English Year 2 — Lesson 86: Listening 18

**Unit:** Get dressed!
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.4
**Learning objectives:** Pupils understand four details from a very short supported spoken narrative and follow two short classroom instructions.
**Success criteria:** 4/5 accurate details and two appropriate actions.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.92, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original teacher-created narrative (fallback only):** 'A boy puts on his coat. The wind blows. His hat falls. His friend catches the hat. They smile.' **Questions:** Who puts on a coat? (a boy); What blows? (the wind); What falls? (his hat); Who catches it? (his friend). **Instructions:** 'Turn around.' 'Catch the ball.' Model safely with a soft ball.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.92 (Year 2 SOW audit); exact Teacher's Book procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two outfit pictures and elicit clothing words.
**Pupils:** Name two clothing items.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Preview Student's Book p.92 and model listening for a named clothing detail.
**Pupils:** Listen and point to the correct picture.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Use page-linked audio if available or a clearly labelled teacher-created listening sequence; tell the short supported narrative twice, ask four story-detail questions and give two oral instructions.
**Pupils:** Listen twice, select five answers and act on two instructions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record four supported narrative answers (SP 1.2.3) and two instruction-following actions (SP 1.2.4).
**Pupils:** Give individual listening evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful listening clue.
**Pupils:** Name a word heard.
