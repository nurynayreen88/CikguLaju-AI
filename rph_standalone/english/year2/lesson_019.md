# English Year 2 — Lesson 19: Writing

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK according to existing Year 2 mapping. All writing prompts and examples below are teacher-created.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can write a four-sentence description of their free-time activities using capital letters, word spacing and full stops, and check and improve their writing using a simple checklist.  
**Success criteria:** Four meaningful sentences with at least three correctly punctuated sentences; at least two independently identified or corrected errors.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, four day cards (Monday, Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday), activity word bank and checklist below.

**Word bank:** read a book; ride my bike; play football; draw pictures; go swimming; watch TV; play computer games.  
**Writing frame:** “On Monday, I ____.” / “On Wednesday, I ____.” / “On Saturday, I ____.” / “On Sunday, I ____.”  
**Model paragraph:** “On Monday, I read a book. On Wednesday, I ride my bike. On Saturday, I play football. On Sunday, I draw pictures.”  
**Self-check:** (1) Each sentence begins with a capital letter. (2) Day names begin with capital letters. (3) Words have spaces. (4) Every sentence ends with a full stop. (5) My sentences make sense.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show two day cards and mime reading and cycling. Ask pupils to say “On Monday, I read a book” and “On Wednesday, I ride my bike.”  
**Pupils:** Name the activities and form one oral sentence.

## Activity 1 — Model a short paragraph (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the model paragraph. Read it aloud, circle the capital letters and underline the full stops. Deliberately write “on saturday i play football” and model correcting it to “On Saturday, I play football.”  
**Pupils:** Identify the corrections and practise the writing frame orally.

## Activity 2 — Plan, write and revise (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to choose four activities and pair them with the four day cards. Ask pupils to draft four sentences using the word bank as needed. Then direct them to apply the five-point checklist and correct at least two errors or improve two words. Support emerging writers with sentence frames; invite confident writers to add a reason using “because”.  
**Pupils:** Plan, write four sentences, check their own work and revise at least two items.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect the revised paragraph and checklist. Independently assess sentence meaning, capitals, spaces and full stops. Compare draft and final version to verify two actual corrections; where the first draft is already correct, accept two explained checks rather than inventing errors.  
**Pupils:** Submit the draft, final paragraph and checked checklist.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pupils to read a sentence and explain one improvement they made.  
**Pupils:** Share a sentence and one proofreading strategy.
