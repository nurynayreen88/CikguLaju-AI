# English Year 2 — Lesson 19: Writing 4

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.3
**Complementary SK/SP (existing SOW mapping; verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Duration (draft):** 60 minutes
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils write two punctuated sentences about free-time activities and use a self-check checklist.
**Success criteria (draft):** Two understandable sentences with capitals, spacing and full stops.
**Materials:** Topic picture cards, word cards, board and pupils' exercise books.
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task in the prior Year 2 audit; detailed SOW procedure remains unverified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display pictures connected to Free Time and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and respond to one short question.

## Activity 1 — Model (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a free-time sentence with capital, spacing and full stop.
**Pupils:** Identify the activity word and notice the sentence punctuation.

## Activity 2 — Practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide two picture-based sentences and an independent punctuation checklist.
**Pupils:** Write two sentences and self-check punctuation.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Complete a short independent demonstration of the objective.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two useful words and summarise the learning.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word or response.
