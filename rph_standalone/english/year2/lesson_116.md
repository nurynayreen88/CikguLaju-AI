# English Year 2 — Lesson 116: Reading 24

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 2.2 / 2.2.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify four of five details in simple printed sentences and use two appropriate non-verbal responses during exchanges.
**Success criteria:** four correct reading details and two appropriate non-verbal interaction cues.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.103, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Non-verbal practice:** pupils maintain eye contact and nod to show understanding while discussing the quiz and skeleton pictures. **Original supplementary passage (teacher-created, not Student's Book text):** “Pip is a little robot in the school garden. It has a green body, two yellow eyes and three wheels. Every morning, Pip waters the flowers.” **Five detail questions/key:** What is its name?—Pip; Where is it?—school garden; What colour is its body?—green; How many eyes?—two; How many wheels?—three. **Two oral questions/key:** What does Pip water?—the flowers; When does Pip water them?—every morning.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.103 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified. The passage and answers above are independent teacher-created alternatives.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot illustration and elicit two observations.
**Pupils:** Name familiar robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Display the complete supplementary Pip passage above and model underlining “green body” as evidence; do not claim this passage appears on Student's Book p.103.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one model response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give pupils the printed Pip passage. Pupils read five quiz questions, identify supporting details, and practise nodding and eye contact while exchanging answers.
**Pupils:** Underline supporting details and give two oral answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Score five reading details (SP 3.2.2) and two non-verbal responses (SP 2.2.1). Score five individual detail answers (0–5) and two oral responses (0–2); record independent accuracy and any support.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot-related expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or phrase.
