# English Year 2 — Lesson 54: Writing 11

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior mapping. Materials below are original teacher-developed resources; detailed SOW activity verification pending.  
**Objective:** Pupils write three meaningful house sentences with clear word spacing.  
**Success criteria:** Meet each component of the objective independently.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, picture/word cards and the text below.

**Original room plan:** bedroom—bed and lamp; kitchen—table and two chairs; living room—sofa.
**Model:** “There is a bed in the bedroom. There are two chairs in the kitchen. My living room has a sofa.”
**Writing frames:** “There is a ____ in the ____.” “There are two ____ in the ____.”
**Editing checklist:** capitals, spaces, *is/are*, full stops.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display relevant picture cards and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name the pictures and words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Draw the room plan and model the three sentences, highlighting spaces and singular/plural language.  
**Pupils:** Follow the example and practise with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils draw a three-room plan, write three sentences, peer-check using the editing checklist and revise independently.  
**Pupils:** Complete the tasks and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect three sentences; record meaning, word spacing and correct punctuation. Note independent responses and support required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the success criteria individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and invite a relevant example.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and a short sentence.
