# English Year 2 — Lesson 92: Speaking 19

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 2.1 / 2.1.4
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask and answer three ability questions and identify two details from supported spoken sentences.
**Success criteria:** three ability exchanges and two correct listening-detail responses.
**Teaching materials:** Two original robot action cards and response checklist.

**Original cards:** Robot A can dance, clap and spin. Robot B can jump, wave and walk.
**Question models:** 'Can Robot A dance?' 'Yes, it can.' 'Can Robot B swim?' 'No, it cannot.' **Listening details:** 'Robot A can dance.' 'Robot B can jump.'
**Model language:** “My robot can dance. It can clap. It can spin.”
**Two oral instructions:** “Show me a wave.” “Point to the robot that can jump.”
**Answer key:** Pupils wave; pupils point to Robot B.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 SOW audit); detailed SOW procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a robot picture and invite actions.
**Pupils:** Suggest two possible movements.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Demonstrate dance, clap and spin with gestures, and model all three sentences from Robot A.
**Pupils:** Rehearse short phrases.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Give pairs different action cards. Pupils ask and answer three questions using can/cannot and identify two details from spoken robot sentences.
**Pupils:** Describe three actions and respond to two instructions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three ability exchanges (SP 2.1.4) and two listening-detail responses (SP 1.2.2).
**Pupils:** Demonstrate speaking individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review clear pronunciation and turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Share one action phrase.
