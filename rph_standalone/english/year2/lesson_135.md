# English Year 2 — Lesson 135: Language Arts 27

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 5.2 / 5.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a labelled beach collage and respond to three spoken cues.
**Success criteria:** one labelled collage and three appropriate listening responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two beach objects.

## Activity 1 — Model
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a teacher-created labelled beach collage.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide collage making and three spoken identification prompts.
**Pupils:** Make a labelled collage and respond to three prompts.

## Assessment / PBD
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
