# English Year 2 — Lesson 131: Listening 27

**Unit:** At the beach
**Main SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify four of five spoken beach details and write two short captions.
**Success criteria:** Four correct listening responses and two meaningful captions.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original listening script:** A girl is at the beach. She has a red bucket and a blue spade. She sees three shells near the water. The sun is hot. **Five details/key:** place beach; bucket red; spade blue; shells three; sun hot. **Caption models:** The bucket is red. There are three shells.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a beach picture and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Recall familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model listening for details in a short teacher-created beach description.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and respond.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Read the script twice, then elicit five listening details and two original captions.
**Pupils:** Respond to five listening prompts and write two captions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record five detail choices and two meaningful captions. Record individual support needed.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful beach expression.
**Pupils:** Share one thing learned.
