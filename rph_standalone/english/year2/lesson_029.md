# English Year 2 — Lesson 29: Writing 6

**Unit:** Free Time
**Main SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils write two sentences about free-time activities using spacing and basic punctuation.
**Success criteria (draft):** two understandable sentences with capital letters and full stops.
**Teaching materials:** pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (classified in prior Year 2 SOW audit). Detailed SOW task steps not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show two free time pictures and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a picture-based sentence and explain capital, spacing and full stop.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide two independently written sentences and self-checking.
**Pupils:** Write and check two sentences.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two target words and one useful example.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.
