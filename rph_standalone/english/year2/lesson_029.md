# English Year 2 — Lesson 29: Writing 6

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.3 / 4.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the prior Year 2 audit; the tasks and examples below are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** Pupils spell at least four familiar free-time words accurately in guided writing and use capital letters and full stops correctly in three sentences.  
**Success criteria:** At least four of five target words spelled accurately, plus three meaningful sentences with at least two correctly punctuated.  
**Materials:** Board, exercise books, word bank and editing checklist below.

**Word bank:** read a book; play football; ride my bike; draw pictures; go swimming.  
**Spelling check words:** read, play, bike, draw, swim. Teacher says each word without showing the word bank; pupils write independently.  
**Sentence frames:** “After school, I ____.” “On Saturday, I ____.” “On Sunday, I ____.”  
**Model paragraph:** “After school, I read a book. On Saturday, I play football. On Sunday, I draw pictures.”  
**Editing task:** Correct “on sunday i ride my bike” to “On Sunday, I ride my bike.”  
**Checklist:** Capital letter at the start; capital for day names; spaces between words; full stop at the end; sentence makes sense.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime football and reading and ask pupils to describe the actions.  
**Pupils:** Name two activities.

## Activity 1 — Model and edit (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the model paragraph and editing task. Underline the day names and circle the capitals and full stops. Demonstrate one correction aloud.  
**Pupils:** Identify punctuation and practise correcting the model sentence.

## Activity 2 — Write, check and revise (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model spelling practice with the five target words, then ask pupils to select three different activities and complete the sentence frames. Guide a partner checklist exchange, then ask pupils to revise their own work. Provide word cards for support and invite confident writers to add a fourth sentence.  
**Pupils:** Write three sentences, review the checklist and revise their work.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Dictate the five spelling-check words without displaying them. Collect the spelling check, draft, revised sentences and checklist. Record word spelling separately from capitals and full stops.  
**Pupils:** Write five dictated words, submit three sentences and explain one punctuation decision.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pupils to read a corrected sentence and name the rule they used.  
**Pupils:** Share one improvement.
