# English Year 2 — Lesson 86: Listening 18

**Unit:** Get dressed!  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.3  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 1.2 / 1.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** SB p.92; TB unverified. All scripts and tasks below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Objective:** Pupils identify four of five clothing details and follow two instructions.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the stated numerical targets independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original spoken clues:** “A red shirt.” “Two blue socks.” “A yellow hat.” “Black shoes.” “A green jacket.” **Five picture choices/key:** shirt red, socks blue, hat yellow, shoes black, jacket green. **Oral instructions:** “Point to the yellow hat.” “Touch the card with black shoes.”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a relevant picture and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name visible items and predict the topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model selecting the red shirt card after hearing the first clue.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the five original clues twice in mixed order; pupils select cards and follow two oral instructions.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check five independent listening choices and two correctly performed actions. Note independent accuracy and prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
