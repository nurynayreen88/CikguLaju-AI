# English Year 2 — Lesson 108: Writing 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three robot sentences using 'and' to connect words or phrases and apply capitals and full stops.
**Success criteria:** three appropriate conjunction uses and accurate capitals and full stops.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.101, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.

**Teacher-created standalone resource (not verified textbook content):** **Conjunction models:** 'It has two eyes and four wheels.' 'Its arms are long and red.' **Punctuation rule:** Begin each sentence with a capital and end with a full stop. **Original robot picture prompt:** square head, two yellow eyes, four wheels. **Models:** It has a square head. Its eyes are yellow. It has four wheels. **Editing key:** 'it has four wheels.' → 'It has four wheels.'; 'Its eyes is yellow.' → 'Its eyes are yellow.'
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.101 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name a robot feature.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model planning a short sentence and checking punctuation.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Pupils write three sentences joining words or phrases with 'and', then check capitals and full stops.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record three conjunction uses (SP 4.2.5) and capitals/full stops in three sentences (SP 4.3.1). Note individual support required.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
