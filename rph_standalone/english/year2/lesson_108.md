# English Year 2 — Lesson 108: Writing 22

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.3 / 4.3.1
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three robot sentences and correct two errors.
**Success criteria:** three meaningful sentences and two corrections.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.101, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.101 (Year 2 SOW audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name a robot feature.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model planning a short sentence and checking punctuation.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide three-sentence writing from a robot picture and two revisions.
**Pupils:** Write three sentences and correct two errors.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record individual evidence against the success criteria.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review a useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one word or sentence.
