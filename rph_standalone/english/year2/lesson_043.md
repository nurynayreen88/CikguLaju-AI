# English Year 2 — Lesson 43: Writing 9

**Unit:** The old house  
**Main SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.1  
**Complementary SK/SP (prior mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Mapped resource:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book SB p.77. Exact Teacher's Book procedure remains unverified. The scripts, cards and examples below are original teacher-created alternatives, not claimed to reproduce textbook activities.  
**Objective:** Pupils write two understandable house messages with greetings and closings.  
**Success criteria:** Independently complete all stated components.  
**Materials:** Board, room cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original message models:** “Hi Sam, There is a bed in my bedroom. From Ali.” “Hi Mia, My kitchen has a table. From Sara.” **Frames:** “Hi ____, / My ____ has a ____. / From ____.” **Editing check:** greeting, room, object, capital, full stop, closing.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show room pictures and elicit two familiar house words.  
**Pupils:** Name rooms or objects.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the original message models, label greeting/body/closing and show the editing check.  
**Pupils:** Practise the model with support.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils select two different rooms, draft messages to two classmates, peer-check and independently revise.  
**Pupils:** Complete the activity and explain their answers.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Collect two individual messages; assess clarity, room vocabulary and basic punctuation. Note accuracy and prompts needed.  
**Pupils:** Respond independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap two words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share a sentence.
