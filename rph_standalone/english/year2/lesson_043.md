# English Year 2 — Lesson 43: Writing 9

**Unit:** The old house
**Main SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.1
**Complementary SK/SP:** 4.2 / 4.2.5
**Learning objectives (draft):** Pupils write two short messages about house objects using a clear model.
**Success criteria (draft):** two understandable written messages.
**Teaching materials:** Super Minds 1 Student's Book p.77, pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Audited source mapping:** Student's Book p.77 (Year 2 SOW mapping audit); Teacher's Book sequence not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show house pictures and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two familiar words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model a short house-related message with word-card support; page 77 is a mapped resource only.
**Pupils:** Give one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide two short messages and peer checking for meaning.
**Pupils:** Write two short messages and check clarity.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record each pupil's independent evidence against both success criteria.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the skill independently.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review two useful house words.
**Pupils:** Share one learned word.

**Source:** SOW mapping. **QA:** Preliminary RPH; Teacher's Book verification pending.
