# English Year 2 — Lesson 158: Reading 32

**Unit:** Get dressed
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Complementary SK/SP:** 1.2 / 1.2.5
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read four clothing sentences and identify three stated details.
**Success criteria:** Four intelligible sentences and three correct detail responses.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards and classroom resources.
**Original four clothing sentences:** Ben has a blue shirt. His sister has a red dress. Mum has a yellow hat. Dad has black shoes. **Three questions/key:** What colour is Ben's shirt? Blue. Who has a red dress? His sister. What does Dad have? Black shoes.

**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Show a picture related to Get dressed and elicit two familiar words.
**Pupils:** Name two relevant words.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model reading a teacher-created clothing sentence.
**Pupils:** Rehearse one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Guide reading four clothing sentences. Ask pupils to reread a word, phrase or sentence when unsure of its meaning, then answer three detail questions using familiar context clues.
**Pupils:** Read four sentences and locate three details.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Record four intelligible sentences, three correct detail answers and individual evidence of rereading for meaning.
**Pupils:** Provide individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful expression.
**Pupils:** Share one useful word.
