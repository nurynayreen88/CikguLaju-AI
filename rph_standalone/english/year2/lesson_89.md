# English Year 2 — Lesson 89: Writing 18

**Unit:** The robot  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 4.2 / 4.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping; SOW verification pending):** 3.2 / 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source:** SB p.95; TB unverified. All scripts and tasks below are original teacher-created materials, not verified textbook extracts.  
**Objective:** Pupils write three robot sentences and identify four of five printed words.  
**Success criteria:** Meet the stated numerical targets independently.  
**Materials:** Board, picture/word cards, exercise books and resources below.

**Original robot plan:** round head, two eyes, four arms, short legs. **Word bank:** head, eyes, arms, legs, round. **Model:** “My robot has a round head. It has two eyes. It has four arms.” **Five word-identification prompts/key:** head, eyes, arms, legs, round (all appear in the word bank).

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a relevant picture and elicit two familiar words.  
**Pupils:** Name visible items and predict the topic.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show the original robot plan, read five words and model writing the first sentence.  
**Pupils:** Practise one supported example.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils locate five printed words, draw a different robot and write three sentences about its features.  
**Pupils:** Complete the task and improve answers after feedback.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check five word identifications and three independent meaningful sentences. Note independent accuracy and prompts required.  
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill individually.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review two target words and ask for one relevant sentence.  
**Pupils:** Share one word and sentence.
