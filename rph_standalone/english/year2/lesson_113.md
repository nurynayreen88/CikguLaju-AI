# English Year 2 — Lesson 113: Reading 23

**Unit:** The robot
**Main SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.2
**Complementary SK/SP:** 3.2 / 3.2.3
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify four of five details in simple printed sentences and reread two sentences to clarify meaning.
**Success criteria:** four accurate detail choices and two demonstrated rereading strategies.
**Teaching materials:** Pictures, word cards, individual PBD record and original resources below.

**Rereading prompts:** 'Rex carries food for the animals.' 'Rex has four wheels.' Pupils reread each sentence, point to its evidence and explain the meaning using pictures. **Original reading:** “Rex is a robot at the zoo. It has a yellow body, two arms and four wheels. Rex carries food for the animals every morning.” **Five details/key:** Rex; zoo; yellow; two arms; four wheels. **Questions/key:** What does Rex carry? Food. When? Every morning.
**Audited source mapping:** NON-TEXTBOOK / SOW task (Year 2 audit); detailed procedure not yet verified.

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Display a robot picture and elicit familiar vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name two robot features.

## Activity 1 — Modelling (15 minutes)
**Teacher (15 min):** Model locating evidence in a short printed robot paragraph.
**Pupils:** Practise one supported response.

## Activity 2 — Guided practice (25 minutes)
**Teacher (25 min):** Ask pupils to locate five printed details, then reread two selected sentences and explain their meaning with picture support.
**Pupils:** Underline evidence and answer questions.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher (10 min):** Check five printed details (SP 3.2.2) and two demonstrated rereading strategies (SP 3.2.3). Note independent performance and support needed.
**Pupils:** Submit individual evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher (5 min):** Review one useful robot expression.
**Pupils:** Share one new word or phrase.
