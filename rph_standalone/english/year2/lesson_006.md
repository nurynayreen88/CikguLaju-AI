# English Year 2 — Lesson 6: Listening

**Unit:** Free Time  
**Main SK/SP (existing mapping):** 1.2 / 1.2.2  
**Complementary SK/SP (existing mapping):** 2.1 / 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Source classification:** NON-TEXTBOOK in the existing Year 2 mapping. The clues, answer key and activities are teacher-developed.  
**Learning objectives:** By the end of the lesson, pupils can identify four out of five free-time activities from spoken descriptions and answer two simple questions about their own activities.  
**Success criteria:** At least four correct listening matches and two relevant oral answers.  
**Materials:** Five activity picture cards (reading, cycling, swimming, football, drawing), board, pupil answer grid below.

## Original teacher-read listening clues
Read each clue twice without naming the picture until checking answers.
1. “I open my book and look at the words.”  
2. “I wear a helmet and ride on two wheels.”  
3. “I move my arms and legs in the pool.”  
4. “I kick a ball with my friends.”  
5. “I use pencils to make a picture.”

**Pupil answer grid:** Write 1–5 beside these pictures in mixed order: football [ ]; drawing [ ]; reading [ ]; swimming [ ]; cycling [ ].  
**Answer key:** Reading—1; cycling—2; swimming—3; football—4; drawing—5.  
**Speaking prompts:** “Do you like swimming?” “What do you do after school?”

## Set Induction (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the five picture cards, mime cycling and drawing, and ask pupils to identify the activities.  
**Pupils:** Name the activities and repeat the words.

## Activity 1 — Listen and match (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the first clue, demonstrate how to choose the reading picture, then read all five clues without showing the text. Repeat the clues in the same order.  
**Pupils:** Listen, select the corresponding pictures and write clue numbers in the answer grid.

## Activity 2 — Verify and speak (25 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read the clues in a different order for checking. Ask pupils which words helped them decide (e.g. *pool*, *helmet*, *pencils*). Model the two speaking prompts, then organise pairs to ask and answer, changing roles. Provide response frames “Yes, I do / No, I don't” and “I ____ after school.”  
**Pupils:** Check their listening choices, explain one clue, ask and answer two questions in pairs.

## Assessment / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Re-read five clues in random order and assess pupils' independent picture selections. Ask each pupil one yes/no question and one open question. Record listening and speaking evidence separately, including prompting.  
**Pupils:** Match five spoken clues and answer two oral questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify the easiest and hardest clue and explain why.  
**Pupils:** Share one key listening word and a favourite activity.
