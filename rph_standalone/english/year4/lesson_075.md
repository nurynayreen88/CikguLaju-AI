# English Year 4 — Lesson 075: Listening 15

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 1.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.57 Activities 3–4; TB p.89; simple sample menus

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Show a sample menu and elicit the meanings of menu, order, pay, waiter and customer in context.
**Pupil action:** Identify menu, waiter, customer and order vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Read the TB Activity 3 questions aloud, model a polite answer and guide pupils through paired practice.
**Pupil action:** Listen to and answer restaurant questions.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model natural dialogue with clarification such as 'To drink?'
**Pupil action:** Practise natural restaurant dialogue and clarification.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Use Workbook p.44, the healthy-eating reading passage, as the main written practice; follow its printed instructions and check pupils' answers. Separately assess pupils' understanding of spoken restaurant questions and clarification requests from Student's Book p.57 Activities 3–4 as the main Listening PBD evidence for SP 1.2.5. Assign waiter and customer roles and observe pupils' spoken clarification during menu-ordering role-play for complementary Speaking SP 2.2.1.
**Pupil action:** Complete Workbook p.44 according to the printed instructions, respond to spoken restaurant questions and role-play ordering from a sample menu.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Invite pupils to share a favourite meal and model a polite response to a partner's choice.
**Pupil action:** Describe a favourite restaurant meal.

**PBD:** Check pupils' understanding of spoken restaurant questions and menu-ordering exchanges for main Listening SP 1.2.5; observe appropriate requests for clarification in role-play for complementary Speaking SP 2.2.1. Do not treat written menu answers alone as listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
