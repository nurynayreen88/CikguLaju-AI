# English Year 4 — Lesson 139: Listening 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 1.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.97 Activities 3–4; TB p.143; CD Track 60; Year 4 SOW Lesson 139

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review adjectives and their superlative forms.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and recall vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare -est and most in meaningful sports sentences.
**Pupils:** Follow the example and complete the first task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Listen to Track 60 and identify sports mentioned.
**Pupils:** Carry out the specified reading, writing, listening or speaking task.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activities 3–4 and identify basketball as the suggested sport.
**Pupils:** Provide an observable response and revise after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review listening gist and personal opinions.
**Pupils:** Share a short answer or reflection.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against 1.2.1 and 2.1.1; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
