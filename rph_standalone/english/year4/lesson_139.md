# English Year 4 — Lesson 139: Listening 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 1.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_139_sports_listening_gist_recommendation_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.97 Activities 3–4; TB p.143; Track 60 (SOW says CD without disc number); Year 4 SOW Lesson 139

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review adjectives and their superlative forms.
**Pupils:** Recall superlative forms.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Contrast -est and most in two sports sentences and ask pupils to explain which form fits each adjective.
**Pupils:** Compare -est and most in sports examples.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 60 and ask pupils to identify the sports mentioned, then replay a relevant segment to check their answers.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 60 and identify sports.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activities 3–4 and ask pupils to identify basketball as the sport the boys decide is best, then cite a listening clue supporting that answer.
**Pupils:** Complete Activities 3–4 and report the suggested sport.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to state the listening gist and give a reasoned opinion about one suggested sport.
**Pupils:** State a personal opinion about the listening.

**PBD:** Check pupils' recognition of the overall sports message in Track 60 for main Listening SP 1.2.1; observe their spoken sports recommendations and supporting opinions for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.1. The SOW does not establish the disc number, so do not assign one without verification.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
