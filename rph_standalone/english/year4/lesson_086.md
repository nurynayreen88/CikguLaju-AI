# English Year 4 — Lesson 086: Listening 17

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.61 Activity 4; TB pp.94–95; **CD2 Track 7 (as printed in KPM SOW)**

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review location and trip vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall trip and location words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview the questions in TB Activity 4, play the listening material and ask pupils to note key location details.
**Pupils:** Prepare for the Activity 4 listening task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the relevant sections, organise pair checking and ask pupils to explain which words supported their answers.
**Pupils:** Listen for details and compare answers in pairs.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.52, the transport-language task, as the main written practice, following the printed instructions and checking answers. Separately check each pupil's answers to the spoken location and trip details in Student's Book p.61 Activity 4 (CD2 Track 7) as the main Listening PBD evidence for SP 1.2.2; check comprehension of follow-up spoken questions for complementary Listening SP 1.2.5. Model accurate location phrases and provide brief feedback.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.52 according to the printed instructions, confirm details heard in Activity 4 and answer follow-up listening questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to report one location detail from the listening and explain the word that helped them.
**Pupils:** Recall one detail from the recording.

**PBD:** Check specific trip details pupils identify from CD2 Track 7 for main Listening SP 1.2.2 and their understanding of follow-up spoken questions for complementary Listening SP 1.2.5. Workbook p.52 supports written practice; its answers alone are not evidence of listening.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
