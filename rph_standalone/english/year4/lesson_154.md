# English Year 4 — Lesson 154: Language Arts 29

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook p.77; resources/lesson_154_jungle_book_creative_response_pbd_DRAFT.md (pupil writing and individual PBD); Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 154

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review characters, feelings and actions.
**Pupils:** Recall story characters and feelings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain guidebook p.77 steps 1–4, model the opening response and monitor pupils as they complete the tasks.
**Pupils:** Complete guidebook page 77 steps 1–4.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide groups through guidebook steps 5–8, prompting them to connect their creative response to a character or event in the story.
**Pupils:** Develop a creative response using steps 5–8.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite groups to present their creative work and explain which story detail supports their main idea.
**Pupils:** Present work and support opinions with story details.

Pupils complete a follow-up task based on The Jungle Book graphic novel and Teaching Guidebook; a Workbook page is not prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a gallery walk and prompt pupils to give one evidence-based strength and one constructive suggestion.
**Pupils:** Give classmates stars and wishes during a gallery walk.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' personal responses to The Jungle Book, supported by relevant story events, for main Language Arts SP 5.2.1; check the clarity and relevance of pupils' written creative descriptions for complementary Writing SP 4.2.4. Group presentation alone is not sufficient evidence of the Writing standard.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
