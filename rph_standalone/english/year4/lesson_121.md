# English Year 4 — Lesson 121: Writing 23

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 4.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.84–85 Activities 3–4; TB p.127; resources/lesson_121_dinosaur_listening_writing_pbd_DRAFT.md (original supplementary listening and PBD); Year 4 SOW Lesson 121

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show dinosaur pictures and ask pupils to label body parts before forming descriptive sentences.
**Pupils:** Name dinosaur body parts.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Student's Book Activities 3–4 using Teacher's Book p.127 and check pupils' understanding of dinosaur descriptions.
**Pupils:** Complete textbook activities in pairs.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model two descriptive sentences about a dinosaur's body parts and abilities, then guide pairs to invent, draw and describe a new dinosaur.
**Pupils:** Draw and describe an original dinosaur.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use pupils' written descriptions of their invented dinosaurs from Activity 2 as the main Writing Assessment/PBD evidence for SP 4.2.4. Check whether pupils describe body parts and abilities in their own sentences, and provide brief corrective feedback. Do not assign Workbook p.72, Activity 1 here: its Elephant Orphanage reading questions do not match this dinosaur-writing task.
**Pupils:** Write and improve short descriptions of their invented dinosaurs, including body parts and abilities.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pairs to present their invented dinosaur and explain one distinctive feature.
**Pupils:** Present the dinosaur.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' original written dinosaur description, including relevant body parts and abilities, for main Writing SP 4.2.4; check specific details identified from any verified spoken input for complementary Listening SP 1.2.2. The supplementary teacher-read listening task provides separate provisional evidence for SP 1.2.2; verify source vocabulary and administer the listening questions before recording achievement. Do not infer listening from written work.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
