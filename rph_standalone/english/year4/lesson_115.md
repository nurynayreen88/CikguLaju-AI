# English Year 4 — Lesson 115: Reading 22

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.82 Activity 1; TB pp.124–125; dinosaur pictures; resources/lesson_115_dinosaur_dictionary_speaking_pbd_DRAFT.md; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 115

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce dinosaur pictures and animal comparison words.
**Pupils:** Name dinosaurs and comparative adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to read the dinosaur text, model identifying one unfamiliar comparative word and guide them to mark others.
**Pupils:** Read the text and mark unfamiliar comparisons.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate using a nearby sentence and a dictionary to infer one word meaning, then let pupils check their own inferences.
**Pupils:** Use dictionaries and context clues to find meanings.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.71, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.71, Activity 1; complete the comparative animal sentences using the word bank.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to explain one inferred dinosaur word meaning and identify the context clue that helped.
**Pupils:** Explain one newly learned word.

**PBD:** Check pupils' use of dictionaries to establish meanings of unfamiliar dinosaur comparison words for main Reading SP 3.2.4; observe spoken comparisons of dinosaurs with other animals for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.5. Workbook p.71 is the main written comparative practice, not the sole evidence of reading or speaking.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
