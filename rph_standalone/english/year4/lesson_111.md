# English Year 4 — Lesson 111: Language Arts 21

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.35–36; Chapters 3–4; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 111

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap Chapters 1–2 and predictions.
**Pupils:** Recall Chapters 1–2 and earlier predictions.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Chapters 3–4 and pause to ask questions about important events and characters.
**Pupils:** Read Chapters 3–4 and identify key events.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain guidebook steps 1–7, model ordering the first picture and ask groups to sequence the remaining events.
**Pupils:** Sequence story pictures using guidebook steps 1–7.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model sequencing one Chapter 5 picture, then ask groups to order the remaining pictures and predict the next event.
**Pupils:** Order Chapter 5 pictures and predict events.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Prompt groups to explain their predictions using picture details or earlier story events.
**Pupils:** Give a reason for a prediction.

**PBD:** Check pupils' ordering of story pictures and explanations of predictions against SP 5.3.1 and 2.1.4.
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
