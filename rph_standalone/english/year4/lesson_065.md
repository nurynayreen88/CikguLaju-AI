# English Year 4 — Lesson 065: Listening 13

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 1.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.49 and Phonics p.120; TB pp.78, 175; Tracks 70–71; Year 4 SOW Lesson 65

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display familiar food pictures and ask pupils to group them.
**Pupils:** Sort foods into categories such as fruit and sweet foods.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the target vocabulary following TB Vocabulary and Track 70.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 70 and match food words with pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 71 with books closed; elicit likely meanings of unfamiliar song words.
**Pupils:** Listen without written lyrics and infer meanings using context.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activity 1 and phonics practice for /eə/ as in pear.
**Pupils:** Complete the listening activity and identify the /eə/ sound in words.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to share a guessed meaning and a phonics example.
**Pupils:** Explain one inferred word and pronounce one /eə/ example.

**PBD:** Observe contextual inference and sound recognition against SP 1.3.1 and 1.1.1.  
**Differentiation:** Use picture prompts, repeated listening and peer support.
