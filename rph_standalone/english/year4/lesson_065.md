# English Year 4 Lesson 065 — Listening 13

Topic: Eating right. Main SP: Listening 1.3.1. Complementary SP: Listening 1.1.1. References: SB p.49 and Phonics p.120; TB pp.78,175; Tracks 70–71. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Categorise familiar foods such as fruit and sweet foods. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Follow Vocabulary with Track 70. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Play Track 71 first with books closed; pupils infer unfamiliar song words. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Follow Activity 1 and phonics, focusing on /eə/ in pear. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Pupils share a guessed meaning and a phonics example. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Listening 1.3.1 and Listening 1.1.1. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 65.
