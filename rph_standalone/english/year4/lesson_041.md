# English Year 4 — Lesson 041: Writing 8

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Writing 4.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.31 Activity 2; TB p.53; The Curse listening CD1 Track 43 (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Ancient Egypt and story characters.
**Pupils:** Recall Ancient Egypt and the story characters.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Grammar Box to model past-simple questions.
**Pupils:** Form past-simple questions using the Grammar Box.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 43 with books closed, ask pupils to answer listening questions, then guide them to verify answers against the text.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 43 with books closed, then check the text.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a complete past-simple answer, then organise pairs to write and check statements about the characters.
**Pupils:** Write full-sentence answers about the characters.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.26, Story time sentence completion, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.26, Story time sentence completion; complete the past-tense sentences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to revise one answer and explain the change.
**Pupils:** Improve one complete answer.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' complete written answers about the story characters for main Writing SP 4.2.4, including relevant details and sentence accuracy. Check responses to the spoken story on CD1 Track 43 for complementary Listening SP 1.2.3. Workbook p.26 is the main written practice.  
**Differentiation:** Provide past-verb prompts and partner support.
