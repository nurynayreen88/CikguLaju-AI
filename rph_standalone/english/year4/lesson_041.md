# English Year 4 — Lesson 041: Writing 8

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Writing 4.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.31 Activity 2; TB p.53; CD1 Track 43

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Ancient Egypt and story characters.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and recall relevant language.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Grammar Box to model past-simple questions.
**Pupils:** Follow the source-based model and perform the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 43 with books closed; pupils answer and then check against the text.
**Pupils:** Complete the central task and practise the main skill.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs write full-sentence answers and statements about the characters.
**Pupils:** Show learning evidence and use feedback to improve answers.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Improve one full-sentence answer.
**Pupils:** Share a short response or reflection.

**PBD:** Check responses against Writing 4.2.4 and Listening 1.2.3.  
**Differentiation:** Provide past-verb prompts and partner support.
