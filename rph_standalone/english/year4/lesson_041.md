# English Year 4 — Lesson 041: Writing 8

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Writing 4.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.31 Activity 2; TB p.53; CD1 Track 43

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Ancient Egypt and story characters.
**Pupils:** Recall Ancient Egypt and the story characters.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Grammar Box to model past-simple questions.
**Pupils:** Form past-simple questions using the Grammar Box.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 43 with books closed; pupils answer and then check against the text.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 43 with books closed, then check the text.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a complete past-simple answer, then organise pairs to write and check statements about the characters.
**Pupils:** Write full-sentence answers about the characters.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to revise one answer and explain the change.
**Pupils:** Improve one complete answer.

**PBD:** Check past-simple questions and complete written answers using Track 43 (SP 4.2.4 and 1.2.3).  
**Differentiation:** Provide past-verb prompts and partner support.
