# English Year 4 — Lesson 097: Listening 19

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.71 Activity 1; TB p.108; **Class CD2 Track 20 (vocabulary); Class CD2 Track 21 (Activity 1 song), TB p.108**

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce ways of helping others.
**Pupils:** Name ways to help other people.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the first SB Activity 1 response using TB guidance, then check pupils' understanding before independent work.
**Pupils:** Prepare for the Student Book song activity.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the helping-out song, ask pupils to listen for examples of helpful actions and replay key lines to check details.
**Pupils:** Listen to the helping-out song and identify details.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check pupils' identification of at least two helpful actions heard in the song (Student's Book p.71 Activity 1, Track 21), replaying relevant lines as needed; record listening responses as the main PBD evidence for SP 1.2.2. Then model using a lyric or picture clue to infer an unfamiliar word for complementary Reading SP 3.2.3.
**Pupils:** Identify two helpful actions from the song and explain one unfamiliar word using a lyric or picture clue.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to describe one helpful action and explain when they could do it.
**Pupils:** Suggest one helpful action.

**PBD:** Check specific helping actions pupils identify from the spoken song (Class CD2 Track 21; TB p.108 verified) for main Listening SP 1.2.2; assess their inference of unfamiliar words from printed lyrics or picture clues for complementary Reading SP 3.2.3.
