# English Year 4 — Lesson 114: Speaking 22

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB Activity 2; animal pictures; blank cards and dictionaries; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 114

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review animal adjectives and pictures.
**Pupils:** Recall adjectives describing animals.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a comparative sentence about two animals and highlight the adjective form used.
**Pupils:** Practise comparative animal descriptions.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one animal comparison, then guide pairs to write clue cards and check comparative forms and spelling.
**Pupils:** Describe animals and write correctly spelled clues.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.69, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.69, Activity 2; complete sentences comparing the animals using the words in the box.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display an inaccurate comparative sentence and ask pupils to explain and correct its form.
**Pupils:** Correct and share a comparative adjective.

**PBD:** Observe comparative animal descriptions and spelling of written clue cards (SP 2.1.5, 4.3.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
