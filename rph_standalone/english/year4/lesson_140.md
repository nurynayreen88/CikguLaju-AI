# English Year 4 — Lesson 140: Speaking 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.98 Activities 1–2; TB pp.144–145; Year 4 SOW Lesson 140

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the TB revision goals and model one interview response before pupils begin.
**Pupils:** Review sports vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a sports-preference interview question, then ask pairs to complete Activity 1 and record their partner's answers.
**Pupils:** Interview a partner and record sports data.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show how to organise the collected answers in a simple chart, then invite pupils to present one finding.
**Pupils:** Organise the collected data in a simple chart.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Workbook as the main written Assessment/PBD task: instruct pupils to complete Workbook p.80, Activity 2 according to the printed instructions. Check the Workbook answers to assess understanding; retain oral or listening observation when required by the main SP. Observe pupils' responses during the lesson to assess the main listening/speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.80, Activity 2; look at the sports picture and complete sentences using very good at, good at or not good at.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Facilitate the TB optional review and invite pupils to share a conclusion from their interviews.
**Pupils:** Share a conclusion from the survey.

**PBD:** Observe partner interviews, sports-survey recording and spoken summaries of preferences (SP 2.1.1, 2.1.5).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
