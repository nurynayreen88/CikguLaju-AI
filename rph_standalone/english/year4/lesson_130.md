# English Year 4 — Lesson 130: Speaking 25

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.93 Activity 2; TB pp.138–139; Year 4 SOW Lesson 130

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the sports song and key words.
**Pupils:** Recall sports vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Grammar Box and extend the Activity 3 dialogue.
**Pupils:** Practise an extended dialogue.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a question and follow-up response about sports preferences, then organise a class survey in which pupils interview at least three classmates.
**Pupils:** Survey classmates about sports preferences.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to summarise their survey findings and ask one follow-up question; note whether questions and responses are understandable.
**Pupils:** Summarise the survey results.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to state a sports preference and give a supporting reason.
**Pupils:** Share one sports opinion.

**PBD:** Observe sports-preference survey questions, responses and follow-up phrases against SP 2.1.1 and 2.2.1.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
