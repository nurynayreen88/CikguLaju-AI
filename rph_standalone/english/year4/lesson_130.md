# English Year 4 — Lesson 130: Speaking 25

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_130_sports_preference_survey_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.93 Activity 2; TB pp.138–139; Year 4 SOW Lesson 130

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay a short section of the sports song and elicit three key words before the speaking survey.
**Pupils:** Recall sports vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Grammar Box and extend the Activity 3 dialogue.
**Pupils:** Practise an extended dialogue.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a question and follow-up response about sports preferences, then organise a class survey in which pupils interview at least three classmates.
**Pupils:** Survey classmates about sports preferences.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.77, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.77, Activity 2; read the sentences and circle the correct descriptions of sports.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to state a sports preference and give a supporting reason.
**Pupils:** Share one sports opinion.

**PBD:** Observe pupils stating sports preferences and giving relevant reasons in their class survey for main Speaking SP 2.1.1; assess appropriate follow-up questions and clarification phrases for complementary Speaking SP 2.2.1. Workbook p.77 is the main written practice, not a substitute for speaking evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
