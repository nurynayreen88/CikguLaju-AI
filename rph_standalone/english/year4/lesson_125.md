# English Year 4 — Lesson 125: Reading 24

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.88–89 story; main character pictures; Year 4 SOW Lesson 125

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display story character pictures and elicit one prediction about each character's role.
**Pupils:** Identify story characters.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pupils quiet time to read the story independently, then ask them to identify one event they found interesting without interrupting their first reading.
**Pupils:** Read the story independently.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Prompt groups to discuss a favourite event and predict an ending, asking each group to support its prediction with one detail from the story.
**Pupils:** Discuss favourite events and predict the ending.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.74, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.74, Activity 1; read the animal story and complete the sentences using the past simple verbs.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share one personal response and support it with a specific event from the story.
**Pupils:** Share a personal response.

**PBD:** Observe pupils reading the animal story independently and identifying key events for main Reading SP 3.3.1; assess their group narration or role-play of story events for complementary Speaking SP 2.3.1. Workbook p.74 is the main written past-simple story practice, not a substitute for oral evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
