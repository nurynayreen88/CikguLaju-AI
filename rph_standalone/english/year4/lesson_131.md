# English Year 4 — Lesson 131: Reading 25

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.94 Activities 1–3; TB pp.140–141; flashcards; Year 4 SOW Lesson 131

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB warm-up and sports vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall sports vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read Activity 1 and locate the main idea.
**Pupils:** Read Activity 1 and identify the main idea.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Groups choose the best title for the story and explain why.
**Pupils:** Choose a suitable title and justify it.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Complete Activity 2 and review superlative Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 2 and practise superlatives.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play a sports mime review.
**Pupils:** Mime a sport for classmates to guess.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against 3.2.2 and 3.2.1; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
