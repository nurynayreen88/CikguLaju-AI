# English Year 4 — Lesson 084: Writing 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 4.3.3  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.131 CLIL 3; TB p.195; CD3 Track 39

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show famous buildings and review vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name famous buildings from pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign pupils to CLIL texts A and B, explain the information-gap task and model finding one building fact.
**Pupils:** Read an assigned CLIL text A or B.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Activity 2, then pair readers of texts A and B to exchange and check building facts.
**Pupils:** Exchange building facts with a partner using Activity 2.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model matching one question to a building description, then guide pupils to organise facts for a short paragraph.
**Pupils:** Match questions to descriptions and plan a paragraph.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share one fact about a building.
**Pupils:** Share one fact about a building.

**PBD:** Assess reading comprehension of famous-building facts and the accuracy of written descriptions against SP 4.3.3 and 3.2.2.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
