# English Year 4 — Lesson 112: Language Awareness 6

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Complementary SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Unit 7 revision; Teacher Book and SOW self-assessment; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 112

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow the TB Warm-up.
**Pupils:** Respond to the prompts and recall relevant language.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Complete revision Activities 1–3.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and complete the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use formative results for targeted communicative practice.
**Pupils:** Complete the source-based activity individually or in groups.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Complete Unit 7 self-assessment and discuss gaps.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill, compare answers and use feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set one learning goal.
**Pupils:** Share a short response or reflection.

**PBD:** Observe the named learning task against Teacher-selected DSKP SP and Teacher-selected DSKP SP; for teacher-selected standards, establish criteria before class.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
