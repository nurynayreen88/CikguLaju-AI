# English Year 4 — Lesson 126: Writing 24

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 4.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.87 Activities 1–2; TB pp.130–131; animal pictures; Year 4 SOW Lesson 126

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review favourite animal descriptions.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and recall vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 1 and examine a model.
**Pupils:** Follow the example and complete the first task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils draft an animal description using relevant details.
**Pupils:** Carry out the specified reading, writing, listening or speaking task.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 2; check punctuation and improve the writing.
**Pupils:** Provide an observable response and revise after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read one improved sentence.
**Pupils:** Share a short answer or reflection.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against 4.2.5 and 4.3.3; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
