## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the song's key adjectives using neutral picture prompts without revealing the sports theme.
**Pupils:** Recall descriptive adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show possible song topics: animals, food, sports and school.
**Pupils:** Predict whether the song is about sports, food, school or animals.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the song with books closed; pupils identify the sports theme and complete Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Listen with books closed and identify the theme.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.77, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the listening task in Activity 1/2, check pupils' responses to spoken input as evidence for the main Listening SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.77, Activity 1; read the clues and write the names of the sports.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to name a sport they enjoy and connect their response to one word or action from the song.
**Pupils:** Name a sport they enjoy.

**PBD:** Check specific sports words or details pupils identify from the song for main Listening SP 1.2.2; check their recognition of the song's overall sports theme for complementary Listening SP 1.2.1. Workbook p.77 is written sports-vocabulary practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
