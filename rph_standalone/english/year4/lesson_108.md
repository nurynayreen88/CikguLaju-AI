# English Year 4 — Lesson 108: Speaking 21

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 2.1.3  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.76–77; TB p.116; recycled containers; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 108

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review recycling and the idea of reusing containers.
**Pupils:** Name ways to reuse containers.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the TB warm-up response, then guide pupils through Activity 1 and check understanding of reused materials.
**Pupils:** Follow the model activity and identify materials.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate how to assemble a simple object from clean reused containers, check materials are safe, then guide pairs through Activity 2.
**Pupils:** Make an object from reused containers in pairs.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.61, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.61, Activity 1; match the objects to the correct recycling bins.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit action verbs from the construction process and check pupils' use of sequencing words.
**Pupils:** Recall useful action verbs.

**PBD:** Observe pupils explaining the steps for making a reused-container object using First and Then for main Speaking SP 2.1.3; check how they ask for clarification and respond to peer questions for complementary Speaking SP 2.2.2. Workbook p.61 provides written recycling practice, not speaking evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
