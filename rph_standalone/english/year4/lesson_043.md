# English Year 4 — Lesson 043: Listening 9

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Listening 1.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.121 Phonics 3; TB pp.177–178; **Activity 1 CD3 Track 10; Activity 2 CD3 Track 11** (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review regular past-tense verbs.
**Pupils:** Recall regular past-tense verbs.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Teach /d/, /t/ and /id/ endings; follow Phonics 3 Activity 1 with Track 10.
**Pupils:** Distinguish /d/, /t/ and /id/ with Track 10.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 11 for Activity 2, pause for pupils to repeat and check pronunciation of the three past-tense endings.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 11 and repeat target words.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model sorting one past-tense verb by its final sound, then guide pupils to classify verbs including opened, wrapped and painted. Check pupils' ability to distinguish the /d/, /t/ and /ɪd/ endings heard in CD3 Tracks 10–11 as the main Listening PBD evidence; provide corrective pronunciation feedback. Do not assign a Workbook phonics activity until its printed page and task are verified.
**Pupils:** Listen to the target verbs, sort opened, wrapped and painted by the ending sounds they hear, and pronounce one word from each group.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to pronounce one verb from each sound group and give corrective pronunciation feedback.
**Pupils:** Pronounce one verb from each sound group.

**PBD:** Check discrimination and pronunciation of past-tense /d/, /t/ and /id/ endings using Tracks 10–11 (Listening 1.1.1; 1.2.5).  
**Differentiation:** Provide extra sound-discrimination practice.
