# English Year 4 — Lesson 091: Listening 18

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.63 Activities 3–4; TB p.97; SOW specifies CD2 Track 11 for Activity 3 and CD2 Track 12 for Activity 4 (audio-file content unverified)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review transport vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall transport vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview SB Activities 3–4, play the listening material and pause to check key transport details.
**Pupils:** Listen and complete Student Book Activities 3–4.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify transport details and gerunds they heard, then replay the relevant sections to verify responses.
**Pupils:** Identify transport details and gerund forms.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.53, the transport picture-prompt task, as the main written practice; follow the printed instructions and check pupils' answers. Separately check each pupil's responses to the spoken transport details in Student's Book p.63 Activities 3–4 (CD2 Tracks 11–12) as the main Listening PBD evidence for SP 1.2.2. Model a short written transport answer and assess the accuracy of pupils' written responses for complementary Writing SP 4.2.3.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.53 according to the printed instructions; confirm transport details heard in the recording and write short answers about transport.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit one gerund from the transport activity and remind pupils which recycled materials are needed next lesson.
**Pupils:** Share one gerund and prepare materials for the next lesson.

**PBD:** Check transport listening answers, correct gerund forms and short written responses against SP 1.2.2 and 4.2.3.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
