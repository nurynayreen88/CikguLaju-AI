# English Year 4 — Lesson 091: Listening 19

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.63 Activities 3–4; TB p.97

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review transport vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall transport vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview SB Activities 3–4, play the listening material and pause to check key transport details.
**Pupils:** Listen and complete Student Book Activities 3–4.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify transport details and gerunds they heard, then replay the relevant sections to verify responses.
**Pupils:** Identify transport details and gerund forms.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write short responses to questions about transport.
**Pupils:** Write short answers about transport.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review a useful gerund and prepare recycled materials.
**Pupils:** Share one gerund and prepare materials for the next lesson.

**PBD:** Check transport listening answers, correct gerund forms and short written responses against SP 1.2.2 and 4.2.3.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
