# English Year 4 — Lesson 093: Reading 18

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.66–67 story; TB pp.102–103; story listening CD2 Track 16 (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story pictures and elicit predictions.
**Pupils:** Predict events from the story pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a gist question and ask pupils to read the story independently before checking their first impressions.
**Pupils:** Read the story independently.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model identifying the first story event, then guide pupils to sequence the remaining events with supporting details.
**Pupils:** Sequence and discuss the story events.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the story pictures to prompt pupils to retell an event in sequence with an appropriate time connector.
**Pupils:** Retell an event using picture prompts.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.55, the Story time task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.55, the Story time task; read the story and answer the comprehension questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to identify a favourite story moment and explain their choice using a story detail.
**Pupils:** Describe a favourite story moment.

**PBD:** Observe pupils reading the transport story independently and identifying supporting events for main Reading SP 3.3.1; assess their oral retelling using picture prompts for complementary Speaking SP 2.3.1. Workbook p.55 is the main written story practice, not a substitute for oral evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
