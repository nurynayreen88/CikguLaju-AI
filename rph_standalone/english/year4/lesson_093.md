# English Year 4 — Lesson 093: Reading 18

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.66–67 story; TB pp.102–103

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story pictures and elicit predictions.
**Pupils:** Predict events from the story pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a gist question and ask pupils to read the story independently before checking their first impressions.
**Pupils:** Read the story independently.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model identifying the first story event, then guide pupils to sequence the remaining events with supporting details.
**Pupils:** Sequence and discuss the story events.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Retell an event using pictures as prompts.
**Pupils:** Retell an event using picture prompts.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share a favourite moment.
**Pupils:** Describe a favourite story moment.

**PBD:** Observe independent reading and accurate oral retelling of the transport story against SP 3.3.1 and 2.3.1.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
