# English Year 4 — Lesson 157: Reading 30

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.110–111, TB pp.162–163; alternative SB p.133 and TB p.197; Year 4 SOW Lesson 157

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce What are germs? using the title and pictures on Student's Book page 110.
**Pupils:** Predict the meaning of the title What are germs?.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the TB warm-up to elicit pupils' questions about germs, then guide reading of What are germs? on SB pp.110–111.
**Pupils:** Read the information text in Student's Book pages 110–111.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through textbook Activities 1–2. Model how to locate a sentence that supports an answer, then prompt pupils to infer unfamiliar vocabulary using nearby words and pictures.
**Pupils:** Complete Activities 1–2 and infer unfamiliar words from context.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.91, Activity 4 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.91, Activity 4; read the statements about germs and circle the correct answers.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to cite one fact about germs from the text and use it to justify a true-or-false response.
**Pupils:** Answer a true-or-false quiz about germs.

**PBD:** Check comprehension answers about germs against the information text and pupils' use of context to infer unfamiliar words (SP 3.2.2, 3.2.3).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
