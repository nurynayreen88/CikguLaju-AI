# English Year 4 — Lesson 149: Language Arts 28

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook p.73; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 149

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare video scenes of a village and a jungle.
**Pupils:** Describe village and jungle settings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to choose the village or jungle and model a reasoned sentence before inviting pair responses.
**Pupils:** Explain a preferred place to live.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow guidebook p.73 steps 1–2.
**Pupils:** Complete guidebook page 73 tasks in pairs.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to read their written opinions about Mowgli's choice and prompt them to support each opinion with a story detail.
**Pupils:** Write an opinion about Mowgli's choice.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Summarise pupils' differing opinions and ask the class to explain a shared conclusion with story evidence.
**Pupils:** Share a reasoned class view.

**PBD:** Check written opinions on Mowgli's village or jungle choice for relevant reasons and punctuation (SP 5.2.1, 4.3.1).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
