# English Year 4 — Lesson 132: Writing 25

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 4.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SOW Lesson 132; Get Smart Plus 4 Unit 9; Year 4 SOW Lesson 132

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit sports words and model an accurate superlative sentence before the writing task.
**Pupils:** Recall sports words and superlatives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a written invitation to a class mini competition.
**Pupils:** Study a model invitation and identify its purpose.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide groups to choose a mini competition, agree on key invitation details and draft invitations using the model.
**Pupils:** Plan a mini competition and write invitations.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to write a connected paragraph reporting competition results using conjunctions.
**Pupils:** Record results and write a short connected report.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share one revised report sentence and explain how a conjunction connects its ideas.
**Pupils:** Share an improved report sentence.

**PBD:** Assess written invitations and the competition-results report for clarity and conjunctions against SP 4.2.2 and 4.2.5.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
