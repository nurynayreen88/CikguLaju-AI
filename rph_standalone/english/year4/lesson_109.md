# English Year 4 — Lesson 109: Reading 21

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SOW Lesson 109; teacher-prepared short local recycling project text; monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a local recycling-project picture and ask what materials can be collected.
**Pupils:** Name materials that can be recycled.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model dictionary headwords, definitions and alphabetical order; run a dictionary race using recycle, collect and sell.
**Pupils:** Find recycle, collect and sell in a monolingual dictionary.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give a short age-appropriate text about a local recycling project including money earned or saved; ask two gist questions before reading.
**Pupils:** Read the local recycling text and answer two gist questions.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to underline one sentence stating the main idea, check the meanings of two unfamiliar words in a monolingual dictionary, and explain one benefit of the project. Record who can locate the main idea and use the dictionary independently.
**Pupils:** Identify the main idea and explain how the project helps people.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pupils to share a way recycling can save money and recap how the text helped them find the answer.
**Pupils:** Describe one way recycling saves money.

**PBD:** Use pupils' underlined main-idea sentence, two dictionary lookups and one oral explanation as evidence for SP 3.2.4 and 3.2.1; check final alignment against the official SOW before release.  
**Differentiation:** Offer picture prompts, word banks and extra challenge tasks.
