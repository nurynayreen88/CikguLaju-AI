# English Year 4 — Lesson 109: Reading 21

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SOW Lesson 109; Get Smart Plus 4 Unit 7; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 109

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the text topic and activate relevant vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Respond to the prompts and recall relevant language.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils skim the source text for its main idea.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and complete the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read again and infer meanings of unfamiliar words from context.
**Pupils:** Complete the source-based activity individually or in groups.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs explain the main idea and justify vocabulary choices.
**Pupils:** Demonstrate the target skill, compare answers and use feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share one newly understood word.
**Pupils:** Share a short response or reflection.

**PBD:** Observe the named learning task against 3.2.4 and 3.2.1; for teacher-selected standards, establish criteria before class.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
