# English Year 4 — Lesson 109: Reading 21

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SOW Lesson 109; teacher-prepared local recycling reading text (see accompanying draft resource); monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a local recycling-project picture and invite pupils to name recyclable materials with reasons.
**Pupils:** Name materials that can be recycled.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate how to locate recycle, collect and sell alphabetically in a dictionary, then guide a timed meaning-check activity.
**Pupils:** Find recycle, collect and sell in a monolingual dictionary.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give a short age-appropriate text about a local recycling project including money earned or saved; ask two gist questions before reading.
**Pupils:** Read the local recycling text and answer two gist questions.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.61, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.61, Activity 1; identify recyclable objects and sort them into the appropriate bins.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pupils to share a way recycling can save money and recap how the text helped them find the answer.
**Pupils:** Describe one way recycling saves money.

**PBD:** Check pupils' dictionary lookups of recycle, collect and sell for main Reading SP 3.2.4; assess identification of the main idea in the teacher-prepared recycling text for complementary Reading SP 3.2.1.
