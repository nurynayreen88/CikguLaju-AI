# English Year 4 — Lesson 095: Language Arts 18

**Topic:** The King of Kites  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** CCL Guide pp.60–64; Worksheets 10a–10b

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Examine notes of encouragement.
**Pupils:** Read examples of encouraging notes.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify suitable occasions for encouragement notes and explain why each recipient might need support.
**Pupils:** Discuss when encouragement is helpful.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the tasks in Guide p.60 and Worksheets 10a–10b, then guide pupils to complete them independently or in pairs.
**Pupils:** Complete Worksheets 10a and 10b.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check worksheet responses, model a supportive sentence and ask pupils to draft an encouraging message.
**Pupils:** Check answers and draft an encouraging message.

Pupils complete a follow-up task using CCL Worksheets 10a–10b and the short-story guide; no specific Workbook page is prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on whether each encouraging message is polite, clear and suitable for its intended recipient.
**Pupils:** Improve the wording using feedback.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' original encouraging message and completion of CCL Worksheets 10a–10b for main Language Arts SP 5.3.1; check punctuation and sentence accuracy in the message for complementary Writing SP 4.3.3. Use the CCL worksheets as specified rather than inventing a Workbook page.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
