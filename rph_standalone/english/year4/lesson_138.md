# English Year 4 — Lesson 138: Language Arts 26

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_138_mowgli_creative_story_performance_pbd_DRAFT.md; Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.68–69,128; Year 4 SOW Lesson 138

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask how pupils would plan to defeat Shere Khan as Mowgli.
**Pupils:** Suggest a plan as Mowgli.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to compare their ideas and explain which plan best fits Mowgli's situation.
**Pupils:** Compare ideas in small groups.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain guidebook p.68 steps 1–4, model a short imaginative opening and guide groups as they create a story or poem.
**Pupils:** Create a story or poem using the guidebook tasks.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite groups to present their story or poem, then assess each presentation using the guidebook p.128 form.
**Pupils:** Present the creative work and use assessment criteria.

Pupils complete a follow-up response to The Jungle Book using the Teaching Guidebook; no specific Workbook page is prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite feedback on a specific strength and one improvement in each presentation.
**Pupils:** Give constructive feedback.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' original story or poem responding to Mowgli's dilemma for main Language Arts SP 5.3.1; observe clarity and sequence in group presentations for complementary Speaking SP 2.3.1. Use the Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook tasks without inventing a Workbook activity.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
