# English Year 4 — Lesson 113: Listening 22

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 1.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** 1.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.81 Activity 1; TB p.122; animal pictures; resources/lesson_113_animal_listening_pbd_DRAFT.md; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 113

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce animal pictures and familiar adjectives.
**Pupils:** Name animals and describing words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview the animal pictures and model how to predict an answer before pupils attempt SB Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Prepare for the Student Book listening task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play or read the animal questions, pause for pupils to infer unfamiliar words from context and check their answers.
**Pupils:** Listen to animal questions and infer unknown words.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.68, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the listening task in Activity 1/2, check pupils' responses to spoken input as evidence for the main Listening SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.68, Activity 1; complete the animal crossword using the picture clues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to explain one unfamiliar animal word using a picture or sentence clue.
**Pupils:** Share a newly learned animal word.

**PBD:** Check pupils' understanding of spoken animal questions for main Listening SP 1.2.5; assess their inference of unfamiliar animal words from the listening context for complementary Listening SP 1.3.1. Workbook p.68 crossword is the main written practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
