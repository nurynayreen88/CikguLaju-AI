# English Year 4 — Lesson 145: Listening 28

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 1.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_145_health_vocabulary_listening_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.103 Activity 1; TB p.152; Vocabulary Track 68 (disc unspecified in SOW); Year 4 SOW Lesson 145

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display health flashcards and ask pupils to match common symptoms to suitable expressions.
**Pupils:** Name symptoms and match them to picture clues.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show health-related picture clues without displaying the song lyrics and invite pupils to predict three expressions they may hear.
**Pupils:** Predict the topic and identify health expressions from pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Vocabulary Track 68 following TB p.152; ask pupils to raise a hand when they hear target words. Verify the CD and audio file before release.
**Pupils:** Listen without written lyrics, recognise target words and infer unfamiliar expressions from context.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.86, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the listening task in Activity 1/2, check pupils' responses to spoken input as evidence for the main Listening SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.86, Activity 1; look at the health pictures and complete the crossword.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to use one health expression from the listening in a suitable spoken sentence.
**Pupils:** Say and demonstrate one health expression.

**PBD:** Check pupils' inference of unfamiliar health expressions from spoken context for main Listening SP 1.3.1; assess recognition of target sounds in heard words for complementary Listening SP 1.1.1. Workbook p.86 crossword is written vocabulary practice, not listening evidence. Track 68's disc remains unverified.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture prompts, repetition, paired support and extension vocabulary.
