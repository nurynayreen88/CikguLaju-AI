# English Year 4 — Lesson 090: Language Arts 17

**Topic:** The King of Kites  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** CCL Short Story Guide p.66; invitation/card examples

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Examine wedding invitations and congratulatory cards.
**Pupils:** Examine invitations and congratulatory cards.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display an invitation and guide pupils to locate the date, time, venue and RSVP information.
**Pupils:** Identify date, time, venue and RSVP details.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate a card layout with date, time, venue and RSVP, then support pupils in creating cards on paper or with presentation software.
**Pupils:** Create an invitation or card using paper or software.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to present their cards and check that each includes clear event details and suitable wording.
**Pupils:** Present the card and check key information.

Pupils revise their invitation or card using the CCL guide and a writing checklist; no specific Workbook page is prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give specific feedback on the clarity of invitation details, including date, time, venue and RSVP.
**Pupils:** Give feedback on clarity.

**PBD:** Assess invitations or cards for clear date, time, venue and response details against SP 5.3.1 and 4.2.2.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
