# English Year 4 — Lesson 090: Language Arts 17

**Topic:** The King of Kites  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** CCL Short Story Guide p.66; invitation/card examples

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Examine wedding invitations and congratulatory cards.
**Pupils:** Respond to the prompts and recall relevant vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Identify date, time, venue and RSVP.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and complete the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Create a card using presentation software or paper.
**Pupils:** Complete the named skill activity and practise the target language.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Present cards and check key information.
**Pupils:** Show learning evidence and improve responses after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give feedback on clarity.
**Pupils:** Share one result or reflection.

**PBD:** Assess task responses against 5.3.1 and 4.2.2; teacher selects specific criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
