# English Year 4 — Lesson 033: Listening 7

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Listening 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.27 Activity 1; TB p.48; teacher-prepared spoken quiz text (see resources/lesson_033_egypt_listening_DRAFT.md)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of Ancient Egypt and elicit prior knowledge.
**Pupils:** Describe what is known about Ancient Egypt.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display pictures and explain that the topic concerns the past.
**Pupils:** Predict the topic using historical pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read or play the prepared quiz text and ask pupils to identify the matching Ancient Egypt pictures, checking which clues they heard.
**Pupils:** Listen to the prepared quiz text and match pictures.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce page vocabulary; pairs complete the SB quiz and check answers using SB p.118.
**Pupils:** Complete the SB quiz and check answers on p.118.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.22, Smart kids past-tense question task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.22, Smart kids past-tense question task; answer questions using the past tense.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to tell a partner one Ancient Egypt fact and identify the quiz clue that supports it.
**Pupils:** Tell a partner one fact from the quiz.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Check task-specific responses and note achievement of Listening 1.2.2 and Listening 1.2.1.  
**Support:** Use visuals and allow first-language contributions when discussing historical knowledge.
