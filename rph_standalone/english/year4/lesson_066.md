# English Year 4 Lesson 066 — Speaking 13

Topic: Eating right. Main SP: Speaking 2.2.1. Complementary SP: Listening 1.2.5. References: SB p.49 Activity 3; TB p.79; CD1 Track 72. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Review countable and uncountable food nouns. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Use Grammar Box to model want and need. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Follow Activity 3 with Track 72; pupils discuss ingredients. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Pairs deliberately change ingredients and request clarification politely. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Pupils describe ingredients for a favourite dish. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Speaking 2.2.1 and Listening 1.2.5. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 66.
