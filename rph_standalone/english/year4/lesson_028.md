# English Year 4 — Lesson 028: Speaking 6

**Topic:** My Week  
**Main SP:** Speaking 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.21 Activities 1–3; TB pp.40–41

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the optional seat-switch game or TB Warm-up to review Unit 2.
**Pupils:** Recall Unit 2 vocabulary in the warm-up game.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Activity 1 and model the target question-answer form.
**Pupils:** Practise the target question-and-answer form.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Complete TB Activities 2–3; pupils prepare and describe their work.
**Pupils:** Complete Activities 2–3 and prepare a description.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise pupil presentations, give classmates a listening purpose and model constructive peer feedback.
**Pupils:** Present work while classmates complete a listening task.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review work, celebrate progress and correct one unclear description.
**Pupils:** Improve one unclear description after feedback.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Check task-specific responses and note achievement of Speaking 2.1.5 and Listening 1.2.5.  
**Support:** Give early finishers extension questions; do not leave presentation listeners without a task.
