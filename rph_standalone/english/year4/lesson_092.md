# English Year 4 — Lesson 092: Speaking 18

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 2.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.65 Activity 1; TB pp.100–101

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review transport model examples.
**Pupils:** Recall examples of transport models.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the model-making materials and read the project instructions aloud, checking pupils' understanding of safe steps.
**Pupils:** Read the materials list and project instructions.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model checking the project instructions, then supervise pairs as they confirm the steps and build a transport model safely.
**Pupils:** Check steps with a partner and build a model.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to present their models and explain the process using First, Second and Next in sequence.
**Pupils:** Present the model using First, Second and Next.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.56, the Unit 6 revision task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.56, the Unit 6 revision task; review the transport prompts and give short comparisons.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Elicit action verbs used during model construction and ask pupils to use one in a sequence sentence.
**Pupils:** Recall action verbs used in the task.

**PBD:** Observe pupils asking for clarification about model-making instructions for main Speaking SP 2.2.2; assess their spoken sequencing of construction steps using First, Second and Next for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.3. Workbook p.56 is written revision practice, not a substitute for speaking evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
