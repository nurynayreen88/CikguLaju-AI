# English Year 4 — Lesson 102: Listening 20

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.73 Activity 4; TB p.111; **Class CD2 Track 25 (Activity 4, TB p.111)**; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 102

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review helping-out vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall helping-out words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain the listening goal for TB Activity 4 and preview the type of details pupils should identify.
**Pupils:** Follow the listening instructions for Activity 4.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the Activity 4 recording twice. Ask pupils to note two details, then prompt them to explain each detail using evidence from what they heard.
**Pupils:** Listen and identify details from the recording.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check the two details each pupil recorded from Student's Book p.73 Activity 4 against the spoken information, replaying Track 25 where necessary; record accuracy as the main Listening PBD evidence for SP 1.2.2. Then give pairs a description frame and assess their spoken descriptions separately for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.5.
**Pupils:** Confirm and correct two details heard in the recording, then describe a person or object from the listening in pairs.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to report one listening detail and explain which words helped them identify it.
**Pupils:** Recall one key listening detail.

**PBD:** Check specific details pupils identify from the Activity 4 recording (Class CD2 Track 25; TB p.111 verified) for main Listening SP 1.2.2; observe spoken descriptions of a person or object from the recording for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.5. Do not treat written answers alone as listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
