# English Year 4 Lesson 061 — Reading 12

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Reading 3.2.2. Complementary SP: Writing 4.1.2. References: SB p.43 Activities 1–2; TB p.70; CD1 Track 65. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Show a diary and discuss its purpose. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Follow Activity 1 with Track 65; pupils read Sabrina's diary. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Pairs locate details and complete Activity 2 using neat cursive writing. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Pupils discuss their birthday plans and check the written answers. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Pupils share one diary detail and one personal plan. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Reading 3.2.2 and Writing 4.1.2. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 61.
