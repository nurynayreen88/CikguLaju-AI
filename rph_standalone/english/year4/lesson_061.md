# English Year 4 — Lesson 061

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.43 Activities 1–2; TB p.70; CD1 Track 65; Year 4 SOW Lesson 61

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show a diary and ask what it is used for.
**Pupils:** Explain the purpose of a diary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview the diary questions, play Track 65 for Activity 1 and check pupils' understanding of Sabrina's plans.
**Pupils:** Read Sabrina's diary and listen to Track 65.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pairs to locate details and complete Activity 2 in cursive.
**Pupils:** Find diary details and write answers neatly in cursive.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a short discussion about birthday plans and check pupils' written responses against diary details.
**Pupils:** Check answers and discuss birthday plans.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask for one diary detail and one personal plan.
**Pupils:** Share a diary detail and a personal plan.

**PBD:** Check text-supported answers from Sabrina's diary and legibility of cursive writing (SP 3.2.2, 4.1.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
