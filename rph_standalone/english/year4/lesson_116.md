# English Year 4 — Lesson 116: Writing 22

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 4.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.82 Activity 2; TB pp.124–125; animal cards; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 116

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Brainstorm comparative adjectives for animals and dinosaurs.
**Pupils:** Suggest animal comparison words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model short and long comparative forms using Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Practise short and long comparative forms.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide a short comparative-writing checklist and ask pupils to draft animal description clues on cards.
**Pupils:** Write animal description clues on cards.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a clue-card exchange, ask groups to justify their guesses and guide revisions for spelling and clarity.
**Pupils:** Guess animals from clues and correct spelling.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share one corrected comparative sentence.
**Pupils:** Share an improved comparative sentence.

**PBD:** Check the accuracy of written comparative animal clues and spelling corrections against SP 4.2.4 and 4.3.2.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
