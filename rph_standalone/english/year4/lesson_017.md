# English Year 4 Lesson 17 — Listening 4

Topic: My Week. Main SP 1.2.2; complementary 1.1.1. Student Book p.15, Teacher Book p.32, CD1 Track 19. 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher shows subject flashcards. Pupils identify subjects and indicate preferences.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher models pronunciation and picture-word matching. Pupils repeat words and match pictures to words.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher plays Track 19 without visible lyrics, then replays for Activity 1. Pupils listen for school subjects and preferences.

Assessment (10 min): Teacher highlights rhyme 'too'/'you' and uses TB TPR activity. Pupils practise sounds and follow physical prompts.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a subject they like or are good at.

PBD: Teacher records listening accuracy and pronunciation attempts. Differentiation: allow chanting instead of singing and extend vocabulary with timetable subjects.
