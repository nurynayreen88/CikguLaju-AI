# English Year 4 — Lesson 017: Listening 4

**Topic:** My Week  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 1.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.15; TB p.32; CD1 Track 19; Year 4 SOW Lesson 17

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show school-subject flashcards and ask about preferences.
**Pupils:** Identify subjects and indicate preferences.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model pronunciation and picture-word matching.
**Pupils:** Repeat subject words and match pictures to words.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 19 without visible lyrics, then replay for Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Listen for school subjects and preferences.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the too/you rhyme, then lead the TB physical-response activity and check pupils' recognition of the rhyming words.
**Pupils:** Practise the rhyme and respond to physical prompts.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to share a school subject preference.
**Pupils:** Name a subject they like or are good at.

**PBD:** Record listening accuracy and pronunciation attempts against SP 1.2.2 and 1.1.1.  
**Differentiation:** Allow chanting instead of singing and extend vocabulary with timetable subjects.
