# English Year 4 — Lesson 124: Speaking 24

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_124_animal_preference_comparison_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.86 Activity 1; TB pp.128–129; animal picture cards; Year 4 SOW Lesson 124

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review animal names using TB warm-up.
**Pupils:** Name animals from picture cards.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the TB vocabulary prompts to elicit animal adjectives and check their meaning in a comparative sentence.
**Pupils:** Practise target animal vocabulary.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one animal comparison from the picture cards, then organise pair exchanges using Activity 1 and prompt pupils to expand their descriptions.
**Pupils:** Compare animals and describe them to a partner.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to state an animal preference with one reason, observe their use of comparative language and provide brief oral feedback.
**Pupils:** Exchange opinions using comparative language.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to express an animal preference and justify it with a comparative clue.
**Pupils:** Give one reason for an animal preference.

**PBD:** Observe pupils stating animal preferences and giving reasons for main Speaking SP 2.1.1; assess their spoken comparative descriptions of animals for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.5. Use SB Activity 1 without inventing a Workbook task.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
