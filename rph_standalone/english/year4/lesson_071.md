# English Year 4 — Lesson 071: Speaking 14

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.53 Activity 4; TB pp.82–83; CD1 Track 80

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Review containers such as a cup of tea and a bottle of water.
**Pupil action:** Name food and drink containers.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Follow TB Activity 4 with Track 80.
**Pupil action:** Listen to Track 80 and complete Activity 4.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model a short opinion about an eating habit, then organise pair discussion and prompt pupils to give reasons.
**Pupil action:** Discuss eating habits and give opinions in pairs.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Give pupils a sentence frame and ask them to write one accurate sentence about a partner's eating or drinking habits.
**Pupil action:** Write one sentence about a partner's habits.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Invite pupils to explain one reason a balanced diet matters, using a familiar food example.
**Pupil action:** Explain one reason to eat a balanced diet.

**PBD:** Observe opinions about eating habits and assess written sentences about partners against SP 2.1.1 and 4.2.3.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
