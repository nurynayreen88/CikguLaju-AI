# English Year 4 — Lesson 100: Writing 19

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 4.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.73 Activity 3; TB p.111

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask three short story questions and use pupils' answers to identify ownership expressions for revision.
**Pupils:** Recall the story through a short quiz.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model Whose and possessive pronouns.
**Pupils:** Practise Whose and possessive pronouns.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Contrast possessive apostrophe-s and plural -s.
**Pupils:** Distinguish possessive apostrophe-s from plural -s.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Activity 3, model checking an apostrophe in one possessive sentence, then ask partners to use a short punctuation checklist before revising their work.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 3 and check punctuation with a partner.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display one sentence with an incorrect possessive form and invite pupils to explain and correct it.
**Pupils:** Correct one possessive sentence.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' independently punctuated possessive sentences in SB Activity 3 for main Writing SP 4.3.1, including apostrophe-s and sentence punctuation. Check their identification of ownership clues in the printed story for complementary Reading SP 3.2.2. Do not invent a Workbook task.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
