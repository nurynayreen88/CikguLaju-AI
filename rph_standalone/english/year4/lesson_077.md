# English Year 4 — Lesson 077: Reading 15

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.130 CLIL 2; TB p.194; CD3 Track 38

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Sort foods into healthy and less healthy categories.
**Pupil action:** Sort foods by health value.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Follow TB Activity 1 with CD3 Track 38.
**Pupil action:** Read and listen to CLIL Activity 1 with Track 38.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model locating strong and half in the reading, then ask pupils to match target words to definitions using context clues.
**Pupil action:** Find words such as strong and half and match meanings.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Guide pupils through TB Activity 2 and elicit useful facts for the next food-health poster project.
**Pupil action:** Complete Activity 2 and collect facts for a poster.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Use the TB Before leaving review to check one food-health fact and one newly learned word.
**Pupil action:** Recall a fact about healthy food.

**PBD:** Check healthy-food text details and inference of unfamiliar words in CLIL Activities 1–2 against SP 3.2.2 and 3.2.3.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
