# English Year 4 — Lesson 146: Speaking 28

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 2.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.103 Activity 3; TB p.153; mixed-up dialogue lines; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 146

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review health vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall health words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Grammar Box and Activity 3 dialogue.
**Pupils:** Practise the model dialogue.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model how to sequence a short health conversation, then ask pairs to reorder dialogue lines including Really? and Oh poor you!.
**Pupils:** Reorder and rehearse dialogue lines.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pairs a health situation and a response checklist, then invite them to create, rehearse and perform a short dialogue.
**Pupils:** Perform an original dialogue.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share a useful response.
**Pupils:** Share a helpful response.

**PBD:** Observe use of clarification and appropriate responses in the health-dialogue role-play (SP 2.2.1, 1.2.5).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
