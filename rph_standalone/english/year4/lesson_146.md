# English Year 4 — Lesson 146: Speaking 28

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 2.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_146_health_dialogue_clarification_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.103 Activity 3; TB p.153; mixed-up dialogue lines; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 146

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review health vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall health words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Grammar Box and Activity 3 dialogue to model an appropriate response to a health complaint.
**Pupils:** Practise the model dialogue.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model how to sequence a short health conversation, then ask pairs to reorder dialogue lines including Really? and Oh poor you!.
**Pupils:** Reorder and rehearse dialogue lines.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.87, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.87, Activity 2; look at each health problem and write what the person should do.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to demonstrate one polite response to a health problem and explain when to use it.
**Pupils:** Share a helpful response.

**PBD:** Observe pupils asking for clarification and responding appropriately during health-dialogue role-play for main Speaking SP 2.2.1; check their understanding of spoken health advice and dialogue prompts for complementary Listening SP 1.2.5. Workbook p.87 is the main written advice practice, not a substitute for speaking or listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
