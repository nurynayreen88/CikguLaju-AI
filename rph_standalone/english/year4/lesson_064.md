# English Year 4 — Lesson 064: Language Awareness 4

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Complementary SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.48 Revision 4; TB pp.76–77; Year 4 SOW Lesson 64

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow the TB Warm-up to review Unit 4.
**Pupils:** Recall dates, ordinal numbers and celebration words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Revision 4 Activity 1 and provide targeted support.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 1 and compare answers.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide the remaining revision activities and optional practice.
**Pupils:** Complete remaining tasks and practise identified weak points.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead self-assessment of ordinal numbers, dates and celebrations.
**Pupils:** Review their answers and complete a self-assessment checklist.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare Unit 4 checklists with pupil responses and identify one priority for the next revision session.
**Pupils:** Set one personal revision goal.

**PBD:** Select relevant DSKP standards and success criteria before delivery; collect revision and checklist evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide examples, peer support and optional extension practice.
