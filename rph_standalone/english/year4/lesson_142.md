# English Year 4 — Lesson 142: Writing 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 4.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.99 Activities 1–2; TB p.146; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 142

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB warm-up.
**Pupils:** Recall famous people and descriptive words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain Activities 1 and 2, model the first response and draw attention to the Writing Tip before pupils complete the tasks.
**Pupils:** Complete Activities 1–2 and examine the Writing Tip.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a planning outline with appearance, achievements and clues, then ask pupils to plan a paragraph without revealing the person's name.
**Pupils:** Plan a description without naming the person.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to write their descriptions from the planning questions and check for clear clues, connected sentences and punctuation.
**Pupils:** Write and check a short description.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a gallery walk in which pupils guess the famous person and explain which written clue helped.
**Pupils:** Read classmates' descriptions and guess names.

**PBD:** Assess written descriptions of famous people for relevant details and whether peers can infer the identity (SP 4.2.1, 3.2.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
