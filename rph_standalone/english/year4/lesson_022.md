# English Year 4 — Lesson 022: Listening 5

**Topic:** My Week  
**Main SP:** Listening 1.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.3  
**References:** SB pp.16–17 Activity 1; CD1 Track 22; sentence strips  
**Duration:** 60 minutes

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play an activities-vocabulary review game.
**Pupils:** Respond to the prompt and recall key words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay Track 22; pupils listen for why the girls enter the wrong classroom.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and complete the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask how the girls feel and whether the problem is solved; pupils respond to supported questions.
**Pupils:** Practise the target skill using the stated source.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils arrange sentence strips in story order and compare with partners.
**Pupils:** Show evidence of learning, compare responses and correct errors.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils recount the story sequence and correct errors.
**Pupils:** Summarise a learning point or share a response.

**PBD:** Check task responses against Listening 1.2.5 and Listening 1.2.3, recording individual progress.  
**Differentiation:** Offer shorter or partly ordered sentence strips for support.
