# English Year 4 — Lesson 068: Writing 13

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 4.3.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.51 Activities 4–5; TB p.81; **Activity 4: CD1 Track 75; Activity 5: CD1 Track 76 (KPM SOW)**

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Display three challenging food words from the previous lesson and ask pupils to identify and correct spelling errors.
**Pupil action:** Correct challenging food spellings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Introduce the picnic context, model a food-list entry and guide TB Activity 5 while checking spelling.
**Pupil action:** Prepare picnic food lists and check spelling.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model two picnic shopping sentences using “some” and a suitable container phrase. Write one example together, then guide pupils to compose their own sentences.
**Pupil action:** Write shopping sentences using some and containers.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Display a short checklist for food spelling, commas in lists and appropriate use of “some”. Model checking one sentence, then organise peer feedback.
**Pupil action:** Check commas and spelling in a partner's list.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.40, Activity 3 according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.40, Activity 3; write sentences about the food in a fridge.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Display one deliberately incorrect shopping-list sentence and invite a pupil to explain and correct its spelling or punctuation.
**Pupil action:** Correct one picnic shopping sentence.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' own picnic shopping lists and food sentences for main Writing SP 4.3.2, including correct spelling and appropriate food quantities; check punctuation, especially commas, for complementary Writing SP 4.3.1. Workbook p.40 is main written practice but does not replace the picnic-list task.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
