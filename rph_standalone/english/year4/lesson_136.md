# English Year 4 — Lesson 136: Reading 26

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.96 Activity 1; TB p.142; sports pictures; Year 4 SOW Lesson 136

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce pictured sports and equipment.
**Pupils:** Name sports equipment from the pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Read Activity 1 for its main idea.
**Pupils:** Read the text and identify the main idea.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Locate specific details about the sports.
**Pupils:** Find specific details about the sports.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pairs compare answers and support them with text evidence.
**Pupils:** Compare answers using text evidence.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall one sport fact.
**Pupils:** State one fact from the reading.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against 3.2.2 and 3.2.1; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
