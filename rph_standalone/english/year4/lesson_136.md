# English Year 4 — Lesson 136: Reading 26

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_136_sports_equipment_reading_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.96 Activity 1; TB p.142; sports pictures; Year 4 SOW Lesson 136

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce pictured sports and equipment.
**Pupils:** Name sports equipment from the pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a gist question for Activity 1 and ask pupils to find evidence in the text.
**Pupils:** Read the text and identify the main idea.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate how to find one detail about sports equipment, then ask pupils to locate two more details in the text.
**Pupils:** Find specific details about the sports.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.83, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.83, Activity 1; find the sports words in the word search.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to report one fact about sports equipment and point to the sentence that supports it.
**Pupils:** State one fact from the reading.

**PBD:** Check pupils' text-supported answers about sports equipment and specific facts for main Reading SP 3.2.2; assess their identification of the overall main idea for complementary Reading SP 3.2.1. Workbook p.83 word search provides written vocabulary practice, not the sole evidence of reading comprehension.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
