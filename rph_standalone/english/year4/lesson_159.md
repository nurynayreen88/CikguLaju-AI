# English Year 4 — Lesson 159: Language Arts 30

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.84–88,128; resources/lesson_159_readers_theatre_pbd_DRAFT.md (supplementary pupil cards and individual PBD); Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 159

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show or demonstrate a short Readers' Theatre performance and ask pupils to notice voice, expression and turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Observe how Readers' Theatre is performed.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain guidebook steps 1–3, assign Readers' Theatre roles and check that each pupil understands their lines.
**Pupils:** Read the script and take assigned roles.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide groups through rehearsal steps 4–7, coach expressive reading and invite each group to perform.
**Pupils:** Rehearse and perform scenes in groups.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess group performance using guidebook p.128 form.
**Pupils:** Use the guidebook form to assess performances.

Pupils review their Readers' Theatre performance using the Teaching Guidebook assessment form on p.128; a Workbook activity is not prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide peers to give one specific performance strength and one achievable improvement target.
**Pupils:** Offer another group a medal and a mission.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' original and expressive Readers' Theatre performance using the guidebook criteria for main Language Arts SP 5.3.1; observe their independent reading of assigned script parts for complementary Reading SP 3.3.1. Use the Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook rather than an invented Workbook exercise.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
