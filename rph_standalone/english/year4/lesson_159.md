# English Year 4 — Lesson 159: Language Arts 30

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.84–88,128; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 159

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show or demonstrate a short Readers' Theatre performance and ask pupils to notice voice, expression and turn-taking.
**Pupils:** Observe how Readers' Theatre is performed.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow guidebook steps 1–3 and assign roles.
**Pupils:** Read the script and take assigned roles.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide groups through rehearsal steps 4–7, coach expressive reading and invite each group to perform.
**Pupils:** Rehearse and perform scenes in groups.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assess group performance using guidebook p.128 form.
**Pupils:** Use the guidebook form to assess performances.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give medals and missions to other groups.
**Pupils:** Offer another group a medal and a mission.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' reading and expressive performance in Readers' Theatre using the guidebook assessment form (SP 5.3.1, 3.3.1).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
