# English Year 4 — Lesson 141: Reading 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.100–101 Activity 1; TB pp.148–149; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 141

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Part One of the story from Lesson 125.
**Pupils:** Recall Part One events.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the five Part Two pictures, ask pupils to predict their order and guide them to match headings before reading.
**Pupils:** Order five pictures and match headings.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to read Part Two quietly, play the relevant story audio if available and guide them to check their predictions in Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Read Part Two and check predictions while listening.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB after-reading activity to locate supporting story details.
**Pupils:** Locate story details to justify answers.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss opinions of the story in pairs.
**Pupils:** Discuss an opinion of the story.

**PBD:** Check picture sequencing, matching of headings and story details justified from Part Two (SP 3.2.2, 3.3.1).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
