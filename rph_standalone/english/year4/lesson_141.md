# English Year 4 — Lesson 141: Reading 27

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.3.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_141_story_part_two_reading_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB pp.100–101 Activity 1; TB pp.148–149; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 141

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review Part One of the story from Lesson 125.
**Pupils:** Recall Part One events.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display the five Part Two pictures, ask pupils to predict their order and guide them to match headings before reading.
**Pupils:** Order five pictures and match headings.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to read Part Two quietly, play the relevant story audio if available and guide them to check their predictions in Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Read Part Two and check predictions while listening.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Workbook as the main written Assessment/PBD task: instruct pupils to complete Workbook p.82, Activity 1 according to the printed instructions. Check the Workbook answers as the main written exercise and assess reading comprehension for Main SP 3.2.2 through pupils' identification of story details and evidence from Part Two; use the picture sequencing and heading-matching responses as additional reading evidence for SP 3.3.1.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.82, Activity 1; read the story and complete the sentences using the past simple verbs.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise pairs to share opinions about Part Two and support each opinion with a story detail.
**Pupils:** Discuss an opinion of the story.

**PBD:** Check pupils' text-supported answers to Part Two story questions and matching of headings for main Reading SP 3.2.2; observe independent reading of the story for complementary Reading SP 3.3.1. Workbook p.82 is the main written past-simple story practice, not the sole reading evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
