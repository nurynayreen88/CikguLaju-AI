# English Year 4 — Lesson 134: Listening 26

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.95 Activity 4; TB pp.140–141; sample sports paragraph; Year 4 SOW Lesson 134

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review sports and superlative adjectives.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and recall vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the three interviews from Activity 4 and prepare a listening task.
**Pupils:** Follow the example and complete the first task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils identify information in the three interviews.
**Pupils:** Carry out the specified reading, writing, listening or speaking task.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use Grammar Boxes on pages 93 and 95 to write a paragraph about a liked or disliked sport.
**Pupils:** Provide an observable response and revise after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share one detail heard.
**Pupils:** Share a short answer or reflection.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against 1.2.2 and 4.3.3; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
