# English Year 4 — Lesson 134: Listening 26

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 1.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.95 Activity 4; TB pp.140–141; sample sports paragraph; Year 4 SOW Lesson 134

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display sports picture cards and elicit two accurate superlative descriptions before listening.
**Pupils:** Recall sports adjectives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the three interviews from Activity 4 and prepare a listening task.
**Pupils:** Listen to three interviews.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay each interview, pause after key information and ask pupils to record a relevant detail for each speaker.
**Pupils:** Record details from the interviews.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check pupils' recorded details from all three sports interviews as the main Listening PBD evidence for SP 1.2.2. Model a short paragraph using the Grammar Boxes on pages 93 and 95, then guide pupils to write and check their own paragraph about a liked or disliked sport for complementary Writing SP 4.3.3.
**Pupils:** Confirm and correct the three interview details from Activity 2, then write and check a short sports paragraph.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to report one accurate interview detail and identify the listening clue that supported it.
**Pupils:** Share one listening detail.

**PBD:** Check details from three sports interviews and the follow-up paragraph against SP 1.2.2 and 4.3.3.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
