# English Year 4 — Lesson 070: Listening 14

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 1.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB pp.52–53 Activities 1–3; TB pp.82–83; Warm up/Vocabulary CD1 Track 77; Activity 3 CD1 Track 79 (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Introduce health vocabulary using TB Warm-up and Track 77.
**Pupil action:** Recall health words from Track 77.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Read the Health Quiz questions aloud one at a time, allow pupils to select answers on the question-free worksheet and check understanding.
**Pupil action:** Listen to Health Quiz questions and choose answers.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model a question with how much, how many or how often, then organise pairs to discuss their Health Quiz answers.
**Pupil action:** Discuss answers using how much, how many and how often.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Play Track 79 for TB Activity 3, pause for key details and ask pupils to justify one answer from what they heard.
**Pupil action:** Listen to Track 79 and check details in Activity 3.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.41, Activity 1 according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.41, Activity 1; complete the food-container expressions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Invite pupils to share one healthy habit and give a short reason for their choice.
**Pupil action:** Share one healthy habit.

**PBD:** Check pupils' responses to spoken Health Quiz questions and their recognition of the speaker's intended meaning for main Listening SP 1.2.5; check specific details heard in CD1 Track 79 for complementary Listening SP 1.2.2. Workbook p.41 is written food-container practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
