# English Year 4 — Lesson 106: Language Arts 20

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.6–12; Chapters 1–2; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 106

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play animal sounds and elicit animal names.
**Pupils:** Identify animals from sound clues.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Chapters 1 and 2, drawing attention to picture panels, speech bubbles and key events.
**Pupils:** Read Chapters 1–2 and notice graphic-novel features.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give groups a beginning-middle-end prompt and invite them to retell key events from both chapters.
**Pupils:** Retell key events in groups.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask groups to predict the next chapter and justify their ideas with a detail from Chapters 1 or 2.
**Pupils:** Predict the next chapter and give reasons.

Pupils complete a relevant activity in the Workbook based on the vocabulary and language skills learned. Teacher checks pupils' answers and provides guidance where necessary.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to explain one prediction using a story clue.
**Pupils:** Share one prediction.

**PBD:** Observe retelling of Chapters 1–2 and whether pupils justify a prediction about the next chapter (SP 5.2.1, 2.1.4).  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
