# English Year 4 — Lesson 030: Writing 6

**Topic:** My Week  
**Main SP:** Writing 4.3.2  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.20 Activities 1–2; TB pp.38–39; Workbook p.15 Activity 3 (written option); CD1 Track 29 (SB p.20 Activity 2; confirmed by teacher 5 Nov 2026)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review spelling of household chores; leave difficult phrases on the board.
**Pupils:** Recall spelling of household chores.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one correctly spelled chore card, then ask pupils to prepare cards for the TB hot-card pantomime game.
**Pupils:** Write correctly spelled cards for the pantomime game.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain TB Activity 1 and guide pupils to read the prompts before completing the written task.
**Pupils:** Read and complete the written Activity 1 task.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the Student's Book p.20 Activity 2 recording **Class CD1 Track 29** as confirmed by the teacher and Teacher's Book p.39; never substitute SOW's CD2 Track 29. If the CD1 recording is unavailable in class, use the printed questions for reading/speaking practice only. Check spelling of chore cards and written Activity 1. For Listening SP 1.2.5, assess listening separately only when the pupils actually hear the correct CD1 recording.
**Pupils:** Listen to the teacher-confirmed CD1 Track 29 and respond to the Student's Book p.20 Activity 2 questions when the recording is available. Otherwise read and discuss the printed questions, **without counting this as evidence of Listening SP 1.2.5**. Check spelling and written Activity 1 answers. For optional written practice, complete Workbook p.15 Activity 3 using Phil and Gina's timetable to write *have to* or *don't have to* sentences; this supports Writing SP 4.3.2 and does not replace listening assessment.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the optional hopscotch review if time permits, then ask pupils to identify and correct one chore spelling error.
**Pupils:** Correct one spelling error.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Assess Writing SP 4.3.2 from correctly spelled chore cards, Activity 1 and optional Workbook p.15 Activity 3. For Listening SP 1.2.5, record achievement **only after pupils have actually listened to the correct CD1 Track 29** and responded appropriately. Do not mark Listening achieved from silent reading, role-play, or successful written work alone.  
**Support:** Use mixed-ability pairs and targeted feedback on high-frequency spelling.
