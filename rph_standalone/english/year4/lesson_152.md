# English Year 4 — Lesson 152: Reading 29

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_152_health_reading_dictionary_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB pp.106–107 Activities 1–2; TB pp.156–157; Vocabulary CD2 Track 75; Activity 1 CD2 Track 76; dictionaries; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 152

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show health picture prompts and elicit symptom words pupils will need for the listening activity.
**Pupils:** Recall health words.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate looking up one health-related word in a monolingual dictionary, then ask pupils to check two to four unfamiliar words and explain their meanings.
**Pupils:** Look up unfamiliar words.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm up and Vocabulary using CD2 Track 75.
**Pupils:** Listen and read for context.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play CD2 Track 76 for Activity 1, guide pupils to check their listening answers, then model one should or shouldn't sentence before Activity 2.
**Pupils:** Answer reading questions with evidence.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to explain one newly learned health word and use it in a meaningful sentence.
**Pupils:** Explain a new word.

**PBD:** Check pupils' answers supported by specific details in the health reading text for main Reading SP 3.2.2; assess appropriate use of a monolingual dictionary to check unfamiliar words for complementary Reading SP 3.2.4. CD2 Tracks 75 and 76 support the lesson but listening responses alone do not replace reading evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
