# English Year 4 — Lesson 117: Language Arts 22

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.33–34; Chapter 5; resources/lesson_117_mowgli_chapter5_response_pbd_DRAFT.md; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 117

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss characters, feelings and relationships.
**Pupils:** Discuss characters and their feelings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap Chapters 1–4 with two questions, then guide pupils through Chapter 5 and check a key event.
**Pupils:** Recall earlier chapters and read Chapter 5.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to predict Mowgli's next decision after page 63 and prompt them to explain their predictions using story events.
**Pupils:** Predict what will happen to Mowgli.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute five synopsis strips, guide pupils to reorder them and model a sentence describing Mowgli's feelings.
**Pupils:** Order five synopsis strips and describe his feelings.

Pupils complete a follow-up response to The Jungle Book using the Teaching Guidebook; no specific Workbook page is prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to describe a feeling about the story and give a reason.
**Pupils:** Share a response to the story.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' personal responses to The Jungle Book Chapter 5, including their interpretation of Mowgli's feelings with reasons, for main Language Arts SP 5.2.1; observe their spoken opinions and explanations for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.1. Use the graphic-novel guide, not an invented Workbook task.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
