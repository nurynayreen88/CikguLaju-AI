# English Year 4 — Lesson 117: Language Arts 22

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook pp.33–34; Chapter 5; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 117

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss characters, feelings and relationships.
**Pupils:** Discuss characters and their feelings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recap Chapters 1–4 and read Chapter 5.
**Pupils:** Recall earlier chapters and read Chapter 5.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs to predict Mowgli's next decision after page 63 and prompt them to explain their predictions using story events.
**Pupils:** Predict what will happen to Mowgli.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute five synopsis strips, guide pupils to reorder them and model a sentence describing Mowgli's feelings.
**Pupils:** Order five synopsis strips and describe his feelings.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share feelings about the story.
**Pupils:** Share a response to the story.

**PBD:** Observe story sequencing and pupils' descriptions of Mowgli's feelings with reasons against SP 5.2.1 and 2.1.1.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
