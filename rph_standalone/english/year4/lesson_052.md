# English Year 4 Lesson 52 — Writing 10

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Writing 4.2.4. Complementary SP: Writing 4.1.2. References: SB p.38 Activities 1–3; TB pp.64–65; CD1 Track 55. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Review Independence Day.

Activity 1 (15 min): Follow Vocabulary with Track 55 and model future plans.

Activity 2 (20 min): Use Grammar Box and Activity 3 to practise going to.

PBD (10 min): Pupils draw a parade costume and write three to five cursive sentences.

Closure (5 min): Review cursive handwriting.
