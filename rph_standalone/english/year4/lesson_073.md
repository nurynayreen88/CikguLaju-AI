# English Year 4 — Lesson 073: Writing 14

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 4.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.55; TB p.87; adapted Activity 2 table

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Lead TB Supermarket Frenzy and check that pupils name food items using accurate category vocabulary.
**Pupil action:** Recall food words in Supermarket Frenzy.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Categorise food words and introduce the adapted health table.
**Pupil action:** Sort food words and inspect the health table.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Demonstrate how to fill one row of the health table, then prompt pupils to express choices using or.
**Pupil action:** Complete the table and write choices using or.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model a sentence with enough and but, then guide pupils to describe yesterday's eating habits in writing.
**Pupil action:** Write sentences with enough and but about yesterday's habits.


**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.43, healthy-eating diary project, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.43, healthy-eating diary project; write a short diary entry about food choices.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Ask pupils to compare their draft and revised sentences and explain one improvement.
**Pupil action:** Improve one sentence after feedback.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' written healthy-eating table and linked sentences using or, enough and but for main Writing SP 4.2.5; check whether the sentences communicate relevant food choices for complementary Writing SP 4.2.3. Workbook p.43 diary is the main written practice and should be checked against its printed instructions.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
