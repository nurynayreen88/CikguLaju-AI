# English Year 4 — Lesson 067: Reading 13

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB pp.50–51 Activities 1 and 3; TB pp.79–80; monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Play TB Market Races to review food words.
**Pupil action:** Respond to prompts and activate prior knowledge.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Pairs find definitions of food vocabulary and noun phrases in dictionaries.
**Pupil action:** Follow the model and complete the first guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Pupils check meanings in SB and complete TB Activity 1.
**Pupil action:** Carry out the stated source-based activity and practise the main skill.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Complete TB Activity 3; compare food in the story with food at home.
**Pupil action:** Show evidence of learning and improve work after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Pupils write three newly learned words on exit cards.
**Pupil action:** Share one learning point or reflection.

**PBD:** Check pupil responses and products against 3.2.4 and 3.2.2; for teacher-selected standards, agree criteria before delivery.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
