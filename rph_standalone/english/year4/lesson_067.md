# English Year 4 — Lesson 067: Reading 13

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 3.2.4  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB pp.50–51 Activities 1 and 3; TB pp.79–80; monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Play TB Market Races to review food words.
**Pupil action:** Recall food words in Market Races.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Demonstrate how to locate a food word in a monolingual dictionary, then guide pairs to find definitions of selected food words and noun phrases.
**Pupil action:** Find definitions of food words and noun phrases.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Ask pupils to verify dictionary meanings against Student Book context and guide them through TB Activity 1.
**Pupil action:** Check meanings in the Student Book and complete Activity 1.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Guide TB Activity 3, then prompt pupils to compare food mentioned in the story with familiar food at home.
**Pupil action:** Compare food in the story with food at home.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.40, Activities 1–2 according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.40, Activities 1–2; identify food items and quantities from the kitchen pictures.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Distribute exit cards, ask pupils to record three new food words and check that they can explain one meaning.
**Pupil action:** Write three newly learned words on an exit card.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' use of a monolingual dictionary to determine unfamiliar food-word meanings for main Reading SP 3.2.4 and their text-supported answers about food and quantities for complementary Reading SP 3.2.2. Workbook p.40 provides main written practice alongside the Student's Book reading tasks.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
