# English Year 4 — Lesson 029: Reading 6

**Topic:** Social Studies (CLIL 1)  
**Main SP:** Reading 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** Reading 3.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.129 Activities 1–2; TB p.193; Warm up Track 35; Activity 1 Track 36 (KPM SOW; CD1 designation requires independent TB/audio verification); monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare pictures of Malaysian schools with the pupils' own school.
**Pupils:** Compare pictures of schools with their own school.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write library, country, special, strange and forest; pupils use monolingual dictionaries to check meanings.
**Pupils:** Use a dictionary for library, country, special, strange and forest.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm-up with Track 35 and Activity 1 with Track 36; pupils read the school texts.
**Pupils:** Read school texts while listening to Tracks 35–36.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activity 2 by modelling how to locate a detail in the text, then ask pairs to produce four accurate statements about their own school.
**Pupils:** Locate details and write or say four facts about their school. Pupils use the CLIL school-facts task to check their four facts against the source text; no specific Workbook page is prescribed.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share one fact from the school text and explain a word they checked in the dictionary.
**Pupils:** Share a fact about another school and a new word.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Check task-specific responses and note achievement of Reading 3.2.2 and Reading 3.2.4.  
**Support:** Divide three reading texts between groups if needed, then exchange information.
