# English Year 4 — Lesson 056

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 3.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.40 Activities 1–2; TB pp.66–67; Vocabulary CD1 Track 59 (KPM SOW); Year 4 SOW Lesson 56

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of celebrations and introduce the topic.
**Pupils:** Name familiar celebrations from pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Vocabulary with Track 59.
**Pupils:** Listen and match celebration words with pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign the two celebration texts to different groups and set a question about each festival's main features.
**Pupils:** Read an assigned text and identify its country.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model extracting one festival fact for Activity 2, then pair pupils from different texts to exchange information.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 2 and exchange answers with a partner.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.35, Story time reading-and-matching task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.35, Story time reading-and-matching task; read the story and match the details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to explain two celebration words and use one in a factual sentence.
**Pupils:** Sort vocabulary by celebration and share a response.

**PBD:** Check each pupil's identification of the main ideas and countries in the printed celebration texts for main Reading SP 3.2.1, and their answers to supporting-detail questions in Activity 2 for complementary Reading SP 3.2.2. Workbook p.35 is the main written practice, not the sole reading evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
