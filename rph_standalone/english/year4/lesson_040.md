# English Year 4 — Lesson 040: Reading 8

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Reading 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** Speaking 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.30 The Curse; TB p.52; Vocabulary CD1 Track 42 (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use pyramids and Tutankhamun pictures to introduce the setting.
**Pupils:** Identify pyramids and the historical setting.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use TB Background Note and Vocabulary with Track 42.
**Pupils:** Learn background words using Track 42.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to predict what Carter and Carnarvon found using picture clues, then assign silent reading of The Curse.
**Pupils:** Predict the discovery and read The Curse silently.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to check their predictions against the text, explain their evidence and discuss one interesting story detail.
**Pupils:** Check predictions with evidence from the story. Where a matching Workbook activity is available, pupils complete it to practise the language skills taught in this lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share a reaction to The Curse and justify it with one story detail.
**Pupils:** Share a reaction to the story.

**PBD:** Check pupils' prediction of The Curse, supporting reading details and spoken response (Reading 3.3.1; Speaking 2.1.4).  
**Differentiation:** Vary prediction question complexity.
