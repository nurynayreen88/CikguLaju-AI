# English Year 4 — Lesson 147: Reading 28

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 3.2.3  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.104 Activity 1; TB p.154; Activity 1 Track 72 (disc unspecified in SOW); enlarged story pictures; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 147

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce story pictures and predict events.
**Pupils:** Predict story events.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce should and ask pupils to predict from story pictures.
**Pupils:** Discuss the meaning of should.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a gist question before playing the story audio and check pupils' understanding of the main event.
**Pupils:** Listen and answer the gist question.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.90, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.90, Activity 1; read the short statements about health problems and match each one to the correct condition.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify one value shown in the story and cite the character action that demonstrates it.
**Pupils:** Share one story lesson.

**PBD:** Check inference of story vocabulary and listening gist, supported by story evidence (SP 3.2.3, 1.2.3).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
