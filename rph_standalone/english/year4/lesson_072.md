# English Year 4 — Lesson 072: Reading 14

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.55 Project Activities 1–2; TB p.87; CD1 Track 83

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Review and categorise food vocabulary.
**Pupil action:** Sort familiar foods into categories.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Assign food categories and guide groups to brainstorm relevant words before checking spelling.
**Pupil action:** Brainstorm category words before checking spelling.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Use Track 83 for Project Activity 1, model finding one supporting detail and ask pupils to locate another independently.
**Pupil action:** Read Project Activity 1 and locate details with Track 83.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Guide Project Activity 2 and demonstrate checking category spellings using the Student Book or a dictionary.
**Pupil action:** Complete Project Activity 2 and correct spelling using references.

Pupils complete a relevant activity in the Workbook based on the vocabulary and language skills learned. Teacher checks pupils' answers and provides guidance where necessary.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Invite pupils to share one challenging food word and explain how they checked its spelling.
**Pupil action:** Share a challenging food word and spell it.

**PBD:** Check reading answers from Project Activity 1 and spelling of food categories in Activity 2 against SP 3.2.2 and 4.3.2.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
