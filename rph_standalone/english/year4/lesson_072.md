# English Year 4 — Lesson 072: Reading 14

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.55 Project Activities 1–2; TB p.87; CD1 Track 83

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Review and categorise food vocabulary.
**Pupil action:** Sort familiar foods into categories.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Groups brainstorm words for food categories without initial spelling pressure.
**Pupil action:** Brainstorm category words before checking spelling.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Follow Project Activity 1 with Track 83 and locate reading details.
**Pupil action:** Read Project Activity 1 and locate details with Track 83.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Complete Project Activity 2 and correct category spellings using books or dictionaries.
**Pupil action:** Complete Project Activity 2 and correct spelling using references.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Pupils share a challenging word and its spelling.
**Pupil action:** Share a challenging food word and spell it.

**PBD:** Check pupil responses and products against 3.2.2 and 4.3.2; for teacher-selected standards, agree criteria before delivery.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
