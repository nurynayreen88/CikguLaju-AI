# English Year 4 — Lesson 060

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 2.1.4  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.42 Activity 1; TB pp.68–69; magazine pictures; Year 4 SOW Lesson 60

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review vocabulary from SB p.42.
**Pupils:** Recall celebration vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm-up and Activity 1 to model predictions.
**Pupils:** Predict what people will do in the pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to select magazine pictures and justify predictions using because.
**Pupils:** Choose pictures and explain predictions using because.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide a prediction checklist and guide pupils to attach pictures, write sentences using because and present their reasons.
**Pupils:** Write a correctly spelled prediction and present it to a group.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review spelling and reasons for predictions.
**Pupils:** Correct one word and share a reason.

**PBD:** Observe spoken predictions about celebrations with reasons and check spelling of written predictions (SP 2.1.4, 4.3.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
