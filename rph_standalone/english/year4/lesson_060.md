# English Year 4 Lesson 060 — Speaking 12

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Speaking 2.1.4. Complementary SP: Writing 4.3.2. References: SB p.42 Activity 1; TB pp.68–69; magazine pictures. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Review key vocabulary from SB p.42. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Follow TB Warm-up and Activity 1; pupils predict what people will do. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Pupils choose magazine pictures and explain predictions using because. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Pupils attach a picture, write a correctly spelled prediction and present in groups. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Pupils correct a word and share their reason. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Speaking 2.1.4 and Writing 4.3.2. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 60.
