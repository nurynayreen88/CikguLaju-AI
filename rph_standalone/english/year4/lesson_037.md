# English Year 4 — Lesson 037: Language Arts — Woodpecker

**Topic:** Woodpecker (Poem)  
**Main SP:** 5.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** CCL Poetry Guide pp.75, 79; Year 4 SOW Lesson 37

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce the poem with a picture puzzle.
**Pupils:** Use picture clues to predict the poem's topic.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow CCL Activity 2 steps 1–4 and guide discussion.
**Pupils:** Discuss images and vocabulary from the poem.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow CCL Activity 3 steps 1–6, modelling question words and dialogue.
**Pupils:** Practise wh-questions and act out the dialogue.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow CCL Activity 2 steps 7–10 and ask for reasons.
**Pupils:** Express simple opinions about the poem with reasons.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to share an idea from the poem.
**Pupils:** Share one idea or response to Woodpecker.

**PBD:** Observe the poem tasks against SP 5.3.1 and 4.2.1.  
**Differentiation:** Provide vocabulary prompts and invite confident pupils to recall more details.
