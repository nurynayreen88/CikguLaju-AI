# Lesson 037 — Woodpecker

Main: Language Arts 5.3.1. Complementary: Writing 4.2.1. Source: Year 4 SOW Lesson 37; CCL Poetry Guide pp.75,79.

Set induction (10m): Use picture puzzle to introduce the poem; pupils infer the topic.
Activity 1 (15m): Follow CCL Activity 2 steps 1–4; pupils discuss images and vocabulary.
Activity 2 (20m): Follow CCL Activity 3 steps 1–6; practise wh-questions and act out the dialogue.
PBD (10m): Follow CCL Activity 2 steps 7–10; pupils give simple opinions and reasons.
Closure (5m): Pupils share one idea from the poem.

Differentiation: Invite confident pupils to recall additional details. Status: DRAFT; guidebook and Word/PDF QA pending.
