# English Year 4 — Lesson 158: Writing 30

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 4.3.3  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_158_home_safety_poster_clarification_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.109 Activities 1–2; TB pp.160–161; Unit 10 safety poster; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 158

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the TB revision prompts to recall home-safety rules and check pupils' use of imperative language.
**Pupils:** Recall safety rules at home.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one concise home-safety instruction, then guide pupils to plan and draft poster instructions in their notebooks.
**Pupils:** Draft safety instructions in notebooks and check their meaning.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise poster-making for Activity 1, then facilitate group presentations for Activity 2 and check that instructions are understandable.
**Pupils:** Make safety posters and present them to groups.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Compare groups' original safety ideas and check punctuation.
**Pupils:** Identify original safety ideas and revise punctuation.

Pupils review their home-safety poster instructions against the lesson success criteria; no specific Workbook page is prescribed in the SOW.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide end-of-year self-assessment and help pupils choose one language skill to practise next.
**Pupils:** Complete an end-of-year self-assessment.

**PBD:** Assess the organisation, clarity and punctuation of pupils' written home-safety posters for main Writing SP 4.3.3; observe pupils' use of suitable clarification during spoken group explanations for complementary Speaking SP 2.2.2. Do not count the poster alone as evidence of the Speaking standard.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
