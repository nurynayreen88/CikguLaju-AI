# English Year 4 — Lesson 156: Speaking 30

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 2.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_156_board_game_clarification_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.108 Activity 1; TB p.159; Vocabulary CD2 Track 79; board-game instructions; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 156

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead a short word game and check pupils' recall of advice and home-safety vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall advice vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Vocabulary using CD2 Track 79 and the Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Listen to vocabulary recording.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain and demonstrate the Activity 1 board-game rules, then organise group play and support pupils who need clarification.
**Pupils:** Play the board game.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.89, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.89, Activity 2; look at the picture and answer the questions about what each person should or shouldn't do.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify one clarification phrase that helped their group follow the game rules.
**Pupils:** Reflect on group communication.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' ability to ask for and provide clarification during the board game for main Speaking SP 2.2.2; check their understanding of spoken game instructions for complementary Listening SP 1.2.4. CD2 Track 79 supports vocabulary; Workbook p.89 is written advice practice, not speaking or listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
