# English Year 4 — Lesson 156: Speaking 30

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 2.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.108 Activity 1; TB p.159; board-game instructions; Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 156

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play a word game to review vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall advice vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Vocabulary using CD2 Track 79 and the Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Listen to vocabulary recording.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain and demonstrate the Activity 1 board-game rules, then organise group play and support pupils who need clarification.
**Pupils:** Play the board game.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite groups to give playful advice in the optional task and ask them to restate a rule when misunderstandings arise.
**Pupils:** Give playful advice and explain rules.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Reflect on effective communication.
**Pupils:** Reflect on group communication.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' comprehension of board-game instructions and use of suitable advice or clarification during group play (SP 2.2.2, 1.2.4).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
