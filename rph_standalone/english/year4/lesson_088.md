# English Year 4 — Lesson 088: Reading 17

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.62 Activities 1–2; TB pp.96–97; CD2 Track 9

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review vocabulary with Track 9.
**Pupils:** Recall transport words from Track 9.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show pictures of tuk-tuks, gondolas and snowmobiles.
**Pupils:** Identify tuk-tuks, gondolas and snowmobiles.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Groups read one assigned vehicle text and complete Activity 2.
**Pupils:** Read an assigned vehicle text and complete Activity 2.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils compare experiences and transport preferences.
**Pupils:** Compare transport experiences and preferences.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall one detail and prepare building research.
**Pupils:** Recall a detail from the reading.

**PBD:** Check comprehension of the vehicle texts and spoken comparisons of transport preferences against SP 3.2.2 and 2.1.5.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
