# English Year 4 — Lesson 2: Speaking

**Unit/Topic:** 1 — Where are you from?  
**Main SK/SP:** Speaking 2.1.5  
**Complementary SK/SP:** Listening 1.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Learning objective:** Pupils describe two people or flags using suitable statements and follow two supported English instructions.
**Success evidence:** Teacher checklist: flag identified, two spoken descriptions attempted, two supported instructions followed.
**Materials:** Student's Book p.5, Teacher's Book p.18, Activity 3 and speaking role-play Activity 2; other materials specified in each activity.  
**Audio:** CD1 Track 4 (Activity 3 dialogue); CD1 Track 3 (post-lesson song).

## Set Induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Match flags to country names; demonstrate each instruction in English before asking pupils to follow.
**Pupils:** Match each displayed country flag to its name and follow the teacher's spoken instructions.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the memory-game model on CD1 Track 4 (Student's Book p.5 Activity 3). Demonstrate how to describe a person using their country and ask a partner to identify the person.
**Pupils:** Repeat the recorded exchange, then take turns describing one person and guessing who their partner has described.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a strict short drawing time; pupils select a country flag and practise Activity 2 in pairs using introductions and descriptions. Keep flags for later lessons.
**Pupils:** Draw one flag within the time limit; use it in Activity 2 to exchange introductions and describe a partner's chosen country.

## PBD / Reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Check whether pupils can describe a person or flag and follow two-step instructions; offer paired speaking before individual responses where needed.
**Pupils:** Give a short description of a partner and follow two demonstrated English instructions.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.4, Activity 2, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.4, Activity 2; answer the questions about countries and nationalities.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay CD1 Track 3 and check pupils' recall of one country name through a short oral response.
**Pupils:** Sing along to CD1 Track 3 and name one country.

## Differentiation and assessment
Provide visual cues, demonstration and paired rehearsal for pupils needing support; extend with additional descriptions, details or independent language for pupils ready for challenge. Record evidence for main SP 2.1.5 and complementary Listening 1.2.4.
