# English Year 4 — Lesson 089: Writing 17

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 4.3.3  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.131 CLIL 3; TB p.195; Lesson 84 building plans

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review descriptions and earlier writing plans.
**Pupils:** Recall descriptions of famous buildings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to review their building research notes and images, checking that each selected fact supports the planned description.
**Pupils:** Review building research and pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a topic sentence about a famous building, then guide pupils to draft a paragraph using their research notes.
**Pupils:** Draft a paragraph describing a famous building.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Provide a peer-review checklist for spelling, punctuation and factual descriptions, then ask pupils to revise their paragraphs.
**Pupils:** Check spelling, punctuation and descriptive details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Share and display a revised description.
**Pupils:** Display and read a revised description.

**PBD:** Assess a famous-building paragraph for accurate details, spelling and punctuation against SP 4.3.3 and 4.2.4.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
