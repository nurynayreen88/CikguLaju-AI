# English Year 4 — Lesson 098: Speaking 19

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.71 Activities 2–3; TB pp.108–109; Song replay Track 21; Activity 3 Track 22 (KPM SOW; CD2 designation pending independent TB/audio verification)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay Track 21 and mime the actions.
**Pupils:** Mime actions heard in Track 21.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review helping-out verbs and model legible cursive joins before pupils write two action phrases.
**Pupils:** Practise the vocabulary and cursive writing.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 22, model a present-continuous sentence and check pupils' spoken responses against the recording.
**Pupils:** Use Track 22 to practise present continuous sentences.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a mime-and-describe pair task, prompting pupils to distinguish actions happening now from past experiences.
**Pupils:** Mime and describe experiences of helping others.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to contrast one present-continuous sentence with a past-action sentence and check object-pronoun use.
**Pupils:** Review a tense form and an object pronoun.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' spoken descriptions of helping actions and experiences for main Speaking SP 2.1.5; assess relevant personal responses in partner exchanges for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.2. Tracks 21 and 22 support the lesson, but their CD designation remains unverified.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
