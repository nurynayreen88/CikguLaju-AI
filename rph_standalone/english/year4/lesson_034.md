# English Year 4 — Lesson 034: Speaking 7

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Speaking 2.1.2  
**Complementary SP:** Reading 3.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.27 Activity 2; TB pp.48–49; monolingual dictionaries

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Ancient Egypt theme with pictures and key words.
**Pupils:** Recall Ancient Egypt vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate how to look up an unfamiliar word from the previous lesson, then support pupils as they verify meanings in dictionaries.
**Pupils:** Check unfamiliar words in a monolingual dictionary.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss ancient cultures in and around Malaysia and model past-tense statements.
**Pupils:** Discuss ancient cultures and make past-tense statements.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a simple-past sentence and organise pairs to describe past experiences or cultures before presenting their ideas.
**Pupils:** Describe a past experience or culture with a partner.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.24, Let's play question prompts, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.24, Let's play question prompts; ask and answer questions about yesterday.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead the optional TB line-jumping game to check pupils' understanding of past-tense statements.
**Pupils:** Practise past-time expressions in the optional game.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Check task-specific responses and note achievement of Speaking 2.1.2 and Reading 3.2.4.  
**Support:** Focus on meaningful past-time context before complex grammar terminology.
