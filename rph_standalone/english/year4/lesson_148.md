# English Year 4 — Lesson 148: Writing 28

**Topic:** What's the matter?  
**Main SP:** 4.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.105 Activity 3; TB p.155; resources/lesson_148_health_text_messages_pbd_DRAFT.md (original message worksheet and PBD); Primary Year 4 SOW Lesson 148

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play hot-card pantomime to review health words.
**Pupils:** Act out and name health problems.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display a model text-message conversation and highlight the symptom, reply and polite expression.
**Pupils:** Identify an offer and a reason in the model messages.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a brief exchange about an illness, then guide pairs through Activity 3 to plan their own messages with an appropriate reply.
**Pupils:** Plan a message exchange with a health problem, offer and reason.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.92, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. Assess pupils' own written sentences or text for the main Writing SP; do not rely on isolated vocabulary answers alone.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.92, Activity 2; look at the health problems and write sentences using should or shouldn't.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to read a revised health message and explain one change made after peer feedback.
**Pupils:** Read a revised message aloud.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' written health text-message exchanges for a clear offer, reason and suitable response for main Writing SP 4.2.2; check the relevance and clarity of the health information conveyed for complementary Writing SP 4.2.1. Workbook p.92 advice sentences are written follow-up practice, not a replacement for the message exchange.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
