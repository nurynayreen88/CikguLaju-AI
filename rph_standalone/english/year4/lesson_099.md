# English Year 4 — Lesson 099: Reading 19

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.72, Activity 1 (camping story); TB p.110, Activity 1 — Class CD2 Track 24; Year 4 SOW Lesson 99

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce camping using pictures or objects.
**Pupils:** Describe camping pictures and objects.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set a gist question for SB p.72 Activity 1; play TB-prescribed Class CD2 Track 24 as pupils follow the printed camping story, then guide them to reread for its main event.
**Pupils:** Listen to CD2 Track 24 while following the printed story, then reread Activity 1 and identify its main event.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one 'Whose ...?' question, then ask pupils to find ownership clues in the camping story and explain how they know.
**Pupils:** Identify Whose questions and ownership clues.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pairs two story questions and prompt each pair to point to a sentence or picture supporting its answer.
**Pupils:** Answer story questions and explain evidence.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to name an item and its owner using a complete sentence.
**Pupils:** Name an item and its owner.

**PBD:** Observe independent reading of the camping story and pupils' use of ownership clues for main Reading SP 3.3.1; use one oral gist question about the CD2 Track 24 story to gather listening-comprehension evidence for complementary Listening SP 1.2.3. Do not infer listening achievement from written answers alone.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
