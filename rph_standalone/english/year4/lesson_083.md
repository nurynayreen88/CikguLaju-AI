# English Year 4 — Lesson 083: Reading 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.60–61 Activities 1 and 3; TB pp.94–95; Vocabulary CD2 Track 5; Activity 1 CD2 Track 6 (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review vocabulary with Track 5.
**Pupils:** Recall words heard in Track 5.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set the Activity 1 listening question, play Track 6 and check pupils' answers against details they heard.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 6 and answer Activity 1.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model finding a supporting detail in the text, then guide pupils through Activity 3.
**Pupils:** Read the text and complete Activity 3.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise pairs to discuss a recent school trip, prompting them to reuse relevant language from the text.
**Pupils:** Discuss a school trip using details from the text.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.51, the transport and timetable task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.51, the transport and timetable task; match transport choices and read timetable details.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to cite one school-trip detail from the text.
**Pupils:** Share one fact from the reading.

**PBD:** Check text-supported transport and timetable answers for main Reading SP 3.2.2. Check understanding of the overall message heard in CD2 Track 6 for complementary Listening SP 1.2.1. Workbook p.51 is written reading practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
