# English Year 4 — Lesson 035: Reading 7

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Reading 3.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** Listening 1.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.28 Activity 1; TB p.50; **Activity 1 CD1 Track 39; Vocabulary CD1 Track 38** (KPM SOW)

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils what they did yesterday and elicit simple past responses.
**Pupils:** Answer what they did yesterday using past tense.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play CD1 Track 39 with books closed; pupils listen to Greg and Anna and respond to Activity 1.
**Pupils:** Listen to Greg and Anna on Track 39 and respond.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to reread the printed dialogue and identify the words that confirm or correct their listening answers.
**Pupils:** Read the printed dialogue to check listening answers.

## Reinforcement and PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Vocabulary using CD1 Track 38; pupils identify the dialogue's main idea.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 38 and identify the dialogue's main idea.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.23, Our world Ancient Egypt vocabulary task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.23, Our world Ancient Egypt vocabulary task; complete the Ancient Egypt vocabulary exercise.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead a past-tense verb review game and ask pupils to use one verb in a complete sentence.
**Pupils:** Review past-tense verbs in a game.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Check pupils' identification of the main idea in the printed Greg-and-Anna dialogue for Reading 3.2.1 and their responses to the spoken dialogue for Listening 1.2.3. Workbook vocabulary responses are supporting written practice.  
**Support:** Vary the number and complexity of detail questions to match pupils' needs.
