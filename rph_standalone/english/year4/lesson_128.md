# English Year 4 — Lesson 128: Language Awareness 7

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Complementary SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_128_unit8_teacher_selected_revision_pbd_DRAFT.md; Unit 8 revision; TB activities 1–3; SOW self-assessment; Year 4 SOW Lesson 128

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the TB warm-up to elicit animal adjectives and check one comparative form before revision.
**Pupils:** Recall animal vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the first revision item and explain the instructions for Activities 1–3, then monitor pupils' independent answers.
**Pupils:** Complete Unit 8 revision Activities 1–3.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Group pupils according to the language needs observed in their revision answers and provide targeted practice on animal vocabulary or comparative forms.
**Pupils:** Practise language targets identified by the teacher.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through the Unit 8 self-assessment and record the skills they identify as difficult.
**Pupils:** Complete self-assessment and identify difficulties.

Pupils complete the Unit 8 self-assessment and revise a language point they find difficult; no specific Workbook page is prescribed in the SOW.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils to choose a specific Unit 8 revision goal.
**Pupils:** State one revision target.

**PBD:** Assess the source-based task against Teacher-selected DSKP SP and Teacher-selected DSKP SP; choose relevant criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
