## Individual teacher PBD
**Language Arts 5.2.1:** Gives a personal response supported by a relevant event from the approved story. Record Independent / With prompts / Not yet.
**Writing 4.2.4:** Writes understandable original descriptive sentences relevant to the chosen character or event. Record 3 clear / 1–2 clear / Needs support.
Teacher evidence or quoted pupil sentence: __________

Support: Story-event cards, word bank and sentence starters. Extension: Compare two characters' possible feelings with evidence.

## Assessment guidance
There is no fixed correct personal opinion. Accept a coherent response supported by an event that actually appears in the approved edition. Do not infer individual Writing achievement from a group poster alone.
