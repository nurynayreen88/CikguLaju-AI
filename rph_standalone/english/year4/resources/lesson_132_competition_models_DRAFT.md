# Lesson 132 — Mini-competition writing models (teacher-created DRAFT)

The Year 4 SOW calls for a **model invitation**, a **model short report**, and an actual mini-competition. The examples below are original classroom models, not textbook passages.

## Model invitation
**Come to our Tallest Tower Challenge!**
Please join our class challenge on Thursday at 10 a.m. in Room 4.
Bring five books. We will build towers in teams.
Can you make the tallest tower? We hope you can join us!

## Model response
Thank you for inviting me. I would love to join your challenge.

## Model report (illustrative results only)
Our class held a tower challenge. Team A built a tall tower, but Team B built the tallest tower. Team C made the shortest tower. Everyone worked carefully, and we enjoyed the activity.

## Pupil writing tasks
1. Choose a simple, safe classroom challenge; decide its name, place and time.
2. Write an invitation. A partner replies to accept or decline politely.
3. Hold the challenge and record **real** group results.
4. Write a short paragraph with **and**, **but** or **because** and a superlative adjective. Use real results, not the sample's fictional results.
5. Revise the paragraph after feedback.

**PBD:** Check offer/invitation and response (4.2.2), connected report (4.2.5), and accurate superlatives.
