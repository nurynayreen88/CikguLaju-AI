# Lesson 118 — Listening and speaking pack (DRAFT)

**Topic:** Amazing animals | **Main SP:** 1.3.1 | **Complementary SP:** 2.1.4

Teacher-created material; no Student's Book or Workbook page is required for this activity. Check final alignment with the official SOW and DSKP before approval.

## Teaching materials
- Two pictures labelled *Malayan tiger* and *Malayan tapir* (teacher-selected, suitable for classroom display).
- Board word bank: forest, stripes, nose, danger, protect, habitat.
- A copy of the listening script for the teacher; pupils receive the question sheet, not the script, before listening.

## Teacher-read listening script (read twice, at natural pace)
“Two animals live in Malaysian forests. The Malayan tiger has stripes. It needs a safe forest to find food and raise its young. The Malayan tapir has a long nose and a black-and-white body. Both animals need healthy forests. When forests become smaller, animals may find it harder to get food and stay safe. People can help by keeping forests clean and protecting animal habitats. We can also learn about wildlife and tell other people why it matters.”

## Pupil assessment sheet
**A. Listen for the main message (first listening).** Circle one answer.
1. The talk is mainly about: (a) buying pets (b) protecting forest animals (c) visiting a zoo.

**B. Infer meaning from context (second listening).** Circle the best meaning and underline the clue you heard.
2. “Habitat” means: (a) the place where an animal lives (b) an animal's food (c) an animal's colour.
3. “Protect” means: (a) keep safe (b) make noisy (c) make smaller.
4. Write one phrase from the talk that helped you understand *habitat* or *protect*.

**C. Pair speaking task.** Answer your partner in complete sentences.
5. Why should we protect forest animals? Give one reason.
6. What is one action people can take to help? Explain your choice.
Sentence starters: “We should protect them because …” / “We can … because …”

## Answer guide
1. (b). 2. (a); clues include “live in Malaysian forests” and “animal habitats”. 3. (a); clues include “stay safe” and “protecting animal habitats”. 4. Accept a relevant phrase from the script. 5–6. Accept a sensible reason/action supported by the listening; do not require one fixed response.

## PBD observation (individual)
- **Main Listening 1.3.1:** Correctly infers meanings of 2 target words using context. Record 0, 1 or 2; ask pupils to identify a context clue.
- **Complementary Speaking 2.1.4:** Expresses an understandable reason or opinion and provides relevant support. Record: Independent / With prompting / Not yet demonstrated.
- **Support:** Replay the teacher reading, provide pictures or a word bank, and assess the same target skill without changing the answer.
- **Extension:** Ask pupils to explain why a forest habitat matters to more than one animal.
