# Lesson 153 — health advice poster planner (DRAFT)

Original non-textbook scaffold for SOW Lesson 153. This is classroom language practice, not personalised medical advice.

**Poster title:** Healthy Habits

**Planning grid:** Body part | Healthy habit | Sentence with should / shouldn't

**Model sentences:** “We should brush our teeth every day.” / “We should wash our hands before eating.” / “We shouldn't share toothbrushes.”

**Draft checklist:** I planned my poster before writing. / I labelled the body parts. / I checked spelling in a dictionary. / My advice is easy to understand. / I improved one sentence after feedback.

**PBD:** Assess planning, drafting and revision (4.3.3), plus spelling of high-frequency words (4.3.2). Teacher checks the appropriateness of health statements before display.
