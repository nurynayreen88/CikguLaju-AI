# Lesson 153 — health advice poster planner (DRAFT)

Original non-textbook scaffold for SOW Lesson 153. This is classroom language practice, not personalised medical advice.

**Poster title:** Healthy Habits

**Planning grid:** Body part | Healthy habit | Sentence with should / shouldn't

**Model sentences:** “We should brush our teeth every day.” / “We should wash our hands before eating.” / “We shouldn't share toothbrushes.”

**Draft checklist:** I planned my poster before writing. / I labelled the body parts. / I checked spelling in a dictionary. / My advice is easy to understand. / I improved one sentence after feedback.

**PBD:** Assess planning, drafting and revision (4.3.3), plus spelling of high-frequency words (4.3.2). Teacher checks the appropriateness of health statements before display.


## Printable pupil poster and assessment sheet
Pupil: __________ Class: __________
Poster title: __________
Plan three advice sentences. For each, name a body part or health habit and use should or shouldn't.
1. Topic: ______ Advice: ______
2. Topic: ______ Advice: ______
3. Topic: ______ Advice: ______
Draw or label two relevant body parts. Write the final poster, check two words in a dictionary, and improve one sentence after partner feedback.

## Teacher PBD rubric
Writing SP 4.3.3: Evidence of planning, drafting and revising. Record 3 stages / 2 stages / Needs support.
Writing SP 4.3.2: Correct spelling of two checked high-frequency words. Record 0, 1 or 2.
Content: Three understandable health-advice sentences including should and shouldn't.
Example: We should wash our hands before eating. We should brush our teeth every day. We shouldn't share toothbrushes.
Support: Word bank and labelled pictures. Extension: Add a reason with because.
Teacher checks accuracy and SOW/DSKP alignment before approval. Status: DRAFT.
