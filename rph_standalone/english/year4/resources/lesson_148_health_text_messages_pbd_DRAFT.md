# Lesson 148 — Health text-message writing pack (DRAFT)
Topic: What's the matter? | Main Writing SP 4.2.2 | Complementary Writing SP 4.2.1
Supplementary original resource for SB p.105 Activity 3 and Workbook p.92 Activity 2. Does not replace printed tasks.

## Model exchange
Pupil A: Hi. I have a headache, so I cannot join the game.
Pupil B: Oh, poor you! Would you like me to bring your homework?
Pupil A: Yes, please. That would help because I need to rest.
Pupil B: No problem. I hope you feel better soon.

## Pupil worksheet
Name: ______ Partner: ______
Situation: Your friend has a stomach ache and cannot come to school.
1. Write a message describing the problem and why your friend needs help.
2. Write a polite offer of help in a reply.
3. Write a response accepting or declining the offer with a reason.
4. Check that the message is clear, relevant and polite. Revise one sentence.
5. Swap roles. Write a second exchange for a different problem chosen by your teacher.

## Assessment and sample responses
Main SP 4.2.2: Check written offer, reason and suitable response. Record Complete / Partly complete / Needs support.
Complementary SP 4.2.1: Check that the messages communicate relevant information clearly. Record Clear / Developing / Needs support.
Sample offer: Would you like me to bring your homework? Sample response: Yes, please, because I need to rest.
Accept other sensible and polite responses. Do not require identical wording.
Support: Provide message starters and a word bank. Extension: Write a polite refusal with a reason.
Teacher verifies SOW/DSKP and printed book alignment before approval. Status: DRAFT.
