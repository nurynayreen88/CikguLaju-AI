# Lesson 044 — Past-tense story sequencing cards (teacher-created DRAFT)

The Year 4 SOW permits a short story about a Malaysian/local historical figure **or** recycling earlier Ancient Egypt language. This resource uses a **fictional museum visit** to avoid presenting invented historical events as fact. Teachers can replace it with researched local history.

## Jumbled note cards (print and cut)
- Card A: The class visited a history museum last Saturday.
- Card B: The guide showed an old photograph and a map.
- Card C: A pupil asked where the people in the photograph lived.
- Card D: The guide explained what the old building was used for.
- Card E: The class thanked the guide and went back to school.

**Teacher sequence:** A, B, C, D, E. Shuffle before distributing.

## Speaking roles
Narrator 1: opening and sequence. Narrator 2: closing. Actors: guide, pupil and classmate. Pupils **use note cards**, not a memorised script.

## Prompt language
First, we visited ... / Next, the guide showed ... / Then, a pupil asked ... / Finally, we thanked ...

## PBD checklist
Can the pupil narrate at least two events in order? Use a past-tense verb? Participate in a short group story? Ask or answer a simple question about the past?

**Status:** Original fictional classroom scenario; DRAFT. Historical accuracy is not implied. Check fit with the chosen lesson theme and obtain teacher approval.
