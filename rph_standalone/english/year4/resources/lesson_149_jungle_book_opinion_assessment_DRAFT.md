# Lesson 149 — Literature response and writing PBD (DRAFT)

**Topic:** The Jungle Book | **Main SP:** 5.2.1 | **Complementary SP:** 4.3.1
Teacher-created supplementary assessment; use alongside the approved graphic novel and Teaching Guidebook p.73, not as a substitute for the source story.

## Pupil response card
Name: __________ Date: __________
1. Would you prefer to live in the village or the jungle? Circle one: VILLAGE / JUNGLE.
2. Complete: I would choose the __________ because __________.
3. Think about Mowgli. What do you think he should choose? I think Mowgli should __________ because __________.
4. Find one event or detail from the story that supports your view. Write it in your own words: __________.
5. Write two connected sentences explaining how Mowgli might feel about this choice: __________.
6. Read your sentences again. Check capitals, full stops and question marks where needed. Correct one punctuation error if you find it.

## Partner discussion
Tell a partner your opinion and a story-based reason. Ask: Why do you think that? Listen and respond respectfully.

## Teacher PBD observation
- Main Language Arts SP 5.2.1: Personal response is clear and supported by at least one relevant detail from the story. Record Independent / Prompted / Not yet demonstrated.
- Complementary Writing SP 4.3.1: Pupil checks and uses appropriate basic punctuation in the final written response. Record Secure / Developing / Needs support.
- Record pupil evidence: __________. Give one specific next step: __________.
- Support: Provide sentence starters and point to a familiar story event. Extension: Compare how two characters might feel differently.

## Answer guidance
There is no single correct preference. Accept village or jungle if supported by a coherent reason. Story details must be checked against the approved edition; do not invent quotations or mark unsupported invented events as correct.
