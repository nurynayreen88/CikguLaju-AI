# Lesson 039 — past-action speaking cards (DRAFT)

Original teacher-created prompts for SOW Lesson 39; no official audio required.

**Cut-out cards:** opened a book / walked to the door / picked up a pencil / wrote a word / sat on a chair / drew a picture / put a bag on the table / closed a window.

**Sequence task:** Pupil A silently mimes three actions in order. Pupil B gives instructions to repeat the sequence: “First, pick up the pencil. Next, open the book. Finally, sit down.”

**Hot-seat questions:** What did you do first? Did you open the book? What happened next? What did you do yesterday?

**PBD:** Can pupils give a longer ordered sequence of basic instructions (2.1.3) and ask about or describe past experiences (2.1.2)? Use sentence frames for support. Do not encourage unsafe actions.
