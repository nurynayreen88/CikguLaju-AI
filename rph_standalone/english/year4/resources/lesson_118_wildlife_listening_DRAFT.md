# Lesson 118 — Teacher-read wildlife listening script (DRAFT)

**Purpose:** Year 4 Listening 1.3.1 (infer unfamiliar words from topic clues) and Speaking 2.1.4 (give reasons for predictions). SOW permits a teacher-prepared listening passage about Malaysian wildlife. **Original sample; not a transcript of an existing MP3.** No downloadable track is implied.

## Teacher-read script
Malaysia is home to many amazing animals. The Malayan tiger is bigger than a house cat. It lives in forests and needs a safe place to hunt. The orangutan has long arms and moves through the trees. Both animals need healthy forests.

Some wild animals are **endangered**. This means there are fewer of them than before, and they may disappear if people do not protect them. When forests become smaller, animals have less space to live. We can help by caring for forests and never buying products made from protected wild animals.

## Before listening
Show a tiger and an orangutan. Ask: Which animal is bigger? Which one uses long arms to move through trees? Why might animals need large forests?

## First listening — gist
Ask: What is the passage mainly about? (Malaysian wildlife and protecting animals.)

## Second listening — context clues
Ask pupils to infer **endangered** from “fewer of them than before” and **protect** from the surrounding examples. Replay by rereading the relevant sentences, not by pretending an MP3 exists.

## Pair speaking and PBD
- Predict: What might happen if forests become smaller? Give a reason.
- Say one action people can take to protect animals.
- Record whether pupils infer a meaning using a clue and justify a simple prediction.
