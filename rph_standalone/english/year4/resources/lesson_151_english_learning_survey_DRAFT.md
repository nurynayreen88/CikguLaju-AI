# Lesson 151 — English-learning advice survey (DRAFT)

Original non-textbook speaking resource aligned to SOW Lesson 151.

**Pair questions:** What should we do to learn new words? Should we read English stories? How can we practise speaking? What should we do when we do not understand a word?

**Response frames:** “We should ___ because ___.” / “We shouldn't ___ because ___.” / “Could you repeat the question, please?”

**Survey grid:** Partner | One useful learning habit | Reason | Question I asked

**Group report:** “Three pupils think we should ___ because ___.”

**PBD:** Express opinions with reasons (2.1.1) and respond to longer supported questions (1.2.5). Provide paper-based options; no internet or paid learning app is required.


## Printable assessment sheet
Pupil: __________ Partner: __________
1. How do you learn new English words?
2. What should pupils do when they do not understand a word?
3. How can you practise speaking English at home?
4. Why is reading English stories useful?

Record your partner's answers. Then give two suggestions using 'should' and 'shouldn't' and explain why.

## Teacher assessment record
Speaking SP 2.1.1: Pupil gives two relevant suggestions and at least one reason. Record Independent / Prompted / Not yet.
Listening SP 1.2.5: Pupil responds relevantly to the partner's answers and follow-up question. Record Independent / Prompted / Not yet.
Sample acceptable response: We should read short stories because we learn new words.
Support: Provide question cards and sentence starters. Extension: Ask pupils to justify a different learning strategy.
