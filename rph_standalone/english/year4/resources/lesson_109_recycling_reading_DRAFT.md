# Lesson 109 — Teacher-prepared reading resource (DRAFT)

**Purpose:** Year 4 Reading 3.2.4 (dictionary features) and 3.2.1 (main idea). The SOW explicitly allows a short local recycling/environmental project text. **This is an original sample, not a textbook passage or verified account of a real project.** Teacher should adapt to a genuine local example if available.

## Pupil reading passage: Our School Recycling Day

On Friday, pupils at Sekolah Ceria collected clean paper, plastic bottles and metal cans. They put the materials into three boxes. Their teacher helped them weigh the materials. A recycling centre paid the school RM24 for the collected items.

The pupils decided to use the money to buy seeds for a small school garden. They learnt that recycling can reduce rubbish and help a school save or earn money. They want to organise another collection next month.

## Reading tasks
1. What is the main idea of the passage?
2. Name two materials the pupils collected.
3. How much money did the school receive?
4. What did the pupils plan to buy?
5. Use a **monolingual dictionary** to find the headwords **collect** and **reduce**. Write a simple meaning for each and identify which word comes first alphabetically.

**Teacher key:** (1) A school recycling project and its benefits; (2) any two of paper, plastic bottles, metal cans; (3) RM24; (4) seeds; (5) meanings depend on pupil-friendly dictionary; collect comes before reduce.
