# Lesson 078 — healthy-food poster planner (DRAFT)

Original classroom scaffold for SOW Lesson 78. Pupils should research food information using teacher-approved sources with adult guidance.

**Poster title:** Our Food Choices

**Planning grid:** Food chosen | One fact from an approved source | Why it matters | Source checked by teacher

**Sentence frames:** “___ is good for ___.” / “We can eat ___ as part of a balanced diet.” / “Our group chose ___ because ___.”

**Checklist:** My writing is neat and in cursive. / I checked spelling. / Our poster has a clear title. / We checked our food information. / Everyone helped.

**PBD:** Focus on cursive writing (4.1.2) and spelling in guided writing (4.3.2), not on unsupported health claims. Teacher validates factual statements and supervises internet use.
