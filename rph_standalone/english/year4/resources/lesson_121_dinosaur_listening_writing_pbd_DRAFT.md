# Lesson 121 — Dinosaur listening and writing PBD (DRAFT)
Topic: Amazing animals. Main Writing SP 4.2.4; complementary Listening SP 1.2.2.
Original teacher-read supplementary task; Student's Book pp.84–85 Activities 3–4 and Teacher's Book p.127 remain the primary lesson sources. Do not present this as an official audio track.

## Teacher-read listening text
Read twice at a steady pace without showing the text to pupils.
My dinosaur has a long neck and four strong legs. It has a small head and a long tail. It can walk slowly, but it cannot fly. It eats leaves from tall trees.

## Pupil listening sheet
Name: __________
Listen and circle or write the correct answer.
1. How many legs does the dinosaur have? Two / Four / Six
2. Which body part is long? Neck / Ears / Wings
3. Can it fly? Yes / No
4. What does it eat? Leaves / Fish / Stones
Teacher key: 1 Four; 2 Neck; 3 No; 4 Leaves.

## Student's Book writing follow-up
Complete SB pp.84–85 Activities 3–4 as instructed. Then draw an invented dinosaur and write three or four original sentences about its body parts and abilities. Do not copy the teacher-read dinosaur description as the invented-dinosaur answer.
Writing frame: My dinosaur has __________. It can __________. It cannot __________.

## Individual PBD record
Listening SP 1.2.2: Number of specific details correctly identified from the teacher-read text: ___ / 4.
Writing SP 4.2.4: Relevant descriptive sentences: ___ / 4; understandable language: Yes / Developing / Needs support.
Support: Picture prompts and second reading. Extension: Add a contrasting feature with but.

Verify dinosaur vocabulary and grammar against the exact Student's Book unit before approval. This is teacher-created listening input, not the book's official dialogue. The invented dinosaur has no textbook character name. Status: DRAFT.
