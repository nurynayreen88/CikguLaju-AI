# Lesson 080 — Project-based learning planning kit (DRAFT)

SOW Lesson 80 lets teachers choose theme, materials and skills. This is an **optional** project example, not a mandated curriculum topic.

**Sample project:** My School, Less Waste. Groups make a short English poster with two practical ways to reduce classroom waste.

**Before class:** Teacher chooses actual Year 4 main/complementary SP from DSKP and records success criteria. Prepare scrap paper, colouring tools and suitable picture cards.

**Planning sheet:** Our question: ___. Our main SP: ___. Complementary SP: ___. Our product: ___. Team roles: writer / illustrator / speaker / checker. Materials: ___. Two messages: ___ and ___.

**Useful language:** We can reuse paper. / We should put rubbish in the correct bin. / Our idea is useful because ___.

**PBD evidence:** Group planning sheet, final poster, each pupil's spoken explanation. Record achievement **against the SP actually selected by the teacher**, not an invented fixed SP.

**Status:** DRAFT; teacher must select topic/SP and approve.
