# Lesson 033 — Ancient Egypt listening cards (teacher-created DRAFT)

The Year 4 SOW asks the teacher to prepare a short spoken or recorded text based on the quiz, then use SB p.27 Activity 1 and the answer check on p.118. This original script is **not** a transcript of the SB quiz and must be checked against the exact SB pictures before use.

## Spoken text (read slowly twice)
Long ago, people lived beside the River Nile in Egypt. They used boats on the river. Some people built large stone pyramids. Inside ancient tombs, people sometimes placed objects that belonged to the dead. A mummy is a body that was carefully preserved. People today can learn about Ancient Egypt by studying old buildings and objects.

## Picture prompts
Prepare or select pictures showing: a river boat, a pyramid, a mummy, an ancient tomb and an object from a museum. Avoid claiming every image appears in SB p.27 until the page has been checked.

## Listening checks
1. Which river is named? (The Nile)
2. Name one building mentioned. (A pyramid)
3. What is a mummy? (A preserved body)
4. What helps us learn about Ancient Egypt? (Old buildings and objects)
