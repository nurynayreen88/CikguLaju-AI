# Lesson 023 — timetable and opinion strips (DRAFT)

Original classroom example based on SOW Lesson 23; not a reproduced textbook page.

| Day | Subjects |
|---|---|
| Monday | English, Mathematics, Art |
| Tuesday | Science, English, Music |
| Wednesday | Mathematics, Physical Education, English |
| Thursday | Science, Art, English |
| Friday | English, Music, Mathematics |

**Speaking frame:** I think ___ will be ___ because we have ___.

**Cut-out opinion strips:** I like ___ because ___. / I think ___ is interesting because ___. / I do not like ___ because ___.

**Task:** Each pupil writes two opinions on separate strips. Mix strips, read, guess authors and respond politely: “I agree because…” or “I disagree because…”.

**PBD:** Give a reason for a prediction (2.1.4) and explain a simple written opinion (4.2.1). Teacher must replace the timetable with the actual class timetable if appropriate.
