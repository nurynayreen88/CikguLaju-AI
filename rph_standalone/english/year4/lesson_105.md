# English Year 4 — Lesson 105: Writing 20

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 4.2.3  
**Complementary SP:** 4.3.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.75 Activities 3–4; TB p.113; Track 29; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 105

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review recycling and indefinite pronouns.
**Pupils:** Recall recycling words and indefinite pronouns.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play a spelling game and review Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Play the spelling game and practise Grammar Box examples.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 3; write true and false sentences about recycling habits.
**Pupils:** Write true and false sentences about recycling habits.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Partners read sentences and identify which are true; optionally use Track 29.
**Pupils:** Exchange sentences and identify which are true.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss one recycling habit.
**Pupils:** Share one recycling habit.

**PBD:** Observe the named learning task against 4.2.3 and 4.3.2; for teacher-selected standards, establish criteria before class.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
