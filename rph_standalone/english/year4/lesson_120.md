# English Year 4 — Lesson 120: Reading 23

**Topic:** Amazing animals  
**Main SP:** 3.2.2  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_120_dinosaur_fact_reading_speaking_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB pp.84–85 Activities 1–2; TB pp.126–127; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 120

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce dinosaur pictures and body-part vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Name dinosaur body parts from pictures.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model scanning Activity 1 for a named dinosaur and underline one supporting fact before asking pupils to find another detail independently.
**Pupils:** Read Activity 1 and find dinosaur facts.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activity 2, model locating one supporting sentence and ask pupils to answer the remaining text-specific questions.
**Pupils:** Read Activity 2 and answer specific questions.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.72, Activity 1 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the reading task, check pupils' understanding of the text and supporting details as evidence for the main Reading SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.72, Activity 1; read the Elephant Orphanage passage and answer the comprehension questions.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to state one dinosaur fact and identify the sentence in the text that supports it.
**Pupils:** Recall one dinosaur fact.

**PBD:** Check pupils' text-supported answers to dinosaur fact questions in SB Activities 1–2 for main Reading SP 3.2.2; observe their spoken explanations of a dinosaur fact for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.4. Workbook p.72 Elephant Orphanage passage provides additional written reading practice, not a replacement for the dinosaur text.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
