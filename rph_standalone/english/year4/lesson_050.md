# English Year 4 — Lesson 050

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 2.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.37 Activity 3; TB pp.62–63; CD1 Track 54; Year 4 SOW Lesson 50

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review ordinal numbers using dates.
**Pupils:** Say dates using ordinal numbers.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 3 with Track 54 and model when questions.
**Pupils:** Listen and practise when questions.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a when question and clarification response, then organise pairs to exchange celebration dates and check understanding.
**Pupils:** Ask about dates and use clarification phrases.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pairs to extend their celebration-date dialogues and give peers a checklist for clear questions and clarification.
**Pupils:** Perform a dialogue and improve after feedback.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.30, Sing a song ordinal-number task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.30, Sing a song ordinal-number task; use ordinal numbers to express dates.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate one clarification phrase from their celebration-date dialogues.
**Pupils:** Use one clarification phrase accurately.

**PBD:** Observe pupils asking and answering when questions about dates and requesting clarification in paired dialogue for main Speaking SP 2.2.1. Check their understanding of spoken clarification prompts for complementary Listening SP 1.2.5. Workbook p.30 provides written practice only.  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
