# English Year 4 Lesson 50 — Speaking 10

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Speaking 2.2.1. Complementary SP: Listening 1.2.5. References: SB p.37 Activity 3; TB pp.62–63; CD1 Track 54. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Review ordinal numbers using dates. Pupils participate and demonstrate the target skill.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Follow Activity 3 with Track 54 and model when questions. Pupils participate and demonstrate the target skill.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Pairs ask celebration dates and use clarification phrases. Pupils participate and demonstrate the target skill.

PBD (10 min): Teacher guides: Pairs perform an extended dialogue and give feedback. Pupils participate and demonstrate the target skill.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Recall a useful clarification phrase. Pupils participate and demonstrate the target skill.

PBD: Observe and check pupil responses against Speaking 2.2.1 and Listening 1.2.5. Differentiation: Use pictures, models, pair support and optional extension tasks as appropriate.
