# English Year 4 — Lesson 110: Writing 21

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 4.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.76; TB p.115; Track 31; board-game templates; Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 110

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the TB optional activity.
**Pupils:** Recall language from the previous activity.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Teach vocabulary with Track 31 and review Grammar Box.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 31 and practise the target vocabulary.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model the first Activity 1 question, then guide groups to draft clear board-game questions using the target language.
**Pupils:** Write board-game questions using the model.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.61, Activity 2 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. Assess pupils' own written sentences or text for the main Writing SP; do not rely on isolated vocabulary answers alone.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.61, Activity 2; complete the sentences using someone, anyone, everyone or no one.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify one language correction they made while preparing their board-game questions.
**Pupils:** Share one improvement in writing.

**PBD:** Check written board-game questions for capitals, punctuation and relevant source details (SP 4.3.1, 3.2.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
