# English Year 4 — Lesson 082: Speaking 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 2.1.3  
**Complementary SP:** 4.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.59 Activity 2; TB p.92

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the safety song and review its rules.
**Pupils:** Recall safety rules from the song.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Underline two rules from the song, model positive and negative imperatives and ask pupils to explain the difference.
**Pupils:** Identify and practise positive and negative imperatives.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise groups to discuss safety rules for cars, bicycles and streets, prompting one positive and one negative imperative.
**Pupils:** Discuss safety rules for cars, bicycles and streets.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model legible cursive letter formation, then guide pupils to write and present their safety rules.
**Pupils:** Write rules in cursive and present them.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.50, the road-safety task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.50, the road-safety task; write positive and negative road-safety rules.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show safety flashcards and ask pupils to classify each action as safe or unsafe, giving one reason.
**Pupils:** Classify flashcard actions as safe or unsafe.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' spoken explanations of road-safety rules for main Speaking SP 2.1.3; assess legibility of their cursive written imperatives for complementary Writing SP 4.1.2. Workbook p.50 provides the main written practice but does not replace speaking observation.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
