# English Year 4 — Lesson 082: Speaking 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 2.1.3  
**Complementary SP:** 4.1.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.59 Activity 2; TB p.92

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Replay the safety song and review its rules.
**Pupils:** Respond to the prompts and recall relevant vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Underline rules and model positive and negative imperatives.
**Pupils:** Follow the model and complete the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Groups discuss rules for cars, bicycles and streets.
**Pupils:** Complete the named skill activity and practise the target language.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils write neat cursive rules and present them.
**Pupils:** Show learning evidence and improve responses after feedback.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Identify safe and unsafe actions using flashcards.
**Pupils:** Share one result or reflection.

**PBD:** Assess task responses against 2.1.3 and 4.1.2; teacher selects specific criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
