# English Year 4 — Lesson 094: Writing 18

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 4.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 4.2.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.64; TB p.99; transport fare advertisement

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm-up and Vocabulary.
**Pupils:** Recall public transport vocabulary.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display transport advertisements and model finding the price and departure time for one option.
**Pupils:** Read fares and departure times in advertisements.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Give pairs a tourist-attraction scenario and guide them to compare public transport options using price and departure-time information.
**Pupils:** Choose a transport option for a tourist attraction.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model a sentence giving a transport choice and reason, then guide pupils to write their own explanations.
**Pupils:** Write a description and reasons for the choice.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Organise a gallery walk and prompt pupils to give specific feedback on the clarity of each transport choice and reason.
**Pupils:** Give peer feedback during a gallery walk.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' written description of a recommended transport option for main Writing SP 4.2.1, including relevant fare and departure details; check written reasons supporting the recommendation for complementary Writing SP 4.2.4. Do not add an unverified Workbook activity.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
