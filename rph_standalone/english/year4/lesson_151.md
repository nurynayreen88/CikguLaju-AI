# English Year 4 — Lesson 151: Speaking 29

**Topic:** Learning English  
**Main SP:** 2.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SOW Lesson 151; resources/lesson_151_english_learning_survey_DRAFT.md; ICT / Creativity and Innovation

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display examples of English games and stories and ask pupils to explain how each resource supports practice.
**Pupils:** Name useful English learning resources.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model advice using should and shouldn't to improve English.
**Pupils:** Suggest good learning habits using should and shouldn't.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model an advice-seeking question, then organise pair interviews about practising English at school and at home.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer questions about learning English at home and school.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Prompt pupils to explain why one learning habit is helpful and assess whether each response includes a clear reason.
**Pupils:** Explain reasons for chosen learning habits.

Pupils record one practical way to improve their English after the speaking survey; no specific Workbook page is prescribed in the SOW.

**Teacher:** Instruct pupils to complete Workbook p.88, Activity 2 according to the printed instructions. Check answers and assess individual understanding.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.88, Activity 2; look at the picture and write advice using should or shouldn't.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to demonstrate a useful speaking strategy in a short exchange.
**Pupils:** Share one strategy to practise.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' questions and responses about English-learning habits, including reasons for advice (SP 2.1.1, 1.2.5).  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, peer support and optional extensions.
