# English Year 4 — Lesson 096: Language Awareness 5

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Complementary SP:** Teacher-selected DSKP SP  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.70 Revision 6; TB p.106; self-assessment; optional support: resources/lesson_096_unit6_self_assessment_DRAFT.md

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm-up.
**Pupils:** Recall language for getting around.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Demonstrate one revision question, then ask pupils to complete Revision 6 Activities 1–3 and discuss common errors.
**Pupils:** Complete Revision 6 Activities 1–3.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Select a short follow-up exercise targeting the most common errors identified in pupils' revision responses.
**Pupils:** Practise language skills identified by assessment.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide pupils through Unit 6 self-assessment and ask them to identify one skill requiring more practice.
**Pupils:** Complete Unit 6 self-assessment and note gaps.

Pupils complete the Unit 6 self-assessment and revise a language point they need to improve; no specific Workbook page is prescribed in the SOW.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Use the self-assessment results to identify one priority skill and recommend a focused practice task.
**Pupils:** Name one priority for further practice.

**PBD:** Assess task responses against Teacher-selected DSKP SP and Teacher-selected DSKP SP; teacher selects specific criteria for Language Awareness.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, examples, partner support and extension tasks as needed.
