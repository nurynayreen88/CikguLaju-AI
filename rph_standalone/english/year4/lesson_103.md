# English Year 4 — Lesson 103: Speaking 20

**Topic:** Helping out  
**Main SP:** 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.5  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.73 Activity 5; TB p.111; Activity 5 Class CD2 Track 26 (TB p.111); Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 103

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review classroom objects such as rulers and notebooks.
**Pupils:** Name classroom objects and belongings.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model Whose is this? and It's mine using real objects.
**Pupils:** Ask and answer Whose is this? and It's mine.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 26 for Activity 5, model a Whose question and monitor pupils' pair exchanges for accurate possessive responses.
**Pupils:** Listen to Track 26 and practise ownership questions.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to sketch one belonging without naming its owner. Model a 'Whose ...?' question, then organise a guessing exchange and check possessive pronouns.
**Pupils:** Draw belongings and guess their owners.



## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model correcting one possessive-pronoun sentence and check pupils' understanding.
**Pupils:** Correct a possessive pronoun sentence.

**PBD:** Observe pupils asking and answering ownership questions during the guessing task for main Speaking SP 2.1.5; check understanding of spoken ownership questions in Activity 5 (Track 26; disc unverified) for complementary Listening SP 1.2.5. Do not infer a CD number.  
**Differentiation:** Provide word banks, teacher models, peer support and optional extension.
