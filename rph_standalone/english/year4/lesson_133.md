# English Year 4 — Lesson 133: Language Arts 25

**Topic:** The Jungle Book  
**Main SP:** 5.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_133_red_flower_response_pbd_DRAFT.md; Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook p.63; Year 4 SOW Lesson 133

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask three short questions about Chapter 3 events and request story evidence for one answer.
**Pupils:** Recall Chapter 3 events.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to draw the red flower from the story and label one relevant detail.
**Pupils:** Draw the red flower from the story.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Explain guidebook p.63 steps 1–3 and organise the Flying Carpets activity to compare evidence-based answers.
**Pupils:** Discuss guidebook questions in Flying Carpets groups.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils why Mowgli calls it a red flower and request one supporting story detail.
**Pupils:** Explain why Mowgli uses the name red flower.

Pupils complete a follow-up task based on The Jungle Book and the Teaching Guidebook; no specific Workbook page is prescribed for this literature lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two different personal interpretations and ask pupils to give reasons.
**Pupils:** Share a personal interpretation.

**PBD:** Assess pupils' personal interpretation of the red flower and their response to The Jungle Book for main Language Arts SP 5.2.1; observe their spoken opinions and reasons during Flying Carpets discussion for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.1. Use the Graphic Novel Teaching Guidebook rather than an invented Workbook page.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
