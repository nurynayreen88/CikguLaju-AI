# English Year 4 — Lesson 081: Listening 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 1.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.59; TB p.92; CD2 Tracks 2 and 4; Year 4 SOW Lesson 81

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce transport-safety words with Track 2 and elicit familiar rules.
**Pupils:** Listen and recall safety words and rules.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show rhyming flashcards and six pictures; ask which actions are safe.
**Pupils:** Predict safe and unsafe actions and give simple reasons.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the song on Track 4 without displaying the lyrics.
**Pupils:** Listen for target sounds and check predictions against the song.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one safe-versus-unsafe judgement with a reason, then guide Student Book Activity 1 and ask pupils to explain their choices.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 1 and explain one safety judgement.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead the physical-response activity in TB p.92.
**Pupils:** Respond to safety instructions with appropriate movements.

**PBD:** Observe sound recognition and spoken explanations against SP 1.1.1 and 2.1.4.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, sentence starters and repeated listening as needed.
