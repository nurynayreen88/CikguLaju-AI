# English Year 4 — Lesson 081: Listening 16

**Topic:** Getting around  
**Main SP:** 1.1.1  
**Complementary SP:** 2.1.4  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.59; TB p.92; **Warm up/Vocabulary CD2 Track 2; Activity 1 CD2 Track 4** (KPM SOW); Year 4 SOW Lesson 81

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce transport-safety words with Track 2 and elicit familiar rules.
**Pupils:** Listen and recall safety words and rules.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show rhyming flashcards and six pictures; ask which actions are safe.
**Pupils:** Predict safe and unsafe actions and give simple reasons.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play the song on Track 4 without displaying the lyrics.
**Pupils:** Listen for target sounds and check predictions against the song.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model one safe-versus-unsafe judgement with a reason, then guide Student Book Activity 1 and ask pupils to explain their choices.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 1 and explain one safety judgement.

**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.50, the road-safety picture task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.50, the road-safety picture task; identify safe and unsafe actions from the pictures.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Lead the TB p.92 physical-response activity and check that pupils match actions to safe transport rules.
**Pupils:** Respond to safety instructions with appropriate movements.

**PBD:** Check recognition of target sounds in the CD2 Track 4 song for main Listening SP 1.1.1; observe spoken explanations of safe and unsafe actions for complementary Speaking SP 2.1.4. Workbook p.50 is written road-safety practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, sentence starters and repeated listening as needed.
