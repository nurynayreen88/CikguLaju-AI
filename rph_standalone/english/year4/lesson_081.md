# English Year 4 — Lesson 081: Listening 16

Topic: Getting around
Main SP: Listening 1.1.1
Complementary SP: Speaking 2.1.4
Duration: 60 minutes
References: Student Book p.59; Teacher Book p.92; CD2 Tracks 2 and 4.

Set induction (10 minutes): Introduce transport safety vocabulary with Track 2; pupils recall rules.
Activity 1 (15 minutes): Show rhyming flashcards and six pictures; pupils predict which actions are safe and explain why.
Activity 2 (20 minutes): Play the song on Track 4 without showing lyrics; pupils check predictions by listening.
Reinforcement/PBD (10 minutes): Pupils complete Activity 1 and explain one safety judgement.
Closure (5 minutes): Follow Teacher Book total physical response activity.

Differentiation: Picture cues and sentence starters.
