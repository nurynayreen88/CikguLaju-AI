# English Year 4 Lesson 049 — Listening 10

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP Listening 1.2.1. Complementary SP Reading 3.2.3. Resources: Student Book p.37 Activity 1; Teacher Book pp.62–63; CD1 Tracks 52 and 53. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher introduces celebrations with pictures. Pupils name familiar celebrations.
Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher follows TB Warm-up and teaches ordinal numbers with Track 52. Pupils listen and repeat.
Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher plays Track 53 without visible lyrics first. Pupils identify the main idea of the song.
PBD (10 min): Teacher provides a matching worksheet for celebrate, celebration, fun and special. Pupils infer meanings and listen again.
Closure (5 min): Pupils chant the song and recall an ordinal number.
