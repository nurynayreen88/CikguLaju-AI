# English Year 4 — Lesson 049

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 1.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.3  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.37 Activity 1; TB pp.62–63; Vocabulary CD1 Track 52; Activity 1 CD1 Track 53 (KPM SOW); Year 4 SOW Lesson 49

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Introduce celebrations with pictures.
**Pupils:** Name familiar celebrations.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Warm-up and teach ordinal numbers with Track 52.
**Pupils:** Listen, repeat and use ordinal numbers.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 53 without displaying lyrics.
**Pupils:** Listen to the song and identify its main idea.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Distribute a word-matching worksheet, replay the song and ask pupils to verify their answers using words they heard.
**Pupils:** Infer meanings of celebrate, celebration, fun and special.
**Teacher:** Use Workbook p.30, Sing a song picture-word matching task, according to the printed instructions. Treat this as the main written practice and check pupils' answers; assess the lesson's main SP through its relevant task evidence.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.30, Sing a song picture-word matching task; match the celebration words to the pictures.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to identify an ordinal number heard in the song and use it in a short sentence.
**Pupils:** Chant a line and recall an ordinal number.

**PBD:** Check each pupil's identification of the celebrations song's overall message after CD1 Track 53 for main Listening SP 1.2.1. Check inference of key celebration words from the printed context for complementary Reading SP 3.2.3. Workbook p.30 is written vocabulary practice, not listening evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
