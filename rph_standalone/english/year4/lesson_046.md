# English Year 4 — Lesson 046: Writing 9

**Topic:** In the Past  
**Main SP:** Writing 4.2.5  
**Complementary SP:** Reading 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.33 Activities 1–2; TB p.56; CD1 Track 49

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Discuss sending and receiving emails.
**Pupils:** Respond to prompts and recall relevant language.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Activity 1 with Track 49 and Activity 2.
**Pupils:** Follow the source-based model and perform the guided task.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Identify the four Writing Tip points in the model email.
**Pupils:** Complete the central task and practise the main skill.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Write an email about a past trip using and/but; exchange and review emails.
**Pupils:** Show learning evidence and use feedback to improve answers.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Revise one connected sentence.
**Pupils:** Share a short response or reflection.

**PBD:** Check responses against Writing 4.2.5 and Reading 3.2.2.  
**Differentiation:** Vary writing length targets but retain the email format.
