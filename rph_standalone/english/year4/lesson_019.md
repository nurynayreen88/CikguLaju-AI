# English Year 4 — Lesson 019: Reading 4

**Topic:** My Week  
**Main SP:** Reading 3.3.1  
**Complementary SP:** Reading 3.2.1  
**References:** SB p.16 story; TB pp.34–35  
**Duration:** 60 minutes

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB warm-up and review timetable words.
**Pupils:** Recall timetable words from the warm-up.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show story character pictures and ask pupils to predict what they are looking at.
**Pupils:** Predict the story using character pictures.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Set two reading questions about the characters' location and timetables, then guide silent reading and ask pupils to cite story details.
**Pupils:** Read silently and identify the characters' location and timetable problem.

## PBD and reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to explain the story's main idea and give a reasoned opinion; use the optional true/false game if time allows.
**Pupils:** Explain the main idea and give an opinion about the story. Where a matching Workbook activity is available, pupils complete it to practise the language skills taught in this lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Pupils share one thing they enjoyed about the story.
**Pupils:** Share something enjoyed about the story.

**PBD:** Check task responses against Reading 3.3.1 and Reading 3.2.1, recording individual progress.  
**Differentiation:** Allow unhurried silent reading and give fast finishers a meaningful detail task.
