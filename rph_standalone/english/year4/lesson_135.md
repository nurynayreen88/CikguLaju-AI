# English Year 4 — Lesson 135: Speaking 26

**Topic:** Get active!  
**Main SP:** 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** resources/lesson_135_sportspeople_speaking_listening_pbd_DRAFT.md; SB p.95 Activity 5; TB p.141; Year 4 SOW Lesson 135

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Show adjective cards and guide pupils to match each word with its superlative form.
**Pupils:** Match adjectives with superlatives.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to recall two characters from the reading and describe their sports using text details.
**Pupils:** Recall sportspeople from the reading.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 5 and practise high jumper, windsurfer and javelin thrower.
**Pupils:** Practise the words high jumper, windsurfer and javelin thrower.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Assign Workbook p.79, Activity 3 as the main Workbook exercise in Assessment/PBD. Follow its printed instructions and check answers. During the speaking task in Activity 1/2, observe pupils' spoken responses as evidence for the main Speaking SP.
**Pupils:** Complete Workbook p.79, Activity 3; complete the opinions about sports using the picture prompts.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite two pupils to share an accurate description and explain the superlative used.
**Pupils:** Give one accurate sports description.

**PBD:** Observe pupils' spoken descriptions of sportspeople using suitable superlatives for main Speaking SP 2.1.5; check specific details understood from verified spoken input for complementary Listening SP 1.2.2. Workbook p.79 provides written sports-opinion practice, not listening or speaking evidence.  
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues, word banks, paired support and extensions where needed.
