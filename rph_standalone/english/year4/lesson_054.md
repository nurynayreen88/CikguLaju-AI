# English Year 4 Lesson 054 — Listening 11

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Listening 1.2.3. Complementary SP: Listening 1.2.2. References: SB pp.38–39 Activities 1 and 4; TB pp.64–65; CD1 Track 56. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Recall the story on SB p.38. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Play Track 56; pupils answer true/false questions about the story. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Use Activity 4 pictures, then follow TB listening instructions. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Pairs discuss Independence Day using supported opinion phrases. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Correct one true/false answer and share an opinion. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Listening 1.2.3 and Listening 1.2.2. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 54.
