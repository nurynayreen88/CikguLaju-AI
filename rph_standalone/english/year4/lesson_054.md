# English Year 4 — Lesson 054

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 1.2.3  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.38–39 Activities 1 and 4; TB pp.64–65; Activity 1 story CD1 Track 56 (KPM SOW); Year 4 SOW Lesson 54

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Recall the story on SB p.38.
**Pupils:** Retell one event from the story.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 56 and ask true/false questions.
**Pupils:** Listen and decide whether statements are true or false.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Preview the Activity 4 pictures, explain the listening purpose and replay relevant audio sections to check pupils' answers.
**Pupils:** Use pictures to predict and check listening details.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Model an opinion with a reason about Independence Day and invite pupils to share their own views.
**Pupils:** Discuss Independence Day in pairs and justify an opinion. Where a matching Workbook activity is available, pupils complete it to practise the language skills taught in this lesson.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Ask pupils to justify one true/false response with a listening clue and express a reasoned opinion.
**Pupils:** Correct an answer and share a reason.

**PBD:** Check true/false listening answers and supporting details from Track 56 (SP 1.2.3 and 1.2.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
