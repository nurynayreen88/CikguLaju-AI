# English Year 4 Lesson 059 — Listening 12

Topic: Celebrations. Main SP: Listening 1.2.1. Complementary SP: Listening 1.2.2. References: SB pp.40–41 Activities 3–4; TB pp.66–67; Track 61. Duration: 60 minutes.

Set induction (10 min): Teacher guides: Review Grammar Box on SB p.41. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 1 (15 min): Teacher guides: Play Track 61; pupils identify what the conversation is about. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Activity 2 (20 min): Teacher guides: Follow TB Activity 3 and ask pupils to justify their gist answer. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD and reinforcement (10 min): Teacher guides: Follow Activity 4, checking specific details from the dialogues. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

Closure (5 min): Teacher guides: Review using the TB Revision box. Pupils complete the indicated skill task and respond to feedback.

PBD: Observe the specified learning task and record evidence against Listening 1.2.1 and Listening 1.2.2. Differentiation: Provide source-based models, partner support or an extension task according to pupil needs. Source: Primary Year 4 SK SOW Lesson 59.
