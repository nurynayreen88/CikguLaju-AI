# English Year 4 — Lesson 059

**Topic:** Celebrations  
**Main SP:** 1.2.1  
**Complementary SP:** 1.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB pp.40–41 Activities 3–4; TB pp.66–67; Track 61; Year 4 SOW Lesson 59

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the Grammar Box on SB p.41.
**Pupils:** Recall language used in celebration conversations.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Play Track 61 and ask about the conversation topic.
**Pupils:** Listen and identify the main topic.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide Activity 3 and ask pupils to justify their gist answers.
**Pupils:** Complete Activity 3 and explain a gist answer.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow Activity 4 and check dialogue details.
**Pupils:** Listen for details and complete Activity 4.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Review the TB Revision box.
**Pupils:** Share one detail remembered from the dialogue.

**PBD:** Record identification of the dialogue's main topic and specific details in Activities 3–4 (SP 1.2.1, 1.2.2).  
**Differentiation:** Provide models, picture prompts, peer support and extension tasks as appropriate.
