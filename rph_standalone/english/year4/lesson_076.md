# English Year 4 — Lesson 076: Speaking 15

**Topic:** Eating right  
**Main SP:** 2.1.5  
**Complementary SP:** 2.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Resources:** SB p.54 Activity 1; TB p.84; CD1 Track 81; gapped instructions

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Follow TB Warm-up and Vocabulary with Track 81.
**Pupil action:** Recall food words from Track 81.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Show the board-game instructions, model filling one missing step and ask groups to complete the remaining gaps.
**Pupil action:** Complete gaps in the board-game instructions.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Model a clarification question and invite pupils to ask and answer questions about unclear game steps.
**Pupil action:** Ask clarification questions about game steps.

## PBD / reinforcement (10 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Monitor the group board game and prompt pupils to describe food objects using the target collective nouns.
**Pupil action:** Play the game and describe food using collective nouns.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher action:** Correct anonymous language errors together.
**Pupil action:** Correct a language error from the activity.

**PBD:** Observe food descriptions and understanding of board-game instructions, including clarification questions, against SP 2.1.5 and 2.2.2.  
**Differentiation:** Use word banks, modelling, peer support or extension according to pupil needs.
