# English Year 4 — Lesson 008: Reading 2

**Topic:** Where are you from?  
**Main SP:** 3.2.3  
**Complementary SP:** 3.2.2  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**References:** SB p.8; TB pp.22–23; CD1 Track 11; Year 4 SOW Lesson 8

## Set induction (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Mime daily routines and ask pupils to guess.
**Pupils:** Name the routines shown through mime.

## Activity 1 (15 minutes)
**Teacher:** Display titles and pictures; elicit meanings of unfamiliar words.
**Pupils:** Predict meanings of figure skater, chess player, ice-skate and ice-skating rink.

## Activity 2 (20 minutes)
**Teacher:** Follow TB Activity 1 and use Track 11 with the reading text.
**Pupils:** Read the text and listen to Track 11.

## Reinforcement / PBD (10 minutes)
**Teacher:** Guide TB Activity 2 and ask pupils to explain context clues.
**Pupils:** Locate reading details and explain vocabulary clues.

## Closure (5 minutes)
**Teacher:** Invite pupils to share a routine and a new word.
**Pupils:** Name a routine and explain one new word.

**PBD:** Observe reading comprehension and contextual vocabulary inference against SP 3.2.3 and 3.2.2.  
**Differentiation:** Use shared reading, picture cues and pair support.
