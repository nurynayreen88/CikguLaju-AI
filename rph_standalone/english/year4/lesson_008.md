# English Year 4 Lesson 8 — Reading

Main SP 3.2.3; complementary SP 3.2.2. SB p.8, TB pp.22–23, CD1 Track 11.

Set induction (10m): Mime routines; pupils guess.
Activity 1 (15m): Predict meanings of figure skater, chess player, ice-skate and ice-skating rink using titles and pictures.
Activity 2 (20m): Read and follow TB Activity 1, Track 11.
Assessment (10m): Complete TB Activity 2, identify details and explain vocabulary clues.
Closure (5m): Share a routine and new word.

Differentiation: Shared reading in pairs and picture cues. QA pending.
