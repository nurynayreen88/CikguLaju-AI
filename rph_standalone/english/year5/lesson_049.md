# English Year 5 Lesson 49 — Reading 9

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 49, printed p.85. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.29 Activities 1–3, TB p.43, a Caribbean map and Workbook p.25 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer the meaning of two carnival words from clues in the photo or text and retell two events from the carnival.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the Caribbean on a map. Pupils examine the carnival photo and predict what people do at the celebration.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 1, pairs describe the photo and suggest the meaning of two unfamiliar carnival words. The teacher leaves the suggestions on the board for pupils to revisit.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils put three paragraph topics in a predicted order, read the Notting Hill text in SB Activity 2 and check the order. Pairs point to a detail in the text that confirms or changes one word meaning.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils complete SB Activity 3. After the teacher models a link between two events with also, and or but, partners take turns retelling what happens at the carnival from memory.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils use the photo as a final reminder and retell two details with books closed. If time permits, pupils find one activity from the World Book Day text in Workbook p.25 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings with clues and listen for two accurate events in each pupil’s retelling.
**Differentiation:** Supply Where, When and What prompts. Ask confident pupils to use a further detail from the text.
