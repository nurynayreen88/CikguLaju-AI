# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 088 (Project 1)

**Status:** DRAF — CikguLaju project proposal, teacher approval pending. **Official SOW:** Project-based Lesson 1, Unit 4, printed p.126. **Theme, topic, main skill, complementary skill and learning standards:** To be selected by the teacher according to the Year 5 DSKP; no fixed official codes are assigned. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.

**Suggested topic (not prescribed by SOW):** My Dream School Day. **Materials:** Blank A4 paper, drawing materials, sample school timetable and optional word bank about school subjects and activities. **Product:** A labelled timetable/poster and a short group presentation.

**Suggested learning objectives:** Pupils collaborate to plan a school day with at least four timetable entries, explain at least two choices and present a coherent proposal.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher asks what makes a school day enjoyable. Pupils name school subjects and activities from Unit 4.

**Activity 1 — Plan (15 min):** In groups of three or four, pupils select four to six school periods and identify the starting times. Teacher checks realistic order, appropriate vocabulary and equitable assignment of roles.

**Activity 2 — Create (20 min):** Pupils prepare a clear poster with a timetable, subjects, activities and two short explanations using language familiar from Unit 4. Groups compare information for clarity and correct wording before finalising.

**Activity 3 — Present (10 min):** Each group presents its dream school day. Other pupils ask one relevant question; presenters answer and justify a choice.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher uses a simple checklist: complete timetable, understandable vocabulary, two reasons, balanced contributions and responses to one question. Pupils correct an identified weakness.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils give one idea they would adopt in their own school day.

**Differentiation:** Provide a sample timetable and sentence frames where needed; encourage independent groups to justify additional choices. **Source rule:** The topic, activities, skills and assessment are CikguLaju suggestions, NOT official SOW prescriptions. No mandatory Student Book, Workbook or audio is attached. Teacher must approve the chosen standards and project before READY.
