# English Year 5 Lesson 80: Writing 14

Official SOW Lesson 80, printed p.119. Topic: Learning World. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.4; Speaking 2.2.2.
Materials: SB p.47 Activities 5-6; TB p.61.

1. Set induction (8 min): Play finger writing with verbs play, speak, chat, relax and practise.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Identify present simple and continuous, then complete Activity 5.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Groups choose separate texts and agree the order for reading and questions.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Complete Activity 6 imagining a lottery winner.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs write photo questions and finish with a memory-chain review.
