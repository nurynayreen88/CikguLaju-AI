# English Year 5 Lesson 154 — Consolidation 16

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 154, printed p.196. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.2 and Speaking 2.1.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.90 Activities 3, 4, 5 and 7, TB p.104, CD2.37, WB p.74 Ex3 and a Europe map.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify specific travel details in a conversation and ask about and describe future plans.
**Success criteria:** Record two accurate details from the dialogue and exchange one clear question and answer about a future plan.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the Europe map and ask pupils to recall words for holiday transport and equipment.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils compare positive and negative plans in SB Activity 3, make plan questions in Activity 4 and contrast these with predictions in Activity 5.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Locate Dover and the English Channel. Play CD2.37 for SB Activity 7. Pupils listen for route or transport details and verify their answers on replay.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs ask and answer about their own future trips, then find one difference. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.74 Exercise 3.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Each pupil names one travel detail heard and tells the class one of their own plans.

**PBD:** Check two listening details and hear each pupil ask a future-plan question and give a relevant answer.
**Differentiation:** Provide a route with one place labelled. Ask confident pupils to add a follow-up question about transport.
