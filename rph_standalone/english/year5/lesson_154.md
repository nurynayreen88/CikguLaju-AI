# Year 5 Lesson 154 — Consolidation 16

Status: DRAF. Unit 8. Main LS 1.2.2; complementary LS 2.1.4.
Materials: Student Book p.90 Activities 3, 4, 5 and 7; Teacher Book p.104; CD2-37; Europe map. Workbook mapping and audio playback remain unverified.

Objective: Pupils demonstrate the skills through the specific activities.

Set induction (5 min): Recall holiday vocabulary and future plans.

Main activities (40 min): Distinguish affirmative and negative be going to sentences; form future-plan questions and will predictions. Listen to CD2-37 and identify travel details on the map. Discuss cleaner transport. Teacher models instructions and provides feedback.

PBD (10 min): Assess listening for specific details and questions about plans. Pupils improve one response based on feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and submit an exit response.

Differentiation: Use visuals, sentence frames and paired support where needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent work.
