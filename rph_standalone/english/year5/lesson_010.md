# English Year 5 Lesson 10 — Language Arts 2

**Official SOW topic:** This Tooth.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 10, printed p.44. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.3.1 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.18, This Tooth by Lee Bennett Hopkins, BPK Poetry Guidebook, mask materials and paper.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils respond imaginatively to This Tooth by making a character mask and narrate a new loose-tooth story.
**Success criteria:** Use a mask to represent an original character and narrate three events in a clear sequence, with each pupil contributing a line.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils recall the poem through a memory chain and retell its ending briefly.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Show a sample mask. Groups choose a character and plan what happens before, during and after the tooth comes out.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils make the mask and rehearse their new story. Each pupil contributes at least one line using a sequence cue where suitable.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Groups present their masked story. Prompt listeners to note an event they remember from another group.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils record one memorable story event and tell a partner why they remember it.

**PBD:** Check that the mask and story show an imaginative response. Listen for each pupil’s line and the order of the three events.
**Differentiation:** Use a mask outline and first, next and finally cards. Give pupils time to rehearse their own lines.
