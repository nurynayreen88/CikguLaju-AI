# English Year 5 Lesson 156 — Speaking 27

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 156, printed p.198. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.2.2 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.99 Activities 4–5, TB p.113, CD3.20 and a Malaysia map.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils agree how to carry out a pair task and give a short weather report that presents conditions in sequence.
**Success criteria:** Agree roles, place and questions, then give an intelligible report with two linked weather details.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review compass points and weather symbols from SB p.99.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Play CD3.20 for SB Activity 4. Pupils identify the speakers’ roles and practise the exchange about Brighton.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Before SB Activity 5, pairs agree who will ask first, which city to use and which questions to ask. They practise and swap roles.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs mark predicted conditions for a Malaysian city before giving a brief forecast in time order. One partner asks a question about the report.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Listeners name the place and repeat one condition they heard.

**PBD:** Hear both partners agree on the task steps. Check that each pupil gives two coherent weather details in a short spoken report.
**Differentiation:** Provide question stems and a map key. Ask confident pupils to add a time transition or contrast conditions in two places.
