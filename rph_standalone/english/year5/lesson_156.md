# Year 5 Lesson 156 — Speaking 27

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.

Status: DRAF. Unit 8. Main LS 2.2.2; complementary LS 2.3.1.
Materials: Student Book p.99 Activities 4–5; Teacher Book p.113; CD3-20; Malaysia map and weather data. Workbook mapping and audio playback remain unverified.

Objective: Pupils agree with a partner on a weather-report plan (roles, city and questions), ask and answer relevant questions, and deliver a short understandable forecast.

Set induction (5 min): Recall weather vocabulary and directions.

Main activities (40 min): Listen to CD3-20; pairs agree who speaks first, which city to describe and which questions to ask. Prepare and present a short forecast for Malaysian locations. Teacher models instructions and provides feedback.

PBD (10 min): Teacher observes one successful turn allocation, a relevant forecast question and response, and an understandable forecast using place and weather language.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and submit an exit response.

Differentiation: Use visuals, sentence frames and paired support where needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent work.
