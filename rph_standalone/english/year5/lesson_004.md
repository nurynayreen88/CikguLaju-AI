# English Year 5 — Lesson 004 — Writing: Classroom Objects

**Official SOW topic:** Free time.

**SOW:** Primary Year 5 SK, Lesson 4, printed p.38. **Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends. **CCE:** Language and Creativity. **Suggested time:** 60 minutes.
**Main CS/LS:** Writing 4.2 / 4.2.4 — Describe people, places and objects using suitable statements.
**Complementary CS/LS:** Listening 1.2 / 1.2.4 — Understand a sequence of supported classroom instructions.
**Grammar:** Prepositions of place: on, near, next to.
**Objective:** Write sentences describing positions of classroom objects and follow oral descriptions to draw object arrangements.
**Success criteria:** Pupils write intelligible object-location sentences and draw the arrangement described by a partner.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student's Book p.6 Activities 1–5; Teacher's Book p.20; drawing paper and pencils. No audio is prescribed by SOW.

## Teaching sequence
1. **Set induction (8 min):** Teacher creates a classroom-objects mind map; pupils group objects as furniture, electrical items, writing/reading items and wall items.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils do SB p.6 Activity 1 and discuss spelling challenges including *dictionary*. Teacher follows TB p.20 Exercise 2 to introduce or check position descriptions.
3. **Activity 2 (12 min):** Pairs study Activity 2 sentences for one minute, close books and write remembered sentences (Activity 3). Teacher asks who remembers three, five, seven or more.
4. **Activity 3 (20 min):** Pupils draw a desk and write sentences describing objects on it (Activity 4). Partners compare drawings and sentences. Following TB p.20 Exercise 5, one pupil describes their picture while the other listens and draws it.
5. **PBD and closure (8 min):** Teacher assesses prepositions in sentences and whether pupils follow supported oral instructions. Discuss creativity in pupils' drawings and the wall drawing in Activity 1. Use TB p.20 optional vocabulary activity if time allows.

**Differentiation:** Mixed-ability partners; one pupil dictates and the other writes, then swap. More confident pupils use several accurate position descriptions.
