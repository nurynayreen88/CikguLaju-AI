# English Year 5 Lesson 4 — Writing 1

**Official SOW topic:** Free time.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 4, printed p.38. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.4 and Listening 1.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.6 Activities 1–5, TB p.20, WB p.6 Ex3, paper and pencils.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write descriptions of classroom objects and follow a sequence of spoken instructions to draw a desk arrangement.
**Success criteria:** Write three accurate object-location sentences and place three objects in response to spoken instructions given in order.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Build a classroom-object mind map by grouping items found in the room.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activities 1–2 to name objects and complete prepositions. For Activity 3, pupils write as many locations as they recall, then check.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** In SB Activity 4, pupils draw and label an original desk scene, then write three sentences to describe it.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Adapt SB Activity 5 by having one pupil give three supported placement instructions while a partner draws. They compare drawings and switch roles. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.6 Exercise 3.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs choose one sentence that best explains where an object belongs.

**PBD:** Read three written location sentences and check each pupil’s drawing against the ordered oral instructions.
**Differentiation:** Provide a desk outline, preposition cards and a model. Challenge confident pupils to use between or opposite accurately.
