# English Year 5 Lesson 127 — Reading 22

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 127, printed p.168. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.4 and Speaking 2.1.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.73 Activity 7 and Key Phrases, SB p.61 Activity 5, TB p.87 and a picture glossary or dictionary.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils check two unfamiliar appearance words using a familiar reference and use at least three suitable details to describe a person.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show photos and ask pupils which clues would help identify the people. Review phrases for saying how sure they are.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** For SB p.73 Activity 7, pairs read the written clues and use the photos to identify the people. They flag two words they want to check.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils consult a picture glossary, familiar page or dictionary to verify the two words. Pairs show each other the reference and use one checked word in a description.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use SB p.61 Activity 5. Each pupil describes a person in the drawings with three details while a partner listens and identifies the drawing.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share one word they checked and the reference that they used.

**PBD:** Check two accurate meanings supported by a reference and three clear details in the spoken description.
**Differentiation:** Provide a labelled picture bank. Ask confident pupils to distinguish two people who share one feature.
