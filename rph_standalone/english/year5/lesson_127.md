# Year 5 Lesson 127 — Reading 22

Status: DRAF. Unit 7. Topic: Growing up. Official SOW p.168. Main LS 3.2.4; complementary LS 2.1.5.
Materials: SB p.73 Activity 7 and p.61 Activity 5; TB p.87. Workbook page not verified. Audio playback not tested.

Objectives: Pupils complete the stated tasks and demonstrate both prescribed standards.

Set induction (5 min): Set a reading goal and review phrases for guessing people's identities.

Main activities (40 min): Pairs use photos and written clues in Activity 7 to identify people, check unfamiliar appearance vocabulary with familiar resources, and describe a child from p.61 for partners to guess. Teacher models instructions, monitors work and gives feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check resource-based meaning verification and accurate descriptions. Pupils correct or improve one response.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share one learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide word banks, visual cues, shorter instructions and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with explanations or more detailed output.
