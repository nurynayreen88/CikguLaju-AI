# English Year 5 Lesson 130 — Consolidation 13

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 130, printed p.171. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.2 and Listening 1.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.98 Activities 1–5, TB p.112, CD3.17, CD3.18 and photograph prompts.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify two specific details from the recorded conversations and respond to three supported questions about a photo in order.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show a photo. Pupils predict where the people are and what they might be doing.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 1 to compare the photos. Play CD3.17 for Activity 2 and have pupils match the conversation with the correct scene.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Replay CD3.17 for SB Activity 3. Ask three supported questions in order about the people, the activity and the time. Pupils record brief answers.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD3.18 for Activity 4 so pupils can check how the speakers refer to people and places in a photo. In Activity 5, partners ask and answer a similar sequence about a different photo.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils which detail helped them recognise one person or place in the recording.

**PBD:** Check two specific details drawn from the recording and three replies to the spoken question sequence.
**Differentiation:** Provide name cards and picture choices. Ask confident pupils to explain which words ruled out another photo.
