# English Year 5 Lesson 31 — Reading 6

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 31, printed p.66. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.4 and Writing 4.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.106 Activities 1–2, TB p.120, CD 3-27, Workbook p.18 Exercise 2, maps and a dictionary.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils check the meaning of two words with a familiar dictionary and write two accurate statements about the locations of Malaysian cities.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review UK capital cities on the map and ask pupils to name Malaysia’s capital.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils match the places on the map in SB Activity 1, read Toby’s text and identify which city is outside the list of capitals.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Model compass directions. Pairs use the map and a print or digital dictionary to check two unfamiliar words from the text.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD 3-27 for SB Activity 2. Pupils write and compare answers, then find three things Toby likes about Liverpool.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Groups describe four cities on the Malaysia map. If time permits, pupils answer two items from Workbook p.18 Exercise 2 using the cruise-ship text.

**PBD:** Check two recorded word meanings with their sources and two written location statements against the map.
**Differentiation:** Give a map with compass labels and model one dictionary entry.
