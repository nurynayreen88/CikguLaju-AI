# English Year 5 Lesson 31 — Reading 6

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.

**Official SOW:** Lesson 31, printed p.66. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main/complementary CS and LS:** Reading 3.2.4; Writing 4.2.4.
**Materials:** SB p.106 Activities 1–2; TB p.120; CD 3-27; UK and Malaysia maps.
**Learning objectives:** Main (Reading 3.2.4) — pupils use with support familiar print and digital resources to check meaning in the Towns and cities activities. Complementary (Writing 4.2.4) — pupils describe people, places and objects using suitable statements in the linked writing task.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review UK capitals using TB warm-up and ask for Malaysia's capital.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Read Activity 1 and identify Liverpool as the city that is not a capital.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use a compass and UK map to locate cities and practise directions.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Listen to CD 3-27 for Activity 2; write and compare answers; find three things Toby likes about Liverpool.
5. **PBD and closure (8 min):** Describe positions of four Malaysian cities on a map; assess reading, dictionary/resource use and descriptions.

**Differentiation:** Allow more reading time; fast finishers identify the pictured city.
**PBD:** Teacher checks two retrieved facts from the Liverpool reading task and one accurate Malaysian city-location statement; pupils explain where the text or map supports their answer.
