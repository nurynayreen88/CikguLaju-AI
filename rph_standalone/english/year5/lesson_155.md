# English Year 5 Lesson 155 — Listening 27

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 155, printed p.197. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.3 and Listening 1.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.99 Activities 1–3, TB p.113, CD3.19, WB p.74 Ex4 and a map of Britain.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow a forecast from current to weekend conditions and identify specific weather details.
**Success criteria:** Distinguish current and weekend reports, and record three accurate details about place, condition or temperature.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show a weather symbol and ask pupils to explain what a forecast tells people.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils interpret symbols in SB Activity 1 and use the map to locate Edinburgh, London and Brighton.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Play CD3.19 for SB Activity 2. Pupils match sections of the forecast to places and arrange current and weekend headings in the order heard.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Replay for SB Activity 3. Pupils identify weather and temperatures under the correct heading. If time and the Workbook recording are available, pupils complete Workbook p.74 Exercise 4 as a separate listening task.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils use a map symbol to report one forecast detail and identify when it applies.

**PBD:** Check the order of the forecast and three details under the correct time heading.
**Differentiation:** Offer a two-column chart and a symbol key. Ask confident pupils to retrieve one further detail about the coast.
