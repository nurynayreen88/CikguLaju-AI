# English Year 5 Lesson 125 — Listening 22

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 125, printed p.166. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.1.1 and Speaking 2.1.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.72 Activities 1–3, TB pp.86–87, CD2.21–CD2.22 and Workbook p.60 Exercise 1.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise and reproduce the target vowel sounds in four words and give two specific, positive details about themselves.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Draw a simple avatar. Pupils suggest words for its features and predict how it might be described.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** For SB Activity 1, pairs match the appearance words to pictures. Use CD2.21 in Activity 2 to check descriptions and practise saying true details about themselves.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD2.22 for SB Activity 3. Pupils classify words by vowel sound and pronounce four examples individually after the listening task.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils say two true, specific details about themselves to a partner. Play a short spot-the-change game using voluntary descriptions without naming classmates.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share one sound contrast. If time permits, pupils match two sentence halves in Workbook p.60 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Listen for four accurate vowel sounds and two intelligible personal details.
**Differentiation:** Display picture and sound cards. Ask confident pupils to identify another word with one of the target sounds.
