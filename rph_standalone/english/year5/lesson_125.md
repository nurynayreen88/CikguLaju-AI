# Year 5 Lesson 125 — Listening 22

Status: DRAF. Unit 7. Topic: Growing up. Official SOW p.166. Main LS 1.1.1; complementary LS 2.1.1.
Materials: SB p.72 Activities 1–3; TB pp.86–87; CD2-21 and CD2-22. Workbook page unverified; audio playback not tested.

Objective: Pupils demonstrate learning through the specific tasks below, covering both selected or prescribed skills.

Set induction (5 min): Draw a stick figure and brainstorm appearance vocabulary.

Main activities (40 min): Use Activity 1 to describe features and discuss avatars. Listen to CD2-21 for Activity 2 and practise factual self-descriptions. Use CD2-22 in Activity 3 to identify long sounds; play Spot the mistake using changed adjectives. Teacher models instructions and monitors pupil responses, giving actionable feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check sound discrimination and respectful, accurate self-description. Pupils revise or explain one response.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide word banks, visual prompts and sentence frames for support; extend proficient pupils with independent explanations or richer outputs.
