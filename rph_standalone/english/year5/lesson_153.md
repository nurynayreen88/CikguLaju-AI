# English Year 5 Lesson 153 — Language Arts 26

**Official SOW topic:** Gulliver’s Travels.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 153, printed p.195. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.3.1 and Writing 4.3.1.
**Materials:** Gulliver’s Travels graphic novel, BPK Graphic Novel Teaching Guide Join the Pieces jigsaw, blank panels and pencils.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils adapt two graphic novel panels to a Malaysian setting and punctuate their own dialogue.
**Success criteria:** Produce two recognisable adapted panels with suitable setting details and check capitals, full stops and any question marks.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the guide’s Join the Pieces jigsaw. Groups assemble and label the three settings.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs select two panels and identify what happens. Model how to transfer one setting detail to a Malaysian context.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pairs adapt setting or clothing and write new speech bubbles. Ask each pupil to include a question or a statement suited to their character.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs exchange panels and check punctuation. Pupils revise and read the adapted dialogue to the class.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils give a star rating and explain which detail made the new setting recognisable.

**PBD:** Check that both panels retain an identifiable story event and adapt it imaginatively. Check the finished speech bubbles for appropriate punctuation.
**Differentiation:** Offer a setting word bank and empty speech bubbles. Ask confident pairs to show how a detail of the setting affects the dialogue.
