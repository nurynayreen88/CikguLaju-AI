# English Year 5 Lesson 158 — Writing 27

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 158, printed p.200. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.3 and Speaking 2.1.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.111 Activities 4–5, TB p.125 and three Malaysian theme park fact files.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recount factual events about a theme park in writing and ask and answer about an actual experience at a park or another outing.
**Success criteria:** Write two accurately sequenced facts using the fact file. Ask one experience question and give a relevant spoken answer.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review two dates or numbers from the theme park article and ask pupils what happened at each point.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Assign each group a different Malaysian park. Pupils complete the table in SB Activity 4 with the opening date, attractions and other activities.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils write a short factual account that includes the opening and a later fact or an activity at the park. They use a suitable superlative where the facts support it.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** For SB Activity 5, mixed groups exchange park facts. Partners then ask about a ride, park or other outing they have experienced and share what they did.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one written fact and report an experience a partner described.

**PBD:** Check that two facts follow a clear sequence and match the source. Listen for an experience question and a relevant answer.
**Differentiation:** Supply a fact table and time connectors. Ask confident pupils to include an accurate comparison supported by the supplied facts.
