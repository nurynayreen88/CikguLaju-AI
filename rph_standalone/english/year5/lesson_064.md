# English Year 5 Lesson 64 — Consolidation 6

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 64, printed p.101. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.2.1 and Listening 1.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.38 Activities 2–5, TB p.52, CD1.36 and Workbook p.35 Exercise 7.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow two supported questions in the recorded dialogue and sustain a short exchange by asking permission and adding a follow-up question.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the SB photograph. Pupils predict where Lottie and her father are and what she might ask to do.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Play CD1.36 for SB Activity 2. Pupils track Lottie’s requests and match two of her questions to her father’s replies. Replay the key lines for pairs to practise the intonation.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs complete SB Activity 3 and rehearse the exchange. The teacher compares the language for granting and politely refusing permission before pupils practise Activity 4.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils change partners for SB Activity 5 and act out a new permission request. The caller asks a follow-up when the first reply is a refusal, and partners swap roles.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Two pairs demonstrate their exchange. If time permits, pupils complete two gaps in the permission dialogue in Workbook p.35 Exercise 7.

**PBD:** Check two matches between heard questions and responses. Listen for a clear permission request, a suitable follow-up and a polite reply.
**Differentiation:** Provide request and response cards. Ask confident pupils to offer a different time or activity after a refusal.
