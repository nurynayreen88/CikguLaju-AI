# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 052 (Language Awareness 3)

**Status:** DRAF — final source and A4 checks pending. **Topic:** Days, Unit 2 review. **Main/complementary LS:** Selected by the teacher from pupils' Unit 2 needs; no fixed pair is asserted here. **Duration:** 60 minutes.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student Book p.30 Activities 3–6 and p.31 Activity 3; Teacher Book pp.44–45; Unit 2 self-assessment sheet. Workbook pp.26–27 Progress Review (previous mapping; optional).

**Objective:** Pupils consolidate Unit 2 language, give a reason for one answer and identify a personal strength and one area to practise.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher revisits vocabulary and structures from Days using a short pre-lesson task.

**Activity 1 — Review independently (15 min):** Pupils complete SB p.30 Activities 3–5. After each, they compare answers with a different partner; teacher checks responses and asks which item was difficult and why.

**Activity 2 — Oral practice (15 min):** In pairs, pupils complete SB p.30 Activity 6, ask the questions in turn and respond in full enough to be understood. Teacher notes recurring language needs.

**Activity 3 — Targeted follow-up (15 min):** Pupils complete SB p.31 Activity 3 using TB p.45. Teacher uses observed errors and written work to select additional Unit 2 practice appropriate to the class; pupils correct selected answers.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher samples each pupil's written responses, observes one pair exchange and records one specific recurring error or successful language feature.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils complete a brief self-assessment (one strength, one next step). Teacher collects it to plan subsequent support.

**Differentiation:** Offer language prompts and paired explanation; provide an extension question for confident pupils. **Workbook:** WB pp.26–27 Ex1–8 are optional aligned review activities, not a replacement for the SOW tasks. **QA:** Teacher choice of LS, workbook exercise, source pages and A4 output await final approval.
