# Year 5 Lesson 52 — Language Awareness 3

Official SOW p.89–90. Topic: Days, Unit 2 review.
Standards: SOW leaves main and complementary CS/LS to teacher selection from Year 5 DSKP.
Sources: Student Book p.30 Activities 3–6 and p.31 Activity 3; Teacher Book pp.44–45.

1. Review Unit 2 language about routines and celebrations.
2. Pupils complete Activities 3–5 on p.30 and compare answers with different partners.
3. Pairs complete Activity 6 and practise asking and answering.
4. Complete p.31 Activity 3 following Teacher Book p.45.
5. Pupils complete Unit 2 self-assessment using Great, OK or A little. Teacher reviews responses.

Differentiation: Pupils may use their own language for self-assessment.
PBD: Review task responses and self-assessment to plan support.
