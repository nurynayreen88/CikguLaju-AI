# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 052 (Language Awareness 3)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned revision, pending final visual/audio and teacher QA. **Topic:** Days. **Main LS:** Teacher selects. **Complementary LS:** Teacher selects. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Review of language from Unit 2. **Materials:** Student Book p.30 Activities 3–6 and p.31 Activity 3; Teacher Book pp.44–45..

**Learning objective:** Pupils use Days language to complete the prescribed Language Awareness 3 tasks and demonstrate main LS teacher-selected.

**Set induction (5 min):** Review language from Unit 2 with an activity from list of pre-lesson tasks.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do p.30, Activities 3, 4 and 5 individually. After each activity they compare answers with a different partner. Check answers after each activity. Ask: Which activity was easy and which was hard? Why was Activity 3/4/5 easy/hard?

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 6 then take turns to ask the questions and say answers.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils do p.31, Activity 3. Follow instructions for Exercise 3 on p.45 in Teacher’s Book. Note:  Plan further activities for this unit to develop language skills according to your pupils’ needs.  You can use information about your pupils’ performance which you collected using formative assessment strategies while teaching this unit.  The information could include your observation of pupils interacting in classroom activities and their performance in written activities.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher assesses pupils' responses to the specific SOW activities and gives corrective feedback.

**Closure (5 min):** *Pupils think about their learning and performance in this unit. They complete self- assessment worksheet. 6. Collect worksheets from pupils and review them to note pupils’ responses. If there are any areas of concern, prepare a review of these in future lessons.

**Workbook link (from existing verified mapping):** Workbook p.26-27, Unit 2 Progress Review — complete Unit 2 review, Ex1-8. Use as brief practice or evidence without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Provide visual models or paired support for pupils who need help; extend confident pupils through independent responses. **QA note:** Workbook mapping is a prior source QA result; Student Book, Teacher Book, audio-content and A4 layout have not all been independently verified.
