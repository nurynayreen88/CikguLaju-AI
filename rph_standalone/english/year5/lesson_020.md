# English Year 5 Lesson 20 — Writing 4

Official SOW: Lesson 20, printed p.56. Unit 1: Towns and cities. Suggested time: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2/4.2.4; Speaking 2.2/2.2.1.
Learning objective: Pupils demonstrate the stated skills through SOW-prescribed tasks.
Materials: SB p.15 Activities 1, 5, 6 and Finished!; TB p.29.

1. Set induction (8 min): Pairs recall facilities on the cruise ship from Lesson 19.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete SB Activity 1 following TB p.29 Exercise 1.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Imagine a holiday park and complete SB Activity 5, selecting its facilities.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Use SB Activity 6 and TB p.29 Exercise 6 to ask group questions; write a holiday park description with adjectives, adverbs, conjunctions and there is/are.
5. Assessment and closure (8 min): Exchange descriptions for peer feedback and finish with Correct the Mistake; assess descriptive writing.

PBD: Collect task-specific spoken, written, listening or reading evidence. Record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching.
Differentiation: Praise three to five accurate sentences, with support for pupils requiring it.
