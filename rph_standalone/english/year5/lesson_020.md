# English Year 5 Lesson 20 — Writing 4

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 20, printed p.55. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.4 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.15 Activities 1, 5–6 and Finished?, TB p.29 and WB p.13 Ex4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write a description of an imagined holiday park and ask a follow-up question about a partner’s park.
**Success criteria:** Write three clear statements about park facilities and keep an exchange going with a relevant follow-up question.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall facilities on the cruise ship and brainstorm which could appear in a holiday park.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Review questions about places in SB Activity 1. In Activity 5, pupils plan an imaginary park and draft statements about its facilities.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use SB Activity 6 for a group exchange. Pupils ask about each park and follow up on one detail heard before choosing the park they prefer.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pupils use Finished? to polish their independent descriptions with appropriate adjectives, adverbs or conjunctions. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.13 Exercise 4.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs read one statement and explain what makes the description clear.

**PBD:** Check three intelligible written descriptions and listen for a follow-up question linked to the previous answer.
**Differentiation:** Give a model park map and question stems. Ask confident pupils to add a useful contrast between two parks.
