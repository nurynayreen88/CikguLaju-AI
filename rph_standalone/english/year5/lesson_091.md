# English Year 5 — Lesson 91 (Reading 16)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** Reading 3.2.3. **Complementary LS:** Reading 3.2.4. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.54 Activities 1–3; TB p.68; CD2.05; vitamin A/B/C fact handout. Workbook mapping and audio content (where applicable) not independently verified.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills through observable lesson tasks.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher displays The sports interview… Sumo Style; pupils predict text content from photographs.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils read the interview for Activity 1, identify the sumo wrestler's routine and discuss healthy and unhealthy practices.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 2 with CD2.05 and summarise the interview. Teacher uses a vitamins fact file to connect food choices with health.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils infer blue vocabulary from text and pictures in Activity 3, check meanings in a dictionary, and explain typical, train and enormous in context.

**PBD (5 min):** Pupils infer two unfamiliar expressions from contextual clues and support one answer with information from the reading.

**Closure (5 min):** Pairs test one another with two interview questions.

**Differentiation:** Use photo and word clues; extend with evidence about nutrition.
