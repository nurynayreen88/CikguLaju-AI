# English Year 5 Lesson 91 — Reading 16

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 91, printed p.130. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Reading 3.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.54 Activities 1–3, TB p.68, CD2.05, dictionaries and Workbook p.48 Exercise 5.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer two meanings from the sumo interview and use a dictionary or classroom reference to check one of them.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the title and photos. Pupils predict whether the interview will focus on training, food or both.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 1, pupils read the interview and match its sections to the prompts. Pairs flag two unfamiliar words and underline nearby clues.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Read and listen with CD2.05 for SB Activity 2. Pupils complete the summary and revisit their two guesses using details from the text.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** In SB Activity 3, pupils look up one word and choose the meaning that fits the interview. They explain why the dictionary meaning fits the clue.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one inferred meaning and how they checked it. If time permits, they complete one related word in Workbook p.48 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Check two plausible meanings backed by clues and one accurate reference check.
**Differentiation:** Mark the relevant sentence for pupils who need support. Ask confident pupils to explain why an alternative dictionary meaning does not fit.
