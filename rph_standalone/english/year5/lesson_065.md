# English Year 5 Lesson 65 — Listening 12

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 65, printed p.102. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.1.1 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.40 Activities 1–3 and 7, TB p.54, CD1.37 and Workbook p.34 Exercise 1.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise and reproduce contrasting vowel sounds in at least four words and choose one interesting fact from the printed animal information.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils take turns naming an animal for a short alphabet game. The teacher writes bear and eagle and asks whether the highlighted letters sound alike.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils complete SB Activity 1. The teacher models the vowel contrast and pairs find and pronounce more examples while sorting the words by sound.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete SB Activity 2 and read the animal information in Activity 3. Each chooses a fact that interests them, then explains the choice to a partner.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD1.37 for SB Activity 7 and allow pairs to check the information they heard. Pupils find and say east and eat, then compare the vowel with the sound in bear.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs create a true-or-false statement about an animal using the table. If time permits, pupils complete two animal words in Workbook p.34 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Listen for accurate vowel sounds in four target words and check that each chosen reading fact is supported by the printed information.
**Differentiation:** Display the words in two sound groups and model them before individual practice. Ask confident pupils to add and pronounce another example.
