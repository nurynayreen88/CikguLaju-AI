# English Year 5 — Lesson 65 (Listening 12)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source audit. **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** Listening 1.1.1. **Complementary LS:** Reading 3.3.1. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.40 Activities 1–3 and 7; TB p.54; CD1.37. Workbook/audio contents are not independently verified; use only SOW-prescribed resources.

**Objective:** Pupils carry out the skill-specific tasks and demonstrate the stated learning standards.

**Set induction (5 min):** Class recalls animal vocabulary in an ABC game.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 1 and compare spelling. Teacher contrasts ea in bear/eagle using pear/peach to model sound differences.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs complete Activity 2; find more ea examples such as hear, heavy and ear and sort by pronunciation. Pupils practise repeating each word accurately.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils study the animal-information table in Activity 3 and then listen to CD1.37 for Activity 7, paying attention to east/eat and differences in ea sounds.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks recognition and reproduction of ea sounds using four selected words; pupils respond orally and self-correct.

**Closure (5 min):** Pairs write two or three true/false questions from the animal table for another pair.

**Differentiation:** Provide spoken models and repeated listening; extend with a new word using a target sound.
