# English Year 5 Lesson 92 — Writing 16

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 92, printed p.131. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.1 and Reading 3.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.54 Activity 4, p.55 Activities 1–3, pp.52–53 school-food texts, TB pp.68–69, dictionaries and Workbook p.45 Exercise 1.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils check unfamiliar food vocabulary using a printed resource and independently write an opinion paragraph with appropriate punctuation and a question.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review key words from the sumo interview. Pupils recall one detail about the wrestler’s routine.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB p.54 Activity 4 to discuss whether the routine sounds interesting. Pupils independently write a short opinion paragraph with a list of reasons and a question for the reader.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** For SB p.55 Activities 1–2, pupils consult the earlier food texts and photographs to check two word meanings before classifying food words.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils complete SB p.55 Activity 3 and apply the food terms in a shared example. They review their own paragraph for capitals, commas in the list, full stops and the question mark.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils read one sentence from their paragraph. If time permits, they classify two foods in Workbook p.45 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Check two word meanings against the source and inspect punctuation in the pupil’s own paragraph.
**Differentiation:** Provide a paragraph frame and point pupils to the relevant text. Ask confident pupils to explain which reference helped most.
