# English Year 5 — Lesson 92 (Writing 16)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** Writing 4.3.1. **Complementary LS:** Reading 3.2.4. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.54 Activity 4 and p.55 Activities 1–3; TB pp.68–69; SB pp.52–53. Workbook mapping and audio content (where applicable) not independently verified.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills through observable lesson tasks.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils complete a vocabulary review based on the previous sumo interview.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pairs decide whether a sumo wrestler's life is interesting and list three reasons. They draft a paragraph starting We think a wrestler's life is… because… and share opinions.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete SB p.55 Activities 1–2, rereading earlier food texts and using photos to check vocabulary about fruit and vegetables.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 3, compare answers and revise their paragraph for punctuation and clear reasoning.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks the draft for three connected reasons, understandable sentence punctuation and a completed revision.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils draw a fruit-and-vegetable face and describe it using there's/there are with appropriate quantifiers.

**Differentiation:** Offer a reason organizer and punctuation checklist; extend to contrasting viewpoints.
