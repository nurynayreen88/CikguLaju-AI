# English Year 5 Lesson 66: Speaking 12

Official SOW Lesson 66, printed p.104. Topic: Wild Life. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Speaking 2.2.1; Writing 4.3.2.
Materials: SB p.40 Activities 4-6; TB p.54.

1. Set induction (8 min): Play Sit Down Stand Up with can/cannot ability statements.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete Activity 4 and check understanding.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Complete Activity 5; pairs read yes/no questions and answers.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Complete Activity 6, then practise asking permission and refusing politely.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs practise borrowing objects politely; groups describe family members' abilities.
