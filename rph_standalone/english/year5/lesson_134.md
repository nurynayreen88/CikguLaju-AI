# English Year 5 Lesson 134 — Writing 23

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 134, printed p.175. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.1 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.77 Activities 4–5 and Finished?, TB p.91 and Workbook p.63 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three specific details about themselves and orally recount three connected events from a short story.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask pupils to recall one past activity and give a specific detail about when or where it happened.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 4, pairs create a short imaginary paragraph, ask questions about it and tell its events in order using their own words.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use SB Activity 5 to form and answer past questions. Pupils independently write a short personal account that includes three details about their own experiences.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use Finished? for pupils to ask a further question. Each tells a partner a short account of a real or imaginary event with a clear start, next event and ending.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite one pupil to share a connected account. If time permits, pupils write one past interview question using Workbook p.63 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Check three specific personal details in the writing and listen for an intelligible three-part oral recount.
**Differentiation:** Offer time and sequence prompts. Ask confident pupils to show how the final event follows from an earlier one.
