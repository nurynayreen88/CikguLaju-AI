# Year 5 Lesson 134 — Writing 23

Status: DRAF. Unit 7 Growing up. Main LS 4.2.1; complementary LS 2.3.1. Materials: SB p.77 Activities 4–5; TB p.91.

Objective: Demonstrate the prescribed skills in the listed tasks.

Set induction (5 min): Recall film director's past events.

Main activities (40 min): Write a paragraph about a famous city, actor and sports star. Narrate it to a partner; write and exchange past-experience questions using When did you last...? Display the illustrated paragraph. Teacher models, monitors and supports.

PBD (10 min): Check detailed writing and short oral narrative. Pupils revise one response.

Closure (5 min): Share a learning point.

Differentiation: Sentence frames for support; independent detail for extension.

Verification: Official SOW checked; book-page, workbook and audio audit pending. Do not mark READY.
