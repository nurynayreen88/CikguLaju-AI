# Year 5 English Lesson 63 - Language Arts 11

SOW page 101. Main and complementary skills: Language Arts 5.3.1 and Writing 4.2.4.
Sources: Non-textbook. And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby, Chapter 4 pages 25-32; BPK Short Stories Guidebook.

Set induction: Play Beat the Teacher using pizza, ice cream and dinosaur. Main activity: Read Chapter 4 in groups; draw Joel's Jelly Baby ice cream and imagined dinosaurs; invent new versions in pairs; describe creations using simple sentences. Closure: Predict what happens when Joel meets dinosaurs.

Assessment: Check pupil responses and work against the specified skills.
Differentiation: Accept words, phrases or full sentences.
