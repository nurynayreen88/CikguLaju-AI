# English Year 5 Lesson 140 — Writing 24

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 140, printed p.181. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.5 and Listening 1.1.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.119 Activity 5 and the song text, TB p.133, CD3.36 and rhyme cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise and reproduce three rhyming sound pairs and connect sentences about an imagined life into a coherent paragraph.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils recall events from the song and predict which words might sound alike at the ends of lines.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Replay CD3.36. Pupils identify three rhyming pairs, then pronounce the words and compare their sounds even when the spellings differ.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use SB Activity 5 for pupils to interview a partner about an imagined life. Each notes a few details that can be written in order.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils write one coherent paragraph about the imagined life. They link two details with a conjunction and use a clear pronoun reference.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Partners read the paragraphs and identify one effective link. Ask pupils to say one of the sound pairs without a model.

**PBD:** Listen for three accurately recognised and pronounced sound pairs. Check a conjunction and a clear pronoun reference in the pupil’s paragraph.
**Differentiation:** Provide rhyme cards and a paragraph frame. Ask confident pupils to add a new rhyme and connect an extra event.
