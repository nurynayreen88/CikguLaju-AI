# Year 5 Lesson 140 — Writing 24

Status: DRAF. Unit 7; topic: Growing up. SOW lesson 140. Main LS 4.2.5; complementary LS 1.1.1.
Materials: SB p.119 Activity 5; TB p.133; CD3-36. Workbook page unverified; listed audio not playback-tested.

Objectives: Pupils perform the lesson task and show evidence of both prescribed standards.

Set induction (5 min): Recall words from The Story of Your Life.

Main activities (40 min): Listen for rhymes in CD3-36, distinguish fun/one and sun/son, complete Activity 5, then imagine being 60 and write a coherent life story using connectors and pronouns; exchange for feedback. Teacher models instructions, monitors work and gives feedback.

PBD (10 min): Assess connected paragraphs and recognition of rhyming sounds. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils state one learning point and complete a brief exit response.

Differentiation: Use visuals, sentence frames and paired rehearsal for support; extend proficient pupils with fuller explanations and independent work.
