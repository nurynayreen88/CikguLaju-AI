# English Year 5 Lesson 33 — Language Arts 6

**Official SOW topic:** Serenade.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 33, printed p.68. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.3.1 and Speaking 2.1.5.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.17, Serenade, BPK Poetry Guidebook p.63 Activity 7 and clean recycled craft materials.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create an instrument inspired by Serenade and describe it clearly during a gallery walk.
**Success criteria:** Make an instrument that can create a sound effect from the poem. Describe two features and answer one visitor question.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Reread Serenade and ask pupils to distinguish the sounds in the poem.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Show the BPK Orchestra activity example. Pairs choose a sound they want to recreate and sketch an instrument using the materials available.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pairs build and test their instrument. Each pupil tries a sound and prepares a description of a feature, such as its material or how it works.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Hold a gallery walk. Partners alternate demonstrating the sound and describing the instrument to visitors.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Use some of the instruments to add sound effects as pupils recite a stanza.

**PBD:** Check the imaginative link between the instrument and poem. Hear each pupil describe two features and answer a relevant question.
**Differentiation:** Provide a sample design and material words. Invite confident pupils to explain why they chose a particular material.
