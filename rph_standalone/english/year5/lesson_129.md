# English Year 5 Lesson 129 — Language Arts 22

**Official SOW topic:** Gulliver’s Travels.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 129, printed p.170. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** Gulliver’s Travels graphic novel pp.6–13 and the BPK Graphic Novel Teaching Guide.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils select a panel they enjoy reading and explain a like or dislike with a reason linked to that panel.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the cover and blurb to predict where the traveller might go and what he might find.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Groups read pp.6–13 and select a panel that seems impossible in real life. Each pupil points to a visual or written detail and shares a reaction.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** One pupil stays with the panel while the others visit another group. Each visitor listens to a reason and returns to report a detail they noticed.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Groups reread the pocket inventory and explain why Gulliver might carry one object. Each pupil then chooses a favourite panel and gives one reason for their preference.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils choose one object they would carry to an unfamiliar place and relate it to an idea from their reading.

**PBD:** Check a personal preference with one clear, text-linked reason and a detail the pupil independently selected after reading.
**Differentiation:** Provide enlarged panel cues and because stems. Ask confident pupils to contrast two panels.
