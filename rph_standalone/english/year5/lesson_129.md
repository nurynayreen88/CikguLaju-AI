# Year 5 Lesson 129 — Language Arts 22

Status: DRAF. Unit 7. Topic: Gulliver's Travels. Official SOW p.170. Main LS 5.2.1; complementary LS 3.3.1.
Materials: Gulliver's Travels graphic novel pp.6–13; BPK CCL Graphic Novel guide. Workbook is not prescribed. Audio playback not tested.

Objectives: Pupils complete the stated tasks and demonstrate both prescribed standards.

Set induction (5 min): Discuss destinations pupils would visit if they had money.

Main activities (40 min): Read cover, blurb and graphic novel pp.6–13 in groups; identify one impossible panel and justify it in a 1 Stay 3 Stray exchange; discuss plausible reasons for Gulliver's comb, silver coin and watch. Teacher models instructions, monitors work and gives feedback.

PBD (10 min): Assess textual engagement and a reasoned personal response to a chosen scene. Pupils correct or improve one response.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share one learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide word banks, visual cues, shorter instructions and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with explanations or more detailed output.

Verification: Based on official Year 5 SOW. Full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.

**Non-textbook source rule:** Use the SOW-designated literature and CCL teaching guide. Do not assign an English Plus Student Book, Workbook or CD track to this Language Arts lesson.
