# Year 5 Lesson 132 — Speaking 23

Status: DRAF. Unit 7 Growing up. Official SOW Lesson 132. Main LS 2.1.1; complementary LS 4.2.1. Materials: Student Book p.76 Activities 4–5, p.73 Key Phrases; Teacher Book p.90.

Objective: Pupils give detailed spoken and written information about a family member's life.

Set induction (5 min): Recall facts and dates from Robert Wadlow's story.

Main activities (40 min): Teacher reviews was born, grew up, went to school, left school, got a job, travelled to. Pupils write a short account of a family member, using cautious language where facts are unknown. Pupils share their account orally; listeners note the past-tense verbs they hear. Complete Activity 5 and compare answers.

PBD (10 min): Check clarity of spoken details and accurate written life events. Pupils correct one verb form.

Closure (5 min): Share one past-tense expression.

Differentiation: Offer life-event sentence frames or require more detailed accounts.
