# English Year 5 Lesson 132 — Speaking 23

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 132, printed p.173. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.1 and Writing 4.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.76 Activities 4–5 and p.73 Key Phrases, TB p.90 and life-event cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils share two specific personal details about past experiences and write two clear sentences giving details about their own lives.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall two life events from the previous lesson. Ask pupils when or where something similar happened to them.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB p.76 Activity 4 for pupils to practise affirmative and negative past forms and draft a short description about someone they know.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** For SB Activity 5, pairs talk about their own past experiences, respond to surprising details and ask when or where an event happened.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils independently write two true personal sentences with specific details such as a time, place or person. Partners compare one detail that is similar and one that is different.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share one detail they have written or heard. Use the Key Phrases on p.73 to distinguish certainty from guesses where relevant.

**PBD:** Listen for two intelligible personal details and check two specific, independently written sentences.
**Differentiation:** Offer time and place prompts. Ask confident pupils to explain why one event mattered to them.
