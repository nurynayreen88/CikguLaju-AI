# English Year 5 Lesson 103 — Reading 18

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 103, printed p.142. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.3.1 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.59 Activities 1–4, TB p.73 and pictures of ingredients or recipe steps.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils choose a detail they enjoy in Sally’s food blog and retell three recipe steps in the correct order.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask what pupils have seen someone cook. Use the pictures to predict which dishes might appear in a food blog.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Sally’s blog for SB Activity 1 and check their predictions. Each marks one detail they find interesting and explains it to a partner.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activities 2–3, pairs find the key phrases and identify words that show order. The teacher models an oral retelling of two short steps.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** For SB Activity 4, pairs order the pictures and complete the recipe prompts. Each pupil retells three steps aloud using the pictures only as cues.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite pupils to share which part of the blog they enjoyed and retell how the dish is prepared.

**PBD:** Check one personal response linked to the blog and listen for three steps in a clear sequence.
**Differentiation:** Provide picture prompts and sequence words. Ask confident pupils to retell the steps without looking at the pictures.
