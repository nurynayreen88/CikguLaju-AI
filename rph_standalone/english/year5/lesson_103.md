# English Year 5 — Lesson 103 (Reading 18)

**Status:** DRAF — awaiting page-by-page book QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** Reading 3.3.1; **Complementary LS:** Speaking 2.3.1. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested). **CCE:** ICT.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student's Book p.59 Activities 1–4; Teacher's Book p.73; food photographs or real ingredients.

**Objective:** Pupils read and enjoy a simple food blog, locate details and orally retell recipe steps in order.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher asks the Think! question on SB p.59. Pupils name dishes they can prepare.

**Activity 1 — Explore a recipe blog (15 min):** Teacher explains blog, starter, main course, dessert, ingredients, chop and serve using visuals. Pupils read the Easy menu blog and complete Activity 1.

**Activity 2 — Find key phrases (15 min):** Pupils reread the blog for SB Activity 2, highlight useful phrases and discuss why some words are easy to locate. Discuss how blogs help share recipes and information.

**Activity 3 — Order and retell (15 min):** Pupils complete Activities 3–4. Partners practise instructions using “first”, “next”, “after that” and “finally”; each partner listens and helps correct the order.

**PBD (5 min):** Pupils locate two correct details from the blog and recount at least three steps in sequence. Teacher records evidence of reading and speaking.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils give one imperative and one sequencing adverb from the text.

**Differentiation:** Provide picture-sequence cards; challenge confident pupils to retell without looking at the text. **QA:** Workbook mapping not verified; no specific audio track prescribed for this lesson.
