# English Year 5 Lesson 56: Writing 10

SOW p.94. Topic Wild Life. Main Writing 4.3.2; complementary Speaking 2.1.5. Student Book p.35 Activities 1-4 and p.34 reading text; Teacher Book p.49.

Set induction: Brainstorm animal adjectives.
Activity 1: Complete comparative and superlative adjective exercises.
Activity 2: Locate relevant words in the reading text on p.34.
Activity 3: Use a map of Canada and spell superlatives including hottest and biggest.
Activity 4: Write sentences with superlative adjectives and compare with a partner.
Closure: Write three sentences about Malaysia using superlatives.

Assessment: Check spelling and animal descriptions. Differentiation: Extra time or extension sentences. Workbook exercise not verified. Draft pending final QA.
