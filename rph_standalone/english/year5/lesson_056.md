# English Year 5 Lesson 56 — Writing 10

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 56, printed p.93. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.2 and Speaking 2.1.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.34 reading text, SB p.35 Activities 1–4, TB p.49, a map of Canada and Workbook p.29 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils spell the superlative adjectives accurately in two independently written sentences and describe a place or animal orally using a suitable superlative.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils suggest adjectives to describe two wild animals. The teacher reviews one pair of comparative and superlative forms.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils complete SB p.35 Activities 1–2, returning to the p.34 text for examples. Partners explain how they found the forms.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Locate the Rocky Mountains on the map. In SB Activity 3, pupils complete the text about Canada and compare two spellings that need a doubled consonant.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils use the SB Activity 4 study strategy to write two superlative sentences. Partners compare after writing, then pupils correct one spelling if needed.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Groups share a superlative description of a place or animal in Malaysia. If time permits, pupils write one additional local example using Workbook p.29 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Check the spelling of two independently written superlatives and listen for one suitable oral description.
**Differentiation:** Provide a base-adjective list. Ask confident pupils to explain how an irregular form differs from a regular one.
