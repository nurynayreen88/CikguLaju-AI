# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 056 (Writing 10)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned revision, pending final visual/audio and teacher QA. **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** 4.3.2. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.5. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Comparative and Superlative adjectives: e.g. weirder, weirdest, rarer, rarest. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1–4 p.35 (text on p.34 for Activity 2 p.35) Teacher’s Book p.49.

**Learning objective:** Pupils use Wild Life language to complete the prescribed Writing 10 tasks and demonstrate main LS 4.3.2 and complementary LS 2.1.5.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils brainstorm adjectives to describe animals then do Warm-up. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.49 Warm-up.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do p.35 Activity 1. Follow instructions for Exercise 1 on p.49 of Teacher’s Book.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils look at text on p.34 again and do Activity 2. They compare answers with a partner. Check answers. How quickly did pupils find the words?

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Show map of Canada on board. Point out the Rocky Mountains. Explain there are many very big National Parks with lots of wild animals in the mountains. Write on board: ‘hot’, ‘cold’, ‘high’, ‘good’, ‘beautiful’, ‘interesting’. Pupils do Activity 3. Choose pupils to go to board and write the superlative of the adjectives. Help pupils to notice double ‘t’ in ‘hottest’. Write ‘big’ on board. Ask: How do you spell biggest? 5. Pupils do Activity 4. Read study strategy aloud to whole class.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher observes and records: Pupils who are slow at writing sentences with superlative adjectives.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils in small groups write three or four sentences about Malaysia using superlative adjectives and ideas from text about Canada.

**Workbook link (from existing verified mapping):** Workbook p.29, Unit 3 Language Focus — comparative/superlative-related adjective practice, Ex1-4. Use as brief practice or evidence without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Provide visual models or paired support for pupils who need help; extend confident pupils through independent responses. **QA note:** Workbook mapping is a prior source QA result; Student Book, Teacher Book, audio-content and A4 layout have not all been independently verified.
