# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 056 (Writing 10)

**Status:** DRAF — final source and A4 checks pending. **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** 4.3.2. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.5. **Duration:** 60 minutes.

**Focus:** Comparative and superlative adjectives, including weirder/weirdest and rarer/rarest. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student Book p.34 text and p.35 Activities 1–4; Teacher Book p.49; Workbook p.29 (prior mapping, optional).

**Objective:** Pupils identify and write correctly formed superlatives in the Student Book tasks and produce three intelligible sentences about Malaysia using superlatives.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher elicits adjectives describing animals and follows the warm-up guidance from TB p.49.

**Activity 1 — Notice forms (15 min):** Pupils complete SB p.35 Activity 1; teacher checks answers following TB p.49. Pupils reread the p.34 text and attempt p.35 Activity 2, comparing answers with partners.

**Activity 2 — Form superlatives (15 min):** Teacher locates the Rocky Mountains on a map of Canada and introduces adjectives hot, cold, high, good, beautiful and interesting. Pupils complete Activity 3 and explain forms on the board, including hottest and biggest.

**Activity 3 — Apply the strategy (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 4 and discuss the Student Book study strategy. Teacher prompts pupils to use an appropriate superlative and checks spelling and word choice.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks two superlative forms and one contextual sentence from each pupil; pupils correct an error after feedback.

**Closure (5 min):** Small groups draft three sentences about Malaysia using the superlatives practised, then share one.

**Differentiation:** Give an adjective-to-superlative model and sentence frame; extend with an irregular form and explanation. **Workbook:** WB p.29 Unit 3 Language Focus Ex1–4 may reinforce, not replace, SOW tasks. **QA:** Book exercise mapping and printed A4 output require final review.
