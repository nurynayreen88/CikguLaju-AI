# Year 5 Lesson 112 — Consolidation 11

Status: DRAF. Unit 6. Topic: Sport. Official SOW p.152. Main LS 4.2.3; complementary LS 2.1.2.
Materials: SB p.65 Activities 4, 5, 6 and 8; TB p.79. Workbook page not verified. Audio playback not tested.

Objectives: Pupils complete the stated lesson tasks and show evidence of both listed learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Compare pictures of Malaysia 100 years ago using there was/were.

Main activities (40 min): Guide Activity 4 about the first marathon and check wasn't/weren't; pairs discuss Activity 5, then complete Activities 6 and 8 and compare their past-life sentences. Teacher models the task, monitors pair or group participation and provides corrective feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check four accurate past-event sentences and one oral comparison. Pupils improve an answer based on teacher feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share one new word, form or fact and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide vocabulary prompts, visuals or sentence starters for pupils needing support; ask proficient pupils to justify or extend their responses.
