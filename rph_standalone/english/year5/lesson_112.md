# English Year 5 Lesson 112 — Consolidation 11

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 112, printed p.152. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.3 and Speaking 2.1.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.65 Activities 4, 5, 6 and 8, TB p.79, photographs and Workbook p.53 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write a short factual account of a past event and find out about and describe one past experience shared by a partner.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Compare older photographs of life in Malaysia with life today. Pupils describe one visible difference using there was or there were.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils complete SB Activity 4 about the first marathon and check contractions. Each writes a short factual account in order using the text as evidence.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 5, pairs compare their sentences. They then ask a partner about a game, sports day or trip they remember and report one specific detail.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils use SB Activities 6 and 8 to check past forms and ask a further experience question. They tell another pair what they learned about their partner’s experience.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Share one written fact and one detail from the exchange. If time permits, pupils write one sentence about a former interest using Workbook p.53 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Check an ordered factual account and listen for a question about an experience and one intelligible reported detail.
**Differentiation:** Use a fact timeline and past-experience prompts. Ask confident pupils to distinguish what happened from how their partner felt.
