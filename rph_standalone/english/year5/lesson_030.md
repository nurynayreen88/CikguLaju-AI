# Year 5 English Lesson 30 — Speaking 6

Official SOW: Lesson 30, printed p.66. Topic: Options: Extra Listening and Speaking. Duration: 60 minutes.
Skills: Speaking 2.2.1; Listening 1.2.5.
Sources: SB p.92 Activities 4–5; TB p.106; CD 3-04.

1. Set induction: Review What, How, Where, When and Have you questions with flashcards.
2. Activity 1: Listen to CD 3-04 and identify the five questions in Activity 4.
3. Activity 2: Find question words, numbers, adjectives and adverbs in the model dialogue.
4. Activity 3: Pairs use Eriko and Liam information in Activity 5 to prepare a new dialogue and swap roles.
5. Assessment and closure: Repeat dialogue using own information; assess ability to sustain short exchanges.

PBD: Observe listening responses, short exchanges and written accuracy. Differentiation: Replay or pause audio for pupils needing support; allow extra rehearsal time for dialogues.
