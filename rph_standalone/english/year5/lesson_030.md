# Year 5 English Lesson 30 — Speaking 6

Official SOW: Lesson 30, printed p.65. Topic: Options: Extra Listening and Speaking. Duration: 60 minutes.
Skills: Speaking 2.2.1; Listening 1.2.5.
Sources: SB p.92 Activities 4–5; TB p.106; CD 3-04.
Objective: Pupils recognise five question forms from the recording and sustain a short spoken exchange with one follow-up question.

1. Set induction (8 min): **Teacher:** Display the SOW question-word flashcards (*What's...?*, *How...?*, *Where...?*, *When's...?*, *Have you...?*) and elicit examples before referring to the SB p.92 Key Phrases. **Pupils:** Recall personal-information questions and identify any missing question form.
2. Activity 1 — Listening for five questions (12 min): **Teacher:** Play CD3.04 for SB p.92 Activity 4, following TB p.106, and ask which question differs from the Key Phrases. **Pupils:** Listen for the five spoken questions and locate the differing question in the dialogue.
3. Activity 2 — Analyse the model (15 min): **Teacher:** Guide pupils to find the two numbers, question words, adjectives and *very/really* in the printed model; ask which Key Phrase question is absent (*When's...?*). **Pupils:** Categorise the examples and explain the sequence of questions in the dialogue.
4. Activity 3 — Use it! dialogue (17 min): **Teacher:** Follow SB p.92 Activity 5: assign Eriko/Liam roles, let pairs prepare the model dialogue, then swap roles; allow extension questions for confident pupils. **Pupils:** Prepare, perform and reverse roles while asking suitable follow-up questions.
5. Assessment and closure (8 min): **Teacher:** Ask pairs to repeat the dialogue using their own names and personal details. Record pupils' ability to sustain exchanges (Speaking 2.2.1) and to recognise a supported sequence of spoken questions (Listening 1.2.5), with targeted feedback. **Pupils:** Conduct a short personal exchange, answer follow-up questions and improve one unclear response.

PBD: Teacher observes a two-turn exchange using Eriko/Liam information, including one follow-up question; pupils switch roles and improve an unclear response.
