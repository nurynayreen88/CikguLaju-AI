# English Year 5 Lesson 71: Listening 13

Official SOW Lesson 71, printed p.110. Topic: Learning World. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Listening 1.3.1; Reading 3.3.1.
Materials: SB pp.42-43 Activities 1-2; TB p.56; CD 1-38.

1. Set induction (8 min): Discuss Think questions about school subjects.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Guess subjects from teacher-read definitions.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Listen to CD 1-38 and identify ten subjects and unfamiliar words.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Answer the school questionnaire and distinguish facts from opinions.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Draw and label subjects; tally the class's three favourite subjects.

PBD: Record evidence from the stated activities and skills.
Differentiation: Allow extra time for questionnaire answers.
