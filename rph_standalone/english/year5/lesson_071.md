# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 071 (Listening 13)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** 1.3.1. **Complementary LS:** 3.3.1. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Nouns: school subjects; Adjective phrases: good at, great at, not good / great at. **Materials:** SOW-prescribed literary text and BPK CCL guide; audio CD1.38.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the prescribed skills through specific Learning World activities and an observable output.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Pupils in pairs answer questions in Think! p.42. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.56.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Read aloud 5 or 6 definitions of school subjects in the box in Activity 1 e.g. In this subject you draw and paint. Sometimes you make models; In this subject you do calculations with numbers and money. Pupils guess the school subject from the box.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Explain pupils are going to listen to the school questions. They listen for ten school subjects and guess the meaning of words they don’t know. Pupils do p.42 and 43 Activity 1, CD1.38. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.56 Exercise 1.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2. Pupils read questions again and think of answers. With a partner, they answer questionnaire orally. Find out which pairs have same answers to questions 1, 3, 4, 8. Explain answers are opinions. Write on board 2, 5, 6, 7, 9. Check answers then say these are facts. Ask: Do you know the answer to 10? Which questions are easy to answer, opinions or facts? 5. Pupils do Optional Activity: Vocabulary. Explain pupils are going to be creative.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records pupils who have difficulty understanding meaning of: organised, geography, physical, economic when they’re listening to CD.

**Closure (5 min):** 6. Find out pupils’ three favourite subjects. Say a subject and pupils put hand up if they like it. Do a tally on board. Ask: Which are the class’s top three favourite subjects?

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.36, Unit 4 Vocabulary — School subjects, Ex1-4. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Use a model and partner support as needed; extend confident pupils with an independent task.
