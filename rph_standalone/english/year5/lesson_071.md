# English Year 5 Lesson 71 — Listening 13

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 71, printed p.109. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.3.1 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB pp.42–43 Activities 1–2, TB p.56, CD1.38 and Workbook p.36 Exercise 1.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer the meaning of two unfamiliar school words from the recorded questions and share one school-quiz item that interests them.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show school-subject pictures. Model how familiar words about numbers and money help a listener identify a subject.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 1 with CD1.38. Pupils match spoken questions to subjects and note familiar words that help them interpret two unfamiliar terms.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Replay selected questions. Pairs compare their guesses and explain why each clue fits before the teacher confirms the meanings.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** In SB Activity 2, pupils read the questionnaire and take turns answering. Each chooses one question they would like to ask another class and explains their choice.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs draw or describe one subject for others to identify. If time permits, pupils find two subject names in Workbook p.36 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings and the spoken clues for them. Ask each pupil to point to one chosen question and explain their interest.
**Differentiation:** Offer picture labels and one replay. Ask confident pupils to compare two questions that give different clues about the same subject.
