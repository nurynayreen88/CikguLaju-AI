# English Year 5 Lesson 86: Writing 15

Official SOW Lesson 86, printed p.125. Topic: Learning World. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.3.3; Reading 3.2.2.
Materials: SB p.49 Activities 3-4; TB p.63.

1. Set induction (8 min): Brainstorm email ideas about school, subjects and hobbies.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Read Danny's email to Ela and identify greeting and ending.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Plan and draft an email to an overseas pupil using Activity 4.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs exchange emails and check school details, paragraphs and sign-offs.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Revise email and complete Unit 4 self-assessment.

PBD: Assess work against the stated or teacher-selected skills.
Differentiation: Allow shorter emails without the fourth paragraph.
