# Year 5 Lesson 142 — Language Awareness 7

Status: DRAF. Unit 7; topic: Growing up. Official SOW Lesson 142. SOW does not assign fixed LS; teacher selects Year 5 DSKP standards. No official code invented.
Materials: SB p.81 Activities 1,2,5; TB p.95; Unit 7 self-assessment. Workbook mapping and audio playback not yet verified.

Objective: Pupils complete the specified activities and demonstrate the relevant learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Review appearance and past-tense vocabulary.

Main activities (40 min): Read and complete the three-boy table in Activity 1; work in pairs on Activity 2 and individually on Activity 5. Discuss difficult items and complete Unit 7 self-assessment. Teacher models instructions, monitors and supports pupils.

PBD (10 min): Collect self-assessment to plan targeted revision; SOW leaves LS selection to teacher. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide visual cues, word banks and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent answers.

Verification: Official SOW checked; full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.
