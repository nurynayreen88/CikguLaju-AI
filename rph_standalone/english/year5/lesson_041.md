# English Year 5 Lesson 41 — Listening 8

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 41, printed p.77. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.4 and Reading 3.2.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.24 Activities 1–3, TB p.38, CD 1-23, Workbook p.21 Exercise 5 and a Venn diagram.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow four spoken directions in order and infer the meaning of one family word from the text.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils brainstorm family members and answer the Think! question in pairs.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Give four spoken directions for SB Activity 1. Pupils look at the photo, read the three titles, choose and write one, then compare it with a partner.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD 1-23. Pupils listen and read to confirm the title, then use a second reading to find details for SB Activity 2.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** In SB Activity 3, pairs use clues to infer one family word and sort the words into the two circles and their overlap.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask a short TB p.38 reading question. If time permits, pupils write one family routine sentence from the prompt in Workbook p.21 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Observe whether each pupil follows the four directions in sequence. Ask each pair to show the clue supporting one word meaning.
**Differentiation:** Repeat directions once and model the first Venn diagram entry.
