# English Year 5 Lesson 35 — Listening 7

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 35, printed p.71. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.1 and Writing 4.3.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.22 Activities 1, 3 and 4, TB p.36, CD 1-21 and 1-22, Workbook p.21 Exercise 1 and a routines table.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main routine described by two speakers and spell two frequency adverbs in their own sentences.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils list the days of the week and vote on their favourite day.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils sort the routines in SB Activity 1 into five categories and listen to CD 1-21 to check.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD 1-22 for SB Activity 3. Pupils match speakers to routines and tell a partner what each speaker mainly does.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils place the adverbs on a frequency line. Replay CD 1-22 for SB Activity 4, then pupils use two adverbs in sentences of their own.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Compare two examples on the frequency line. If time permits, pupils complete two items from Workbook p.21 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Check the main routine for two speakers and the spelling of two adverbs in pupils’ independent sentences.
**Differentiation:** Pause after each speaker and offer two routine choices.
