# English Year 5 Lesson 21 — Language Arts 4

**Official SOW topic:** Cats.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 21, printed p.56. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.3.1 and Writing 4.2.4.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.15, Cats by John Kitching, BPK Poetry Guidebook p.35 Activity 4, cat outlines and paper.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils create a cat-shaped poem inspired by Cats and write suitable statements describing the cat and its actions.
**Success criteria:** Create an original cat-shaped poem with two lines describing an action or what the cat is like.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall Cats and mime actions from the poem.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Show the BPK Collage Shape Poem example. Pupils think of new cat actions and share precise verbs with a partner.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pairs choose actions and draft two lines that describe the cat or what it does. Each pupil contributes a line.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs arrange the lines in a cat outline, then read the poem aloud to check that their descriptions make sense.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Display the poems. Pupils choose a line from another pair’s work and say what they can picture.

**PBD:** Check that the shape and text form a creative response. Read the two descriptive lines and note each pupil’s contribution.
**Differentiation:** Provide a cat outline and pictures of actions. Ask confident pupils to extend a line with a place or manner detail.
