# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 011 (Listening 3)

**Source:** Official Primary Year 5 SK Scheme of Work Lesson 11, printed p.46. **Theme:** World of Knowledge. **Topic:** Free time. **Duration:** 60 minutes suggested.
**Main CS/LS:** Listening 1.2/1.2.5. **Complementary CS/LS:** Reading 3.2/3.2.3. **Language focus:** countries and nationalities; question words.
**Learning objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills using the exact SOW lesson activities with appropriate teacher support.
**Success criteria (teacher-set):** Pupils complete the core activities and demonstrate an observable response for each skill.
**Materials:** SB p.10 Activities 1–2; p.11 Activities 1–3; TB pp.24–25; CD 1-12.

## Teaching and learning
1. **Set induction (8 min):** World map warm-up and identify Malaysia.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs identify countries on globe and repeat names; identify six blue quiz words by oral clues.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Complete country/nationality quiz; compare -ish, -an, -ian, -ese endings and discuss Malaysia facts.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use Where/What/Who flashcards, complete SB p.11 Activities 1–3, listen twice to CD 1-12 and finish five key-phrase sentences.
5. **PBD and closure (8 min):** Circle question game: each pupil asks a Where, What or Who question; teacher checks supported question comprehension.

**PBD:** Observe and record evidence from the main-skill activity and complementary-skill output. Record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching.
**Differentiation:** Invite proficient pupils to form longer nationality sentences.
