# English Year 5 Lesson 11 — Listening 3

**Official SOW topic:** Free time.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 11, printed p.45. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.5 and Reading 3.2.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.10 Activities 1–2 and p.11 Activities 1–3, TB pp.24–25, CD1.12, WB p.10 Ex2 and a world map.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow a sequence of supported classroom instructions and infer unfamiliar words from clues in the printed quiz.
**Success criteria:** Carry out three instructions in the order given and explain the meanings of two quiz words using printed clues.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Locate Malaysia on the world map and revisit familiar country names.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB p.10 Activity 1 and the Activity 2 quiz. Pupils use the photos and known words to infer two quiz words, then compare nationality endings.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Give three supported instructions for SB p.11 Activities 1–2. Pupils read the questions, listen to CD1.12 and mark the speakers’ answers in that order.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Replay CD1.12 for SB p.11 Activity 3. Pupils complete the Key Phrases in sequence. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.10 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** In a circle, pupils take turns asking a Where, What or Who question.

**PBD:** Observe the order in which each pupil completes the supported task. Record two reasonable word meanings and the clues used.
**Differentiation:** Display numbered instruction cues after the first listening attempt. Model how to link a printed clue to an unfamiliar word.
