# English Year 5 Lesson 48 — Speaking 9

Official SOW Lesson 48, printed p.85. Topic: Days. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Main and complementary learning standards: Speaking 2.1.4; Writing 4.2.2.
Materials: SB p.28 Activities 4–6; TB p.42.

1. Set induction (8 min): Recall a partner's weekend family routine using frequency adverbs.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Sort Key Phrases into plans and suggestions.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Complete Activity 4 and identify advice using What about and Let's.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Practise Activity 5 mini-dialogue with suitable intonation.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs create a cinema plan dialogue; groups prepare a short cinema programme.

PBD: Assess task-specific spoken, listening, reading or written evidence and note pupils needing support.
Differentiation: Point pupils to the order of target words in the dialogue.
