# English Year 5 Lesson 96: Speaking 17

Official SOW Lesson 96, printed p.136. Topic: Food and health. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Speaking 2.2.1; Speaking 2.2.2.
Materials: SB p.96 Activities 4-6; TB p.110; CD3-13 and CD3-14.

1. Set induction (8 min): Review fractions, weights and money expressions.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete Activity 4 and compare cheap and expensive items.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Listen to CD3-14 and practise the market role-play in Activity 5.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs plan seller and customer roles, fruit and quantities for Activity 6.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Perform market dialogues and review prices using a memory task.

PBD: Assess responses and work against stated skills.
Differentiation: Allow one fruit purchase or extend to fractional kilograms.
