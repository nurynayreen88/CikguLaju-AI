# English Year 5 Lesson 96 — Speaking 17

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 96, printed p.135. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.2.1 and Speaking 2.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.96 Activities 4–6, TB p.110, CD3.13, CD3.14 and fruit and price cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils agree on roles and steps for a market role-play and sustain the exchange using a shopping question and a relevant follow-up.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Review fruit, weights and price expressions. Pupils say which prices seem cheap or expensive.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Replay CD3.13 for SB Activity 4 so pupils can check purchase details. Use CD3.14 in Activity 5 to model a question, reply and follow-up.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Before SB Activity 6, pairs agree who will sell, which fruit and weights to use, and who will calculate the total. They note the steps on their price card.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pairs perform the market exchange. The buyer follows up about a price or amount, listens to the reply and confirms the purchase. Partners swap roles for a second exchange.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs report what they agreed to buy and how they checked the total. Invite one pair to share a useful follow-up.

**PBD:** Observe agreement on roles and steps, then check one relevant question, a follow-up and an appropriate response in the role-play.
**Differentiation:** Offer a short price list and question frames. Ask confident pupils to respond to a change in quantity.
