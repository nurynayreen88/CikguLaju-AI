# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 096 (Speaking 17)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned, awaiting source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** 2.2.1. **Complementary LS:** 2.2.2. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Weights and prices: gram, kilo, a quarter / three quarters of a kilo, half a kilo, fraction. How much is…? It costs…. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 4, 5 and 6 p.96 Teacher’s Book p.110; Audio: CD3.13, CD3.14.

**Objective:** Pupils complete the specific Speaking 17 lesson tasks using Food and health vocabulary and produce observable assessment evidence.

**Set induction (5 min):** Brainstorm fractions, weights of food and money on board. Accept all suggestions e.g.10 kilos of beans, £1. Make sure pupils can pronounce half, quarter, pound.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 5, CD3.14. Follow instructions for role-play in Teacher’s Book p.110 Exercise 5. If two boys are in a pair, change Woman to Man. If two girls, change George to Gill. Explain ‘in your head’ and ‘mentally’. Say many market sellers count money in their heads /mentally.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 6, Use It!. They agree on 1) who is the market seller and who is the customer; 2) fruit they will buy; 3) how much fruit they will buy. Explain that sellers can write down the cost of the fruit then work out the total, or add the money in their heads.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils do Optional Activity: Speaking. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.110. Note how well pupils keep interaction going in the dialogues and how well they agree with each other in Activity 6.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher observes responses to the prescribed SOW tasks and records an individual improvement.

**Closure (5 min):** See instructions in Section 3 Post-lesson Task 8: Test your Memory. Use the picture of the fruit, prices and amounts in Activity 6.

**Workbook practice (mapped):** p.51, Unit 5 Speaking — In a cafe, Ex6. Use briefly; do not replace SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Give suitable vocabulary or sentence prompts; challenge confident pupils with independent responses. **Final check:** Full printed-page and audio-content audit, A4 export and teacher approval pending; not READY.
