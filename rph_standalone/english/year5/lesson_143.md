# English Year 5 Lesson 143 — Listening 25

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 143, printed p.185. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.3.1 and Listening 1.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB pp.82–83 Activities 1–4, TB pp.96–97, CD2.29, CD2.30 and Workbook p.68 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer two holiday-item words using familiar words heard in spoken clues and identify two specific details in the holiday recording.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show a case or bag and invite pupils to predict what someone might pack for a trip.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Read short clues about two items from SB Activity 1. Pupils identify the familiar spoken words that help them infer each meaning, then use CD2.29 to check answers.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs compare their choices in Activities 2–3 and classify several items by use before the teacher introduces the holiday recording.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use CD2.30 for SB Activity 4. Pupils identify two things the speakers mention and note who mentions them or why they are needed.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Share one clue or detail. If time permits, pupils solve one item clue in Workbook p.68 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings supported by heard clues and two accurate details from the recording.
**Differentiation:** Show picture options and offer a replay. Ask confident pupils to distinguish two speakers’ reasons for packing different items.
