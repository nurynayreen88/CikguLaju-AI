# Year 5 Lesson 143 — Listening 25

Status: DRAF. Unit 8; topic: Going away. Official SOW Lesson 143. Main LS 1.3.1; complementary LS 1.2.2.
Materials: SB pp.82–83 Activities 1–4; TB pp.96–97; CD2-29/30. Workbook mapping and audio playback not yet verified.

Objective: Pupils complete the specified activities and demonstrate the relevant learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Discuss things to pack for a holiday.

Main activities (40 min): Match travel items to photos with CD2-29, use picture clues for unfamiliar words, compare packing lists and listen to four holiday accounts on CD2-30. Teacher models instructions, monitors and supports pupils.

PBD (10 min): Check inferred vocabulary and specific details from listening. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide visual cues, word banks and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent answers.

Verification: Official SOW checked; full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.
