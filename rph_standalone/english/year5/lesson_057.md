# English Year 5 Lesson 57 — Language Arts 10

**Official SOW topic:** And Something Weird Happened.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 57, printed p.94. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.3.1 and Reading 3.2.2.
**Materials:** And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby, Chapters 2–3, BPK Short Stories Teaching Guidebook and teacher-prepared true-or-false cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils verify two statements using details from Chapters 2–3 and act out one episode so a partner can recognise it.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Read two familiar statements about the story. Pupils stand or sit to show their choice and explain how they know.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Give pairs a mix of true and false statements about Joel’s schoolwork and his visit to the library. Pupils predict which are true and underline the words they need to check.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs read Chapters 2–3 and confirm or revise their predictions. Each pair locates two details that support its answers and explains one to the class.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pairs choose a statement and plan a short scene with a clear action or line of dialogue. After each performance, classmates identify the episode and decide whether the statement matches the story.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils correct one false statement and point to a detail from the relevant chapter.

**PBD:** Check two answers against the chapter evidence and assess whether the role-play communicates one recognisable episode.
**Differentiation:** Provide character and place cards. Ask confident pupils to add a detail that helps the audience identify the episode.
