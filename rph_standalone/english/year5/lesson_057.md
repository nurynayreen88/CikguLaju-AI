# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 057 (Language Arts 10)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned revision, pending final visual/audio and teacher QA. **Topic:** And Something Weird Happened. **Main LS:** 5.3.1. **Complementary LS:** 3.2.2. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** True and false statements. **Materials:** Non-textbook: And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby, Chapters 2 and 3; BPK Short Stories Guidebook..

**Learning objective:** Pupils use And Something Weird Happened language to complete the prescribed Language Arts 10 tasks and demonstrate main LS 5.3.1 and complementary LS 3.2.2.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher reads a sentence and pupils have to sit down if the sentence is false and stand up if the sentence is correct (e.g. Yuki was playing basketball – False). [Pre-Lesson Task 5 – Sit down Stand up]

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils are put in pairs and given a set of statements.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils will guess whether the statements are true or false by writing T or F at the end of each statement.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Teacher instructs pairs to read Chapter 2 and Chapter 3 of the book and they need to verify their guesses. 5. Upon completion, teacher informs pupils that they need to choose any statements to be acted out in front. 6. Pairs act out their chosen statement and others will guess whether it is true or false (make sure most of the pairs have the opportunities to act out).

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records a relevant imaginative response to the literature and pupils' explanation.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils rewrite the false statements to become true statements.
