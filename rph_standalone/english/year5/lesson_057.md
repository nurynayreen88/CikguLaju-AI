# English Year 5 Lesson 57: Language Arts 10

Official SOW Lesson 57, printed p.95. Topic: And Something Weird Happened. Duration: 60 minutes.
Main and complementary learning standards: Language Arts 5.3.1; Reading 3.2.2.
Sources: Non-textbook: And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby, Chapters 2 and 3; BPK Short Stories Guidebook.

1. Play Sit Down Stand Up with true or false statements about the story.
2. Pairs predict whether supplied story statements are true or false.
3. Read Chapters 2 and 3 to verify their predictions.
4. Pairs act out or read chosen statements while classmates decide if they are true.
5. Rewrite false statements so they become true.

PBD: Record evidence from the described activities against the stated skills.
Differentiation: Let pairs choose acting or reading.
