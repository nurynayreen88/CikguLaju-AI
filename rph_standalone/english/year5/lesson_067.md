# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 067 (Reading 12)

**Status:** DRAF — source/audio and A4 checks pending. **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** 3.3.1. **Complementary LS:** 1.2.1. **Duration:** 60 minutes.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student Book p.117 Activities 1–3; Teacher Book p.131; audio CD3-34. Workbook pp.31 and 35 (prior mapped follow-up only). This lesson uses the SOW Student Book song task, **not** the BPK non-textbook poetry guide.

**Objective:** Pupils follow the song lyrics, identify verbs describing abilities and use appropriate superlative adjectives to create and answer three animal questions.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher introduces the title I can tell you and recalls the animal ability language from Unit 3.

**Activity 1 — Listen for the gist (15 min):** Pupils cover the lyrics, listen to CD3-34 for SB p.117 Activity 1 and share what they heard. Teacher groups examples into bird, mammal and reptile, following TB p.131.

**Activity 2 — Read and complete (15 min):** Teacher revises the action verbs in Activity 2 and pre-teaches shell and far. Pupils listen again while reading, complete missing words and check answers before singing or reading selected lines.

**Activity 3 — Ask and answer (15 min):** Pupils examine superlatives in Activity 3, write three animal questions with known answers, exchange questions between pairs and justify their answers.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks completion of lyric gaps and each pair's three intelligible questions and responses; note who needs replay or reading support.

**Closure (5 min):** Each pupil states one animal ability or superlative fact from the song and explains the word used.

**Differentiation:** Allow supported pupils to complete selected lyric gaps first; extend with an additional question and explanation. **Workbook:** WB pp.31/35 may provide aligned can/can't and review practice. **QA:** Audio identity by filename only; listening content, page exercises and A4 export remain unverified.
