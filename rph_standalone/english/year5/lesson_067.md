# English Year 5 Lesson 67: Reading 12

Official SOW Lesson 67, printed p.105. Topic: Wild Life. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Reading 3.3.1; Listening 1.2.1.
Materials: SB p.117 Activities 1-3; TB p.131; CD 3-34.

1. Set induction (8 min): Introduce the song I Can Tell You using the TB warm-up.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Listen without reading the lyrics; group animals as birds, mammals or reptiles.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Listen again and complete song verb gaps.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Read and complete Activity 3; practise animal superlatives.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs write and exchange three animal superlative questions.

PBD: Observe lesson-specific task evidence against the stated skills.
Differentiation: Offer selected gaps and replay for pupils needing support.
