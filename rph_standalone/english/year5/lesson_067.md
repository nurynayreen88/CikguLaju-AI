# English Year 5 Lesson 67 — Reading 12

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 67, printed p.104. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.3.1 and Listening 1.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.117 Activities 1–3, TB p.131, CD3.34 and Workbook p.34 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of the song, then read the lyrics and explain why one animal detail interests them.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Introduce the song title and ask pupils to predict what an animal song might say. Gather examples of animal abilities.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 1, pupils match photos and cover the lyrics before listening to CD3.34. Each pair agrees on the main idea and identifies an animal mentioned.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils read the lyrics in SB Activity 2 and predict the missing verbs. They replay the song to check, then choose an interesting detail and explain it to a partner.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils answer the SB Activity 3 questions using the lyrics. Pairs write and exchange short questions using superlatives to review animal information.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite pupils to share a favourite detail from the song. If time permits, pupils complete two superlative items in Workbook p.34 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check one statement of the song’s main idea and one personal response based on a detail pupils read.
**Differentiation:** Offer pictures and one replay. Ask confident pupils to identify and explain a second interesting line.
