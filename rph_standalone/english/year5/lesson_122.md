# Year 5 Lesson 122 — Writing 21

Status: DRAF. Unit 6. Topic: Sport. Official SOW p.162. Main LS 4.3.3; complementary LS 4.2.5.
Materials: SB p.69 Activity 4; TB p.83; teacher-prepared sports-star fact cards. Workbook page unverified; audio playback not tested.

Objective: Pupils demonstrate learning through the specific tasks below, covering both selected or prescribed skills.

Set induction (5 min): Brainstorm and categorise facts about Usain Bolt.

Main activities (40 min): Review seven key phrases; use fact cards to plan and draft a short sports-star profile using past tense and time expressions. Partners check birthplace, achievements, present life and coherence, then pupils revise. Teacher models instructions and monitors pupil responses, giving actionable feedback.

PBD (10 min): Assess plan, paragraph organisation, conjunctions and revisions from peer feedback. Pupils revise or explain one response.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide word banks, visual prompts and sentence frames for support; extend proficient pupils with independent explanations or richer outputs.
