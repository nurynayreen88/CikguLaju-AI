# English Year 5 Lesson 122 — Writing 21

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 122, printed p.162. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.3 and Writing 4.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.69 Activity 4 and model profile, TB p.83, teacher-prepared athlete fact cards and Workbook p.57 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils plan and draft one or two paragraphs about a sports star, connect ideas coherently and revise one part after feedback.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Build a mind map of details from the model profile. Pupils group the facts by early life and achievements.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Review the model’s key phrases and time expressions. For SB Activity 4, pupils select facts from a verified card and outline the order of their profile.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils draft one or two short paragraphs, linking related facts with a conjunction and a clear reference word so the reader can follow the athlete’s story.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners check that events are in order and references point to the correct person or event. Each pupil improves one sentence after receiving a specific comment.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Share one revised sentence. If time permits, pupils outline a short sports-star article using Workbook p.57 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Compare the plan and draft, check one effective conjunction and reference, and identify the revision made after feedback.
**Differentiation:** Offer fact headings and sentence stems. Ask confident pupils to link two achievements without repeating the athlete’s name unnecessarily.
