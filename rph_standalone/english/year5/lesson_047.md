# English Year 5 Lesson 47 — Listening 9

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 47, printed p.83. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.3.1 and Reading 3.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.28 Activities 1–3, TB p.42, CD 1-26, Workbook p.22 Exercise 1 and a dictionary.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer the meanings of two words from the spoken dialogue and check one meaning using a familiar dictionary.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils name activities they would join on a school special day.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 1 to look over the programme. Pairs note words that sound similar in English and Malay.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD 1-26 for SB Activity 2. Pupils use nearby words in the dialogue to infer two meanings, then check one in a dictionary.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pairs sort dialogue reactions by purpose. Replay the recording to compare pronunciation, then pupils practise the dialogue in SB Activity 3.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs agree on a morning and an afternoon activity. If time permits, pupils complete two clues in Workbook p.22 Exercise 1.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings and one spoken clue per pupil. Ask pupils to show the dictionary entry used to confirm a meaning.
**Differentiation:** Pause the track and provide two possible meanings after the first listen.
