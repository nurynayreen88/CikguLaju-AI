# English Year 5 Lesson 146 — Writing 25

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 146, printed p.188. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.2 and Speaking 2.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.84 Activity 4, p.85 Activities 1–3, TB pp.98–99 and Workbook p.69 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils spell three high-frequency words accurately in independent writing and tell a short holiday story with three connected events.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the Class News photos to recall different holiday activities and their settings.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB p.84 Activity 4, pupils write reasons for a holiday choice. They independently add two details and check spelling only after writing.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use SB p.85 Activities 1–3 to practise going to and not going to. Pupils write short sentences giving specific plans and check two common words.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners turn a holiday plan into a short imagined story. Each speaks about three connected events using time words to make the order clear.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share a story ending. If time permits, pupils complete one personal plan in Workbook p.69 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check three accurate spellings in the pupil’s own writing and listen for three intelligibly connected events in the oral story.
**Differentiation:** Offer a short verb bank and a three-part story strip. Ask confident pupils to add a reason for the final event.
