# Year 5 Lesson 146 — Writing 25

Status: DRAF. Unit 8; topic: Going away. Official SOW Lesson 146. Main LS 4.3.2; complementary LS 2.3.1.
Materials: SB p.84 Activity 4, p.85 Activities 1–3; TB pp.98–99. Workbook mapping and audio playback not yet verified.

Objective: Pupils complete the specified activities and demonstrate the relevant learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Recall three holiday photographs.

Main activities (40 min): Write three or four reasons for a preferred holiday; practise be going to affirmative/negative and complete the p.85 tasks. Share future holiday plans orally. Teacher models instructions, monitors and supports pupils.

PBD (10 min): Check spelling and clear short spoken account. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide visual cues, word banks and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent answers.

Verification: Official SOW checked; full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.
