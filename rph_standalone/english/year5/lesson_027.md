# English Year 5 Lesson 27 — Language Arts 5

**Official SOW topic:** Serenade.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 27, printed p.62. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.17, Serenade, BPK Poetry Guidebook and simple sound-making objects.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read and enjoy Serenade and explain a personal response to an event or sound in the poem.
**Success criteria:** Choose and reread a line or stanza for pleasure and explain why they like or dislike a specific moment.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a missing-letter word game to recall names of musical instruments.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Serenade and predict sounds for the fiddle, drum and bubble. They choose a section they would like to read again.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Groups take turns reading a stanza and adding sound effects. Pupils reread their chosen section and notice how sound shapes the scene.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs share one response about a moment or sound in the poem and explain their reasons. Pupils note one preference their partner shared.
5. **Closure (8 min):** In two facing lines, pupils share their partner’s reason and compare it with their own.

**PBD:** Ask pupils which section they chose to reread. Listen for a simple reason tied to an event or sound from the poem.
**Differentiation:** Offer picture and sound prompts. Allow a short because response or a reading partner for less confident readers.
