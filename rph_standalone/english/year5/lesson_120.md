# Year 5 Lesson 120 — Speaking 21

Status: DRAF. Unit 6. Topic: Sport. SOW p.160. Main LS 2.3.1; complementary LS 4.3.1.
Materials: SB p.68 Activities 4–5; TB p.82. Workbook mapping unverified. Listed audio is assigned by SOW but playback is not tested.

Objectives: Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary standards through the activities below.

Set induction (5 min): Quick oral responses to familiar questions including How was your last birthday party?

Main activities (40 min): Recall Alfie and Lois dialogue; complete Activity 4 in pairs. In Activity 5, pupils imagine visiting a rock concert or American diner and narrate the event to a new partner. Pupils draw a simple event poster with accurate capitals and punctuation. Teacher models task instructions, monitors pupils and gives corrective feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check oral past-event narration and written poster punctuation. Pupils improve one response using feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and submit a short exit response.

Differentiation: Offer visual cues, word banks, shorter instructions and sentence stems as needed; extend proficient pupils with more detailed independent responses.

Verification: Aligned to official Year 5 SOW outline; exact book-page content and audio playback still require final audit. Do not mark READY.
