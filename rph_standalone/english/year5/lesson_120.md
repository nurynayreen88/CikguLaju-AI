# English Year 5 Lesson 120 — Speaking 21

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 120, printed p.160. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.3.1 and Writing 4.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.68 Activities 4–5, TB p.82, paper for an event poster and Workbook p.59 Exercise 6.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recount three connected events from an imagined weekend visit and write a short event notice with a correctly punctuated list and question.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a quick exchange about a weekend event. Pupils recall words or reactions from Alfie and Lois’s conversation.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** For SB Activity 4, pairs complete and practise the dialogue. The teacher models how a listener’s question can help someone tell their story in order.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use the concert and diner posters in SB Activity 5. Partners imagine a visit, then narrate three events with a clear sequence to a new partner.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils independently make a short poster for a local event. Include a list of activities and a question, then check capitals, commas, full stops and the question mark.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Have one pupil tell a short event story. If time permits, pupils complete one missing word in the conversation in Workbook p.59 Exercise 6.

**PBD:** Listen for three connected story events and check the capitals, full stops, list commas and question mark in the pupil’s notice.
**Differentiation:** Give a sequence strip and poster model. Ask confident pupils to link an event to how they felt.
