# English Year 5 Lesson 90: Speaking 16

Official SOW Lesson 90, printed p.129. Topic: Food and health. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Speaking 2.2.1; Writing 4.3.1.
Materials: SB pp.52-53 Activities 4-6; TB p.67; CD2-04.

1. Set induction (8 min): Brainstorm food preferences with like and really like.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Listen to CD2-04 and record students' preferred foods in a table.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Complete Activity 5 and identify details from School Food texts.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs discuss likes and dislikes using Activity 6.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Write and compare ideal and unpleasant school lunches.

PBD: Assess work against the stated or teacher-selected skills.
Differentiation: Allow longer response time and model preference phrases.
