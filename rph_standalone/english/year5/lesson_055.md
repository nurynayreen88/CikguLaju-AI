# English Year 5 Lesson 55 — Reading 10

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 55, printed p.92. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.34 Activities 1–3, TB p.48, dictionaries and Workbook p.32 Exercise 5.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer two adjective meanings from the blobfish article and give a personal response supported by a detail from their reading.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Play a short animal alphabet game. Pairs choose which animal in the SB photo set they would most like to read about.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Elicit or check key conservation vocabulary. In SB Activity 1, pupils read the article and identify which ideas the writer supports.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils reread the article for SB Activity 2 and mark a line that confirms or corrects one answer.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** For SB Activity 3, pairs make two guesses about the highlighted adjectives using nearby words. They explain their clues before checking with a dictionary.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share which animal in the article interested them and why. If time permits, they complete two adjective items in Workbook p.32 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings and the clues used. Listen for one personal response that refers to a detail in the article.
**Differentiation:** Highlight the sentence containing each clue for pupils who need support. Ask confident pupils to explain what the writer wants readers to do.
