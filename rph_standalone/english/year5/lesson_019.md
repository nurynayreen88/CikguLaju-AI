# English Year 5 Lesson 19 — Reading 4

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 19, printed p.54. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.4 and Speaking 2.1.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.14 Activities 1–3, TB p.28, CD1.15, WB p.16 Ex5 and dictionaries.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils use print or digital resources to check meanings and describe the cruise ship using information from the text.
**Success criteria:** Verify four words with a dictionary and give a spoken description with two accurate details about the ship.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask pupils to decide what would make a good holiday destination. Set one goal for using the text or dictionary.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use the photos in SB Activity 1 to predict which facilities are on the ship. Pupils read City on the Sea to check.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use SB Activity 2 with CD1.15. Pupils find and mark the detail supporting each answer in the text.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** In SB Activity 3, pupils look up four words and record a context-appropriate meaning for each. Pairs describe the ship using two verified details. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.16 Exercise 5.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils say whether they would choose the ship for a holiday and explain why.

**PBD:** Check each word with its reference meaning. Listen for two spoken details that can be traced to the reading.
**Differentiation:** Demonstrate one dictionary lookup. Give pupils a shortlist of words or ask confident pupils to distinguish two possible meanings.
