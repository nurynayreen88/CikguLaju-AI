# English Year 5 Lesson 19 — Reading 4

Official SOW: Lesson 19, printed p.54. Unit 1: Towns and cities. Suggested time: 60 minutes.
Standards: Reading 3.2/3.2.4; Speaking 2.1/2.1.5.
Learning objective: Pupils demonstrate the stated skills through SOW-prescribed tasks.
Materials: SB p.14 Activities 1–3; TB p.28; CD 1-15; dictionary.

1. Set induction (8 min): Pupils write two or three learning goals.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Pupils suggest a holiday destination and give reasons using teacher's sentence starters.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Predict information about the cruise ship using SB Activity 1 and TB p.28 Exercise 1; read and check predictions.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Listen and read using CD 1-15 for Activity 2; use dictionaries for Activity 3 unfamiliar words including adjectives.
5. Assessment and closure (8 min): Groups decide whether the cruise ship is a good holiday destination and explain why; assess dictionary skills.

PBD: Collect task-specific spoken, written, listening or reading evidence. Record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching.
Differentiation: Provide extra dictionary time; extend vocabulary search for confident readers.
