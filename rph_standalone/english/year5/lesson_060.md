# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 060 (Speaking 11)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** 2.1.2. **Complementary LS:** 4.3.2. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Verbs: e.g. communicate, survive, kill, grow. **Materials:** SOW-prescribed literary text and BPK CCL guide.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the prescribed skills through specific Wild Life activities and an observable output.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Pupils in small groups say a sentence each about what animals can and can’t do. Choose some sentences to write on board. How many animal skills can pupils remember from previous lesson?

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 5, Use It! Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.50 Exercise 5. They use information in course book and prior knowledge. Check possible answers e.g. 1. Ostrich (Activity 5) 2. Snake (Activity 4) 3. Frog (Activity 1) 4. Scorpion (Activity 3) 5. Camel (Activity 1) 6.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 1. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.51 Exercise 1.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 2. Ask: How do you think a parrot speaks? And a snake? Pupils make parrot and snake sounds. Choose pupils to say the dialogue making sounds of parrot and a snake. 5. Pupils do Activity 3. Follow instructions Teacher’s Book p.51 Exercise 3. Note how well pupils communicate their knowledge of animals in English.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records how well pupils communicate their knowledge of animals in English.

**Closure (5 min):** 6. Pupils in small groups write two or three more questions about animal skills. They hand to another group to answer.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.30, Unit 3 Vocabulary and Listening — Amazing animals, Ex1-5. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Step 2: More proficient pupils are likely to be able to answer 3 and 6 because the animals are mentioned in previous lessons and they may know about them in the L1.
