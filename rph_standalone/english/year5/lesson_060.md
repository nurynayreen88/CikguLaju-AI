# English Year 5 Lesson 60: Speaking 11

Official SOW Lesson 60, printed p.98. Topic: Wild Life. Duration: 60 minutes.
Main and complementary learning standards: Speaking 2.1.2; Writing 4.3.2.
Sources: SB p.36 Activity 5 and p.37 Activities 1-3; TB pp.50-51.

1. Recall what animals can and cannot do.
2. Complete p.36 Activity 5 using animal knowledge.
3. Complete p.37 Activity 1.
4. Practise Activity 2 dialogue with parrot and snake sounds.
5. Complete Activity 3 and write new animal-skill questions for another group.
