# English Year 5 Lesson 60 — Speaking 11

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 60, printed p.97. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.2 and Writing 4.3.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.36 Activity 5 and p.37 Activities 1–3, TB pp.50–51 and Workbook p.30 Exercise 5.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils find out about and report one experience of seeing an animal, then spell two familiar animal or action words correctly in independent writing.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils recall animal abilities from Lesson 59 and give an example using can or can’t.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB p.36 Activity 5, pairs complete a short animal quiz using facts from the preceding listening lesson. They exchange their questions with another pair.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete the can and can’t tasks in SB p.37 Activities 1–2. Pairs rehearse the animal dialogue and revise one answer after feedback.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use the ability questions in SB p.37 Activity 3. Partners then ask whether they have seen one of the animals and what it was doing. Each reports a partner’s experience and independently records one sentence.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one fact or experience from their notes. If time permits, they write one animal-ability sentence using Workbook p.30 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Listen for a question about a partner’s experience and an intelligible report. Check the spelling of two familiar words in the independent sentence.
**Differentiation:** Provide animal pictures and question stems. Ask confident pupils to add when or where the animal was seen.
