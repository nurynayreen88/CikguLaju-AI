# English Year 5 Lesson 152 — Writing 26

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 152, printed p.194. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.3 and Reading 3.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.89 Activities 1, 2 and 4, TB p.103 and WB p.73 Ex2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate specific details in Natalie’s email to Gemma and plan, draft and revise an email about a visit.
**Success criteria:** Identify the meeting arrangements and Sunday weather, then show one meaningful revision in a three-part email.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the London photo and ask pupils what they would plan for a visitor to their town.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Natalie’s email in SB Activity 1 and mark the lines with the meeting and weather details. They retrieve useful phrases in Activity 2.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use the SB Activity 4 Writing Guide. Pupils plan the reason for writing, meeting arrangements and weekend activities, then draft with going to, will and because.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Partners check one detail and give feedback. Pupils revise that part and mark the change. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.73 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Each pupil reads one revised sentence and explains which information is clearer.

**PBD:** Collect the marked reading answers, planning notes and revised email. Check both specific details and one meaningful change after feedback.
**Differentiation:** Provide paragraph starters. Ask confident pupils to give distinct plans for good and bad weather.
