# English Year 5 Lesson 76: Consolidation 7

Official SOW Lesson 76, printed p.115. Topic: Learning World. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.4; Speaking 2.2.1.
Materials: SB p.45 Activities 4-5; TB p.59.

1. Set induction (8 min): Discuss book photos using present continuous questions.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete Activity 4 and compare descriptions.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Make true and false present continuous statements about picture A.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs invent Activity 5 dialogues using I am and I cannot at the moment.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Review spelling rules for -ing and describe more actions.
