# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 076 (Consolidation 7)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** 4.2.4. **Complementary LS:** 2.2.1. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Present continuous tense affirmative and negative. **Materials:** SOW-prescribed literary text and BPK CCL guide.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Writing 4.2.4) — pupils describe people, places and objects using suitable statements in the Learning World activities. Complementary (Speaking 2.2.1) — pupils keep interaction going in short exchanges by asking suitable questions in the linked speaking task.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Hold up English Plus 1 book. Point to two photos on cover: Ask: What are they doing? What’s she doing? Pupils respond in sentences. In pairs they find a photo from Units 1–3 and ask partner: What’s he/she doing?

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 4. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.59 Exercise 4.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils look at picture A again. In pairs, they take turns to say a true and a false statement using present continuous tense. Give examples: The boy is writing. (True) The girl is looking at the birds. (False) Choose pupils to say their true and false sentences to whole class.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 5. Write on board: 1) ‘Do you want to…’ 2) ‘Oh, sorry, I can’t at the moment.’ 3) ‘I’m…’ Chose two pupils to read speech bubbles aloud. Explain they use words in box and words on board to invent their dialogues. Choose pairs to say dialogues aloud. Explain that when you invent something, you’re being creative. CCE – Creativity and Innovation – Ask pupils why it’s important to be a creative thinker. 5.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records pupils who use present continuous accurately when describing people and their actions.

**Closure (5 min):** 6. Pupils in small groups do Finished? Remind pupils of verbs they can use e.g. speak, listen, read, write, think, use, make, sit, stand, climb, wear.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.37,42-43, Unit 4 present continuous + Progress Review, Ex1-4 / Ex1-8. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Step 3: Encourage more proficient pupils to say three longer true and false statements with a conjunction e.g. The teacher is sitting in his chair and reading a book.
