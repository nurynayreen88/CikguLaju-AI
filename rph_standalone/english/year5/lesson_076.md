# English Year 5 Lesson 76 — Consolidation 7

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 76, printed p.114. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.4 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.45 Activities 4–5, TB p.59, photos and Workbook p.37 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write two suitable descriptions of people in a picture and sustain a short conversation with a question and a suitable follow-up.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show a photo and ask pupils to identify what two people are doing. Groups compare their descriptions.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 4, pupils compare the pictures and write one affirmative and one negative sentence to describe the people shown. Partners check the descriptions against the picture.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use SB Activity 5 to model an invitation and a reply that explains what someone is doing. Pupils practise changing the question when a partner is busy.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pairs choose a different picture and improvise another invitation. Each partner asks a follow-up and then writes a clear sentence about what the other person is doing.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share one description. If time permits, pupils complete one pair of contrasting sentences in Workbook p.37 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check two appropriate written picture descriptions and listen for a relevant question and follow-up.
**Differentiation:** Provide a choice of pictures and question stems. Ask confident pupils to offer an alternative invitation.
