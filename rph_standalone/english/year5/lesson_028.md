# English Year 5 Lesson 28 — Consolidation 2

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 28, printed p.63. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.2 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.20 Activities 2, 5–6, TB p.34, WB p.19 Ex5, a local street map and adjective cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils spell familiar comparative adjectives in independent writing and use follow-up questions in a short directions exchange.
**Success criteria:** Spell four comparative forms correctly without copying and ask one relevant follow-up question during a map exchange.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Start a memory chain using places pupils might see in town.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 2 to review adjective opposites and a or an. Pupils independently write comparative sentences for SB Activity 5.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Show a local map. Pupils complete and practise the model dialogue in SB Activity 6, then adapt it to ask directions to another place.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Partners switch roles and use a follow-up question to check a route or travel time. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.19 Exercise 5.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils write comparative forms from adjective cards and compare spellings after the independent work is collected.

**PBD:** Check four independently written comparative forms and listen for a question that follows up on a partner’s directions.
**Differentiation:** Provide map symbols and spoken question prompts. Offer one spelling example before pupils complete the independent task.
