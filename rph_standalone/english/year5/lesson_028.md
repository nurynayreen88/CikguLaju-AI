# Year 5 English Lesson 28 — Consolidation 2

SOW: Lesson 28, p.64. Towns and cities.
Main: Writing 4.3.2. Complementary: Speaking 2.2.1.
Student Book p.20 Activities 2, 5, 6. Teacher Book p.34.

Set induction: Memory Chain using town vocabulary.
Activity 1: Complete Activity 2 and check a/an.
Activity 2: Complete Activity 5 and spell comparative adjectives.
Activity 3: Study a local street map and complete Activity 6 dialogue. Practise questions and answers in pairs.
Closure and assessment: Groups spell comparative forms of pretty, ugly, busy, friendly, noisy and dirty. Teacher records accuracy and ability to maintain short exchanges.

Differentiation: Give supported dialogue roles to pupils who need them.
