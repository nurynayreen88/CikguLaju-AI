# English Year 5 — Lesson 83 (Listening 15)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** Listening 1.1.1. **Complementary LS:** Speaking 2.2.1. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.48 Activities 1–2; TB p.62; CD1.44–1.45; alphabet cards. Workbook mapping and any audio playback remain unverified.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills through observable lesson tasks.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils answer the Think! question about getting help when they cannot remember words.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils listen to CD1.44 in Activity 1 and identify phrases used when asking for language help. Pairs repeat dialogue with matching intonation.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Teacher contrasts capital and small letters and revises capital-letter uses. Pupils complete Activity 2 using CD1.45 and practise spelling names aloud.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pairs listen, write and exchange spelled names and letters, checking sounds that are frequently confused.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks whether pupils recognise and reproduce six target letters and ask for repetition politely.

**Closure (5 min):** Pairs hold up the correct A, E, I, O, U, S, C or Q cards to spoken letters.

**Differentiation:** Repeat letter sounds and display alphabet chart; extend with unfamiliar names.
