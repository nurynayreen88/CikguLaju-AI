# English Year 5 Lesson 83 — Listening 15

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 83, printed p.121. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.1.1 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.48 Activities 1–2, TB p.62, CD1.44–CD1.45, alphabet cards and Workbook p.43 Exercise 7.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils recognise and reproduce four target letter-name sounds and sustain a short exchange by asking for language help and a follow-up.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask what pupils do when they cannot remember a word. Elicit a polite question they could use to ask for help.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Play CD1.44 for SB Activity 1. Pairs identify the request and response and practise a short exchange using the model.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD1.45 for SB Activity 2. Pupils sort letter names by sound, then listen and say four examples without a spoken model.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners spell a name or word to each other. The listener asks for repetition or clarification and checks the letters against what they heard.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Call out selected letter names for pupils to identify. If time permits, pupils match one request to a reply in Workbook p.43 Exercise 7.

**PBD:** Check four letter sounds and listen for a relevant request and follow-up.
**Differentiation:** Offer visible letter cards and a rehearsal. Ask confident pupils to spell an unfamiliar word from a short description.
