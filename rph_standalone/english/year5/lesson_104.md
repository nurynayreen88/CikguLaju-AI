# English Year 5 Lesson 104 — Writing 18

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 104, printed p.143. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.3.3 and Writing 4.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.59 Activity 5, Writing Guide and the Easy menu blog, TB p.73 and Workbook p.51 Exercise 7.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils plan and draft a short food blog, link sentences coherently and improve one part after feedback.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall cookery verbs from Sally’s blog. Pupils pair each verb with a suitable ingredient or action.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils reread the model and identify how a recipe step connects with the next one. Use the Writing Guide in SB Activity 5 to plan ingredients, instructions and a closing.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils independently draft one or two short paragraphs. They use sequence words and link ideas with a conjunction and a reference word such as it or them.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners check whether steps flow logically and references are clear. Each pupil revises one part after feedback and marks the change.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share a revised sentence and explain their change. If time permits, they choose a sequencing word in Workbook p.51 Exercise 7.

**PBD:** Compare the plan and draft, check one clear conjunction and reference, and verify a purposeful revision.
**Differentiation:** Offer a recipe organiser and model links. Ask confident pupils to vary how they connect two steps.
