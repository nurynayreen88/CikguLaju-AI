# English Year 5 — Lesson 104 (Writing 18)

**Status:** DRAF — awaiting page-by-page book QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** Writing 4.3.3; **Complementary LS:** Writing 4.2.5. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested). **CCE:** ICT.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student's Book p.59 Activity 5 and Writing Guide; Teacher's Book p.73; previous lesson's Easy menu blog.

**Objective:** Pupils plan, draft, review and improve a short food blog using sequencing words to link recipe steps.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pairs finish phrases after “chop”, “add”, “cook for” and “serve with”; share examples.

**Activity 1 — Examine model (10 min):** Teacher revisits the blog on SB p.59; pupils identify blue sequencing words at paragraph beginnings and its final “Enjoy your meal!” ending.

**Activity 2 — Think and plan (15 min):** Pupils use Activity 5 Writing Guide. They plan a heading, menu and ingredients for one chosen dish, recipe steps and a reason they like the food (guide points B1–B5; ignore B6 as directed in SOW).

**Activity 3 — Draft and peer review (20 min):** Pupils write a blog with connected sentences using “first”, “next”, “after that” and “finally”. Partners check heading, required content, linking words and ending; writers revise after feedback. Volunteers read aloud.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher compares plan and revised text, checking appropriate sequencing and meaningful improvement.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils name another food blog they would like to write and why.

**Differentiation:** Supply a planning frame and model connectors; challenge confident pupils to add an explanatory detail. **QA:** Workbook mapping not verified; no prescribed audio.

**Workbook (existing source QA map):** p.49,51, Unit 5 Writing — sequencing First/After that/Next/Finally, Ex1-4 / Ex7. Optional brief follow-up; do not replace the SOW tasks.
