## QA interpretation
1. Textbook/Teacher's Book references above are **official SOW page/activity references**, not claims that every printed page was independently visually inspected.
2. For Lessons 5 and 10, use Anthology p.18 and BPK Poetry Guidebook instead of forcing English Plus SB/WB/audio.
3. The SOW does **not** prescribe specific Workbook exercises for these ten lessons. Any workbook addition requires a separate page-by-page check and explicit alignment to the relevant lesson focus; do not fabricate activities.
4. No lesson is marked fully READY until book-page, audio-content and Word/PDF export checks pass.
