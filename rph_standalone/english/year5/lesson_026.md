# English Year 5 Lesson 26 — Writing 5

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 26, printed p.61. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.5 and Reading 3.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.19 Activity 5 and Writing Guide, TB p.33, WB p.17 Ex4 and a planning sheet.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of the model text and connect sentences into a short description of a town or city.
**Success criteria:** State the overall idea of the Edinburgh model and write two coherent paragraphs with suitable conjunctions and pronouns.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall nouns and adjectives from the Edinburgh text and sort them into two groups.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Reread the model on SB p.19. Pupils match its three paragraphs to topic headings, then state the overall idea in one sentence.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use the Writing Guide in SB Activity 5 to plan the location, favourite features and other places or transport. Pupils draft their own description.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pupils link related sentences into two coherent paragraphs and check pronouns with a partner. If time permits, pupils use Workbook p.17 Exercise 4 to practise with another place.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils read one linked pair of sentences and explain how the sentences fit together.

**PBD:** Collect one explicit main-idea response. Check two paragraphs for a clear order, suitable links and pronouns with clear references.
**Differentiation:** Supply a paragraph organiser and linking words. Ask confident pupils to incorporate an extra detail without losing the main idea.
