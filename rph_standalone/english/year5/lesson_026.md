# English Year 5 Lesson 26 — Writing 5

Source: Official Year 5 SOW Lesson 26, printed p.62. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Topic: Towns and cities.
Main and complementary CS/LS: Writing 4.2/4.2.5; Reading 3.2/3.2.1.
Materials: SB p.19 Activity 5 and Writing Guide; TB p.33.
Objective: Pupils complete the specified SOW activities and demonstrate the corresponding learning standards.

1. Set induction (8 min): Sort nouns and adjectives remembered from Edinburgh lesson.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Reread Edinburgh text and match paragraph purposes: location, liked features, other places and transport.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Use Activity 5 Writing Guide to plan a website description of a chosen town or city; write notes for each paragraph.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Write three coherent paragraphs using conjunctions, reference pronouns, there is/are, adjectives and comparisons.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Self-check against Writing Guide and review vocabulary with Correct the Mistake.

PBD: Teacher checks a three-paragraph town description for relevant details, use of conjunctions and appropriate adjective placement; pupils revise one sentence after feedback.
Workbook: Do not assign a Workbook page or exercise until verified. Audio/source status: No SOW audio.
