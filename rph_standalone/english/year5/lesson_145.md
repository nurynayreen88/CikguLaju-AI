# Year 5 Lesson 145 — Reading 25

Status: DRAF. Unit 8; topic: Going away. Official SOW Lesson 145. Main LS 3.2.2; complementary LS 1.2.3.
Materials: SB p.84 Activities 1–2; TB p.98; CD2-31. Workbook mapping and audio playback not yet verified.

Objective: Pupils complete the specified activities and demonstrate the relevant learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Predict holiday destinations.

Main activities (40 min): Read Class News webpage to find pupils' summer plans, discuss webpage features, then listen with text covered to CD2-31 and check details. Infer cool, expensive, boring and huge from clues. Teacher models instructions, monitors and supports pupils.

PBD (10 min): Check specific details from reading and supported listening. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide visual cues, word banks and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent answers.

Verification: Official SOW checked; full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.
