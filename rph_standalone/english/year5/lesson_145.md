# English Year 5 Lesson 145 — Reading 25

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 145, printed p.187. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.2 and Listening 1.2.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.84 Activities 1–2, TB p.98, CD2.31 and a three-box listening strip.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate two details in different Class News paragraphs and follow three stages of a short, supported account about a holiday.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the photos to predict which things the pupils on the school webpage will do during their holiday.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 1, pupils read the Class News posts. Each finds two details from different posts and points to the supporting lines.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** For SB Activity 2, pupils cover the text and listen to CD2.31 for the answers. They open the text afterwards to check the details against the relevant paragraphs.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** The teacher reads a short connected account based on a post, retaining its details. Pupils use the strip to put three stages or events in order.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs compare their order and point to one detail in the Class News text that helped them understand the account.

**PBD:** Check two accurate details from separate posts and three stages placed in a plausible order from the supported oral account.
**Differentiation:** Offer event pictures and a replay or second reading. Ask confident pupils to explain which detail identifies the writer.
