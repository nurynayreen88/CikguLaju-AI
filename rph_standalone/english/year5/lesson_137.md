# English Year 5 Lesson 137 — Listening 24

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 137, printed p.178. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.4 and Speaking 2.1.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.78 Activities 1–3, TB p.92, CD2.27 and three-step listening prompts.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow three supported oral instructions and find out about and report one past experience described by a partner.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Discuss what pupils might ask a singer or actor about something they did when they were younger.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Give three spoken steps for SB Activity 1. Pupils read the possible answers, listen to CD2.27 and place the answers in the gaps. They compare their work.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Replay CD2.27 for SB Activity 2. Pupils follow the teacher’s directions and identify which key phrases are used by the interviewer or guest.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use SB Activity 3 for pairs to practise the interview. Each asks a partner about a past experience and reports one clear detail they learned.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite one pupil to report a partner’s answer and another to describe the listening sequence they followed.

**PBD:** Observe the three instructions being followed in order and check one intelligible question and report about a past experience.
**Differentiation:** Provide a numbered prompt strip and one replay. Ask confident pupils to report when or where the experience happened.
