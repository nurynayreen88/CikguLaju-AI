# Year 5 Lesson 118 — Consolidation 12

Status: DRAF. Unit 6. Topic: Sport. SOW p.158. Main LS 1.2.4; complementary LS 2.2.1.
Materials: SB p.97 Activities 1–4; TB p.111; CD3-15 and CD3-16. Workbook mapping unverified. Listed audio is assigned by SOW but playback is not tested.

Objectives: Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary standards through the activities below.

Set induction (5 min): Elicit video-game vocabulary and game preferences.

Main activities (40 min): Preteach strategy and simulation; discuss game covers in Activity 1. Pupils complete the table while listening to CD3-15 in Activities 2–3, ask clarifying questions and listen to CD3-16 for Activity 4. Teacher models task instructions, monitors pupils and gives corrective feedback.

PBD (10 min): Observe whether pupils follow sequenced instructions and ask suitable questions. Pupils improve one response using feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and submit a short exit response.

Differentiation: Offer visual cues, word banks, shorter instructions and sentence stems as needed; extend proficient pupils with more detailed independent responses.

Verification: Aligned to official Year 5 SOW outline; exact book-page content and audio playback still require final audit. Do not mark READY.
