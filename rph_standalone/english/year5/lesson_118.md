# English Year 5 Lesson 118 — Consolidation 12

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 118, printed p.158. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.4 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.97 Activities 1–4, TB p.111, CD3.15–CD3.16, a listening table and game-cover pictures.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow three supported oral instructions in sequence and ask a suitable follow-up in a short conversation about a game.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the warm-up to elicit familiar types of games and ask pupils to predict what they can learn from the covers.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs match covers and descriptions in SB Activity 1. Before playing CD3.15 for Activity 2, give three oral steps for finding the row, recording a clue and comparing the answer.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 3, pupils listen to CD3.15 again and complete the game details. Partners compare their preferred games and ask a follow-up question.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD3.16 for SB Activity 4. Pupils use the ratings to check their table and ask a partner to explain one opinion or score.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs describe a game they like, what they do in it and the score they would give it.

**PBD:** Observe whether the pupil carries out the three steps in order and ask for one relevant follow-up during the pair exchange.
**Differentiation:** Use a partially completed table and a replay. Ask confident pupils to ask a second question about an answer that surprises them.
