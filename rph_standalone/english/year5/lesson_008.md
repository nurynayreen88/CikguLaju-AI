# English Year 5 — Lesson 008 — Reading 2

**Official SOW:** Primary Year 5 SK Lesson 8, printed p.42; Starter Unit; **Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends; **Topic:** Free time. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main CS/LS:** Reading 3.2/3.2.2. **Complementary CS/LS:** Writing 4.2/4.2.4.
**Language focus:** adjectives and opposites.
**Learning objectives:** Main (Reading 3.2.2) — pupils understand specific information and details of two paragraphs or more in the Free time activities. Complementary (Writing 4.2.4) — pupils describe people, places and objects using suitable statements through the linked writing task.
**Success criteria (teacher-set):** Complete the SOW-aligned core tasks and give observable spoken, written, listening, reading or creative evidence with appropriate support.
**Materials:** SB p.8 Activities 1–2; TB p.22; CD 1-09; phone-parts handout.

## Lesson sequence
1. **Set induction (8 min):** Teacher elicits adjective opposites using big/small and fast/slow, including animal examples.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Activity 1 adjectives, discuss un- prefix, listen and read CD 1-09, and match eight blue adjectives to opposites.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils reread the text and answer Activity 2 questions about three writers; teacher labels mobile-phone parts with a prepared handout (ICT).
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils follow TB p.22 optional vocabulary task and write sentences about belongings using 'I've got' plus adjectives; partners exchange and identify adjectives.
5. **PBD and closure (8 min):** Groups ask what objects they have in pockets, on desks or under beds, giving descriptive answers; teacher records specific-detail comprehension.

## Assessment and differentiation
**PBD:** Teacher records independent/with-support/needs-reteaching performance against the main and complementary standards, using the outputs described in the lesson stages. **Differentiation:** Allow two written sentences rather than three for pupils needing reduced writing load.
