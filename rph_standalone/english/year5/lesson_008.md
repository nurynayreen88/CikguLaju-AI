# English Year 5 Lesson 8 — Reading 2

**Official SOW topic:** Free time.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 8, printed p.42. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.2 and Writing 4.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.8 Activities 1–2, TB p.22, CD1.09, WB p.8 Ex2 and an image of a mobile phone.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils retrieve specific details from different paragraphs and use adjectives to describe their belongings in writing.
**Success criteria:** Locate two accurate details from two different writers’ paragraphs and write three clear object descriptions.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use contrasting animals or objects to review adjective opposites.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read SB Activity 1 and match highlighted adjectives with opposites. Use CD1.09 to check and discuss unfamiliar words.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils reread different writers’ paragraphs for SB Activity 2. They underline the evidence for two answers and identify the writer for each. Use the phone image to clarify one object word from the reading.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Use the optional task in TB p.22. Pupils write three sentences describing things in their bag and compare them with a partner. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.8 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Partners read one description and identify the object.

**PBD:** Check the two marked details from different paragraphs and three suitable adjective–noun descriptions.
**Differentiation:** Offer a paragraph-by-paragraph question guide and an adjective bank. Ask confident pupils to explain why a contrasting adjective fits.
