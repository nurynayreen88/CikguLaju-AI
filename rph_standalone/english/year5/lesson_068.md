# English Year 5 Lesson 68: Writing 12

Official SOW Lesson 68, printed p.106. Topic: Wild Life. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.3.2; Listening 1.1.1.
Materials: SB p.117 Activity 4 and song; TB p.131; CD 3-34.

1. Set induction (8 min): Recall nouns, verbs and adjectives from the song.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Listen and record three to five words per category, then check spelling.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Find rhyming pairs including see/me and frog/dog.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Write six to eight yes/no questions about a chosen animal.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs ask questions to identify animals and count their attempts.

Differentiation: Provide spelling word bank and question starters.
