# English Year 5 Lesson 37 — Reading 7

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 37, printed p.73. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.3.1 and Writing 4.3.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.22 Activity 4 and p.23 Activities 5–6, TB p.37, WB p.20 Ex2 and a frequency chart.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils read short routine texts for interest and spell familiar words accurately in independent sentences.
**Success criteria:** Choose a routine detail they enjoyed reading and explain why. Spell four target words in original sentences about their day.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall daily routines and ask pupils which routines they most enjoy.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Revisit adverbs in SB p.22 Activity 4. Complete the frequency chart for p.23 Activity 5 and check adverb position.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils read My daily routine on SB p.23, select a line that interests them and share why. Use Activity 6 to discuss which habits are true for them.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pupils write three original routine sentences from memory. Collect their first attempt before checking spellings. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.20 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite pupils to share a routine they found interesting or surprising.

**PBD:** Record the reading choice and reason. Check four high-frequency spellings in the original sentences.
**Differentiation:** Provide picture cues and model the frequency chart. Remove the written word bank when checking independent spellings.
