# Year 5 English Lesson 37: Reading 7

Official SOW Lesson 37, page 74. Topic: Days. Duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Reading 3.3.1; Writing 4.3.2.
Sources: SB p.22 Activity 4, p.23 Activities 5–6; TB p.37.

2. Place frequency adverbs on a chart from never to always.
3. Model adverb placement with be and other verbs and complete grammar rules.
4. Read My daily routine and complete Activity 6 using frequency adverbs.
5. Pairs describe personal routines; assess reading and accurate spelling.
