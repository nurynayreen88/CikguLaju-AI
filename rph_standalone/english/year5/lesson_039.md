# English Year 5 Lesson 39 — Language Arts 7

**Official SOW topic:** Watching a Bumble Bee.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 39, printed p.75. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Reading 3.2.2.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.21, Watching a Bumble Bee, BPK Poetry Guidebook p.73 A Bee’s Life, statement strips and mind-map paper.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils find specific details across stanzas and explain a personal response to an action or description.
**Success criteria:** Classify three statements using details from at least two stanzas and explain one like or dislike with a reason.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall insect names with an alphabet prompt, then introduce the poem.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read the poem silently. They use the BPK statement format to sort true and false statements and mark where the poem supports each choice.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils compare answers with a partner. Each uses two details from different stanzas to compare themselves with the bee in a mind map.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Ask pupils to choose one description or action they like or dislike and give a reason linked to the poem. Pairs share and review their responses.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Read a changed detail aloud. Pupils explain what the poem actually says.

**PBD:** Check three correct classifications supported by at least two stanzas. Record each pupil’s reason for a personal response.
**Differentiation:** Mark stanza boundaries and use picture cues. Ask confident pupils to justify one correction with a precise detail.
