# Year 5 English Lesson 39: Language Arts 7

Official SOW Lesson 39, page 76. Topic: Watching a Bumble Bee. Duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Language Arts 5.2.1; Reading 3.2.2.
Sources: Non-textbook: Watching a Bumble Bee, Anthology p.21; BPK Poetry Guidebook Activity 1 p.73.

1. Play ABCs with animal and insect words.
2. Read the poem silently and check comprehension.
3. Classify poem-related statements as True or False.
4. Check peers' choices and make a Bumble bees and me mind map with reasons.
5. Exchange mind maps and give feedback; finish with Spot the Differences.
