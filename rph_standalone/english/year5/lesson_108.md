# English Year 5 Lesson 108 — Speaking 19

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 108, printed p.148. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.3.1 and Listening 1.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.63 Activities 4–6 and Finished?, TB p.77, CD2.13 and Workbook p.52 Exercise 2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of the sports interviews and recount a personal or partner’s sport experience aloud in three connected steps.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a short guessing game to recall sports from the unit. Ask pupils what a sports interview might be about.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Play CD2.13 for SB Activity 4. Pupils choose a main-idea statement and discuss a detail from one answer that supports it.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 5, pairs listen again for useful phrases and practise questions. Each adds a question about a sport somebody played, watched or tried.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** For SB Activity 6, pupils interview a partner. They recount an experience to another pair, naming the activity and explaining what happened at the start and later.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite one pair to retell an experience. If time permits, pupils complete one sports item in the dialogue in Workbook p.52 Exercise 2.

**PBD:** Check one accurate main idea from the interviews and listen for three intelligible, connected points in the recount.
**Differentiation:** Provide a three-part recount frame. Ask confident pupils to include a further detail such as where it happened.
