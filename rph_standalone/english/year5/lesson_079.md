# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 079 (Reading 14)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** 3.2.3. **Complementary LS:** 4.2.4. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Present continuous and simple tenses. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1–4 p.47 Teacher’s Book p.61.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Reading 3.2.3) — pupils guess the meaning of unfamiliar words from clues provided by title, topic and other known words in the Learning World activities. Complementary (Writing 4.2.4) — pupils describe people, places and objects using suitable statements in the linked writing task.

**Set induction (5 min):** Use Pre-lesson Task 12: Word Salad. Use sentences 1–3 in Activity 1 p.47. Write each word on a card for groups of pupils to reorder.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 1. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 1. Pupils then write the question and answers changing pronouns and verb forms.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 2. Pupils in pairs find a different photo from Units 1–3. They ask partner a question about it. Teacher models a present-continuous question and short answer before pupils practise.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 3. Elicit questions from pupils first. Explain if they don’t know an answer be polite and say I’m sorry/ I’m afraid I don’t know. Write this on board. Choose pupils to ask and answer some questions in front of the class. Pupils then complete Activity 4. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 4, but have pupils draw the table in their notebooks. Ask: Which words come after We, I, They?

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks pupils' use of context clues to explain two words or phrases and collects two correctly formed descriptions or questions from the task. Pupils revise one response.

**Closure (5 min):** Hand out flashcards to pupils in groups of six. Pupils take one card each and describe something they’re doing or something they do using word or words on card. Choose pupils from different groups to say sentences.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.39, Unit 4 Language Focus — Present continuous questions; present continuous vs present simple, Ex1-4. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Steps 3 and 5 some pupils may not remember all the vocabulary. Help them with gestures, prompts and visuals.
