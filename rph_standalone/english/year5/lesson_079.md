# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 079 (Reading 14)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** 3.2.3. **Complementary LS:** 4.2.4. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Present continuous and simple tenses. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1–4 p.47 Teacher’s Book p.61.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the prescribed skills through specific Learning World activities and an observable output.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Do Pre-lesson Task 12: Word Salad. Use sentences 1–3 in Activity 1 p.47. Write each word on a card for groups of pupils to reorder.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 1. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 1. Pupils then write the question and answers changing pronouns and verb forms.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 2. Pupils in pairs find a different photo from Units 1–3. They ask partner a question about it. Write gap-fill sentence on board as model. e.g.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 3. Elicit questions from pupils first. Explain if they don’t know an answer be polite and say I’m sorry/ I’m afraid I don’t know. Write this on board. Choose pupils to ask and answer some questions in front of the class. 5. Pupils do Activity 4. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.61 Exercise 4, but have pupils draw the table in their notebooks. Ask: Which words come after We, I, They?

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records pupils who need help to understand meaning of words and those who use clues to help them.

**Closure (5 min):** 6. Hand out flashcards to pupils in groups of six. Pupils take one card each and describe something they’re doing or something they do using word or words on card. Choose pupils from different groups to say sentences.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.39, Unit 4 Language Focus — Present continuous questions; present continuous vs present simple, Ex1-4. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Steps 3 and 5 some pupils may not remember all the vocabulary. Help them with gestures, prompts and visuals.
