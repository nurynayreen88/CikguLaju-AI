# English Year 5 Lesson 79 — Reading 14

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 79, printed p.117. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Writing 4.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.47 Activities 1–4, TB p.61, photo and word cards and Workbook p.39 Exercise 2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer two unfamiliar words using printed clues and write two suitable descriptions of people in the activities.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use word cards to reconstruct a short question from SB Activity 1. Pupils point to the words that show it is about an action happening now.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs complete SB Activities 1–2 and use titles and captions with the pictures to infer the meanings of two action words. They explain their choices before checking.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 3, pupils ask and answer questions about the photos. Each independently writes one description that fits a visible action.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils sort forms and time expressions in SB Activity 4, then write a second description about a usual activity and compare it with the present-action sentence.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to show one clue they used. If time permits, they write one question and short answer from Workbook p.39 Exercise 2.

**PBD:** Check a relevant printed clue for each of two inferred meanings and two clear written descriptions.
**Differentiation:** Highlight clues and offer a verb list. Ask confident pupils to describe a similar scene with a different time expression.
