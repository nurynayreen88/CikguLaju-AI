# English Year 5 Lesson 131 — Listening 23

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 131, printed p.172. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.3 and Reading 3.2.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.76 Activities 1–3, TB p.90, CD2.25, CD2.26 and a timeline.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils order three events from Robert’s story and infer two unfamiliar words from the heading, chart or printed text.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the chart and ask pupils what it might tell them about Robert’s life.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use CD2.25 for SB Activity 1. Pupils read the short text, infer two unfamiliar terms from nearby words or the chart, and then check the verb forms while listening.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD2.26 for SB Activity 2 so pupils can check their prediction about the interview. Ask each pair to name one event they heard.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Replay CD2.26 for SB Activity 3. Pupils record supported details and place three life events in sequence on their timelines.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Partners check the order, then share a clue they used to infer one meaning.

**PBD:** Check three correctly ordered life events and two meanings supported by clues in the printed material.
**Differentiation:** Offer an event bank and one replay. Ask confident pupils to explain how an age or date helped them place an event.
