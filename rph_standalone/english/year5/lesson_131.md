# Year 5 Lesson 131 — Listening 23

Status: DRAF. Unit 7; topic: Growing up. SOW lesson 131. Main LS 1.2.3; complementary LS 3.2.3.
Materials: SB p.76 Activities 1–3; TB p.90; CD2-25/26. Workbook page unverified; listed audio not playback-tested.

Objectives: Pupils perform the lesson task and show evidence of both prescribed standards.

Set induction (5 min): Discuss why people differ and introduce the tallest man who ever lived.

Main activities (40 min): Listen to CD2-25 for past-tense questions; predict then listen to CD2-26 for Robert Wadlow's story and record numbers as digits; compare heights using a metre ruler. Teacher models instructions, monitors work and gives feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check listening comprehension and inferred unfamiliar words. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils state one learning point and complete a brief exit response.

Differentiation: Use visuals, sentence frames and paired rehearsal for support; extend proficient pupils with fuller explanations and independent work.
