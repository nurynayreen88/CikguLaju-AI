# Year 5 Lesson 119 — Listening 21

Status: DRAF. Unit 6. Topic: Sport. SOW p.159. Main LS 1.3.1; complementary LS 3.2.1.
Materials: SB p.68 Activities 1–3; TB p.82; CD2-18 and CD2-19. Workbook mapping unverified. Listed audio is assigned by SOW but playback is not tested.

Objectives: Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary standards through the activities below.

Set induction (5 min): Elicit what pupils did last weekend.

Main activities (40 min): Listen to Alfie and Lois on CD2-18 in Activities 1–2 and infer emotion from familiar words. Read dialogue for main idea and identify question/exclamation punctuation. Listen to CD2-19 Activity 3, matching Peter, Kathy and Rachel with tennis match, restaurant and cinema. Teacher models task instructions, monitors pupils and gives corrective feedback.

PBD (10 min): Check contextual word guesses, dialogue main ideas and name-event matches. Pupils improve one response using feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and submit a short exit response.

Differentiation: Offer visual cues, word banks, shorter instructions and sentence stems as needed; extend proficient pupils with more detailed independent responses.

Verification: Aligned to official Year 5 SOW outline; exact book-page content and audio playback still require final audit. Do not mark READY.
