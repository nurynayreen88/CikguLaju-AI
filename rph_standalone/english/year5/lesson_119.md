# English Year 5 Lesson 119 — Listening 21

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 119, printed p.159. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.3.1 and Reading 3.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.68 Activities 1–3, TB p.82 and CD2.18–CD2.19.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer two meanings from familiar words in the recorded exchanges and state the main idea of the printed weekend dialogue.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Discuss one thing pupils did at the weekend. Ask how they show excitement or disappointment in their voice.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use CD2.18 for SB Activity 1. Pupils identify an expression and use the other words in the exchange to infer how a speaker felt.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 2, pupils read the complete dialogue and choose a main-idea statement. Partners point to cues from the opening and later part of the dialogue.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD2.19 for Activity 3. Pupils match the people to weekend activities and infer the meaning of a second expression from the familiar words around it.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to share one clue they heard and state the main idea of Alfie and Lois’s conversation in one sentence.

**PBD:** Check two inferred meanings supported by spoken clues and one main idea supported by the dialogue.
**Differentiation:** Offer emotion cards and a replay. Ask confident pupils to distinguish how two people felt and cite the clues.
