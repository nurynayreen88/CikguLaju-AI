# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 078 (Speaking 14)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** 2.1.2. **Complementary LS:** 2.2.1. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Present simple tense affirmative and negative; Auxiliary verbs do and don’t. CONTENT STANDARD LEARNING STANDARD. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book p.46 Activities 5 and 6 Teacher’s Book p.60.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Speaking 2.1.2) — pupils find out about and describe experiences up to now in the Learning World activities. Complementary (Speaking 2.2.1) — pupils keep interaction going in short exchanges by asking suitable questions in the linked speaking task.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Pupils look at drawings of children learning a language on p.46 again. In pairs, they choose three and say what the girl or boy is doing. Give an example: In picture ‘h’ the girl is saying the alphabet.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 5. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.60 Exercise 5. Hand out worksheet for each pupil to complete. Read the two headings with do and don’t. Find out how many pupils have more positive than negative strategies.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Explain why it’s helpful to do the things they don’t do e.g. When you know the alphabet, you feel good about spelling words. When you say a new word out loud and write it down, you can remember it better.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 6, Use it! Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.60 Exercise 6. Tell pupils to ask partner: What do you do? When telling the class about their partner’s strategies, pupils with partners who have more positive or more negative answers say two things they do or don’t do using ‘and’. Pupils with partners who have the same number of things say: He/She… but he/she doesn’t… CCE – Values – Ask pupils why they think it’s important to be positive about learning.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher records how well pupils can describe language learning strategies they use.

**Closure (5 min):** 5. Choose a post-lesson task from Section 3.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.38,43, Unit 4 study-language verbs + present simple/continuous review, Ex1-5 / Ex6. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Step 2: Set some pupils the task of writing only 3 strategies they do and don’t do in table.
