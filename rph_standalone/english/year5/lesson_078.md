# English Year 5 Lesson 78 — Speaking 14

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 78, printed p.116. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.2 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.46 Activities 5–6, TB p.60 and Workbook p.38 Exercise 5.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils find out about and describe one language-learning experience, and ask a suitable follow-up question to keep the exchange going.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils pick a study strategy from the unit pictures and say when they last used it.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 5 to discuss what helps people remember new words. Pairs share a method they have tried and say how it helped them.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 6, pupils ask partners about their study habits. They follow up on one reply by asking for an example, a reason or when it happened.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners exchange roles. Each pupil tells another pair about a method their partner has used and one detail they learned from the follow-up.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Compare two experiences. If time permits, pupils complete one personal study-strategy sentence in Workbook p.38 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Listen for a specific learning experience, a relevant follow-up question and an intelligible report of a partner’s answer.
**Differentiation:** Display short question stems. Ask confident pupils to ask how their partner’s method has changed over time.
