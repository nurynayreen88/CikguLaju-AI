# English Year 5 Lesson 78: Speaking 14

Official SOW Lesson 78, printed p.117. Topic: Learning World. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Speaking 2.1.2; Speaking 2.2.1.
Materials: SB p.46 Activities 5-6; TB p.60; strategy table worksheet.

1. Set induction (8 min): Recall language-learning strategies from pictures.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete Activity 5 and sort strategies into I do and I do not.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Discuss why different strategies can help learning.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs complete Activity 6 and ask what their partners do.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Report partner strategies using and or but.

PBD: Observe lesson-specific task evidence for the stated standards.
Differentiation: Offer three strategies as a reduced task or all ten for extension.
