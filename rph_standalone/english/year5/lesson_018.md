# English Year 5 Lesson 18 — Speaking 4

Official SOW: Lesson 18, printed p.54. Unit 1: Towns and cities. Suggested time: 60 minutes.
Standards: Speaking 2.1/2.1.5; Writing 4.2/4.2.1.
Learning objective: Pupils demonstrate the stated skills through SOW-prescribed tasks.
Materials: SB p.13 Activities 5–6; TB p.27.

1. Set induction (8 min): Groups brainstorm town and city words in a mind map; teacher checks spelling.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Model There's a, There isn't a/an, There are some, There aren't any with London examples.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Pupils complete SB Activity 5 and compare sentences in pairs.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Using SB Activity 6 and Key Phrases, pupils describe their own town or village and discuss its positive and less-positive features.
5. Assessment and closure (8 min): Groups write and display one positive statement about where they live; assess oral descriptions.

PBD: Collect task-specific spoken, written, listening or reading evidence. Record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching.
Differentiation: Support three to four sentences; challenge confident pupils to add two more.
