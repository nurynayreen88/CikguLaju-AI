# English Year 5 Lesson 18 — Speaking 4

**Official SOW topic:** Towns and cities.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 18, printed p.53. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.5 and Writing 4.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.13 Activities 5–6, TB p.27, WB p.13 Ex2 and paper for a wall display.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe their area aloud and give written details about their own lives there.
**Success criteria:** Describe three facilities aloud and write two personal details about places they visit or activities they do.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a mind map to recall town and city vocabulary. Pupils choose two places they know.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Review there’s and there are with one London example. Pupils complete SB Activity 5 about their own area and compare sentences.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use SB Activity 6. Pairs take turns describing their area, including one feature they like and one they would change.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Each pupil writes two personal details, such as where they go with a family member and what they do there. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.13 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Groups select one clear description for a class display.

**PBD:** Listen for three distinct spoken details about the area. Check that the two written statements give specific information about the pupil.
**Differentiation:** Use a place-word bank and oral rehearsal. Invite confident pupils to explain why they use a particular place.
