# English Year 5 Lesson 016 — Language Awareness 1

Source: Official Year 5 SOW p.51–52, Starter Unit. Topic: Free Time. Duration: 60 minutes.

Standards: SOW leaves main and complementary skill selection to teacher based on Year 5 DSKP and learner needs. Do not invent official CS/LS.

Materials: English Plus 1 Student's Book p.9 Activities 1, 2, 4, 5; Teacher's Book p.23; SOW Starter Unit self-assessment worksheet.

1. Set induction (8 min): Review vocabulary and grammar from the Starter Unit using an oral recall game.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Follow TB p.23 Exercises 1–2. Pupils study four drawings and distinguish this, that, these and those.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): In pairs pupils arrange six classroom objects at near and far positions, then describe each using demonstratives in full sentences.
4. Activity 3 (15 min): Follow TB p.23 Exercises 4–5. Teacher identifies areas requiring reteaching based on pupils' observed performance.
5. PBD (5 min): Teacher checks two pupil responses from the Starter Unit tasks and notes a specific need for reteaching. Pupils correct one response where necessary.
6. Closure (5 min): Pupils complete the official Starter Unit self-assessment (Great/OK/A little) on free time, describing objects, asking questions and writing a personal email. Teacher uses the responses to plan follow-up.

Differentiation: Support pupils reading the self-assessment; allow home language for reflection if necessary.
