# English Year 5 Lesson 93 — Language Arts 16

**Official SOW topic:** The Meal.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 93, printed p.132. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Speaking 2.1.4.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.20, The Meal by Karla Kuskin, BPK Poetry Guide p.113 Activity 1, food picture pieces and survey slips.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils give a reason for liking or disliking something about the meal in the poem, and ask about and describe a breakfast plan for tomorrow.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use the picture puzzle from the poetry guide. Groups assemble food pictures and describe what they see.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs read The Meal and underline the foods in the poem. They compare the list with the pictures and choose one part of the breakfast they like or dislike.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils give a reason for their response. The teacher then models a question and answer about what someone is going to eat tomorrow.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Partners interview each other about tomorrow’s breakfast. Each draws or notes one food choice and tells another pair what their partner plans to eat.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Use a short whisper chain based on a detail from the poem. Invite a pupil to restate their breakfast plan and one reason for it.

**PBD:** Listen for a reason tied to the poem and an intelligible question and answer about a future plan.
**Differentiation:** Offer food pictures and question stems. Ask confident pupils to compare their plan with the breakfast in the poem.
