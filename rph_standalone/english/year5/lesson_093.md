# English Year 5 — Lesson 93 (Language Arts 16)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** The Meal. **Main LS:** Language Arts 5.2.1. **Complementary LS:** Speaking 2.1.4. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.20, The Meal; BPK CCL Poetry Guide, Activity 1 p.113; simple survey form. Non-textbook Language Arts: no Student Book, Workbook or audio prescribed by this SOW lesson.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Language Arts 5.2.1) — pupils explain in simple language why they like or dislike an event, description or character in a text in the The Meal activities. Complementary (Speaking 2.1.4) — pupils ask about and describe future plans in the linked speaking task.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher follows Poetry Guide Activity 1 Steps 1–4 to introduce the poem.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pairs read The Meal silently, underline the food items and connect them to the illustration.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Teacher asks What are you going to eat for breakfast tomorrow? Pupils prepare a short favourite-breakfast survey list and interview a partner.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Partners draw each other's breakfast choices and describe them using I'm going to eat… for breakfast, giving reasons when possible.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks each pupil's connection to the poem and ability to ask and answer about a breakfast plan.

**Closure (5 min):** Pairs complete a whisper chain using a sentence from the poem and check the final version.

**Differentiation:** Use food picture cards and a sentence frame; extend with two breakfast options.
