# English Year 5 Lesson 98: Writing 17

Official SOW Lesson 98, printed p.138. Topic: Food and health. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.1; Speaking 2.1.3.
Materials: SB p.56 Activity 4 and p.57 Activities 1, 2, 4; TB pp.70-71.

1. Set induction (8 min): Review health adjectives with Sit Down Stand Up.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Pairs answer the health quiz orally, then write personal answers.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Practise verb plus -ing with like, prefer and hate.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Complete p.57 Activities 1, 2 and 4 on preferences and advice.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Give classmates advice using eat, go, drink, do and do not.

PBD: Assess responses and work against stated skills.
Differentiation: Provide sentence starters and fewer preference examples.
