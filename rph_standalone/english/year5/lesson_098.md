# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 098 (Writing 17)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned, awaiting source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** 4.2.1. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.3. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Verbs + gerund: like -ing, prefer –ing, love -ing, don’t like -ing, hate –ing. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student Book p.56 Activity 4 and p.57 Activities 1, 2 and 4; Teacher Book pp.70–71.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Writing 4.2.1) — pupils give detailed information about themselves in the Food and health activities. Complementary (Speaking 2.1.3) — pupils ask for, give and respond to simple advice in the linked speaking task.

**Set induction (5 min):** See instructions in Section 3 for Pre-lesson Task 5: Sit Down, Stand Up. Use the adjectives on p.56, e.g. ‘A lazy teacher does a lot of work’. (false!).

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 4 orally. Ask: Which questions have the same answers for you and your partner? Choose pairs to say quiz letters with the same answers.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils write letters a–h and answers to quiz in sentences. Do a and b as examples: a) I think I’m… b) I usually sleep for… Pupils compare with a partner. Choose pairs to read a sentence to class. CCE – Values – Ask pupils why it’s important to be active and fit, and find out what they do to keep fit.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Write on board: verb + -ing. Give examples such as: I like reading. I prefer watching sport. I hate eating beans. Pupils do Activity 1. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 1. Teacher then writes on the board: love doing, don’t mind doing, hate doing. Pupils do Activity 2. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 2. Pupils complete Activity 4 in pairs. Explain we can use imperatives to give advice. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 4.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks two correct personal statements using verb + -ing and observes an understandable spoken piece of advice with an appropriate imperative. Pupils improve one response after feedback.

**Closure (5 min):** Choose a post-lesson task from Section 3 to review likes and dislikes.

**Workbook practice (mapped):** p.47, Unit 5 Language Focus — Verb + -ing, Ex1-3. Use briefly; do not replace SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Give suitable vocabulary or sentence prompts; challenge confident pupils with independent responses. **Final check:** Full printed-page and audio-content audit, A4 export and teacher approval pending; not READY.
