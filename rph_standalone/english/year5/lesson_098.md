# English Year 5 Lesson 98 — Writing 17

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 98, printed p.137. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.1 and Speaking 2.1.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.56 Activity 4 and p.57 Activities 1, 2 and 4, TB pp.70–71 and Workbook p.47 Exercise 3.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write three specific details about their habits or preferences and ask for, give and respond to simple advice in pairs.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a brief stand-or-sit game to recall adjectives from the health quiz.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils ask and answer the questions in SB p.56 Activity 4. They independently turn three answers about their own habits into full sentences.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB p.57 Activities 1–2, pupils use verbs followed by -ing to describe what they like, prefer or avoid. Each adds a specific detail to one of their sentences.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use SB p.57 Activity 4 for partners to discuss simple suggestions. Each asks for advice about a habit, listens to a suggestion and gives a relevant response.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one personal sentence and one suggestion. If time permits, they add a detail about a leisure activity from Workbook p.47 Exercise 3.

**PBD:** Check three personal details in the written sentences and observe a complete request–advice–response exchange.
**Differentiation:** Provide a model and choices for the quiz. Ask confident pupils to say how the suggestion relates to a preference they have described.
