# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 001

**Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends  
**Starter Unit / Topic:** Free time  
**Focus:** Listening 1  
**Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested classroom allocation; not prescribed by SOW)  
**Lesson type:** Textbook-based  
**Cross-curricular elements:** Language; Values (online safety)

## Curriculum alignment (official Primary Year 5 SK SOW, p.35)
- **Main CS 1.2:** Understand meaning in a variety of familiar contexts.
- **Main LS 1.2.1:** Understand with support the main idea of longer simple texts on a range of familiar topics.
- **Complementary CS 3.2:** Understand a variety of linear and non-linear print and digital texts by using appropriate reading strategies.
- **Complementary LS 3.2.1:** Understand the main idea of simple texts of two paragraphs or more.
- **Language focus:** Present simple affirmative/negative; possessive adjectives (e.g. *my*).

## Learning objectives
By the end of the lesson, pupils should be able to:
1. Identify the main idea of the listening-and-reading passage about a girl's free time, using visual or textual support.
2. Recognise relevant free-time vocabulary in Student's Book p.4, Activities 1–2, and use it to predict missing words before listening.
3. Explain one safe online-chatting habit in a simple sentence.

**Success criteria (teacher-set; not SOW quotations):** Pupils can explain the text's main idea, attempt all missing-word predictions, check their answers against the recording, and give one online-safety response. Record which pupils require further listening/reading support; do not claim a numeric passing score is prescribed by the SOW.

## Teaching and learning sequence

### Set induction / Pre-lesson — 10 minutes
1. Teacher writes **Free time** on the board and asks whether going to school and holidays count as free time, clarifying the meaning.
2. Pupils stand in a circle. Moving clockwise, each pupil says a connected noun, adjective or verb (e.g. *swimming, holidays, beach, sand, sun, hot, picnic, like*). Repeat anti-clockwise to practise recall.
3. Teacher elicits familiar free-time expressions and notices pupils who need vocabulary prompts.

### Activity 1 — Listening for free-time vocabulary — 15 minutes
1. Teacher opens **TB p.18, Exercise 1** and plays **CD 1-02**, following the teacher-book instructions.
2. Pupils listen, then examine **SB p.4, Activity 1** and find words they also said during the circle game.
3. Teacher asks for a show of hands: Was listening to free-time words easy or difficult? Pupils identify what helped them understand (pictures and the printed word box).
4. Teacher checks pronunciation and meaning through short oral responses; do not invent an answer key absent from the accessible source.

### Activity 2 — Read, predict and listen to confirm — 20 minutes
1. Teacher directs pupils to **SB p.4, Activity 2**, reads its vocabulary aloud and checks meaning.
2. Teacher models *photograph* and *photography* and elicits the difference in word stress.
3. Pupils read the passage about a girl's free time. In pairs, they predict words for the gaps before hearing the recording.
4. Teacher plays **CD 1-03**. Pupils check their predictions against the audio and discuss which words were correct. If Track 1-03 is unavailable, **do not claim this audio check occurred**: retain the activity as pending and use a teacher-read version only after checking the original SB text.
5. Teacher asks why care is needed when chatting online and elicits reasonable screen-time habits.

### Assessment and differentiation — 10 minutes
1. Teacher asks: *What is the text mainly about?* Pupils respond orally, with support from SB p.4 where needed.
2. Pupils point out two clues (picture, vocabulary, or passage wording) that helped them understand the topic.
3. Teacher observes whether pupils can follow the longer text while listening and reading, and notes names needing additional support.
4. Support: give slower readers/writers extra processing time, allow pointing before speaking, and pre-teach a small number of essential free-time words (consistent with SOW differentiation Strategy 4).
5. Extension: confident pupils explain one difference between their own free time and the girl's, using a present-simple sentence.

### Closure / Post-lesson — 5 minutes
1. Teacher asks pupils to recall three free-time words and share one sentence about their own free-time routine.
2. Pupils give one practical online-safety reminder. Teacher summarises the lesson's main idea.
