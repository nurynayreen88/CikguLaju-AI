# Year 5 Lesson 151 — Reading 26

Status: DRAF. Unit 8 Going away. Main LS 3.2.3; complementary LS 4.2.5.
Materials: English Plus 1 Student Book p.87 Activities 1, 2, 5 and 6; Teacher Book p.101.

Objective: Infer unfamiliar words from context and write a connected paragraph predicting a partner's future.

Set induction (5 min): Discuss weather and future predictions using I think I'll and I won't.

Main activities (40 min): Teacher models will/won't and contractions. Pupils complete Activities 1–2, identify meaning using topic clues, and draft a paragraph predicting a partner's future using and, but, or and pronouns. Pairs compare and discuss predictions in Activity 6.

PBD (10 min): Check contextual inferences and paragraph cohesion. Pupils revise two linked sentences.

Closure (5 min): State two predictions about their own future.

Differentiation: Provide model sentences or require independent predictions with reasons.
