# English Year 5 Lesson 151 — Reading 26

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 151, printed p.193. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Writing 4.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.87 Activities 1, 2, 5 and 6, TB p.101 and WB p.71 Ex2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer words from the topic and surrounding text and write one connected paragraph predicting a partner’s future.
**Success criteria:** Explain two meanings using printed clues and link three predictions with suitable conjunctions and pronouns.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Recall the Antarctica interview and elicit one prediction about the weather.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read SB Activities 1–2 and underline words that help infer two meanings. Check the clues and model will and won’t.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** For SB Activity 5, pupils ask a partner for ideas and draft a paragraph linking three predictions using suitable conjunctions such as and, but and or.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pairs read and discuss predictions in SB Activity 6. Pupils revise one connection. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.71 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one prediction about themselves and one about their partner.

**PBD:** Check two word-and-clue pairs and a coherent paragraph with clear pronoun references and at least two appropriate links.
**Differentiation:** Provide a prediction bank. Ask confident pupils to connect different predictions without repeating the subject’s name.
