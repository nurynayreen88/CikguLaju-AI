# English Year 5 Lesson 81 — Language Arts 14

**Official SOW topic:** Miss Antrobus.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 81, printed p.119. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Reading 3.2.2.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.16, Miss Antrobus by Richard Edwards, the BPK poetry teaching guide and mind-map paper.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate two details in different stanzas and give a personal preference with a reason connected to the poem.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use an alphabet game to recall sea creatures. Ask pupils to predict what would be unusual about an octopus as a pet.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Read the poem in two groups, alternating lines. Pupils reread and identify two actions of the octopus from different stanzas.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs use a mind map to record why Miss Antrobus likes her pet. They point to the words in each stanza that support two ideas.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils compare their own reactions to the pet’s actions. Each chooses a preference and explains it to a partner using because and one detail from the poem.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite pupils to say whether they would choose an octopus as a pet and connect their answer to something they read.

**PBD:** Check two details from separate stanzas and listen for a personal preference with a reason linked to the poem.
**Differentiation:** Provide action pictures and sentence stems. Ask confident pupils to compare their reason with the speaker’s reason.
