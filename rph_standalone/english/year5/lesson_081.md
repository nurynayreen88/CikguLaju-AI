# English Year 5 Lesson 81 - Language Arts 14

SOW p.120. Non-textbook poem Miss Antrobus. Anthology of Poems p.16 and BPK Poetry Guidebook.
Main LA 5.2.1; complementary Reading 3.2.2.

Set induction: Pupils name sea creatures from alphabet clues.
Activity 1: Read the poem in alternating groups.
Activity 2: Discuss why the character loves the octopus.
Activity 3: Pairs make a mind map using love, like, dislike and hate and explain their reasons.
Closure and assessment: Pupils say whether they would want an octopus as a pet and why.

Differentiation: Provide guided discussion in larger groups. Draft pending QA.
