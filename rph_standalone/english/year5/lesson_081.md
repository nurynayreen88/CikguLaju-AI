# English Year 5 — Lesson 81 (Language Arts 14)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** Miss Antrobus. **Main LS:** Language Arts 5.2.1. **Complementary LS:** Reading 3.2.2. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.16, Miss Antrobus; BPK CCL Poetry Guide. Non-textbook Language Arts: no Student Book, Workbook or audio prescribed by this SOW lesson.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills through observable lesson tasks.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils recall sea creatures using the ABCs game.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Teacher divides the class into two groups. Each group reads alternating lines of the Miss Antrobus poem while pupils follow the text.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs discuss why Miss Antrobus loves her octopus and identify lines that support their explanations.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pairs draw a mind map of reasons and rank reactions as hate, dislike, like or love. Pupils state their own preferences with specific reasons.

**PBD (5 min):** Each pupil points to a relevant part of the poem and gives a clear preference supported by a reason.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils reflect on whether they would like an octopus as a pet and why.

**Differentiation:** Use a visual emotion scale and sentence starters; extend with two reasons.
