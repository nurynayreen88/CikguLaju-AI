# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 109 (Reading 19)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned, awaiting source QA. **Topic:** Sport. **Main LS:** 3.2.2. **Complementary LS:** 3.2.4. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Noun suffixes -er, -or e.g. winner, competitor; Dates e.g. 1988; 2016. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1, 2 and 3 p.64 Teacher’s Book p.78; Audio: CD2.14.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Reading 3.2.2) — pupils understand specific information and details of two paragraphs or more in the Sport activities. Complementary (Reading 3.2.4) — pupils use with support familiar print and digital resources to check meaning in the linked reading task.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils do Think! question at top of p.64. Elicit information about the Olympic Games from pupils. Note: check where next Olympics are and when.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Write current year on board, say it, pupils repeat. Write year pupils were born, year they started school, next year on board. Say the years on board, class repeats then choose individual pupils to say them.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Write years on board ‘1896’, ‘1900’, ‘1912’, ‘1916’, ‘1920’, ‘1924’, ‘1936’, ‘1960’, ‘1988’, ‘2012’, ‘2016’. Pupils choose one year and write it down on a piece of paper. Say a year on the board and pupils who wrote the date, hold it up. Find out the year most pupils wrote. Ask: Are there any years you didn’t write down?

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2, CD2.14. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.78 Exercise 2. Ask: Which four sports are mentioned in the questions? (swimming, marathon running, golf and rugby). 5. Write the five blue words in the text on board. Pupils do Activity 3. After checking meanings in dictionaries, write on board: ‘winner’, ‘competitor’, ‘spectator’, swimmer, ‘medallist’. Ask: Which words have the same letters at the end? (winner / swimmer; competitor / spectator).

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks reading answers and asks pupils to justify one with a detail from the text.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils do Optional Activity: Vocabulary plus to consolidate sports vocabulary. See Teacher’s Book p.78.

**Workbook practice (mapped):** p.56, Unit 6 Reading — A Paralympic sport / sports word forms, Ex1-5. Use briefly; do not replace SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Give suitable vocabulary or sentence prompts; challenge confident pupils with independent responses. **Final check:** Full printed-page and audio-content audit, A4 export and teacher approval pending; not READY.
