# English Year 5 Lesson 109 — Reading 19

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 109, printed p.149. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.2 and Reading 3.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.64 Activities 1–3, TB p.78, CD2.14, dictionary and Workbook p.56 Exercise 5.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils locate two facts from different parts of the Olympics timeline and use a familiar reference to check one sports word.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils name an Olympic sport and predict what information a timeline might show. Model how to read two years aloud.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 1, pupils read the timeline and note the sports it mentions. Pairs point to two different entries and locate a fact in each.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use CD2.14 with SB Activity 2. Pupils decide whether the statements are true or false and mark the entries that verify their answers.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** For SB Activity 3, pupils use a dictionary or classroom reference to check one highlighted word and choose the meaning that best fits the timeline.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils share one event and one checked word. If time permits, they complete one vocabulary item from Workbook p.56 Exercise 5.

**PBD:** Check two accurate facts from different entries and one suitable meaning with its reference.
**Differentiation:** Mark the years to scan and model a dictionary lookup. Ask confident pupils to compare two entries.
