# English Year 5 Lesson 109: Reading 19

Official SOW Lesson 109, printed p.149. Topic: Sport. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Reading 3.2.2; Reading 3.2.4.
Materials: SB p.64 Activities 1-3; TB p.78; CD2-14.

1. Set induction (8 min): Discuss Olympic Games and practise reading years.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Play a year-identification game with timeline dates.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Read seven sports information passages in Activity 1.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Listen to CD2-14 and answer Activity 2 questions.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Use dictionaries for winner, competitor, spectator and suffixes -er, -or.

PBD: Assess pupil responses and work against the relevant standards.
Differentiation: Focus on seven texts rather than all eleven.
