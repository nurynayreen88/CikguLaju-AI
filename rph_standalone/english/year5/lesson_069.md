# English Year 5 Lesson 69 — Language Arts 12

**Official SOW topic:** And Something Weird Happened.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 69, printed p.106. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Speaking 2.1.5.
**Materials:** And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby, BPK Short Stories Guidebook p.73 Handout 1 and a character quiz.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils describe a story character intelligibly and explain why they like or dislike that character.
**Success criteria:** Give two accurate spoken details about a character and explain one preference using a relevant action from the story.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Distribute the Who Am I? character pictures. Pupils name the character and recall one fact.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs look back at the story to connect each selected character with an action. Pupils note a second accurate descriptive detail.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Each pupil chooses a character to describe. Group pupils with the same choice so they can compare details and explain their own reasons.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Pupils share their description and preference with a new partner. The partner checks that the reason fits an action in the story.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Use a short character quiz and discuss the evidence for one answer.

**PBD:** Hear two accurate spoken details and record one reason for a like or dislike that refers to the story.
**Differentiation:** Offer character pictures and because stems. Invite confident pupils to compare the actions of two characters.
