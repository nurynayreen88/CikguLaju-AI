# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 069 (Language Arts 12)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** And Something Weird Happened. **Main LS:** 5.2.1. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.5. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Reasoning (because). **Materials:** SOW-prescribed literary text and BPK CCL guide.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the prescribed skills through specific And Something Weird Happened activities and an observable output.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Teacher distributes pictures of the characters from the guidebook (page 85) in random so that each pupil will receive a random picture of the character. 2.

**Activity 1 (22 min):** Teacher starts the discussion on the characters and their actions in the story. 4. Each pupil has to choose a character that they like the most and provide details of the character (e.g. Joel – likes to eat ice cream, likes to read books, etc.). 5.

**Activity 2 (23 min):** Teacher encourages pairs to provide reasons and share their reasons with the rest of the class.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher observes whether pupils complete the named activities and communicate a response.

**Closure (5 min):** 7. Pop quiz – teacher provides each pupil with short MCQ questions where pupils choose the best answer. E.g.: Joel likes to eat ____________ (a) pizza (b) Nasi Lemak (c) spaghetti And Something Weird Happened.

**Non-textbook materials:** No English Plus Workbook or CD exercise is assigned to this Language Arts lesson.

**Differentiation:** Use a model and partner support as needed; extend confident pupils with an independent task.

**QA:** Workbook mapping is historical QA. Physical book pages, audio content and A4 export not independently verified; not READY.
