# English Year 5 Lesson 69: Language Arts 12

Official SOW Lesson 69, printed p.107. Topic: And Something Weird Happened. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Language Arts 5.2.1; Speaking 2.1.5.
Materials: NON-TEXTBOOK: And Something Weird Happened by Pamela Rushby; BPK Short Stories Guidebook p.85.

1. Set induction (8 min): Distribute story character pictures and recall their details.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Discuss characters and their actions in the story.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Choose a favourite character and describe them.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Pairs with the same favourite character give reasons using because.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Share reasons and complete a short character multiple-choice quiz.
