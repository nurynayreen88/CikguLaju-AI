# English Year 5 Lesson 3 — Reading 1

**Official SOW topic:** Free time.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 3, printed p.37. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.1 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.5 Activities 6–8, TB p.19, CD1.05, WB p.5 Ex5 and a world map.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main idea of a short dialogue and keep an introduction exchange going with a suitable question.
**Success criteria:** State the dialogue’s main purpose and ask one follow-up question linked to a partner’s answer.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Locate Prague on a world map and compare a feature of the houses in the photo with local houses.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read SB Activity 6 and choose the correct pronouns. Clarify their, there and they’re using the text.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Pupils read SB Activity 7 and choose its main purpose. Play CD1.05 to check details such as Yasmin’s hobby, then pairs practise the dialogue.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Using the Key Phrases in SB Activity 8, pairs introduce themselves or a friend and add a relevant follow-up question. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.5 Exercise 5.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Read an altered line from the dialogue. Pupils spot the change and restate the purpose of the exchange.

**PBD:** Check each pupil’s gist response and observe whether the follow-up question relates to their partner’s answer.
**Differentiation:** Offer two gist options and question starters. Ask confident pupils to keep the conversation going with a second question.
