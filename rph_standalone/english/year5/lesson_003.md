# English Year 5 — Lesson 003 — Reading: Free time

**Source:** Official Primary Year 5 SK Scheme of Work, Lesson 3, printed p.37. **Theme:** World of Self, Family and Friends. **CCE:** Language and Environmental Sustainability. **Duration:** 60 minutes suggested.

**Main CS/LS:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.1 — Understand the main idea of simple texts of one or two paragraphs (as stated in the lesson SOW).
**Complementary CS/LS:** Speaking 2.2 / 2.2.1 — Keep interaction going in short exchanges by asking suitable questions.
**Grammar:** Subject pronouns I, you, we, they.

**Objective:** Identify the main idea of a short reading text and use suitable questions to maintain an introduction dialogue. **Success criteria:** Explain the text's main idea with or without prompts and participate in a personalised dialogue using a suitable follow-up question.

**Materials:** English Plus 1 Student's Book p.5 Activities 6–8; Teacher's Book p.19; CD 1-05, Drive MP3 `1teVj-V8NNFaiA5wwreNZzlngiStImVDY`; world map.

## Lesson sequence
1. **Set induction (10 min):** Teacher shows a world map and locates Europe, the Czech Republic and Prague. Pupils describe three visible features of Prague houses in SB p.5 and compare them with Malaysian houses.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Activity 6 and select subject pronouns; compare answers with a partner. Teacher explains differences among *their*, *they're* and *there* using TB p.19.
3. **Activity 2 (13 min):** Teacher introduces the term *eco-house*, eliciting low-energy ideas. Pupils read and listen to Activity 7 (CD 1-05), then pairs read the dialogue aloud.
4. **Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils read Key Phrases for introducing people. With a new partner, they replace blue dialogue words with their own names and hobbies and perform the exchange, asking follow-up questions.
5. **PBD and closure (10 min):** Teacher checks main-idea understanding and interaction. Teacher changes five or six words in the Activity 7 text and reads it aloud; pupils signal detected changes (Spot the Differences).

**Differentiation:** Pair confident and developing readers; give additional processing time. Extension: add a relevant follow-up question and respond naturally.

**PBD:** Observe main-idea responses, pronoun selection and partner exchanges. Record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching. **Reflection:** Met objective __/__; follow-up __.
