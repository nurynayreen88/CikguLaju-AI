# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 097 (Reading 17)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned, awaiting source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** 3.3.1. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.3. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Adjectives: fit, unfit, tired, lazy, hungry; Imperative verbs: Eat, Don’t eat, Go, Don’t go. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1, 2 and 3 p.56 Teacher’s Book p.70; Audio: CD2.06.

**Objective:** Pupils complete the specific Reading 17 lesson tasks using Food and health vocabulary and produce observable assessment evidence.

**Set induction (5 min):** See instructions in Section 3 Pre-lesson Task 7: Beat the Teacher. Use ‘healthy’ as the key topic word.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Ask pupils the Think! question on p.56. They can choose from the six ideas in box. 3. Pre-teach: ‘active’, ‘fit’, ‘hungry’, ‘thirsty’, ‘tired’, ‘lazy’. Elicit type of word (adjectives). Pupils in pairs do Activity 1. Ask: Which words helped you to complete the sentences? e.g. 1) an – needs an adjective with a vowel; 2) eat – hungry; 3) good – healthy; 4) a – needs an adjective with a consonant; 5) don’t sleep – tired.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2, CD2.06. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.70 Exercise 2. Say: Two letters are the same at the start of two adjectives. What are they? (un). Explain ‘un’ means not. Ask: Can they think of other adjectives starting with un? (unhappy, unpopular, untidy).

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Activity 3. Explain body temperature is about 37 degrees. Usain Bolt can run 100 metres in about 10 seconds. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.70 Exercise 3. When checking 1, 2, 5 pupils say: It’s best to/healthiest to… 6. Pupils look at the Health quiz again. They give advice to a partner about one of the things a, b or c that they shouldn’t do e.g. It’s not good to eat snacks because you can’t enjoy your meal.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks reading answers and asks pupils to justify one with a detail from the text.

**Closure (5 min):** : 7. Choose a post-lesson task from Section 3 to review health vocabulary.

**Workbook practice (mapped):** p.46-47, Unit 5 Health adjectives + Imperatives, Ex1-5 / Ex4. Use briefly; do not replace SOW tasks.
