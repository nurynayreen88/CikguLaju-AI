# English Year 5 Lesson 97: Reading 17

Official SOW Lesson 97, printed p.137. Topic: Food and health. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Reading 3.3.1; Speaking 2.1.3.
Materials: SB p.56 Activities 1-3; TB p.70; CD2-06.

1. Set induction (8 min): Play Beat the Teacher using healthy.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Discuss the Think question and health vocabulary.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Read and listen to the health quiz in Activity 2.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Complete Activities 1 and 3 and identify health advice.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Pairs give positive advice about sleep, food and exercise.

PBD: Assess responses and work against stated skills.
Differentiation: Provide extra time for quiz reading.
