# English Year 5 Lesson 42 — Speaking 8

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 42, printed p.78. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.1 and Listening 1.1.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.24 Activity 4 and p.25 Activities 1–3, TB pp.38–39, CD 1-24 and Workbook p.21 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils give one routine detail about each of two family members and distinguish and pronounce the endings /s/ and /ɪz/.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use Say What’s Missing with family flashcards.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs compare the Radfords’ routines with their own using but in SB p.24 Activity 4.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete SB p.25 Activities 1–2 and check the verb form with a partner.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD 1-24 for SB p.25 Activity 3. Pupils sort the target endings, repeat them and use two target verbs when describing family routines.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Groups share two routines. If time permits, pupils write two present-simple sentences using the pictures in Workbook p.21 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Listen for a specific detail about each of two family members. Check that pupils distinguish and produce /s/ and /ɪz/.
**Differentiation:** Provide a verb-ending chart and two model comparisons.
