# English Year 5 Lesson 59 — Listening 11

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 59, printed p.96. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.5 and Reading 3.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.36 Activities 1–4, TB p.50, CD1.33 and CD1.34, dictionaries and Workbook p.30 Exercise 2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils answer a sequence of three supported questions about animal abilities and use a dictionary to check one unfamiliar word.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils suggest animals that move quickly or slowly. The teacher mimes two action verbs and elicits sample animal-ability questions.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pairs use the verbs to complete SB Activity 1. Play CD1.33 and pause between questions so pupils can check their choices.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils look up one word such as ground, distance or intelligent. They use the meaning to attempt the quiz in Activity 2, then listen to CD1.34 in Activity 3 to check the answers.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** For SB Activity 4, pupils listen again to CD1.34 and decide which extra facts the speakers give. Ask three supported questions in sequence and have pupils record their answers in order.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Use one short question dictation from TB p.50. If time permits, pupils complete one of the animal dialogues in Workbook p.30 Exercise 2.

**PBD:** Check three responses to the spoken sequence and one accurate meaning from a dictionary entry.
**Differentiation:** Offer pictures and a second listen. Ask confident pupils to explain which words helped them identify an extra fact.
