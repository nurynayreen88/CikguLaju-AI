# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 059 (Listening 11)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** 1.2.5. **Complementary LS:** 3.2.4. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Modal auxiliary verbs: can (positive use) can’t (negative use) for ability. **Materials (visually cross-checked):** English Plus 1 Student Book p.36 Activities 1–4; Teacher Book p.50; audio CD1-33 and CD1-34. This is a textbook-based listening lesson, not BPK literature.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Listening 1.2.5) — pupils understand a sequence of supported questions in the Wild Life activities. Complementary (Reading 3.2.4) — pupils use with support familiar print and digital resources to check meaning in the linked reading task.

**Set induction (5 min):** 1. Brainstorm fast-running and slow-moving animals. Write on board in two columns. Ask: Which is the fastest land animal? (cheetah) Which is the slowest land animal?

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Read aloud introduction to Animal skills quiz. Pupils in pairs do Activity 1, CD1.33. Read verbs in box aloud but not ‘kill’ and ‘survive’. Pupils mime actions. Pre-teach ‘kill’ and ‘survive’ and relate to animals. Pupils read quiz questions and complete gaps with the verbs. Play CD1.33 again. Pupils listen to questions and check answers.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Teacher writes distance, ground and intelligent; pupils use dictionaries to check meanings before SB Activity 2 with CD1-34. Pairs answer the animal questions. Pupils then complete Activity 3, following TB p.50 Exercise 3, and share an interesting animal ability.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 4, CD1.34. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.50 Exercise 4. Ask: Think about the animals in the podcast. Which animal would you like to find out more information about? What information would you like to find out? Note how well pupils understood the questions in the quiz and podcast.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks answers to two heard animal-skill questions, including one response that depends on CD1-34, and records which pupils can use a dictionary to check one unfamiliar word.

**Closure (5 min):** Teacher conducts the optional short question dictation from TB p.50; pupils check one answer and explain their evidence.

**Workbook (visually cross-checked):** WB p.30 Unit 3 *Vocabulary and Listening — Amazing animals* is the closer optional follow-up to this listening lesson. The previously linked p.31 contains *can for ability* grammar practice and is not the primary listening exercise. Workbook does not replace SB p.36 tasks.

**Differentiation:** At Steps 3 and 4 provide positive comments to pupils who find listening to questions in longer texts hard.
