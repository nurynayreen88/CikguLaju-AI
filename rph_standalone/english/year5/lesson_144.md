# English Year 5 Lesson 144 — Speaking 25

**Official SOW topic:** Going away.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 144, printed p.186. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.4 and Writing 4.2.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.83 Activities 5–6, TB p.97, CD2.30 and a brief past-outing prompt.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask about and describe a future holiday plan, then write a short factual account of a past outing.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils recall one item from the previous listening lesson. Ask what they might take on a future trip.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Replay CD2.30 for SB Activity 5. Pupils complete the key phrases and distinguish what each speaker prefers or needs.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** For SB Activity 6, groups choose a holiday. Pupils ask what a partner is going to do and describe their own future plan with two details.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Each pupil writes a short factual account of an outing they actually had, such as a school trip or family outing. They include what happened first and one later event.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Share one future plan and one detail from an actual outing. Compare how the language shows which event is in the future and which has happened.

**PBD:** Observe a question and specific answer about a future plan and check two ordered factual details in the written recount.
**Differentiation:** Use a future-plan stem and a two-box recount organiser. Ask confident pupils to give a reason for one of their future choices.
