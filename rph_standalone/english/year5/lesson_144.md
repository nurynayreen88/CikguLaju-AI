# Year 5 Lesson 144 — Speaking 25

Status: DRAF. Unit 8; topic: Going away. Official SOW Lesson 144. Main LS 2.1.4; complementary LS 4.2.3.
Materials: SB p.83 Activities 5–6; TB p.97; CD2-30. Workbook mapping and audio playback not yet verified.

Objective: Pupils complete the specified activities and demonstrate the relevant learning standards.

Set induction (5 min): Recall camping equipment.

Main activities (40 min): Listen to CD2-30 for holiday preferences. Discuss future camping plans with I think we need... and It depends where... Groups write three actions to protect nature on an eco-holiday. Teacher models instructions, monitors and supports pupils.

PBD (10 min): Assess questions and descriptions of plans, and written holiday actions. Pupils revise one response after feedback.

Closure (5 min): Pupils share a learning point and complete an exit response.

Differentiation: Provide visual cues, word banks and sentence frames as needed; extend confident pupils with detailed independent answers.

Verification: Official SOW checked; full book-page, workbook and audio audit pending before READY.
