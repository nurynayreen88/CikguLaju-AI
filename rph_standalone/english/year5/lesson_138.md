# English Year 5 Lesson 138 — Speaking 24

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 138, printed p.179. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.3 and Writing 4.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.78 Activity 4 and model interview, TB p.92, advice cards and Workbook p.67 Exercise 6.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask for, give and respond to simple advice in an interview and write a short dialogue performing the same three functions.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Pupils name a goal they would like to achieve. Ask who they might ask for help getting started.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Review the pop-star interview and SB Activity 4 reactions. Model how a guest can ask for advice and signal that they understood the suggestion.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs use the three-part advice card to practise an exchange. The guest asks about a problem, the interviewer offers a suitable suggestion and the guest responds.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Each pupil writes a short exchange with a request, advice and a reply. Partners perform the dialogue, then exchange roles using another prompt.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask how the guest responded to the advice. If time permits, pupils choose a suitable reaction in Workbook p.67 Exercise 6.

**PBD:** Check the three advice functions in both the oral exchange and the written dialogue.
**Differentiation:** Provide a choice of problems and advice starters. Ask confident pupils to respond to the suggestion with a reason.
