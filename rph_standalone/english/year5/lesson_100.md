# English Year 5 Lesson 100 — Consolidation 10

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 100, printed p.139. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.2 and Speaking 2.1.3.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.57 Activities 5–7 and Finished?, TB p.71, advice cards and Workbook p.47 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils ask for, give and respond to simple advice in writing and complete a short spoken advice exchange.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show two familiar signs. Pupils suggest what each one tells people to do and explain when advice is more useful than a rule.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 5, pairs match signs to affirmative or negative imperatives. Pupils explain why an example is a rule.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 6, pupils use the situations to write a short request for advice. Partners write one suitable suggestion, then the first writer replies in writing.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Use SB Activity 7 for a new spoken exchange. Each pupil asks for advice, gives a relevant suggestion and responds before swapping roles.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Compare one rule from Finished? with one piece of advice. If time permits, pupils complete an imperative in Workbook p.47 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Check the written request, suggestion and response and listen for the same three functions in the spoken exchange.
**Differentiation:** Provide a set of advice verbs and sentence starters. Ask confident pupils to respond to a suggestion with a reason.
