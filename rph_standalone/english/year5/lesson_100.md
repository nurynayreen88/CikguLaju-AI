# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 100 (Consolidation 10)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned, awaiting source QA. **Topic:** Food and health. **Main LS:** 4.2.2. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.3. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Verb + gerund e.g. I like playing; Affirmative and negative imperatives e.g. Go, Don’t go. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 5–7 p.57 Teacher’s Book p.71.

**Learning objectives:** Main (Writing 4.2.2) — pupils ask for, give and respond to simple advice in the Food and health activities. Complementary (Speaking 2.1.3) — pupils ask for, give and respond to simple advice in the linked speaking task.

**Set induction (5 min):** Write on board: ‘I like…-ing’, ‘I love…-ing’, ‘I prefer…-ing’. Explain pupils can say a true or false sentence about themselves. After each, pupils put up their right hand if they think the sentence is true, left hand if they think it’s false.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils do p.57, Activity 5. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 5. Ask: What signs can you see in our town/city/village? Choose pupils to draw some on board. 3. Pre-teach: ‘marathon’ and ‘boring’. Pupils do Activity 6. Follow instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 6. Tell pupils to use some verbs from Activity 5.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 7, Use It!. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.71 Exercise 7. CCE – Values – ask pupils why it is important to think about advice people give you?

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pupils in pairs do Finished?. They write 3, 4 or 5 rules for the class. CCE – Values – ask pupils what the difference is between rules and advice. Note pupils who are confident at asking for and giving advice and those who are slow to ask and respond.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher observes responses to the prescribed SOW tasks and records an individual improvement.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils look at pictures of signs in Activity 5 then draw a sign but change words or symbols e.g. 1) a different date, 4) an arrow facing left.

**Workbook practice (mapped):** p.47,50-51, Unit 5 Verb + -ing / Imperatives + Progress Review, Ex1-4 / Ex1-7. Use briefly; do not replace SOW tasks.
