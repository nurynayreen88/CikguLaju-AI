# English Year 5 Lesson 100: Consolidation 10

Official SOW Lesson 100, printed p.140. Topic: Food and health. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.2; Speaking 2.1.3.
Materials: SB p.57 Activities 5-7; TB p.71.

1. Set induction (8 min): Guess true or false preferences using verbs plus -ing.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Complete Activity 5 and discuss local signs.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Complete Activity 6 giving advice for different situations.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Complete Activity 7 and discuss rules versus advice.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Write classroom rules and design signs for a partner to interpret.

PBD: Assess responses and work against stated skills.
Differentiation: Accept two or three rules or extend to five.
