# English Year 5 Lesson 58 — Consolidation 5

**Official SOW topic:** Wild Life.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 58, printed p.95. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.1 and Speaking 2.2.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.94 Activities 2–5, TB p.108, CD3.08 and CD3.09.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils identify the main purpose of a recorded phone call and sustain a short phone exchange with two relevant questions.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Show the SB photograph and ask pairs to predict where Holly is and why a friend might phone her.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Play CD3.08 for SB Activity 2. Pupils identify the main purpose of the call and confirm the location. Replay for Activity 3 so pairs can match the questions and responses.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pairs complete the mini-dialogue in SB Activity 4 and listen to CD3.09 to check it. They rehearse the questions with suitable intonation.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** In SB Activity 5, pupils change partners, choose a new location and improvise a phone call. Each caller asks a location question and a follow-up before agreeing what to do next.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs compare the aquarium and skateboard park. Ask one pair to demonstrate a follow-up question that helped the call continue.

**PBD:** Check that pupils identify the main purpose of the first call and ask two suitable questions in the new exchange.
**Differentiation:** Provide a location card and two question stems. Ask confident pupils to improvise without a written dialogue.
