# Year 5 English Lesson 62 - Writing 11

SOW page 100. Topic: Wild Life.
Main skill: Writing 4.3.3. Complementary: Reading 3.2.2.
References: Student Book page 39 Activities 4-6; Teacher Book page 53.

Set induction: Pupils use such as, like and for example to describe animals.
Main activity: Pupils read supplied information about a Malaysian endangered animal. They plan a leaflet explaining its habitat, threats and ways to help. Pupils draft one or two paragraphs using the writing guide.
Assessment: Pupils check headings, facts, threats, ways to help, adjective forms, examples, and can or cannot. They revise their draft and draw the animal.
Differentiation: Mixed ability pairs and guided feedback.
