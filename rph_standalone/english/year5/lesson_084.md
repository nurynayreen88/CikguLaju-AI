# English Year 5 — Lesson 84 (Speaking 15)

**Status:** DRAF — pending full source QA. **Topic:** Learning World. **Main LS:** Speaking 2.1.5. **Complementary LS:** Listening 1.2.4. **Time:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.48 Activities 3–5; TB p.62. Workbook mapping and any audio playback remain unverified.

**Objective:** Pupils demonstrate the main and complementary skills through observable lesson tasks.

**Set induction (5 min):** Pupils stand when a spoken letter begins their name; class notices rare initial letters.

**Activity 1 (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 3 and identify double letters in names. Teacher models polite requests for spelling.

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete Activity 4; partners ask how to say and spell two or three school subjects, using names from SB p.42.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Pairs complete Activity 5 Use It! and practise a new help-seeking dialogue based on the earlier model; volunteers perform and listen for correct spelling.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks that each pupil asks for clarification and gives an intelligible spelling response.

**Closure (5 min):** Class runs a Memory Chain using remembered words or spellings.

**Differentiation:** Give dialogue cards; extend to city and country names with accurate spelling.
