# English Year 5 Lesson 84 — Speaking 15

**Official SOW topic:** Learning World.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 84, printed p.122. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Speaking 2.1.5 and Listening 1.2.4.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.48 Activities 3–5 and p.42 school-subject list, TB p.62 and alphabet cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils follow three spoken instructions in order and give two intelligible descriptions of a person, object or subject in a help-seeking exchange.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Call out letters and ask pupils to stand when one begins their name. Discuss a name with a double letter.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 3 to practise saying and recording repeated letters. Give three oral instructions to choose a subject, show its card and spell it to a partner.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** For SB Activity 4, pairs ask how to say or spell classroom words. If a partner is unsure, the speaker describes the word using two suitable details.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** In SB Activity 5, pupils prepare a brief new help-seeking dialogue. They ask for help, describe the item or subject and check that their partner has identified it.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs share one useful clue they gave. Invite a pair to demonstrate a new exchange without reading from the book.

**PBD:** Observe whether pupils follow the three instructions in order and check two suitable details in their spoken description.
**Differentiation:** Provide familiar objects and a short sentence frame. Ask confident pupils to describe something without pointing to it.
