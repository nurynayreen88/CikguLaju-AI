# English Year 5 Lesson 110: Writing 19

Official SOW Lesson 110, printed p.150. Topic: Sport. Suggested duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.3; Reading 3.3.1.
Materials: SB p.64 Activity 4 and p.65 Activities 1-2; TB pp.78-79.

1. Set induction (8 min): Review sport vocabulary and writing conventions.
2. Activity 1 (12 min): Discuss the most interesting Olympic fact in Activity 4.
3. Activity 2 (15 min): Write two or three sentences about sports pupils like watching.
4. Activity 3 (17 min): Study was, wasn't, were, weren't in the Olympic timeline.
5. PBD and closure (8 min): Read the marathon story and create a short imagined sport story.

PBD: Assess pupil responses and work against the relevant standards.
Differentiation: Give positive feedback on writing effort.
