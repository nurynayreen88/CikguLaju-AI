# English Year 5 Lesson 110 — Writing 19

**Official SOW topic:** Sport.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 110, printed p.150. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.3 and Reading 3.3.1.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.64 Activity 4 and p.65 Activities 1–2, TB pp.78–79 and Workbook p.53 Exercise 2.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils choose an Olympic event that interests them and write a factual account with three details from the timeline in a clear order.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask pupils which sports they enjoy watching. They look back at the Olympics timeline and select an event or athlete they want to read about.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB p.64 Activity 4, pupils reread a chosen entry and tell a partner why it interests them. Each notes three facts they could use in a short account.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Use SB p.65 Activities 1–2 to review there was and there were. Pupils check two facts against the timeline before deciding the order for their account.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Each pupil writes a short factual account using the details they found. Partners compare one detail with the printed timeline and suggest a correction if needed.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite pupils to read one event detail they found interesting. If time permits, they describe the crowd or venue using Workbook p.53 Exercise 2.

**PBD:** Check three supported, logically ordered facts and a personal response to an entry the pupil chose and read.
**Differentiation:** Give a fact organiser and past-tense stems. Ask confident pupils to link two details without changing the facts.
