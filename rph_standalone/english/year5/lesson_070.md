# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 070 (Language Awareness 4)

**Status:** DRAF (SOW activities revised; final visual and teacher QA pending). **Topic:** Wild Life. **Main LS:** Teacher selects. **Complementary LS:** Teacher selects. **Duration:** 60 min, suggested.

**Language focus:** Review of language from Unit 3. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 3, 4, 5 p.41 Teacher’s Book p.55.

**Learning objectives (teacher-selected):** Pupils review language from Unit 3 through the Wild Life activities and complete a unit self-assessment; teacher selects main and complementary DSKP standards.

**Set induction (5 min):** Teacher revisits Unit 3 animal vocabulary and can/can't structures in a short pre-lesson recall task; pupils contribute examples.

**Activity 1 (22 min):** Write on board ‘Stand in order’ and check pupils understand meaning. Pupils in small groups do p.41, Activity 3. Follow the instructions in Teacher’s Book p.55 Exercise 3. Use the pupils' first language if clarification is needed. Pupils then complete Activity 4 in pairs.

**Activity 2 (23 min):** Write on board ‘What am I?’ Pupils do Activity 5. Fast finishers can write another 3 What am I? sets of words for a partner to identify a different animal. Check answers after each activity. Ask: Which activity is the most fun to do? Which Activity would you like to do again to review new language?

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher checks each pupil's responses to Activities 3–5, asks for one explanation of an answer, and records the most frequent Unit 3 error for follow-up. Pupils correct one error.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils complete a Unit 3 self-assessment: one strength and one area requiring practice. Teacher collects the sheets to plan review.

**Workbook practice:** Workbook p.34-35, Unit 3 Progress Review — complete Unit 3 review, Ex1-8. Use as brief follow-up without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Use a model and partner support as needed; extend confident pupils with an independent task.
