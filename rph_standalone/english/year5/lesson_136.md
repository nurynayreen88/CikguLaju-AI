# English Year 5 Lesson 136 — Consolidation 14

**Official SOW topic:** Growing up.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 136, printed p.177. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.4 and Writing 4.3.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.80 Activities 1, 2, 4 and 5, TB p.94 and a classroom dictionary.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils check two word meanings with a familiar reference and spell three high-frequency words correctly in independently written sentences.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use finger writing to review familiar regular and irregular past verbs.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 1 to discuss changes in appearance. Pupils compare their answers with the printed paragraph and identify one unfamiliar word.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** In SB Activity 2, pupils check two word meanings with a dictionary or familiar picture reference before sequencing the life events.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils use SB Activities 4–5 to write their own past sentences, then compare one positive and one negative form with a partner after writing.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Ask pupils to give one word meaning and show the reference they used. Each pupil checks three chosen spellings.

**PBD:** Verify two meanings against the source and check the spelling of three familiar words in the pupil’s own sentences.
**Differentiation:** Model the first dictionary lookup and offer a short verb bank. Ask confident pupils to explain why an alternative meaning does not fit.
