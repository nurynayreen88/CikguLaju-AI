# Year 5 Lesson 136 — Consolidation 14

Status: DRAF. Unit 7, Growing up. SOW Lesson 136. Main LS 3.2.4; complementary LS 4.3.2. Materials: SB p.80 Activities 1,2,4,5; TB p.94; dictionaries.

Objective: Pupils demonstrate both listed standards through the lesson activities.

Set induction (5 min): Finger-write past verbs.

Main activities (40 min): Use dictionaries to correct the paragraph in Activity 1, complete irregular verbs in Activity 2, then write affirmative and negative sentences in Activities 4–5. Teacher models instructions and monitors responses.

PBD (10 min): Assess resource use and spelling. Pupils revise one response based on feedback.

Closure (5 min): Share one learning point and complete an exit ticket.

Differentiation: Provide visuals and sentence starters for support; require extended responses where appropriate.

Verification: Official SOW checked. Workbook mapping and full book/audio QA pending; status remains DRAF.
