# Year 5 English Lesson 38: Writing 7

Official SOW Lesson 38, page 75. Topic: Days. Duration: 60 minutes.
Standards: Writing 4.2.4; Speaking 2.3.1.
Sources: SB p.23 Activity 7 and Finished!; TB p.37.

1. Review frequency adverbs with Finger Writing.
2. Complete Activity 7 using frequency adverbs.
3. Brainstorm well-known people from sport, music and film.
4. Groups create an imagined daily-routine fact file for a famous person.
5. Groups narrate their routine and add drawings with captions; assess writing and narration.
