# Year 5 English Lesson 29 — Listening 6

Official SOW: Lesson 29, printed p.64. Topic: Options: Extra Listening and Speaking. Duration: 60 minutes.
Skills: Listening 1.2.5; Writing 4.3.2.
Sources: SB p.92 Activities 1–3; TB p.106; CD 3-02, 3-03.

Objective: Pupils recognise five spoken questions, complete Tobias's information table and ask one suitable follow-up question.

1. Set induction (8 min): **Teacher:** Run the printed TB p.106 Warm-up and introduce the personal-information questions. **Pupils:** Recall familiar questions and predict which details a speaker may give.
2. Activity 1 — Listen and correct (12 min): **Teacher:** Follow SB p.92 Activity 1 using CD3.02; show the crossed-out wrong answers and replay where needed. **Pupils:** Listen to the dialogue, write the corrected answers and compare them with a partner.
3. Activity 2 — Questions and meaning (15 min): **Teacher:** Play CD3.03 for SB p.92 Activity 2, asking pupils to tick Luke's questions and check the meaning of *quite big*, *very friendly* and *annoying* in context. **Pupils:** Tick the questions heard, compare with another partner and identify Tobias/Luke from oral evidence.
4. Activity 3 — Listening details and accurate writing (17 min): **Teacher:** Replay CD3.03 for Activity 3; monitor the words entered in Tobias's information table and use TB p.106 optional Listening practice only where appropriate. **Pupils:** Complete the table, check key words and correct high-frequency spelling with textual support as needed.
5. Assessment and closure (8 min): **Teacher:** Check each pupil's response to the supported question sequence (Listening 1.2.5) and the spelling of independently entered high-frequency words (Writing 4.3.2). Invite pairs to ask and answer SB Key Phrases about their town and sibling, using *quite*, *really* or *very*. **Pupils:** Show their corrected table, take turns answering a question and state one detail they heard.

PBD: Teacher checks answers to two listening details about Tobias and observes an intelligible short exchange using the prescribed Key Phrases.
