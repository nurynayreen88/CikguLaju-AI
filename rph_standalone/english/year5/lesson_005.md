# English Year 5 Lesson 5 — Language Arts 1

**Official SOW topic:** This Tooth.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 5, printed p.39. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Language Arts 5.2.1 and Reading 3.2.3.
**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.18, This Tooth by Lee Bennett Hopkins, BPK Poetry Guidebook and stanza cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer the meaning of action verbs in This Tooth and explain a personal response to an event.
**Success criteria:** Infer two verbs using a picture, the topic or familiar words, and give one reason for liking or disliking an event.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use Simon Says to revise body-part words and show the poem illustration.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read the poem silently. Pairs identify what happens to the loose tooth.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Groups receive one stanza. They use the illustration and known words to guess the meaning of the action verbs and show the verbs through mime.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Each pupil chooses an event from the poem, explains a reaction with because and points to one detail from the text or picture.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pupils mime what they might do if their own tooth were loose.

**PBD:** Ask each pupil for two verb inferences with clues and record one reason connected to an identifiable event.
**Differentiation:** Use illustrated verb choices and a because stem. Accept short spoken or written reasons.
