# English Year 5 — Lesson 005 — Language Arts: This Tooth

**SOW:** Primary Year 5 SK, Lesson 5, printed p.39. **Lesson type:** NON-TEXTBOOK. **Theme:** World of Stories. **CCE:** Language, Creativity and Innovation. **Suggested time:** 60 minutes.
**Main CS/LS:** Language Arts 5.2 / 5.2.1 — Explain in simple language why they like or dislike an event, description or character in a text.
**Complementary CS/LS:** Reading 3.2 / 3.2.3 — Guess meanings of unfamiliar words using title, topic and other known words.
**Language focus:** Verbs, including past tense.
**Objective:** Infer the meaning of poem action verbs and give a personal response with a reason.
**Success criteria:** Pupils demonstrate selected action verbs and state whether they like the poem event with a reason.

**Materials:** Anthology of Poems p.18, *This Tooth*; BPK Contemporary Children's Literature Teaching Guidebook (Poetry); number cards 1 and 2. Do not attach English Plus Student's Book, Workbook or audio to this non-textbook lesson.

## Teaching sequence
1. **Set induction (8 min):** Teacher revises body parts using *Simon Says*. Pupils mime prompts such as touching their cheek.
2. **Activity 1 (10 min):** Teacher introduces the poem and discusses its illustrations. Pupils read the poem silently.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Teacher divides pupils into small groups, assigning stanza 1 or 2 with number cards. Group 1 infers *jiggled, jaggled, jerked*; Group 2 infers *pull, pushed, poked*, using illustrations and known-word clues. Groups act out their stanzas.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Pupils discuss whether they like the event in the poem and explain why orally or in writing. Teacher may scaffold with 'I like / do not like it because ...'; this is a teacher-created sentence frame, not a poem quotation.
5. **PBD and closure (10 min):** Teacher observes vocabulary inference, actions and reasons. Ask 'What would you do if your tooth were loose?' Pupils mime their imaginative response.

**Differentiation:** Accept short phrases or incomplete sentences when a pupil cannot yet form a full reason, as recommended in SOW Strategy 3. Extension: justify a preference between the two stanzas.
**PBD evidence:** Verb inference, stanza dramatisation and personal response; record Independent / With support / Needs reteaching.
