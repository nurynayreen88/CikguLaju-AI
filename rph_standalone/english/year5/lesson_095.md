# English Year 5 Lesson 95 — Listening 17

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 95, printed p.134. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Listening 1.2.3 and Listening 1.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.96 Activities 1–3, TB p.110, CD3.12, CD3.13, fraction cards and a price chart.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils sequence three events in the market conversation and answer three supported questions about the purchases, weights or prices.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Ask what people can buy at a market. Use images or cards to review a kilogram, half a kilogram and a quarter.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Use SB Activity 1 to practise the shopping phrases. Pupils match amounts to cards and predict what a customer might ask.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Play CD3.12 for SB Activity 2. Pupils record the fractions they hear and check them with a partner.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Play CD3.13 for SB Activity 3. Pupils order three events in Emma’s shopping trip, then listen again and answer three supported questions about what she buys and what it costs.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Pairs retell the purchase order from their notes and compare one amount with the recording.

**PBD:** Check three events in a plausible order and three accurate responses to the spoken question sequence.
**Differentiation:** Offer a numbered chart and replay the relevant turns. Ask confident pupils to distinguish a unit price from the amount paid.
