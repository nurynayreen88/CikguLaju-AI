# English Year 5 Lesson 94 — Consolidation 9

**Official SOW topic:** Food and health.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 94, printed p.133. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Writing 4.2.3 and Speaking 2.2.2.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.55 Activities 4–5 and Finished?, TB p.69 and Workbook p.45 Exercise 4.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils write a short factual account of a meal they remember and help their group agree on steps for a food-list task.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Use a food-word memory chain with a, an and some. Pupils recall when they last ate one of the foods.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** In SB Activity 4, pupils select quantifiers and explain two choices. Discuss how planning quantities can reduce waste.
3. **Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils complete SB Activity 5. Groups agree who will choose a celebration, list foods and check quantities for the Finished? task.
4. **Activity 3 (17 min):** Each pupil writes three or four factual sentences about a meal they actually had, including what happened first and what food was served. Partners check one quantity phrase.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Invite groups to share their food lists. If time permits, pupils complete two quantity items in Workbook p.45 Exercise 4.

**PBD:** Check whether the pupil recounts an actual meal in a clear order and contributes a proposal or response to the group’s agreed steps.
**Differentiation:** Give a time-sequence frame and food words. Ask confident pupils to add a factual detail about how the meal was prepared or shared.
