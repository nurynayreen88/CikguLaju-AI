# English Year 5 Lesson 43 — Reading 8

**Official SOW topic:** Days.
**Official SOW:** Lesson 43, printed p.79. **Suggested duration:** 60 minutes.
**Main and complementary learning standards:** Reading 3.2.3 and Listening 1.2.5.
**Materials:** English Plus 1 SB p.26 Activities 1–4, TB p.40, CD1.25, WB p.22 Ex2 and picture cards.
**Learning objectives:** Pupils infer meanings using clues in Happy Days! and follow a sequence of supported classroom instructions.
**Success criteria:** Explain two inferred word meanings with clues from the text and carry out three spoken instructions in order.

1. **Set induction (8 min):** Discuss special days pupils know and predict words that might appear in the text.
2. **Activity 1 (12 min):** Pupils read Happy Days! for SB Activity 1. They infer two unfamiliar words from the context and explain their clues before checking.
3. **Activity 2 (14 min):** Use the study strategy in Activity 2. Give three supported instructions for Activity 3. Pupils inspect the photos, listen to CD1.25 and number the pictures in the order heard.
4. **Activity 3 (18 min):** Replay CD1.25 for Activity 4 and check details. Ask pupils to explain which step helped them listen. If time permits, pupils complete Workbook p.22 Exercise 2.
5. **Closure (8 min):** Make a true or false statement about a picture and ask pupils to justify their responses.

**PBD:** Check two plausible meanings with clues from the text. Observe whether pupils follow the three task instructions in sequence.
**Differentiation:** Give numbered picture cues and replay the recording. Model one contextual clue before pupils attempt the others.
