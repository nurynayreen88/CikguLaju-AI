# English Year 5 — Standalone RPH — Lesson 040 (Consolidation 3)

**Status:** DRAF — SOW-aligned revision, pending final visual/audio and teacher QA. **Topic:** Days. **Main LS:** 1.2.2. **Complementary LS:** 2.1.4. **Duration:** 60 minutes (suggested).

**Language focus:** Time expressions: o’clock quarter past, half past, quarter to; at (time) on (day) a.m. p.m. CONTENT STANDARD LEARNING STANDARD. **Materials:** English Plus 1 Student’s Book Activities 1–5 p.93 Teacher’s Book p.107; audio CD3.05, CD3.06, CD3.07.

**Learning objective:** Pupils use Days language to complete the prescribed Consolidation 3 tasks and demonstrate main LS 1.2.2 and complementary LS 2.1.4.

**Set induction (5 min):** Write on board ‘in the morning’, ‘in the afternoon’, ‘in the evening’, ‘at night’. Pupils brainstorm things they do at these times e.g. I have a snack at 11 in the morning. Elicit two ways to write this time (11am or 11.00).

**Activity 1 (15 min):** With a large clock, review quarter past, half past and quarter to. Put clock hands in several positions and pupils say time. Ask: What’s the digital time? Pupils do p.93, Activity 1. Ask: Do you always use a digital clock?

**Activity 2 (15 min):** Pupils do Activity 2, CD3.05. Check answers and write on board.

**Activity 3 (15 min):** Read instructions for Activity 3 aloud. Ask: What do you need to find out? Play CD3.06. Check answers. Play CD again. Ask: Which sport has the same word in English and Malay? (judo). Tell pupils table tennis is sometimes called the same word in English and Malay (ping-pong). 5. Pupils do Activity 4. Follow instructions for Exercise 4 on p.107 in Teacher’s Book. When checking, pupils say both time expressions for questions 1, 2 and 4. For example: At 5.45 p.m. At 5.45 in the afternoon. 6.

**PBD (5 min):** Teacher observes and records: Pupils who can listen for details well and those who find it hard to remember details in longer texts.

**Closure (5 min):** Pupils in pairs say dialogue in Activity 5 but change the days, times and sports.

**Workbook link (from existing verified mapping):** Workbook p.26-27, Unit 2 Progress Review — Daily routines / review practice, Ex1-8. Use as brief practice or evidence without replacing the SOW tasks.

**Differentiation:** Provide visual models or paired support for pupils who need help; extend confident pupils through independent responses. **QA note:** Workbook mapping is a prior source QA result; Student Book, Teacher Book, audio-content and A4 layout have not all been independently verified.
