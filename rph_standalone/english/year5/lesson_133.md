# Year 5 Lesson 133 — Reading 23

Status: DRAF. Unit 7, Growing up. SOW Lesson 133. Main LS 3.2.3; complementary LS 4.3.2. Materials: SB p.77 Activities 1–3; TB p.91.

Objective: Pupils demonstrate both listed standards through the lesson activities.

Set induction (5 min): Recall The Gentle Giant.

Main activities (40 min): Use a question-word table to form past-tense questions. Read about the Avatar film director, infer film director, truck driver and Oscar from pictures and context, and write answers. Teacher models instructions and monitors responses.

PBD (10 min): Check inference and spelling. Pupils revise one response based on feedback.

Closure (5 min): Share one learning point and complete an exit ticket.

Differentiation: Provide visuals and sentence starters for support; require extended responses where appropriate.

Verification: Official SOW checked. Workbook mapping and full book/audio QA pending; status remains DRAF.
