# English Year 5 Lesson 73 — Reading 13

SOW p.112. Unit 4 Learning World. Main Reading 3.2.2; complementary Speaking 2.1.2.
Sources: Student Book p.44 Activities 1, 2, 4; Teacher Book p.58; audio CD1-40.

Set induction: Pupils brainstorm school subjects and discuss the Think question.
Activity 1: Teacher displays a world map and four school photos. Pupils predict countries, then read and listen to the texts.
Activity 2: Pupils identify types of school and locate details in the descriptions.
Activity 3: Pupils complete Activity 2 and identify writers' opinions about schools.
Assessment and closure: Pairs discuss what they like about their school and explain their answers.

Differentiation: Allow rereading and praise effort. Workbook activity not verified; audio playback not independently checked. Draft pending book and output QA.
